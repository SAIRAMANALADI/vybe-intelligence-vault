## Summary

- **1501 Program**: Supports industrial R&D projects with grants up to **75% of eligible costs** (max **₺5M** for SMEs, **₺10M** for large enterprises) for **12–36 months**, focusing on high-tech and innovation-driven sectors.

- **1511 Program**: Funds **priority-area R&D** (e.g., AI, biotech, advanced manufacturing) with **50–75% grants** (max **₺20M** per project) for **24–48 months**, targeting strategic national technologies under the **Teknoloji Odaklı Sanayi Hamlesi** initiative.
