## Summary

arXiv:2606.12200v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We study policy representation learning from unlabeled multi-policy behavioral data. Each episode is generated by a fixed policy, but policy labels are unavailable. This setting appears in robotics play, demonstrations, games, racing, and other datasets where heterogeneous behaviors are mixed without annotations. We introduce \emph{Behavioral INR}, a self-supervised generative model that adapts implicit neural representations (INRs) from vision
