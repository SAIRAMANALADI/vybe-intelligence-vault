## Summary

- **Role Management**: Team admins can assign or modify member roles (admin/user) via the **Member roles** tab under **Operations** in the team dashboard, with changes applicable even to pending invites.
- **Member Invitation**: Admins can invite new members to Compass via the **Teams** dropdown or dashboard, assigning roles (admin/user) during the invitation process.
- **Member Search & Filter**: Admins can search members by name/email and filter by role (admin/user) in the **Member roles** tab
