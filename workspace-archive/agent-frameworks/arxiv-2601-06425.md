Abstract: Leakage power in multicore embedded systems now rivals dynamic power, so DVFS schedulers must respect deadlines and thermal limits, not just average makespan. Existing heuristics lack per-core, temperature-aware control and overlook the irregular execution of OpenMP DAGs. We propose HiDVFS, a general, extensible hierarchical multi-agent DVFS scheduler: a profiler agent selects cores and frequencies, a thermal agent groups cores by temper
