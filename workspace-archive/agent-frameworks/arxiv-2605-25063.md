Abstract: Scan-order design in laser directed energy deposition (LDED) is a delayed, path-dependent thermo-mechanical decision problem, because sequence quality becomes observable only after the complete deposition and cooling cycle. This work formulates LDED scan-order optimisation as a finite-horizon, permutation-constrained reinforcement-learning problem and develops a bilevel finite-element-teacher-labelled AI workflow.
