## Summary

arXiv:2606.13602v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We introduce EpiBench, a verifiable benchmark for short-horizon epigenomics analysis. EpiBench evaluates whether agents can make well-defined analysis decisions from realistic workflow states and return deterministically gradable answers. The benchmark includes 106 evaluations across CUT\&amp;Tag/CUT\&amp;RUN, ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq, and DNA methylation workflows.
