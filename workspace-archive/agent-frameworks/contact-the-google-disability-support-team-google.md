## Summary

- **Support Scope**: Google Disability Support assists users with accessibility features, product functionality, and assistive technology but does not handle Google Account recovery.
- **Availability**: Support is available Monday–Friday via email (4:00 AM–7:00 PM PT / 12:00 PM–3:00 AM GMT) in 9 languages; ASL support is limited to 8:00 AM–5:00 PM PT.
- **Unavailable Channels**: Phone, chat, and Be My Eyes support are currently inactive; refer to the [hours and languages list](https://support.go
