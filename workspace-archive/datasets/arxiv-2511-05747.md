## Summary

arXiv:2511.05747v4 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Long Chain-of-Thought (CoT) traces can improve reasoning accuracy, but repeatedly generating them is costly for smaller or latency-constrained language models. This paper studies a practical alternative: produce a rich rationale once with a capable \emph{thinking} model, compress it, and reuse the compressed trace as context for a cheaper \emph{answering} model.
