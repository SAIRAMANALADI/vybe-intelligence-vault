## Summary

arXiv:2607.07778v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Bubeck, Li and Nagaraj conjectured that, for generic data, any two-layer neural network with $m$ neurons that fits $n$ noisy labels must have Lipschitz constant at least of order $\sqrt{n/m}$, with no restriction on the size of the weights. Bubeck and Sellke proved a universal version of this law for Lipschitz-parameterized classes, but under a polynomial bound on the parameters; at depth three that boundedness hypothesis is genuinely necessary.
