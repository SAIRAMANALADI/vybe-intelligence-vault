Abstract: Handwritten mathematical expression recognition (HMER) requires reasoning over diverse symbols and structures, yet autoregressive models struggle with exposure bias and syntax inconsistency. We present GryphOne, a discrete diffusion framework which reformulates HMER as iterative symbolic refinement instead of sequential generation.
