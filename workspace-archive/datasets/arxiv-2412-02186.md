## Summary

- **VideoICL** introduces a **similarity-based relevant example selection strategy** to optimize in-context learning (ICL) for out-of-distribution (OOD) video understanding by ranking demonstration examples based on relevance to the input video.

- **Confidence-based iterative inference** refines responses by re-selecting examples and re-running inference if the initial output confidence is low, iteratively improving accuracy without fine-tuning.

- **Extends effective context length** for video
