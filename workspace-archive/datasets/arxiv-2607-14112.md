Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are evaluated as though perfect reliability is achievable for any task given sufficient scale. We show this assumption is information-theoretically unjustified. Every generative task has a reliability ceiling that no model can exceed, determined by how much output uncertainty is resolvable from observable context.
