## Summary

arXiv:2607.03825v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Audio-Visual Question Answering (AVQA) extends classical VQA by requiring joint reasoning over video and synchronized audio. However, many AVQA systems rely on deeply stacked layers of self- and cross attention across text, video, and audio. Such sequential stacking may incur loss of information such as subtle inter-modal cues over the layers, causing errors to accumulate across sequential attention layers during the fusion.
