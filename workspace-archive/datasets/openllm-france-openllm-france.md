## Summary

*   Luciole LLMs are a family of open-source, multilingual text generation models supporting French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, and Arabic.
*   The collection includes base models with parameter counts of 1B, 8B, and 23B, catering to diverse computational requirements.
*   A 3.2B token Luciole-Training-Dataset is publicly available, facilitating research and reproducibility.
