## Summary

arXiv:2607.03347v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We consider the Multiscale Single-Index Model (MSIM), first introduced in \cite{oymak2021learning}, as a stylized model for hierarchical learning with \emph{scale separation}. Each layer extracts a shared single-index feature at one physical scale and passes it to the next, thus defining a tractable setting in which to study how deep architectures learn multiscale representations.
