Abstract: Classical proportional--integral--derivative (PID) control remains widely used in industrial control systems, while model predictive control (MPC) is actively studied to achieve higher performance for systems with nonlinear dynamics. Model predictive path integral (MPPI) control is a sampling-based MPC method that optimizes control inputs without gradient calculations and can handle non-differentiable models and objective functions.
