## Summary

arXiv:2606.28486v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: The emergence of low-dimensional structures in the spectra of neural network weight matrices is a common empirical feature of trained models, but the dynamical origin of this phenomenon during learning remains an open problem. We formulate neural network training as the stochastic evolution of an initially random matrix ensemble, driven by stochastic gradient descent (SGD) updates that reshape the spectral bulk while amplifying signal strength.
