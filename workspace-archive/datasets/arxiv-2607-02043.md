Abstract: Disaggregated LLM serving runs prefill and decode on separate GPU pools to keep the two phases from interfering. In practice, this creates a new asymmetry: under bursty, heavy-tailed workloads prefill nodes saturate while decode nodes have compute underutilized, and on a production-style A100 cluster with 2 prefill and 2 decode nodes (2P2D), we find that prefill execution accounts for only 2-23% of P95 Time-to-First-Token (TTFT).
