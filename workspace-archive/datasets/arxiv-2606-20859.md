Abstract: A fundamental assumption in statistics and machine learning is that ``the future looks like the past,'' formalized as exchangeability: the joint data distribution is order-invariant. In practice, this assumption is often violated due to distribution shifts over time. Early detection of exchangeability violations is crucial to prevent performance degradation and enable timely interventions like model retraining. Conformal test martingales
