Abstract: We present the Variational Phasor Circuit (VPC), a deterministic classical learning architecture on the continuous $S^1$ unit-circle manifold. Inspired by variational quantum circuits, VPC replaces dense weight matrices with trainable phase shifts, local unitary mixing, and structured interference in the ambient complex space, giving a unified method for binary and multi-class classification of spatially distributed signals.
