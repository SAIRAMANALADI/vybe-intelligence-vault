## Summary

- **Model Architecture**: CLIP-ViT-B-32-multilingual-v1 combines a Vision Transformer (ViT-B/32) backbone with a text encoder, trained contrastively on multilingual image-text pairs to align visual and textual representations across languages.

- **Parameter Scale**: The model has ~0.1B parameters, balancing computational efficiency with performance for multilingual sentence similarity tasks.
