## Summary

arXiv:2607.02137v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We study timestep allocation for score-based diffusion sampling, where a learned reverse-time dynamics is discretized on a finite grid. Uniform and hand-crafted schedules are standard choices, but they rely on fixed prescriptions and can therefore be suboptimal. To address this limitation, we propose Adaptive Reparameterized Time (ART), a continuous-time control formulation that learns a time change by treating the speed of the sampling
