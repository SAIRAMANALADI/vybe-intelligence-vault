Abstract: In this study we present a formal definition of large discrete sets having, informally, three properties: their elements are easily recognized, easily generated, and the latter tasks are easily learned from examples. The formalism is specialized to sets of binary strings and a definition of "machine-learnability" based on the existence of a bounded-complexity Boolean autoencoder that fixes the elements of the set.
