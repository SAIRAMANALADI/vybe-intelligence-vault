## Summary

- **Charge density waves (CDWs) observed in nickelate superconductors**: Researchers discovered CDWs—a quantum matter phase—emerging *without* doping in a lanthanum-based nickelate, unlike other unconventional superconductors where doping is typically required.

- **Self-doping mechanism in nickelates**: The material exhibits intrinsic electron doping, distinguishing it from cuprates and enabling correlated electron behavior (e.g., superconductivity and CDWs) under undoped conditions.
