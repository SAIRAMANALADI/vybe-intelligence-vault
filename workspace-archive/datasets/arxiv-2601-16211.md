## Summary

arXiv:2601.16211v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Zero-Shot Compositional Action Recognition (ZS-CAR) requires recognizing novel verb-object combinations composed of previously observed primitives. In this work, we tackle a key failure mode: models predict verbs via object-driven shortcuts (i.e., relying on the labeled object class) rather than temporal evidence. We argue that sparse compositional supervision and verb-object learning asymmetry can promote object-driven shortcut learning
