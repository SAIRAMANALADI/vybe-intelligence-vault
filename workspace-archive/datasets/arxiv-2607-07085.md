Abstract: The Adaptive Data Analysis (ADA) problem formalizes the challenge of preventing false discovery and overfitting when a dataset is repeatedly reused. Formally, our input is a dataset containing $n$ i.i.d. samples from an unknown distribution $\mathcal{P}$ over a domain $\mathcal{X}$, and our goal is to answer a sequence of $k$ adaptively chosen statistical queries with respect to $\mathcal{P}$.
