## Summary

arXiv:2606.31087v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Few-shot selection typically assumes that reranking retrieved examples always improves performance. We challenge this view by identifying that the expensive reranking step can in fact degrade performance. Instead, we propose \emph{Training-Free Gated Reranking}, which decides whether to rerank the few-shot examples based on the model's uncertainty.
