## Summary

arXiv:2603.11756v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Deep generative models for anomaly detection in multivariate time-series are typically trained by maximizing observed data likelihood. However, likelihood in observation space measures marginal density rather than conformity to structured temporal dynamics, and therefore can assign high probability to anomalous or out-of-distribution samples. We address this structural limitation by relocating the notion of anomaly to a prescribed latent space
