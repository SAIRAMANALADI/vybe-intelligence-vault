## Summary

arXiv:2511.05879v5 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Hydrogen crossover is a critical safety and efficiency constraint in high-pressure polymer electrolyte membrane water electrolysis (PEMWE), but accurate prediction remains difficult because data are limited, transport physics are strongly coupled, and industrial operation requires reliable extrapolation beyond observed conditions. This study develops a hard-constraint physics-residual network (PR-Net) for hydrogen crossover prediction in PEMWE
