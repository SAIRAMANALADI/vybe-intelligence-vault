## Summary

arXiv:2607.09786v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Length-penalized reinforcement learning can shorten chain-of-thought reasoning while hiding an influence that drives the model's answer. In our experiments, training with length penalties does not stop misleading hints from steering models, even though the models' chains of thought mention the hint much less often. A token-accuracy evaluation would count these runs as successful because they use fewer reasoning tokens with little accuracy loss;
