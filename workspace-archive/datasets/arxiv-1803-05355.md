## Summary

- **Dataset**: Introduced FEVER, a large-scale dataset with 185,445 claims derived from Wikipedia sentences, classified as *Supported*, *Refuted*, or *NotEnoughInfo* with inter-annotator agreement (Fleiss $\kappa$) of 0.6841.

- **Challenge**: Baseline pipeline achieves 31.87% accuracy when using correct evidence and 50.91% without evidence, highlighting the difficulty of claim verification against textual sources.
