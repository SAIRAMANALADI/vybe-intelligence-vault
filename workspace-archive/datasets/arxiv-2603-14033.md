## Summary

arXiv:2603.14033v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Audio anti-spoofing systems are typically trained to assign one authenticity label to an entire speech utterance. This formulation becomes under-specified for transformations where the underlying speaker identity and linguistic content remain unchanged. We study this problem using benign, authenticity-preserving speech transformations, including voice quality conversion and speech restoration, applied to both bona fide and spoofed speech
