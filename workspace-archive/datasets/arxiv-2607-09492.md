## Summary

arXiv:2607.09492v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Reinforcement learning (RL) is increasingly used to align multimodal large language models (MLLMs), but higher rewards do not always imply better task performance. This risk is amplified when visual evidence is evaluated by text-only or weakly grounded rewards. We study reward hacking in MLLM RL across safety VQA, chart VQA, and stress-test settings, varying reward design, data ambiguity, model scale (2B-32B), and RL algorithm (GRPO, RLOO, DAPO).
