## Summary

arXiv:2606.26472v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: As reasoning models emit chains of thought tens of thousands of tokens long, KV cache increasingly becomes a deployment bottleneck. Existing cache eviction methods rank tokens by attention weight, which is a noisy importance proxy in long reasoning traces, and prohibits the use of fused kernels in production inference by forcing the model to materialize the attention matrix. In this work, we instead score tokens with a metric we term the epiphany
