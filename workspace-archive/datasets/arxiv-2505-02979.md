Abstract: We propose a novel inverse-modelling approach that estimates the parameters of a simple land-surface model (LSM) by assimilating data into a differentiable, physics-based forward model formulated using convolutional operations. The governing equations are expressed within the Neural Physics framework, allowing direct gradient-based optimisation of time-dependent parameters without the need to derive and maintain adjoint formulations.
