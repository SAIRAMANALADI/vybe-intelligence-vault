Abstract: Existing hypotheses represent a concept in an LLM as a single point, a linear direction, or a Gaussian cluster, yet it remains unclear how and why such structures emerge. Here, we show that concept geometry can be precisely characterized via Laguerre Geometry, in which a concept is defined as a region--a Laguerre-Voronoi cell or a union of cells--allowing us to strictly define, measure, and separate concepts.
