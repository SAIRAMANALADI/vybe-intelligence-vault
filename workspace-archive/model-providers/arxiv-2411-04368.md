## Summary

- Introduces **SimpleQA**, an adversarially constructed benchmark for evaluating short-form factuality in LLMs, where questions are designed to have a single indisputable answer to enable unambiguous correctness grading.
- Benchmark prioritizes **challenge** (adversarial against GPT-4 responses) and **gradability** (binary correctness: correct/incorrect/not attempted) to assess models' ability to "know what they know."
