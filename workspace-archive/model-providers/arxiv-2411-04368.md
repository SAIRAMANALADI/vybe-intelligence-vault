## Summary

- **SimpleQA Benchmark**: Introduces a benchmark designed to evaluate short-form factuality in LLMs, featuring adversarially collected questions with single indisputable answers to test models' ability to answer fact-seeking queries while avoiding uncertain responses.

- **Evaluation Metrics**: Responses are graded as correct, incorrect, or not attempted, enabling assessment of models' "know-what-they-know" behavior by balancing accuracy with refusal to answer uncertain questions.
