# Landmark German ruling declares Google's AI Overviews are Google's own words and makes it liable for false answers

## Summary

- **German court ruling (Munich Regional Court) establishes Google's direct liability for false claims in AI-generated search overviews**, classifying them as Google's own content rather than third-party search results, thus rejecting traditional search engine liability exemptions.
- **AI overviews generate independent, substantive statements** by synthesizing and paraphrasing third-party sources, creating new claims not present in original content, which the court deemed Google's responsibility
