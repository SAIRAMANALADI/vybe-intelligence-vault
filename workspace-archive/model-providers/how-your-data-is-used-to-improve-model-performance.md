## Summary

- **Opt-in/out controls**: Users can opt out of model training via the [privacy portal](https://privacy.openai.com/policies?modal=take-control) or Temporary Chat; feedback submissions (e.g., thumbs up/down) may still be used for training unless explicitly disabled.

- **Business defaults**: By default, enterprise/business services (ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, API) do not train on inputs/outputs; opt-in is required for data sharing (e.g., feedback in Playground).

- **Data anonymization**: Open
