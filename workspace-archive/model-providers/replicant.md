## Summary

- **Replicant** is a fully free Android distribution emphasizing **freedom, privacy, and security** by removing proprietary components and replacing them with free software alternatives.
- Supports multiple devices, including **maintained** (e.g., Galaxy S3 GT-I9300, Galaxy Note 2 GT-N7100) and **unmaintained** (e.g., Nexus S GT-I902x, Galaxy S GT-I9000) models.
- **Stores** like Tehnoetic sell devices pre-installed with Replicant, with availability and pricing subject to individual store polici
