## Summary

- **Joint Optimization Framework**: Proposes *BetterTogether*, an alternating optimization strategy combining weight fine-tuning and prompt optimization for modular NLP pipelines (e.g., RAG), achieving up to **60% and 6% average performance gains** over weight-only or prompt-only optimization across tasks (multi-hop QA, math reasoning, classification) using models like Mistral-7B, Llama-2-7B, and Llama-3-8B.
