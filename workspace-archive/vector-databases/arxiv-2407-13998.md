## Summary

- **Introduces LFRQA dataset**: A 26K-query long-form QA dataset with human-written answers synthesizing extractive snippets from 7 diverse domains, addressing cross-domain generalization limitations in prior RAG-QA benchmarks.

- **Proposes RAG-QA Arena**: An LLM-based evaluation framework comparing model-generated answers to LFRQA’s gold answers, demonstrating high correlation with human judgments and revealing only 41.3% preference for top LLM outputs.
