## Summary

- Discourse Meta is the official community forum for Discourse software, utilizing a Ruby on Rails-based platform with PostgreSQL for data storage and Redis for caching.
- Key navigation endpoints include `/latest`, `/top`, `/categories`, `/hot`, and `/votes`, supporting RESTful content retrieval and filtering.
- Static pages such as `/guidelines`, `/tos`, and `/privacy` enforce community policies and legal compliance, while JavaScript is required for full functionality.
