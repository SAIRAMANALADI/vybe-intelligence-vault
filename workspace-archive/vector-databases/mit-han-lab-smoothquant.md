## Summary

- **SmoothQuant**: A post-training quantization (PTQ) method for large language models (LLMs) that smooths activation outliers to enable 8-bit weight and activation quantization with minimal accuracy loss (e.g., <1% drop on OPT-175B, BLOOM-176B).

- **Key Technique**: Introduces a mathematically equivalent transformation to migrate quantization difficulty from activations to weights by scaling channels with a smooth factor, leveraging the robustness of weights to quantization noise.
