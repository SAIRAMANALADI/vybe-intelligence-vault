Abstract: Accurate and efficient classification of thoracic diseases in chest X-ray (CXR) images is crucial for timely diagnosis and treatment. However, the presence of multiple pathologies with overlapping visual characteristics poses significant challenges for automated classification systems. In this study, we propose two novel hierarchical multi-label classification techniques, namely the loss-based and logit-based methods, to address these challenges
