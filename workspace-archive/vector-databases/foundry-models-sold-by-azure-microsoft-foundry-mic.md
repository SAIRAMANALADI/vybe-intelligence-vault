## Summary

- **Model Categories & Hosting**: Foundry Models sold by Azure include Azure OpenAI models and selected third-party models, hosted and operated by Azure under the Foundry Models service, billed via Azure subscription with Microsoft SLAs and support.

- **Key Model Series & Capabilities**:
  - **GPT-5.6 Series** (`gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-5.6-terra`, `gpt-5.6-luna`): 1M+ context window, 128K max output tokens, supports reasoning, structured outputs, computer use, and multimodal inputs (text/images).
