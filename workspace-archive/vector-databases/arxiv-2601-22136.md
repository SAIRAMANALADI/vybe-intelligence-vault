## Summary

arXiv:2601.22136v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Agent safety benchmarks measure whether a monitor detects harm, not when. Yet timing is the difference between intervention and autopsy. We introduce StepShield, the first benchmark that treats detection timeliness as a first-class metric. On 9,429 incident-grounded code-agent trajectories, we define the Early Intervention Rate (EIR): the fraction of detected rogue trajectories where the alert fires within a k-step window after the divergence
