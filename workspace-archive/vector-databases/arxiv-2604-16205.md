## Summary

arXiv:2604.16205v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Computational X-ray absorption near-edge structure (XANES) is widely used to interpret local coordination environments, oxidation states, and electronic structure in chemically complex systems. In practice, routine computational XANES at scale is often constrained by workflow complexity rather than by the simulation method. We present ChemGraph-XANES, a large-language-model (LLM)-based agentic framework for XANES simulation and analysis
