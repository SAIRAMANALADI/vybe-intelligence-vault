## Summary

- **Cookie Types & Storage**: Chrome distinguishes between first-party cookies (created by visited sites) and third-party cookies (created by embedded content from other domains), with both stored locally for session persistence, personalization, and authentication.

- **Cookie Management Workflow**: Users can delete all cookies via `Settings > Privacy and security > Delete browsing data` or selectively via `Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > See all site data and permission
