Abstract: Semantic search and recommendation of similar documents, such as news and reports about unusual environmental events (e.g., a dead whale washed ashore in Alaska) that contain spatial and temporal information, is a critical task in Geographic Information Retrieval (GIR). This work presents a novel framework that leverages AI foundation models, including Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), to enable effective similarity
