## Summary

arXiv:2603.08457v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Robust single-vessel tracking from fixed coastal platforms is hindered by modality-specific degradations: cameras suffer from illumination and visual clutter, while LiDAR performance drops with range and intermittent returns. We present a particle-filter tracker that supports sequential measurement-level camera-LiDAR fusion and an information-gain (entropy-reduction) adaptive sensing policy that selects the most informative sensing modal
