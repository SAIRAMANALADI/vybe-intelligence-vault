## Summary

- **Arbitration Clause**: Disputes related to device/subscription purchases are resolved via binding arbitration on an individual, non-class basis, as outlined in Section 23 of the Terms.
- **Subscription Billing**: Automatic recurring charges (monthly/annual) are applied to the linked payment method for subscriptions, with prorated billing for add-ons and no obligation to notify users of free trial expiration.
- **Order & Delivery Policies**: Google reserves the right to cancel orders for fraud
