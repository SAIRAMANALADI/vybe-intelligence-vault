## Summary

arXiv:2606.27216v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Muon-type optimizers construct update directions for dense neural-network weights by applying a finite Newton-Schulz map to momentum-gradient matrices. For an $H \times W$ matrix, with $r=\min\{H,W\}$ and $s=\max\{H,W\}$, $K$ steps of the full-matrix Newton-Schulz update require $O(r^2 s K)$ work and couple all rows and columns through repeated Gram matrix products. We introduce Hierarchical Muon (HiMuon), a tiled Newton-Schulz scheme for Muon-t
