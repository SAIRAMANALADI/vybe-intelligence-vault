Abstract: Modern language models generally represent text as sequences of discrete token embeddings, an assumption deeply rooted in current practice but rarely questioned. We challenge this representation, especially for Chinese, by replacing index-based token embeddings entirely with a single rasterized image of the character sequence, processed by a vision encoder composed of a shared ResNet and a shallow Vision Transformer.
