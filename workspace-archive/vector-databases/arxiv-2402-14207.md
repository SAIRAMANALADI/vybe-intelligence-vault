## Summary

- **STORM system**: Introduces a writing assistant leveraging large language models (LLMs) to generate Wikipedia-like articles from scratch, addressing pre-writing challenges via multi-perspective research and outline synthesis.

- **Three-stage pre-writing pipeline**: (1) **Diverse perspective discovery**, (2) **simulated cross-perspective Q&A** with topic experts grounded in trusted sources, and (3) **curated outline generation** to ensure breadth and depth.
