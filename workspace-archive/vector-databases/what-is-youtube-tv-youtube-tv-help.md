## Summary

- **Service Overview**: YouTube TV provides live TV streaming from 100+ broadcast, cable, and regional sports networks with unlimited DVR storage.
- **Multi-Device & Account Support**: Supports up to 6 user profiles under one subscription and allows simultaneous streaming on up to 3 devices.
- **Language & Content Add-Ons**: Offers Spanish-language programming via the Spanish Plan (standalone) or Spanish Plus add-on (with a YouTube TV plan).
