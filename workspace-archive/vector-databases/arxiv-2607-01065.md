## Summary

arXiv:2607.01065v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The deployment of Large Language Models (LLMs) with extended context windows is increasingly constrained by the linear growth of Key-Value (KV) cache memory. Vector Quantization (VQ), particularly Residual Quantization (RQ), is a promising approach for pushing KV cache storage toward the sub-1-bit regime by progressively encoding residuals with small codebooks. However, most VQ methods still rely on standard $\ell_2$ $K$-means as the core codebook
