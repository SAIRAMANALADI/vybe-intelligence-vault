Abstract: RAG systems retrieve documents optimized for answering one query at a time. Yet enterprise users arrive with sessions, that is, coherent episodes of related questions that span semantically distant parts of the knowledge base. We show that a single retrieval call over a standard knowledge base covers only 41% of a user's session-level information need.
