Abstract: Existing world model-based planners for visual navigation typically follow a verification-centric paradigm, decoupling goal intent from trajectory synthesis. This approach suffers from candidate dependence, heavy computational overhead, and inconsistencies between sampled actions and predicted visuals. To address these issues, we propose SWAM (Spatial-perceiving World Action Model), a task-centric joint observation-action generation framework. G
