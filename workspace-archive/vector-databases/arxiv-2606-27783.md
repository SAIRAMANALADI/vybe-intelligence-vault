Abstract: Continuous attractor neural networks (CANNs) are the canonical computational framework for how the brain encodes continuous variables such as spatial position, head direction, and movement direction, and explain the activity of hippocampal place cells, entorhinal grid cells, and head-direction cells. CANN research, however, is fragmented: most results rest on lab-specific implementations, general-purpose simulators lack CANN-specific abstraction
