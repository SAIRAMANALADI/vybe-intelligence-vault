## Summary

- **Apple Pay Global Availability**: Supports contactless payments across 90+ countries/regions (Africa, Asia-Pacific, Europe, Latin America, Middle East, North America) with participating banks and card issuers.
- **Apple Pay Ecosystem Integration**: Functions on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro; supports in-store, in-app, and web payments via Safari/third-party browsers with WebSocket support.
- **Apple Cash Limitations**: U.S.-only peer-to-peer payments via Green Dot Bank
