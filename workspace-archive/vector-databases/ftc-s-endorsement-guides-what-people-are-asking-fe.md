## Summary

- **Network Effects Amplify Harm**: Loyalty discounts in markets with network effects (e.g., Surescripts' e-prescribing platform) can entrench dominant firms by making it prohibitively costly for rivals to attract users, even if the discounts are below cost.

- **FTC v. Surescripts Case**: The FTC alleged that Surescripts' retroactive loyalty rebates (tying e-prescribing and routing services) excluded competitors like NewCrop, harming innovation and consumer choice in a two-sided market.
