## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Proposes a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" in unified embedding spaces where cross-modal retrieval performance degrades.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers (e.g., document vs. paragraph vs. sentence) to enable fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.
