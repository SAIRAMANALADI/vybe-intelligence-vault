## Summary

- **Satellite Connectivity**: iPhone 14+ models support emergency SOS, roadside assistance, messaging, and location sharing via Globalstar’s satellite network when cellular/Wi-Fi is unavailable; free for 2 years, with regional restrictions (e.g., unavailable in China, Russia).
- **Technical Constraints**: Satellite connections require clear sky visibility; ideal conditions yield ~30s message transmission, while foliage/obstructions can block or delay signals; iOS 18+ includes a Connection Assist
