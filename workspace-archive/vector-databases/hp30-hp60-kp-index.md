## Summary

- **Temporal Resolution & Index Variants**: Hp30 (30-minute resolution) and Hp60 (1-hour resolution) are open-ended geomagnetic indices developed in the H2020 SWAMI project, offering finer temporal granularity than the Kp-index (max value 9).

- **Algorithm & Data Extension**: Hpo V3.0 employs an improved high-value algorithm and extends historical time series back to 1985, as detailed in Yamazaki et al. (2022).
