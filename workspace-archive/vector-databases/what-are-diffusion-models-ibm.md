## Summary

- **Diffusion models** are generative models that iteratively denoise data by learning a reverse diffusion process, typically parameterized by a neural network (e.g., U-Net), to approximate the gradient of the data distribution's log-likelihood.
- **Training involves two key phases**: a forward process that gradually adds Gaussian noise to data and a reverse process that learns to reconstruct the original data by predicting and removing this noise.
- **Key variants** include Denoising Diffusion
