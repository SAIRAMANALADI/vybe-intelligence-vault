## Summary

- **25-Day Reading Challenge**: Wikipedia app initiative requiring daily article reads for 25 consecutive days (until 18 June) to celebrate its 25th anniversary, with progress tracked via the "Baby Globe" mascot widget.

- **Wikipedia App Features**: Offers localized, ad-free content with offline reading, Places-based geolocation, reading lists, activity tracking, trivia games ("Which Came First?"), and "Year in Review" summaries; supports 300+ languages and prioritizes privacy.
