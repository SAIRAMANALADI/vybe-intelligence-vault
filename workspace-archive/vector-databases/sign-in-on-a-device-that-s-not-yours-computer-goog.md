## Summary

- **Guest Mode in Chrome**: Enables temporary sign-in on shared/public devices; browsing history, cookies, and site data are deleted upon closing the Guest window, while activity is still logged to the user's Google Account via activity controls.
- **Private Browsing in Other Browsers**: Functionality varies by browser (e.g., Safari, Firefox, Edge); requires opening a private/incognito window, signing in, and manually signing out or closing all private windows to prevent account access.
