## Summary

arXiv:2602.18364v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Maximum likelihood prediction (MLP) is a core task at the heart of modern large language models. Here, we study a quantum version of this task for a simplified data model consisting of independent and identically distributed samples, as a first step. The quantum maximum likelihood predictor (QMLP) is obtained by embedding of empirical probability distributions into quantum states and performing a minimization of quantum relative entropy
