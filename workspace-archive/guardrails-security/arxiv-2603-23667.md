Abstract: We introduce Echoes, a new dataset for music deepfake detection designed for training and benchmarking detectors under realistic and provider-diverse conditions. Echoes comprises 4,468 tracks (131 hours of audio) spanning multiple genres (pop, rock, electronic), and includes content generated by ten popular AI music generation systems.
