## Summary

arXiv:2607.05364v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Modern autoregressive ASR systems can emit timestamps as decoded tokens, enabling timestamped transcription without frame-level aligners or inference-time post-processing. We show that these generated timestamps can drift across long non-speech spans: the transcript may remain plausible, but the decoded time axis drifts away from the audio. We study this non-speech-induced timestamp drift with self-built gap and long-gap benchmarks across 15 eva
