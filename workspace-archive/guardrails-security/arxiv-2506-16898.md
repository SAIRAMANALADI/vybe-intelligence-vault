## Summary

arXiv:2506.16898v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Diffusion-based text-to-image models are increasingly used for urban analysis and scenario generation, but their geographic knowledge and representational biases remain poorly understood. We evaluate FLUX 1-schnell and Stable Diffusion 3.5-Large in the United States by generating 150 street-view images for each state, each state capital, and a generic "USA" prompt. Images are embedded with DINO-v2 ViT-S/14 and compared with Fr\'echet Inception
