## Summary

arXiv:2606.30423v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: With the increasing adoption of Machine Learning, protecting model ownership has become an essential challenge. We initiate a formal study of Proof of Ownership for machine learning models: under what conditions can one prove that a stolen model originated from a particular creator? We model proofs of ownership as a game among three parties: a model owner, a thief, and a judge. The owner transforms the original model into a slightly perturbed mode
