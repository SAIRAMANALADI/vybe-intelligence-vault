## Summary

arXiv:2601.16172v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: RL-trained Lean theorem provers mode-collapse at inference time: on miniF2F-test with DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5-RL, doubling the i.i.d.\ sampling budget from $k{=}32$ to $k{=}64$ produces zero additional solved theorems (42/244 in both cases). A fixed schedule of 15 tactic skeletons breaks this plateau and recovers a $+45%$ relative improvement at $k{=}16$ (mean $\Delta = +12.3 \pm 4.2$ theorems across $n{=}3$ seeds, sign preserved in every seed).
