## Summary

arXiv:2511.05350v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We argue that training autoencoders to reconstruct inputs from noised versions of their encodings, when combined with perceptually motivated losses, yields encodings that are structured according to a perceptual hierarchy. We demonstrate the emergence of this hierarchy by showing that, after training an audio autoencoder in this manner, perceptually salient information is captured in coarser representation structures than with convention
