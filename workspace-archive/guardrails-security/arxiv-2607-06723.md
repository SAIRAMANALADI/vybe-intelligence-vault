Abstract: Most gradient-based optimization methods move parameters through a fixed background geometry, even when their internal states implicitly define changing notions of length, curvature, and preconditioning. We introduce optimization geometrodynamics, a benchmark language in which optimization is a coupled evolution of a parameter trajectory, a transported distribution of particles, and a controlled time-varying Riemannian metric.
