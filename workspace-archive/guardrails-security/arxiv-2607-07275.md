## Summary

arXiv:2607.07275v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Bubbly flows exhibit complex multiscale dynamics, with deformable bubbles interacting through the surrounding liquid and giving rise to strongly coupled kinematic and morphological behavior. We present BubbleSH, a bubbly flows dataset consisting of transient, three-dimensional bubble-swarm dynamics obtained from high-fidelity direct numerical simulations of bubbles rising in a periodic domain. The dataset provides time-resolved bubble trajectories
