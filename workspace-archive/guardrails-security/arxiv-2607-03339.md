Abstract: Learning dissipative dynamics from discrete observations is essential for reliable long-horizon prediction and physically meaningful parameter identification. For linearly damped Hamiltonian systems, the exact flow is generally not symplectic but conformally symplectic, contracting the canonical symplectic form by a scalar factor that reflects the net dissipation.
