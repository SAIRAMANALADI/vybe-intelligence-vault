## Summary

arXiv:2509.03373v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: Dimensionality reduction methods such as t-SNE and UMAP are popular methods for visualizing data with a potential (latent) clustered structure. They are known to group data points at the same time as they embed them, resulting in visualizations with well-separated clusters that preserve local information well. However, t-SNE and UMAP also tend to distort the global geometry of the underlying data. We propose a more transparent modular approach
