Abstract: The training paradigm of large language models has shifted from traditional one-pass training to multi-epoch training, as reasonable reuse of limited high-quality data can improve both model performance and sample efficiency. Meanwhile, excessive repetition introduces the risk of overfitting and diminishing returns. Determining when and how to reuse data effectively thus emerges as a natural but under-explored question.
