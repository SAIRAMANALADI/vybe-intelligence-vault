## Summary

arXiv:2606.26816v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Heart Rate Variability (HRV) analysis is a key indicator of cardiac physiological state and aids in disease diagnosis. However, research on HRV parameters in healthy individuals remains limited, and no gold standard exists. This study evaluates HRV indices in 40 healthy adults (20 men, 20 women, aged 30-50) to improve HRV's clinical utility. Using computational methods for signal processing and data analysis, time, frequency, and nonlinear indices
