## Summary

arXiv:2607.07179v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Document Visual Question Answering (DocVQA) presents a complex multimodal challenge, requiring models to exploit visual, textual, and layout information from documents. Although Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown remarkable performance in text-vision tasks, their robustness and transferability to different document domains remains underexplored. In this study, we present a comprehensive evaluation of 8 open-source pretrained VLMs on DocVQA
