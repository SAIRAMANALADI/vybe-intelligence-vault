## Summary

arXiv:2604.23743v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Variational quantum circuits train poorly on chaotic forecasting, usually blamed on barren plateaus (exponentially vanishing gradients). Using an exactly simulable four-qubit variational quantum physics-informed circuit fit to Lorenz-63, we show the barren-plateau explanation fails: the failure is an architectural capacity ceiling fixed by the circuit time-encoding, not its trainable depth. Four measurements support this. (i) A McClean-c
