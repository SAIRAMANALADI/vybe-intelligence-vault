Abstract: Observable Matrix Dynamics (OMD) is a diagnostic framework that probes the dynamics of high-dimensional internal representations of inputs by a neural network via a fixed-size $N \times N$ distance matrix $M(t)$ on a held set of $N$ inputs. OMD uses methods of random matrix theory and particle dynamics to explore spectral reorganisations that are missed by scalar loss functions, but are informative of the training process.
