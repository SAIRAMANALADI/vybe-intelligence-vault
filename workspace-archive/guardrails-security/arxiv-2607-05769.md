## Summary

arXiv:2607.05769v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We propose a novel pipeline, Legato 2, for extracting symbolic notation and semantic knowledge from images of sheet music. Legato 2 features the first large-scale neural model for optical music recognition (OMR) to operate sequentially on a system-by-system basis, following the horizontal lines of notation as they are read on the page, rather than treating the page as an undifferentiated image, enabling better scaling to arbitrarily long inputs.
