Abstract: We present TVF (Time-Varying Filtering), an interpretable, low-latency speech enhancement model for real-time, on-device assistive hearing. A lightweight neural controller predicts, in real time, the coefficients of a differentiable cascade of 35 second-order IIR filters (biquads), so the model tracks non-stationary noise while keeping a fully interpretable processing chain: every spectral modification is an explicit, adjustable equalize
