## Summary

arXiv:2606.30356v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We propose Online Latent prediction with Invariant Views and rEconstruction (OLIVE), a self-supervised speech representation learning framework that jointly optimizes analysis and synthesis objectives. OLIVE combines view-augmented masked latent prediction with waveform reconstruction under a unified objective. Reconstruction constrains early encoder features to retain signal-level information, while masked latent prediction shapes later context
