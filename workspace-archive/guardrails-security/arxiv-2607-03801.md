## Summary

arXiv:2607.03801v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We define Tiny Language Models (TLMs) as models below roughly 3B parameters that fit on mainstream consumer devices. We study how to adapt them for and use them on verifiable multiple-choice tasks. We compare three LoRA-based fine-tuning paradigms (label generation, gold only, and our discriminative classification head) on a unified setup across several Qwen3 models from 0.6B to 8B and five benchmarks: HellaSwag, WinoGrande, PIQA, SciQ and ARC-C.
