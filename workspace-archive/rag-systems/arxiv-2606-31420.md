## Summary

arXiv:2606.31420v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) enables models trained on a source domain to adapt online to unlabeled test data under distribution shifts. While recent TTA methods have moved beyond static settings and begun to consider continual domain shifts, they primarily address distribution drift and fail to account for class imbalance in dynamic scenarios. In real-world test-time streams, class imbalance and continual domain shifts often occur at the same time
