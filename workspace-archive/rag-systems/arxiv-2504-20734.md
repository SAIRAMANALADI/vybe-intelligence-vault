## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where retrieval favors same-modality items, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels to tailor retrieval precision based on query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Achieves superior performance across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities
