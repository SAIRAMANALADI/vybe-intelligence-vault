## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism**: UniversalRAG dynamically selects modality-specific corpora for retrieval to mitigate the *modality gap*, where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality retrieval, improving cross-modal knowledge integration.

- **Granularity-level organization**: Each modality is structured into multiple granularity tiers, enabling query-specific retrieval depth tailored to complexity and scope, enhancing precision across diverse query types.
