Abstract: For robots manipulating open-world objects, tactile representations must generalize to unseen materials. We introduce RCT (Robotic Contact Tactile), a robot-collected touch-vision-language dataset with 29,279 tactile frames from full robot presses on 122 industrial reference materials in 7 categories, recorded with three DIGIT sensors at multiple contact positions.
