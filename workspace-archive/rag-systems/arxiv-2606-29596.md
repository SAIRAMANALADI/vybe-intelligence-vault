## Summary

arXiv:2606.29596v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Characterizing the scenario underlying an epidemic from its disease cascade is an important task in simulation analytics. We propose boundary degree, the count of an infected node's contacts in the underlying contact network that were not infected, as a per-node cascade feature for this task. Through systematic ablation on realistic social contact networks of Tennessee and Virginia, we show that boundary degree alone improves scenario id
