Abstract: We develop data-driven algorithms for maintaining $N$ independent identical machines under a \textit{block replacement policy}, in which each machine is replaced upon failure and all machines are jointly replaced at regular intervals of length $k$. The goal is to learn the cost-minimizing interval $k^*$ from operational data when the lifetime distribution is unknown.
