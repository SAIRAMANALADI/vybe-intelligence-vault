Abstract: Looped Transformers, which repeatedly apply a shared transformer block, are an architecturally natural fit for variable-length algorithmic tasks. Although they can exhibit strong length generalization beyond the length of training sequences, this behavior is brittle, yielding high out-of-distribution (OOD) variance, even across well-performing in-distribution solutions.
