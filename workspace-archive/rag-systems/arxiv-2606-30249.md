## Summary

arXiv:2606.30249v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Detecting communities in heterophilic graphs -- where connected nodes often belong to different classes -- is hard for unsupervised methods: classical modularity and spectral methods are feature agnostic, while deep graph-clustering methods rely on contrastive or generative machinery that is opaque. We propose Curvature-Guided Sheaf Diffusion (CGSD), a fully unsupervised community-detection algorithm that uses the discrete Forman--Ricci curvature
