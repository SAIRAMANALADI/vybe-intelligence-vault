## Summary

arXiv:2603.18482v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Standard decoding strategies for text generation, including top-$k$, nucleus sampling, and contrastive search, select tokens based on likelihood, restricting outputs to high-probability regions. In contrast, human language production prioritizes communicative appropriateness, allowing the use of contextually suitable but statistically rare tokens. This mismatch induces a \emph{truncation blind spot}, whereby such tokens remain accessible
