Abstract: Knowledge distillation is usually framed as a choice of what to match in the teacher - its logits, hidden features, or sample relations - which presupposes that the teacher's representation has absolute coordinates to match. It does not: a pretrained representation is identifiable only up to an orthogonal-and-isotropic-scaling equivalence class, so a student should learn the teacher's equivalence class, not its features.
