Abstract: Accurate and efficient classification of thoracic diseases in chest X-ray (CXR) images is crucial for timely diagnosis and treatment. In this study, we propose two novel hierarchical multi-label classification techniques, namely the loss-based and logit-based methods, to address these challenges
