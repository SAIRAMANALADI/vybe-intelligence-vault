## Summary

arXiv:2607.10285v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We study how unsupervised autoencoders trained on microscopic spin configurations from the Ising model learn macroscopic, theory-relevant variables underlying the data-generating process. Without embedding domain knowledge, we mimic a typical discovery setting: We quantify learning across multiple spatial (coarse-graining) scales and reveal two distinct dynamical regimes controlled by main hyperparameters (model depth, width, and learning rate) --
