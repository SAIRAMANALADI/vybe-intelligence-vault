## Summary

- Google Accounts support **2-Step Verification (2FA)** and **Advanced Protection Program** for enhanced security, including hardware keys and strict access controls.
- **Family Link** enables parental management of child accounts, including supervision, content restrictions, and age-based access controls.
- **Account recovery** involves identity verification via recovery emails/phones, security questions, or backup codes, with delays possible for fraud detection.
