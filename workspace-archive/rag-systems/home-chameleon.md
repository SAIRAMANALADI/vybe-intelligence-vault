## Summary

- **Experimental Platform**: Chameleon is an NSF-funded, open-source testbed designed for edge-to-cloud research, enabling reproducible experiments in distributed computing, networking, and storage.
- **Multi-Site Infrastructure**: Comprises heterogeneous hardware across six sites (CHI@TACC, CHI@UC, CHI@NU, CHI@NCAR, KVM@TACC, CHI@NRP) with support for bare-metal provisioning, KVM virtualization, and edge computing via CHI@Edge.
- **Research & Collaboration Tools**: Provides appliances, Jupyter
