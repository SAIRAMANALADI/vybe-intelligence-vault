## Summary

- **Novel distillation method**: Proposes **d-CoT-nR**, a multilingual reasoning distillation approach that integrates **incorrect rationales** as additional guidance to improve step-by-step reasoning in LLMs.
- **Empirical validation**: Demonstrates **significant accuracy gains** in multilingual high-school examinations, particularly in **unseen languages**, outperforming baseline English-focused distillation methods.
- **Scope and gap**: Addresses the **underexplored** challenge of multilingua
