## Summary

arXiv:2606.27578v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Reward models for Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) pool preferences across thousands of annotators and fit one global affine calibrator, collapsing raters with systematically different rating-scale offsets and slopes into a single average-rater fit that does not match any individual annotator. PEBS is a per-rater empirical-Bayes shrinkage estimator: it fits per-rater affine calibrators on a held-out slice of each annotator's ratin
