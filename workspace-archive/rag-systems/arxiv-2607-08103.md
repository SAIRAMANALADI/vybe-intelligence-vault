Abstract: Rank estimation under label noise poses a fundamental challenge, as ordinal annotations often exhibit structured uncertainty rather than simple label corruption. In this paper, we reformulate rank estimation with noisy ordinal labels as a stochastic ordering problem, in which each instance is inherently associated with multiple plausible ranks instead of a single deterministic label.
