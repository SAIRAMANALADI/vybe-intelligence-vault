## Summary

- **Publishing Modes**: Notebooks can be published in two modes: (1) *Reproducible mode* (default) runs all cells top-to-bottom to ensure error-free execution, or (2) *Snapshot mode* publishes the current state without execution (e.g., for typo fixes).
- **Package Dependency Handling**: Additional packages must be installed at the **top** of the notebook when publishing with *Reproducible mode* to ensure availability in the fresh session used for publishing.
