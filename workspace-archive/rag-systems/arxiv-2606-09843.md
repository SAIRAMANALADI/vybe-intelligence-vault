## Summary

arXiv:2606.09843v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) give stable answers to personality questionnaires, yet these self-reports fail to predict how the models actually behave. Is this gap an artifact of forcing human trait categories onto LLMs, or something deeper about LLM self-report itself? To find out, we built the first psychometric instrument whose dimensions are derived bottom-up from LLM behavior rather than borrowed from human psychology. Administering
