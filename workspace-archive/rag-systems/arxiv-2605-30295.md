Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) show promise for clinical reasoning and decision support, but evaluation in realistic, electronic health record-congruent settings remains limited. Existing benchmarks often rely on static datasets or unstructured inputs that do not reflect the structured, interoperable data formats used in clinical systems. We introduce a pipeline for generating clinically realistic HL7 FHIR R4 bundles from unstructured text
