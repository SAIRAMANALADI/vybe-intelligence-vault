## Summary

arXiv:2602.22545v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Tau positron emission tomography supports Alzheimer's disease staging but is difficult to scale because of tracer, scanner, and radiation constraints. Synthesis from structural MRI is therefore attractive, but it is a particularly difficult setting. T1-weighted and FLAIR MRI provide anatomy and disease correlated morphology, but they do not directly measure Tau-PET relevant signal. We introduce SFL-Net, a multi-input synthesis framework
