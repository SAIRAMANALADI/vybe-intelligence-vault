## Summary

- **Compliance Standards**: Harvard mandates conformance with **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** for web-based IT and digital content, and **Section 508** for digital hardware interfaces, with exceptions requiring justification and alternate accessible formats.

- **Implementation Timeline**: New University IT and content must meet standards by **June 1, 2023**, while existing IT/content undergo prioritized remediation based on usage and criticality, overseen by the **Accessibility Steering Committee (ASC)**
