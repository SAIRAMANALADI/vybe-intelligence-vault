## Summary

arXiv:2607.02089v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Vision-language models (VLMs) have achieved strong performance across diverse multimodal tasks, yet they remain vulnerable to unreliable reasoning. Existing self-correction methods mitigate these issues but typically rely on post-training or carefully engineered feedback, incurring high computational cost. In this work, we revisit this challenge through the lens of emotional cues, asking whether they can activate latent self-correction behaviors
