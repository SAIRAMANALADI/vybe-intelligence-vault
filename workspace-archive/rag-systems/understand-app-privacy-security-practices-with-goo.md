## Summary

- **Data Collection & Sharing Disclosure**: Developers must declare how their app collects, shares, and handles user data via Google Play’s *Data safety* section, covering types of data (e.g., location, personal info, financial data) and purposes (e.g., account management, analytics). Disclosures apply to all app versions distributed on Google Play, though practices may vary by region, age, or user behavior.

- **Exemptions for Data Collection/Sharing**: Data accessed/processed *ephemerally*, en
