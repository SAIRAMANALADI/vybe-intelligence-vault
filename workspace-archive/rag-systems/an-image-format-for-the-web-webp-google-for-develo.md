## Summary

- **Compression Efficiency**: WebP achieves **26% smaller lossless** images than PNGs and **25-34% smaller lossy** images than JPEGs at equivalent quality, with transparency support in both modes.

- **Encoding Mechanism**: Lossy WebP uses **predictive coding** (VP8-based) to encode pixel differences, while lossless WebP leverages **reconstructed image fragments** and local palettes for exact pixel reconstruction.
