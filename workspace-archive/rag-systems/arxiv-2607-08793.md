## Summary

arXiv:2607.08793v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Sepsis is a leading cause of mortality, yet optimal treatment policies remain contested. Existing reinforcement learning (RL) approaches learn fixed strategies for sepsis treatment, limiting adaptability to changing clinical objectives during inference. We propose EHRMPC, a framework that decouples learning patient dynamics from optimizing treatment by training a patient digital twin in the form of a generative electronic health record (EHR) mod
