## Summary

- **File Deletion Process**: Files (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Vids) are moved to Google Drive's trash upon deletion, retaining visibility for collaborators until permanently deleted or the trash is emptied.
- **Ownership Impact**: Deleting a file you own removes it entirely for all collaborators; transferring ownership beforehand preserves access for others.
- **Deletion Methods**: Files can be deleted via the home screen ("Remove"), in-app ("Move to trash"), or directly in Drive ("Remove" button).
