## Summary

arXiv:2607.04927v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: World Action Models (WAMs) provide a promising alternative to Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies by using video-based world modeling as dense supervision for robot action learning. Existing WAMs excel at physically grounded execution, but typically lack the explicit language-level planning interface in VLM-based VLAs for decomposing coarse instructions. Such decomposition becomes important when household tasks involve complex multi-step goals
