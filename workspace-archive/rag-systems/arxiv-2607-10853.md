## Summary

arXiv:2607.10853v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Diffusion models faithfully reproduce their training distribution, but also inherit its imbalances and leave rare or under-represented modes hard to reach. A natural inference-time remedy is to sample from the high-temperature target $p^{(\gamma)}_0(x) \propto p_0(x)^{\gamma}$ for $0 < \gamma < 1$, which flattens dominant modes and lifts rare ones. However, naive score scaling while correctly reweighting modes also inflates the per-mode variance
