## Summary

arXiv:2607.04595v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Most classification problems assume the classes are roughly separable, so that an individual sample can usually be assigned to one class. Single-cell perturbation data violates this assumption: two perturbations can produce different populations of cells while overlapping so much that an individual cell could belong to either.
