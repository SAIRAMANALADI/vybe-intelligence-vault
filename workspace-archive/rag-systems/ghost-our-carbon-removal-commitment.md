## Summary

- **Direct Air Capture (DAC) Process**: Climeworks employs renewable geothermal energy and waste heat to extract CO₂ directly from ambient air, concentrating it for sequestration.

- **Permanent Sequestration via Carbfix**: Captured CO₂ is mineralized and stored underground in basaltic rock formations through Carbfix’s technology, ensuring long-term storage.

- **Scalable Removal Commitment**: Climeworks will sequester **322 tons of CO₂** for Stripe Climate, demonstrating scalable DAC deployment
