## Summary

- **API Endpoint**: Single POST `/v1/audio/speech` endpoint for TTS generation, supporting `input`, `voice_id`, `audio_format`, and `model` parameters (e.g., `simba-3.2` for English).
- **Voice Features**: Supports voice cloning from 10-30s samples, SSML for pitch/rate/emotion control, and streaming up to 20k chars with word-level timestamps (`speech marks`).
- **Model Support**: Four models (`simba-3.2`, `simba-3.0`, `simba-multilingual`, `simba-english`) with varying language coverage (30+ lan
