## Summary

arXiv:2607.10137v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Post-training quantization (PTQ) of large language models degrades sharply below 4-bit precision. We identify the root cause as residual stream distributional drift: quantization noise injected at each transformer layer accumulates in the shared residual representation, causing KL divergence from the FP16 baseline to grow super-linearly with depth (Pearson r=0.999 with log-perplexity, p<0.001, confirmed across all tested methods and bit-widths).
