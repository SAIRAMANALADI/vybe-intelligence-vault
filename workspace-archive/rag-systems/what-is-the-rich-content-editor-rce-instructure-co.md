## Summary

- **Core Components**: The Rich Content Editor (RCE) consists of a **content area**, **menubar**, and **toolbar**, enabling creation of formatted text, hyperlinks, images, media, mathematical formulas, embedded objects, tables, and more.

- **Functionality & Features**: Supports **HTML editing**, **keyboard shortcuts** (e.g., `Shift+?`, `Alt+F8`/`Option+F8`), **accessibility checks**, **word count**, **full-screen mode**, and **external tool integration** (e.g., Lucid EDU suite, Equation Editor)
