- **Core Principles**: *Be Internet Awesome* framework teaches five pillars: **Smart** (responsible sharing), **Alert** (scam awareness), **Strong** (password security & privacy), **Kind** (anti-bullying), and **Brave** (seeking adult guidance for online issues).
