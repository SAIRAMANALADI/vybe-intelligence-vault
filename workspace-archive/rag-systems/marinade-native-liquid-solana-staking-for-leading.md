## Summary

- **Stake Auction Marketplace (SAM)**: Validators bid competitively for delegated SOL, sharing priority fees and maximizing yield via transparent, epoch-based auctions.
- **Protected Staking Rewards (PSR)**: Mitigates validator underperformance/slashing risks by absorbing losses into validator bonds, ensuring steady rewards.
- **Liquid Staking (mSOL)**: Enables DeFi composability while maintaining native staking benefits, with instant unstaking and auto-compounding rewards.
