## Summary

arXiv:2606.29069v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Concept-based Explainable AI (C-XAI) seeks human-understandable explanations grounded in semantic concepts, yet validation is limited by the scarcity of fine-grained concept annotations. We evaluate whether mid-scale Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) can perform localized concept naming under strict zero-shot conditions by assigning labels to bounding-box regions at both object and part levels. We propose a reproducible zero-shot evaluation
