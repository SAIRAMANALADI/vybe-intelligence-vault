## Summary

- **Cancellation & Access**: Cancelling a subscription retains access until the end of the paid billing period; auto-renewal stops immediately, preventing future charges unless a payment plan is active.
- **Payment Plan Behavior**: Active payment plans require completion of all committed payments; stopping renewal halts future charges but does not refund prior payments or cancel remaining obligations.
- **Plan Modifications**: Users can switch between prepaid/subscription plans or billing cycles
