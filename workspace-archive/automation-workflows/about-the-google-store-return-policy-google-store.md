## Summary

- **Return Window & Eligibility**: Most items can be returned within **15 calendar days** of receipt; exceptions include thermostats (30 days), Verizon contract phones (30 days), and AT&T contract phones (14 days). SIM cards are **non-returnable**.
- **Refund Process**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **14 days** of device receipt, excluding delivery costs. For cash purchases, store credit is issued.
- **Regional & Policy Variations**: No refunds/exchanges for purchases
