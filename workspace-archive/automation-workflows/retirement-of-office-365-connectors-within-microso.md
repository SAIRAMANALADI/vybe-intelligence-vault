## Summary

- **Deprecation Timeline**: Office 365 Connectors in Teams will be fully disabled between **May 18–22, 2026**, requiring migration to Power Automate Workflows before rollout begins.

- **Migration Requirements**: Legacy `messageCard` payloads must be converted to **Adaptive Cards** for interactive elements (e.g., buttons), as `HttpPost` actions are unsupported in Workflows webhooks.
