## Summary

- **Integration Package**: `langchain-google-vertexai` v3.2.4 bridges Google Vertex AI generative models with LangChain for Python, enabling LLM-powered workflows via Vertex AI's cloud-based generative AI services.

- **Dependencies & Compatibility**: Requires Python ≥3.10.0 and <4.0.0; supports extras for `anthropic`, `mistral`, and `vectorsearch-v2` models.

- **License & Source**: MIT-licensed; source available at [GitHub](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain-google/tree/master/libs/vert
