## Summary

- **eScholarship Platform**: Managed via Janeway, a modern manuscript management system for journals, replacing legacy systems like eScholarship PLUS/Lulu.

- **Content Workflow**: Supports postprints, working papers, and other scholarly works via departmental sites, with author/depositor tools for submission and management.

- **Advanced Features**: Includes multi-affiliation support, preprint hosting (e.g., EarthArXiv), and automated email systems for journal submissions.
