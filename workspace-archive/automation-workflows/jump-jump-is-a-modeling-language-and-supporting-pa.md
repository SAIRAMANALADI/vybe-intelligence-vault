## Summary

- **JuMP** is a domain-specific modeling language embedded in **Julia**, designed for mathematical optimization, supporting linear, integer, conic, semidefinite, and constrained nonlinear programs with efficient solver interfaces (e.g., Ipopt).
- **Applications** span industrial optimization (e.g., Renault’s inventory routing, Thales’ train scheduling), academic teaching (MIT, KAIST), and large-scale energy system modeling (e.g., MIT’s GenX, NREL’s Sienna).
