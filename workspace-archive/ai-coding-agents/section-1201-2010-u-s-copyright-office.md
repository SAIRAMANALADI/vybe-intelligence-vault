## Summary

- **Exemptions under DMCA §1201(a)(1)**: Six classes of works exempted from circumvention prohibitions for noninfringing uses, including:
  - Motion pictures on CSS-protected DVDs for educational, documentary, or noncommercial criticism/commentary.
  - Wireless phone software interoperability for lawfully obtained applications.
  - Used phone firmware circumvention to connect to authorized networks.
