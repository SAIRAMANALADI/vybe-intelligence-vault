## Summary

- **Designated Agent**: Stanford has a designated DMCA agent (via ServiceNow, email `DMCA-claim@stanford.edu`, or mail) to handle copyright infringement notifications for domains including `stanford.edu`, `stanford.org`, and others.
- **Notification Requirements**: Complaints must include: (1) a description of the copyrighted work, (2) infringing material location, (3) contact info, (4) good-faith belief statement, (5) perjury statement, and (6) a physical/electronic signature.
