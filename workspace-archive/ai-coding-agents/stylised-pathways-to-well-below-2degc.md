## Summary

- **Negative emissions scaling**: To achieve 1.5°C, immediate zero emissions are required within 10–20 years without negative emissions; with negative emissions, mitigation can be slower but must be offset by later removals, with total negative emissions increasing proportionally to delayed mitigation.

- **Net-zero timing invariance**: The year of net-zero emissions (~2050) remains consistent regardless of negative emission deployment levels, as cumulative carbon budgets are fixed for a given t
