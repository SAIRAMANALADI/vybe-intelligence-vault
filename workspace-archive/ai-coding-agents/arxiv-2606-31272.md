## Summary

arXiv:2606.31272v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: AI agents increasingly acquire and execute skills at runtime: bundles of prompt instructions, executable code, and tool declarations fetched from marketplaces and other agents. Governing them needs a stable notion of skill identity, yet cryptographic hashing is engineered to destroy the very similarity we need, as a one-character edit scrambles the digest. We present a compact, locality-sensitive fingerprint that embeds each component of a skill
