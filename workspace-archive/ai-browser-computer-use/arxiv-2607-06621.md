## Summary

arXiv:2607.06621v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The pre-softmax score of an attention head is a bilinear form $score(i,j) = x_i^T M x_j$ in a learned operator $M = W_q^T W_k$. Because M is generally non-symmetric, hence non-normal, it has a complex eigenspectrum and non-orthogonal eigenvectors, the regime where non-Hermitian and random-matrix tools apply.
