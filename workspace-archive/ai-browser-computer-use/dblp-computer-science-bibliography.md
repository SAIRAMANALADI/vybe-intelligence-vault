## Summary

- **dblp** is a comprehensive computer science bibliography with 8.69M+ indexed publications, supporting XML/RDF dumps, SPARQL queries, and JSON/XML/JSONP APIs for programmatic access.

- **Search functionality** includes case-insensitive prefix matching, exact word search (via `$`), boolean operators (`AND` via space, `OR` via `|`), and external cross-referencing to Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, and ORCID.
