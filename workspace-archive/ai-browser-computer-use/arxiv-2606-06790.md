## Summary

arXiv:2606.06790v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: This paper presents ERNEST, a four-wheeled planetary rover concept equipped with a two-degree-of-freedom Active Gimbal Suspension that combines yaw and roll actuation to enable wheel reconfiguration, steering, and active load redistribution. A single neural network controller, trained to track a desired path across challenging terrain, fully unlocks the capabilities of this actuated suspension system for autonomous obstacle negotiation.
