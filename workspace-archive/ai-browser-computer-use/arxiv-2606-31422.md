## Summary

arXiv:2606.31422v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Long-horizon language agents do not only choose actions; they carry a private model of the world from one decision to the next. When that model drifts, a later failure can be decided before the failing action is ever taken. We study a direct repair mechanism: before committing to the next task action, an agent may ask the environment about one belief field and write the answer back into its world model.
