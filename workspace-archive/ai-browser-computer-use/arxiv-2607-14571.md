## Summary

arXiv:2607.14571v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We introduce \emph{gate-zero growth}, a function-preserving (FP) operator for continual learning that adds new residual blocks through a zero-initialised gate. Under a transversality condition, gate-zero growth induces \emph{rank separation} in the functional Jacobian: old directions are unchanged, new-weight directions are exactly flat at the growth point, and new gate directions are the only first-order source of new functional variation.
