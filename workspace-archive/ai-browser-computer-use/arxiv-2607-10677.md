Abstract: Self-attention is a ubiquitous primitive in modern sequence models, yet its operator-level geometry is only partially understood. We view a token sequence as a vector field over the token-position graph and identify attention as a connection walk: messages are aggregated by a nonnegative walk matrix while being transported along each edge by a learned linear map.
