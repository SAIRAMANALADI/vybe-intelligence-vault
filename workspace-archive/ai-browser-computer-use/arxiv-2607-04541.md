## Summary

Abstract: Camera-radar (CR) fusion is a practical sensing configuration for autonomous driving, but existing models are typically trained with task-specific supervision, limiting reusable representation learning. We present CRISP, a spatiotemporal CR backbone pretrained through forecasting-based representation learning.
