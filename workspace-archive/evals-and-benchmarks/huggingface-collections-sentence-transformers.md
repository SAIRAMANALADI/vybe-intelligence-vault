## Summary

- **Dataset**: `sentence-transformers/NanoBEIR-en` is a compact benchmark dataset derived from the BEIR (Benchmarking IR) collection, optimized for sentence-transformer evaluation.
- **Size**: Significantly smaller (~63.6k entries) than the original BEIR, enabling efficient testing of retrieval models with minimal computational overhead.
- **Use Case**: Designed for zero-shot evaluation of sentence embeddings in information retrieval tasks, supporting 14 diverse datasets across domains like fact
