Abstract: Model cards quote trust-benchmark scores without recording when they were measured, and the same number is routinely carried across successive checkpoints of one release line as if the model behind it had not shifted. We test whether it has shifted by auditing four open-source release lines, Yi, Qwen, Mistral, and Gemma, at three successive generations each, on a fixed basket of trust benchmarks under multiple prompt templates.
