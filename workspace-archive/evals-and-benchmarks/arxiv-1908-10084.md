## Summary

- **SBERT Architecture**: Modifies BERT/Roberta using Siamese and triplet networks to generate fixed-size sentence embeddings optimized for cosine similarity, enabling efficient semantic comparisons.

- **Performance Gains**: Reduces inference time for semantic similarity search from ~65 hours (BERT/RoBERTa) to ~5 seconds (SBERT) while preserving accuracy on STS and transfer learning tasks.
