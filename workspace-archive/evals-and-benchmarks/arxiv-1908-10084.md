## Summary

- **SBERT Architecture**: Modifies BERT/RoBERTa using siamese and triplet networks to generate semantically meaningful sentence embeddings optimized for cosine similarity comparisons.
- **Performance Efficiency**: Reduces semantic similarity search time from ~65 hours (BERT/RoBERTa) to ~5 seconds (SBERT) for 10,000 sentences while preserving BERT-level accuracy.
- **Evaluation & Superiority**: Outperforms state-of-the-art sentence embeddings on STS and transfer learning tasks, as validated in EM
