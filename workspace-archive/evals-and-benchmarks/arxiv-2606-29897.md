## Summary

arXiv:2606.29897v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Voice anonymization aims to protect speaker identity while preserving linguistic content and speech usability. However, most anonymization systems are developed on adult speech, leading to degraded performance when applied to child speech. This paper investigates child-centric anonymization by adapting a self-supervised learning (SSL) based anonymization pipeline to the child speech domain. The system is adapted using child speech from the MyST
