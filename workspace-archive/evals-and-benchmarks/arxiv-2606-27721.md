## Summary

Abstract: Compositional generalization, the ability to solve complex problems by combining solutions to simpler sub-problems, is a fundamental capability of both natural and artificial intelligence, and a key mechanism underlying chain-of-thought reasoning. However, the theoretical underpinnings of compositional generalization remain poorly understood: when and why does decomposing a problem into parts yield more efficient learning than solving it directly?
