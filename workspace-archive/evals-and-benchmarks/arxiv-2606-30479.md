## Summary

arXiv:2606.30479v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Mitigating an observed adversary in an enterprise network typically takes weeks of expert work: an analyst derives a mitigation tailored to that adversary, validates it without breaking production, and verifies it disrupts the specific attack. The procedure relies on expert judgment and cannot safely be exercised against the production network. COHORT is the first end-to-end framework to automate this procedure for deployable mitigations.
