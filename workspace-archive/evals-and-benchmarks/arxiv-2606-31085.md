Abstract: Drug-drug interaction (DDI) prediction is essential for medication safety, yet it requires reasoning over heterogeneous biomedical evidence whose relevance changes across interaction mechanisms. We propose DDIAgents, a mechanism-conditioned multi-agent framework that performs DDI prediction through dynamic knowledge orchestration.
