## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.
