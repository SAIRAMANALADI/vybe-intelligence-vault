## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* where unified representations favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.
- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes corpora into multiple granularity levels per modality, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope.
