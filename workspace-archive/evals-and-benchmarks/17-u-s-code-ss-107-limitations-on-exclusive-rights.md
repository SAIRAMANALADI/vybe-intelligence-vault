## Summary

- **Fair Use Criteria**: Four statutory factors govern fair use analysis: (1) purpose/character of use (commercial vs. nonprofit educational), (2) nature of copyrighted work, (3) amount/substantiality of portion used relative to whole, and (4) effect on market/value of copyrighted work.

- **Exclusion of Unpublished Works**: Unpublished status does not automatically preclude fair use; determination requires analysis of all four statutory factors.

- **Educational Exemption Limitation**: No blank
