## Summary

arXiv:2606.30647v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Dense volumetric reconstruction of cloud microphysical fields from sparse ground-based instruments remains an open problem, largely because the available measurements are heterogeneous in both modality and spatial coverage. We present AtmoFuseNet, a framework that fuses multi-view sky camera imagery with millimeter-wave cloud radar and ceilometer observations to produce 4D (three spatial dimensions plus time) estimates of cloud state and wind.
