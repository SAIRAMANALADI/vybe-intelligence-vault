Abstract: Traditional reinforcement learning (RL) for recovery in autonomous systems lacks causal understanding and generalizes poorly to novel failure scenarios. RL policies often stall in failure states, spending up to 70% of an episode immobilized. Rule-based recovery alone is inadequate, and adding heuristic recovery to a pretrained PPO policy worsens rewards because policies cannot coordinate well with unanticipated interventions.
