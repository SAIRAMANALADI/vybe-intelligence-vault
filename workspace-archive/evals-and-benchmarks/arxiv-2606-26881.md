Abstract: We introduce an accelerated Langevin-based sampling method that is based on two complementary devices: \emph{SamAdams} adaptive timestepping, which automatically shrinks the effective integration step in stiff regions of phase space using a relaxed stiffness monitor, and \emph{position-adaptive Langevin} (PAL) dynamics, which concentrates friction along the local force direction while preserving the canonical distribution as the exact invariant
