## Summary

- **RAG Architecture**: Combines a *retriever* (FAISS index for similarity search on document embeddings) and a *generator* (FLAN-T5 model) to produce context-aware responses from retrieved documents.

- **Implementation Workflow**: Documents are embedded via `SentenceTransformer`, indexed with FAISS, and queried using cosine similarity; responses are generated by conditioning FLAN-T5 on retrieved context.
