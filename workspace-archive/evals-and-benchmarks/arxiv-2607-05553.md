## Summary

arXiv:2607.05553v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Transient stability control in smart grids requires rapid post-fault damping of generator frequency and rotor angle deviations to prevent cascading failures. This paper proposes FedPPO-PG, a Federated Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization framework with Physics-Grounded neighborhoods, which reformulates transient stability control as a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning problem optimized directly against closed-loop stability object
