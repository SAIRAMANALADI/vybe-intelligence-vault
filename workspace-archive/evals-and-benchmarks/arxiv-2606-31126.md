## Summary

arXiv:2606.31126v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Predicting biomolecular properties from limited labeled data is a central bottleneck in protein engineering and small-molecule design. As strong pretrained encoders now supply rich fixed-length representations, the difficulty has shifted from representation learning to building a data-efficient predictor for the few-shot regime. Tabular foundation models such as TabPFN3 and TabICL are unlikely candidates for this role: they are in-context learners
