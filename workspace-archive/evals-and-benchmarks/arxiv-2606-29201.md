## Summary

arXiv:2606.29201v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Behavior-cloned policies often learn multiple behavior modes from demonstration datasets, including modes that are unsafe or otherwise undesired at deployment. For example, a policy trained on diverse handover demonstrations may learn to pass a knife blade-first. Standard remedies such as data curation and inference-time steering either require access to the original demonstrations for full retraining or add substantial inference-time overhead.
