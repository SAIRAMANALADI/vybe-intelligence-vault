## Summary

*   UniversalRAG is an any-to-any Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework designed for heterogeneous corpora comprising diverse modalities and granularities, extending beyond conventional text-only or single-modality RAG systems.
*   It implements modality-aware routing, which dynamically identifies and performs targeted retrieval within appropriate modality-specific corpora, theoretically justified to mitigate the "modality gap" observed in unified representation spaces.
