## Summary

arXiv:2606.29322v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Collaborative learning is sustainable only when it benefits each participant. Standard federated learning optimizes a global average objective, which can under perform for clients whose data distributions differ substantially from the population. We study selfish personalization: how a designated target client can use peer gradients to minimize its own risk while avoiding negative transfer. We propose SP-CACW, a convergence-aware client-weighting
