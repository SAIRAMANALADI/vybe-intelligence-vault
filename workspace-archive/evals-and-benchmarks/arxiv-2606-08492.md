## Summary

arXiv:2606.08492v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Despite the impressive capabilities of text-to-image (T2I) models, an intent-generation gap often persists due to the brevity and ambiguity of user prompts. Existing approaches primarily polish the prompt for fluency and readability. However, the enhancement process still lacks visual grounding. As a result, the rewriter may over-infer missing details, causing an intent-generation gap. To address this limitation, we propose FaithRewriter
