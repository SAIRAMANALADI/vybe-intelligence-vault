Abstract: Reviewing nuclear regulatory documents requires multi-hop reasoning across tens of thousands of pages, where judgments depend on evidence assembled across multiple chapters. We frame this task as planning: an LLM-based agent observes the evidence collected so far, picks the next document fragment to inspect, and stops when the evidence is sufficient.
