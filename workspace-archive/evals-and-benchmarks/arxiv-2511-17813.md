Abstract: LLM-based simulations can enable controlled studies of civic deliberation, but current systems lack speaker-attributed data and methods for evaluating long-form institutional behavior. ASR transcripts typically use anonymous labels such as $Speaker\_1$, preventing models from learning stable participant behavior across meetings. We present a reproducible pipeline that converts public Zoom recordings into speaker-attributed transcripts en
