## Summary

arXiv:2607.01674v2 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: In multi-source ECG deployment, models may need to incorporate new data sources when earlier raw ECGs cannot be retained or replayed. Freezing a pretrained backbone and assigning each source an isolated classifier prevents parameter interference, but deployment still requires selecting an expert when source metadata are unavailable. We study this distinction through IRFE-ECG, an incremental expert bank built on frozen 1024-dimensional ECGFound
