## Summary

- **Agent Definition**: Speechify Agents are reusable configurations combining prompt, voice, tools, and evaluation criteria, enabling real-time voice interactions via web or phone with low-latency speech processing.

- **Core Features**: Includes a curated voice catalog (`GET /v1/agents/voices`), tools for backend/code execution/MCP integration, persistent transcripts with tool traces, and post-call LLM-graded evaluations.
