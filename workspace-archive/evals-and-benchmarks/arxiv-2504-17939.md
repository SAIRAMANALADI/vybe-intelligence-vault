arXiv:2504.17939v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: We present a computational model of the mechanisms that may determine infant behavior in the "mobile paradigm". This paradigm has been used in developmental psychology to explore how infants learn the sensory effects of their actions. In this paradigm, a mobile (an articulated and movable object hanging above an infant's crib) is connected to one of the infant's limbs, prompting the infant to preferentially move that "connected" limb.
