## Summary

arXiv:2606.06379v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Medical vision-language models (VLMs) have shown increasing potential for clinical image interpretation, including lesion detection and report generation. However, their practical utility remains limited by insufficient sensitivity to subtle lesions, whose visual evidence is often sparse, low-contrast, and embedded within complex anatomical context. As local visual tokens are aggregated, these weak lesion cues can become underrepresented
