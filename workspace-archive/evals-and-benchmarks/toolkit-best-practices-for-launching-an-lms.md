## Summary

- **Define and scope project requirements** prior to LMS provider selection to align technical, functional, and business objectives with measurable KPIs for success.
- **Evaluate platform capabilities** against use cases (e.g., customer/partner training) by assessing integration needs, scalability, and compliance (e.g., SCORM/xAPI support, SSO, data security).
- **Plan launch phases** with stakeholder alignment, pilot testing, and post-launch analytics to optimize adoption and ROI.

## Use Cases

- Model performance auditing
- Prompt regression tracking
- Hallucination testing
