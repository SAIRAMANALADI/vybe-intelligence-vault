Abstract: The generalized Ising problem captures a broad spectrum of hard combinatorial problems, including MAX-CUT, Number Partitioning (NPP), and Maximum Independent Set. In this work, we consider the notion of one-flip local minima for this problem. We construct a polynomial relaxation and prove the landscape equivalence theorem: there exists a one-to-one correspondence between the local minima of the relaxation and the one-flip minima of the original
