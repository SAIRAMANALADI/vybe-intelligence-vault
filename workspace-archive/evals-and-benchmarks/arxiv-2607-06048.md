## Summary

arXiv:2607.06048v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: We aim to identify scattering network architectures that maximize the separation capacity on data with low intrinsic dimension. The networks we consider employ a fixed monomial nonlinearity and no pooling, so that the only design variable is the frame generated by the network filters. For data modeled as rectifiable sets, we first characterize and bound the separation capacity of general feature extractors in terms of the geometry of the dataset
