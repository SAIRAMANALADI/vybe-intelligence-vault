## Summary

- **SURGE Framework**: Proposes a subgraph retrieval-augmented generation (SURGE) method that retrieves relevant subgraphs from knowledge graphs (KGs) to enhance dialogue generation, ensuring contextually grounded responses.
- **Consistency Mechanism**: Enforces factual consistency by perturbing word embeddings conditioned on retrieved subgraphs, followed by contrastive learning to align generated text with KG facts.
