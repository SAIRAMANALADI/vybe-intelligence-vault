## Summary

- **SimpleQA Benchmark**: Introduces a benchmark designed to evaluate short-form factuality in LLMs, featuring adversarially collected questions with single, indisputable answers to ensure unambiguous grading (correct/incorrect/not attempted).
- **Knowledge Confidence Metric**: Proposes a framework where models should maximize correct answers while avoiding responses when uncertain, enabling assessment of "knowing what they know" via abstention.
