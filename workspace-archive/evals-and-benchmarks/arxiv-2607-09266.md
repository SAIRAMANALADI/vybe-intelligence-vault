## Summary

arXiv:2607.09266v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Per-layer diagnostics reveal that, at the prescribed learning rate, Lion's effective scale is 2.6-2.8x too high for attention and MLP parameters and ~2x too high for normalization layers on ViT-Tiny/CIFAR-100; this 32% cross-layer-type disparity cannot be reproduced by a single global rate. The measurement comes from LionVote, a per-layer learning rate mechanism in which each parameter tensor maintains a compound level, a persistent integer update
