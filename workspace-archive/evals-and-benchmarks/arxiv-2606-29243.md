## Summary

arXiv:2606.29243v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We present KrishokChat, the first citation-grounded Bengali agricultural instruction-tuning dataset for crop advisory in low-resource settings. We establish a foundation of 290 hierarchical Knowledge Nodes, extracting disease symptoms, management practices, chemical dosages, and verbatim citations from 129 domain-filtered agricultural manuals. Every training instance inherits a verified citation header, guaranteeing 100% citation provenance. Using
