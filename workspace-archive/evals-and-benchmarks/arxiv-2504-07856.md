Abstract: Curriculum learning enhances Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) for aligning Large Language Models (LLMs), yet existing methods rely on a one-dimensional view of difficulty. In this work, we reframe alignment difficulty as a two-dimensional space spanned by Prompt Complexity (PC) and Pairwise Distinguishability (PD), providing a more principled foundation for alignment.
