Abstract: Generated videos provide useful visual motion priors for robot manipulation, but their visual plausibility does not imply physical executability. A generated video usually lacks metric geometry, grasp grounding, robot kinematic feasibility, and execution-time feedback, which makes direct trajectory replay unreliable in real-world manipulation.
