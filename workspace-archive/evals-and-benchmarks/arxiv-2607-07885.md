## Summary

Abstract: Dynamic obstacle avoidance in unstructured outdoor environments remains a critical challenge for autonomous mobile robots, particularly when large-scale robot-specific training data and simulation-based policies are impractical. We present a data-efficient, interpretable method for vision-based dynamic obstacle avoidance that operates entirely on real-world data, avoiding the sim-to-real transfer problem inherent in simulation-trained policies.
