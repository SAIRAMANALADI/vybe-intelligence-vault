## Summary

arXiv:2607.07557v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: One-shot pruning methods like Wanda and SparseGPT apply the same sparsity ratio to every layer of a transformer, ignoring known variation in layer importance. We propose PALS (Percentile-Aware Layerwise Sparsity), which adjusts per-layer sparsity based on the 99th percentile of activation magnitudes, bounded to $\pm 5\%$ around the target ratio.
