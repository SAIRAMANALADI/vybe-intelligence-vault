## Summary

arXiv:2607.00956v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Time-series models are often evaluated by what they can forecast or classify, but those scores do not show whether their representations preserve the process state a user may want to inspect: event timing, phase, amplitude, frequency, or regime variables. We introduce Aionoscope, a generator-based diagnostic tool for debugging latent-state accessibility in frozen time-series representations. Aionoscope separates process generation from observation
