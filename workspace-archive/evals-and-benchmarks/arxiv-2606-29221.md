## Summary

arXiv:2606.29221v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We address the problem of online multi-human multi-robot teaming through the lens of a linear matching bandit framework, where a learner assigns robots with unknown features from a fixed pool to distinct sets of human agents over multiple rounds. To solve this problem, we propose LinMatch, an online learning algorithm that updates the confidence intervals of the unknown features and makes the optimistic matching under uncertainty.
