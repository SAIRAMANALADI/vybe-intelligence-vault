## Summary

arXiv:2509.03758v5 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: We propose a data-driven interpolation framework for reconstructing real-valued functions on smooth manifolds from scattered pointwise observations. The method combines a Gaussian Nadaraya--Watson kernel interpolant with a Voronoi-adaptive bandwidth determined entirely by the geometry of the sampled data, yielding an explicit closed-form construction that requires neither training, iterative optimization, preprocessing, nor parameter tuning.
