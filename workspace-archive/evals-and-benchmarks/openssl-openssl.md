## Summary

- OpenSSL provides third-party binary distributions for select OSes (Windows, Solaris, NonStop) and architectures (x86, ARM, WebAssembly), with no official endorsement; users must evaluate risks independently.

- Engine support is deprecated in OpenSSL 4.0; third-party engines/providers (e.g., Intel QAT, wolfSSL wolfEngine, SafeLogic FIPS providers) offer alternative cryptographic acceleration and compliance modules.
