## Summary

- **Memory Refreshing**: DRAM cells require periodic refreshing (typically every 32-64ms) to maintain data integrity due to capacitor charge leakage.
- **Refresh Mechanisms**: Modern DRAM uses distributed refresh (per-bank or per-row) or targeted refresh (e.g., temperature-aware) to optimize power/performance.
- **Impact on Latency**: Refresh operations introduce latency jitter; mitigations include refresh scheduling (e.g., staggered refresh) and pseudo-random refresh patterns.
