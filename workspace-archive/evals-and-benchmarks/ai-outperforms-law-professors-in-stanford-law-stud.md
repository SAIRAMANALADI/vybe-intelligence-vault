## Summary

- **Blind evaluation study**: In a blind comparison of ~3,000 anonymized responses, law professors preferred AI-generated answers over peer-written ones 75% of the time, with AI flagged as pedagogically harmful only 3.5% vs. 12% for human responses.

- **Legal reasoning benchmark**: The study tested AI on contract law questions requiring nuanced judgment, ambiguity navigation, and synthesis of competing arguments—fields where prior AI evaluations were limited.
