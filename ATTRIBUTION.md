# Sourcing and Attribution Policy

The **Vybe Intelligence Vault** values intellectual property and aims to preserve clear pathways to original publications.

## Sourcing
The data represented in this repository is sourced entirely from public feeds:
* Hacker News (via algolia API)
* Reddit (via public developer endpoints)
* YouTube (via public developer feeds)
* Preprints and papers (arXiv, Papers with Code)
* Engineering and company blogs (RSS Feeds)

## Attribution
Every generated Markdown file preserves the source details in its frontmatter:
* **`source_url`**: Direct hyperlink to the original article, video, or paper.
* **`title`**: Original title of the publication.
* **`local_vault_path`**: Relative location inside this database.

If you are the author of an article or paper indexed here and want it modified or removed, please contact us at [sairamanladi2007@gmail.com](mailto:sairamanladi2007@gmail.com) and we will fulfill your request promptly.
