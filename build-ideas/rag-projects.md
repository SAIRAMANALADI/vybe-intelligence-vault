### Academic Paper Multi-PDF Search

*   **Difficulty**: Intermediate
*   **Stack**: `FastAPI, PyPDF, Qdrant, SentenceTransformers`
*   **What to Build**: A FastAPI backend that processes academic papers, chunks text, generates vector embeddings, and supports hybrid semantic search.
*   **Why It Matters**: Validates hybrid retrieval and vector database storage.
*   **Recommended Vault Resources**: [rag-systems/](../workspace-archive/rag-systems/README.md)
*   **Portfolio Angle**: Great for showcasing document ingestion pipelines.
*   **Monetization Angle**: Research helper SaaS tool.
