## Summary
FengChaoSkills is an agent-agnostic tool that creates a business fact ledger for AI coding sessions, automatically capturing and organizing business truths, evidence, and decisions after real-world delivery. It ensures AI sessions route relevant historical context while strictly separating current facts from historical references.

## Key Features
- Agent-agnostic business fact ledger with six-layer memory model (business-context, task-records, changelog, plan-records, conversation-records, FENGWANG.md)
- Automatic memory routing for new AI sessions based on minimal necessary context
- Explicit negation tracking to prevent AI from revisiting rejected solutions
- Zero third-party dependencies, zero telemetry, and zero network requests
- Git-based immutable records with human-readable Markdown documentation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI coding sessions retain accurate, structured business context and evidence, reducing hallucinations and improving decision-making in real-world development workflows.
