## Summary
A Chinese-language guide repository focused on training individuals to become Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), who bridge the gap between AI model capabilities and real-world enterprise business value through hands-on deployment and integration. It breaks down the FDE role into five core competencies: product management, development engineering, data analysis, automation, and AI application expertise.

## Key Features
- Defines the FDE role as a hybrid of five critical competencies for AI deployment in enterprises
- Provides a structured learning path covering product management, development, data analysis, automation, and AI application
- Focuses on real-world business value rather than just technical implementation
- Includes practical examples like AI-driven testing, dashboards, and knowledge base automation
- Emphasizes hands-on deployment and overcoming enterprise-specific challenges

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It equips AI engineers with the cross-functional skills needed to bridge the gap between AI capabilities and enterprise business value, a critical need in the current AI adoption landscape.
