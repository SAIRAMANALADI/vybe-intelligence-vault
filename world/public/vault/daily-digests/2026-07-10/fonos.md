## Summary
Fonos is a hotkey-first voice terminal for dictation, listening, and spoken agents, enabling users to press a shortcut, speak naturally, and send results to the cursor, clipboard, notebook, or AI agent. It is local-first, provider-agnostic, and supports both cloud and local AI models.

## Key Features
- Hotkey-driven voice workflows for dictation, listening, and agent interactions
- Provider-agnostic support for local and cloud AI models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, etc.)
- Unified history with full-text search and activity tracking
- Built-in vocabulary correction and domain-specific term biasing
- Real-time conversation with short-term memory and persona overrides

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Fonos streamlines voice-driven AI interactions, making it essential for developers building RAG systems that require hands-free, efficient input methods and seamless integration with local or cloud-based AI models.
