## Summary
A Claude Code plugin that automates the entire React Native mobile app lifecycle, from market research to App Store and Google Play submission. It uses a generator-evaluator architecture with hard gates and file-based handoffs to ensure quality and completeness.

## Key Features
- End-to-end React Native app lifecycle automation from idea to store submission
- Generator-Evaluator separation with hard gates and contract negotiation
- Market research, planning, design system, development, QA, and submission phases
- Automated EAS builds, screenshots, and store submissions for iOS and Android
- File-based handoffs and state management for pause/resume functionality

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the entire React Native app development lifecycle, reducing manual effort and ensuring quality through automated phases and hard gates for RAG and AI stack builders.
