## Summary
Reviva is a local-first desktop application that integrates AI Agents, Wiki knowledge bases, and creation tools to enhance learning and knowledge management workflows. It enables users to organize materials, engage in contextual Q&A, and generate structured outputs like flashcards, quizzes, and research reports.

## Key Features
- Local-first data storage with SQLite for full user control and privacy
- Multi-Agent system with customizable roles, skills, and tools for specialized tasks
- Wiki-style knowledge base for organizing and retrieving documents, notes, and references
- Built-in creation tools for generating flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, and research reports
- Support for multiple AI model providers and OpenAI-compatible APIs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Reviva provides a unified, local-first environment for RAG pipelines, enabling persistent knowledge storage, contextual AI interactions, and structured output generation essential for long-term learning and research workflows.
