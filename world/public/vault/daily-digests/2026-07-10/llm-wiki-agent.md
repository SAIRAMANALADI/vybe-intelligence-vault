## Summary
A coding agent that autonomously ingests documents, extracts knowledge, and builds a persistent, interlinked wiki with entities, concepts, and a knowledge graph. The system compounds knowledge over time, flags contradictions, and provides structured synthesis without manual maintenance.

## Key Features
- Automated ingestion of 15+ file formats (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, etc.) with built-in conversion
- Persistent wiki with auto-generated entity and concept pages, updated on every ingest
- Knowledge graph with deterministic and semantic edges, including community detection
- Contradiction flagging and linting for data gaps during ingestion
- Obsidian integration for seamless browsing and visualization of the knowledge graph

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It transforms unstructured documents into a structured, queryable knowledge base that compounds over time, eliminating manual note-taking and enabling deeper AI-driven synthesis and retrieval.
