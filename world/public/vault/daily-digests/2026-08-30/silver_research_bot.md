## Summary
Silver Research Bot is an autonomous AI research assistant that performs deep, multi-stage analysis of academic papers uploaded as PDFs. It automates extraction, translation, multi-dimensional analysis, formula interpretation, visualization, citation mapping, peer review, and quality auditing through an 8-stage pipeline.

## Key Features
- 8-stage automated paper analysis pipeline (extraction, translation, multi-dimensional analysis, formula interpretation, visualization, citation mapping, peer review, auditing)
- LaTeX-aware translation with formula preservation and reconstruction
- Hybrid RAG system combining BM25, vector search, and cross-encoder reranking
- Built-in nanoBot agent framework with ReAct loops, memory systems, and 14+ tools
- Multi-platform chat integration (14 channels including WeChat, Telegram, Discord, Slack)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a comprehensive, automated pipeline for deep academic paper analysis, integrating LLM capabilities, RAG, and multi-agent collaboration to accelerate research workflows and improve understanding of complex papers.
