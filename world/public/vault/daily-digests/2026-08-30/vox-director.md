## Summary
vox-director automates the creation of professional paper-collage style explainer videos from a user-provided topic. It handles scriptwriting, visual sourcing, voiceover generation, and video editing without requiring programming knowledge.

- Uses AI agents for script generation and voice synthesis
- Royalty-free asset sourcing for paper-collage visuals
- Cloud-based processing for heavy video rendering tasks
