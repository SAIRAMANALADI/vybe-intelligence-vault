## Summary
An open-source, local-first AI workbench designed for scientists to conduct rigorous, reproducible research. It integrates literature search, code execution, artifact generation, and report writing into a single auditable workflow, acting as an alternative to proprietary AI-for-science tools.

## Key Features
- End-to-end workflow automation for scientific research, including data analysis, figure generation, and report writing
- Full artifact provenance tracking with versioned records and traceability reviews
- Local-first architecture ensuring data and compute stay on the user's machine
- Model-agnostic design supporting BYOK, OpenAI-compatible, and local models
- Pluggable skills and connectors for literature, experiments, and domain-specific tools

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical open-source alternative for scientists to conduct reproducible AI-driven research locally, reducing reliance on proprietary tools while ensuring transparency and auditability of artifacts.
