## Summary
claude-compass is a Claude Cowork plugin designed to assist globally-minded job seekers by providing tools to discover remote-hire countries, identify visa sponsors, and calculate local-market salaries with evidence-based insights.

## Key Features
- Slash commands for country discovery and salary calculation
- Automated routing of complex tasks to specialized subagents (deep-reasoner, calculator)
- Evidence-based decision-making for international job opportunities
- Integration with Claude Cowork for seamless workflow
- Open-source and community-contributable

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
