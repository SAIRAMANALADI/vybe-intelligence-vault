## Summary
A portable skill for AI agents (Claude Code, opencode, etc.) designed to teach language models how to communicate with human-like presence and authenticity, rather than just sounding human. It includes two operating modes: conversational presence and de-AI composition, along with a self-audit pass to refine responses.

## Key Features
- Two operating modes: conversational presence (Mode A) and de-AI composition (Mode B)
- Self-audit pass (detect → repair → soul) to refine responses before delivery
- Six-family AI-tell taxonomy and voice calibration for authentic output
- 16 modules (~304 book-grounded rules) covering high-failure emotional territories
- Portable skill with no runtime dependencies, deployable across multiple agent platforms

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, measurable approach to making AI responses feel authentically human rather than robotic or performative, which is critical for RAG systems interacting in emotionally nuanced contexts.
