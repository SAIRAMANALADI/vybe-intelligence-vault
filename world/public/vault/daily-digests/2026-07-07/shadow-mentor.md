## Summary
Shadow is an AI compliance council for regulated lending that encodes a bank's loan policy into 5 past decisions, providing signed, attestation-bound verdicts in milliseconds. It runs in a VPC and integrates with tools like Claude Desktop via MCP for real-time compliance checks.

## Key Features
- Encodes bank loan policy into 5 past decisions for deterministic verdicts
- Provides signed, attestation-bound compliance decisions with Ed25519 or HMAC-SHA-256
- Runs entirely in a VPC with no data leaving the bank's servers
- Includes a 5-voice AI council for multi-perspective deliberation
- Offers SOC 2 Type 1 readiness mapping and regulatory citation tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Shadow provides a critical governance layer for AI-driven lending decisions, ensuring regulatory compliance and auditability in a high-stakes financial domain.
