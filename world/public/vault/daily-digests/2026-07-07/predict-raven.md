## Summary
Predict Raven is an open-source forecasting agent framework that enables AI agents to estimate real-world event probabilities, gather evidence dynamically, and act on predictions. It powers both autonomous prediction-market trading on Polymarket and market-blind public forecasting, such as live World Cup probability models.

## Key Features
- Dynamic evidence gathering and Bayesian updating for probability estimation
- Dual-mode operation: market-blind forecasting and autonomous trading on Polymarket
- Risk-controlled trading with hard service-layer constraints (e.g., 15% per trade, 80% exposure limit)
- Modular AI agent runtime with provider switching (Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw)
- Transparent decision logging and public Brier scoring for accountability

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Predict Raven demonstrates how AI agents can autonomously gather evidence, reason about probabilities, and act in real-world markets with rigorous risk controls, making it a critical reference for building production-grade AI trading and forecasting systems.
