## Summary
Facework is an open protocol designed to turn ideas into coherent, ownable, and runtime-portable systems through 8 lifecycle phases that produce 20 canonical artifacts. It emphasizes coherence, quality governance, and explicit pass/fail evidence gates, enabling seamless handoffs to any runtime without rebuilding context.

## Key Features
- 8 lifecycle phases with explicit pass/fail evidence gates
- 20 canonical artifacts spanning strategy, taste, architecture, operations, and runtime ports
- Machine-readable manifests validated by `bin/validate-manifest`
- Runtime portability layer (manifest schema v1.4.0) for seamless handoffs to any agent harness
- DesignInfrastructure (v1.3.0) for active design governance and LLM-callable quality rules

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Facework provides a structured, discipline-driven methodology to ensure coherence and portability in AI systems, making it essential for building scalable, maintainable, and interoperable RAG and agent-based architectures.
