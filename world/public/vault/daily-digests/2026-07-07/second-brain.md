## Summary
A file-based knowledge vault operated by an AI agent (Cline) acting as an executive secretary. It organizes raw material into a structured, indexed system, enabling cited answers, automated reports, and offline-ready documents while preserving original data integrity.

## Key Features
- Three-layer data pipeline (inbox → library → memory) with strict separation of originals and agent-generated content
- DOX-inspired navigation tree with mechanical sharding to maintain model reliability as the vault grows
- Append-only logging, dated facts, and source-cited answers for transparency and auditability
- Automated workflows for meetings, performance evaluations, reports, and decision records
- Offline HTML/PDF report generation with customizable design kits and template evolution

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, agent-operated system for organizing and querying knowledge that preserves data integrity while enabling reliable RAG and automated reporting for AI engineering workflows.
