## Summary
Vibe-Research is an open-source personal AI-driven investment research dashboard for A-shares, US stocks, and Hong Kong stocks. It aggregates market data, news, financial reports, and user-defined portfolios, enabling users to integrate their own AI models for analysis without making stock recommendations.

## Key Features
- Real-time stock market data aggregation for A-shares, US, and Hong Kong stocks
- Customizable AI integration for personalized investment analysis
- Daily market recap with sentiment analysis and sector trends
- Portfolio tracking with local storage (no cloud uploads)
- News radar with AI-summarized insights from 108 RSS sources

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
