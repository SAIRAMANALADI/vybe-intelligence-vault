## Summary
S.A.M. (Smart Artificial Mind) is a local-first, private AI assistant that operates as a team of autonomous agents to perform real tasks on your machine, including research, file management, terminal operations, email, GitHub actions, and more. It runs entirely on your device or with free cloud AI tiers, ensuring privacy and cost efficiency.

## Key Features
- Deploys a team of specialist AI agents to tackle tasks in parallel, including research, coding, writing, and strategy.
- Supports 30+ free AI models with auto-rotation to avoid rate limits, including Groq, Cerebras, NVIDIA, and GitHub Models.
- Integrates 150+ tools for web, files, terminal, email, iMessage, GitHub, calendar, and more, enabling real-world actions.
- Offers semantic memory, scheduled tasks, and a native desktop app with a global hotkey for seamless interaction.
- Prioritizes privacy with local-first operation, ask-first safety for risky actions, and no telemetry or cloud dependency.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SAM provides a robust, local-first framework for building autonomous AI agents that can perform real tasks, making it essential for developers seeking to integrate multi-agent systems into RAG pipelines or workflow automation.
