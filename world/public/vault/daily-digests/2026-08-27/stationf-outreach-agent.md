## Summary
An autonomous AI agent that automates end-to-end job outreach by scraping French job boards, identifying decision-makers, researching companies, and sending personalized cold emails with follow-ups. It operates on a scheduled cron job, logs interactions, and self-improves using a strategy bandit system.

## Key Features
- Multi-source job board scraping (7 platforms) with role and location filtering
- Automated decision-maker discovery and email verification for targeted outreach
- Evidence-gated personalized email generation with company-specific facts
- End-to-end automation including follow-ups, inbox syncing, and reply handling
- Self-healing system with pre-flight checks, dead-man's switch, and strategy bandit learning
