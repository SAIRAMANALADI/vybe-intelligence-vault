## Summary
GuardCall is a real-time AI-powered web application that acts as a silent co-pilot during phone calls, detecting scam tactics and providing instant coaching to users. It leverages edge-based speech-to-text and AI analysis to intercept and neutralize scams during the call, with features like auto-generated police reports and a community-sourced scam database.

## Key Features
- Real-time AI coaching during calls to expose scammers with exact phrases
- Auto-generated police reports (FIR) with PII scrubbing for legal action
- Community database for crowdsourced scam number identification
- Zero-hop audio processing via Deepgram for ultra-low latency transcription
- Silent, non-intrusive UI with sliding coaching cards and PWA support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
GuardCall demonstrates how agentic AI can intercept and neutralize threats in real-time, offering a blueprint for proactive security applications in voice-based interactions.
