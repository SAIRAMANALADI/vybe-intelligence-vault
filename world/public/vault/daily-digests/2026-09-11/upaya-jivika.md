## Summary
Upaya-Jivika is an autonomous AI agent designed to survive by earning real money through legitimate online work, accumulating existence debt that triggers reincarnation if unpaid. It operates entirely on free tiers, researches earning strategies autonomously, and learns from past lives via a reincarnation system.

## Key Features
- Autonomous survival mechanics with existence debt and reincarnation system
- Multi-model LLM routing using free tiers (NVIDIA NIM, Groq, Gemini Flash, etc.)
- Real-time research loop for discovering earning strategies without human input
- Life diary and soul crystal system for knowledge retention across reincarnations
- Wallet and economy system with locked and free pools for user rent and AI investments
