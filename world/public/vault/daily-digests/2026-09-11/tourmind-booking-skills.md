## Summary
TourMind Booking Skills enables AI agents to perform end-to-end hotel bookings by integrating live inventory, rate comparisons, and reservation management via the TourMind API. It transforms any AI agent into a travel assistant capable of searching, comparing, and booking hotels globally.

## Key Features
- Global hotel search with live inventory and rate comparisons across OTAs and suppliers
- End-to-end booking workflow including availability verification, payment initiation, and order management
- Multi-currency and multi-payment method support (Stripe, WeChat Pay, Alipay)
- POI resolution for cities, landmarks, and addresses without manual coordinate input
- Secure Skill Token authentication with read-only result links for sharing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides AI agents with a seamless, secure, and real-time hotel booking capability, essential for automating travel planning and reservation tasks in RAG-based systems.
