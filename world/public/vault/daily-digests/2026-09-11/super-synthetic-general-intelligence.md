## Summary
AetherForge is an execution-first AI platform that converts natural language intent into production-ready outputs and deploys persistent, self-evolving autonomous agents through a browser-based workspace. It bridges intent to action with versioned project workspaces, constitutional AI safety, and multi-target deployments.

## Key Features
- Transforms natural language intent into production-ready files (documents, slide decks, reports, code archives)
- Deploys persistent, self-evolving autonomous agents (Aetherlings) with configurable personality and memory
- Project workspaces for versioned asset organization and collaboration
- Constitutional AI with safety checks, alignment scoring, and mutation gating
- Multi-target deployment (cloud, browser, desktop) with runtime monitoring and kill-switch controls
