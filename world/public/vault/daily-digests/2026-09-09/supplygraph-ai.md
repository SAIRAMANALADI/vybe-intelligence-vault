## Summary
SupplyGraph AI is an agentic supply-chain intelligence platform that provides autonomous agents for HTS/HS classification, U.S. tariff calculation, trade compliance, due diligence, and multi-tier supply chain risk analysis via A2A, MCP, and Agent API protocols.

## Key Features
- Exposes autonomous agents for customs classification, tariff calculation, and risk analysis via standard A2A, MCP, and Agent API
- No customer supplier lists or proprietary data uploads required; operates on a continuously updated global supply graph
- Provides explainable and auditable outputs with verifiable evidence chains
- Supports integration with AI orchestrators, IDEs, and traditional REST clients
- Offers sandbox environments for testing and development without exposing real data

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SupplyGraph AI enables RAG builders to integrate specialized supply chain and trade compliance agents into their systems, enhancing AI-driven decision-making for global trade and risk assessment.
