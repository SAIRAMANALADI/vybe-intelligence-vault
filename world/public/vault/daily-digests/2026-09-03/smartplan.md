## Summary
Smartplan is a Hermes Agent skill designed to generate structured, non-code process plans for business workflows, automation setups, data migrations, and operational rollouts. It ensures plans include context, sequenced execution steps, GO gates for validation, rollback procedures, and final verification criteria.

## Key Features
- Structured plan generation with 7-section format (context, steps, GO gates, rollback, verification, risks, notes)
- Explicit GO gates for human validation before execution
- Three-level rollback strategy (immediate cancel, restore from backup, full rollback)
- Integration with Hermes Agent for seamless skill deployment
- Predefined templates and examples for quick adoption

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Smartplan provides a standardized, structured approach to planning non-code business processes, ensuring clarity, validation, and rollback readiness for critical operational deployments.
