## Summary
Weles is an undetectable browser solution designed to enable AI agents to interact with the internet seamlessly, avoiding CAPTCHAs and bans. It combines custom C++-patched Chromium and Firefox forks with rotating fingerprints, caches website interactions as trajectories, and provides failure recovery with detailed evidence logging.

## Key Features
- Undetectable browser with rotating fingerprints to avoid bans and CAPTCHAs
- Trajectory caching for efficient website interaction replay
- Detailed failure recovery with video evidence and terminal state logging
- Secure credential management via Skarbiec lifecycle integration
- Enterprise-grade deployment with reviewed trajectories and operational support
