## Summary
VayuPress is a sovereign, all-in-one platform that consolidates a website, blog, PGP email server, encrypted chat, anti-bot shield, privacy analytics, Tor .onion services, and monetization tools into a single Go binary. It eliminates third-party dependencies, telemetry, and vendor lock-in while prioritizing user control and privacy.

## Key Features
- Single binary deployment with zero third-party dependencies
- Built-in PGP email server with automatic key management and recovery options
- Ephemeral end-to-end encrypted chat with cross-platform sync
- Enterprise-grade bot shield with self-learning defense mechanisms
- One-click Tor .onion deployment and dual Clearnet/Tor operational modes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
VayuPress provides a turnkey, privacy-preserving alternative to fragmented SaaS stacks, enabling AI engineers to deploy secure, self-contained infrastructure for RAG and AI applications without vendor lock-in or telemetry exposure.
