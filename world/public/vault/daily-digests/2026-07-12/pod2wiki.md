## Summary
pod2wiki automates the conversion of podcast audio and long-form articles into structured, wiki-style summaries for knowledge management systems. It leverages AI transcription and summarization to save users time by distilling hours of content into key insights.

## Key Features
- Converts podcast audio and articles into structured summaries using Whisper and DeepSeek
- Supports RSS feeds for automated batch processing of multiple podcasts
- Generates output compatible with local LLM knowledge bases like karpathy-claude-wiki
- Offers configurable summarization modes (short, detailed, bullet points)
- Ensures data privacy by processing content locally without tracking user habits

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the ingestion of podcast and article content into structured knowledge bases, reducing manual note-taking and enabling seamless integration with RAG pipelines.
