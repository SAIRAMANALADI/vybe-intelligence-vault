## Summary
Vibe-Trading is an AI-powered personal trading agent that enables users to automate trading strategies, research, and analysis through a unified platform with one-command setup. It integrates broker connectors, quantitative alpha libraries, and research automation tools for end-to-end trading workflows.

## Key Features
- End-to-end trading automation with 10+ broker connectors (IBKR, Robinhood, etc.) and paper/live trading modes
- Alpha Zoo library with 452+ pre-built quantitative alphas and research workflows for hypothesis testing and backtesting
- Research Autopilot for automated hypothesis-to-backtest pipelines, including attribution analysis and Monte Carlo simulations
