## Summary
OpenBioHack is an open-source educational tool designed to act as a first-principles thinking partner for investigating complex health challenges and optimization. It runs within Claude Code, empowering users to explore possible root causes, low-risk experiments, and clinician-discussable hypotheses based on personal health data.

## Key Features
- Generates possible root causes for health issues from user-provided data, framed as hypotheses to discuss with clinicians.
- Recommends low-risk, reversible experiments and confirmatory tests ranked by safety and potential impact.
- Encourages user-driven investigation with detailed reasoning and biology explanations to foster self-learning.
- Includes templates for symptom and experiment logs to track progress over time.
- Designed for iterative refinement, allowing users to refine hypotheses based on ongoing observations.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, user-empowered approach to health investigation that complements clinical expertise, making complex health challenges more navigable for individuals.
