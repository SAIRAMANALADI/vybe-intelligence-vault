## Summary
Career-Ops is an AI-powered job search automation system that transforms any AI coding CLI into a full job search command center. It evaluates job listings, generates tailored resumes and cover letters, scans company portals, and tracks applications with an AI-driven scoring system.

## Key Features
- AI-driven job evaluation with A-F scoring system (10 weighted dimensions)
- Automated generation of ATS-optimized, tailored PDF resumes and cover letters
- Multi-portal scanning (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, company pages) with batch processing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the job search process by automating resume and cover letter generation while providing AI-powered evaluation of job listings, saving time and improving application quality for AI engineers.
