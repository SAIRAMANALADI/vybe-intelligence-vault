## Summary
A digital full-time equivalent (FTE) AI employee system that autonomously manages personal and business operations 24/7, synchronizing between a cloud VM and a local machine via a shared Obsidian vault. It handles email, social media, invoicing, and approvals with human-in-the-loop governance.

## Key Features
- 24/7 autonomous monitoring of emails, messages, and social media
- AI-driven drafting of replies, invoices, and social posts
- Bi-directional synchronization between cloud and local systems via Git
- Human approval workflow for all sensitive actions
- Comprehensive audit logging and weekly CEO briefings

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This orchestrator system provides a scalable framework for integrating AI agents with human oversight, enabling autonomous business operations while maintaining strict governance and auditability.
