## Summary
A local-first A-share shortline trading ledger with a public, anonymized watchlist that refreshes daily at 18:00 China time. It provides a SQLite-based trading journal, automated watchlist updates, and backtest dashboards for short-term stock trading strategies.

## Key Features
- Publicly refreshed anonymized watchlist updated daily at 18:00 China time
- SQLite-based local trading ledger for tracking positions, key levels, and reviews
- Automated rule-based candidate discovery and removal for watchlist maintenance
- Strategy backtesting and performance dashboards with raw data exports
- Bilingual documentation (Chinese and English) for accessibility

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
