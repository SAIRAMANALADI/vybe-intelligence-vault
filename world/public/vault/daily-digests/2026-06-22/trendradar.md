## Summary
TrendRadar is an AI-driven public opinion and trend monitoring tool that aggregates hot topics from multiple platforms, filters content via AI, and delivers personalized news digests through various notification channels like RSS, email, and messaging apps.

## Key Features
- Aggregates and filters trending news from multiple platforms using AI to reduce noise and focus on relevant content.
- Supports multi-channel notifications (RSS, email, Slack, Telegram, WeChat, Feishu, DingTalk, etc.) with customizable formats.
- Offers MCP integration for AI-driven natural language analysis, sentiment insights, and trend prediction.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
TrendRadar streamlines AI-driven trend analysis by automating data aggregation, filtering, and delivery, reducing manual effort for RAG builders.
