## Summary
slide-maker converts research papers, code repositories, documents, or topics into fully editable native PowerPoint (PPTX) presentations with source-traced content, independent critic reviews, and multi-format layout support. It emphasizes accuracy, editability, and design intelligence over rapid generation.

## Key Features
- Converts papers, repos, or topics into native editable PPTX with source-traced content to prevent fabricated data
- Includes independent critic review to validate slide accuracy, design, and layout before finalization
- Supports multi-format layouts (16:9, 4:3, 1:1, A4, etc.) with adaptive design rules for each
- Uses specialized agents to plan story structure, design slides, and generate real PowerPoint objects (charts, equations, etc.)
- Provides template gallery with 12+ pre-built decks for common use cases (pitches, research talks, corporate training)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI-generated presentations are accurate, editable, and professionally designed, reducing manual post-processing for RAG builders who need reliable, source-backed visuals.
