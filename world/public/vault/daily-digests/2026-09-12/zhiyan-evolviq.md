## Summary
An open-source AI-native industrial agent platform designed for electronics manufacturing and semiconductors, featuring 20 pre-built agents, multi-agent orchestration, and self-learning capabilities for enterprise decision-making and shop-floor operations.

## Key Features
- 20 pre-built industrial agents covering supply chain, R&D, manufacturing, and enterprise decision-making
- Multi-agent orchestration with 8 preset collaboration templates for complex goals
- Self-learning and self-evolution loops with human feedback integration
- Cross-agent knowledge graph with Neo4j and in-memory fallback
- Graceful degradation for all external dependencies (PostgreSQL, Neo4j, industrial protocols)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a production-ready framework for building and deploying specialized industrial agents with built-in orchestration, memory, and self-improvement capabilities, critical for scaling RAG systems in manufacturing and enterprise environments.
