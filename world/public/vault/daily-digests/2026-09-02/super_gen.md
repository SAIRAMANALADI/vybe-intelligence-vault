## Summary
SceneGen is a professional AI-powered short drama generation platform that automates the entire production pipeline from script import to final video output. It integrates AI-driven scene parsing, multi-model video generation, and an online video editor for seamless content creation.

## Key Features
- End-to-end AI-driven short drama generation from script to final video
- Multi-modal reference generation (images, videos, audio) for video synthesis
- Interactive canvas node orchestration for video composition
- Online multi-track video editor with subtitle burning and frame-by-frame preview
- Team collaboration, quota management, and enterprise asset library

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a unified platform for AI-driven video content creation, reducing manual effort in script parsing, scene generation, and video editing for RAG and AI stack builders.
