## Summary
Sleepwalker provides AI Visibility and Content Intelligence tools to help teams monitor how AI systems reference their brand and identify content gaps. It enables running prompts across multiple AI platforms, capturing answers and citations, and scoring content quality with results accessible via CLI, API, MCP, or a hosted app.

## Key Features
- Run AI search checks across multiple platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini) and capture full answers with citations
- Content Intelligence for scoring page depth, freshness, and providing actionable recommendations
- Multi-access workflows via CLI, API, MCP, and hosted app with unified result storage
- Free local page export (`okf export`) for agent-ready markdown without requiring an account
- Pay-as-you-go credit system for visibility checks and content scoring with unmetered reads and status polling

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Sleepwalker enables RAG builders to monitor AI-generated content about their brand, identify missing or outdated information, and ensure their knowledge base aligns with AI search results across platforms.
