## Summary
Styx is a personal transaction data store that captures and enriches transaction data from email confirmations, categorizes merchants, and stores queryable purchase history with deduplication and time-range filtering capabilities.

## Key Features
- Extracts transaction data from email confirmations
- Enriches merchants with category and location details
- Stores deduplicated and queryable purchase history
- Supports time-range filtering for transactions
- Automated categorization of transactions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
