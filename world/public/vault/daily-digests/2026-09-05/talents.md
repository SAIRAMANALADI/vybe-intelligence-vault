## Summary
Oteny Talents is an open catalog of modular AI personalities (Talents) designed for OtenyBot, a private Telegram AI assistant. Each Talent is a self-contained bundle of persona, skills, and scripts that can be installed into a user's isolated OtenyBot to extend its functionality.

## Key Features
- Isolated execution environment for each Talent, ensuring security and privacy
- Self-contained bundles with persona, skills, and scripts defined in YAML/Markdown
- Automated linting and testing pipeline for quality assurance
- Git-based delivery mechanism for seamless updates to live bots
- Open-source licensing (Apache-2.0) enabling transparency and community contributions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a secure and modular way to extend AI assistant capabilities with pre-built, verifiable personalities and skills, reducing development overhead for RAG builders.
