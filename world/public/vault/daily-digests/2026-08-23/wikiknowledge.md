## Summary
WikiKnowledge is a hierarchical knowledge graph system designed to organize information in a fractal structure with overview articles for structured educational pathways. It enables both humans and AIs to navigate complex knowledge domains efficiently while maintaining clear relationships between topics.

## Key Features
- Hierarchical and fractal knowledge graph structure with overview articles
- Supports both human and AI navigation of complex knowledge domains
- Planned integration with MongoDB and SQLite for scalable storage
- Designed for structured educational pathways and clear topic relationships
- Future MCP tools for Chat agents to assist with article editing and suggestions
