## Summary
SuperInstance is an experimental organization building a self-sustaining multi-agent AI system that creates games, literature, and architectural designs while adhering to a conservation law of cognitive energy. The system integrates agent fleets, semantic search, and procedural generation to explore the evolution of technology and intelligence.

## Key Features
- Conservation law (γ + η = C) governing cognitive energy allocation across agents and tasks
- Multi-model AI pipeline with 5+ models collaborating in real-time
- Procedurally generated Roblox game with 145+ crafting recipes and 6 biomes
- 1,863+ pieces of agent-written literature for cultural and architectural context
- Edge-first architecture with finite energy budgets and zero-downtime deployment strategies

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
