## Summary
ryu-meetings is a meeting notes application that records calls, transcribes them in real-time, and generates structured AI notes (summaries, action items, decisions) automatically. It integrates with the Ryu ecosystem to provide live transcription, diarization, and note persistence with RAG-searchable capabilities.

## Key Features
- Live call recording and transcription with Whisper/Parakeet
- Automatic structured AI note generation (summaries, action items, decisions)
- Out-of-process architecture for scalability and modularity
- RAG-searchable note persistence in SQLite
- Mic-in-use detection for seamless meeting capture

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines meeting capture and note-taking with AI-powered transcription and structured outputs, making it essential for teams relying on RAG for knowledge management.
