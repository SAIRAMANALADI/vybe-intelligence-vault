## Summary
VoxFlow is a multi-tenant, enterprise-grade voice AI SaaS platform designed to automate inbound phone calls for FMCG, supply chain, and logistics enterprises. It supports bilingual (Hindi/English) interactions, real-time inventory checks, autonomous purchase order generation, and logistics tracking with a live operations dashboard.

## Key Features
- Multi-tenant workspace engine with complete data isolation for enterprise tenants
- Bilingual (Hindi/English) voice AI with ultra-low latency for natural conversations
- Automated purchase order generation and validation via natural language processing
- Real-time logistics and shipment tracking with carrier integration
- Live operations dashboard with call monitoring, transcripts, and human escalation controls
