## Summary
Shojiku is a deterministic PDF generation engine that converts YAML-based templates and data into business documents like invoices, receipts, and forms. It supports AI agents, humans, and CI/CD pipelines with a local-first, multi-platform approach.

## Key Features
- Human and AI-readable YAML templates for document layout and data binding
- Multi-platform support (CLI, Docker, WASM, MCP server, and SDKs for 7 languages)
- Deterministic rendering with locale-aware formatting (currency, dates, tax rules)
- Built-in electronic signature and verification pipeline
- Extensive examples and presets for rapid template authoring

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Shojiku enables AI agents to autonomously generate, validate, and iterate on business documents in YAML, reducing manual intervention and ensuring consistency across diverse locales and formats.
