## Summary
ScreenBlock MCP is a server-client system that enables AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT to enforce real-time app blocking and screen time management on iOS, macOS, and via a menu-bar app. It leverages Apple's Screen Time APIs to enforce policies server-side, ensuring compliance without manual intervention.

## Key Features
- Real-time app blocking and unblocking via AI commands (e.g., 'block everything' or 'give me 10 minutes of Snapchat')
- Server-side enforcement with Apple's ManagedSettings and APNs pushes for instant re-shielding
- Support for schedules, daily limits, temporary grants, and earned time policies
- Cross-platform enforcement (iOS, macOS) with a unified server and group-based management
- Privacy-first design: server only handles group names and policies, not app identities or content

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ScreenBlock MCP enables AI assistants to directly enforce screen time policies on devices, bridging the gap between AI-driven automation and real-world device control for productivity and digital wellness.
