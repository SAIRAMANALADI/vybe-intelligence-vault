## Summary
A virtualized, high-performance widget for building terminal UIs for AI agents, chat interfaces, and log feeds in Textual. It efficiently renders variable-height entries with O(viewport) complexity, enabling smooth scrolling even with thousands of messages.

## Key Features
- Virtualized rendering for O(viewport) complexity, supporting thousands of entries without performance degradation
- Sticky-bottom follow and infinite scroll-back for seamless user experience
- Per-message state gutter with live spinners and status indicators (running, success, error, cancelled)
- Modular Model-Presenter-Decorator architecture for clean separation of data and presentation
- Built-in minimap for scrollbar replacement and error visibility at a glance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a high-performance, scalable foundation for building AI agent UIs and chat interfaces in the terminal, ensuring smooth scrolling and efficient rendering even with large datasets.
