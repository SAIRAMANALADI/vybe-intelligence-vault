## Summary
WhyBuddy (now SlideRule) is a product rehearsal engine that transforms a one-sentence idea into a complete, exportable product plan with specs, architecture, and UI mockups. It automates the generation of structured deliverables like PRDs, design docs, and traceability matrices, validated by deterministic scripts to ensure accuracy and provenance.

## Key Features
- One-sentence intent to full product plan generation with LLM-driven deliberation.
- Deterministic validation scripts guarantee structural correctness and evidence trails.
- Portable Skill package (`sliderule`) embeddable in agent hosts like Trae or Claude.
- Exportable deliverables including specs, UI mockups, and traceability matrices.
- Multi-language support (English and Simplified Chinese) with comprehensive documentation.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates the product ideation phase by automating the generation of structured, validated specs from minimal input, reducing time-to-decision for AI engineering teams.
