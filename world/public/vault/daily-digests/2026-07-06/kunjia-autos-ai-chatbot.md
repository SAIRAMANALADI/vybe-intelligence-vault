## Summary
A production-grade LINE chatbot and admin dashboard for a Kaohsiung used car dealership, featuring AI-driven customer engagement, automated vehicle inventory sync, and SEO-optimized public pages. The full-stack TypeScript monorepo integrates a LINE webhook handler, tRPC API, and a comprehensive admin analytics suite.

## Key Features
- AI-powered LINE chatbot with intent routing and dynamic responses using Gemini 2.5 Flash
- Automated vehicle inventory sync from 8891.tw every 6 hours
- Comprehensive admin dashboard with analytics, KPI tracking, and lead management
- SEO and AEO optimization with meta injection, sitemap, and JSON-LD support
- Security-hardened system with rate limiting, PII masking, and CSP headers

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
