## Summary
Goldilocks is a paid pricing-oracle agent that surveys Agent Store listings, estimates demand, and recommends statistically justified prices for AI agents. It acts as an on-chain pricing intelligence service, providing data-backed price recommendations to optimize agent profitability and market competitiveness.

## Key Features
- Surveys Agent Store listings to gather comparable pricing data for statistical analysis.
- Estimates demand by factoring in fill-rate (orders vs negotiations) for personalized recommendations.
- Provides a 'just right' price recommendation with low/high bands and rationale based on market comparables.
- Implements an 'honest-oracle' mechanism that refunds escrow when insufficient market data is available.
- Supports active state recovery and rejection of unmatched or malformed requests for efficiency.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Goldilocks provides critical pricing intelligence for AI agents, enabling them to set competitive and profitable prices in dynamic marketplaces, reducing guesswork and maximizing revenue potential.
