## Summary
DramaClaw is an open-source, industrialized pipeline for generating short dramas, ads, product videos, and interactive content from scripts to final film. It automates scriptwriting, storyboard generation, voice-over synthesis, and video composition using AI models via an OpenAI-compatible gateway.

## Key Features
- End-to-end drama production pipeline from script to final film
- Modular architecture with OpenAI-compatible gateway for AI model integration
- Storyboard and first-frame generation with style consistency
- Emotion-aware voice-over synthesis and video composition
- Freezone (infinite canvas) for creative exploration and asset reuse

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DramaClaw democratizes AI-driven video production by providing an open-source, self-hostable pipeline that automates the entire creative process, reducing reliance on proprietary black-box tools.
