## Summary
EduAgent is an open-source RAG-based AI tutoring system designed for K-9 (1-9 grade) students, leveraging large language models and LanceDB vector databases to provide adaptive, localized, and interactive education. It emphasizes guided learning, textbook-aligned responses, and Socratic questioning to foster independent problem-solving.

## Key Features
- Dual-mode tutoring engine (playful for grades 1-3, logical for grades 4-9)
- Textbook-aligned RAG with forced citation of curriculum sources to minimize hallucinations
- Socratic questioning to guide students toward self-solving rather than providing direct answers
- Multi-modal question input (photo upload with OCR for instant step-by-step guidance)
- Automated wrong-answer tracking and adaptive practice worksheet generation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
