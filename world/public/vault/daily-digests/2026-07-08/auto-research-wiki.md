## Summary
Auto Research Wiki is an AI-driven research workspace that integrates knowledge management, paper retrieval, agent workflows, model training, and progress tracking into a unified system. It serves as a knowledge hub for research workflows, connecting literature acquisition, document organization, knowledge graphs, and AI agent collaboration.

## Key Features
- Automated paper retrieval from arXiv, DOI, and multiple sources with fallback mechanisms
- Semantic search and evidence packaging for AI-assisted writing and research
- Knowledge graph construction and multi-hop retrieval for contextual understanding
- Claude Code agent templates for Wiki knowledge loop integration
- Training checkpoint validation, experiment logging, and milestone tracking
