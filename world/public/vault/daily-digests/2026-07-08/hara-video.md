## Summary
hara-video is a terminal-based tool that automates video production workflows, enabling script generation, local TTS, Chinese-first captioning, and rendering via the open-source HyperFrames engine. It integrates with AI agents like hara, Claude Code, or Codex to produce MP4 videos from text descriptions.

## Key Features
- Staged production workflow with quality gates (script approval, editing, rendering)
- Curated video recipes (talking-head shorts, product promos, explainers) with script rules
- Chinese-first captioning with SRT-to-JSON conversion for HyperFrames
- Pluggable asset backends for images and voice (local or API, no vendor lock-in)
- Platform presets for short-form video formats (Douyin, Bilibili, YouTube Shorts, etc.)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines video production for RAG builders by automating script-to-video pipelines with local TTS and Chinese captioning, reducing reliance on external APIs and enabling customizable, open-source workflows.
