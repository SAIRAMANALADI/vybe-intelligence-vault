## Summary
ClozeHive is a wardrobe intelligence platform that combines a digital closet with AI-driven styling, outfit building, travel packing, and wear analytics. It leverages RAG-grounded insights, vector search, and an in-process AI agent (FANI) to deliver personalized fashion recommendations and insights.

## Key Features
- In-process AI agent (FANI) for outfit generation, packing lists, and conversational styling with RAG grounding
- Vision pipeline for garment uploads with background removal and structured metadata extraction
- PostgreSQL + pgvector for vector embeddings and RAG-based retrieval of fashion insights
- Real-time WebSocket notifications for user interactions and AI responses
- Weather-aware travel packing with occasion-specific recommendations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ClozeHive demonstrates a production-ready integration of RAG, vector search, and AI agents in a real-world application, making it a valuable reference for AI engineering stacks focused on personalized recommendations and multimodal data processing.
