## Summary
Quantitative forecasting tool for PBX market decline and VoIP adoption across 12+ countries using time-series analysis (Logistic Growth Model) and survival models (Cox Proportional Hazards). Provides market saturation/death timelines and product survival probabilities based on telecom policy and infrastructure data.

## Key Features
- Logistic Growth Model (S-Curve) for market decline prediction per country
- Cox Proportional Hazards Model for product survival analysis in shrinking markets
- Automated data collection from World Bank, ITU, BEREC, and national telecom regulators
- GitHub Actions workflow for scheduled report generation and asset deployment
- Cloud RAG endpoint integration for evidence-based retrieval (Cloudflare Workers/Hugging Face Spaces)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides critical market intelligence for AI/VoIP stack builders to identify high-potential regions and timeframes for product launches in a rapidly evolving telecom landscape.
