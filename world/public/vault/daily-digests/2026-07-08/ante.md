## Summary
Ante is a personal automated trading infrastructure designed to support AI agents in trading by acting as a safety gate and back-office system. It enables autonomous AI agents to submit trading strategies, validates them, and executes trades while monitoring performance and mitigating risks.

## Key Features
- AI agent integration for autonomous trading strategy submission
- Static validation and backtesting of trading strategies
- Real-time trade execution monitoring and performance tracking
- Risk mitigation through approval gates and safety checks
- Support for KRX (Korean stock market) trading via broker adapters

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Ante provides a critical safety and operational layer for AI-driven trading systems, ensuring strategies are validated and risks are managed before and during execution.
