## Summary
Ansvisor is an open-source AI Visibility Platform designed to help brands track and optimize their presence in AI-generated answers across multiple platforms like ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, and Microsoft Copilot. It provides real-time visibility scores, competitor analysis, and content optimization tools to improve AI search rankings.

## Key Features
- Real-time visibility scores across major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Copilot, etc.)
- Competitor analysis and comparison tools to identify visibility gaps
- AI-generated content briefs and optimization recommendations
- Multi-language and multi-region support (13 languages, 18 regions)
- Self-hosted or cloud deployment with all features unlocked

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Ansvisor provides critical insights into how brands are represented in AI-generated answers, enabling better optimization strategies for AI search rankings and visibility.
