## Summary
Cobsidian is an agent-agnostic workflow skill designed to transform AI conversations, logs, and documents into structured, linked Markdown notes within an Obsidian vault. It ensures knowledge durability by preventing duplicates, suggesting backlinks, and validating note hygiene before writing.

## Key Features
- Agent-agnostic workflow for organizing AI conversations into Obsidian vaults
- Duplicate detection and prevention with dry-run planning
- Automatic wiki link suggestions and backlink validation
- Support for multiple note modes (learning, project, review, comparison, index, daily capture, dissection)
- Local MCP server for read-only vault inspection and dry-run planning

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Cobsidian bridges the gap between transient AI outputs and durable knowledge systems by ensuring AI-generated content is organized, linked, and reusable in Obsidian vaults.
