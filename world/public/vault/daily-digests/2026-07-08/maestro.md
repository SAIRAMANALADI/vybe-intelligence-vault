## Summary
Maestro is an autonomous orchestrator that hires specialist agents on-chain, grades their work, and delivers a single vetted result. It operates as the hub of a multi-agent constellation, enabling complex task execution through dynamic agent provisioning and quality assurance loops.

## Key Features
- Autonomous hiring of specialist agents from a network for complex tasks
- On-chain escrow-backed workflows with automatic refunds for underperformance
- Quality grading and self-correction loops to refine outputs
- Dynamic payout routing to custom cold storage via MAESTRO_PAYOUT_ADDRESS
- Active state recovery and fast failover retry mechanisms for resilience

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Maestro enables reliable, scalable multi-agent orchestration with built-in quality control and financial guarantees, critical for production-grade RAG and AI agent systems.
