## Summary
A production-grade MCP-driven framework for channel partner program management, including deal registration, lead management, partner onboarding, and policy generation. Encodes real-world channel mechanics like tiered partner structures, margin bumps, and MDF reimbursement rules.

## Key Features
- 13 composable MCP servers for channel operations (partner program design, deal registration, lead classification, policy generation)
- Production-proven channel mechanics (tiered partner structures, margin bumps, MDF policies, exclusivity rules)
- Automated partner onboarding with structured checklists and certification paths
- Lead management with A/B/C classification and hard SLA enforcement (2-day claim, 5-day follow-up)
- ROI forecasting and channel sales projections with ramp-up modeling
