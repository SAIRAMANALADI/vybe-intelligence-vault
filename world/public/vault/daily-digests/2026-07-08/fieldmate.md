## Summary
FieldMate is a guided smartphone capture workflow that enables households to collect and submit structured site data (meter boxes, roofs, power poles) in exchange for energy credits. The system bridges gaps in digital energy infrastructure data by leveraging resident participation and MCP-based tooling.

## Key Features
- Guided 3-section capture workflow (meter box, roof, power pole) with validation checks
- Incentivized participation via energy credits for households
- MCP-based server architecture for seamless integration with AI tools
- Browser autofill and minimal manual data entry for user convenience
- Structured data output for solar/EV installers, utilities, and government resilience teams

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
FieldMate enables AI-driven energy infrastructure planning by providing high-quality, resident-collected site data that APIs and smart meters cannot capture, reducing failed site visits and accelerating electrification efforts.
