## Summary
A research platform for discovering and analyzing Y Combinator companies using an autonomous agent that crawls public sources to generate evidence-backed company dossiers with full source traceability. It combines a directory explorer, natural language search, and real-time research capabilities.

## Key Features
- Explore 5,600+ YC companies with advanced filtering and natural language search
- Autonomous research agent that crawls public sources to generate structured company dossiers
- Real-time streaming of research events and live VNC view of browser activity
- Hybrid semantic/lexical search via pgvector for efficient company discovery
- Budget-bounded, cancellable research runs with diminishing-returns stopping rules

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a scalable, evidence-backed research agent for building RAG systems that require up-to-date, structured intelligence on companies and startups.
