## Summary
Fundi is a governed AI runtime designed for the Tokeo CLI framework, enabling typed contracts, guarded tool execution, and full traceability for AI-driven workflows. It bridges AI models with backend systems through explicit pipelines, sandboxes, and policy enforcement.

## Key Features
- Typed message and tool contracts for explicit AI interactions
- Governed pipeline with before/after guards for policy enforcement
- Multiple sandboxing options (in-process, subprocess, WebAssembly)
- Full traceability of every AI run for auditing and debugging
- Support for deterministic mock providers, local micro models (akili), and remote OpenAI-compatible endpoints

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Fundi provides a structured, governed approach to integrating AI models into backend systems, ensuring safety, traceability, and flexibility for RAG builders.
