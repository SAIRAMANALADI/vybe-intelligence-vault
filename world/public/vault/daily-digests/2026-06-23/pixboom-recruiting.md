## Summary
Pixboom Recruiting automates resume screening and candidate sourcing for hiring teams by leveraging AI to parse, filter, and deduplicate resumes from platforms like Liepin, while allowing human reviewers to focus on final evaluations and JD refinement.

## Key Features
- Two-stage resume filtering (coarse-grained hard constraints → fine-grained JD alignment)
- Automated deduplication of candidate profiles to avoid redundancy
- Cross-platform support (Windows/macOS/Linux) with headless browser execution
- Integration with Claude Code and other AI tools via structured skill definitions
- Local-only storage of resumes and candidate data for privacy compliance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the tedious initial stages of recruitment by automating resume parsing and filtering, allowing AI engineers to focus on higher-value tasks like refining job descriptions and evaluating top candidates.
