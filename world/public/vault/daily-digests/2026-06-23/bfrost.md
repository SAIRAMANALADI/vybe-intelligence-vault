## Summary
BFrost is a local-first AI operations platform that enables users to run scheduled research, local assistants, tool-using chats, and worker pipelines entirely on their own machine. It provides a dashboard, scheduler, chat workspace, and a typed Item Bus for building custom AI workflows without relying on hosted services.

## Key Features
- Worker-first architecture where every capability is a modular worker with a standardized contract
- Local dashboard for managing workers, models, jobs, and approvals with no telemetry or remote dependencies
- Typed Item Bus for building and chaining custom AI workflows (e.g., news → research → publish)
- Full chat workspace with projects, artifacts, prompt templates, and document-based assistants
- Supports local models (LM Studio, Ollama) and cloud providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, etc.)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
BFrost provides a self-contained, local-first platform for orchestrating AI workflows, making it ideal for builders who prioritize privacy, control, and modularity in their RAG or agentic systems.
