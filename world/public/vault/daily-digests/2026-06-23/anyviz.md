## Summary
anyviz is a specification and workflow library designed for AI-era data visualization, providing professional-grade chart selection, aesthetic systems, multi-stack rendering, and design consistency for AI tools. It bridges the gap between default-style charts and expert-crafted visualizations with 34 production-ready templates and a five-stage pipeline.

## Key Features
- 34 production-ready chart templates spanning statistical, geospatial, relational, and 3D visualizations
- Five-stage pipeline (Analysis → Aesthetics → Adaptation → Consistency → Accessibility) for end-to-end professional visualization
- Multi-stack rendering (Web, Python, R) with automatic engine selection (D3.js, ECharts, Plotly, etc.)
- Gold-standard aesthetic system rooted in Tufte's data-ink ratio, ColorBrewer, and Viridis color science
- Built-in accessibility compliance (contrast, colorblind-friendly palettes, alt text) and consistency enforcement across multi-chart layouts

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
