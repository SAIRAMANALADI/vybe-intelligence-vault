## Summary
A Python-based tool for adding accurate, frame-aligned captions to videos in any language, including Hinglish, with full offline/local processing. It supports speech, music, and multilingual content, offering forced alignment, vocal isolation, and customizable caption styles.

## Key Features
- 100% local and offline processing with no cloud dependency
- Supports 99 languages for transcription and 1100+ for alignment
- Frame-accurate word timing via forced alignment (WhisperX/MMS)
- Built-in vocal isolation for music and song lyric captioning
- 15+ customizable caption styles and Hinglish/code-switching support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables RAG builders to generate accurate, time-aligned captions for videos or audio in any language without relying on cloud services, ensuring privacy and cost efficiency.
