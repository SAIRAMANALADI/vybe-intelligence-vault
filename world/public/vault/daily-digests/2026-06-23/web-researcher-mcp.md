## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that provides an AI research assistant capable of searching the web, verifying citations, and reading full articles from trusted sources. It enables AI agents to perform grounded, source-cited research across domains like academia, law, medicine, and news.

## Key Features
- Search the web or restrict results to trusted sources via customizable 'search lenses'
- Verify citations and ensure links are real and functional before use
- Read full articles, PDFs, Word docs, YouTube transcripts, and other media types
- Multi-domain support including academic papers, legal documents, patents, and news
- Privacy-focused with local execution and no third-party data exposure

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical layer for RAG systems by ensuring accurate, verifiable citations and trusted sources, eliminating hallucinated or unreliable references in AI-generated outputs.
