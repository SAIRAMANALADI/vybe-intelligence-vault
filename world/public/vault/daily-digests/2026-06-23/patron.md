## Summary
Patron is a zero-cloud, local AI agent designed for Polish law firms, providing a self-hosted desktop application with 6 connectors to Polish and EU legal databases. It includes an audit trail compliant with AI Act Article 12, input security pipelines, and support for multiple AI models.

## Key Features
- Zero-cloud desktop application with local SQLite by default
- 6 MCP connectors for Polish and EU legal databases (SAOS, NSA, ISAP, KRS, EUR-Lex, EU-Compliance)
- Hash-chain audit trail compliant with AI Act Article 12 for record-keeping
- Input and MCP security pipelines for document and connector validation
- Bring-your-own-model support (Gemini, Claude, Ollama, OpenRouter)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Patron provides a compliant, self-hosted AI agent framework tailored for legal workflows, ensuring data privacy and regulatory adherence while enabling secure integration with specialized legal databases.
