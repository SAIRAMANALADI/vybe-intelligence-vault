## Summary
LLMwiki transforms raw research folders into a reviewed, queryable, and self-improving Obsidian wiki for teams. It compiles unstructured notes into structured knowledge with citations, integrates with MCP-compatible AI agents, and enforces review workflows via Git/Gitea.

## Key Features
- Compiles raw research into structured, cited wiki pages with summaries and backlinks
- Integrates with MCP-compatible AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini CLI) for querying and knowledge ingestion
- Enforces review workflows via Git/Gitea for team collaboration and governance
- Supports local project management with Markdown-based issue tracking and Kanban boards
- Provides modular ingest pipelines for web, media, and social platforms (e.g., X/Twitter, YouTube, podcasts)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LLMwiki provides a structured, reviewable, and AI-integrated pipeline for transforming raw research into actionable team knowledge, bridging the gap between unstructured data and reliable RAG systems.
