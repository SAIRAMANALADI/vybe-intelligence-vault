## Summary
Quetzalcoatl is a meaning-first AI project operating system that provides a structured workflow for validating, planning, and executing AI projects from ideation to deployment. It emphasizes rigorous feasibility checks, alternative comparisons, cross-model validation, and systematic documentation to ensure AI-driven projects align with core values and goals.

## Key Features
- Meaning-first validation to prioritize value and feasibility before execution
- GarryTan-style Office Hours for problem framing and pressure testing
- Cross-model validation (e.g., Claude-GPT) to ensure evidence-based decisions
- Structured decision logging, risk registers, and dashboards for transparency
- Autonomous execution within approved scopes with recovery mechanisms
