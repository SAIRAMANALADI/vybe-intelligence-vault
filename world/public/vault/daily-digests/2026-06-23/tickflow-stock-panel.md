## Summary
A self-hosted, zero-maintenance A-share quantitative trading workbench for stock selection, monitoring, and backtesting. Supports custom data integration, AI-generated strategies, and real-time alerts with a modular architecture.

## Key Features
- 20+ built-in Polars-based stock selection strategies with customizable parameters
- Real-time monitoring engine with SSE push notifications and persistent alert records
- Vectorized backtesting engine with T+1 constraints, fees, slippage, and portfolio management
- AI-powered strategy generation via natural language prompts (OpenAI-compatible APIs)
- Modular data pipeline supporting third-party sources (Tushare, CSV/Excel) and DuckDB unified queries

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides a complete, self-hosted toolkit for A-share quantitative analysis, enabling RAG builders to integrate real-time stock data, backtesting, and monitoring capabilities into AI-driven financial applications.
