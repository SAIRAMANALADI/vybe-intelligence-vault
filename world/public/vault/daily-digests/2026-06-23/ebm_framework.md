## Summary
EBM_Framework is an end-to-end Evidence-Based Medicine (EBM) pipeline that automates literature search, cross-verification of studies (anti-hallucination), and GRADE evidence appraisal. It generates structured reports while ensuring data integrity through automated consistency checks and source validation.

## Key Features
- Multi-source literature search across medical databases (Crossref, PubMed, Epistemonikos, etc.)
- Automated cross-verification of studies to prevent hallucinated or non-existent papers
- GRADE evidence appraisal for assessing study reliability and certainty
- Machine-verified consistency checks (arithmetic, data integrity, PRISMA compliance)
- Interactive pipeline with user approvals at critical stages to ensure transparency
