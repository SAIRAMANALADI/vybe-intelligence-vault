## Summary
A machine-parseable YAML specification for GHG Protocol Scope 2 carbon accounting, designed to standardize and automate carbon accounting processes for AI agents. It implements deterministic rules, a knowledge base, and fallback chains to ensure compliance with corporate carbon accounting standards.

## Key Features
- Dual-layer architecture (deterministic rules + semantic knowledge base) for robust carbon accounting
- Machine-executable YAML specifications with JsonLogic and external functions for automated rule evaluation
- Fallback chains for data quality hierarchy and emission factor selection
- Cross-method validation for dual reporting compliance (location-based and market-based methods)
- Comprehensive documentation and integration guides for AI agent developers

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
