## Summary
Vidorq is an open-source AI-powered video editor that integrates with DaVinci Resolve Free to automate video editing workflows. Users describe edits in plain language, and Vidorq executes them by analyzing footage, applying style profiles, and generating cinematic animations.

## Key Features
- Transcript-first AI editing with speech boundary detection
- Customizable style training from user-provided video examples
- Cinematic animations generated from prompts (React/CSS or Fusion templates)
- Supports both cloud-based and local AI models (no subscription required)
- Seamless integration with DaVinci Resolve Free via MCP bridge

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Vidorq bridges the gap between AI-driven automation and professional video editing by enabling users to describe edits in natural language while leveraging DaVinci Resolve's powerful tools.
