## Summary
OmniAgent Casper is a verifiable AI agent for RWA collateral risk decisions on Casper Testnet, providing a replayable trail of agent reasoning, deterministic policy gates, and on-chain decision receipts. It integrates with Casper's blockchain for transparent, auditable AI-driven financial decisions.

## Key Features
- Autonomous agent loop for continuous RWA risk assessment with configurable intervals
- Deterministic policy gates and proposer/critic agent architecture for decision-making
- Native Casper Rust contract for storing and verifying decision receipts on-chain
- Public proof endpoint and verifier script for replayable decision trails without private key exposure
- Integration with x402 payment rail for paid evidence and receipt binding
