## Summary
A collection of self-contained chatbots, widgets, browser extensions, and platform connectors for Vaquill AI, a legal research API. Integrations span multiple platforms (Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, etc.) and include MCP servers for legal data sources like CourtListener and CanLII.

## Key Features
- Pre-built chatbots for Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Microsoft Teams
- Embeddable chat widgets for websites (Next.js and Docker-based)
- Browser extension for Chrome/Edge/Brave
- MCP servers for CourtListener and CanLII legal data sources
- Rate limiting, multi-turn conversations, and structured source citations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides plug-and-play integrations for legal AI workflows, enabling rapid deployment of chatbots and data connectors across multiple platforms while leveraging Vaquill's legal research API.
