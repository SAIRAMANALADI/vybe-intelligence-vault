## Summary
FootyDraft is a Telegram-first AI-powered bot that automates the creation and management of a football-focused Twitter account by ingesting real-time news, generating context-aware tweet drafts, and learning from engagement metrics to refine content over time.

## Key Features
- Real-time news ingestion from multiple sources (RSS, Reddit, Google News, API-Football)
- Hybrid tweet drafting with three personas (pundit, fan, analyst) and automated variants
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard for draft management and analytics
- Automated learning from engagement metrics to refine writing style
- 24/7 deployable on Fly.io with optional Xquik posting and Dropbox backups
