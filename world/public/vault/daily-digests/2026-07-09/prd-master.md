## Summary
PRD Master is a tool that transforms raw ideas into structured, agent-ready Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) to prevent misinterpretation and wasted development effort by AI coding agents.

## Key Features
- Converts vague ideas into structured, agent-ready PRDs with 8 predefined sections
- Includes operational mode detection (Personal vs. Commercial projects) and confidence scoring
- Real-time streaming PRD generation with live feedback and error flagging
- Agent-optimized output format compatible with tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and Windsurf
- Part of The Vibe Stack, a production-ready toolkit for frugal developers

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PRD Master eliminates ambiguity in AI-driven development by generating structured specifications that ensure agents build the right product the first time, saving time and tokens.
