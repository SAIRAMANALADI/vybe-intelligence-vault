## Summary
A full-stack autonomous AI agent platform that operates as a first-class entity with its own identity, credentials, and knowledge base. It integrates local-first execution, code-driven workflows, and mandatory reasoning for every action, enabling transparent and auditable AI decision-making.

## Key Features
- Autonomous AI entity with its own identity, credentials, and permissions, not a proxy for users
- Mandatory reasoning for every action, ensuring full audit trails and debuggability
- Code-first workflows with n8n, where logic is version-controlled in Git and auto-generated on startup
- Epistemic knowledge base with confidence levels, temporal validity, and contradiction handling
- Local-first deployment with optional local LLM support (llama.cpp, Qwen3.5) and GPU-accelerated computer vision

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This framework enables the building of fully transparent, auditable, and autonomous AI agents that can operate independently with their own knowledge and reasoning, critical for trustworthy and explainable RAG systems.
