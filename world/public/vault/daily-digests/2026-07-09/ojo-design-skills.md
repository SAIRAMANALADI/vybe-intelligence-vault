## Summary
OJO Design Skills provides reusable UI/UX design methodologies and guardrails for AI coding agents, enabling them to generate brand-driven, anti-slop design outputs. It includes two design tracks (Convention and Innovation) and enforces strict quality standards for visual direction, tokens, and components.

## Key Features
- Two-track design methodology (Convention and Innovation) for SaaS and brand-driven products
- Anti-AI-slop guardrails enforcing real imagery and banned design patterns
- Agent-agnostic installer supporting multiple AI coding clients (Codex, Claude Code, ZCode, etc.)
- Code-grade output with atomic Tailwind class strings and 8-state interaction models
- Mandatory style-direction confirmation gate for 2-3 distinct design proposals

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
