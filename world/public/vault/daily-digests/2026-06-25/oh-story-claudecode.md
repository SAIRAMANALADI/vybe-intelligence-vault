## Summary
oh-story-claudecode is a comprehensive skill package for AI-assisted online novel writing, covering full workflows from trend analysis and story breakdown to drafting, AI-flavor removal, and cover generation. It integrates with Claude Code, OpenCode, and OpenClaw to automate and streamline novel creation for both long-form and short-form fiction.

## Key Features
- Automated full workflow from trend scanning to cover generation for novels
- Multi-agent system (7 specialized agents) for collaborative writing and consistency checks
- Structured project organization with modular references, outlines, and tracking files
- AI-flavor removal and style adaptation tools for human-like prose
- Platform-specific market analysis and optimization for major Chinese novel platforms
