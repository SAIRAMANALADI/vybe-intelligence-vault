## Summary
An autonomous AI Executive Assistant named Sadia that automates professional email responses using n8n, Google Gemini, and RAG. It monitors Gmail in real-time, retrieves business data from Google Sheets, and replies in the sender's language with human-like context-aware responses.

## Key Features
- Real-time Gmail monitoring with minute-level polling
- Google Sheets-based RAG system for dynamic knowledge retrieval
- Multilingual response generation (English, Bangla, Banglish)
- Thread-aware memory for coherent long-term conversations
- Automated HTML-formatted email drafting and sending

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a production-ready template for integrating RAG with email automation, demonstrating how to combine workflow orchestration, LLM reasoning, and external knowledge retrieval for practical business applications.
