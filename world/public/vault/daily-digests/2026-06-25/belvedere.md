## Summary
Belvedere is an AI-powered Scrum facilitation agent designed to enhance both ticket quality and ceremony execution in Jira-like PM services. It integrates DevOps and AI to address common Scrum pitfalls such as poor ticket quality, ritualized ceremonies, and lack of strategic alignment.

## Key Features
- AI-driven ticket quality assessment (DoD, SP, User Story validation)
- Specialized AI agents for Scrum ceremonies (Planning, Daily, Refinement, Review, Retrospective)
- Multi-agent orchestration with ADK and A2A protocol
- Firestore-backed vector search for RAG and semantic ticket retrieval
- MCP server integration for direct AI agent ecosystem connectivity

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Belvedere provides a structured, AI-augmented approach to Scrum ceremonies and ticket management, bridging gaps in quality and strategic alignment that plague traditional Agile implementations.
