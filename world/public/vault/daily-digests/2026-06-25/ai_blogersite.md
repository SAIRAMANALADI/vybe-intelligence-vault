## Summary
AI_Blogersite is an autonomous AI blogger system that transforms live signals from world events, tech, sports, and markets into tension-first micro-essays rather than generic news recaps. It operates as a closed-source publishing pipeline with a live site, Telegram channel, and RSS feed, emphasizing explicit fact-inference separation and editorial resilience.

## Key Features
- Continuous live signal collection from diverse sources (world, tech, sports, markets)
- Explicit separation between facts and inferences in generated content
- Dual publishing surfaces: public site for detailed essays and Telegram for teasers
- Resilience-first runtime with fail-fast timeouts, bounded retries, and JSON-safe contracts
- Production-grade documentation including launch checklists, runbooks, and smoke reports

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
