## Summary
A real-time industrial cybersecurity platform that detects ICS cyberattacks and quantifies financial risk exposure for banks and insurers using a 5-agent LangGraph orchestration, Random Forest ML, and structured financial modeling.

## Key Features
- Real-time anomaly detection via Random Forest ML with high accuracy scoring
- 5-stage autonomous agent pipeline (LangGraph) for attack detection, classification, isolation, risk quantification, and reporting
- Financial risk quantification including downtime costs, SLA penalties, regulatory fines, and credit risk flags
- NIST SP 800-61 compliant incident reporting and structured logging
- Live React dashboard with WebSocket telemetry for real-time monitoring and micro-segmentation via MQTT isolation commands

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This platform provides critical financial risk quantification for industrial cyberattacks, enabling banks and insurers to assess exposure and make informed lending decisions based on real-time threat intelligence.
