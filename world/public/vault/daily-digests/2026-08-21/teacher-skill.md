## Summary
teacher-skill is a Windows application that captures a teacher's unique teaching style, personality, and knowledge to generate AI skill files. These files can be used with AI chat systems to provide personalized tutoring that mimics the teacher's approach.

## Key Features
- Captures and extracts a teacher's teaching style, personality, and knowledge from provided documents
- Generates portable AI skill files for use with AI chat systems
- Supports local processing to ensure privacy and data safety
- Allows customization of the teacher's personality traits via a slider
- Works offline for file creation and extraction

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables the creation of highly personalized AI tutors that replicate a specific teacher's methods, enhancing the effectiveness of RAG systems for educational applications.
