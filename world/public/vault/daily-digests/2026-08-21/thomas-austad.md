## Summary
A personal-use AI career platform MVP designed to assist with job applications through evidence-grounded candidate profiling, job matching, compensation estimation, and resume/cover-letter tailoring. It integrates with job platforms like Greenhouse and Lever and provides MCP tooling for ChatGPT integration.

## Key Features
- Evidence-grounded candidate profile agent for personalized insights
- Multi-factor job-match scoring with compensation estimation
- Integration with Greenhouse and Lever job platforms
- MCP tool surface for seamless ChatGPT interaction
- DOCX resume export and cover-letter tailoring with approval workflows
