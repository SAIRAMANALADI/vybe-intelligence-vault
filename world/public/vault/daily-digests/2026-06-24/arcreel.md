## Summary
ArcReel is an open-source AI-driven video generation workspace that automates the entire pipeline from novel to short video using multi-agent orchestration. It supports multi-vendor image and video generation, role consistency tracking, and project versioning with a web-based visual interface.

## Key Features
- End-to-end AI-driven video creation pipeline from novel to final video
- Multi-agent architecture with Skill + Subagent orchestration for robust task handling
- Multi-vendor support for image, video, and text generation (Gemini, Grok, OpenAI, Vidu, 火山方舟)
- Role consistency and clue tracking for visual coherence across scenes
- Web-based visual workspace with real-time task monitoring and version history

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ArcReel streamlines the complex RAG pipeline for video generation by automating multi-step creative workflows with AI agents, reducing manual intervention and enabling scalable, consistent content production.
