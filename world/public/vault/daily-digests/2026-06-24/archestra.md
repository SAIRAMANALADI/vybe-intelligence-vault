## Summary
Archestra.AI is an all-in-one enterprise AI platform designed to simplify the deployment and management of AI agents, MCP servers, and RAG systems with built-in governance, security, observability, and cost tracking features.

## Key Features
- Unified platform for LLM and MCP access with multi-provider support (Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure, etc.)
- Built-in guardrails, dual LLM checks, and deterministic security policies for safe AI operations
- Kubernetes-native deployment with Helm charts and Terraform provider for scalable production environments
- Comprehensive observability with OpenTelemetry traces, Prometheus metrics, and per-team cost tracking
- Private MCP registry, agent runtime with sub-agent delegation, and reusable agent skills for modular AI workflows

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Archestra.AI provides a production-ready, enterprise-grade orchestrator that simplifies the integration and governance of RAG systems, MCP servers, and AI agents, reducing complexity and risk for AI stack builders.
