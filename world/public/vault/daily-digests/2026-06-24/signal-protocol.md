## Summary
A production-ready static data API providing South Korea market intelligence, including search trends, crypto premiums (Kimchi Premium), and KRW exchange rates. Monetized via RapidAPI with automated discovery surfaces for AI agents.

## Key Features
- Hourly-updated Korean search trends with traffic labels and related news
- Real-time Kimchi Premium tracking for major cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE)
- KRW exchange rates for 10 major currencies updated hourly
- Automated AI-friendly discovery surfaces (llms.txt, AI manifest, catalog.json)
- Monetized via RapidAPI with no-touch distribution and passive discovery
