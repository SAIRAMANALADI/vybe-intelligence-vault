## Summary
SeekMoney-ai is a desktop application that analyzes social media trends across platforms like TikTok, Douyin, and YouTube to identify business opportunities. It uses AI-driven clustering and scoring to evaluate demand, market size, and competition, helping users discover profitable niches.

## Key Features
- Multi-platform social media data aggregation (TikTok, Douyin, YouTube, etc.)
- Semantic clustering for grouping similar content and identifying trends
- Opportunity scoring based on demand, market size, and competition
- Customizable settings for platform selection, date ranges, and scoring weights
- Exportable reports in CSV or PDF formats
