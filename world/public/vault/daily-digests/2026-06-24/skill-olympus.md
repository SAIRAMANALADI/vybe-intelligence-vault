## Summary
Skill Olympus is a production-grade agent harness and loop-engineering stack for Claude Code, Codex CLI, and Gemini CLI, enabling autonomous SaaS development through a pantheon of 12 specialized agents. It automates the entire pipeline from design to deployment with zero human intervention.

## Key Features
- Twelve specialized agents (e.g., Zeus, Chronos, Minos) for autonomous design, build, test, and deployment
- Persistent 3-layer memory (Mnemo) across sessions and CLIs for context retention
- Autonomous loop-engineering with act-observe-verify cycles until completion criteria are met
- Cross-CLI compatibility (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI) with seamless integration
- Pre-launch quality gates, rollback playbooks, and decision records for production-grade reliability
