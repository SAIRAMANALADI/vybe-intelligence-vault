## Summary
FinanceOS is an open-source, AI-powered personal finance command center designed for planning, portfolio analysis, and advisor workflows. It offers direct indexing, tax-loss harvesting, retirement planning, and equity research in a self-hosted platform with AI-assisted tools for decision-making.

## Key Features
- Direct-index portfolio simulation with tax-loss harvesting and backtesting
- Retirement income modeling with tax-aware withdrawal sequencing
- AI-powered recommendation agent for equity research and trading ideas
- Advisor workspace for taxable account transition planning and CSV exports
- SEC 13F manager research and copycat portfolio performance simulation
