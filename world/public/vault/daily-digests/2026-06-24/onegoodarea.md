## Summary
OneGoodArea provides a deterministic UK property intelligence layer with configurable scoring and portfolio monitoring for regulated B2B workflows like mortgage lending and insurance. It offers a typed AI query plane over monthly area time-series data, enabling model risk compliance for financial institutions.

## Key Features
- Deterministic signals and configurable scoring for UK property data
- Portfolio monitoring with version-pinned methodology and confidence per dimension
- Typed AI query plane over monthly area time-series data
- Regulated workflows support for mortgage lenders, insurers, and PropTech
- Comprehensive documentation and ADR-driven decision trail
