## Summary
Hermes Health Apollo is a local-first desktop application that integrates wearable health device data (e.g., Oura ring) with Google Calendar to provide personalized health insights, schedule optimization, and privacy-focused data management for Windows users.

## Key Features
- Syncs Oura ring health metrics (sleep, activity, readiness) with Google Calendar
- Provides offline access to health and calendar data after initial sync
- Suggests breaks between meetings based on readiness scores
- Encrypts local health data storage for privacy compliance
- Exports health data to CSV for professional analysis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to access structured health and schedule data locally, enhancing personalized recommendations while preserving user privacy.
