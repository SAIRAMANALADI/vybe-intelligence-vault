## Summary
EvoMind PDF Intelligence is an autonomous research agent that ingests PDFs, generates its own research questions, solves them with grounded evidence and citations, reflects on answers to spawn deeper questions, and builds a living knowledge graph. It operates as a continuous research loop, synthesizing insights across documents and tracking an evolving intelligence score.

## Key Features
- Autonomous research loop with 9 question categories per document
- Hybrid retrieval with Qdrant and BM25 for grounded answers and citations
- Recursive curiosity generating follow-up questions up to configurable depth
- Cross-document synthesis, hypothesis generation, and contradiction detection
- Real-time SSE research feed and evolving intelligence score with persistent memory

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
EvoMind automates the entire RAG pipeline by autonomously generating, solving, and synthesizing research questions from PDFs, making it essential for building self-improving knowledge systems.
