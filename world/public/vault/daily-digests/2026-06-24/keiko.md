## Summary
Keiko is a governed agentic workspace for knowledge work that enables regulated engineering teams to inspect repositories, generate reviewable unit tests, investigate bugs, and maintain audit trails through local-first, human-controlled workflows.

## Key Features
- Human-controlled agentic workflows with explicit local actions and audit trails
- Encrypted at-rest memory (AES-256-GCM) with semantic recall and governance rules
- Quality Intelligence for evidence-backed test generation and requirement traceability
- Figma design connector for design-to-tests and accessibility baselines
- Governed delegation with configurable control layers and policy enforcement
