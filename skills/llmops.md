# LLMOps

## What It Is

Large Language Model Operations (LLMOps) encompasses the practices, techniques, and tools used to deploy, monitor, evaluate, and manage LLMs in production environments.

## Why It Matters

LLMOps ensures the reliability, efficiency, performance, and security of production AI systems through automated testing, cost tracking, latency tuning, and drift monitoring.

## Core Topics

- LLM Tracking, Tracing, and Logging
- Evaluation Metrics (Accuracy, Hallucinations, Cost, Latency)
- Model Fine-Tuning and Distillation Pipelines
- Model Serving Engines (vLLM, Ollama, TGI)
- Guardrails, Safety Filters, and Output Scanners
