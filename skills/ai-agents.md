# AI Agents

## What It Is

Autonomous software entities powered by large language models that can perceive environments, make decisions, use tools, and collaborate to achieve specific goals.

## Why It Matters

AI agents represent the transition from passive text generation to active, goal-driven automation, enabling complex multi-step reasoning and action pipelines.

- Agent Architectures and Design Patterns
