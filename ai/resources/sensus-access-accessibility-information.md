# Sensus Access – Accessibility Information

## Summary

- **SensusAccess** enables Cornell affiliates to auto-convert documents (PDFs, PPTs, images) into accessible formats (MP3, DAISY, EPUB, Braille) via NetID authentication.
- **Free service** for students, faculty, staff, and alumni, but does not replace official disability accommodations or comply with copyright laws for unauthorized conversions.
- Supports batch processing of image-only PDFs and PowerPoint files into text-based or audio formats for improved accessibility.
