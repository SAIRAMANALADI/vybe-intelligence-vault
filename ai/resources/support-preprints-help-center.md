# Support : Preprints Help Center

## Summary

- **Preprint Removal & Withdrawal**: Research Square allows preprint withdrawal via account settings or direct support request, with removal processed within 24-48 hours; dashboard submissions can also be deleted from the user account.

- **In Review Opt-Out**: Users can disable the "In Review" feature in their Research Square account settings to prevent preprints from being submitted to partner journals automatically.

- **Preprint Versioning**: Updated versions of preprints are submitted via the dashboard, replacing prior versions while maintaining a version history accessible to readers and reviewers.
