# Self-Query / View Your Report Sign In

## Summary

- **Personal Account Requirement**: Healthcare professionals must create a personal account via [NPDB portal](https://www.npdb.hrsa.gov/pract/aboutPersonalAccount.jsp) to access Self-Query ordering, view reports, and manage NPDB data, with identity verified through **ID.me**.
- **Profile Matching & Subscriptions**: Upon profile completion, NPDB auto-matches reports to the user; subscribers receive **certified Self-Query responses** for compliance tracking.
- **Organizational Access**: Executives/owners can request **Self-Query** or **view reports** for their organization via ID.me authentication, requiring letter details if a notification was received.
