# U.S. Copyright Office | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **Group Registration of Two-Dimensional Artwork (GR2D)**: Introduces a new registration option for multiple two-dimensional artworks under a single claim, streamlining the process for artists and creators.

- **Copyright Claims Board (CCB)**: Provides an alternative dispute resolution mechanism for copyright claims up to $30,000, offering a less expensive and faster process compared to federal court litigation.

- **Copyright Public Records System (CPRS)**: Offers advanced search capabilities, filters, and improved interfaces for accessing copyright registration and recordation data in a public, searchable database.
