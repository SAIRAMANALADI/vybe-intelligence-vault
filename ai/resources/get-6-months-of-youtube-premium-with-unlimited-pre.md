# Get 6 months of YouTube Premium with Unlimited Premium - Google Fi Wireless Help

## Summary

- **Eligibility & Activation**: YouTube Premium is available for 6 months at no extra cost with Google Fi’s Unlimited Plus plan; requires active participation in the plan for the full duration.
- **Management & Renewal**: After 6 months, YouTube Premium auto-renews at $13.99/month via Google Fi; only the Google Fi group manager can control renewal or cancellation for all members.
- **Plan Switching & Family Plans**: Switching from Unlimited Plus to other plans or pausing the plan terminates YouTube Premium; family plans require separate cancellation of existing subscriptions before transitioning.
