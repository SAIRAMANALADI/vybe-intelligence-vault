# Learn More About Google's Secure and Protected Accounts - Google

## Summary

- **Cross-service integration**: Google Account synchronizes data (e.g., Gmail flight confirmations) across Calendar, Maps, and other services to streamline scheduling and location-based tasks automatically.

- **Unified security & privacy controls**: Built-in tools like Security Checkup, Google Password Manager, and spam filters (99.9% blocking rate) provide centralized protection with granular on/off toggles for data retention and privacy settings.

- **Cross-device personalization**: Autofill (passwords, addresses, payments), YouTube progress sync, and third-party app sign-ins ensure a consistent, device-agnostic experience with instant access to preferences and controls via profile management.
