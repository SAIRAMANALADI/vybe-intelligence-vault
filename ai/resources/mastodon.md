# mastodon

## Summary

- **Bridgy** is an open-source service that bridges social networks with the **IndieWeb**, enabling interactions like replies, likes, and reposts to flow back to personal websites via **Webmention**.
- It supports **POSSE (Publish Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere)** by allowing users to post content on platforms like Twitter or Mastodon while receiving responses on their own site.
- Uses **OAuth** for authentication with major platforms (e.g., Twitter, Mastodon) and **Webmention** to notify personal domains of interactions.
