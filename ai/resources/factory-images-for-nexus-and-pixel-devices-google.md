# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but **erase all data** and require **bootloader unlocking**, increasing security risks.
- **May 2025/2026 updates** for Pixel 6/8/10 series increment bootloader anti-rollback, preventing rollback to older builds; flashing requires updating both slots to avoid unbootable states.
- **OTA images are preferred** for safer updates (no data wipe), but factory images are provided for custom build restoration with mandatory **bootloader relocking** post-flash.
