# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but erase all data; OTA images are safer alternatives.
- May 2025/2026 Pixel updates include bootloader anti-rollback increments requiring post-update bootloader flashing to inactive slot.
- Unlocking bootloader reduces security; relock after factory image use; OTA sideloading avoids data wipe.
