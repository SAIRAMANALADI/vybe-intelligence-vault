# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but **erase all data** and require **bootloader unlocking**, making them less secure; **full OTA images** are safer alternatives.
- **May 2025/2026 updates** for Pixel 6/8/10 series increment **anti-rollback bootloader versions**, preventing downgrades; post-update, users must **flash the bootloader to the inactive slot** after first successful boot to avoid unbootable states.
- **Seamless updates** use dual slots (active/inactive); flashing incompatible builds can trigger fallback to the inactive slot, which may contain an older bootloader, causing boot failures if anti-rollback is enforced.
