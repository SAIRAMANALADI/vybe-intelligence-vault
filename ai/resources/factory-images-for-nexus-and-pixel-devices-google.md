# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware, requiring bootloader unlock and data wipe; OTA images are safer alternatives.
- May 2025/2026 updates for specific Pixel devices increment bootloader anti-roll back versions, necessitating post-update bootloader flashing to inactive slots to avoid unbootable states.
- Post-flashing, relocking the bootloader is recommended for security; all actions are governed by Google's Terms of Service and device-specific legal terms.
