# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but erase all data and require bootloader unlock, with OTA updates preferred for safety.
- May 2025/2026 Pixel updates (e.g., Pixel 6/8 series) include bootloader anti-rollback increments, risking unbootable states if inactive slots aren't updated post-install.
- Post-update, sideloading the corresponding OTA image ensures both slots are bootable, mitigating rollback-related boot failures.
