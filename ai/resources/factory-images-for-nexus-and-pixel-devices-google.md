# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but require bootloader unlock, data wipe, and relock post-installation.
- May 2025/2026 Pixel updates increment bootloader anti-rollback versions, necessitating manual bootloader flashing to inactive slot to prevent unbootable states.
- OTA images are preferred over factory images due to ease of use and no data loss, with seamless updates utilizing device slots for fallback.
