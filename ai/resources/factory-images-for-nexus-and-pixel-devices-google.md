# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but erase all data; OTA images are safer alternatives.
- May 2025/2026 updates for Pixel 6/8/10 series increment bootloader anti-rollback, requiring post-update bootloader flashing to inactive slot to prevent unbootable states.
- Unlocking bootloader reduces security; relock after factory image use. Data must be backed up as flashing erases device contents.
