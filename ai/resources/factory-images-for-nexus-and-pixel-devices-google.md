# Factory Images for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- Factory images restore Nexus/Pixel devices to original firmware but require bootloader unlock, leading to data loss and security risks; OTA images are safer alternatives.
- May 2025/2026 Pixel updates (e.g., Pixel 6/8 series) include bootloader anti-rollback increments, necessitating post-update bootloader flashing to inactive slots to prevent unbootable states.
- Flashing factory images triggers a full device wipe; users must back up data via Google Account and comply with Google’s Terms of Service and third-party legal terms.
