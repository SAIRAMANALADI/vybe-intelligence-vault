# Justin's Linklog – (Things I found interesting recently.)

## Summary

- **Liability Classification**: German court ruled AI-generated search overviews are Google's own content, making the company directly liable for false claims, rejecting user responsibility for fact-checking.

- **Legal Precedents Established**: Court differentiated AI output from search/autocomplete, asserting AI lacks free expression protections as it generates statements without beliefs, treating it as a commercial product.

- **Broader Implications**: Ruling applies to AI products beyond search, potentially eliminating insurance viability due to mandatory fact-based output (e.g., advertising) and eliminating "report and takedown" protections for AI errors.
