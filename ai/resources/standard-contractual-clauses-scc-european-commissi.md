# Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) - European Commission

## Summary

- **Modernized EU SCCs**: Adopted on 4 June 2021 under GDPR, replacing prior Directive 95/46 clauses, to govern data transfers from EU/EEA controllers/processors to non-EU entities, with dynamic Q&A guidance for compliance.

- **Global Model Clauses**: Jurisdictions like UK, Switzerland, ASEAN, and Council of Europe have developed or endorsed model contractual clauses for cross-border data transfers, aligning with EU SCC principles but with localized adaptations.

- **Joint EU-ASEAN Guidance**: Released a 2023 guide to harmonize EU SCCs and ASEAN MCCs, identifying commonalities and best practices for operationalizing safeguards in dual-jurisdiction compliance scenarios.
