# Office of the University Ombuds – Confidential, Independent, Informal, Impartial

## Summary

- **Confidential Resource**: Cornell University Ombuds provides confidential, impartial, and informal conflict resolution for students, staff, and faculty (excluding Weill Cornell Medicine), adhering to International Ombuds Association (IOA) standards.

- **Access & Limitations**: Appointments available via Zoom, phone, or in-person during business hours; not an emergency service—immediate concerns require Cornell Police contact.

- **Voluntary Compliance**: Users voluntarily agree to IOA ethical principles, including confidentiality, non-recording of meetings, and non-testimony regarding confidential communications.
