# Exoplanets - NASA Science

## Summary

- **Exoplanet Discovery Milestone**: Over **6,000 exoplanets** confirmed (as of 2025), with detection accelerating due to advanced telescopes (e.g., JWST, Roman Space Telescope) and methods like transit photometry and radial velocity spectroscopy.
- **Exoplanet Diversity**: Categorized into **gas giants, Neptunian, super-Earths, and terrestrial** types, with compositions ranging from molten lava worlds to low-density "puffy" planets; some are rogue planets untethered to stars.
- **Habitable Zone Focus**: The **TRAPPIST-1 system** (7 Earth-sized planets, 40 ly away) exemplifies targets in the "Goldilocks zone" where liquid water could exist, a key criterion for potential life. Future missions (e.g., Habitable Worlds Observatory) aim to characterize atmospheres for biosignatures.
