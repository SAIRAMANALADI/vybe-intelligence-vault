# KAIST 전산학부 인턴 및 채용공고 관리시스템 CSCAREER

## Summary

- **Platform Overview**: KAIST School of Computing's **CSCAREER** system connects students with internship/job postings, requiring employer registration (max 3 accounts per business registration number) and administrative review (~3 business days) before posting approval.

- **Access & Application**: Students can view all postings without logging in, while applications must be submitted directly via provided links or emails; employers must re-submit modified postings for re-approval.

- **Current Postings**: System lists **3,471 total postings** (4 active, 3,467 closed), including roles like **Palantir’s Forward Deployed Software Engineer**, **Cisco’s Associate Solutions Engineer**, and **SwiftSight’s Research Scientist** (AI/ML-focused).
