# YouTube Research - Policies

## Summary

- **Eligibility**: Applicants must be students, research-focused staff, or faculty at an accredited higher-education institution with degree-granting authority, excluding any affiliation with Alphabet or Google (e.g., interns, temps, vendors, or consultants).

- **Research Requirements**: Applicants must have a clear research goal, intend to publish findings (non-commercial), and comply with YouTube’s Researcher Program Terms of Service, API Services Terms, and data security/privacy requirements.

- **Publication & IP**: Researchers must publicly disclose findings for peer review and provide YouTube with free access to research based on YouTube data; YouTube retains no editorial control or IP rights over research outcomes.
