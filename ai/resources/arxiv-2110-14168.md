# [2110.14168] Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems

## Summary

- **GSM8K Dataset**: Introduced an 8.5K high-quality, linguistically diverse dataset of grade school math word problems to benchmark multi-step mathematical reasoning in language models.

- **Verification Mechanism**: Proposed training verifier models to evaluate candidate solutions, significantly improving performance by selecting the highest-ranked solution at test time.

- **Scalability Findings**: Demonstrated that verification scales more effectively with increased data compared to fine-tuning baselines, addressing robustness issues in transformer models.
