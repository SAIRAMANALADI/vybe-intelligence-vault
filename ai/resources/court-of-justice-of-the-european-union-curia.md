# Court of Justice of the European Union - curia

## Summary

- **CJEU Clarifies Liability Conditions for Cross-Border Information Society Services**: In *WebGroup Czech Republic* (C-188/24) and *Coyote System* (C-190/24), the Court ruled on Member State restrictions for services from another Member State and the liability of operators for stored/circulated information, emphasizing proportionality and EU fundamental freedoms.

- **EU Solidarity Contribution on Energy Sector Challenged**: Cases *Varo Energy Belgium* (C-358/24), *Vermilion Energy Ireland* (C-533/24), and *Acea Produzione* (C-153/25) examine the legality of the EU’s temporary solidarity contribution imposed during the energy crisis, focusing on its compatibility with internal market rules.

- **CJEU Rules on Vaccination Mandates and Retail Discounts**: In *Ministero della Difesa* (C-522/24), the Court upheld compulsory SARS-CoV-2 vaccination for military personnel as non-discriminatory under EU law, while in *Penny Market* (C-658/24), it struck down Hungary’s mandatory discounts for large retailers as violating EU free movement and competition law.
