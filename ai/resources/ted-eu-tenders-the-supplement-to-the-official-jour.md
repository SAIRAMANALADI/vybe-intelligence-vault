# TED - EU Tenders, the Supplement to the Official Journal - TED

## Summary

- **TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)** is the online version of the 'Supplement to the Official Journal' of the EU, publishing **contract notices** for public procurement across EU member states, accessible via [ted.europa.eu](https://ted.europa.eu).

- **SIMAP (Information System for Public Procurement)** provides **standardized eProcurement tools**, including **eForms**, **European Single Procurement Document (ESPD)**, and an **eProcurement ontology**, facilitating electronic notice submission and reuse of procurement data.

- **TED Developer Portal** ([developer.ted.europa.eu](https://developer.ted.europa.eu)) and **TED Developer Docs** ([docs.ted.europa.eu](https://docs.ted.europa.eu)) offer **APIs, ontologies, and technical resources** for integrating and reusing TED procurement data programmatically.
