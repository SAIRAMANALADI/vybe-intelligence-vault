# Export your account data – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Export Process**: Users can export personal data and stories as HTML files in a `.zip` archive via **Settings > Security and apps > Download your information** on Medium’s web platform or mobile apps (iOS/Android).
- **Confirmation & Delivery**: After initiating the export, users receive a download link via email upon completion of the archive generation.
- **Platform-Specific Steps**: Web users access the feature through the profile picture > **Settings**, while mobile users navigate via the bottom bar profile picture > **Settings > Account > Security and apps**.
