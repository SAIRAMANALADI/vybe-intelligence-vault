# Export your account data – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Data Export Process**: Medium allows users to export personal data and stories as HTML files in a `.zip` archive via the **Security and apps** tab in **Settings**.
- **Desktop/Web Instructions**: Navigate to profile picture → **Settings** → **Security and apps** → **Download your information** → Confirm with **Export**; download link sent via email upon completion.
- **Mobile App Instructions**: Tap profile picture → **Settings** → **Account** → **Security and apps** → **Download your information** → Confirm with **Export**; download link sent via email upon completion.
