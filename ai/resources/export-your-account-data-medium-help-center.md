# Export your account data – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Export Process**: Users can request an export of their Medium account data (including stories) via the **Security and apps** tab in settings, triggering an email-delivered `.zip` archive of HTML files upon completion.
- **Platform-Specific Steps**:
  - **Desktop/Web**: Navigate via profile picture → **Settings** → **Security and apps** → **Download your information** → **Export**.
  - **Mobile (iOS/Android)**: Tap profile picture → **Settings** → **Account** → **Security and apps** → **Download your information** → **Export**.
- **Delivery Method**: Export archives are delivered as download links via email after processing, requiring user confirmation to initiate.
