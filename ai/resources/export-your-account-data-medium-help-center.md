# Export your account data – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Data Export Process**: Medium allows users to export personal data and stories as HTML files in a `.zip` archive via the **Settings > Security and apps > Download your information** option.
- **Desktop & Mobile Steps**: On desktop, access via profile picture → Settings → Security and apps → Download your information. On iOS/Android, tap profile picture → Settings → Account → Security and apps → Download your information.
- **Confirmation & Delivery**: Users must confirm the export request, after which a download link is emailed upon completion.
