# [2106.09685] LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models

## Summary

- **Methodology**: LoRA freezes pre-trained model weights and injects trainable low-rank decomposition matrices into Transformer layers, reducing trainable parameters by 10,000x and GPU memory by 3x compared to full fine-tuning (e.g., GPT-3 175B).

- **Performance**: Achieves on-par or better model quality than full fine-tuning across RoBERTa, DeBERTa, GPT-2, and GPT-3, with higher training throughput and no additional inference latency.

- **Theoretical Insight**: Empirical analysis reveals rank-deficiency in language model adaptation, validating LoRA’s efficacy via low-rank parameter updates.
