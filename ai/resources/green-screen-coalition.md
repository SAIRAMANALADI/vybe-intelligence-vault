# Green Screen Coalition

## Summary

- **Mission & Scope**: Green Screen Coalition funds and supports initiatives at the intersection of climate justice and digital rights, prioritizing marginalized communities disproportionately impacted by environmental harms and digital inequities, particularly in the Global North.

- **Funding Eligibility**: Open to individuals and not-for-profit organizations (including fiscally hosted groups), with a preference for projects centered on climate justice, digital rights advocacy, and public interest technology; multiple applications per entity are permitted but unlikely to receive multiple grants.

- **Definitions & Focus Areas**: Climate justice emphasizes equity for affected communities, while digital rights encompass advocacy for data protection, AI policy, internet governance, and digital security; Indigenous rights are explicitly recognized per UN declarations.
