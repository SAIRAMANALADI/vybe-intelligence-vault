# Transparency Center - Google Product Policies and Policy Areas

## Summary

- **Policy Framework & Enforcement**: Google enforces product-specific policies (e.g., Ads, YouTube, Search) via automated systems and human review, removing 8.3B+ bad ads (2025), 20.7M+ YouTube videos (H2 2025), and 264K+ CSAM-related URLs (H2 2025) for violations.
- **Transparency & Accountability**: Publicly discloses enforcement metrics, government content removal requests, and policy development processes to maintain user trust and regulatory compliance.
- **Developer & Creator Collaboration**: Engages with global developer/creator communities (e.g., Google Play, YouTube) to co-develop scalable, data-driven policies ensuring responsible product usage.
