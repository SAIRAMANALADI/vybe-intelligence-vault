# AP Stylebook Online

## Summary

- **AP Stylebook Online** is a web-based, searchable, and customizable writing/editing reference with real-time updates, email notifications, and specialized topical guides (e.g., AI, criminal justice, self-editing checklists).

- **Subscription models** include named-user (individual logins, custom notes) and concurrent-user (shared IP-based access) licenses, with pricing tiers ranging from $31/year (single user) to $200,655/year (10,000 users + Merriam-Webster).

- **Features** include "Ask the Editor" Q&A archives, mobile optimization, Merriam-Webster dictionary integration (optional), and advanced search across custom/user-generated content.
