# Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowship

## Summary

- **Key Research Domains**: NUS focuses on interdisciplinary research areas including Ageing, Asian Studies, Biomedical Science & Translational Medicine, Finance & Risk Management, Integrative Sustainability Solutions, Maritime, Materials Science, and Smart Nation initiatives.

- **Sustainable Research Infrastructure**: The university supports research through the NUS Sustainable Futures program, which includes specialized hubs like the NUS Energy Solutions Hub (NESH), Initiatives for Climate, Ocean & Nature (ICON), and the National Institute for Marine and Ocean Sciences (NIMOS).

- **Research Governance & Compliance**: NUS enforces research integrity via the Integrated Research Information Management Suite (iRIMS) for project tracking, alongside ethical oversight through the Institutional Review Board (IRB), IACUC for animal research, and compliance training via CITI RCR and HBRA E-Training.
