# Cornell University | Search Pages

## Summary

- Cornell University operates under a **land-grant, sea-grant, and space-grant** institutional framework, integrating research, education, and public engagement across its seven undergraduate colleges and seven graduate divisions.

- The **Cornell Research & Innovation** portal aggregates over 100 interdisciplinary centers and institutes, with the Cornell Technology Licensing (CTL) managing patent filings and technology transfer for ~100 disclosures annually.

- The **Cornell Chronicle** serves as the primary research dissemination platform, publishing ~1,200 articles annually with a focus on high-impact discoveries in fields such as agriculture, engineering, and life sciences.
