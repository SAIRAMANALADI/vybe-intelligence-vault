# Front Page — Free Software Foundation — working together for free software

## Summary

- **Mission & Advocacy**: FSF promotes user freedom in computing, opposing DRM, software patents, and proprietary JavaScript, while certifying hardware under the "Respects Your Freedom" program.
- **Key Initiatives**: Maintains the Free Software Directory, enforces GPL compliance via the Licensing and Compliance Lab, and organizes LibrePlanet activism events.
- **Current Opportunities**: Open nominations for the 2026 Free Software Awards (deadline: July 12) and a US-based Engineering and Certification Manager role.
