# Associated Press Stylebook

## Summary

- **58th Edition Features**: Spiral-bound AP Stylebook (ISBN: 978-0-917360-73-2) includes updated chapters on AI, criminal justice, self-editing checklists, sports, business, punctuation, religion, data journalism, inclusive storytelling, health/science, news values, and polls/surveys.

- **Digital & Tooling Support**: AP Stylebook Online offers searchable, customizable access with Ask the Editor, Topical Guides, and Merriam-Webster integration; AP StyleGuard Pro provides style-checking for Microsoft Office, web browsers, and Google Docs.

- **Learning Resources**: AP provides workshops (e.g., Storytelling for Any Platform, PR Pitching), study guides, and a Spanish-language *Manual de Estilo* for journalists, alongside e-book and Kindle editions for portable access.
