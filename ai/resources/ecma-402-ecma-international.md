# ECMA-402 - Ecma International

## Summary

- **Standard Definition**: ECMA-402 (13th edition, June 2026) specifies the ECMAScript Internationalization API for adapting programs to linguistic and cultural conventions across languages/countries.

- **Governance & Access**: Developed under Ecma RF patent policy by TC39; normative HTML version available, with PDF for printable output.

- **Version History**: Successive annual editions (2012–2026) track evolving ECMAScript i18n support, hosted at `402.ecma-international.org/{version}/`.
