# Free K–12 Curriculum for Digital Fluency | CodeAI

## Summary

- **AI Education Expansion**: Code.org rebranded to CodeAI, shifting focus to teach students AI comprehension, ethical use, and creation, with new curricula (AI Foundations for high school, AI Discoveries for middle school) and tools for educators.

- **Policy & Impact Metrics**: Advocates for AI-integrated education policies across 50 U.S. states and 190 countries, having engaged 3M+ teachers and reached 150M+ students with 2B+ learning hours.

- **Digital Fluency Framework**: Defines digital fluency as understanding AI mechanics, intentional AI direction, critical evaluation of AI outputs, and AI-driven problem-solving, aiming to prepare students for an AI-shaped future.
