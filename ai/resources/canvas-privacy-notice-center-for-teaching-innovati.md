# Canvas Privacy Notice | Center for Teaching Innovation

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Analytics**: Automatically gathers anonymized user metrics (geolocation, navigation paths, device/browser info) via Google Analytics, with no personally identifiable data collected unless voluntarily shared via email.
- **Third-Party Integration Disclosures**: Shares limited data with 20+ third-party tools (e.g., Turnitin, Zoom, Gradescope) integrated into Canvas, each governed by their own privacy policies.
- **GDPR Compliance for EU Residents**: Acknowledges EU GDPR rights (access, correction, erasure, processing restrictions) and provides contact channels (`itservicedesk@cornell.edu`) for exercising these rights.
