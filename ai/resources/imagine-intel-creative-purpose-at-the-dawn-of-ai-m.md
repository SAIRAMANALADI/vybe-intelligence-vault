# Imagine Intel: Creative Purpose at the Dawn of AI - Mozilla Foundation

## Summary

- **Purpose & Scope**: *Imagine Intel* is a Mozilla Foundation zine documenting the 2025 *Imaginative Intelligences Assemblies*, a collaborative experiment with 91 creatives/technologists exploring **creative purpose in the AI era**, framed against "Hollywood's 8 Rules for AI" report.

- **Methodology**: Assemblies used **facilitated dialogue + hands-on modeling** (Creative Assembly approach) across five half-day sessions, synthesizing insights into actionable frameworks for creative workers amid AI disruption.

- **Output & Dissemination**: Free digital zine (500 limited-edition physical copies) featuring **original collage art by Dakarai Akil**, distributed via Mozilla’s *Nothing Personal* platform to advocate for **community-driven, anti-corporate AI cultural futures**.
