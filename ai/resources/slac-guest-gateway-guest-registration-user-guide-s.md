# SLAC Guest Gateway: Guest Registration User Guide | SLAC IT

## Summary

- **Registration Process**: Invited guests must click the "REGISTER HERE" link in the email from *no-reply@slac.stanford.edu*, complete the mobile-friendly registration form in one session (no partial saves), and submit it to trigger an approval email with a QR code to their host.

- **Post-Submission Workflow**: SLAC Security may request additional documentation via an email from *noreply@threatswitch.com*, requiring the guest to complete a form by clicking "View Form" and "Continue," then submitting the requested data for review.

- **Approval & Access**: Upon approval (auto or manual), the guest receives an email with a QR code and visit instructions; the host is notified simultaneously upon initial submission.
