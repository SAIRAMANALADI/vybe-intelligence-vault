# Cornell AI4S Initiative – Artificial Intelligence for Sustainability

## Summary

- **Interdisciplinary AI4S Framework**: CAISI integrates AI research across 16 graduate programs in five Cornell colleges, the Institute for Computational Sustainability, and the Cornell Institute for Digital Agriculture to address sustainability challenges in energy and agri-food systems.

- **NSF Research Traineeship (NRT) Program**: Provides interdisciplinary training combining AI research with practical sustainability applications, equipping students with skills for AI-driven leadership in sustainability.

- **AI4S Visiting Professorship**: Facilitates global collaboration by hosting visiting scholars to conduct joint research, enhance teaching, and mentor students within the CAISI ecosystem.
