# Flag an app or review on Google Play - Computer - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Flagging Mechanism:** Google Play provides a direct reporting system to flag apps, reviews, or developer replies for violations of [Developer Program Policies](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/), illegal content, or fraudulent practices (e.g., incentivized reviews, hate speech, or off-topic promotional content).

- **User Review System:** Public reviews are user-generated, visible to all users, and must adhere to content guidelines (e.g., genuine experiences, no fake reviews, or misleading ratings) to maintain platform integrity.

- **Reporting Workflow:** Flagging involves selecting a reason (e.g., "inappropriate content") via the Google Play app or web interface, while policy violations (e.g., illegal content) require submission through dedicated forms (e.g., [Policy Violation Report](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/contact/policy_violation_report)).
