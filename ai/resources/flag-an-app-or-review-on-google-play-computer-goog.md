# Flag an app or review on Google Play - Computer - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Flagging Mechanism**: Google Play provides a direct reporting system to flag apps or reviews for violations of Developer Program Policies (e.g., fraudulent reviews, illegal content) or inappropriate user/developer comments, triggering internal review for potential enforcement actions.

- **User Reviews**: Publicly visible reviews must adhere to content guidelines (e.g., no fake reviews, hate speech, or off-topic promotional content) to maintain authenticity and inform other users, with enforcement via flagging by the community.

- **Developer Replies**: Developers’ public responses to reviews are subject to the Developer Comment Posting Policy; violations can be reported via a dedicated form linked to the specific comment and developer details.
