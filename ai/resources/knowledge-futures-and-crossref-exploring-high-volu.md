# Knowledge Futures and Crossref: Exploring High-Volume DOI Management

## Summary

- **High-volume DOI management**: Knowledge Futures and Crossref are collaborating to prototype and develop infrastructure for registering and managing DOIs at unprecedented scale and granularity, beyond current tool capabilities, with a $258k investment from Crossref.

- **Cross-domain infrastructure application**: Knowledge Futures is extending its publishing infrastructure expertise beyond PubPub to broader scholarly communication, emphasizing open collaboration and cross-organizational stewardship.

- **Iterative, use-case-driven development**: The project prioritizes rapid prototyping and real-world testing to address technical, UX, operational, and infrastructure challenges in high-volume DOI management.
