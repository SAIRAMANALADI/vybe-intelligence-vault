# Volobsis - IPGP

## Summary

- **Observatories & Networks**: IPGP operates four volcanological and seismological observatories (Guadeloupe, Martinique, Piton de la Fournaise, Mayotte) with permanent instrument networks for real-time monitoring and data acquisition, managed in collaboration with BRGM, IFREMER, and CNRS.

- **Data Distribution & Access**: Geophysical/geochemical data are validated and distributed via the VOLOBSIS portal, with real-time access available; datasets include seismic catalogs, volcano reports, and GNSS data, accessible via DOI-referenced repositories (e.g., `10.18715/guadeloupe.ovsg`).

- **Mission & Alerting**: Primary mission includes volcanic/seismic monitoring, scientific research, and civil protection alerts; observatories process continuous data streams to detect abnormal activity and issue warnings.
