## Summary

- **ADA Compliance**: The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) mandates accessibility standards for public accommodations, employment, and government services to ensure equal opportunity for individuals with disabilities.

- **Legal Framework**: Enforced by the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) and the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC), the ADA includes **Title I** (employment), **Title II** (public entities), **Title III** (public accommodations), and **Title IV** (telecommunications).

- **Regulatory Updates**: The ADA requires compliance with **2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design**, including technical specifications for wheelchair ramps, signage, restrooms, and digital accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA for web content).
