# Accueil - FranceConnect

## Summary

- **Single Sign-On (SSO) Infrastructure**: FranceConnect provides a centralized authentication system enabling French citizens to access multiple online administrative services with a single digital identity (via a unique identifier and password).

- **Decade-Long Deployment**: Launched in 2016, the platform has operated for 10 years (as of 2026), establishing itself as a core component of France’s digital government-to-citizen (G2C) infrastructure.

- **Interoperable Identity Federation**: The system integrates with various government agencies and third-party identity providers (e.g., social security, tax services) using standardized protocols (e.g., OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect) for secure, cross-domain authentication.
