# NASA’s Webb Studies How Planet Survived Death of its Star - NASA Science

## Summary

- **WD 1856 b survival mechanism**: JWST observations confirm a Jupiter-sized exoplanet survived its host star’s red giant phase by migrating inward *after* stellar death, evidenced by residual heating (~260°F) and atmospheric methane signatures detected via transmission spectroscopy (NIRSpec PRISM).

- **Atmospheric characterization**: Webb’s transit data revealed hydrocarbon compounds (likely methane) and sub-micron cloud particles in WD 1856 b’s atmosphere, marking the first atmospheric detection of a planet orbiting a white dwarf.

- **Solar system analog**: The system provides a model for Earth’s fate, demonstrating gas giants may survive post-main-sequence evolution if they avoid engulfment during the red giant phase, with migration occurring *after* stellar death due to dynamical interactions.
