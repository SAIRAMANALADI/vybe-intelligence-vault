- **Voice AI Assistant Capabilities**: Integrates voice dictation (160 WPM vs. 40 WPM typing), contextual Q&A, meeting summarization, and AI-powered podcast generation from text inputs, enabling hands-free document interaction, note-taking, and content synthesis.
