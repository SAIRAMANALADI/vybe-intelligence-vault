## Summary

- YouTube operates under Google LLC, with core functions including content hosting, advertising, and creator monetization, governed by [Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.youtube.com/t/privacy).

- YouTube’s infrastructure supports live streaming (e.g., [NFL Sunday Ticket](https://tv.youtube.com/learn/nflsundayticket)), dynamic ad insertion, and AI-driven content recommendations as outlined in [How YouTube Works](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks).
