# Bridgy Fed

## Summary

- **Cross-Platform Bridging**: Bridgy Fed enables bidirectional interactions (posts, replies, likes, reposts) between IndieWeb sites, the Fediverse (e.g., Mastodon), and Bluesky, synchronizing activity across platforms.

- **Open-Source Implementation**: The service is free, non-commercial, and open-source (GitHub: [snarfed/bridgy-fed](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed)), with community-driven development and issue tracking.

- **User Integration**: Supports Fediverse/Bluesky users linking external sites and IndieWeb users bridging their domains to federated platforms via a login-based setup ([fed.brid.gy/login](https://fed.brid.gy/login)).
