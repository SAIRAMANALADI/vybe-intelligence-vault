# Schmidt Sciences Announces Its Latest Cohort Of AI2050 Fellows

## Summary

- **AI2050 Fellows Program**: Schmidt Sciences' 2025 cohort includes 28 researchers (7 senior, 21 early-career) receiving over $18M to address AI's societal benefits and risks by 2050, focusing on 10 "hard problems" in AI advancement and safety.

- **Fellows' Research Focus**: Senior Fellows (e.g., Aspuru-Guzik, Ganguli, Ho) target specialized AI for chemistry, neuroscience, AGI, human-AI collaboration, and secure code generation; Early Career Fellows (e.g., Adelani, Bunne, Cranmer) explore AI models, interpretability, and cross-disciplinary applications.

- **Program Impact**: Fellows form a global network, publish 100+ papers annually, and develop tools like *Humanity’s Last Exam*; annual gatherings and collaborative funding foster innovation in trustworthy, scalable AI for societal resilience.
