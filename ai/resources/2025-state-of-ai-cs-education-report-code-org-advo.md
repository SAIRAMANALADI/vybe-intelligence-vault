# 2025 State of AI + CS Education Report | Code.org Advocacy Coalition

## Summary

- **State-by-state AI policy analysis**: First-time inclusion of AI education policies in the **State of AI + CS Education** report, tracking AI-specific standards, graduation requirements, and guidance across U.S. states.

- **AI + CS policy rubric criteria**: Defines four key policy metrics—**standards integration**, **school guidance**, **professional development funding**, and **graduation requirements**—with explicit criteria for evaluation and "in progress" status tracking.

- **Interactive data dashboard**: Provides raw, state-level data on AI/CS access, participation, and policy implementation via an **open-access dashboard**, enabling comparative analysis and policy benchmarking.
