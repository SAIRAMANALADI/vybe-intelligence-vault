# Online Safety Prize Challenge (Low-Resource Detection of Harmful Memes with Social Bias) - AI Singapore

## Summary

- **Challenge Objective**: Develop multimodal, multilingual, and zero-shot AI models to classify harmful memes (combining images/text) targeting attributes like ethnicity, race, religion, or gender identity, with a focus on Singapore’s diverse linguistic and cultural context.

- **Technical Requirements**: Teams must submit containerized Docker models for end-to-end classification, handling zero-shot scenarios due to limited labeled datasets, and innovate via data augmentation or synthetic meme generation.

- **Evaluation & Incentives**: Submissions ranked on a public leaderboard; top 5 teams present at ACM Web Conference 2024, with prizes of USD 30K (1st), USD 15K (2nd), and USD 7.5K (3rd) for winners.
