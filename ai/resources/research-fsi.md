## Summary

- **FSI at Stanford** conducts nonpartisan, cross-disciplinary research across governance, security, global health, energy, and international development through specialized centers like the **Center for Democracy, Development and the Rule of Law** and **Stanford Health Policy**.

- **Security research** spans nuclear nonproliferation, military defense, cybersecurity, and biosecurity via the **Center on International Security and Cooperation**, while **Energy & Environment** studies global energy markets and environmental degradation at the **Program on Energy and Sustainable Development**.

- **Regional expertise** includes global scholars like former diplomats Michael McFaul (U.S. ambassador to Russia) and Rose Gottemoeller (NATO Deputy Secretary General), with 193 publications in the last academic year covering topics from nuclear security to poverty reduction.
