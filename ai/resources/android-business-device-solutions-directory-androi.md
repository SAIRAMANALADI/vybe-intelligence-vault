# Android Business Device Solutions Directory - Android Enterprise

## Summary

- **Android Enterprise Management for XR**: Google extends Android Enterprise management capabilities to Android XR (Extended Reality) devices, enabling enterprise-grade control and security for AR/VR deployments.

- **Partner Program & Recommended Devices**: The [Android Enterprise Partner Program](https://androidenterprisepartners.withgoogle.com/) validates partners via Silver/Gold badges, while [Android Enterprise Recommended](https://androidenterprisepartners.withgoogle.com/) certifies devices/solutions meeting elevated enterprise requirements.

- **Solution Categories**: Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM), zero-touch enrollment via resellers, dedicated/knowledge worker device deployment, and Mobility Consulting Services (MCS) for deployment optimization.
