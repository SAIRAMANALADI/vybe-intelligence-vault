# Job-a-Thon

## Summary

- **Quarterly Hiring Challenge**: GeeksforGeeks hosts a quarterly recruitment contest (Job-A-Thon) for both freshers and experienced professionals, simulating real-time hiring rounds and offering potential job opportunities.
- **Contest Structure**: Participants are evaluated on DSA, programming logic, logical reasoning, and quantitative aptitude; top performers (30-50 candidates) are shared with hiring companies based on their criteria.
- **Company & Participant Guidelines**: Companies must request participation early, provide detailed job descriptions, and receive candidate data post-contest; participants must register individually and adhere to anti-fraud policies.
