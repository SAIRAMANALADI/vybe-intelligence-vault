# Job-a-Thon

## Summary

- **Quarterly Hiring Platform**: GeeksforGeeks' Job-A-Thon is a quarterly recruitment contest for both freshers and experienced professionals, simulating real-time hiring rounds to explore job opportunities with participating companies.

- **Participation Guidelines**: Candidates must register for live events; performance influences job prioritization on the GFG Jobs portal. Contests cover DSA, programming logic, logical reasoning, and quantitative aptitude, with strict anti-fraud policies and individual participation only.

- **Company Integration**: Companies must initiate hiring requests early, provide detailed job descriptions (salary, experience, location, roles), and receive candidate shortlists (top 30-50 performers) post-contest. Post-contest, companies receive candidate data in batches with expected updates within 5 working days.
