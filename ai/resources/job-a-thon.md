# Job-a-Thon

## Summary

- **Quarterly hiring platform**: Job-A-Thon by GeeksforGeeks is a quarterly recruitment contest for freshers and experienced professionals, simulating real-time hiring rounds with DSA, programming logic, logical reasoning, and quantitative aptitude assessments.

- **Participation & selection criteria**: Open to freshers, final-year students, and professionals with 0+ years of experience; registration required, but no guaranteed callbacks; rankings prioritize job matches on GFG Jobs portal; companies receive top 30-50 candidate profiles post-contest.

- **Company & contest logistics**: Companies must initiate hiring requests early in the month, provide detailed job descriptions (salary, location, roles), and receive candidate data in batches; contest rules include no late entries, individual participation, and potential disqualification for fraudulent activity.
