# Job-a-Thon

## Summary

- **Quarterly Recruitment Contests**: GeeksforGeeks hosts quarterly hiring challenges (Job-A-Thon) for freshers and experienced professionals, simulating real-time hiring rounds with DSA, programming logic, logical reasoning, and quantitative aptitude assessments.

- **Participation & Selection Criteria**: Individual participation is mandatory; late entries are disqualified. Performance determines eligibility for job matches on the GFG Jobs portal, but does not guarantee direct callbacks. Companies receive top 30-50 candidate profiles post-contest for further shortlisting.

- **Company Onboarding & Post-Contest Process**: Companies must engage early in the month, provide detailed job descriptions (salary, location, roles), and receive candidate data post-contest. Disputes are resolved solely by GeeksforGeeks, and fraudulent actions lead to disqualification.
