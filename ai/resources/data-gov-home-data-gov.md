# Data.gov Home - Data.gov

## Summary

- **Dataset Repository**: Hosts **361,184 datasets** from U.S. government agencies, categorized by popularity, recency, organization, and geospatial attributes, accessible via [catalog.data.gov](https://catalog.data.gov/).

- **Mission & Impact**: Aims to **enhance transparency**, **support decision-making**, **drive innovation**, and **fulfill agency missions** through open data dissemination, with metrics tracking dataset views, downloads, and external engagement.

- **Supporting Resources**: Provides structured guidance via [resources.data.gov](https://resources.data.gov) (tools, governance, case studies) and [strategy.data.gov](https://strategy.data.gov) (annual action plans and progress reports).
