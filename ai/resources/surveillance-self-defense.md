# Surveillance Self-Defense

## Summary

- **Surveillance Self-Defense (SSD)** is an EFF project providing expert guides on online privacy, covering **basics of surveillance**, **tool guides** (e.g., encryption, password managers), and **security scenarios** tailored to specific threats (e.g., protesters, journalists).
- **Technical resources** include **end-to-end encryption deep dives**, **metadata risks**, **phishing mitigation**, **device encryption methods**, and **Tor/VPN usage** for circumvention and anonymity.
- **Multi-language support** (15+ languages) and **scenario-based guides** (e.g., abortion access, human rights defenders) ensure accessibility and threat-specific countermeasures.
