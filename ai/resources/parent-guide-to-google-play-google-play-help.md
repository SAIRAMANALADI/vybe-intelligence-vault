# Parent guide to Google Play - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Content Filtering**: Google Play offers curated sections (e.g., "Kids" for apps/games, "Family" for movies, "Children’s Books") with **Teacher Approved** apps and maturity-rated content to ensure age-appropriate access.
- **Parental Controls**: Features include **purchase approvals**, **screen time limits**, **app blocking**, and **password protection** (mandatory for Kids section purchases) via **Google Family Link** to supervise child accounts.
- **Ad & Purchase Safeguards**: Ads in Kids apps must align with maturity ratings, with reporting options for violations; **content restrictions** block mature downloads, and **Family Library** enables shared access to purchased media among up to 6 family members.
