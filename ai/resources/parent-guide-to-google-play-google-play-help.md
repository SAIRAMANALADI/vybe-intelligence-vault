# Parent guide to Google Play - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Content Filtering**: Google Play offers curated sections like "Kids" (for apps/games), "Family" (for movies/TV), and "Children’s Books" with **Teacher Approved** badges to ensure age-appropriate content.
- **Parental Controls**: Features include **purchase approvals**, **screen time limits**, **app blocking**, and **content restrictions** (e.g., maturity levels) via **Google Family Link** or Play Store settings.
- **Security & Sharing**: **Password protection** for purchases, **Family Library** for shared content (up to 5 members), and **ad reporting** for inappropriate ads in kids' apps.
