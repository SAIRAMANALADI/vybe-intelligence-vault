# Culture | Working at Cornell

## Summary

- **Core Values & Workplace Culture**: Cornell's workplace culture is driven by "educational verve," emphasizing integrity, inclusion, vision, communication, initiative, judgment, and growth, fostering an environment where employees can thrive authentically.

- **Diversity, Inclusion & Sustainability**: Cornell prioritizes diversity of perspective in all roles, an inclusive workplace empowering individuals to contribute fully, and integrates sustainability across policies, procurement, and personal wellbeing.

- **Health Promoting Campus Initiative**: Cornell's Health Promoting Campus initiative focuses on transforming health and sustainability through systemic policies and structures that support holistic wellbeing for faculty, staff, and students.
