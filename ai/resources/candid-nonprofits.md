# Candid Nonprofits

## Summary

- **Open-source nonprofit transparency tool**: Candid Nonprofits (formerly GuideStar) provides a platform for nonprofits to publish detailed financials, governance, and programmatic data in standardized formats (e.g., IRS Form 990, audited financials).

- **Data standardization & APIs**: Aggregates and normalizes nonprofit data from multiple sources (IRS, state filings, self-reported) into a unified schema, enabling programmatic access via RESTful APIs for research and integration.

- **Benchmarking & impact metrics**: Offers comparative analytics (e.g., overhead ratios, program efficiency) and third-party validation (e.g., Platinum Seal of Transparency) to assess nonprofit performance and transparency.
