# Welcome to Oversight.gov | Oversight.gov

## Summary

- **Centralized Repository**: Oversight.gov serves as the official platform for the federal Inspector General (IG) community, consolidating reports, audits, investigations, evaluations, and special reviews from CIGIE-member Offices of Inspectors General (OIGs).

- **Open Recommendations Tracking**: The platform provides a searchable database for tracking unresolved open recommendations where management has not yet implemented final corrective actions.

- **CIGIE Integration**: The site integrates CIGIE’s oversight functions, providing a unified interface for federal integrity and efficiency oversight activities.
