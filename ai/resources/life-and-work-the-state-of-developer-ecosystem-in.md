# Life and work - The State of Developer Ecosystem in 2025

## Summary

- **Job Market & Career Stages**: Junior and senior developers globally perceive job markets as challenging, with career stage influencing challenges; senior roles shift focus from technical to team coordination.

- **Geographic & Workplace Impact**: Context switching is 4x more problematic for Eastern Europe/UK developers than Japan/South Korea; CI/CD delays are 3x higher in India vs. Japan, highlighting infrastructure’s role in productivity.

- **Compensation & Burnout**: Scala developers lead top earners despite low adoption; large companies have nearly double the top earners vs. small firms; burnout rates drop from 40% (1-2 years experience) to 10% (16+ years).
