# How misinformation about archaeology spreads online | UCLA

## Summary

- UCLA’s Fiat Lux seminar *“#Archaeology: The Ancient Near East Goes Viral”* examines how social media platforms amplify pseudoscientific claims about archaeology, using case studies like Göbekli Tepe to dissect viral misinformation mechanics.

- The course integrates critical analysis of hyper-realistic AI-generated content trends, emphasizing discernment between fact and fiction in digital archaeology discourse amid rising synthetic media proliferation.

- Fabian advocates for **critical internet engagement** over avoidance, training students to deconstruct popularized historical narratives by applying rigorous academic frameworks to viral pseudoscientific theories.
