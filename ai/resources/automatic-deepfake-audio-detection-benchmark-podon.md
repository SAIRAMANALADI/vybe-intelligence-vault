# Automatic Deepfake Audio Detection Benchmark - Podonos | Make Your Voice AI Monetizable in Every Language

## Summary

- **Benchmark Design**: Private-label, format-diverse (mp3, m4a, webm, etc.) test set with 50/50 real/fake balance (4,524 clips), using modern TTS systems (ElevenLabs, F5-TTS) and verified via Whisper; labels held private to prevent vendor tuning.

- **Performance Gap**: Resemble AI (98.05% accuracy, 1.4% FNR, 2.5% FPR) and Aurigin AI (96.75% accuracy, 1.5% FPR, 5.0% FNR) lead by >13% over open-source baselines (48–63% accuracy) and commercial competitors like Reality Defender (71.3% accuracy, 53.7% FPR).

- **Latency & Trade-offs**: Resemble/Aurigin AI achieve real-time factor (RTF) 0.33–0.40; Reality Defender fails (RTF 1.52). Choice depends on error tolerance: Resemble for low FNR (voice fraud), Aurigin for low FPR (content moderation).
