## Summary

- **Core Services Data Collection**: Google Workspace for Education core services (e.g., Gmail, Drive, Classroom) collect **customer data** (user-created content) and **service data** (account info, activity logs, device/browser metadata, location, feedback) under strict privacy controls, with **no ads or advertising use** of personal data.

- **Additional Services Data Handling**: Additional services (e.g., YouTube, Search) collect broader data (activity, location, device info) for personalized services and ads, but **K-12 accounts are excluded from personalized ads**; voice data in Read Along is processed locally or temporarily on servers for speech recognition.

- **Data Sharing & Admin Access**: Personal data is shared **only with school admins** (for account management/legal compliance) or **with explicit consent**; users retain control over shared content, though public sharing may persist beyond account deletion.
