# About Posit | The Open-Source Data Science Company

## Summary

- **Open-Source Leadership**: Posit (formerly RStudio) was founded in 2009 with a mission to develop high-quality open-source tools for data science, maintaining a strong commitment to transparency, community collaboration, and long-term knowledge preservation.

- **Public Benefit & B Corp Certification**: Posit operates as a Public Benefit Corporation and Certified B Corp, legally obligated to balance business goals with social, economic, and environmental impact while ensuring accountability and transparency.

- **Key Technological Milestones**: Notable developments include RStudio IDE (2011), Shiny (2012), Tidyverse (2016), Quarto (2022), and Posit AI (2026), alongside Python/Jupyter support (2019) and cloud-based solutions like Posit Connect Cloud (2024).
