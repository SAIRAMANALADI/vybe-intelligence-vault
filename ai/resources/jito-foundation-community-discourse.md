# Jito Foundation - Community Discourse

## Summary

- **Jito DAO Governance Structure**: Forum hosts official DAO announcements, Jito Improvement Proposal (JIP) stages (Ideas, Drafts, Official), and governance delegate discussions with 5, 12, 15, and 37 threads respectively.
- **Proposal Workflow**: JIPs follow a formalized process—informal idea discussion → structured draft submission → moderator-approved official proposal.
- **Technical Infrastructure**: Platform is Discourse-based, requiring JavaScript for full functionality, with additional sections for community guidelines, terms of service, and privacy policy.
