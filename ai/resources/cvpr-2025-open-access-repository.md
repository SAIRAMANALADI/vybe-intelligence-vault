# CVPR 2025 Open Access Repository

## Summary

- **VideoICL Framework**: Proposes a confidence-based iterative in-context learning (ICL) method for out-of-distribution (OOD) video understanding, addressing limitations of traditional fine-tuning and ICL in video-language tasks due to high token length requirements.

- **Key Components**: Introduces a similarity-based example selection strategy to rank relevant demonstrations and an iterative inference mechanism that refines responses by re-selecting examples until high-confidence outputs are achieved.

- **Performance & Validation**: Demonstrates significant OOD performance gains on multiple benchmarks, particularly in domain-specific scenarios, by extending effective context length without additional fine-tuning costs.
