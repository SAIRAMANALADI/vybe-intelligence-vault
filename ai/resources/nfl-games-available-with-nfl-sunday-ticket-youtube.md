# NFL games available with NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **NFL Sunday Ticket** provides out-of-market Sunday afternoon NFL games (Eastern Time) not broadcast locally on CBS, Fox, or NBC, via **YouTube TV add-on** ($82.99/month) or **Primetime Channels** (standalone NFL Sunday Ticket streaming).
- **YouTube TV with NFL Sunday Ticket add-on** includes preseason, regular season, and postseason games, while **Primetime Channels** only streams out-of-market regular season Sunday afternoon games on CBS/Fox.
- **Exclusions**: NFL Sunday Ticket does not cover preseason (except via YouTube TV), postseason, digital-exclusive, locally televised, national, or international games.
