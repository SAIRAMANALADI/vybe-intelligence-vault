# NFL games available with NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **NFL Sunday Ticket** provides out-of-market Sunday afternoon NFL games (Eastern Time) not broadcast locally on CBS, Fox, or NBC, excluding preseason/postseason games.
- **Two purchase options**: Add-on to YouTube TV ($82.99/month) for full access (preseason/postseason included) or standalone via YouTube Primetime Channels for out-of-market Sunday games only.
- **Exclusions apply**: Digital exclusives, locally televised games, national games, and international games are not included in NFL Sunday Ticket.
