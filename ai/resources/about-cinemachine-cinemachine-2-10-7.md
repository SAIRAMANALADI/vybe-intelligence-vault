# About Cinemachine | Cinemachine | 2.10.7

## Summary

- **Modular Camera Control**: Cinemachine provides a suite of procedural modules for dynamic camera operations, including target tracking, shot composition, blending, and transitions, reducing manual scripting and adjustments during development.

- **Real-Time Adaptability**: Automatically adjusts camera behavior in response to scene changes (e.g., animations, terrain, or object movements) without requiring script revisions, ensuring robustness across genres (FPS, 2D, RTS, etc.).

- **Unity Integration**: Seamlessly integrates with Unity tools like Timeline, Post-Processing Stack v2, and HDRP, offering extensible virtual camera behaviors and conditional adapters (e.g., `ifdef` directives for Post-Processing). Supports Unity 2019.4+ and later.
