# What are common credit report errors that I should look for on my credit report? | Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

## Summary

- **Identity Errors**: Verify personal details (name, address, phone) and check for mixed files (another consumer's data due to similar names) or signs of identity theft (unrecognized accounts).

- **Account Status & Data Inaccuracies**: Confirm account statuses (e.g., closed vs. open), authorized user vs. owner roles, delinquency dates, and ensure no duplicate reporting of the same debt.

- **Data Management Mistakes**: Validate account balances, credit limits, and ensure no incorrect reporting of payment history or account details by furnishers.
