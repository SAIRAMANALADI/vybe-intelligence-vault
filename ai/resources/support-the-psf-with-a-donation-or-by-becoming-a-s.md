# Support the PSF with a Donation or by becoming a Supporting Member! | Python Software Foundation

## Summary

- **Grants & Development**: Awards grants and resources to advance Python development/adoption, including funding PyCon US ($137,200 to 143 attendees in 2019) and supporting CPython via a Developer in Residence.
- **Infrastructure & Legal Protection**: Hosts python.org servers, maintains the Python Packaging Index (PyPI), and defends Python’s copyright/IP rights as a 501(c)(3) public charity.
- **Community & Fiscal Sponsorship**: Recognizes contributors via awards, sponsors 13 regional conferences/projects, and accepts donations via credit card, PayPal, checks, or international transfers (Zelle, ACH, Wire, Transferwise).
