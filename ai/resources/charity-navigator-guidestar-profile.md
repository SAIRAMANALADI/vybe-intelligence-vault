# Charity Navigator - GuideStar Profile

## Summary

- **Rating System**: Charity Navigator evaluates charities across four key dimensions—Leadership & Adaptability, Accountability & Finance, Culture & Community, and Impact & Results—using a nuanced, evolved methodology to assess organizational health and performance.

- **Programs & Tools**: Operates programs like Charity Ratings (donor guidance), Charity Alerts (misconduct warnings), Curated Lists (crisis/performance-based selections), and Giving Basket (multi-charity donation tool) to democratize philanthropy and enhance transparency.

- **Governance & Metrics**: Board practices include formal orientation, CEO oversight, conflict-of-interest reviews, and diversity in recruitment; metrics historically tracked (e.g., site visits, donations via CN) aimed to measure reach and donor engagement.
