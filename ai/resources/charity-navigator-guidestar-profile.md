# Charity Navigator - GuideStar Profile

## Summary

- **Rating Framework**: Charity Navigator evaluates charities across four key areas—Leadership & Adaptability, Accountability & Finance, Culture & Community, and Impact & Results—using a nuanced, holistic methodology to assess organizational health and performance.

- **Programs & Tools**: Offers **Charity Ratings** (donor guidance), **Charity Alerts** (misconduct warnings), **Curated Lists** (trending/impactful charities), and **Giving Basket** (multi-charity donation tool) to democratize philanthropy and enhance transparency.

- **Governance & Strategy**: Board practices include formal CEO oversight, conflict-of-interest policies, inclusive recruitment, and annual self-assessments; strategic goals focus on **REACH** (user growth), **RATINGS** (deeper guidance), **TEAM** (capacity building), **PLATFORM** (UX improvements), and **REVENUE** (sustainable funding).
