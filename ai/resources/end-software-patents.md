# End Software Patents

## Summary

- **PERA and PREVAIL Acts Delayed**: Congressional votes on the PERA and PREVAIL Acts, initially scheduled for November 18, 2024, have been postponed, preventing immediate legislative action on patent eligibility reforms.

- **Software Patent Reform Opposition**: The Free Software Foundation (FSF) opposes these acts, arguing they undermine software freedom by potentially expanding patentability of abstract ideas and algorithms.

- **FSF Advocacy for Patent Restrictions**: FSF urges blocking the acts to prevent retroactive patent eligibility expansions, which they claim would harm open-source innovation and increase litigation risks.
