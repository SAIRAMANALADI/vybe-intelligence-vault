# Crossmark

## Summary

- **Discrepancies in carbon budget estimates** arise from methodological differences, including varying definitions of "cumulative CO₂ emissions" and baseline periods for historical emissions.

- **Key contributors to uncertainty** include Earth system feedbacks (e.g., permafrost thaw, carbon-climate sensitivity) and non-CO₂ forcings (e.g., methane, aerosols), which are inconsistently accounted for across models.

- **Consensus frameworks** (e.g., IPCC AR5) reconcile estimates by standardizing metrics like transient climate response to emissions (TCRE), but residual uncertainties persist due to incomplete observational constraints.
