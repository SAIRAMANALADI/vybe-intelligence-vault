# Welcome to the Academic Senate

## Summary

- The **UC Academic Senate** operates under shared governance, with authority delegated by the **Board of Regents** to oversee academic policies, curriculum, admissions, degree requirements, faculty appointments, promotions, and budgetary advice.
- The **2025-26 leadership** includes **Ahmet Palazoglu (Chair)** and **Susannah Scott (Vice Chair)**, with **Amani M. Nuru-Jeter** designated as the **2026-27 Vice Chair**.
- Key initiatives include the **Academic Senate AI Workgroup Report**, the **Oliver Johnson Award recipients (2026)**, and a **dedication ceremony honoring UC President Emeritus Michael V. Drake**.
