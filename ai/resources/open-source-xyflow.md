# Open Source - xyflow

## Summary

- **Licensing & Growth**: xyflow maintains all core libraries (React Flow, Svelte Flow, Pro Platform) under the MIT License, ensuring perpetual free usage, modification, and redistribution while fostering organic adoption and ecosystem expansion since 2019.

- **Open-Core Funding Model**: xyflow employs a thin-crust open-core model where the MIT-licensed core (libraries, docs, community support) remains free, while paid services (Pro examples, prioritized support) fund ongoing development without external investors.

- **Ecosystem Contributions**: xyflow supports open-source dependencies via Open Collective/GitHub donations, offers free Pro access to non-commercial projects/students, and publishes developer surveys (e.g., 2023 React Flow survey) to share insights and drive community engagement.
