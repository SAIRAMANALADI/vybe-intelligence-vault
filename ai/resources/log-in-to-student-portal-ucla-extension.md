# Log-In to Student Portal | UCLA Extension

## Summary

- **Account Recovery**: For existing/former students experiencing login issues, contact `enroll@uclaextension.edu` or the provided phone number to restore account access instead of creating a new account.

- **Login Credentials**: Username is the preferred email address; password is case-sensitive. Forgot username/password links redirect to recovery pages.

- **New Student Registration**: New students must enter a primary email address to initiate account creation; a username and password are auto-generated and emailed upon completion.
