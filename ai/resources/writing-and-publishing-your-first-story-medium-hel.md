# Writing and publishing your first story – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Draft Creation & Autosave**: New stories begin as drafts, automatically saved to the Medium account and accessible via the Stories page; web app uses the **Write** button, while mobile apps require the green plus button in the profile menu.
- **Embedding Third-Party Media**: Supported via URL pasting (e.g., YouTube, GitHub) on a new line—press Enter to render interactive embeds; unsupported services default to preview cards.
- **Publishing Workflow**: Finalize via **Publish** (web) or **Post/Publish** (mobile), with options to add topics, schedule, paywall (Partner Program), and customize preview images; published stories distribute to followers, publications, and may earn revenue based on engagement.
