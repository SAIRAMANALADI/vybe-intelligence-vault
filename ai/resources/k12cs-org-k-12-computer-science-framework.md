# k12cs.org – K–12 Computer Science Framework

## Summary

- **Framework Vision**: Aims to democratize K–12 computer science education, transitioning it from a niche subject to a universal opportunity, emphasizing computational literacy as a 21st-century competency.

- **Collaborative Development**: Created by ACM, Code.org, CSTA, and others in partnership with states/districts to establish conceptual guidelines for standards, curriculum, and teacher capacity building.

- **Structured Progression**: Provides grade-band-specific framework statements organized by concepts (e.g., algorithms, systems) and practices (e.g., abstraction, debugging) to guide equitable implementation.
