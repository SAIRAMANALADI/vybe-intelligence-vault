# Fair Use - Copyright - LibGuides at Cornell University

## Summary

- **Fair Use Framework**: Determined by four statutory factors: (1) purpose/character of use (transformative, commercial, educational), (2) nature of copyrighted work, (3) amount/substantiality of portion used, and (4) effect on market value; no fixed quantitative thresholds exist.

- **Case-by-Case Assessment**: Users must self-evaluate fair use potential; Cornell provides a [checklist](https://guides.library.cornell.edu/ld.php?content_id=63936868) for documentation, though educational status alone does not guarantee exemption under U.S. copyright law.

- **Resources & Limitations**: Educational institutions remain bound by copyright law; additional guidance includes U.S. Copyright Office materials, Stanford’s Fair Use Center, and discipline-specific best practices (e.g., CMSI’s Fair Use Library).
