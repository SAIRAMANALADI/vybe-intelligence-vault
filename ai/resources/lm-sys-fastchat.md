## Summary

- **FastChat** is an open-source framework from `lm-sys` (4.8k forks, 39.5k stars) designed for evaluating LLM responses, with structured prompts for pairwise and single-judge evaluations across general and math domains.

- **Prompt Templates**: Includes 8 JSONL-defined templates (`pair-v2`, `pair-math-v1`, `single-v1`, etc.), supporting both single-turn and multi-turn evaluations with standardized output formats (e.g., `[[A]]`, `[[rating]]`).

- **Evaluation Criteria**: Judges assess responses based on correctness, helpfulness, relevance, and depth, with strict formatting requirements to avoid bias (e.g., position order, response length).
