# Troubleshoot video removals - YouTube Help

## Summary

- **Community Guidelines Violation**: Videos removed for "Inappropriate content" violate YouTube's [Community Guidelines](https://www.youtube.com/yt/policyandsafety/communityguidelines.html); resolve via [Studio dashboard](https://studio.youtube.com) or appeal if erroneous (see [Community Guidelines strike basics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802032)).

- **Copyright Infringement**: Videos flagged for "Includes copyrighted content" are processed via YouTube’s [Content ID](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370) system; copyright strikes (from valid [removal requests](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2807622)) require resolution (deletion does not lift strikes; see [copyright strike resolution](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2814000#resolve)).

- **Terms of Service/Trademark Violations**: Videos removed for "Terms of Service violation" may involve copyright or usage terms (see [Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)); "trademark issue" removals violate [trademark policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6154218).
