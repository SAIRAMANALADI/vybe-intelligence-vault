# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- **Position Sensitivity**: Language models (LMs) exhibit significant performance degradation when relevant information is placed in the middle of long input contexts, with optimal performance observed when such information is at the beginning or end, even for models explicitly designed for long contexts.

- **Task-Specific Analysis**: Evaluated on multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval, the study demonstrates that LMs struggle to robustly utilize information distributed across long contexts, highlighting a critical limitation in their contextual processing capabilities.

- **Evaluation Protocols**: Proposes new benchmarking methodologies to assess long-context utilization in LMs, emphasizing the need for standardized tests that account for positional biases in model performance.
