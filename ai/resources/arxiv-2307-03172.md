# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- Language models exhibit degraded performance when relevant information is positioned in the middle of long input contexts, with optimal performance at the beginning or end, even in explicitly long-context models.

- Analysis conducted on multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval tasks demonstrates that current models do not robustly utilize information distributed across long contexts.

- Findings highlight the need for improved evaluation protocols to assess long-context utilization in language models, as highlighted by significant performance drops in middle-context scenarios.
