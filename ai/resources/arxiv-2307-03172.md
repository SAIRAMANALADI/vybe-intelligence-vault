# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- **Position Sensitivity**: Language models (LMs) exhibit significant performance degradation when relevant information is placed in the middle of long contexts, with peak performance observed at the beginning or end of the input, despite claims of long-context capabilities.

- **Task-Specific Evaluation**: Analysis on multi-document QA and key-value retrieval tasks reveals that current LMs fail to robustly utilize information distributed across long contexts, highlighting a critical limitation in their contextual reasoning.

- **Evaluation Protocols**: The study proposes new protocols for assessing long-context LMs, emphasizing the need for standardized benchmarks that test models' ability to access and integrate information regardless of positional placement.
