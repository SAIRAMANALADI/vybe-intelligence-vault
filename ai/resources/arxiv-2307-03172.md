# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- **Position Sensitivity in Long-Context Models**: Performance degrades significantly when relevant information is placed in the middle of long input contexts, with optimal results observed when key data is at the beginning or end, even for models explicitly designed for long contexts.

- **Evaluation Tasks**: Study analyzes language models on multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval, demonstrating that current architectures fail to robustly utilize information distributed across extended contexts.

- **Methodological Insight**: Findings highlight the need for new evaluation protocols to assess long-context language models, emphasizing positional bias as a critical failure mode in current architectures.
