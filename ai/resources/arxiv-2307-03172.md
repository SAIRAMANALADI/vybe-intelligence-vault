# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- **Positional Bias in Long-Context Models**: Language models (LMs) exhibit significant performance degradation when relevant information is placed in the middle of long input contexts, with peak performance at the beginning or end, revealing a positional bias not fully addressed by explicitly long-context models.

- **Task-Specific Evaluation Protocols**: The study evaluates LMs on multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval, demonstrating that current models fail to robustly utilize information distributed across long contexts, necessitating new benchmarks for long-context evaluation.

- **Empirical Findings on Context Utilization**: Experiments show that even models designed for long contexts struggle with middle-positioned critical data, highlighting a fundamental limitation in how LMs process and retrieve information from extended inputs.
