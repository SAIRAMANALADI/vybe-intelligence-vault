# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- **Position Sensitivity**: Language models (LMs) exhibit degraded performance on long-context tasks (e.g., multi-document QA, key-value retrieval) when relevant information is placed in the middle of input contexts, despite explicit long-context training.

- **Boundary Bias**: LMs perform best when critical information is at the **beginning** or **end** of the context, suggesting a positional bias that limits robust long-context utilization.

- **Evaluation Gap**: Current long-context LMs fail to generalize effectively, highlighting the need for improved architectures and benchmarks to assess true long-context comprehension.
