# research!rsc: Go & Versioning

## Summary

- **Semantic Import Versioning (SIV)**: Proposes embedding major version numbers in import paths (e.g., `github.com/user/repo/v2`) to enforce backward compatibility and avoid dependency conflicts in Go modules.

- **Minimal Version Selection (MVS)**: Introduces an algorithm to resolve dependencies by selecting the minimal compatible versions, ensuring reproducible builds without exhaustive version constraints.

- **vgo Proposal & Adoption**: The original `vgo` proposal (March 2018) was accepted into Go (May 2018), leading to the formalization of Go Modules, replacing `dep` with built-in versioning support.
