# About privacy information on the App Store and the choices you have to control your data - Apple Support

## Summary

- **App Privacy Labels**: Apple mandates developers to self-report data collection practices in a standardized "App Privacy" section on the App Store, categorizing data types (e.g., location, health, contact info) and disclosing whether data is linked to the user or used for tracking (e.g., advertising, analytics).

- **Data Classification & Tracking Controls**: Data is classified as "linked to you" (e.g., account/device identifiers) or "not linked" (e.g., anonymized identifiers). Tracking involves linking app data with third-party sources for ads or data brokers, with Apple providing iOS/iPadOS/macOS settings to limit such sharing.

- **Apple’s Own Data Practices**: Apple’s apps (e.g., Music, Maps, Health) disclose minimal data collection, often using anonymized identifiers (e.g., random IDs for Fitness+, coarse location for Apple Pay fraud prevention). Core services like iCloud backups may omit certain data types unless explicitly tied to in-app functionality.
