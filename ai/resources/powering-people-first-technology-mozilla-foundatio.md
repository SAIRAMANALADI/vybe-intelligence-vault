# Powering People-First Technology - Mozilla Foundation

## Summary

- **Mission & Approach**: Mozilla Foundation promotes human-centric, ethical technology through collaborative co-creation, mobilization, and grantmaking, prioritizing community-driven development and open-source principles.

- **Core Initiatives**: Focuses on funding ethical alternatives, surfacing unconventional insights (e.g., *Imagine* program), mobilizing global communities, convening stakeholders at *Mozilla Festival*, and spotlighting digital culture via *Nothing Personal* magazine.

- **Structural Role**: Serves as the philanthropic arm of the Mozilla Project, operating as a non-profit with transparent financials, licensing under Creative Commons, and global engagement via localized content and partnerships.
