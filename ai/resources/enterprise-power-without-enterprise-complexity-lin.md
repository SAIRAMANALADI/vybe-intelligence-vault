# Enterprise power without enterprise complexity | Linkerd

## Summary

- **Performance & Efficiency**: Linkerd outperforms other service meshes (e.g., Istio) in benchmarks while consuming significantly fewer system resources, attributed to its ultralight Rust-based `linkerd2-proxy` dataplane.

- **Security & Simplicity**: Implements zero-config mutual TLS (mTLS) and zero-trust policies by default, with a minimalist, Kubernetes-native design requiring minimal YAML/configuration and no hidden complexity.

- **Production-Ready & Open Source**: CNCF-graduated, battle-tested in production for 8+ years, with a self-contained control plane, incrementally deployable data plane, and extensive debugging tools.
