# jmason.org: Where To?

## Summary

- **Open-source contributions**: Developed and maintained [Apache SpamAssassin](http://spamassassin.apache.org), an early open-source email spam filter used by ~100M users globally.
- **Web pioneer**: Established Ireland’s first non-academic website ([iona.ie](http://www.iona.ie)) in 1993, among the world’s first ~70 public HTTP servers.
- **Linkblog & social presence**: Runs a [daily linkblog](https://jmason.ie/) on tech, security, and society; active on BlueSky ([@jmason.org](https://bsky.app/profile/jmason.org)) and Mastodon ([@jmason@mastodon.ie](https://mastodon.ie/@jmason)).
