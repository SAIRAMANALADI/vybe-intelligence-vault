# CPO CalFresh Initiative at UCLA | Basic Needs

## Summary

- **Eligibility & Benefits**: UCLA CalFresh Initiative assists students in applying for California's SNAP-equivalent (CalFresh), providing up to **$291/month** for groceries via EBT card at participating retailers (e.g., Ralphs, Whole Foods).

- **Application Process**: Students can apply in **~30 minutes** with support from CPO CalFresh Initiative via **in-person (SAC B02-B)** or **Zoom office hours**, followed by a **county interview** and approval/denial via mail or BenefitsCal.

- **Appeals & Resources**: Denied cases can be appealed via **LAFLA partnership**; eligibility checks, applications, and renewals are available at [basicneeds.ucla.edu](https://basicneeds.ucla.edu).
