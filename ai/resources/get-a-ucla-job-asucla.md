# Get a UCLA job — ASUCLA

## Summary

- **Job Application Process**: ASUCLA offers over 1500 student jobs with online applications available 24/7 via their Jobs page; applicants must select a job, fill out, and submit the application.
- **UCLA Extension Student Eligibility**: Only UCLA Extension students concurrently enrolled at UCLA are eligible; separate UCLA Extension courses do not qualify.
- **Incoming Student Requirements**: Incoming freshman/transfer students without a BruinCard must present an acceptance letter and photo ID when applying.
