# Consultation on new electronic mail marketing rules for charities – summary of responses | ICO

## Summary

- **Updated guidance on charitable soft opt-in**: Added practical examples (including good/bad practices) to clarify requirements for charities, including cases where contact details are collected by third parties (e.g., fundraisers, trading subsidiaries), aligning with the "products and services soft opt-in" model.

- **Terminology revision**: Replaced "commercial soft opt-in" with "products and services soft opt-in" to avoid misalignment with charity sector motivations, while expanding guidance to define permissible marketing content (e.g., excluding fundraising appeals).

- **Revised transaction criteria**: Purchases from charities (e.g., second-hand goods) may now qualify as "support" under the charitable purposes soft opt-in if the intent is evident, enabling combined marketing emails when both soft opt-ins apply, provided separate opt-in mechanisms are not required.
