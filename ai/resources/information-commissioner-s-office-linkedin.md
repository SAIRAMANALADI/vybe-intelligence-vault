# Information Commissioner's Office | LinkedIn

## Summary

- **Breach Details**: Metropolitan Police Service (MPS) exposed sensitive data in two incidents: unredacted victim/witness details in stalking protection orders and visible email addresses in a "honeytrap" investigation, risking further exposure of involved parties.

- **Root Causes**: Systemic failures in MPS policies, procedures, and training led to non-technical errors, highlighting inadequate safeguards for sensitive personal information and poor governance.

- **Regulatory Action**: ICO issued an enforcement notice and reprimand, mandating improvements in MPS data protection training, monitoring, and governance to address persistent shortcomings.
