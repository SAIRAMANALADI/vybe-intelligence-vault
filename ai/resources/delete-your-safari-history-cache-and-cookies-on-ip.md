# Delete your Safari history, cache and cookies on iPhone – Apple Support (UK)

## Summary

- **Clearing Safari Data**: Navigate to **Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data** to delete browsing history, cache, and cookies; confirm timeframe and tap **Clear History** (greyed out if no data exists or restrictions apply).
- **Selective Data Removal**: Access **Settings > Safari > Advanced > Website Data** to **Remove All Website Data**, preserving history while clearing tracking/cookies; or manually delete specific sites via **Safari > Bookmarks > History > Select Websites > Delete**.
- **Cookie/Content Blocking**: Enable **Block All Cookies** in **Settings > Safari > Advanced** (may break site functionality) or install third-party **content blockers** via **App Store > Safari > Extensions** to filter ads/trackers.
