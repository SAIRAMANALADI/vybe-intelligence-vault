# iPad - Apple

## Summary

- **Education Pricing**: Apple offers discounted pricing on iPads for college students and educators through the Apple Store, accessible via the education savings link.
- **iPad Lineup**: Four models available—iPad Pro (M4 chip, OLED/ProMotion), iPad Air (M1/M2, 11" or 13"), standard iPad (A14 Bionic, 10.9"), and iPad mini (A15 Bionic, 8.3").
- **Integration & Accessories**: Supports Apple Pencil (Pro/USB-C), Magic Keyboard, and seamless ecosystem features like Handoff, Sidecar, and Universal Control with macOS/iOS devices.
