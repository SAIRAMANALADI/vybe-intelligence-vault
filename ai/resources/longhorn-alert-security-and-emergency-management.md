# Longhorn Alert | Security and Emergency Management

## Summary

- **Longhorn Alert System**: Multi-channel emergency notification system (SMS, email, phone calls) for UT Austin community, tested monthly via Everbridge platform.
- **CUORS Portal**: Allows users to customize building-specific alerts (utility outages, weather emergencies) via Everbridge SSO integration.
- **Subscription Management**: UT Direct portal enables students/faculty/staff to subscribe/unsubscribe from Longhorn Alerts.
