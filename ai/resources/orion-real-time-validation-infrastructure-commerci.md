# Orion-Real-Time Validation Infrastructure | Commercial UAV Expo

## Summary

- **Orion Infrastructure**: Real-time validation infrastructure integrating distributed simulation, live field testing, and hardware to evaluate UAS systems under dense traffic, degraded communications, dynamic obstacles, and emergency scenarios, bridging gaps between isolated simulations and physical flight limitations.

- **Applications & Users**: Deployed for UTM development, multi-airport test-site infrastructure, public-safety missions, V2V evaluation, and autonomy validation; key users include test-site operators, OEMs, regulatory stakeholders, and public-safety teams.

- **Impact Case Study**: Reconstructed a drone collision incident in Arizona using Orion, enabling end-to-end testing of hardware, autonomy, and communications against dynamic hazards in a real-time sim-field pipeline, accelerating test workflows by 100x and validating recovery behaviors pre-deployment.
