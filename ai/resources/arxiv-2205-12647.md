# [2205.12647] Overcoming Catastrophic Forgetting in Zero-Shot Cross-Lingual Generation

## Summary

- **Catastrophic Forgetting in Zero-Shot Cross-Lingual Generation**: Standard fine-tuning of multilingual generative models on English-only labeled data leads to catastrophic forgetting of non-English generation capabilities, particularly in less-related language pairs (e.g., English → Thai).

- **Prompt Tuning for Mitigation**: Parameter-efficient prompt tuning (Lester et al., 2021) outperforms standard fine-tuning in zero-shot cross-lingual tasks but still lags behind fully supervised baselines, indicating room for improvement.

- **Enhancement Strategies**: Mixing unlabeled multilingual data and decomposing prompts into language/task-specific components further improves cross-lingual transfer, narrowing the gap toward robust zero-shot generation.
