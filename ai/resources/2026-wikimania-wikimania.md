# 2026:Wikimania - Wikimania

## Summary

- **Event Details**: Wikimania 2026, the 21st Wikimedia conference, is scheduled for **21-25 July 2026** in **Paris, France**, as a hybrid in-person and online event, coinciding with the **25th anniversary of Wikipedia**.

- **Core Theme & Values**: The conference focuses on **Freedom, Equity, and Reliability** (*Liberté, Équité, Fiabilité*), emphasizing **universal participation, verifiability, and resistance to political/media attacks** on Wikimedia projects.

- **Organizers & Partners**: Organized by **Wikimedia France, Wikimedia Foundation, and WikiFranca**, with institutional support from **UNESCO, Council of Europe, French Ministries (Education, Culture, Digital Affairs), CNES**, and partners like **Internet Archive Europe** and **Worteks**.
