# How to Opt Out - The NAI: Network Advertising Initiative

## Summary

- **Global Privacy Control (GPC) Signal**: Install the NAI GPC Browser Extension to broadcast a signal requesting websites not to sell or share personal data for targeted advertising.
- **Platform-Specific Privacy Adjustments**: Modify privacy settings on web browsers, mobile devices (Android/iOS), and TV/streaming platforms to limit data collection and targeted advertising.
- **Direct Opt-Out Tools**: Use NAI member websites and DAA AdChoices tools to exercise privacy rights and opt out of interest-based advertising with participating companies.
