# Teknoloji Transfer Ofisi

## Summary

- **Established in 2012 (A.Ş. status since 2018)**, Boğaziçi Üniversitesi Teknoloji Transfer Ofisi (TTO) serves as a centralized interface for managing university-industry collaboration, intellectual property (IP) commercialization, and entrepreneurship support, transitioning from decentralized internal units.

- **Operates under Boğaziçi Üniversitesi’s 1863 legacy**, integrating traditional creativity with modern R&D to facilitate technology transfer, industry partnerships, and funding acquisition across scientific, artistic, and literary domains.

- **Provides end-to-end innovation services**, including IP management, commercialization, incubation, and venture support, directly linking researchers, entrepreneurs, investors, and industry stakeholders to drive economic and societal value.
