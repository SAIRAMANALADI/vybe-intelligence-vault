# Cal Fresh at the UCLA Store in Ackerman Union — ASUCLA

## Summary

- **CalFresh Integration**: UCLA Store Market implemented CalFresh EBT acceptance in August 2022 via a new POS system, expanding to multiple campus locations (Ackerman, LuValle, Health Sciences, Hilltop) to enhance food security for students.

- **Bruin Rewards Program**: Students earn redeemable points through purchases, reducing costs on everyday grocery items while supporting long-term food accessibility initiatives.

- **Location & Accessibility**: Operates at Ackerman Union B-Level (308 Westwood Plaza, LA 90095) with extended hours and campus-wide support from UCLA Basic Needs Committee and CalFresh initiatives.
