# [1910.10683] Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer

## Summary

- Unified text-to-text framework standardizes NLP tasks (classification, QA, summarization) into a single generative model architecture, enabling systematic comparison of pre-training objectives and transfer methods.

- Introduces the **Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus (C4)**—a 750GB pre-training dataset—and demonstrates state-of-the-art results across benchmarks by scaling model size (up to 11B parameters) and fine-tuning strategies.

- Releases open-source code, pre-trained models, and C4 dataset to facilitate reproducibility and future research in transfer learning for NLP.
