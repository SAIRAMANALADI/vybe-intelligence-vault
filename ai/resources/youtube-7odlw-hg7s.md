# - YouTube

## Summary

- YouTube operates under Google LLC, with core services including content hosting, advertising, and creator monetization governed by [Terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), [Privacy](https://www.youtube.com/t/privacy), and [Policy & Safety](https://www.youtube.com/about/policies/) frameworks.

- The [Developers](https://developers.google.com/youtube) portal provides APIs, tools, and documentation for integrating YouTube functionality (e.g., video upload, analytics) into third-party applications via OAuth 2.0 and RESTful endpoints.

- YouTube’s infrastructure supports real-time transcoding, adaptive bitrate streaming (DASH/HLS), and distributed CDN delivery, as detailed in [How YouTube Works](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks).
