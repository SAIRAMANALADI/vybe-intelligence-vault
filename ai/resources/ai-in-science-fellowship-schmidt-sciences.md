# AI in Science Fellowship - Schmidt Sciences

## Summary

- **Program Scope**: Supports ~160 Postdoctoral Fellows and 20 Faculty Fellows annually across nine global universities to integrate AI into STEM research, addressing uneven adoption and accelerating scientific breakthroughs.

- **Research Focus**: Targets AI-empowered research in fields like agriculture, chemistry, astronomy, and sustainable energy, fostering community and professional development alongside technical innovation.

- **University Partnerships**: Collaborates with institutions including University of Chicago, Cornell, Imperial College London, University of Michigan, NTU Singapore, and others to facilitate AI integration in scientific workflows.
