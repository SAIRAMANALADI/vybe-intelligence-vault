## Summary

- **Meta-Wiki** serves as the central coordination hub for Wikimedia Foundation projects, including documentation, planning, and analysis, with specialized sub-projects like Wikimedia Outreach originating from it.
- **Key operational tools** include cross-wiki request systems (e.g., bot status, global blocks, username changes) and grant/funding mechanisms managed via structured workflows (e.g., Steward requests, U4C cases).
- **Community engagement** is facilitated through mailing lists, IRC channels, recurring online meetings, and events like Wikimania 2026 (Paris), alongside multilingual forums (e.g., Wikimedia Forum) and dispute resolution frameworks.
