# - YouTube

## Summary

- **Platform Overview**: YouTube is a video-sharing platform owned by Google LLC, offering services including content hosting, live streaming, monetization (via YouTube Partner Program), and analytics tools for creators and advertisers.

- **Developer & API Access**: Provides APIs (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API) for third-party integration, enabling programmatic access to video metadata, uploads, and channel management.

- **Policy & Compliance**: Enforces strict [Terms of Service](https://youtu.be/t/terms), [Privacy Policy](https://youtu.be/t/privacy), and [Content Safety Policies](https://www.youtube.com/about/policies/), with automated and manual moderation systems for copyright (Content ID) and policy violations.
