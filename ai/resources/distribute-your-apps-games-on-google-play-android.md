# Distribute Your Apps & Games on Google Play | Android Developers

## Summary

- **Distribution & Reach**: Google Play enables publishing to **2.5B+ active Android devices** (phones, tablets, watches, cars, TVs) via **Play Console**, supporting rapid global distribution.
- **Monetization & Security**: Supports **135+ countries** with **Play Billing** for subscriptions/in-app purchases, while **Play Integrity API** detects threats and prevents unauthorized access.
- **Quality & Engagement**: Enforces **target API levels**, offers **Play Feature/Asset Delivery** for dynamic feature/asset distribution, and provides **Play Engage SDK** for user re-engagement.
