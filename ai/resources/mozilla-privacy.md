# Mozilla Privacy

## Summary

- **Definition & Handling of Personal Data**: Mozilla defines "personal information" as data that directly identifies an individual (e.g., name, email, IP address) or can be reasonably linked to them; non-personal data is aggregated or anonymized. Combining personal and non-personal data reclassifies the set as personal, and de-identification processes are applied to remove personal identifiers where possible.

- **Data Collection & Sharing Constraints**: Data is collected via user input, automated product interactions, third-party disclosures, or derived inferences (e.g., IP-based localization). Sharing occurs only with explicit consent, contractual obligations, legal mandates, harm prevention, or organizational changes, with anonymization prioritized for transparency (e.g., open-source contributions).

- **Security & Retention Policies**: Mozilla enforces physical, technical, and organizational safeguards for data protection, with breach notifications provided to users. Data retention is minimized to functional necessity, with destruction protocols post-use unless legal retention requirements apply. Policy updates are communicated via standard channels, and user acceptance is inferred through continued service usage.
