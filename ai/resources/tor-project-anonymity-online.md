# Tor Project | Anonymity Online

## Summary

- **Isolation & Cookie Management**: Tor Browser isolates each website session, automatically clearing cookies and browsing history upon completion to prevent third-party tracking.
- **Traffic Obfuscation**: Traffic is encrypted three times via the Tor network (composed of volunteer-run relays), concealing destination websites from network observers.
- **Anti-Fingerprinting**: Browser and device characteristics are standardized across users to mitigate unique identification via fingerprinting techniques.
