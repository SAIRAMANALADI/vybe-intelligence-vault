# Captioning and Accessibility

## Summary

- MIT mandates **proactive accessibility** for all new digital content (websites, apps, documents, videos) under **CREATE** initiative to align with legal and ethical standards.
- **FIX** prioritizes systematic remediation of existing inaccessible content, ensuring strategic resource allocation for compliance and usability.
- **RESPOND** enforces prompt resolution of reported accessibility barriers via public forms (e.g., [MIT Accessibility Request Form](https://mit.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_bfL80IdctDylEqx)).
