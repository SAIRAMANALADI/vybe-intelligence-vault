# Education | FSI

## Summary

- **Program Offerings**: FSI provides Stanford students with career- and research-focused programs, including internships, honors programs, research grants, fieldwork, and a minor in Global Studies (specializing in European Studies), alongside the two-year Ford Dorsey Master's in International Policy (MIP) for graduate-level training in international policy.

- **Curriculum & Research**: FSI faculty teach over **145 courses annually** (80+ graduate/undergraduate and 65+ undergraduate-only), covering topics from healthcare to liberation technology, while mentoring students in advanced research through honors programs, assistantships, and fieldwork.

- **Professional Development**: FSI offers targeted training for international affairs professionals via centers like CDDRL (e.g., Fisher Family Summer Fellows, Leadership Academy for Development) and SPICE K-12, alongside initiatives like REAP, which collaborates with Chinese officials on rural child health and education.
