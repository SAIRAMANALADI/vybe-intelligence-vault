# Welcome - Copyright Crash Course - LibGuides at University of Texas at Austin

## Summary

- **Purpose & Scope**: The Copyright Crash Course (CCC) is a comprehensive guide covering copyright management, Creative Commons licensing, AI-generated content, digitization, orphan works, and institutional policies, originally designed for faculty but applicable to all users.

- **Structural Evolution**: Version 3 of the CCC modularized content into separate guides (e.g., Creative Commons, Public Domain) and is updated annually to reflect legal changes, court rulings, and emerging issues like AI training and open access.

- **Licensing & Attribution**: The CCC is licensed under CC BY 4.0, with proper attribution to Georgia Harper and Colleen Lyon, and includes resources for librarians, fair use guidelines, and Texas-specific copyright clearance tools.
