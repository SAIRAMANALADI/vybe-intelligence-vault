# Related Website Sets | Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Deprecation Status**: Google has ceased accepting new submissions for Related Website Sets (RWS), formerly First-Party Sets, as part of the Privacy Sandbox phase-out, per the [Update on Plans for Privacy Sandbox Technologies](https://privacysandbox.com/news/update-on-plans-for-privacy-sandbox-technologies).
- **Functionality & Use Cases**: RWS allowed cross-site cookie access for specific purposes (e.g., shared identity, SSO) across related domains (e.g., `brandx.com`, `brandx.co.uk`), but is now deprecated with no new submissions accepted.
- **Technical Implementation**: RWS required domain declarations with a "set primary" and "set members," submitted via GitHub, and used for determining first/third-party contexts, including Chrome’s IP Protection feature for masked domains.
