# Related Website Sets | Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Deprecation Status**: Google has ceased new submissions for Related Website Sets (RWS), previously known as First-Party Sets, as part of its Privacy Sandbox phase-out plan ([Update](https://privacysandbox.com/news/update-on-plans-for-privacy-sandbox-technologies)).

- **Purpose & Functionality**: RWS allowed cross-site data access (e.g., third-party cookies) for specific use cases by declaring domain relationships, with a "set primary" domain and optional "set members" (e.g., `brandx.com`, `fly-brandx.com`).

- **Use Cases & Scope**: RWS supported shared identity across domains for branding, localization, or services (e.g., `brandx.co.uk`, `calendar-brandx.com`), but was limited to domains not requiring IP Protection proxying.
