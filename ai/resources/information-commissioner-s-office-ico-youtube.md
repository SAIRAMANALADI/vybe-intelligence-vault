# Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) - YouTube

## Summary

- **Platform Overview**: YouTube is a video-sharing platform owned by Google LLC, offering services including content hosting, streaming, monetization (via YouTube Partners/Advertisers), and creator tools (YouTube Creators).
- **Technical Infrastructure**: Operates under Google’s developer ecosystem ([developers.google.com/youtube](https://developers.google.com/youtube)), with backend systems optimized for scalability, real-time transcoding, and recommendation algorithms (detailed in [How YouTube Works](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks)).
- **Compliance & Policies**: Adheres to regional regulations (e.g., UK ICO guidelines), enforces [Terms of Service](http://www.youtube.com/t/terms), [Privacy Policy](http://www.youtube.com/t/privacy), and [Content Safety Policies](https://www.youtube.com/about/policies/) for copyright, ads, and user-generated content.
