# CSforALL Bringing Computer Science & AI to Every Student

## Summary

- **Hour of AI**: Provides year-round, hands-on AI and computer science activities for learners to explore, understand, and create with AI.

- **Unlock8 Initiative**: Advocates for making computer science and AI a mandatory high school graduation requirement to ensure equitable access to tech education.

- **TeachAI Framework**: Develops structured guidelines for AI and computer science education to empower both students and educators with standardized teaching methodologies.
