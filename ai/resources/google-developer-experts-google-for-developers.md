# Google Developer Experts - Google for Developers

## Summary

- **Global Network & Expertise**: Google Developer Experts (GDE) is a global network of 1,000+ professionals with expertise in Google technologies (Android, Cloud, ML, Web), offering mentorship, networking, and early access to Google products.

- **Eligibility & Application**: Requires demonstrated expertise in Google tech, significant community contributions (speaking, content, mentoring), English proficiency, and a referral from a Google employee or existing GDE.
