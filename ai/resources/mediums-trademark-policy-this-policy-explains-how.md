# Medium’s Trademark Policy. This policy explains how Medium… | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- Medium prohibits use of trademarks in ways likely to cause consumer confusion about the source of goods/services or to free-ride on another company’s branding investment.
- Trademark complaints require evidence of likely confusion; fair use (e.g., nominative use) or descriptive references without endorsement are permitted.
- To report suspected infringement, submit a claim via [Medium’s trademark violation form](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=4422743654679&form=true).
