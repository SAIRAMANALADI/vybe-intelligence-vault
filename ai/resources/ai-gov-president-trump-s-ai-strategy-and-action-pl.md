# AI.Gov | President Trump's AI Strategy and Action Plan

## Summary

- **Policy Pillars**: The U.S. AI Action Plan under President Trump focuses on three pillars: *Accelerating Innovation* (e.g., removing barriers to AI adoption), *Building AI Infrastructure* (e.g., expediting data center permitting), and *Leading International Diplomacy and Security* (e.g., promoting U.S. AI tech exports).

- **Executive Actions**: Key directives include *Promoting Export of American AI Tech Stack* (7/23/2025), *Accelerating Federal Data Center Permitting* (7/23/2025), *Preventing "Woke AI" in Federal Government* (7/23/2025), and *Advancing AI Education for Youth* (4/23/2025).

- **Governance & Procurement**: OMB released memos (4/3/2025) on *AI Innovation & Governance* (M-25-21) and *Efficient AI Acquisition* (M-25-22), while federal AI use is streamlined via new procurement policies (4/7/2025).
