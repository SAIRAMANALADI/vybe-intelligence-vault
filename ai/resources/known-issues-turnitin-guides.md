# Known issues – Turnitin Guides

## Summary

- **Geographical Restrictions**: Users in mainland China cannot download AI reports, and Classic Standard assignments may fail to display similarity scores due to licensing/location-based limitations; engineering team is investigating.

- **Non-Latin Language Rendering Issues**: Downloaded Similarity Reports in non-Latin scripts (e.g., Thai, Arabic) exhibit character display errors, formatting corruption, or incorrect PDF rendering in the new report view.

- **Classic Workflow Bugs**: Flags feature fails for submissions <500 characters (incorrectly triggers error), weighted rubrics in classic TFS with non-100% totals are unsupported in new TFS, and XLS file uploads to Classic Standard Assignments intermittently fail.
