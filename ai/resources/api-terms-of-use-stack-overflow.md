# API Terms of Use - Stack Overflow

## Summary

- **API Usage & Compliance**: Use of Stack Exchange APIs requires acceptance of their [Terms of Service](https://stackexchange.com/legal/terms-of-service/public) and adherence to modified terms; Stack Exchange may revise these terms unilaterally.

- **Attribution & Branding**: All applications must visibly credit Stack Exchange as the content source; logos/branding may be used unmodified for identification, subject to [Trademark Guidelines](https://policies.stackoverflow.co/company/trademark-guidance).

- **Termination & Liability**: Stack Exchange may immediately suspend API access for violations; surviving terms include ownership, liability limits, and indemnification obligations.
