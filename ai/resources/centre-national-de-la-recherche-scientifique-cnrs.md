# Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS)

## Summary

- **CNRS at VivaTech 2026**: Emphasized the role of basic research in advancing technological sovereignty, showcasing CNRS-led innovations and strategic collaborations to bolster France/EU's tech independence.

- **Mission Neptune**: Launched a multidisciplinary scientific coalition to map and model deep-sea ecosystems (e.g., coral reefs in Tonga) using 3D photogrammetry and genomics, addressing high-seas biodiversity and climate resilience.

- **Nickel Mining Impact**: Demonstrated that intensive nickel mining in New Caledonia’s Thio Lagoon disrupted microbial biodiversity, with implications for heavy metal biogeochemistry and ecosystem restoration strategies.
