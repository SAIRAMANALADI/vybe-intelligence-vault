# Entertainment - Services - Apple

## Summary

- **Apple One Subscription**: Consolidates up to six Apple services (Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, Apple Fitness+, Apple News+, and iCloud+) into a single bundle with potential cost savings.

- **Apple TV+**: Offers award-winning Apple Originals (e.g., *Severance*, *Foundation*, *Silo*) streamed in 4K HDR with Spatial Audio, available across all devices.

- **Apple Music**: Provides lossless and Spatial Audio tracks (up to 24-bit/192kHz), ad-free listening, and integration with Apple Fitness+ for curated workouts.
