# CORDIS | European Commission

## Summary

- **CORDIS** is the EU’s central repository for disseminating results from **Horizon Europe** and predecessor programs, providing multilingual access to project data, publications, and thematic collections (e.g., *Results Packs*) across 13 high-level application domains (e.g., Health, Digital Economy, Space).

- **Interactive analytics** via the **Horizon Dashboard** enable filtering of proposals, projects, and participants by thematic, geographic, and organizational parameters, offering real-time insights into EU R&I funding landscapes.

- **Multimedia dissemination** includes curated **videos** (e.g., drone-based food delivery), **podcasts** (e.g., sea-level modeling), and **public summaries** (e.g., cosmic web mapping), structured under domains like Climate, Fundamental Research, and Security.
