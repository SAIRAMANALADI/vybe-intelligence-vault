# Notetaking (Notetakers) Home

## Summary

- **Eligibility & Requirements**: Volunteer notetakers must attend classes consistently and share lecture notes post-class for students with disabilities (e.g., ADHD, visual/hearing impairments, autism, or medical conditions). Service hours are verified for participants.

- **Process & Submission**: Notetakers must register via the D&A Portal using UT EID, select eligible courses, and upload sample notes for confirmation. Post-selection, typed notes (preferred) or legible, numbered handwritten scans (compiled into a single document) must be submitted after each lecture.

- **Privacy & Compliance**: Notetaker identities are anonymized in shared documents. Personal data (e.g., name) must be removed from notes using Microsoft Support guidelines. Withdrawal from the role requires immediate availability updates in the portal.
