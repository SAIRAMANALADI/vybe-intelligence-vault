# Atlassian knowledge base | Atlassian Support

## Summary

- Atlassian provides multiple support channels including direct contact via [Atlassian Support](https://support.atlassian.com/contact), community forums ([Atlassian Community](https://community.atlassian.com/), [Atlassian Developer Community](https://community.developer.atlassian.com/)), and a dedicated [Developer Support Service Desk](https://ecosystem.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/14).

- For custom or enterprise-level requests, Atlassian offers partnerships through its [Atlassian Partner](https://www.atlassian.com/partners) network.

- Support resources are segmented by user type, with distinct channels for general users, developers, and partners.
