# IGNET | Council of the Inspectors General on Integrity and Efficiency; IGnet

## Summary

- **CIGIE Structure**: Independent executive branch entity established by the *Inspector General Act of 1978* (amended 2008), consolidating PCIE/ECIE into 7 committees (Audit, IT, Inspections/Evaluations, Investigations, Integrity, Legislation, Professional Development) with 73 statutory IGs across federal agencies.

- **Core Functions**: Mandates fraud/waste/abuse detection, legal violation prevention, and operational efficiency promotion via OIGs; oversees CIGIE Training Institute for specialized workforce development and publishes annual reports (e.g., 2025 report to the President).

- **Resources & Tools**: Provides technical guidance (e.g., *FISMA Capstone Report 2024*, *Cloud Security Best Practices*), peer-review frameworks, FOIA compliance tools, and *Oversight.gov* as a centralized audit reporting portal for public transparency.
