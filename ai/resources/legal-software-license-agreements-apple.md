# Legal - Software License Agreements - Apple

## Summary

- **Dynamic License Terms**: Software license agreements for Apple products may change post-purchase, with the version at time of purchase superseding any later versions reviewed online.
- **Mandatory Acceptance**: Installation or use of Apple software/hardware requires explicit agreement to the then-current license terms, which must be read and accepted prior to usage.
- **Regional Compliance**: Specific terms, such as the [Apple Pay & Wallet Terms and Conditions for the US](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/apple-pay-wallet/us/), are jurisdiction-specific and must be reviewed for regional compliance.
