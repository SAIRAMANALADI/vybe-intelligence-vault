# Cornell University AI for Science Institute

## Summary

- **Research Themes**: CUAISci focuses on AI-driven scientific discovery across materials science, physics, biological sciences, and sustainability sciences, with cross-cutting synergies in quantum physics, biophysics, climate/ecosystems, and sustainable materials.

- **Postdoctoral Fellowship Program**: Hosts ~20 Schmidt Sciences AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellows annually, part of a broader initiative across nine universities to advance AI applications in scientific research.

- **AI for Sustainability Initiative (CAISI)**: Targets global sustainability challenges via AI, prioritizing decarbonization of energy and agri-food systems, spanning sustainable materials to energy-food-climate nexus solutions.
