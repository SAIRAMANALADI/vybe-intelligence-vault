# Apple Vision Pro - Apple

## Summary

- **M5 Chip Integration**: The Apple Vision Pro incorporates the new M5 chip, enhancing performance for spatial computing tasks, including high-resolution displays, real-time sensor fusion, and advanced machine learning for eye/hand tracking and environmental understanding.

- **Dual Knit Band & Fit System**: Features a breathable, counterbalanced Dual Knit Band with an adjustable Fit Dial, allowing independent upper/lower strap tuning for personalized comfort and prolonged immersion in spatial experiences.

- **ZEISS Optical Inserts & Spatial Audio**: Supports magnetically attached ZEISS Optical Inserts for prescription lens integration, paired with near-ear speakers delivering Spatial Audio that blends digital and real-world sound while maintaining situational awareness.
