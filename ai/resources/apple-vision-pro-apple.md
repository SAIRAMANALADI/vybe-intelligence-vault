# Apple Vision Pro - Apple

## Summary

- **M5 Chip Integration**: Apple Vision Pro incorporates the new M5 chip, designed for high-performance mobile and wearable computing, enabling seamless blending of digital content with physical space while maintaining efficiency and thermal management.

- **Dual Knit Band & Fit System**: Features a counterbalanced Dual Knit Band with breathable straps and an independent Fit Dial for upper/lower strap adjustment, ensuring prolonged comfort and personalized fit via magnetically attached Light Seal for light occlusion and precise facial conformance.

- **Spatial Audio & External Battery**: Integrated spatial audio speakers deliver immersive sound while maintaining environmental awareness; powered by an external aluminum-encased battery supporting up to 2.5 hours of general use or 3 hours of video playback, with ZEISS Optical Inserts providing magnetically attached, prescription-compatible lenses for optimized visual clarity.
