# [2112.07916] LongT5: Efficient Text-To-Text Transformer for Long Sequences

## Summary

- **Transient Global (TGlobal) Attention**: Introduces a novel attention mechanism combining ETC's local/global attention with T5's scalability, eliminating the need for additional side-inputs while enabling efficient long-sequence processing.

- **Scalable Architecture**: Integrates PEGASUS pre-training strategies into T5, achieving state-of-the-art results on summarization tasks and outperforming original T5 models in question answering by scaling both input length and model size.

- **Performance Gains**: Demonstrates improved efficiency and accuracy over prior models (e.g., T5) on long-sequence tasks, validated across multiple benchmarks in the NAACL 2022 evaluation.
