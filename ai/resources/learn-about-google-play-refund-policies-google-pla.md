# Learn about Google Play refund policies - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Refund Eligibility & Restrictions**: Refunds for Google Play purchases depend on product type, transaction timing, location, and payment method; unauthorized transactions must be reported within 120 days, while accidental purchases by known users require direct refund requests. Donations to nonprofits are explicitly non-refundable.

- **Developer & Policy Variability**: Third-party developers handle refunds for their apps per their policies and local laws; contacting them directly is often the fastest resolution. Refund policies differ by product (e.g., subscriptions, books, gift cards) and region (e.g., EEA/UK-specific rules).

- **Payment Method & Points Impact**: Refunds for Google Play Points purchases follow specific guidelines, while cash/bank transfer/code payments have separate policies. Partial subscription refunds are available only in select regions (Israel, France, Germany).
