# Learn about Google Play refund policies - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Refund Eligibility & Conditions**: Refunds for Google Play purchases depend on transaction type, timing, location, and payment method; unauthorized charges must be reported within 120 days, while accidental purchases by authorized users require direct refund requests. Abuse or lack of account security (e.g., missing 2FA) typically voids refund eligibility.

- **Third-Party & Product-Specific Policies**: Most refunds are handled by app developers per their policies and local laws; exceptions include non-refundable donations, Google Play Points adjustments, and region-specific rules (e.g., EEA/UK refunds post-March 2018). Product-specific refund timelines vary (e.g., subscriptions, books, gift cards).

- **Regional & Payment Method Nuances**: Partial subscription refunds are available in Israel, France, or Germany; cash/bank transfer/code payments follow distinct refund processes. EEA/UK users have dedicated refund pathways, while payment methods like Google Play Points or gift codes have separate policies.
