# Learn about Google Play refund policies - Google Play Help

## Summary

- Google Play refunds are processed based on purchase type, timing, payment method, and user location, with developers handling third-party app refunds per their policies and applicable laws.
- Unauthorized transactions must be reported within 120 days; accidental purchases by others require a direct refund request, while donations to nonprofits are non-refundable.
- EEA/UK users have specific refund rights for purchases made post-March 28, 2018, and partial refunds for subscriptions are available in Israel, France, or Germany.
