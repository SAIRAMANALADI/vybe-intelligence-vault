# Learn about Google Play refund policies - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Refund Eligibility & Abuse Prevention**: Refunds are determined by purchase type, timing, payment method, and location; Google typically denies refunds for unauthorized account access, policy abuse, or lack of authentication. Unauthorized transactions must be reported within 120 days, while accidental purchases by known users require a direct refund request.

- **Third-Party Developer Role**: Most Play Store purchases are managed by developers, who handle refunds per their policies and local laws; contacting them directly is often the fastest resolution. Donations to nonprofits are explicitly non-refundable.

- **Regional & Product-Specific Policies**: EEA/UK users have distinct refund processes for purchases post-March 28, 2018; policies vary by product (e.g., subscriptions, gift cards, YouTube) and payment method (e.g., Google Play Points, cash transfers). Partial subscription refunds are available in Israel, France, or Germany.
