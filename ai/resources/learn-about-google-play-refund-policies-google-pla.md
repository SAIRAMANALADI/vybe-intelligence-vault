# Learn about Google Play refund policies - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Refund Eligibility & Restrictions**: Refunds for Google Play purchases depend on transaction type, timing, location, and account security; unauthorized transactions must be reported within 120 days, while accidental purchases by family/friends require direct refund requests. Donations to nonprofits are explicitly non-refundable.

- **Developer vs. Google-Managed Refunds**: Most refunds for third-party apps/games are handled by developers under their policies and applicable laws, while Google processes refunds for Google-managed products (e.g., Play Books, Pass, gift cards) with region-specific exceptions (e.g., EEA/UK post-March 2018).

- **Payment Method & Regional Nuances**: Refunds vary by payment method (e.g., Google Play Points, cash/bank transfer) and region (e.g., partial subscription refunds in Israel/France/Germany, EEA/UK-specific policies). Users must check product-specific policies (e.g., YouTube, Subscribe with Google) for tailored timelines and processes.
