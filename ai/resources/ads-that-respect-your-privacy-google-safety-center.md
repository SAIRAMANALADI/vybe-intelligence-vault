# Ads that Respect Your Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Privacy Controls**: Google enforces strict data minimization by never selling personal information, auto-deleting activity data after 18 months by default, and prohibiting ads personalization for users under 18. Users can manage ad preferences via [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/personalizationoff), including disabling personalization or reducing sensitive ad topics (e.g., health, religion).

- **Transparency & Verification**: The "Why this ad" feature explains ad targeting logic, while the [Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com) reveals verified advertiser identities (name/country) and ad formats. Advertisers must undergo identity verification to serve ads on Google platforms.

- **Secure & Privacy-Preserving Ad Tech**: Google employs advanced security infrastructures to detect fraud/scams and develops privacy-focused technologies like [Privacy Sandbox](https://privacysandbox.com/) and [confidential computing](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-confidential-matching-data-privacy/) to enable ad measurement without exposing user data.
