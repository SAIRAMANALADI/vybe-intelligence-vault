# Ads that Respect Your Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Privacy Protection**: Google explicitly avoids using sensitive personal data (e.g., health, race, religion, sexual orientation) for ad targeting and prohibits the use of content from Gmail, Drive, and Photos for ads; auto-deletion of activity data is set to 18 months by default.
- **Transparency & User Control**: "Why this ad" and "Who paid for this ad" features provide visibility into ad targeting logic and advertiser identity; users can manage ad personalization via [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/personalizationoff), including opting out entirely or restricting sensitive ad categories.
- **Security & Industry Collaboration**: Google enforces advertiser verification via the [Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com) and contributes to privacy-enhancing initiatives like the [Privacy Sandbox](https://privacysandbox.com/) and confidential computing to balance ad relevance with user privacy.
