# UCLA Recognized as one of the Nation’s Top Fleets for 2024 | Transportation

## Summary

- **Award Recognition**: UCLA ranked among the [2024 NAFA 100 Best Fleets](https://www.nafa.org/100-best-fleets-2024/), one of only four universities honored, evaluated on performance, service efficiency, technology adoption, and environmental stewardship.

- **Sustainability Leadership**: UCLA operates the largest battery-electric fleet in North American higher education, deploying innovative electric vehicles (e.g., Lithium-Ion battery-powered mini refuse trucks) and phasing out gas-powered alternatives.

- **Technological & Operational Innovations**: Implemented advanced telematics for utilization, safety, and vehicle health monitoring, alongside a QR code-based system for streamlined repairs and preventative maintenance, supporting a ~1,000-vehicle fleet critical for campus operations.
