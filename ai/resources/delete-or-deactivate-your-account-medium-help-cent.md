# Delete or deactivate your account – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Deactivation** removes profile/data from public view but retains account data in Medium's database; restoration is possible via login.
- **Deletion** permanently erases all account data per Medium's Privacy Policy; requires confirmation by typing "delete".
- Process differs slightly between desktop and mobile (iOS/Android) but follows the same core steps for deactivation/deletion.
