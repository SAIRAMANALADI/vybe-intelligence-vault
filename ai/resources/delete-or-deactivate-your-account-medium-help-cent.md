# Delete or deactivate your account – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Deactivation**: Deactivating an account removes profile/data from Medium's frontend but retains backend data; restoration is possible via re-login.
- **Deletion (Permanent)**: Account deletion is irreversible, removing all data per Medium's Privacy Policy; requires confirmation by typing "delete".
- **Cross-Platform Process**: Both actions (deactivation/deletion) are accessible via **Settings** > **Account** on desktop and mobile (iOS/Android).
