# Delete or deactivate your account – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Deactivation**: Account profile/data is hidden but retained in Medium's database; deactivation is reversible via re-login.
- **Deletion (Permanent)**: Initiates irreversible data removal per Medium's Privacy Policy; requires explicit confirmation via text input.
- **Cross-Platform Process**: Identical deactivation/deletion steps apply on desktop and mobile (iOS/Android) via profile settings.
