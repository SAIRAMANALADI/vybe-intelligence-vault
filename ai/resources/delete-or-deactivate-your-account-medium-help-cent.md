# Delete or deactivate your account – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Deactivation**: Profile and data are hidden from Medium but retained in the database; accounts can be restored by re-signing in.
- **Deletion (Permanent)**: All account data is permanently removed per Medium’s Privacy Policy; requires confirmation by typing "delete".
- **Platform Navigation**: Deactivation/deletion accessible via **Settings** > **Account** on both desktop and mobile (iOS/Android).
