# Delete or deactivate your account – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Deactivation**: Medium accounts can be deactivated via desktop/mobile (Settings > Deactivate account), hiding profile/data from public view while retaining backend data for potential reactivation via login.
- **Deletion**: Permanent account erasure requires confirmation (typing "delete") via Settings > Delete account, removing all data per Medium’s Privacy Policy.
- **Recovery**: Deactivated accounts are restorable by signing in; deleted accounts are irreversible.
