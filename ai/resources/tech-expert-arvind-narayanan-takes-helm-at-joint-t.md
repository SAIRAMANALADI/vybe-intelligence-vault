# Tech expert Arvind Narayanan takes helm at joint technology-policy center - Princeton Engineering

## Summary

- **Leadership Transition**: Arvind Narayanan, a Princeton computer science professor specializing in algorithmic fairness, AI, and privacy, becomes the fourth director of the Center for Information Technology Policy (CITP) on July 1, 2023, succeeding interim director Prateek Mittal.

- **Research Focus & Impact**: CITP, a joint initiative of SPIA and SEAS, emphasizes interdisciplinary research on tech policy, privacy, and societal impacts, with Narayanan’s work on AI fairness, web transparency, and algorithmic amplification gaining significant traction in policy and academic circles.

- **Tech Accountability & Advocacy**: Narayanan’s career highlights include exposing Netflix’s anonymized dataset vulnerabilities (2006), co-authoring *AI Snake Oil* (Princeton University Press), and leading projects like OpenWPM and the Princeton Web Transparency and Accountability Project to monitor and critique Big Tech practices.
