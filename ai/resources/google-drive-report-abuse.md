# Google Drive - Report Abuse

## Summary

- **Policy Violations & Enforcement**: Google may remove violating content, restrict sharing, limit visibility, disable product access, delete accounts, or report illegal materials to law enforcement if policies (e.g., spam, fraud, NCII, hate speech) are breached.
- **Reportable Content Categories**: Includes spam/fraud (phishing, malware), non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), disturbing content (violence, hate speech, harassment), and harmful activities (e.g., promoting immediate harm).
- **Report Submission**: Users can submit reports for policy violations, but enforcement actions are not guaranteed and depend on Google’s assessment of the reported content.
