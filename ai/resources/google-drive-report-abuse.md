# Google Drive - Report Abuse

## Summary

- Google's enforcement actions for policy violations include item removal, sharing restrictions, access limitations, account deletion, or reporting to law enforcement.
- Prohibited content categories encompass spam/fraud, NCII, disturbing/inappropriate material (violence, hate speech, harassment), and harmful activities (e.g., promoting immediate harm).
- Reports do not guarantee resolution but may trigger policy-based penalties if violations are confirmed.
