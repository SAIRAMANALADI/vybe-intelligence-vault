# Google Drive - Report Abuse

## Summary

- **Policy Violation Actions**: Google may remove violating content, restrict sharing, limit visibility, disable product access, delete accounts, or report illegal materials to law enforcement.
- **Reportable Content Types**: Includes spam/fraud, NCII (non-consensual intimate imagery), disturbing/inappropriate content (violence, hate speech, harassment), and harmful activities (e.g., terrorism, bullying).
- **Report Submission**: Users can submit reports for policy violations, but reports do not guarantee removal or action.
