# MIT Open Learning brings Online Learning to MIT and the world

## Summary

- **AI-Enabled Lifelong Learning Platforms**: MIT Learn offers AI-driven lifelong learning resources, including MITx MicroMasters® programs for professional credentials, MITx Courses for MIT faculty-taught online classes, and MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW) providing free, open-licensed course materials from thousands of MIT classes.

- **Cross-Disciplinary AI & Global Challenges Curriculum**: Universal Learning provides interdisciplinary online curricula addressing global challenges (e.g., AI, climate, energy), while MIT Horizon delivers workforce-focused AI education, and MIT pK-12 creates learning experiences for young learners and educators.

- **Faculty & Institutional Support Tools**: MIT Open Learning supports faculty via Residential Education, Canvas integration, and Residential MITx for hybrid teaching; grants, awards, and research-based learning findings further enhance digital pedagogy and credentialing through the Digital Credentials Consortium.
