# Supported media formats | Android media | Android Developers

## Summary

- **Audio Codec Support**: Android supports a wide range of audio formats, including AAC (LC, HE-AACv1/v2, xHE-AAC, ELD), AMR (NB/WB), FLAC, MIDI, MP3, Opus, PCM/WAVE, and Vorbis, with varying encoder/decoder availability across Android versions. Encoder support starts from Android 3.0+ (e.g., AAC LC) to Android 16+ (e.g., APV), while decoders are generally backward-compatible.

- **Video Codec & Container Support**: Android supports video codecs like H.263, H.264 (BP/MP), H.265 (HEVC), MPEG-4 SP, VP8/VP9, AV1, and APV, with mandatory encoder/decoder support for AV1 (Android 14+) and APV (Android 16+). Containers include 3GPP (.3gp), MPEG-4 (.mp4), Matroska (.mkv), WebM, and MPEG-TS, with specific requirements for streaming (e.g., `moov` atom precedence).

- **Image & HDR Formats**: Android supports image formats BMP, GIF, JPEG, PNG, WebP (lossless/transparency from Android 10/4.2.1), HEIF (Android 8.0+), and AVIF (baseline profile, mandatory from Android 14). HDR formats include HLG10, HDR10, HDR10+, and Dolby Vision (8.4), with OEMs required to support HLG10 (user-generated content) and HDR10 (professional content).
