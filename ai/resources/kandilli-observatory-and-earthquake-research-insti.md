# Kandilli Observatory and Earthquake Research Institute

## Summary

- **Institute Overview**: Kandilli Observatory and Earthquake Research Institute (KOERI) at Boğaziçi University operates specialized departments (Earthquake Engineering, Geodesy, Geophysics), centers (e.g., Regional Earthquake-Tsunami Monitoring, Nuclear Tests Monitoring), and labs (e.g., Meteorology, Geomagnetism) for seismic and geophysical research.

- **Recent Collaborations & Initiatives**: Hosted Tsukuba University (Japan) delegation; participated in UNESCO CoastWAVE 2.0 Seferihisar project; recovered ocean-bottom seismometers in the Marmara Sea for data analysis; and advanced Seferihisar’s "Tsunami Ready" certification via community preparedness programs.

- **Data & Services**: Provides real-time earthquake monitoring (latest quakes, near real-time magnetograms), weather forecasting, and earthquake-tsunami information lines; archives 115+ years of climate data, reinterpreted via hackathons and seminars.
