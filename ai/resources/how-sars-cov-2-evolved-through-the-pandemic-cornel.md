# How SARS-CoV-2 evolved through the pandemic | Cornell Chronicle

## Summary

- **Pre-Omicron Evolution**: SARS-CoV-2 prioritized mutations enhancing transmissibility (e.g., Alpha, Delta variants), with ~80% protection from prior infection lasting ~1 year against reinfection.

- **Post-Omicron Shift**: Omicron’s emergence marked a transition to immune evasion, reducing natural immunity durability to negligible levels within months, driving rapid emergence of diverse subvariants (e.g., XBB, JN.1).

- **Population Immunity Dynamics**: Waning immunity post-Omicron led to recurrent waves, mirroring endemic coronaviruses/flu; updated vaccines remain critical for high-risk groups due to the virus’s adaptive persistence.
