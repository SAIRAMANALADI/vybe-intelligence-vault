# WeTeach_CS Home

## Summary

- **Mission & Impact**: WeTeach_CS (WTCS) is a K-12 teacher professional development program that has trained **600+ educators**, increasing CS-certified teachers, high school CS course offerings, student enrollment, and K-8 computational thinking access nationwide.

- **Key Initiatives**:
  - **AI Literacy for Innovation, Growth, and Networking (ALIGN)**: Prepares educators for AI-partnered education via white papers and task force recommendations.
  - **Texas CS Professional Development Week (TX CSPDWeek)**: Multi-format PD sessions (1-day to full-week) to enhance CS teaching practices.
  - **micro:bit Live USA South**: Promotes physical computing for accessible CS learning through educator/researcher collaboration.

- **Summit & Resources**:
  - **2026 WeTeach_CS Summit**: 2-day event with strands in AI/ML, cybersecurity, leadership, and broadening participation, targeting K-12 CS/cybersecurity educators and policymakers.
  - **Grade-Specific PD**: Offers structured resources for elementary, middle, high school, and administrators via workshops and newsletters.
