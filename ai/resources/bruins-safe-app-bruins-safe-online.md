# Bruins Safe App | Bruins Safe Online

## Summary

- **Real-time emergency response**: Bruins Safe app provides instant notifications, direct emergency contact with campus safety, and hazard reporting via mobile integration.

- **Air quality monitoring**: UCLA deploys strategically placed sensors across campus for localized, real-time air quality assessment, accessible via the [UCLA AirQual Dashboard](https://map.ucla.edu/aq/).

- **Multi-platform access**: App available for iOS ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bruins-safe/id1188801627)) and Android ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cutcom.apparmor.ucla)), complemented by [BruinALERT](https://bso.ucla.edu/bruinalert) and [Bruins Safe Online](https://bso.ucla.edu/).
