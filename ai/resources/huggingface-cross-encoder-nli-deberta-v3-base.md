## Summary

- **Dataset Overview**: SNLI (Stanford Natural Language Inference) is a collection of 570k human-written English sentence pairs labeled for entailment, contradiction, or neutral relationships, designed for training and evaluating natural language inference (NLI) models.

- **Structure & Format**: Each entry consists of a premise, hypothesis, and a gold label indicating the logical relationship (entailment, contradiction, or neutral) between them, standardized for machine learning applications.

- **Usage & Impact**: Widely used in NLP research for benchmarking NLI models, SNLI has significantly contributed to advancements in semantic understanding, transfer learning, and evaluation frameworks in deep learning.
