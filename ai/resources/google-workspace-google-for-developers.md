## Summary

- **Custom App Development**: Supports building Google Workspace add-ons, Chat apps, and automation scripts (via Apps Script) to extend functionality, integrate AI, and automate workflows (e.g., Sheets formulas, Form responses, Chat bots).

- **Distribution & Marketplace Integration**: Offers the Google Workspace Marketplace for publishing and distributing custom solutions to millions of users, with documentation and quickstart guides for rapid deployment.
