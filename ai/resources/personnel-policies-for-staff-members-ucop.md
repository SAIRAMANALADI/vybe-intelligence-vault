# Personnel Policies for Staff Members | UCOP

## Summary

- **Employment Framework**: UC staff are public sector employees governed by statute, with personnel policies subject to amendment or deletion at any time; variances require approval from the President, Chancellor, Laboratory Director, or Senior VP—Business and Finance.
- **Key Policies**: Includes FMLA, CFRA, pregnancy disability leave, compensation structures (PPSM-34), complaint resolution (PPSM-70), disciplinary actions (PPSM-62/64), and whistleblower protections.
- **Operational Policies**: Covers recruitment (PPSM-20), performance management (PPSM-23), reasonable accommodation (PPSM-81), anti-discrimination, and lactation accommodation (PPSM-84).
