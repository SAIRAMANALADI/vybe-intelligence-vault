# Publications & Data - Cornell Research & Innovation

## Summary

- **Cornell Research Expenditures Reports** provide high-level analytics of research funding, broken down by college, discipline, and funding source, with archival data available from fiscal year 2020 onward.

- **Monthly Research Awards Reports** document extramural funding finalized by Cornell’s Office of Sponsored Programs, with archives dating back to 2019.

- **Publication Access** requires contacting `research@cornell.edu` for reports prior to fiscal year 2020, while post-2020 reports are directly downloadable in PDF format.
