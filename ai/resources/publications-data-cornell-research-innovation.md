# Publications & Data - Cornell Research & Innovation

## Summary

- **Cornell Research Expenditures Reports (FY 2018–2025)**: Annual PDFs detailing high-level Cornell research funding, broken down by college, discipline, funding source, and other analytics, with archival access via `research@cornell.edu` for pre-2018 data.

- **Monthly Research Awards Archives (2019–2026)**: Granular monthly PDF reports from Cornell’s Office of Sponsored Programs listing extramural funding awards, including 2026–2025 (A2026xx/A2025xx) and 2024–2019 (A2024xx–A2019xx) datasets.

- **Annual Research Reports (FY 2018–2022)**: Aggregated institutional reports summarizing key metrics of research activity, technological innovation, and faculty honors/awards, available for FY 2022–2018 via Cornell’s research portal.
