# Our Opportunities - University of California-Los Angeles Scholarship Application

## Summary

- **Scholarship Portal**: UCLA's AcademicWorks platform provides a centralized system for students to apply for over 2,000 merit-based, need-based, and department-specific scholarships, fellowships, grants, and prizes, with application processing in 20-30 minutes.

- **Eligibility & Maintenance**: Open to all students in good academic standing (no strict GPA requirement); recipients must maintain Satisfactory Academic Progress, and financial need-based awards require submission of FAFSA/Dream Act by the priority deadline (typically March 2).

- **Award Structure & Timing**: Awards are typically for the upcoming academic year/summer term, with disbursement details outlined in the Bruin Financial Aid Letter (BFAL); prospective students can only apply post-admission.
