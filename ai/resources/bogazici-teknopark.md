# Boğaziçi Teknopark

## Summary

- **Boğaziçi Teknopark** operates two portals: [Kandilli](https://argeportal.kandilliteknopark.com) and [Etiler](https://argeportal.bogaziciteknopark.com), with separate application links for incubation ([Kuluçka](https://kulucka.bogaziciteknopark.com/)) and techpark entry ([Teknopark Başvuru](https://argeportal.kandilliteknopark.com/onbasvuruformu)).

- **Application workflow** consists of five stages: online submission, preliminary project review, peer evaluation, board approval, and lease finalization before onboarding.

- **Ecosystem highlights** include 50+ resident firms (e.g., Mission Control Games, AEMRobot, Agrowiss) and strategic collaborations (e.g., TUSAŞ, STM) with recent investments (e.g., X Barista’s €6M valuation, Mission Control Games’ $4M funding).
