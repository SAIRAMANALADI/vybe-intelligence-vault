# plos-thesaurus/README.md at master · PLOS/plos-thesaurus · GitHub

## Summary

- PLOS Thesaurus employs a polyhierarchical structure with >10,000 terms, linking broader/narrower relationships (e.g., "White blood cells" → "Blood cells" and "Immune cells") to top-tier categories like "Biology and life sciences."

- Subject Area indexing uses Machine Aided Indexing (MAI) with a Rulebase to disambiguate terms (e.g., "Sodium" → "Voltage-gated sodium channels" vs. elemental sodium) and ranks matches by frequency to select top eight terms for article display.

- Hierarchical term relationships enable precise searches: Subject field queries return only top-eight terms, while All fields searches include any article with a term match; narrower terms (e.g., "White blood cells") inherit broader term (e.g., "Blood cells") results.
