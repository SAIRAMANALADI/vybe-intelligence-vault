# Get an app for Mastodon - Mastodon

## Summary

- **Official Mastodon apps** are available for iOS (`org.joinmastodon.android` on Google Play, `Mastodon for iPhone` on App Store) and Android, providing native mobile experiences with features like reblogging and polls.
- **Mastodon’s API** is open-source, well-documented, and supports third-party app development, enabling custom clients and integrations via [docs.joinmastodon.org/client/intro](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/client/intro/).
- **Third-party clients** span platforms (Android/iOS/Web/Desktop) with options like **Tusky** (Android, open-source), **Ice Cubes** (iOS, open-source), and **Pinafore** (Web, open-source), offering diverse UX/UI approaches.
