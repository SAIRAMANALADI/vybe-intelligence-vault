# Get an app for Mastodon - Mastodon

## Summary

- **Official Mastodon apps** are available for iOS ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mastodon-for-iphone/id1571998974)) and Android ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.joinmastodon.android)), providing native mobile access with features like reblogging and polling.

- **Mastodon’s API** is open-source, well-documented, and supports third-party app development across platforms (Android, iOS, web, desktop, retro computing).

- **Third-party clients** include options like **Tusky** (Android), **Ice Cubes** (iOS), and **Pinafore** (web), offering varied UIs, open-source availability, and platform-specific optimizations.
