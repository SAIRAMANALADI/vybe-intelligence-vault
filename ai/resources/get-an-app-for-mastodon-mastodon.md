# Get an app for Mastodon - Mastodon

## Summary

- **Official Mastodon apps** are available for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mastodon-for-iphone/id1571998974) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.joinmastodon.android), providing native mobile client support with push notifications and home screen integration.

- **Mastodon’s open API** enables third-party development, with well-documented endpoints ([docs](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/client/intro/)) facilitating custom clients, integrations, and alternative UIs.

- **Third-party clients** span platforms (Android/iOS/Web/Desktop) with varying licenses (free/paid/open-source), including **Tusky** (Android), **Ivory** (iOS), **Pinafore** (Web), and **Elk** (Web).
