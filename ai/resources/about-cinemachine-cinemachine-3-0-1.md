# About Cinemachine | Cinemachine | 3.0.1

## Summary

- **Modular Camera Control**: Cinemachine provides a suite of procedural modules for real-time camera control, including tracking, composition, blending, and shot transitions, reducing manual scripting and adapting dynamically to scene changes (e.g., terrain, animations, or object movements).

- **Unity Integration & Compatibility**: Supports Unity 2022.2.16f1+, integrates with Timeline, Post-Processing (HDRP/URP), UGUI, and allows custom extensions; deprecated CM2.X classes are supported but slated for removal in future releases.

- **Version Upgrades**: CM 3.0 introduces a major API/data format change; manual intervention is required for upgrades from CM 2.X or legacy Asset Store versions, with deprecated CM2 support removable via `CINEMACHINE_NO_CM2_SUPPORT` define.
