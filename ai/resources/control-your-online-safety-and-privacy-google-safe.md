# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Non-Sale Policy**: Google enforces strict data minimization principles and explicitly prohibits selling user personal data to third parties, embedding privacy as a core architectural constraint in all products.
- **User-Centric Privacy Controls**: Provides granular, accessible privacy settings (e.g., ad personalization toggles, data deletion tools) enabling users to dynamically adjust data exposure without compromising functionality.
- **Privacy-by-Design Framework**: Implements "Private by Design" principles across development lifecycles, integrating legal compliance (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) and security measures (e.g., encryption, differential privacy) as default configurations.
