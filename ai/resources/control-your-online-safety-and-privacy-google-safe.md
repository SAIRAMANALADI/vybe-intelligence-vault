# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Non-Sale Policy**: Google enforces strict data minimization principles and explicitly prohibits selling user personal information, embedding privacy as a core architectural requirement in all products.

- **User-Controlled Privacy Tools**: Provides granular, accessible privacy controls enabling users to customize settings (e.g., ad personalization, data sharing) without requiring technical expertise.

- **Responsible AI Integration**: Privacy-by-design extends to AI systems, ensuring compliance with ethical frameworks (e.g., differential privacy, federated learning) to mitigate risks in data processing and model training.
