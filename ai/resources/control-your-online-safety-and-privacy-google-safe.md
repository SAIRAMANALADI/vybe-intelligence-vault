# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Non-Sale Policy**: Google enforces strict data minimization principles and explicitly prohibits selling user personal data to third parties, embedding privacy as a core architectural requirement in all products.
- **User-Centric Privacy Controls**: Provides granular, accessible privacy settings (e.g., auto-delete controls, ad personalization toggles) to empower users with transparent data management without compromising functionality.
- **Responsible AI Integration**: Privacy-by-design extends to AI systems, ensuring models are trained/operated under strict data governance frameworks to prevent unauthorized data exposure or misuse.
