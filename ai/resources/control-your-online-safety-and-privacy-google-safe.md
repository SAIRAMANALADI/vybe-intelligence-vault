# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Non-Commercialization**: Google enforces a strict policy of not selling user personal data to any third parties, embedding privacy as a core architectural principle in all products and services.

- **User-Centric Privacy Controls**: Provides granular, accessible privacy settings enabling users to customize data sharing, retention, and exposure according to individual preferences.

- **Responsible AI Integration**: Implements privacy-by-design frameworks in AI systems, ensuring data minimization, secure processing, and compliance with evolving privacy regulations.
