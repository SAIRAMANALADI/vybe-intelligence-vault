# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Non-Sale Policy**: Google enforces strict data minimization principles and explicitly prohibits the sale of personal user data to third parties, embedding privacy as a core architectural requirement in all products.
- **User-Centric Privacy Controls**: Provides granular, accessible privacy settings (e.g., auto-delete timers, activity controls) enabling users to customize data retention and sharing preferences without sacrificing functionality.
- **Responsible AI Integration**: Implements "privacy-by-design" frameworks in AI systems, ensuring default encryption, on-device processing where feasible, and compliance with global privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) during model training and inference.
