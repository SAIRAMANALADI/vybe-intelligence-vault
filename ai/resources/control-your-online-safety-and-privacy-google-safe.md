# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Non-Sale Policy**: Google enforces strict data minimization principles and explicitly prohibits selling user personal information to third parties, embedding privacy as a core architectural requirement in all products.
- **User-Centric Privacy Controls**: Provides granular, accessible privacy settings enabling users to customize data sharing, retention, and visibility preferences across Google services.
- **Responsible AI Integration**: Implements privacy-by-design frameworks in AI/ML systems, ensuring compliance with global regulations (e.g., GDPR) through techniques like federated learning and differential privacy.
