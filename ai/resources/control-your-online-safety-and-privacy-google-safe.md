# Control Your Online Safety and Privacy - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Non-Sale Policy**: Google enforces strict data minimization principles and explicitly prohibits the sale of user personal information, embedding privacy as a core architectural requirement in all products.
- **User-Centric Privacy Controls**: Provides granular, accessible privacy settings (e.g., ad personalization, location history) allowing users to dynamically adjust data exposure without compromising core functionality.
- **Responsible AI Integration**: Privacy-by-design extends to AI systems, ensuring default encryption, on-device processing where feasible, and compliance with frameworks like GDPR and CCPA through technical safeguards.
