# SLAC Training

## Summary

- **SLAC Web Training Portal**: Provides centralized access to web-based training modules; accessible via [SLAC Training Portal](https://slactraining.slac.stanford.edu/web-training-portal-0).
- **SLAC Training Assignment (STA) Tool**: Manages mandatory training assignments for SLAC personnel; requires SLAC Windows Authentication ([STA Portal](https://www-bis2.slac.stanford.edu/training/sta/eta_reportall.asp?opt=6)).
- **Internal Training Resources**: Includes course catalog, registration system, and calendar for SLAC-specific training; all require SLAC Windows Authentication ([Course Catalog](https://www-bis2.slac.stanford.edu/training/sta/course_catalog_index.asp), [Registration](https://www-bis2.slac.stanford.edu/slaconly/training/), [Calendar](https://www-bis2.slac.stanford.edu/training/sta/calendar.asp)).
