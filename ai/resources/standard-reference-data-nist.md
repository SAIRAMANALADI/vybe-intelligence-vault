# Standard Reference Data | NIST

## Summary

- **NIST Standard Reference Data (SRD)**: Curated, expert-assessed databases covering chemistry, physics, materials science, and engineering, with 49 free and 41 fee-based datasets compliant with rigorous evaluation criteria.
- **Key SRD Products**: Includes REFPROP (thermodynamic/transport properties), NIST Mass Spectral Libraries (2026 edition), ICSD (210,000+ inorganic crystal structures), and SESSA (electron spectroscopy simulation, now free).
- **Access & Support**: SRD data available via [NIST SRD portal](https://www.nist.gov/srd), with customer support at `data@nist.gov` and curated portals for mass spectrometry, atomic spectroscopy, and thermodynamics.
