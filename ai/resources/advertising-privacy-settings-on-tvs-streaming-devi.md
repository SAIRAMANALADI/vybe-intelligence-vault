# Advertising Privacy Settings on TVs & Streaming Devices - The NAI: Network Advertising Initiative

## Summary

- **Advertising ID Management**: TV platforms (Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Google TV, Roku, Samsung TV) provide settings to opt out, reset, or restrict access to unique advertising IDs used for interest-based or targeted advertising across apps and services.

- **Platform-Specific Controls**:
  - **Amazon Fire TV**: Disable "Interest-Based Ads" or reset the advertising ID via `Settings > Preferences > Privacy Settings`.
  - **Apple TV**: Toggle "Allow Apps to Ask to Track" off in `Settings > General > Privacy & Security > Tracking`.
  - **Google TV/Android TV**: Delete or reset the advertising ID via `Settings > Privacy > Ads`.
  - **Roku**: Disable "Personalize ads" or reset the advertising ID via `Settings > Privacy > Advertising`.
  - **Samsung TV**: Reset the PSID (Personalized Service ID) via `Settings > All Settings > General & Privacy > Terms & Privacy > Reset PSID`.

- **Cross-Platform Note**: Default settings vary; opting out or resetting IDs may not fully prevent tracking but limits cross-context identification for advertising purposes.
