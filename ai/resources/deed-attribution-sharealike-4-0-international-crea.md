## Summary

- A **canonical URL** is a web address designated as the preferred version of a page to consolidate ranking signals and avoid duplicate content issues.
- Specified via the `<link rel="canonical">` HTML tag or HTTP header, it instructs search engines to prioritize the canonical version over duplicate or similar URLs.
- Critical for SEO, it prevents dilution of link equity and ensures consistent indexing, particularly in cases of URL parameters, session IDs, or mirrored content.
