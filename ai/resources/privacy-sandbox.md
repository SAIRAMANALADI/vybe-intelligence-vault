# Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Phased Deprecation**: Google is phasing out certain Privacy Sandbox technologies, including third-party cookies, as part of its initiative to enhance user privacy while maintaining ad relevance.
- **Status Tracking**: The [Privacy Sandbox feature status](https://developers.google.com/overview/status) page provides real-time updates on the implementation and deprecation status of individual APIs and platform features.
- **Retention of Documentation**: Google will retain documentation for all Privacy Sandbox technologies, including those not implemented or scheduled for deprecation, for reference and historical purposes.
