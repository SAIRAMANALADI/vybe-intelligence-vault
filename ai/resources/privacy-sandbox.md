# Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Phased Deprecation**: Google is phasing out certain Privacy Sandbox technologies, including third-party cookies, as part of its initiative to enhance user privacy while maintaining ad relevance.
- **Status Tracking**: The [Privacy Sandbox feature status](https://developers.google.com/overview/status) page provides real-time updates on the lifecycle (deprecation, testing, or retention) of individual APIs and platform features.
- **API Alternatives**: Private advertising APIs (e.g., Topics, Protected Audience) are being developed to replace third-party identifiers, enabling interest-based advertising without cross-site tracking.
