# Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Phased Deprecation**: Google is phasing out certain Privacy Sandbox technologies, with detailed status updates available on the [Privacy Sandbox feature status](https://developers.google.com/overview/status) page.
- **Third-Party Cookie Restrictions**: Third-party cookies are being restricted, impacting tracking and cross-site functionality as part of the Privacy Sandbox initiative.
- **Private Advertising APIs**: New APIs (e.g., Topics, Protected Audience) are being developed to replace third-party identifiers for privacy-preserving advertising use cases.
