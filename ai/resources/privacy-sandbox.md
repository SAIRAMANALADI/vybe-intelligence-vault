# Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Phased Deprecation**: Google is phasing out certain Privacy Sandbox technologies, with detailed status updates available on the [Privacy Sandbox feature status](https://developers.google.com/overview/status) page.
- **Third-Party Cookie Restrictions**: Third-party cookies are being restricted, with documentation on their functionality and alternatives provided in the [Cookies](https://privacysandbox.google.com/cookies) section.
- **Private Advertising APIs**: New APIs for private advertising (e.g., Topics, Protected Audience) are being developed to replace third-party identifiers, with technical details in the [Private advertising APIs](https://privacysandbox.google.com/private-advertising) section.
