# Privacy Sandbox

## Summary

- **Phased-out Privacy Sandbox technologies**: Google is deprecating certain Privacy Sandbox APIs and features, as detailed in the [Privacy Sandbox feature status](https://developers.google.com/overview/status) and [Update on Plans for Privacy Sandbox Technologies](https://privacysandbox.com/news/update-on-plans-for-privacy-sandbox-technologies/).

- **Third-party cookie restrictions**: Google is moving forward with plans to limit third-party cookies, as outlined in the [Cookies](https://privacysandbox.google.com/cookies) section of the Privacy Sandbox initiative.

- **Private advertising APIs**: Google continues to develop and refine APIs for privacy-preserving advertising, as described in the [Private advertising APIs](https://privacysandbox.google.com/private-advertising) section.
