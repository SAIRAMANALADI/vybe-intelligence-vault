# Jennifer Moss - Professional Speaker on Burnout and Work Culture

## Summary

- **Burnout Research & Prevention**: Jennifer Moss, Chief Research and Strategy Officer at the Global Wellbeing Group, specializes in identifying the six root causes of workplace burnout using behavioral science and organizational psychology, providing evidence-based strategies to mitigate chronic stress and enhance team performance.

- **Keynote & Workshop Expertise**: Delivers over 750 in-person/virtual sessions blending behavioral science, leadership psychology, and relatable storytelling, offering actionable tools to improve culture, prevent burnout, and drive high-performing teams (e.g., "Combating Burnout to Reach Our Goals" keynote).

- **Publications & Tools**: Author of *The Burnout Epidemic* (Harvard Business Press) and *Unlocking Happiness at Work* (2nd Ed.), providing research-backed frameworks (e.g., 7-factor Burnout Self-Assessment) and practical resources (e.g., Burnout Workbooks) for leaders to redesign work structures sustainably.
