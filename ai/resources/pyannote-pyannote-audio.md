# pyannote/pyannote-audio

## Summary

- **Modular Neural Toolkit**: Provides PyTorch-based neural modules for core speaker diarization tasks: speech activity detection (SAD), speaker change detection (SCD), overlapped speech detection (OSD), and speaker embedding extraction.

- **Pretrained Models & Pipelines**: Includes pre-trained models for end-to-end diarization pipelines, optimized for real-time and offline processing with support for multi-speaker scenarios.

- **Research & Deployment Focus**: Designed for reproducibility (Jupyter Notebooks) and integration into production systems, with modules for speaker verification and recognition.
