# DMCA | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **Expanded Copyright Office Authority**: H.R. 6028 grants the U.S. Copyright Office (USCO) expanded regulatory and enforcement powers, including the ability to unilaterally issue binding regulations without public notice-and-comment procedures under the Administrative Procedure Act (APA).

- **Reduced Judicial Oversight**: The bill weakens judicial review of USCO decisions by limiting courts' ability to invalidate regulations or rulings, shifting power from the judiciary to the Copyright Office for copyright-related disputes.

- **Accelerated Legislative Process**: The bill was passed via voice vote with minimal debate, bypassing standard committee review and public scrutiny, raising concerns about transparency and stakeholder input in copyright policy.
