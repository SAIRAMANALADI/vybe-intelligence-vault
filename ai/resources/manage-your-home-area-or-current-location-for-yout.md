# Manage your home area or current location for YouTube TV - Computer - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Home Area & Local Networks**: YouTube TV requires a verified home zip code (via device location or manual entry) to stream local networks; outside this area, only local-to-current-location networks are accessible, and home-area local networks cannot be recorded or watched.

- **Location Management**: Users can update their home area (max 2 changes/year) or current playback area (via web/Smart TV app) but must be physically present in the new area; VPN/proxy usage blocks streaming for restricted channels.

- **Troubleshooting**: Location permissions must be enabled in browser/device settings; clearing cache/cookies or disabling VPNs may resolve playback issues, while managed devices (e.g., work/school Chrome devices) may restrict location changes.
