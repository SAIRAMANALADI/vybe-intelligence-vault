# Canvas@Cornell

## Summary

- **Authentication Endpoints**: Cornell Canvas login endpoints include NetID (`https://canvas.cornell.edu/login/saml`), Weill CWID (`https://canvas.cornell.edu/login/41`), and Guest (`https://canvas.cornell.edu/login/canvas`), with security logout procedures via CUWebLogin credential clearing.

- **Copyright & Academic Integrity**: Course materials on Canvas are protected under Cornell’s copyright policy; unauthorized distribution or commercialization of materials constitutes academic misconduct per the [Code of Academic Integrity](https://cuinfo.cornell.edu/aic.cfm).

- **Terms of Use**: Accessing Canvas binds users to Instructure’s terms, enforceable by continued use or agreement via "I Accept," with non-compliance blocking access to services.
