# Bedside Medicine Exam Skills | Stanford Medicine 25 | Stanford Medicine

## Summary

- **Stanford Medicine 25** promotes mastery of bedside clinical examination techniques, emphasizing hands-on diagnostic skills over reliance on technology, led by Dr. Abraham Verghese.

- **Core initiative** began with 25 foundational physical exam maneuvers (expandable beyond this number) as a structured starting point for skill development in medical training.

- **Resources include** video guides (e.g., Parkinson’s disease exams, dermatological assessments), podcasts, and research-backed articles on therapeutic touch and patient-centered draping techniques.
