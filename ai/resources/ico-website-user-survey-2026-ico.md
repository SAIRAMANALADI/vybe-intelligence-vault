# ICO website user survey 2026 | ICO

## Summary

- **Lawful Basis**: Processing of survey data relies on explicit consent under **UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)**; special category data (e.g., disability/accessibility needs) requires **explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a)**.

- **Data Collection & Linking**: Anonymous survey responses become identifiable if users opt into future research, linking responses to **name/email** and accessibility data for targeted improvements.

- **Data Processors & Retention**: Survey data is processed by **SmartSurvey** (data processor); retention follows ICO’s [retention schedule](https://ico.org.uk/media2/anjfdsms/retention-and-disposal-policy.pdf), with users retaining rights to withdraw consent or request deletion.
