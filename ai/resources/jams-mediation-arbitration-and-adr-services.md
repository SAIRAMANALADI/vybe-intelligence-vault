# JAMS: Mediation, Arbitration and ADR Services

## Summary

- **JAMS** is the world's largest private provider of **Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)** services, handling **21,000+ disputes annually** with **500+ neutrals** across **29 locations** globally.

- Offers specialized **arbitration, mediation, neutral analysis, and court-appointed neutral (referee/special master)** services, supporting **business/commercial, class action, construction, employment, entertainment, energy, family law, healthcare, insurance, personal injury, and real property disputes**.

- Recognized in **Chambers USA 2026** with **26 neutrals earning 30 rankings** across seven categories, and provides a **dedicated Technology Industry Group** for resolving complex, cross-border technology disputes with tailored ADR processes.
