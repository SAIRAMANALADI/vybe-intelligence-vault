# Google For Families Help

## Summary

- **Family Link** enables parental control over child Google Accounts, including app management, screen time limits, and activity monitoring via supervised accounts.

- **Family Group Management** allows creation/management of shared Google services (e.g., Play Library, YouTube TV, family calendar) with granular permissions and payment methods.

- **Digital Wellbeing & Content Controls** provide supervised account settings for activity tracking, sensitive content filtering, and Digital Wellbeing features.
