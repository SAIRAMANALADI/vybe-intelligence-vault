# Google For Families Help

## Summary

- **Family Link** enables parental control over child Google Accounts, including app management, screen time limits, and content filtering via supervised accounts.

- **Family Group Management** allows creation/management of multi-user family units, shared payment methods, and resource allocation (e.g., Google Play balances, subscriptions).

- **Family Services** facilitate shared access to Google ecosystem resources (e.g., YouTube TV, Play Library, Photos) with granular controls for supervised accounts (e.g., sensitive content warnings, Digital Wellbeing monitoring).
