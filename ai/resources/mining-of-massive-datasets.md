# Mining of Massive Datasets

## Summary

- **Book Overview**: *"Mining of Massive Datasets"* (3rd ed.) covers scalable data mining techniques, including Spark, TensorFlow, minhashing, community detection, SimRank, graph algorithms, decision trees, and deep learning (new Ch. 13), based on Stanford’s CS246/CS345A courses.

- **Course Resources**: Stanford provides lecture slides (PPT/PDF), video lectures (YouTube), and supplementary materials (e.g., French slides for Ch. 4–5, 8–10) for CS246 (MapReduce-focused) and CS345A (advanced projects), with EC2 support via Amazon.

- **Accessibility**: Full book (603-page PDF) and errata are freely available; instructors can use Gradiance Automated Homework System; non-Stanford students can earn a graduate certificate via SCPD.
