# Materials in Human Experience | Materials Science and Engineering | MIT OpenCourseWare

## Summary

- **Course Code & Focus**: MIT 3.094 (Spring 2004) explores **materials science in human culture**, covering ancient/modern societies' selection, transformation, and symbolic use of natural materials (e.g., glass in Egypt/Rome, metals in Mesoamerica, Inca textiles).

- **Interdisciplinary Structure**: Combines **materials science/engineering** (metallurgy, solid-state chemistry) with **humanities** (ancient history, history of science/technology), taught by Prof. Heather Lechtman.

- **Pedagogical Approach**: Features **laboratory/workshop sessions** for hands-on material analysis, complemented by curated readings/tools/specimens (e.g., Silver-Copper alloy microstructures) to bridge theory and archaeological/technical applications.
