# Android Enterprise Help

## Summary

- **Android Enterprise management** requires selecting an EMM solution, sourcing compatible devices, and configuring enrollment methods (e.g., Zero-touch, work profiles) for device deployment and policy enforcement.

- **App distribution** is managed via Managed Google Play, supporting public, private, and system apps with advanced features like license management, silent updates, and app configuration.

- **Security & compliance** are enforced through Google Play Protect, Device Trust policies, and factory reset protection, with additional options for managed Google domains and GDPR-compliant app hosting.
