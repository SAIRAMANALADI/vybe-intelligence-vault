# Privacy @ Oracle | Oracle

## Summary

- Oracle maintains **multiple specialized privacy policies** tailored to different business operations (e.g., general web use, cloud services, recruiting, healthcare research, and IoT/Dyn services), each detailing data collection, processing, and user rights under GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations.

- **Data subject rights** (access, deletion, correction, or restriction of personal data) are explicitly supported under GDPR/CCPA, with mechanisms for users to exercise these rights via policy-specific links or the [Privacy Choices](https://www.oracle.com/legal/privacy/privacy-choices/) portal.

- Oracle adheres to **international data transfer frameworks**, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and UK Extension, with compliance details available in the [Services Privacy Policy](https://www.oracle.com/legal/privacy/services-privacy-policy/).
