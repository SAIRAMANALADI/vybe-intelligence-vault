# Welcome to Utilities & Energy Management | Utilities & Energy Management

## Summary

- **Mission & Services**: Utilities and Energy Management (UEM) at UT Austin provides essential utilities (power, cooling, steam, air) to sustain campus operations, research, and infrastructure with a focus on sustainability and resilience.
- **Team & Infrastructure**: UEM employs over 200 professionals to maintain and operate critical utility systems, ensuring uninterrupted service across the campus.
- **Outage & Safety Tools**: UEM offers real-time power outage tracking via [Power Outage Map](https://outagemap.utilities.utexas.edu/) and mandates compliance with the [UT Dig Safe](https://operations.utexas.edu/resources/ut-dig-safe) program for all excavation projects.
