# Google’s Efforts to Combat Online Child Sexual Abuse Material FAQs - Transparency Report Help Center

## Summary

- **Detection & Reporting Mechanisms**: Google employs hash matching (e.g., CSAI Match) and machine learning classifiers to proactively detect known and novel CSAM, followed by human review; detected CSAM is reported via NCMEC’s CyberTipline, with supplemental reports for severe cases (e.g., hands-on abuse or production).

- **GenAI Safety Protections**: Google integrates child safety measures into generative AI models, including filtering CSAM-seeking prompts, blocking violative outputs, and conducting pre-launch safety testing; collaborates with coalitions (e.g., Technology Coalition) to share best practices.

- **Ecosystem Collaboration & Enforcement**: Google shares CSAM hashes (via NCMEC’s hash database) and signals (via Project Lantern) with industry partners; enforces account restrictions (warnings, suspensions) and demotes sites with high CSAM prevalence; provides tools like the Content Safety API to aid third-party detection.
