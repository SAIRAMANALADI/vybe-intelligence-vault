# Tidyverse

## Summary

- **Unified Framework**: The tidyverse is a cohesive collection of R packages (`dplyr`, `ggplot2`, `stringr`, `tibble`, `readr`, `forcats`, `tidyr`, `lubridate`, `purrr`) designed for data science, sharing consistent syntax, grammar, and data structures (e.g., tibbles).

- **Installation & Learning**: Installed via `install.packages("tidyverse")`; core functionality is documented in *R for Data Science (2e)*, available [online](https://r4ds.hadley.nz/) or as a [book](http://amzn.to/2aHLAQ1).

- **Support & Community**: Troubleshooting follows [reprex](http://tidyverse.org/help/#reprex) guidelines; the ecosystem is maintained with community resources and [privacy policies](https://www.tidyverse.org/google_privacy_policy).
