# GeeksforGeeks Practice - Leading Online Coding Platform - GeeksforGeeks

## Summary

- **Curated Interview Practice**: GeeksforGeeks Practice offers **GFG 160**, a structured repository of 160 high-frequency interview problems categorized by topic and difficulty, complete with editorials and video explanations for targeted preparation.

- **Multi-Faceted Problem Filtering**: Supports **topic-wise, company-wise, and difficulty-based filtering** (Easy/Medium/Hard) for coding problems, including dedicated sections for DSA fundamentals (arrays, trees, graphs) and language-specific practice (C++, Java, Python).

- **Daily & Advanced Resources**: Features **Problem of the Day (POTD)** for consistent practice, alongside advanced algorithmic topics (e.g., segment trees, Trie, BIT) and interview-focused courses like **Placement 360** and **DSA + System Design**.
