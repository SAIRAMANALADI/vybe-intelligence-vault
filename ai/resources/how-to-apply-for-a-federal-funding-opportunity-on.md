# How to Apply for a Federal Funding Opportunity on Grants.gov – Grants.gov Community Blog

## Summary

- **Registration**: Single-time account creation on [Grants.gov](https://www.grants.gov) required; supports both individual and organizational profiles with role-based access control (e.g., AOR, Expanded AOR).
- **Eligibility & Search**: Funding opportunities filtered by entity type (e.g., nonprofit, government) and eligibility criteria; use [Grants.gov Search](https://www.grants.gov/search-grants) to identify relevant NOFOs.
- **Application Workflow**: Applications submitted via Workspace, a collaborative environment for form completion, error-checking (`Check Application` tool), and submission by authorized users.
