## Summary

- **PECR & UK GDPR Compliance**: Direct marketing under PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations) requires adherence to UK GDPR when personal data is involved; non-compliance risks legal penalties.

- **Soft Opt-In for Charities (PECR 22(3A))**: Charities can send electronic marketing without explicit consent if specific conditions (e.g., existing relationship, clear opt-out) are met under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
