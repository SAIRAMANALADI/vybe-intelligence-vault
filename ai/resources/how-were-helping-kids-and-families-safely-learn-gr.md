# How we’re helping kids and families safely learn, grow and play online

## Summary

- **Content Curation & Promotion**: YouTube Kids enforces stricter content guidelines via third-party expert-defined quality principles, increasing high-quality content viewership by 45%; Google Play’s Teacher Approved program expands to more app categories, requiring age-appropriateness, enrichment, and security compliance reviewed by educators and child specialists.

- **Parental Controls & Supervision**: Family Link introduces "Today Only" screen time overrides and unified child location tracking; Google TV and Assistant enable parent-managed watchlists and media provider restrictions, enforcing supervised content access for minors.

- **Default Protections & Compliance**: Default SafeSearch, autoplay disablement, and location-sharing restrictions for users under 18; enforced Families Policy for child-targeted apps with a compliance badge in Play Data safety sections; age-sensitive ad targeting blocked for minors.
