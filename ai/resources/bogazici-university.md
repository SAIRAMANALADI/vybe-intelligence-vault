## Summary

- **Institutional Structure**: Boğaziçi University comprises 6 faculties, 3 institutes, 6 schools of higher education, and multiple research centers, with governance overseen by a rectorate and gender equality policies enforced via a dedicated plan.

- **Academic & Research Infrastructure**: Offers 150+ undergraduate/graduate programs, 100+ exchange partnerships (Erasmus+), a technopark (Boğaziçi Teknopark), and centralized project management via the Research Web Site and Project Office.

- **Operational Systems**: Implements BUIS (student management), IT services (bilgiislem.boun.edu.tr), emergency protocols (1100), and mobile access via iOS/Android apps (BUCampus) with multilingual support (TR/EN).
