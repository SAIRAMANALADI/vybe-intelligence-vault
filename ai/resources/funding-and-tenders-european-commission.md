# Funding and tenders - European Commission

## Summary

- **EU funding** supports projects aligning with policy objectives (e.g., research, innovation, climate action) via competitive "calls for proposals," where selected applicants receive financial contributions for project execution.

- **EU tenders** are procurement mechanisms where the European Commission awards contracts for goods/services/works through "calls for tenders," selecting vendors based on submitted offers to fulfill operational needs.

- **Management framework** ensures EU funding/tenders adhere to structured rules (eligibility, transparency, accountability) with tools like Tenders Electronic Daily (TED) and mobile apps for access and reporting.
