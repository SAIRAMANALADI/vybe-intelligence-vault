# [1907.12748] Influence Flowers of Academic Entities

## Summary

- **Visual Metaphor**: Introduces *Influence Flower*, an ego-centric graph visualizing bidirectional influence between academic entities (e.g., researchers, papers, institutions) using petal-based strength encoding, derived from Microsoft Academic Graph (212M authors, 176M publications, 1.2B citations).

- **Interactive Framework**: Deploys *Influence Map*, a web app enabling dynamic queries, temporal comparisons, and cross-domain analysis (e.g., academic vs. Twitter influence) with case studies on career trajectories, delayed recognition, and interdisciplinary trends.

- **Methodology**: Implements a visual comparison method to highlight temporal shifts in influence patterns, supporting data-driven inquiries into research evolution, institutional profiles, and conference topical trends.
