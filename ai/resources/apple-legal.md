# Apple - Legal

## Summary

- **Hardware Warranties**: Apple’s limited warranty supplements statutory consumer rights, with terms, exclusions, and regional variations detailed in [Apple’s warranty documentation](https://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/).
- **Software Licensing**: End-user license agreements (EULAs) for Apple software are publicly accessible; developer-specific licensing terms are outlined separately on the [Apple Developer portal](https://developer.apple.com/softwarelicensing/).
- **Privacy & Compliance**: Apple’s [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) governs data handling across services, while the [Compliance program](https://www.apple.com/compliance/) ensures adherence to legal and ethical standards.
