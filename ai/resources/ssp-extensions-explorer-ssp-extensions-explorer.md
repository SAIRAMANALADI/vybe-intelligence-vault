# SSP Extensions Explorer | SSP Extensions Explorer

## Summary

- **SSP Extensions Explorer** is an interactive platform by IIASA providing extensions of the Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs) for comparative analysis of socioeconomic and environmental indicators across countries and scenarios.

- **Core modules** include *Global Overview* (cross-country indicator comparison), *Country Profiles* (country-specific SSP trajectories), *Behind The Numbers* (methodological rationale), and *Data Explorer* (custom visualization and data extraction).

- **Additional resources** include *Learn More & Download* (documentation and raw data access) and *About* (platform purpose and IIASA affiliation).
