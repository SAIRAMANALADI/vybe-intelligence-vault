# Home | SAM.gov

## Summary

- **SAM.gov** is the official U.S. government system for federal contracting, entity information, federal hierarchy, federal assistance, entity reporting, and wage determinations.

- **Key functionalities** include entity registration for Unique Entity ID (UEI), contract awards data search (replacing FPDS.gov), subcontracting plan reporting (via SAM.gov), and a new Federal Assistance Listings API for bulk data access.

- **Modernization updates** include retiring eSRS.gov (subcontracting reporting now in SAM.gov) and releasing modernized FAR/DFARS Representations and Certifications on March 24, 2026.
