# Creating a Happier Workplace Is Possible — and Worth It

## Summary

- **Causal productivity boost**: Employee happiness correlates with a **13% productivity increase**, demonstrating direct ROI for workplace well-being initiatives.

- **Engagement deficit drivers**: Workplace disengagement stems from lack of autonomy, purpose, and intrinsic motivation, akin to "no art, gym, or recess" in adult work environments.

- **Author expertise**: Jennifer Moss, workplace culture researcher, links burnout mitigation to **data-driven happiness strategies**, with forthcoming books (*Why Are We Here?*, *Unlocking Happiness at Work* 2nd ed.) expanding on evidence-based frameworks.
