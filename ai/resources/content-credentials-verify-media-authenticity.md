# Content Credentials | Verify Media Authenticity

## Summary

- **Content Credentials (C2PA standard)** enables provenance tracking for digital content (images, videos, audio, documents) via embedded metadata, distinguishing synthetic from authentic media.
- **500+ industry leaders** (Microsoft, Adobe, Intel, BBC, OpenAI, Google, Meta, etc.) collaborate under C2PA to standardize authenticity verification mechanisms.
- **Visual pin system** provides real-time provenance preview, exposing creation method and edit history to combat misinformation and synthetic content deception.
