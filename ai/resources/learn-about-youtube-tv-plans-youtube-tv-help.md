# Learn about YouTube TV Plans - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- YouTube TV offers modular subscription plans (e.g., Sports, Entertainment, News, Family) with tiered pricing, but does not support à la carte channel selection.
- Plan switching is immediate, with prorated credits applied for unused time; DVR content is retained if the new plan includes the original channels.
- Plan availability varies by billing provider (carriers/ISP), with direct billing via YouTube TV providing the fullest plan selection.
