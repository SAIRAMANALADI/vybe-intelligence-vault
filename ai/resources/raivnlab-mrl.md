## Summary

- **Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL)**: Implements hierarchical, nested representations where inner layers contain compressed subsets of outer layers, enabling efficient scaling of model capacity and downstream task performance.

- **Modular Architecture**: Utilizes Jupyter Notebooks for modular, experiment-driven development, facilitating reproducible research and iterative refinement of nested representation strategies.
