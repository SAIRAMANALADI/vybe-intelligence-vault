# Home · Chips Ju

## Summary

- **Chips Joint Undertaking (Chips JU)** is a public-private partnership aiming to strengthen Europe's semiconductor industry through research, innovation, and manufacturing collaboration between the EU, member states, and private sector, aligned with European policy goals for technological sovereignty.

- **Chips for Europe Initiative** establishes pilot lines for advanced semiconductor technologies and large-scale infrastructures, open to EU stakeholders, while **ECS R&I Calls** support low-to-high technology readiness projects to enhance EU strategic autonomy in AI, high-performance computing, cybersecurity, and digital infrastructure.

- **Strategic collaborations**, such as the EU-Republic of Korea partnership, and initiatives like the **Advanced Photonic Integrated Circuits (PIC) Pilot Line**, are key to advancing semiconductor R&D, supply chain resilience, and global leadership in critical technologies.
