# Cornell Tech - Safety & Security

## Summary

- **24/7 Security Operations**: Cornell Tech employs ~25 security professionals providing round-the-clock safety services on Roosevelt Island, collaborating with local public safety and NYPD 114th Precinct for emergency response.
- **Emergency Protocols**: Comprehensive emergency management covers active shooter scenarios, bomb threats, hazardous materials, and weather emergencies, with dedicated response actions and mass notification systems.
- **Compliance & Reporting**: Adheres to Jeanne Clery Act, publishes annual security/fire safety reports, and provides non-emergency incident reporting via Guardian Conduct system.
