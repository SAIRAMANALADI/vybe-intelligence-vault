# Report Content for Legal Reasons - Legal Help

## Summary

- Google provides a process to request removal of content from its products that violates laws or user rights (e.g., phishing, violence, explicit content) via [legal troubleshooter](https://support.google.com/legal/troubleshooter/1114905), requiring specific URLs and detailed justification per product.
- Requests must include exact URLs (not homepage links), violative content specifics, and background context; responses may require follow-up via email, with separate notices needed for content across multiple Google products.
- Google’s [Copyright Help Center](https://support.google.com/legal/topic/4558877) outlines DMCA compliance for copyright infringement claims, while removal applies only to Google products (not external sites), recommending direct contact with website owners for broader takedowns.
