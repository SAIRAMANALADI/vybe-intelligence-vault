# Re-introducing Bridgy Fed | Ryan Barrett

## Summary

- **Bidirectional Bridging**: Bridgy Fed enables full two-way interaction between supported networks (e.g., fediverse, IndieWeb) by translating posts, replies, likes, and reposts, with Bluesky support under active development pending federation enablement.

- **Privacy & Moderation**: Implements opt-in discovery via DM, supports native moderation (blocking, defederation), and uses authorized fetch (secure mode) for authenticated requests, ensuring no proactive bridging without explicit user consent.

- **Decentralized & Open-Source**: Operated independently without monetization, relies on HTTP Signatures for security, avoids indexing/search, and translates handles/IDs across networks while acknowledging feature parity gaps versus native accounts.
