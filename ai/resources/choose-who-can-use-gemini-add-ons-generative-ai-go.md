# Choose who can use Gemini add‑ons | Generative AI | Google Workspace Help

## Summary

- **License Assignment**: Google Workspace with Gemini licenses must be manually assigned to users via Admin console (Directory > Users) with options for individual, CSV bulk upload, or organizational unit assignment; licenses are unavailable to users under 18 and may take up to 24 hours to propagate.

- **License Management**: Admins can filter and view licensed users (via "Licenses assigned" filter) or remove licenses from individuals/organizational units through the Admin console’s bulk operations (Directory > Users > More > Remove licenses).

- **Automation**: New user licenses can be auto-assigned via billing settings, while sub-organizational users require explicit assignment (parent org assignment does not cascade).
