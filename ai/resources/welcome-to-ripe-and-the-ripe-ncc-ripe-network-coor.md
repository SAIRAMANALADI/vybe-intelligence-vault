# Welcome to RIPE and the RIPE NCC — RIPE Network Coordination Centre

## Summary

- **RIPE NCC Role & Scope**: Acts as the Regional Internet Registry (RIR) for Europe, Middle East, and Central Asia, serving 20,000+ members across 76 countries by registering IP addresses (IPv4/IPv6) and ASNs, and coordinating technical infrastructure.
- **ICANN Bylaws Amendments**: ICANN Board approved amendments to Article 18, Section 18.2(b) of its Fundamental Bylaws on 3 May 2026, with implications for the RIR community under ASO review.
- **Service Disruptions**: Documented service issues include RIPEstat performance degradation (resolved), RIS data unavailability (investigating), and RRC18 outage (resolved), impacting real-time data services.
