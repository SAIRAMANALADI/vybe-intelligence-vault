# Personal Safety User Guide - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Safety Check (iOS 16+)**: iOS 16+ devices include a built-in *Safety Check* feature to quickly revoke location sharing, app permissions, and account access for unauthorized users.
- **Legacy Checklists (iOS 15 or earlier)**: For older devices, Apple provides three checklists: *Limit device/account access*, *Manage location information*, and *Stop sharing content*.
- **Advanced Security Tools**: Includes *Stolen Device Protection*, *Lockdown Mode*, *AirTag tracking detection*, and *two-factor authentication* to harden device and account security.
