# 40th General Assembly of the European Seismological Commission - ESC 2026

## Summary

- **Event Details**: The 40th General Assembly of the European Seismological Commission (ESC2026) will be held in Istanbul, Türkiye, from **6–11 September 2026** at the Hilton Istanbul Bomonti Hotel & Conference Center, featuring **1,500+ participants, 850+ presentations, and contributions from 60+ countries**.

- **Technical Scope**: Key topics include **seismic networks, observational seismology, earthquake physics, seismic hazard assessment, induced seismicity, ground motion modeling, site characterization, and societal/engineering applications**, with discussions on recent significant earthquakes and innovative education/outreach methods.

- **Organizers & Sponsors**: Hosted by **METU, AFAD, KOERI-BOUN, and TDMD**, with **Diamond (Geobit Instruments), Silver (Hirun), and multiple Regular sponsors (TDG, Atay Mühendislik, etc.)**, alongside exhibitors (Nanometrics, Raspberry Shake, Kinemetrics) and official airline **Turkish Airlines**.
