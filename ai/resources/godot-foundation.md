# Godot Foundation

## Summary

- **Legal Structure & Purpose**: Dutch non-profit "Stichting Godot" (registered 2022) financially supports the open-source Godot Engine project, managing charitable contributions for development, infrastructure, and community initiatives.
- **Funding Allocation**: Contributions are used for hiring developers/artists, purchasing hardware, hosting services, event travel, and producing open-licensed merchandise to sustain the project.
- **Transparency & Governance**: Publishes annual reports, financial statements, and policies (e.g., trademark/service provider guidelines) to ensure transparent, accountable use of donations while maintaining MIT-licensed openness.
