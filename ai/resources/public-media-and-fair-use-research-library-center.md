# Public Media and Fair Use Research Library - Center for Media and Social Impact

## Summary

- **Fair Use Framework**: CMSI provides educational tools to clarify U.S. copyright law's fair use doctrine, enabling creators (filmmakers, journalists, educators, etc.) to legally reuse copyrighted material without permission or payment under transformative and proportionate use criteria.

- **Codes of Best Practices**: Developed through collaborative judicial precedent analysis, these codes define fair use applicability in common contexts (e.g., documentary filmmaking, teaching, visual arts) by assessing transformative purpose and appropriate material usage.

- **Public Media Reports**: CMSI publishes research on public media's fair use challenges and solutions, offering actionable insights for media professionals navigating copyright complexities in creative and civic media production.
