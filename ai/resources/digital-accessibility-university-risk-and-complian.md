# Digital Accessibility | University Risk and Compliance Services

## Summary

- **Compliance Framework**: UT Austin enforces digital accessibility via **WCAG 2.1**, **TAC 206/213**, and federal law (e.g., Section 508), with policy **HOP 3-3014** mandating accessible EIR procurement and development.
- **Centralized Support**: The **Digital Accessibility Center (DAC)** provides captioning, document remediation, web scanning, and training, while **IAA** oversees ADA/Section 504/PWFA compliance and barrier reporting.
- **Implementation Guidance**: Accessibility is integrated into procurement, web publishing, and document creation, with resources like [Web Accessibility](https://webpublishing.utexas.edu/web-accessibility) and [Document Accessibility](https://webpublishing.utexas.edu/web-accessibility/documents) guidelines.
