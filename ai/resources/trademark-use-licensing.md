# Trademark Use & Licensing

## Summary

- **Ownership & Control**: Stanford University retains exclusive rights to its trademarks, including logos, names, and symbols, under U.S. intellectual property law (Lanham Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 1051–1127).
- **Licensing Framework**: Unauthorized use requires explicit written licensing agreements; non-compliance may result in infringement claims (35 U.S.C. § 292) or cease-and-desist actions.
- **Enforcement**: Stanford actively monitors and enforces trademark protections via legal channels, including litigation for unauthorized commercial use or dilution (15 U.S.C. § 1125(c)).
