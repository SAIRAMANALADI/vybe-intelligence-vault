# Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability

## Summary

- **Interdisciplinary Research Hub**: The Woods Institute for the Environment at Stanford University serves as a nexus for cutting-edge, cross-disciplinary research addressing environmental challenges, integrating natural sciences, social sciences, engineering, and policy.

- **Mission-Driven Initiatives**: Focuses on high-impact, solution-oriented projects spanning climate change mitigation, sustainable resource management, environmental justice, and human-environment interactions to deliver actionable outcomes for global sustainability.

- **Collaborative Ecosystem**: Engages faculty, students, and external partners (e.g., NGOs, governments, industry) to co-develop scalable technologies, policy frameworks, and community-driven strategies for real-world environmental and societal impact.
