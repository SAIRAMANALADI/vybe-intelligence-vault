# Brand guidelines | Branding & Marketing | Android Developers

## Summary

- **Trademark Compliance**: Android, Google Play, and related trademarks are Google LLC property; usage requires prior approval via [brand request form](https://support.google.com/contact/partner_brand_approval), with strict guidelines for hardware, packaging, and marketing materials.
- **Google Play Branding**: "Google Play" name/icon usage is restricted to licensed devices; badges are available for app marketing via [Partner Marketing Hub](http://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com).
- **Android Robot & Wordmark**: Android robot must include CC-BY attribution; wordmark and custom typeface require explicit Google authorization; "Android" must be trademarked (™) on first use, capitalized, and never pluralized/possessive.
