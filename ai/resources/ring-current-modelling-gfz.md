# Ring Current Modelling: GFZ

## Summary

- **Ring current dynamics**: Electrons (~1–200 keV) and ions drift oppositely in the equatorial plane (~3–5 Earth radii), intensifying during geomagnetic storms via plasma sheet injection, with decay mechanisms including wave-particle scattering (e.g., EMIC waves) and charge exchange with neutrals.

- **VERB-4D modeling**: Solves 3D diffusion (wave-particle interactions) and 2D convection (external fields) equations; originally validated for electrons, now extended to ions due to shared governing physics, with sensitivity tested against empirical field models and loss rates.

- **Storm-phase modeling & future system**: Simulates ring current evolution across storm phases; aims for self-consistent coupling with field models and deployment of a publicly accessible predictive system.
