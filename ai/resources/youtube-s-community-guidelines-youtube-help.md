# YouTube's Community Guidelines - YouTube Help

## Summary

- **Content Restrictions**: YouTube prohibits spam, deceptive practices, impersonation, fake engagement, and external link abuse; sensitive content includes nudity, child safety violations, self-harm, and vulgar language; violent/dangerous content encompasses hate speech, graphic violence, harassment, and predatory behavior.
- **Regulated & Misinformation Policies**: Sale of illegal/regulated goods (e.g., firearms) is restricted; misinformation policies target harmful remedies, technically manipulated content, election interference, and medical misinformation with real-world harm potential.
- **EDSA Exceptions**: Content violating guidelines may remain if deemed Educational, Documentary, Scientific, or Artistic (EDSA), or in the public interest (e.g., government proceedings), but exceptions are not blanket approvals and require case-by-case evaluation.
