# ADA/504 Compliance - UCLA Equity, Diversity & Inclusion

## Summary

- **UCLA ADA/504 Compliance Office** oversees compliance with **Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act (1973)** and the **Americans with Disabilities Act (1990)**, monitoring physical accessibility, investigating discrimination complaints, and implementing the ADA Transition Plan.
- **Disability accommodations** for students are managed by the **Center for Accessible Education (CAE)**, while employees (UCLA/Health) must request support via **Disability Management Offices** through an interactive process to determine reasonable accommodations.
- **Reporting mechanisms** include the **Civil Rights Office’s "Report an Incident"** portal for discrimination/harassment and formal grievances (for students) via **grievance@saonet.ucla.edu** within 180 days of the alleged violation.
