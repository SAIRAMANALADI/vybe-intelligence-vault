# Use of machine translation on Europa - European Commission

## Summary

- **Machine Translation Limitations**: eTranslation is an automated, human-intervention-free tool provided by the European Commission, offering only a basic understanding of content with variable accuracy across languages and texts.
- **No Liability for Errors**: The European Commission disclaims responsibility for inaccuracies or errors in machine-translated content, including potential mistranslations of non-textual elements (e.g., images, videos).
- **Official EU Legislation Sources**: Authentic versions of EU legislation in 24 official languages are available on [Eur-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/homepage.html), with additional language policy and legal notices accessible via the Europa portal.
