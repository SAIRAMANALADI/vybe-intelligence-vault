# Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) outlines the types of data collected (e.g., personal info, usage data, device info), purposes (e.g., service provision, personalization, security), and user rights (e.g., access, deletion, opt-out mechanisms).

- **Terms of Service Compliance**: The [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) define legal obligations for users, including acceptable use policies, intellectual property rights, and liability limitations for Google and its services.

- **Privacy & Security Principles**: Google enforces [Privacy and Security Principles](https://safety.google/principles) guiding product development, including data minimization, transparency, user control, and built-in security measures across all services.
