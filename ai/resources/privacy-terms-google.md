# Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Governance Framework**: Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) outlines the types of data collected (e.g., location, search queries, device identifiers), the legal basis for processing (e.g., consent, legitimate interest), and user rights (e.g., access, deletion, correction) under regulations like GDPR and CCPA.

- **Security & Compliance Mechanisms**: Google enforces [security principles](https://safety.google/principles) such as encryption (TLS 1.3 for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest), OAuth 2.0 for authentication, and regular third-party audits (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2) to ensure compliance with global privacy standards.

- **Product-Specific Privacy Controls**: The [Product Privacy Guide](https://policies.google.com/technologies/product-privacy) details granular privacy settings across services (e.g., YouTube’s ad personalization controls, Gmail’s confidential mode), enabling users to manage data retention, ad targeting, and third-party access via centralized dashboards.
