# Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) outlines the types of data collected (e.g., personal info, device data, logs), purposes (e.g., service provision, personalization, security), and user rights to review, update, or delete data.

- **Terms of Service Compliance**: The [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) define legal obligations for users, including prohibited actions, content ownership, and service usage restrictions, with enforcement mechanisms like account termination.

- **Privacy & Security Principles**: Google adheres to [core principles](https://safety.google/principles) (e.g., data minimization, transparency, user control) and provides tools (e.g., [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/), [Product Privacy Guide](https://policies.google.com/technologies/product-privacy)) to enforce privacy and security across services.
