# Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) details the types of data collected (e.g., location, device info, usage data), purposes (personalization, security, ads), and user rights (access, deletion, opt-out mechanisms).

- **Security & Compliance Framework**: Google enforces [privacy and security principles](https://safety.google/principles) (e.g., data minimization, encryption, transparency) and provides tools like [Google Safety Center](https://safety.google) for built-in protections and family controls.

- **Product-Specific Privacy Controls**: The [Google Product Privacy Guide](https://policies.google.com/technologies/product-privacy) outlines granular privacy settings across services (e.g., Gmail, Search, YouTube) to manage data sharing, ad personalization, and activity tracking.
