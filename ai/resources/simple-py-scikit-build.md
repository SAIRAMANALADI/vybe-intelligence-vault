# SIMPLE-Py | scikit-build

## Summary

- **Workshop Focus**: Two-day hands-on training (July 13-14, 2026) on modern Python packaging for scientific computing, covering pure-Python and compiled extensions (scikit-build-core, Pybind11, nanobind, Maturin/PyO3), CI/CD, and secure publishing workflows.

- **Logistics & Support**: NSF-funded with travel/lodging reimbursement (economy flights under $800 per Fly America Act) for U.S. academic participants; includes catering, open hack sessions, and access to PEP authors.

- **Agenda Highlights**: Day 1 covers basic packaging (uv/pixi, CI) and compiled extensions (scikit-build, binding tools); Day 2 dives into scikit-build-core customization (SDist, wheels, stable ABI) and ecosystem tools (conda-forge, distro packaging).
