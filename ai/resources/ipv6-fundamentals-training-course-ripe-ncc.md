# IPv6 Fundamentals Training Course - RIPE NCC

## Summary

- **Course Scope**: One-day IPv6 Fundamentals Training Course for RIPE NCC members, covering IPv6 necessity, deployment planning, and addressing plans, excluding router/server configuration and LIR operations.
- **Key Topics**: IPv4 basics, IPv6 address structure, protocol fundamentals (Neighbor Discovery Protocol, auto-configuration), and IPv6 packet handling.
- **Learning Objectives**: Explain IPv6 format/types, obtain IPv6 allocations, register address space in RIPE Database, and plan IPv6 deployment.
