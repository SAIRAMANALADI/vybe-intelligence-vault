# Licences and regulators when holding money as Interest and Stocks | Wise Help Centre

## Summary

- **Regulatory Licensing**: Wise's investment licences for holding money as Interest or Stocks depend on the user's address at the time of switching from Cash, with specific regulators overseeing compliance (e.g., Estonian Financial Supervision Authority for EEA, FCA for UK/Switzerland, MAS for Singapore, ASIC for Australia, CVM for Brazil).

- **Entity-Specific Authorisations**:
  - **EEA**: Wise Assets Europe AS (Estonia) regulated under Estonian Financial Supervision Authority (licence 4.1-1/174).
  - **UK/Switzerland**: Wise Assets UK LTD regulated by FCA (FRN 839689).
  - **Singapore**: Wise Asia-Pacific Pte. Ltd. holds MAS licences for payment services (PSA) and capital markets activities (SFA).
  - **Australia**: Wise Australia Investments Pty Ltd holds AFSL 545411 (ASIC).
  - **Brazil**: Interest is managed via a partnership with Genial Institucional (CVM-licensed DTVM).

- **Cash vs. Assets Licensing**: Cash holdings are subject to Wise’s e-money/payment institution licences, while Interest/Stocks are governed by investment licences based on the user’s jurisdiction at the time of conversion.
