- **Cancellation process**: Users can cancel renewal via `youtube.com/purchases` under "Memberships," with confirmation email sent post-cancellation.

- **Plan modifications**: Switching payment plans (e.g., installments) or toggling RedZone requires canceling auto-renewal and repurchasing the desired bundle; credit card updates must be done before auto-renewal date via `youtube.com/paid_memberships`.
