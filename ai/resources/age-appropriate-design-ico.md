# Age appropriate design | ICO

## Summary

- **Legal Compliance**: The Data (Use and Access) Act 2026, enacted on 19 June 2025, mandates provisions affecting data protection laws and PECR, with commencement plans outlined by DSIT.

- **Key Principles**: The framework emphasizes accountability, transparency, children’s rights, data minimization, age assurance, and default privacy settings for child data processing.

- **Technical Controls**: Includes DPIAs, data sharing protocols, geolocation restrictions, profiling safeguards, nudge technique limitations, and parental control requirements for connected toys/devices.
