# Report: Electric utilities can use AI to improve service while – hopefully – meeting AI power demands | Bits & Watts Ini

## Summary

- **Charge Density Waves (CDWs) in Nickelates**: Researchers at SLAC observed CDWs in nickelate superconductors, revealing periodic modulations in charge density that coexist with superconductivity, challenging prior assumptions about their incompatibility.

- **Electronic Structure Insights**: The study used resonant X-ray scattering to map the electronic structure, identifying a unidirectional CDW phase in NdNiO₂, distinct from the bidirectional CDWs in cuprates, suggesting unique mechanisms in nickelate superconductivity.

- **Implications for Superconductivity**: The discovery provides a new framework for understanding high-temperature superconductivity in nickelates, potentially guiding the design of materials with enhanced superconducting properties.
