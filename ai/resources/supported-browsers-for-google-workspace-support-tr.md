# Supported browsers for Google Workspace | Support & troubleshooting | Google Workspace Help

## Summary

- **Supported Browsers**: Google Workspace officially supports **Chrome (latest version)**, **Firefox (current + previous)**, **Safari (current + previous)**, and **Microsoft Edge**, with Chrome offering full feature compatibility and support.
- **Limited Functionality on Non-Chrome Browsers**: Firefox, Safari, and Edge lack support for **offline access** (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides), **client-side encryption in Meet**, and **desktop notifications in Gmail**.
- **Browser Requirements**: JavaScript and cookies must be enabled; unsupported/virtual browsers may cause rendering issues or feature limitations. Mobile apps are recommended for optimal performance.
