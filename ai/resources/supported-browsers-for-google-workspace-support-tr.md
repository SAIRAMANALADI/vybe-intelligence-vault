# Supported browsers for Google Workspace | Support & troubleshooting | Google Workspace Help

## Summary

- **Browser Support**: Google Workspace officially supports the latest version of Chrome and the current/previous versions of Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge, with Chrome offering full feature compatibility and support.
- **Feature Limitations**: Offline access (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides), client-side encryption in Meet, and desktop notifications in Gmail are unsupported in Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
- **Requirements & Mobile**: Browser settings must enable cookies and JavaScript; virtual browsers (e.g., Citrix, VMware) may lack full functionality, while mobile apps are recommended for optimal mobile experience.
