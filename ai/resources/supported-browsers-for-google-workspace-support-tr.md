# Supported browsers for Google Workspace | Support & troubleshooting | Google Workspace Help

## Summary

- **Supported Browsers**: Google Workspace officially supports **Chrome (latest version)**, **Firefox (current & previous)**, **Safari (current & previous)**, and **Microsoft Edge**. Chrome offers full feature support, while others have limited offline/encryption functionality.

- **Browser Requirements**: **Cookies and JavaScript must be enabled** for full Google Workspace functionality. Virtualized browsers (e.g., Citrix, VMware) may lack full feature compatibility.

- **Mobile & Unsupported Browsers**: Mobile apps (Android/iOS) are recommended for full functionality; unsupported browsers may restrict features like calendar updates, Docs editor rendering, or desktop notifications.
