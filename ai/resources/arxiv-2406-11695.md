# [2406.11695] Optimizing Instructions and Demonstrations for Multi-Stage Language Model Programs

## Summary

- **MIPRO Algorithm**: Introduces a novel algorithm for optimizing multi-stage Language Model (LM) programs by refining free-form instructions and few-shot demonstrations without module-level labels or gradients, achieving up to **13% accuracy improvement** over baselines using Llama-3-8B.

- **Prompt Optimization Strategies**: Implements **program- and data-aware techniques** to generate effective instructions, a **stochastic mini-batch evaluation function** to surrogate the objective, and a **meta-optimization procedure** that iteratively refines LM-generated proposals.

- **DSPy Framework**: Released as part of the **DSPy library**, providing open-source tools for optimizing LM programs, with benchmarking across seven diverse multi-stage tasks.
