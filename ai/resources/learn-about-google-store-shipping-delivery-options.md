# Learn about Google Store shipping & delivery options - Google Store Help

## Summary

- Standard delivery from Google Store is complimentary in all supported countries, selected by default during checkout, and reflected as $0 in the cart summary unless otherwise specified.

- Each order may consist of multiple shipments, each with a unique order number, status, delivery date range, and itemized contents accessible via the Google Store order history.

- Delivery date ranges are provided per shipment and can be tracked through the order history page, with additional resources available for modifying shipping addresses, order changes, and delivery timing.
