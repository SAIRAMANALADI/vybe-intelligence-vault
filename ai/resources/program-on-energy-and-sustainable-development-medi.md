# Program on Energy and Sustainable Development – Medium

## Summary

- **Carbon pricing mechanisms** (e.g., cap-and-trade or carbon taxes) are proposed as cost-effective tools to reduce U.S. greenhouse gas emissions by internalizing externalities, incentivizing low-carbon innovation, and ensuring market-driven compliance.

- **Economic efficiency** is a key advantage of carbon pricing, as it allows firms to choose the least-cost abatement strategies while maintaining flexibility in compliance, unlike prescriptive regulations.

- **Political and implementation challenges** include public resistance, revenue recycling concerns, and the need for bipartisan support to establish a durable carbon pricing framework at the federal level.
