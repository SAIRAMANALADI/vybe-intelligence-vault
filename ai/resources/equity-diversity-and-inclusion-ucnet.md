# Equity, diversity and inclusion | UCnet

## Summary

- **Diversity as institutional strength**: The University of California (UC) explicitly defines diversity—encompassing race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, and more—as a core strength that enhances public service, teaching, and research, with formal policies (e.g., [Regents Policy 4400](http://regents.universityofcalifornia.edu/governance/policies/4400.html)) to ensure inclusion and respect across faculty, staff, and students.

- **Systemwide and localized equity resources**: UC provides tiered support via systemwide initiatives (e.g., [Faculty Diversity Initiatives](https://www.ucop.edu/faculty-diversity/), [EEO/AA contacts](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/contacts/eeo-aa-da-contact-list.html)) and campus-specific programs (e.g., Berkeley’s [Diversity website](http://diversity.berkeley.edu/)), including accountability metrics (e.g., [Accountability Report](https://accountability.universityofcalifornia.edu/2019/welcome.html)).

- **Structured community values and reporting frameworks**: UC enforces community standards through dedicated resources on healthy workplaces, harassment prevention, and [Principles of Community](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/career-community/building-community/community-values/principles-of-community/), alongside employee resource groups (ERGs) and advocacy networks to foster engagement and address systemic inequities.
