## Summary

- **Python-based framework** for modeling, analyzing, and simulating complex networks with support for social, biological, and infrastructure networks.
- **Extensible architecture** with interfaces to C/C++/FORTRAN, third-party acceleration backends, and compatibility with standard/nonstandard data formats.
- **BSD-licensed** with core functionalities including graph generation, structural analysis, algorithm prototyping, and visualization.
