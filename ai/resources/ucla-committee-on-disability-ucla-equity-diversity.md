# UCLA Committee on Disability - UCLA Equity, Diversity & Inclusion

## Summary

- **Established in 1982** as an advisory committee by UCLA Chancellor, UCOD comprises students, faculty, staff, alumni, and community members, focusing on disability-related issues and compliance with ADA & Section 504 regulations.
- **Primary functions** include analyzing disability concerns, proposing solutions, and making recommendations, with operational support from the Chancellor’s ADA & 504 Compliance Office (e.g., meeting logistics, parking, and documentation).
- **Resources and governance** include mission statements, bylaws, annual reports, meeting minutes (archived since 2016), and compliance tools (e.g., [UCLA Disabilities and Computing Program](https://dcp.ucla.edu/)).
