# University Audit Office

## Summary

- **Governance & Authority**: The University Auditor, as Chief Audit Executive, operates under Cornell University's Bylaws (Article XI) and oversight from the Audit, Risk, and Compliance Committee (ARCC), ensuring independence and objectivity in internal auditing functions.

- **Mission & Scope**: The University Audit Office (UAO) provides risk-based assurance, advice, and insight to enhance organizational value by evaluating and improving governance, risk management, and internal control processes across Cornell University.

- **Operational Structure**: UAO maintains two offices—Ithaca (395 Pine Tree Road) and Weill Cornell Medicine (575 Lexington Avenue)—to support campus-wide audit functions, ensuring systematic and disciplined evaluation of operations.
