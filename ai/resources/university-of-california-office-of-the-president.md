# University of California | Office of The President

## Summary

- **Systemwide Academic Governance**: UCOP oversees UC's academic excellence via divisions like *Faculty Affairs*, *Graduate & Undergraduate Equity Affairs*, and *Institutional Research & Academic Planning*, ensuring systemwide consistency in research, teaching, and public service.

- **Financial & Operational Oversight**: Manages UC’s financial health through *UC Finance* (budget, procurement, risk), *UC Investments*, and *Capital Programs, Energy & Sustainability*, while *UC Operations* handles HR, IT, and shared services.

- **Compliance & Strategic Initiatives**: Enforces civil rights (Title IX, disability rights), drives digital innovation (*Digital Innovation & Technology*), and implements strategic frameworks like *UCOP FlexWork* and *Systemwide Community Safety*.
