# FOSS United Foundation

## Summary

- **Mission & Goals**: Non-profit FOSS United promotes hacking/tinkering (Goal 1), builds quality FOSS for public good (Goal 2), and evangelizes FOSS adoption across sectors (Goal 3).
- **Programs & Events**: Runs city-based FOSS communities, student-focused FOSS Clubs, OASIS for social sector FOSS adoption, and flagship events like IndiaFOSS conference and FOSS Hack hackathon.
- **Partnerships & Infrastructure**: Founded by Frappe (ERPNext) and Zerodha, with Samagata Foundation as a partner; hosts active events (e.g., Maintainer Summit 2026, IndiaFOSS 2026) and provides industry collaboration opportunities.
