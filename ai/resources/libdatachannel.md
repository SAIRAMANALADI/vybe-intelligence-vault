# libdatachannel

## Summary

- **Implementation & Licensing**: Open-source C++17 library (with C bindings) implementing WebRTC Data Channels, Media Transport, and WebSockets under MPL 2.0 license, available on AUR, Vcpkg, and FreeBSD Ports.
- **Compatibility & Bindings**: Cross-compatible with major browsers (Firefox, Chromium, Safari) and other WebRTC libraries; supports community-maintained bindings for Rust, Node.js, and Unity.
- **Portability & Backends**: Supports POSIX (Linux, Android, macOS, iOS) and Windows; TLS backend options include GnuTLS, Mbed TLS, or OpenSSL; supports WebAssembly compilation via `datachannel-wasm`.
