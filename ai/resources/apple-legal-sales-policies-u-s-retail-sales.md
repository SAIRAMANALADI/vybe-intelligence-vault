# Apple - Legal - Sales Policies - U.S. Retail Sales

## Summary

- **Return Policy**: Apple offers a 14-day return window for products in original condition with receipt; refunds processed within 10 business days, with exceptions for cash/equivalent returns over $750 (mailed check) and gift receipts (Apple Gift Card). Returns require inspection, and activation of security features (e.g., Activation Lock) may void eligibility.

- **Pricing & Order Terms**: Apple reserves the right to adjust prices or cancel orders; price adjustments within 14 days of purchase may qualify for refunds (excludes limited-time promotions). Orders may be refused or limited, with risk of loss transferring upon physical possession or carrier pickup.

- **Regulatory & Shipping Compliance**: U.S.-based sales/shipping only; products subject to export controls. In-store pickup requires government-issued ID and order number; third-party pickup allowed but Apple disclaims liability post-transfer. Gift cards are non-refundable.
