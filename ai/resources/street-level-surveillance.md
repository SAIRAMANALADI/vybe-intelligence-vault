# Street Level Surveillance

## Summary

- **Automated License Plate Readers (ALPRs)**: High-speed cameras capture license plate data, timestamps, and GPS coordinates, enabling mass tracking and historical movement analysis of vehicles, often integrated with law enforcement databases.

- **Biometric Surveillance**: Encompasses facial recognition, fingerprint scanning, and other biometric ID systems, enabling real-time identification and tracking of individuals, frequently with high error rates disproportionately affecting marginalized groups.

- **Cell-Site Simulators (IMSI Catchers)**: Mimic cell towers to intercept mobile device communications and location data, allowing law enforcement to track, monitor, and potentially eavesdrop on targets without warrants or user knowledge.
