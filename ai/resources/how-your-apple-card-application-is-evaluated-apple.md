# How your Apple Card application is evaluated - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Credit Evaluation Criteria**: Goldman Sachs evaluates Apple Card applications using credit score (FICO® Score 9), credit report (including debt obligations), and reported income; hard inquiries occur only upon acceptance, not application.
- **Application Requirements & Declining Factors**: Applicants must be ≥18, U.S. residents, have an unfrozen credit report, and meet creditworthiness standards; declines may result from past-due debts, high debt-to-income ratios, recent credit applications, or low credit scores (<600 FICO).
- **Post-Application Process**: Declined applicants receive explanations and can request free credit reports; approvals may have credit limit constraints, with options like installments or trade-ins for device purchases.
