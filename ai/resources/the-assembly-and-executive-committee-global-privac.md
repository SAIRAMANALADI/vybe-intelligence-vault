# The Assembly and Executive Committee – Global Privacy Assembly

## Summary

- **Established in 1979**, the **Global Privacy Assembly (GPA)** is a global coalition of data protection and privacy authorities, originally named the *International Conference of Data Protection and Privacy Commissioners* until its rebranding in 2019.

- **Governed by a seven-member Executive Committee**, led by an elected Chair (currently Canada’s Privacy Commissioner), including four elected members and two ex officio hosts (previous and next conference hosts), with the latter acting as an observer.

- **Strategic plan adopted in 2019** outlines its vision to enhance global privacy enforcement through knowledge sharing, supportive networks, and coordinated action among authorities.
