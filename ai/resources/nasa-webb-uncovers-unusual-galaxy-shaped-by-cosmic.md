# NASA Webb Uncovers Unusual Galaxy Shaped by Cosmic Collision - NASA Science

## Summary

- **Mid-infrared imaging** of Centaurus A by JWST’s MIRI instrument resolves dust structures, stellar populations, and hidden activity, revealing an "S"-shaped feature and parallelogram-shaped dust lane indicative of past galactic merger (~2 billion years ago).

- **Supermassive black hole (SMBH) dynamics** in Centaurus A’s core are mapped via spectroscopy, showing fast-moving ionized gas outflows and a warped molecular hydrogen disk, demonstrating the SMBH’s dual role in triggering and suppressing star formation.

- **JWST’s NIRCam + MIRI composite** enables galactic archaeology by resolving millions of individual stars in Centaurus A’s obscured central region, reconstructing its evolutionary timeline (e.g., merger-induced starbursts, SMBH-driven feedback).
