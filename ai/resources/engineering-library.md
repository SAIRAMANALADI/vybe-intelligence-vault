# Engineering Library

## Summary

- **Engineering Village (Compendex)**: Primary database for engineering literature, indexing ~190M records from 1,800+ journals, conferences, and technical reports with advanced search filters (e.g., controlled vocabulary, citation tracking).

- **Knovel Handbooks & Materials Data**: Provides interactive access to 7,000+ engineering handbooks, databases, and material property data with built-in equation solvers and unit converters.

- **IEEE Xplore**: Comprehensive repository for IEEE and IET publications, offering full-text access to ~5M documents (journals, conference proceedings, standards) with DOIs and citation metrics.
