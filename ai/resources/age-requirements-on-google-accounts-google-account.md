# Age requirements on Google Accounts - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Global Default Age**: 13 years is the default minimum age to manage a Google Account unless specified otherwise by country-specific regulations.

- **Regional Variations**: Minimum age requirements vary by country, e.g., 14+ in Colombia, Chile, and South Korea, 15+ in Czech Republic and France, and 16+ in Germany, Netherlands, and most of Eastern Europe.

- **Service-Specific Thresholds**: Some Google services enforce higher age limits (e.g., YouTube age-restricted content requires 18+, AdSense/Google Ads/Google Analytics require 18+). Failure to meet age requirements may result in account deactivation within 14 days.
