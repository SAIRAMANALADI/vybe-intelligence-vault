# Age requirements on Google Accounts - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Global Default Age**: 13 years is the default minimum age to manage a Google Account unless specified otherwise by country-specific regulations.

- **Country-Specific Variations**: Minimum age requirements vary by country (e.g., 14+ in Chile, Colombia, and Italy; 15+ in Czech Republic, Denmark, and France; 16+ in Germany, Netherlands, and Spain).

- **Service-Specific Age Limits**: Some Google services enforce stricter age requirements (e.g., YouTube age-restricted content requires 18+, while AdSense, Google Ads, and Google Analytics require 18+).
