# OWASP Top 10:2025

## Summary

- **Broken Access Control (A01:2025):** Unauthorized privilege escalation due to improper enforcement of access policies, enabling unauthorized data access or modification.

- **Software Supply Chain Failures (A03:2025):** Exploitation of vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies or build processes, leading to compromised application integrity.

- **Cryptographic Failures (A04:2025):** Weak or improper implementation of cryptographic controls (e.g., weak algorithms, hardcoded keys), resulting in data exposure or tampering.
