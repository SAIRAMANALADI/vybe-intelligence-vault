# Google Drive: Sign-in

## Summary

- **Authentication Flow**: The page initiates a Google OAuth 2.0 sign-in flow for Google Drive access, requiring user credentials (email/phone) for session establishment.

- **Session Security**: Supports private browsing mode (`Guest mode`) to mitigate session persistence risks on shared or untrusted devices via `WebLiteSignIn`.

- **Recovery & Account Creation**: Provides email recovery (`usernamerecovery`) and onboarding (`SignUp`) endpoints, integrating with Google’s lifecycle management for account provisioning.
