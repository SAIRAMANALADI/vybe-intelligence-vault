# CIDER Overview | CIDER

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: Investigates fluid/magma transport at plate boundaries via three themes: (1) aqueous fluid roles in fault interfaces at subduction zones/ridges, (2) magma production rates and tectonic interactions at these settings, (3) magma transport mechanisms from source to crust.
- **Funding & Support**: NSF-funded (GeoPRISMS, CSEDI) with prior support from FESD; emphasizes cross-disciplinary collaboration and integrative Earth dynamics modeling.
- **Educational Mission**: Aims to train next-gen Earth scientists across disciplines (seismology, geochemistry, geodynamics) and foster transformative research via CIDER’s lecture collection (2004–present) and collaborative workshops.
