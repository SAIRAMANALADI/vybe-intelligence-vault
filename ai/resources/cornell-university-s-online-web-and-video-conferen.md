# Cornell University's Online Web and Video Conferencing Service - Zoom

## Summary

- **Access Points**: Cornell Zoom provides direct links for joining (`https://cornell.zoom.us/join`) and hosting meetings (video-off: `https://cornell.zoom.us/start/webmeeting`, video-on: `https://cornell.zoom.us/start/videomeeting`), with authentication required via `https://cornell.zoom.us/signin`.

- **Compliance Requirements**: Users must comply with [Zoom’s Terms of Service](https://www.zoom.com/en/trust/terms/) and [Acceptable Use Guidelines](https://www.zoom.com/en/trust/acceptable-use-guidelines), alongside Cornell’s [Policy 5.1](https://policy.cornell.edu/policy-library/responsible-use-information-technology-resources) and university-specific [Zoom guidelines](https://it.cornell.edu/zoom#toc-acceptable-use-guidelines).

- **Branding**: Cornell’s Zoom instance enforces institutional branding via a dedicated asset (`https://cornell.zoom.us/account/branding/p/c9bbd019-55a0-499d-98f0-cfb8bfc34136.png`).
