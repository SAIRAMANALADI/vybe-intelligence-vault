# Nonresident Supplemental Tuition Exemptions | Registrar’s Office

## Summary

- **AB 540 Exemption**: Nonimmigrant aliens who attended a California high school (or equivalent) for three years and graduated may qualify for NRST exemption by submitting the UCLA AB 540 NRST Exemption Request and official California education transcripts to a residence deputy.

- **Common Exemptions**: Includes holders of T/U visas, dependents of deceased public safety employees, foster youth, active-duty military stationed in California, and dependents of UC faculty/employees, among others, requiring submission of Statement of Legal Residence (SLR) and supporting documentation.

- **Submission Process**: AB 540 exemption requires online application and transcript submission; other exemptions require SLR (via SIR or separate form) with documentation submitted to a residence deputy via Message Center or mail.
