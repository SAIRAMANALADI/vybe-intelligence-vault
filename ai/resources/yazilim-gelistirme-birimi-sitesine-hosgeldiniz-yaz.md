# Yazılım Geliştirme Birimi sitesine hoşgeldiniz | Yazılım Geliştirme Birimi

## Summary

- **Service Eligibility**: All academic and administrative units within the university can utilize web development and application services, with technical support requests submitted via the [İş Takip Sistemi](https://istakip.boun.edu.tr/).

- **Development Workflow**:
  - Requests are logged via [İş Takip Sistemi](https://istakip.boun.edu.tr), prioritized, and planned.
  - Design and content structure are finalized in collaboration with stakeholders, followed by a 3–4 week content entry phase post-training.

- **Post-Launch Support**: Continuous technical support is provided post-launch via [İş Takip Sistemi](https://istakip.boun.edu.tr) for ongoing maintenance and updates.
