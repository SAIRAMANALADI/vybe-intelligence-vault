# Google Drive Terms of Service - Google Drive Help

## Summary

- **Ownership Retention**: Users retain intellectual property rights over their content; Google does not claim ownership of uploaded/stored data.
- **Access Control**: Google restricts file sharing to user-defined permissions; private data is not repurposed for marketing or made public without consent.
- **Data Portability**: Users can export their data via Google Takeout when discontinuing Drive usage, ensuring data retention control.
