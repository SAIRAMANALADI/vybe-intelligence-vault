# Google Drive Terms of Service - Google Drive Help

## Summary

- **Intellectual Property Retention**: Users retain ownership of all intellectual property rights in their uploaded content; Google does not claim ownership of user data, text, files, or information stored in Google Drive.
- **Data Access & Sharing**: Google restricts access to user files, prohibiting unauthorized sharing or conversion to public status; data is only retained as long as explicitly requested by the user.
- **Data Portability**: Users can export their data via Google Takeout when discontinuing Drive usage, ensuring full control over data migration.
