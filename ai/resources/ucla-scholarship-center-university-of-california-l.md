# UCLA Scholarship Center | University of California, Los Angeles

## Summary

- **Services & Accessibility**: The UCLA Center for Scholarships & Scholar Enrichment (CSSE) provides virtual and in-person (Tues-Thurs) scholarship application support, including workshops, individual counseling, and Zoom advising sessions, separate from the Financial Aid Office.

- **Scholarship Resources**: CSSE offers UCLA-specific and external scholarship databases, donor-funded opportunities, and application guidance (e.g., "Secrets to Winning College Cash" workshop), with security protocols (registration required, no recording).

- **Contact & Location**: CSSE is located at 233 Covel Commons, Los Angeles, CA 90095-7247, with virtual access via UCLA Message Center (Logon/non-Logon) and Instagram (@uclascholarshipcenter).
