# How YouTube Works: Giving Everyone a Voice

## Summary

- **AI Content Transparency**: YouTube enforces mandatory disclosure labels for AI-generated or altered content since 2024, enhancing viewer transparency and creator accountability in generative AI usage.

- **Likeness Detection Expansion**: Introduced advanced AI-driven likeness detection tools for civic leaders and journalists to safeguard identities amid rising AI-generated misinformation and deepfake risks.

- **Digital Wellbeing Initiatives**: Allocated $20M to global digital wellbeing programs, including a first-of-its-kind hub, focusing on teen mental health and safer online engagement through structured educational and protective frameworks.
