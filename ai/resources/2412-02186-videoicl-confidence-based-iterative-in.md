# [2412.02186] VideoICL: Confidence-based Iterative In-context Learning for Out-of-Distribution Video Understanding

## Summary

- **Proposed Framework**: Introduces *VideoICL*, a confidence-based iterative in-context learning (ICL) framework for out-of-distribution (OOD) video understanding, addressing limitations of traditional fine-tuning and ICL in video-language tasks.
- **Key Mechanisms**: Implements *similarity-based relevant example selection* and *confidence-based iterative inference* to dynamically refine responses by re-selecting examples until high-confidence outputs are achieved.
- **Performance & Impact**: Demonstrates significant gains in OOD video understanding across benchmarks, particularly in domain-specific scenarios, without fine-tuning, by extending effective context length for video LMMs.
