# IndieWeb

## Summary

- **Decentralized Ownership**: IndieWeb emphasizes owning personal domains and content, avoiding corporate-controlled platforms to prevent data loss from site failures (e.g., [site-deaths](https://indieweb.org/site-deaths)).

- **POSSE & Backfeed**: Content published on personal sites can be syndicated to other platforms (POSSE) while replies/engagement from those platforms are aggregated back (backfeed) via open standards like [Micropub](https://indieweb.org/Micropub) and [Webmention](https://indieweb.org/Webmention).

- **Community-Driven Standards**: Focuses on pluralistic, user-centric development of lightweight protocols (e.g., [IndieAuth](https://indieweb.org/IndieAuth)) and formats, prioritizing design and UX over rigid project monocultures, with regular meetups like [Homebrew Website Club](https://indieweb.org/Homebrew_Website_Club) and [IndieWebCamp](https://indieweb.org/IndieWebCamp).
