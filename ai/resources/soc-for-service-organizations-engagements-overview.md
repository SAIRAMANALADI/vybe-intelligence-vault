# SOC for Service Organizations Engagements – Overview | Resources | AICPA & CIMA

## Summary

- **Third-party risk management (TPRM)**: Organizations must identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with outsourcing functions to service organizations to ensure operational efficiency and cost-effectiveness while maintaining security and compliance.

- **Demand for transparency**: Stakeholders increasingly require detailed information on service organizations' controls, processes, and risk management practices to evaluate effectiveness and ensure alignment with organizational standards.

- **Regulatory and compliance implications**: Outsourcing introduces potential regulatory risks; organizations must ensure third parties adhere to relevant laws, standards (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001), and contractual obligations to mitigate legal and reputational exposure.
