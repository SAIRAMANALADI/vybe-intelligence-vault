# Our Members | European Data Protection Board

## Summary

- **Composition**: EDPB consists of EU national data protection authorities' representatives and the EDPS, with EFTA EEA States' authorities (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway) participating in GDPR matters without voting rights or eligibility for chair/deputy chair positions.
- **Voting Rights**: EFTA EEA States' members lack voting rights and cannot be elected as chair or deputy chairs within the EDPB.
- **Scope**: EFTA EEA States' participation is limited to GDPR-related matters within the EDPB framework.
