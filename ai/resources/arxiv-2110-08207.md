# [2110.08207] Multitask Prompted Training Enables Zero-Shot Task Generalization

## Summary

- **Prompt-based multitask training**: The paper introduces a framework to convert diverse NLP tasks into prompted formats, enabling explicit multitask learning with a pretrained encoder-decoder model (e.g., T5), demonstrating strong zero-shot generalization on held-out tasks.

- **Performance gains over larger models**: Fine-tuning on the prompted multitask mixture achieves superior zero-shot performance on standard datasets and BIG-bench tasks, often surpassing models up to 16x larger in parameter count.

- **Open-source contributions**: Released under [t-zero](https://github.com/bigscience-workshop/t-zero) and [PromptSource](https://github.com/bigscience-workshop/promptsource), providing prompts and trained models for reproducibility and further research.
