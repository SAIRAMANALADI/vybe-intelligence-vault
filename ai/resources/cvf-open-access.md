# CVF Open Access

## Summary

- **Open Access Repository**: The Computer Vision Foundation (CVF) provides open access versions of research papers, identical to accepted versions except for watermarks, with final proceedings available on IEEE Xplore.
- **Conference Proceedings**: CVF sponsors major conferences (e.g., CVPR, ICCV, WACV) with archived open access papers, including workshops and findings, dating back to 2013.
- **Errata Policy**: Corrections to published papers are handled via arXiv updates, with CVF notified via email (`errata@thecvf.com`) and periodic updates to the open access archive.
