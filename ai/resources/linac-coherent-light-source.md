# Linac Coherent Light Source

## Summary

- **LCLS-II** is a hard X-ray free-electron laser (XFEL) at SLAC, enabling atomic-scale snapshots of dynamic processes in materials, technology, and biology via femtosecond X-ray pulses.
- **User facility** operates as an Office of Science DOE user facility, offering instruments like CXI, TMO, and MeV-UED for time-resolved crystallography, spectroscopy, and ultrafast electron diffraction.
- **Proposal cycles** (e.g., Run 27: Sep 2026–Mar 2027) include regular user access, rapid proposals, and proprietary research, with deadlines and instrument-specific calls (e.g., DREAM laser-only at TMO).
