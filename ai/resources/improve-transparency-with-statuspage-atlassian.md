## Summary

- **Real-time incident communication**: Statuspage enables proactive customer updates during incidents via email, SMS, in-app messages, and integrations with tools like Slack, Microsoft Teams, and PagerDuty, reducing support ticket volume.

- **Component monitoring & uptime tracking**: Supports 150+ third-party integrations (e.g., Stripe, Mailgun) to display mission-critical service status, with historical uptime metrics for transparency and trust-building.
