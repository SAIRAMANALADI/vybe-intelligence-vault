# Real-Time Incident Communication with Statuspage | Atlassian | Atlassian

## Summary

- **Real-time incident communication**: Statuspage enables proactive, multi-channel (email, SMS, in-app, Slack, Teams) notifications during incidents, integrating with 150+ third-party tools (e.g., Stripe, PagerDuty) to display component statuses and reduce support ticket volume.

- **Customizable visibility & automation**: Supports public/private/audience-specific status pages with role-based access control, pre-written incident templates, and REST API access for automated workflows, scaling from 100 to 25,000+ subscribers across pricing tiers.

- **Uptime transparency & trust-building**: Features Uptime Showcase to display historical uptime metrics, with pricing tiers ranging from free (100 subscribers) to enterprise ($1,499/month), including SSO, custom domains, and advanced integrations like webhooks and IP allowlisting.
