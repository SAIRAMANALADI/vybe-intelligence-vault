# European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)

## Summary

- **EDAA governs European self-regulatory program for Data-Driven Advertising (OBA)**, providing the **AdChoices Icon** and consumer portal ([youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu)) for transparency, choice, and control over personalized ads.

- **Compliance framework** requires companies to integrate EDAA tools (AdChoices Icon/Consumer Choice Platform), self-certify within 6 months, and undergo independent certification for third-party actors to obtain a Trust Seal.

- **DSA alignment**: EDAA’s pan-European program complements the **Digital Services Act (DSA)**, offering industry-wide standards and enforcement mechanisms to address evolving digital privacy obligations.
