# Web Accessibility Assistance – Cornell University Library

## Summary

- Cornell University Library enforces [University Policy 5.12 Web Accessibility Standards](https://policy.cornell.edu/policy-library/web-accessibility-standards) to ensure barrier-free access to services and collections.
- Accessibility accommodations require advance notice; contact [libaccessibility@cornell.edu](mailto:libaccessibility@cornell.edu) or [Ask a Librarian](https://www.library.cornell.edu/get-help/ask/) for support.
- The [Readability Widget](https://www.lib.ncsu.edu/accessibility/readability-widget) (developed by NC State University Libraries) enhances readability, with feedback directed to [libaccessibility@cornell.edu](mailto:libaccessibility@cornell.edu).
