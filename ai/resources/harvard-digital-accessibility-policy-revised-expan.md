# Harvard Digital Accessibility Policy revised, expanded — Harvard Gazette

## Summary

- **Policy Expansion**: Harvard’s revised Digital Accessibility Policy (effective June 1, 2023) extends WCAG 2.1 AA compliance from public-facing to all internal digital content, including intranets, course materials, PDFs, web apps, and HarvardKey-protected content.

- **Implementation Framework**: The Digital Accessibility Services (DAS) team enforces standards via cataloging, remediation, vendor negotiations (e.g., captioning), and training infrastructure, while a liaison network supports Schools/units in adoption.

- **Inclusive Design Mandate**: New policy requires all University-developed/acquired digital systems to meet the latest accessibility standards, aligning with Harvard’s equity goals for equitable digital participation.
