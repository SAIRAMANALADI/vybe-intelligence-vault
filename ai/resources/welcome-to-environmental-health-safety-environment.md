# Welcome to Environmental Health & Safety | Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)

## Summary

- **Emergency Protocols**: Dial 911 for life-threatening situations; report injuries via [UT Direct](https://utdirect.utexas.edu/apps/campus/safety/incident/nlogon/) or after-hours via UT Police (8 a.m.-5 p.m.: [redacted phone]; after hours: 911). Report hazards via [Smartsheet](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/63ec99b0f7d64130801d556b313adbbe).

- **Nitric Acid Safety**: Container failures in 2019/2021 due to expired nitric acid (>2 years past shelf life) caused spills/splashes. See [full incident report (PDF)](https://ehs.utexas.edu/sites/default/files/Nitric-Acid-Container-Failure-2021.pdf) for mitigation guidelines.

- **Training Updates**: Mandatory OH 202 Hazardous Waste Management course updated in UT Learn (post-Jan 12, 2022). Verify completion via [OH 202 instructions](https://ehs.utexas.edu/media/380) to ensure compliance with new regulations.
