# Share what you know and get recognized - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Apple Support Community** operates on a gamified points system where users earn points for contributing high-quality answers, User Tips, or having their content upvoted, with no penalty for downvotes.
- **High-quality replies** are evaluated based on direct answers, actionable steps, appropriate detail, accuracy, professional tone, and inclusion of relevant resources, earning up to 10 points per reply if recognized.
- **Perks and levels** include custom avatars (Level 3), exclusive Apple news (Level 4), User Tips creation (Level 5), access to The Lounge (Level 6), and leaderboard rankings for top contributors in specific product areas.
