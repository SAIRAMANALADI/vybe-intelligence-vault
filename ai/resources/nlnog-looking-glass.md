# NLNOG Looking Glass

## Summary

- **NLNOG Looking Glass** provides real-time BGP route lookup and monitoring across **160 IPv4** and **164 IPv6** peers, with **152,608,495 IPv4** and **36,796,568 IPv6** prefixes received.
- **Multi-AS Infrastructure**: Aggregates routes from **150+ ASNs** (e.g., AS51088, AS63403, AS2914), enabling cross-provider path analysis and troubleshooting.
- **Hosted by BIT (AS12859)** with sponsorship from [NLNOG](https://nlnog.net); contact: [ring-admins@nlnog.net](mailto:ring-admins@nlnog.net).
