## Summary

- **Account Transfer Policy (May 27, 2026)**: Mandates use of official "Transfer ownership" workflow in Play Console for developer account transfers to maintain ecosystem integrity.

- **Developer Verification Requirement (September 2026)**: Enforces Android developer verification in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand, requiring apps on certified devices to be registered by verified developers.

- **Contacts & Location Permissions Updates (October 28, 2026)**: Introduces Contacts Permissions policy (mandating Android Contact Picker for broad access) and updates Location Permissions policy (recommending location button for precise location).
