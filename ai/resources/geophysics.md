# Geophysics

## Summary

- **Rock magnetism** studies the magnetic properties of rocks/minerals, primarily due to ferromagnetic/ferrimagnetic phases (e.g., magnetite, hematite), enabling paleomagnetic and environmental magnetic analyses.
- **Key mechanisms** include **thermoremanent magnetization (TRM)**—acquired during cooling below the Curie temperature—and **detrital remanent magnetization (DRM)**—aligned magnetic grains settling in sedimentary environments.
- **Applications** span **paleogeography** (plate tectonics), **paleoclimatology** (environmental change proxies), and **archaeomagnetism** (dating fired structures via secular variation records).
