## Summary

- **Regional Collaboration Expansion**: Signed a **Memorandum of Cooperation (MoC)** with Brandenburg and Saxony at ILA Berlin Air Show (10/06/2026) to enhance sustainable aviation innovation through regional partnerships.

- **Legacy & Milestones**: Clean Sky 2 (2000-2024) achieved key demonstrators (e.g., hybrid-electric propulsion, sustainable aviation fuels), with Clean Aviation building on these results to advance next-gen aerospace technologies by 2030.
