# The Hour of AI

## Summary

- **AI Literacy Initiative**: Hour of AI is a global initiative extending Hour of Code, providing hands-on, beginner-friendly AI activities for students and educators to transition from passive users to active creators, fostering responsible AI engagement and innovation.

- **Universal Accessibility**: Designed for inclusivity, the program ensures AI literacy is accessible to all students globally, regardless of location or prior expertise, with resources available year-round and multilingual support.

- **Gamified Learning**: Activities integrate AI education with popular platforms like Minecraft and AI-generated art, enabling students to explore AI concepts through interactive, game-based learning while developing practical technical skills.
