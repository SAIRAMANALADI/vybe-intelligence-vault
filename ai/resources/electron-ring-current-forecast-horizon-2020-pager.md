# Electron Ring Current Forecast - Horizon 2020 PAGER

## Summary

- **Forecast Model**: Three-day ensemble forecast of ring current electron flux at 85° pitch angle for 8keV, 16keV, and 30keV particles using the **VERB-4D** code.
- **Input Models**: Drift velocities computed via **Volland-Stern electric field** and **T89 magnetic field model**, with boundary conditions derived from a **Kp-dependent model**.
- **Output Metrics**: Ensemble mean (top row) and standard deviation (bottom row) of logarithmic electron flux in the equatorial plane, with uncertainty quantified in log10 space.
