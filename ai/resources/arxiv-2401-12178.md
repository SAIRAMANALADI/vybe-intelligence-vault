# [2401.12178] In-Context Learning for Extreme Multi-Label Classification

## Summary

- **Proposed Method**: Introduces a three-step pipeline, **Infer--Retrieve--Rank**, combining language models (LMs) and retrievers to address extreme multi-label classification (XMC) with thousands of classes, avoiding the infeasibility of full-class demonstration in prompts.

- **Implementation & Optimization**: Uses the **DSPy** programming model for declarative in-context system specification and optimizes via DSPy optimizers, requiring only tens of labeled examples per task to achieve state-of-the-art results on HOUSE, TECH, and TECHWOLF benchmarks.

- **Generalization & Efficiency**: Demonstrates competitive performance on BioDEX (a distinct benchmark) without fine-tuning, eliminating prompt engineering while maintaining efficiency and adaptability to new tasks.
