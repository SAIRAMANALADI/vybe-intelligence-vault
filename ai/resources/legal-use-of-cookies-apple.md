# Legal - Use of Cookies - Apple

## Summary

- **Cookie Categories & Purposes**: Apple employs three cookie categories—**Strictly Necessary** (essential for core functions like shopping carts), **Performance** (aggregate usage analytics for site optimization and affiliate tracking), and **Functionality** (store user preferences like localization, text size, and viewed content).

- **Cross-Site Limitation**: Functionality cookies **do not track browsing activity beyond Apple domains**, ensuring no third-party tracking occurs.

- **Disabling Impact**: Disabling cookies (via Safari/device settings or browser-specific methods) **may break core features** (e.g., carts, billing) and restrict access to certain site sections.
