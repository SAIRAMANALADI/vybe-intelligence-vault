# Be Well Bruin | Be Well Bruin

## Summary

- **Comprehensive Wellness Framework**: UCLA's *Be Well Bruin* integrates **100+ campus resources** across **six pillars** (Physical, Academic, Emotional, Financial, Social, Basic Needs) to support holistic student well-being, including medical care, counseling, and peer advocacy.

- **Structured Access Points**: Students can navigate resources via **category-based browsing**, a **personalized quiz (Be Well Bruin Guide)**, or **FAQ-driven pathways**, with mental health services centralized at **CAPS** (individual/group therapy, psychiatric care) and **RISE Center** (resilience-building workshops).

- **Crisis & Community Integration**: Dedicated crisis resources (e.g., *Assisting Bruins in Crisis*) and **student organizations** foster engagement, while **triage-based appointment scheduling** (CAPS) and **daily resilience practices** (RISE) ensure structured support delivery.
