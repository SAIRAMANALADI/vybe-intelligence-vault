# AB 540 nonresident tuition exemption | UC Admissions

## Summary

- **AB 540 Nonresident Tuition Exemption**: California AB 540 allows eligible nonresident students (including undocumented immigrants) to pay in-state tuition rates if they meet specific criteria (e.g., high school attendance in CA for ≥3 years, graduation from a CA high school, or attainment of an AA/AS degree from a CA community college).

- **Eligibility Requirements**: Applicants must file an affidavit (AB 540 form) with the university, certifying compliance with residency and graduation conditions, and submit supporting documentation (e.g., transcripts, proof of attendance).

- **Financial Aid Implications**: AB 540 students remain ineligible for federal financial aid but may qualify for state aid (e.g., Cal Grants) and institutional scholarships under the California DREAM Act.
