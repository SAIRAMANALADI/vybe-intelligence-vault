# [en_us] Blackwell Architecture

## Summary

- **Architectural Breakthrough**: Blackwell introduces a new class of accelerated computing with **unprecedented performance** (up to 25x faster than prior-gen Hopper) via dual-die design, advanced TSMC 4NP process, and 208 billion transistors.
- **Generative AI Optimization**: Features **dedicated Transformer Engine** with FP4 precision, 192GB HBM3E memory, and **50MB L2 cache** to enable trillion-parameter model training and inference at scale.
- **Scalability & Efficiency**: Supports **NVLink-C2C** for multi-GPU scaling, **1.8TB/s interconnect bandwidth**, and **up to 128TB/s memory bandwidth** while reducing power consumption via advanced power management.
