# GenAI in Teaching and Learning – Academic Innovation

## Summary

- **Governance & Principles**: Cornell’s GenAI response is guided by seven principles (e.g., faculty-student integrity, institutional heterogeneity) via the GenAI Education Working Group (est. 2024), under the AI Advisory Council, with cross-campus representation (faculty, staff, students).

- **Initiatives & Tools**: Key efforts include a 2025 campus-wide GenAI survey (700+ faculty, 2,000+ students), Critical AI Literacy programs, syllabus policy icons, and "AI+AI" academic integrity reforms to address GenAI misuse with updated evidentiary standards.

- **Collaboration & Outputs**: The OVPAI-led group partners with CIT, CTI, and AI Research Initiative, producing resources like the Teaching Innovation Case Series (e.g., "Creative Responses to GenAI"), workshops, and pilot tool evaluations to refine GenAI integration in pedagogy.
