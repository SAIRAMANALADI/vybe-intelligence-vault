# [2411.04368] Measuring short-form factuality in large language models

## Summary

- Introduces **SimpleQA**, an adversarially constructed benchmark for evaluating short-form factuality in LLMs, designed to ensure unambiguous single-answer questions.
- Implements a **grading system** where responses are scored as correct, incorrect, or not attempted, enabling assessment of model confidence and self-awareness.
- Benchmark is **challenging** due to adversarial collection against GPT-4 and aims to remain relevant for evaluating future LLM generations.
