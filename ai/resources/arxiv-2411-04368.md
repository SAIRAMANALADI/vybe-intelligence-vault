# [2411.04368] Measuring short-form factuality in large language models

## Summary

- **SimpleQA Benchmark**: Introduces a benchmark designed to evaluate short-form factuality in LLMs, featuring adversarially collected questions with single, indisputable answers to ensure unambiguous grading (correct/incorrect/not attempted).
- **Knowledge Calibration Focus**: Evaluates whether models "know what they know" by measuring their ability to answer confidently only when certain, while avoiding incorrect responses or abstentions.
- **Open-Source Evaluation Tool**: Provides an open-source framework ([GitHub](https://github.com/openai/simple-evals)) for standardized, scalable assessment of factual accuracy in frontier LLMs.
