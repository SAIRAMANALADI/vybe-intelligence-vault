# Ethics and AI | web.dev

## Summary

- **Ethical AI Considerations**: Key areas include content ownership/copyright (legal complexities of AI-generated content), bias/discrimination (human biases in training data affecting outputs), and privacy/security (risks of exposing sensitive data via cloud APIs).
- **Google’s AI Principles**: Focus on bold innovation, responsible development/deployment (lifecycle governance), and collaborative progress (empowering users to harness AI ethically).
- **External Ethical AI Efforts**: Organizations like ForHumanity, CAIDP, W3C AIKR, and COPE are addressing AI ethics, with resources available for further exploration and engagement.
