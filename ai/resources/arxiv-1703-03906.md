# [1703.03906] Massive Exploration of Neural Machine Translation Architectures

## Summary

- Conducted large-scale hyperparameter exploration for Neural Machine Translation (NMT) architectures, analyzing results from over 250,000 GPU hours on WMT English-to-German translation, revealing empirical insights and variance metrics.

- Demonstrated prohibitive computational costs of exhaustive hyperparameter search in NMT, typically requiring days to weeks of GPU time per model, necessitating efficient optimization strategies.

- Released an open-source NMT framework (`seq2seq`) to facilitate reproducible research and enable experimentation with novel techniques for advancing state-of-the-art translation systems.
