# App Privacy Details - App Store - Apple Developer

## Summary

- **Mandatory Privacy Disclosure**: Developers must declare all data types collected by their app or third-party partners in App Store Connect, including purposes (e.g., analytics, advertising, functionality) and whether data is linked to the user’s identity or used for tracking, as required for App Store submissions.

- **Optional Disclosure Criteria**: Data collection may be exempt from disclosure if it meets strict conditions (e.g., not used for tracking/advertising, optional for users, or collected under legally required financial/health research frameworks).

- **Tracking Definition & Exclusions**: "Tracking" involves linking app data with third-party data for targeted ads or sharing with data brokers; exemptions include on-device processing, fraud detection, or consumer reporting agency use.
