# Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) | Covid | CDC

## Summary

- **Vaccination Efficacy**: Up-to-date COVID-19 vaccination significantly reduces the risk of severe illness, hospitalization, and mortality, as evidenced by clinical and epidemiological data.
- **Reinfection Dynamics**: SARS-CoV-2 reinfection is possible due to immune evasion by variants (e.g., Omicron sublineages), with risk factors including waning immunity, exposure frequency, and variant transmissibility.
- **Long COVID Pathophysiology**: Post-acute sequelae (Long COVID) involves persistent symptoms (e.g., fatigue, neurocognitive dysfunction) linked to immune dysregulation, endothelial dysfunction, and viral persistence in reservoirs.
