# Where to watch: Supported devices, cable and satellite providers - YouTube Help

## Summary

- **Game Consoles**: YouTube is supported on Microsoft (Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One family, Xbox 360), Nintendo Switch, and Sony (PlayStation 5/4/3) consoles.

- **Smart TVs & Streaming Devices**: Compatible with major brands (Samsung, LG, Sony, TCL, etc.), Android/Fire/Google TV platforms, and streaming devices (Apple TV 4th gen+, Roku, Chromecast).

- **Cable/Satellite Providers**: Available on most Android-based Operator devices; specific support varies by provider—contact your service for details.
