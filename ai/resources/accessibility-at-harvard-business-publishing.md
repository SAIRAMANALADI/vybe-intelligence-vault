# Accessibility at Harvard Business Publishing

## Summary

- **Employment & HR Accommodations**: Employees/prospective hires can request accommodations via HR ([[email protected]](https://www.harvardbusiness.org/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#266e74664e4754504754424453554f4843555508495441)) or refer to [University Disability Resources](https://accessibility.harvard.edu/) and [Reasonable Accommodations Fact Sheet](https://accessibility.harvard.edu/reasonable-accommodation-fact-sheet) for policies. Digital accessibility support is available via [HUIT](https://accessibility.huit.harvard.edu/).

- **On-Site Visitor Accommodations**: Facilities team ([[email protected]](https://www.harvardbusiness.org/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8ee8efede7e2e7fae7ebfdf9e1fce5fcebfffbebfdfacee6effcf8effceaecfbfde7e0ebfdfda0e1fce9)) handles location-specific accessibility requests for office visitors.

- **Client Accommodations**: HBP clients must coordinate accessibility/accommodation requests through their designated point of contact.
