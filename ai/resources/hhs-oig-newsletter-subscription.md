# HHS-OIG Newsletter Subscription

## Summary

- **Subscription Services**: HHS-OIG offers email notifications for updates to their website (reports, enforcement actions) via the "What's New Newsletter" and updates to the [List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE)](https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/exclusions_list.asp) via the "LEIE Newsletter," both requiring email submission.

- **Social Media Integration**: The HHS-OIG maintains official presences on Twitter ([@oigatHHS](https://twitter.com/oigathhs)), Facebook ([oigatHHS](https://www.facebook.com/oigathhs)), YouTube ([oigatHHS](https://www.youtube.com/oigathhs)), and LinkedIn ([HHS-OIG](https://www.linkedin.com/company/16214318/)) for broader outreach and engagement.

- **Data Usage & Compliance**: Subscriber contact information is strictly used for delivering requested updates or managing preferences, adhering to HHS privacy policies ([Privacy Policy](http://www.hhs.gov/Privacy.html)) and utilizing Salesforce support ([Help](https://help.salesforce.com/home)).
