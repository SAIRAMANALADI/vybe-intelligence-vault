# Welcome | Office of the Chief Risk Officer

## Summary

- **OCRO Scope**: Provides internal audit, compliance, enterprise risk management (ERM), and privacy services across Cornell’s global campuses (Ithaca, Weill Cornell Medicine, Cornell Tech, Qatar), led by the Chief Compliance and Privacy Officer and Chief Audit Executive.
- **Core Functions**: Develops and manages a university-wide risk management program, oversees compliance/privacy programs, and supports strategic objectives via audit/investigation functions for the Board of Trustees’ Audit, Compliance and Risk Committee.
- **Governance Alignment**: Acts as a strategic advisor to management and faculty, ensuring alignment with institutional risk, compliance, and audit frameworks (e.g., policy/privacy portals, hotline reporting, and upcoming institutional risk platform).
