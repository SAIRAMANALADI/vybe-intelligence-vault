## Summary

- **Bridge Configuration**: In censored regions, Tor can be configured to connect via bridges during setup to bypass blocking.

- **System Clock & Privacy Risks**: Incorrect system time can disrupt Tor connections; avoid torrenting over Tor and do not install additional browser plugins/add-ons to prevent IP leaks or privacy compromises.

- **Pre-Installed Protections**: Tor Browser includes HTTPS-Only mode, NoScript, and other patches by default; manual verification and supported languages are documented in the Tor Browser manual and support portal.
