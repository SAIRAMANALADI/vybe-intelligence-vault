# UCLA COVID protocols - COVID-19 and vaccine resources

## Summary

- **Prevention & Reporting Protocols**: UCLA enforces LACDPH guidelines for COVID-19 prevention, isolation, and contact tracing, with updates to its **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)** as of March 6, 2025. The **COVID-19 Prevention Program/IIPP** (last updated June 2023) mandates institutional controls, while training and data privacy protocols (updated Oct. 17, 2023) govern reporting and handling.

- **Vaccination Compliance**: UCLA’s **Action Center** requires vaccine status declaration (attest/opt-out), aligning with the **UC Systemwide Interim Policy** for SARS-CoV-2 vaccination. Training and education resources (e.g., campus LMS, UCLA Health Hub) support compliance and public health education.

- **Operational Resources**: UCLA provides an **Emergency PPE Supply Store** and directs to **Environment, Health & Safety (EH&S)** for institutional safety measures, PPE distribution, and ongoing COVID-19 mitigation efforts.
