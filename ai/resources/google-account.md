# Google Account

## Summary

- Google’s **Data & Privacy** settings allow users to control how their activity data (searches, YouTube history, etc.) is used to personalize services via signed-in or anonymous cookie-based tracking.
- Users can **opt out of interest-based ads** (via [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com)) or **disable Google Analytics tracking** ([gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)) to limit data collection.
- **Search customization** and **YouTube history** settings enable/disable personalized recommendations based on device-specific activity logs.
