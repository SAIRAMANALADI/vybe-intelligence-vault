# IC3 – The Initiative for Cryptocurrencies and Contracts – Advancing the science and applications of blockchains

## Summary

- **IC3 Research Focus**: A consortium of academic researchers across 13 universities specializing in foundational blockchain research, including applied cryptography, consensus protocols, distributed systems, programming language theory, and game-theoretic mechanism design, bridging academic rigor with real-world blockchain applications.

- **Key Publications**: Introduces *monotone erasure codes* for flexible trust assumptions in blockchain systems, contrasting traditional threshold-based models, and analyzes *single-item auctions with a monopolist intermediary*, demonstrating how intermediary control can drastically reduce seller revenue and alter auction outcomes.

- **Events & Engagement**: Hosts global events like the *IC3 Blockchain Camp 2026* (Princeton) and *IC3 Winter Retreat 2026* (Switzerland), fostering collaboration among blockchain industry professionals, researchers, and enthusiasts.
