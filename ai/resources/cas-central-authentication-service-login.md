# CAS - Central Authentication Service Login

## Summary

- **Authentication Requirements**: NetID and password are mandatory fields for login, with password visibility toggle and CAPSLOCK detection.
- **Compliance & Access**: Users agree to comply with university policies and acknowledge the institution's right to access, preserve, and review stored/transmitted data via the [Access to Accounts Policy](https://oit.princeton.edu/policies/access-accounts).
- **Support & Resources**: Password management and assistance are available via [OIT Passwords](http://oit.princeton.edu/passwords).
