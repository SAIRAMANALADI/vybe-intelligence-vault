# Google Family Link - Apps on Google Play

## Summary

- **Parental Controls Framework**: Google Family Link enables granular screen time management (daily limits, school/downtime schedules), app-level restrictions (time limits, blocking), and content filtering across Google services (Chrome, Play, YouTube, Search) via supervised child accounts.

- **Location & Security Management**: Real-time device location tracking (requires active internet/Wi-Fi), geofenced alerts (arrival/departure notifications), and remote device actions (ringing, battery status) with encryption in transit for data safety.

- **Data Handling & Limitations**: Collects personal/financial data but prohibits third-party sharing; updates may disrupt permissions (e.g., auto-approved app updates), and location accuracy depends on device activity/status, with user-reported issues post-updates.
