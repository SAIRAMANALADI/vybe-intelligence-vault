# Google Family Link - Apps on Google Play

## Summary

- **Parental Control Framework**: Google Family Link provides granular parental controls including screen time limits, app-specific time restrictions, content filtering (Chrome/YouTube/Search), and location tracking, with real-time notifications for location changes or device battery status.

- **Data Privacy & Security**: The app enforces encryption in transit, allows user-initiated data deletion requests, and undergoes independent security reviews, while explicitly stating no data sharing with third parties.

- **Compatibility & Limitations**: Supports supervised Google accounts with device-specific feature variations; however, it does not block app updates, pre-installed system apps, or purchases made via alternate billing systems, requiring manual review of permissions and installed apps.
