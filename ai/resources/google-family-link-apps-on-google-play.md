# Google Family Link - Apps on Google Play

## Summary

- **Parental Control Framework**: Google Family Link provides a centralized dashboard for managing screen time limits, app usage, and content filters across Google services (Chrome, Play, YouTube, Search) with granular controls for supervised child accounts.

- **Location & Security Management**: Enables real-time device location tracking (with internet dependency), location-based alerts (arrival/departure notifications), and parental account recovery tools (password reset, data deletion) for child accounts.

- **Data Handling & Limitations**: No third-party data sharing, but collects personal/financial data with encryption in transit; updates may disrupt prior functionality (e.g., delayed location alerts) and lacks granular daily app time extensions.
