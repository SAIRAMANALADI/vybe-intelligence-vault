# KOERI | DEPARTMENT OF GEOPHYSICS

## Summary

- **Scope of Geophysics**: Studies Earth's structure from the inner core (1.5M atm, >1000°C) to the upper atmosphere, including crustal irregularities, ocean basins, and atmospheric layers.

- **Key Departments/Centers**: Includes Earthquake Engineering, Geodesy, Belbaşı Nuclear Tests Monitoring, Regional Earthquake-Tsunami Monitoring, and İznik Earthquake Hazard Mitigation centers.

- **Stakeholders & Infrastructure**: Affiliated with Boğaziçi University’s Technopark, Technology Transfer Office, and lifelong learning initiatives (e.g., BULLC), supporting research and innovation.
