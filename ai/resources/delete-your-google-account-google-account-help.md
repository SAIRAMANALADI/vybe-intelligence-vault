# Delete your Google Account - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Data Loss**: Deleting a Google Account results in permanent loss of all associated data (emails, Drive files, contacts, subscriptions, app purchases, and Android device functionality), including content stored exclusively in the cloud or linked services (e.g., YouTube Premium, Google Play Music).
- **Account Recovery Constraints**: Post-deletion, account recovery is time-limited; Security Checkup and linked app access are disabled immediately, and recovery options (e.g., recovery email/phone) must be pre-configured before deletion.
- **Selective Deletion Options**: Users can remove specific services (e.g., Gmail) without deleting the entire Google Account by associating a non-Gmail recovery email and verifying it via a confirmation link sent to that address.
