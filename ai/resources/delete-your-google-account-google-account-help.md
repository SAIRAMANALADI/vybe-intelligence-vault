# Delete your Google Account - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Data Loss**: Deleting a Google Account results in permanent loss of all associated data (emails, Drive files, Calendar events, photos, contacts) and access to Google services (Gmail, Play Store, YouTube subscriptions, purchased content).
- **Device & Service Impact**: Android devices lose app access, Play Store functionality, and cloud-saved data; Chromebooks lose Chrome app/extension access; linked subscriptions (YouTube Premium, Google Play purchases) are terminated.
- **Recovery Limitations**: Post-deletion, account recovery is time-sensitive and may be impossible; Security Checkup and linked app access are disabled immediately. Gmail can be removed separately without deleting the entire Google Account.
