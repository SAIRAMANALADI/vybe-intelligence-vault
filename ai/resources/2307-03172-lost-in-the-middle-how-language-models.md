# [2307.03172] Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

## Summary

- **Position Sensitivity in Long-Context Models**: Language models (LMs) exhibit degraded performance when relevant information is placed in the middle of long input contexts, with optimal performance observed at the beginning or end, even for explicitly long-context architectures.

- **Task-Specific Degradation**: Evaluated on multi-document QA and key-value retrieval, LMs show significant performance drops when required to access mid-context information, highlighting a lack of robust long-context utilization.

- **Evaluation Protocol Insights**: The study introduces new protocols for assessing long-context LMs, emphasizing positional bias and proposing stricter benchmarks to improve model reliability in extended input scenarios.
