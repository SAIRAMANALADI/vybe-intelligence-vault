# [2502.17956] Towards Better Understanding of Program-of-Thought Reasoning in Cross-Lingual and Multilingual Environments

## Summary

- **PoT vs. CoT**: Program-of-Thought (PoT) prompting outperforms Chain-of-Thought (CoT) in multilingual reasoning by decoupling reasoning from execution, mitigating challenges in non-English languages where CoT struggles due to entangled reasoning/execution processes.

- **Fine-tuning Impact**: Fine-tuning for question-reasoning alignment in PoT significantly enhances multilingual reasoning, surpassing CoT fine-tuned models, as demonstrated through empirical evaluation.

- **Reasoning-Answer Correlation**: Answer correctness in PoT correlates strongly with reasoning quality (measured via code quality), suggesting code quality as a heuristic for test-time performance improvement.
