# Badging at SLAC | Environment Safety & Health

## Summary

- **Foreign National Badging**: DOE mandates valid visa status and SLAC Identification Number with completed training for Foreign Nationals; SLAC badges require a sponsor to initiate the process via the [VUE Center](https://vue.slac.stanford.edu/).

- **Visitor Badging Exemptions**: Short-term visitors (<14 days) not requiring Accelerator Area access may bypass badging via the [SLAC Guest Gateway](https://vue.slac.stanford.edu/), while escorted "Red Badges" are issued for uncertified individuals needing limited access.

- **Special Cases**: Subcontractors must coordinate with their SLAC Project Manager, while users of SSRL/LCLS/Cryo EM or FACET require direct contact with respective User Admin offices for badge issuance.
