# Geomagnetism Laboratory

## Summary

- **Proton Precession Magnetometer**: A locally developed instrument for high-precision geomagnetic field measurements, as highlighted in the laboratory's announcements.
- **Geomagnetic Data Archive**: Historical geomagnetic observation data from 1943–2001, with partial datasets (1943–1947, 1995–1998) currently available for visualization.
- **Magnetic Storm Reports**: Real-time monitoring and reporting of geomagnetic storms, including documented events on **10.10.2024** and **10.05.2024**.
