# NHE Fact Sheet | CMS

## Summary

- **State-level health expenditure data**: Provides annual health spending by state of residence, covering categories like hospital care, physician/clinical services, prescription drugs, and administrative costs.
- **CMS standardized methodology**: Uses CMS-defined expenditure categories and allocation methods to ensure comparability across states and years.
- **Data granularity**: Includes detailed tables (e.g., Table 1: Aggregate and Per Capita Amounts by State) with breakdowns by service type, payer (private/public), and total spending.
