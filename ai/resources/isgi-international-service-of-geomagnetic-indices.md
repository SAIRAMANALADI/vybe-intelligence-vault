# ISGI - International Service of Geomagnetic Indices

## Summary

- **ISGI** serves as the **IAGA-endorsed reference service** for geomagnetic indices, providing **trusted data repositories** and **governance** for standardized geomagnetic data.

- **Real-time quick-look indices (*aa*, *am*)** are **automatically computed** ~30 minutes post-3h intervals using **minute-resolution data** from observatories (e.g., Hartland, Canberra) and INTERMAGNET GINs, though subject to potential **data corruption** (e.g., spikes, pollution).

- **Web services** enable **programmatic access** to ISGI data, including **documented APIs**, **customer integration**, and **non-endorsed indices** (e.g., *ASY/SYM*, *aσ*) for extended analysis.
