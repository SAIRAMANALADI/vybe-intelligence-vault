# Research.gov - Grants Management for NSF Community

## Summary

- **Proposal Management**: NSF's Research.gov platform facilitates proposal preparation, submission, and status tracking via [proposalprep](https://www.research.gov/proposalprep/#/proposal-prep) and [status checks](https://www.research.gov/gapps-web/gapps/searchResults), with a demo site for training.

- **Awards & Compliance**: Supports project reporting ([RPPR](https://www.research.gov/rppr-web/)), supplemental funding requests ([SFR](https://www.research.gov/proposalprep/#/sfr)), and financial disclosures ([FFDR](https://apps.research.gov/foreign-financial-disclosure)).

- **Fellowships & Data Access**: Provides GRFP applications ([GRFP](https://www.research.gov/grfp/Login.do)), award searches ([NSF Award Search](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/)), and scholarly publication access ([NSF-PAR](https://par.nsf.gov)).
