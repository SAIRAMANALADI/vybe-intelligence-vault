# Litmaps | Your Literature Review Assistant

## Summary

- **Visual Literature Mapping**: Litmaps transforms academic literature discovery into an interactive, graph-based visualization tool, enabling researchers to map citation networks and identify key papers in a field via "Seed Maps."

- **Automated Discovery & Monitoring**: Features include AI-driven paper recommendations, real-time updates on new publications, and a "Discover" function to accelerate literature review by prioritizing relevant and novel research.

- **Collaborative Research Platform**: Supports team-based workflows with shared maps, BibTeX integration for citation management, and cross-platform accessibility, used by 350,000+ researchers across 150+ countries.
