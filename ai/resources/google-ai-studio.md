# Google AI Studio

## Summary

- **Cloud-based IDE**: Google AI Studio is a web-based integrated development environment (IDE) designed for building, testing, and deploying AI models, particularly large language models (LLMs), with minimal setup.
- **Model Access & Customization**: Provides direct access to Google’s proprietary LLMs (e.g., PaLM, Gemini) and supports fine-tuning via prompt engineering, parameter adjustments, and dataset integration without requiring local infrastructure.
- **API & Deployment Integration**: Enables seamless export of models or prompts to Google Cloud’s Vertex AI for scalable deployment, with built-in versioning, collaboration tools, and cost monitoring for production workflows.
