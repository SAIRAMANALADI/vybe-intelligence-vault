# Legislative Developments | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **119th Congress (2025-2026)**: Introduced **NO FAKES Act** (S.4591/H.R.8915) to combat unauthorized deepfake content, **Pro Codes Act** (S.4145/H.R.4072/H.R.4009) to reform copyright for software, and **TRAIN Act** (S.2455/H.R.7209) targeting AI transparency.

- **118th Congress (2023-2024)**: Enacted **Servicemember Quality of Life Improvement Act** (H.R.5009) and introduced **Generative AI Copyright Disclosure Act** (H.R.7913) requiring AI training data disclosure.

- **116th-117th Congresses (2019-2022)**: Enacted **CASE Act** (H.R.2426/S.1273) for small copyright claims, **Music Modernization Act** (H.R.1551/S.2823) for music licensing reforms, and **SMART Copyright Act** (H.R.9541/S.3880) for automated infringement detection.
