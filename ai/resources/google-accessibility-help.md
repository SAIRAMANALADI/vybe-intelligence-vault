# Google Accessibility Help

## Summary

- **Google Accessibility Support**: Centralized resources for disability support, ASL specialists, and community connections via Google's Disability Support team and Be My Eyes app integration.

- **Cross-Platform Accessibility Tools**: Comprehensive support for Android, Chrome, Drive/Docs, Calendar/Gmail, Meet, Chromebooks, YouTube, and Pixel devices, including screen readers (TalkBack), live captions, braille displays, and keyboard/mouse alternatives.

- **Specialized Accessibility Features**: Individualized solutions for cognitive, hearing, mobility, speech, and vision needs, with tools like Sound Amplifier, Switch Access, voice typing, and Morse code input across Google products.
