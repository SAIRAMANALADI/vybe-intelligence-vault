# Federal Trade Commission | Protecting America's Consumers

## Summary

- **Loyalty Discounts & Network Effects**: Loyalty discounts by dominant firms with network effects (e.g., Surescripts) can harm competition by reinforcing their market power, making it harder for rivals to enter or expand, even if their technology is superior.

- **FTC v. Surescripts Case**: The FTC alleged that Surescripts' loyalty discounts (e.g., retroactive rebates) excluded competitors in e-prescribing networks, violating Section 5 of the FTC Act by suppressing innovation and choice.

- **Antitrust Implications**: The case highlights how loyalty discounts in network industries can distort competition, emphasizing the need for antitrust scrutiny to prevent exclusionary practices that entrench monopolies.
