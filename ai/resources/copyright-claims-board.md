# Copyright Claims Board

## Summary

- **Jurisdiction & Scope**: CCB is a three-member tribunal resolving copyright disputes with claims up to **$30,000**, offering a streamlined alternative to federal court for low-value cases.

- **Process Efficiency**: Operates via the **eCCB electronic filing system**, enabling remote case management, claim initiation, and respondent responses/opt-outs without formal court procedures.

- **Outcome Transparency**: Publishes quarterly statistics (e.g., [April 2026 report](https://ccb.gov/CCB-Statistics-and-FAQs-April-2026.pdf)) detailing geographic usage, dispute types, and resolution outcomes.
