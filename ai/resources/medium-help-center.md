# Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Account Management**: Medium provides granular control over user profiles, subscription settings, email preferences, and social media integrations via dedicated help articles (e.g., [profile URL customization](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004746707-Your-profile-page-URL), [subscription management](https://medium.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006277374)).
- **Content Distribution & Analytics**: Authors can monitor performance metrics (e.g., [detailed story stats](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/34831991136151), [audience demographics](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405449973015)) and control distribution via Friend Links or curator-driven Boost/General/Network channels.
- **Publication & Monetization Workflows**: Structured guides exist for [publication setup](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004681607), [story submissions](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/213904978), and [Partner Program earnings](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007171314), including eligibility criteria and revenue calculation logic.
