# Harvard Business Publishing

## Summary

- **Account Types & Access**: HBR.ORG Registered accounts enable digital access to articles, while HBR Magazine Subscription accounts include print/digital magazine access; subscriber-only content requires active subscription verification.
- **Content Search & Purchase**: HBR.org allows keyword-based content search (limited for non-subscribers), and the HBR Store supports product searches across formats/languages with copyright permissions for purchased items.
- **Subscription Tiers & Features**: HBR offers Digital, Digital+Print, Premium, and Executive subscriptions, with Executive tier including live event recordings; mobile app access is available for digital magazine subscribers.
