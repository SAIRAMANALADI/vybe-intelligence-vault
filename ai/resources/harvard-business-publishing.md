# Harvard Business Publishing

## Summary

- **Account Types & Access**: HBR.ORG Registered accounts differ from HBR Magazine Subscription accounts; registered accounts provide access to HBR.org content, while magazine subscriptions offer additional curated content and features.

- **Subscription Tiers**: HBR Magazine offers Digital, Digital+Print, and Premium subscriptions, with Premium including executive-focused content; Executive Subscriptions provide additional live event recordings and advanced resources.

- **Machine Translation**: HBR provides machine-translated articles in Spanish and Portuguese via automated translation, with FAQs addressing translation accuracy and limitations.
