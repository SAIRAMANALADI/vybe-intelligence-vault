# research process - How do researchers stay organized when working on multiple papers at once? - Academia Stack Exchange

## Summary

- **Time-blocking and prioritization**: Allocate dedicated time slots for each project (hourly/daily) and enforce strict boundaries; use a shared calendar and a simple to-do list to enforce focus and avoid multitasking overhead.

- **Modular note-taking systems**: Maintain project-specific notebooks (digital or physical) with frequent updates, and use a portable notecard system (e.g., Hipster PDA) for capturing ad-hoc ideas, ensuring continuity across contexts (e.g., bedside, commute).

- **Minimalist tooling**: Prioritize lightweight, low-overhead tools (e.g., Zotero/JabRef for references, basic folder structures for documents) over complex workflows; avoid automation unless it demonstrably saves >30 minutes per task.
