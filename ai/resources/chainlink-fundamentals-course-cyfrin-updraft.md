# Chainlink Fundamentals Course - Cyfrin Updraft

## Summary

- **Course Overview**: Chainlink Fundamentals is a 6-hour, 66-lesson course covering decentralized oracle networks (DON), smart contracts, Solidity, and Chainlink services (Data Feeds, VRF, Automation, CCIP, Functions, Proof of Reserve, and Data Streams).

- **Technical Scope**: Includes hands-on projects, 57 written tutorials, quizzes, and proficiency exams; targets beginners to intermediate learners with blockchain/Solidity prerequisites.

- **Certification & Ecosystem**: Offers a certificate of completion; taught by Chainlink DevRel engineers (Ciara Nightingale, Zubin Pratap, Solange Gueiros); lifetime access to materials and community support.
