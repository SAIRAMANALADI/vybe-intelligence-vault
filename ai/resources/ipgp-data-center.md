# IPGP DATA CENTER

## Summary

- **Mission**: Manages and distributes high-quality, long-term geophysical data archives from IPGP to support global geophysical research, ensuring reliable and efficient access while integrating with national (Résif), European (EIDA, ORFEUS, EPOS), and international (FDSN, IRIS-DMC, NASA PDS) infrastructures.

- **Requirements**: Enforces stringent data standards, including continuous dataset integrity, long-term preservation, and seamless integration with federated observatory networks for interoperability.

- **Scope**: Provides access to seismic, geodetic, and planetary science datasets, facilitating collaboration across French (CNRS, IPGP), European, and global research initiatives.
