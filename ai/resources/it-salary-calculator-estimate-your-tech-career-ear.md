# IT Salary Calculator | Estimate Your Tech Career Earnings | JetBrains: Developer Tools for Professionals and Teams

## Summary

- **Salary Data Source**: Annual gross salary ranges (USD, including bonuses) for C/C++ developers in Australia, segmented by experience (under 1 year to 16+ years), derived from JetBrains' 2025 Developer Ecosystem Survey (30,000+ respondents across 180+ countries).

- **Experience Definition**: Salary bands reflect **total professional coding years**, not years using C/C++ specifically, per survey methodology.

- **Survey Participation**: Developers can contribute to future datasets and earn rewards via JetBrains' Tech Insights Lab ([link](https://lp.jetbrains.com/research/jetbrains-tech-insights-lab)).
