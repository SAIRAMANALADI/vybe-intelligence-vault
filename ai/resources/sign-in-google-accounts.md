# Sign in - Google Accounts

## Summary

- **Authentication Flow**: Utilizes Google Account sign-in with OAuth 2.0 for Firebase Console access, including recovery options via `usernamerecovery` endpoint.

- **Session Management**: Supports private browsing/Guest mode (`signin_privatebrowsing`) to prevent credential persistence on shared devices.

- **Account Provisioning**: Provides a dual-path flow—sign-in (`WebLiteSignIn`) and sign-up (`GlifWebSignIn`)—with lifecycle management via Firebase’s `lifecycle/flows/signup` endpoint.
