# Sign in - Google Accounts

## Summary

- **Authentication Flow**: Google's sign-in page implements a **WebLiteSignIn** flow for user authentication, utilizing a **ServiceLogin** endpoint with **OAuth 2.0** for session management.

- **Recovery & Privacy**: Supports **username recovery** via `usernamerecovery` endpoint and enforces **private browsing mode** (`Guest mode`) to mitigate session hijacking risks.

- **Multi-Path Navigation**: Redirects users to **YouTube TV support** (`symptom=about_nfl_sunday_ticket`) post-authentication, with fallback to **account creation** (`SignUp` flow) if no credentials exist.
