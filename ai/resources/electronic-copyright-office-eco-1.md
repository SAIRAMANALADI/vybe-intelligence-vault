# Electronic Copyright Office (eCO) - 1

## Summary

- **eCO System Overview**: The U.S. Copyright Office's Electronic Copyright Office (eCO) is a web-based application enabling online registration, submission, and management of copyright claims for literary, visual, performing arts, and other works.

- **Key Features**: Supports digital uploads (e.g., PDF, image files), automated claim processing, real-time status tracking, and electronic payment integration for fees (e.g., $45–$65 per claim).

- **Workflow Integration**: Enables secure transmission of deposits (e.g., unpublished works) and synchronization with Copyright Public Catalog (CPC) for public record updates.
