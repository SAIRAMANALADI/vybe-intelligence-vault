# A new "environmental water market" promises ecological benefits alongside risk-management for farmers | The Bill Lane Ce

## Summary

- **Proposed Colorado River Basin water market**: A voluntary, compensated market enabling farmers, irrigation groups, and cities to lease high-priority water rights to governments/environmental groups for ecological reallocation, integrating hydrology, ecology, economics, and law to align transactions with environmental goals.

- **Risk management & transparency**: Water markets provide financial risk management for large users and increase pricing transparency, particularly under worsening drought conditions, though critics warn of potential access limitations for small farmers and vulnerable communities.

- **Regulatory safeguards**: Study emphasizes strong regulations to protect human right to water and ecosystems, with simulations demonstrating that carefully designed markets can direct water to ecologically critical segments while compensating farmers for conservation efforts.
