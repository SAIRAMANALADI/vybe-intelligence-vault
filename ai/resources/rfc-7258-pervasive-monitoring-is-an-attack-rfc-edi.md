# RFC 7258: Pervasive Monitoring Is an Attack | RFC Editor

## Summary

- **Definition & Scope**: RFC 7258 formally classifies **pervasive monitoring (PM)** as a technical attack on Internet privacy, encompassing indiscriminate surveillance via protocol metadata (headers, traffic analysis) or content interception, irrespective of attacker motivation (state, commercial, or criminal).

- **IETF Mitigation Mandate**: The IETF commits to designing protocols that **increase the cost/feasibility of PM** by minimizing metadata exposure, resisting traffic analysis, and enforcing data minimization, while acknowledging residual unavoidable disclosures.

- **Design & Review Requirements**: Protocol specifications must **explicitly address PM relevance** in architectural decisions (e.g., reuse of existing tech), with early review to balance PM mitigation against operational needs (e.g., network management) without rendering systems unmanageable.
