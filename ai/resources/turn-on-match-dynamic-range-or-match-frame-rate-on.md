# Turn on Match Dynamic Range or Match Frame Rate on your Apple TV - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Dynamic Range Matching**: Apple TV 4K can automatically adjust its output to match the original dynamic range of the content, optimizing visual fidelity but potentially causing temporary display issues (blank screen/flickering) during format switching.

- **Frame Rate Matching**: Supports dynamic refresh rate adjustment (24, 25, 30, 50, 60fps) to align with source content, including regional standards (e.g., 29.97fps for NTSC), with frame rate doubling for smoother UI transitions.

- **Unverified Formats**: Users can manually test unsupported display formats via Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Unverified Formats to validate compatibility before enabling.
