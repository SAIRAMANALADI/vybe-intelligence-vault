# Turn on Match Dynamic Range or Match Frame Rate on your Apple TV - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Dynamic Range Matching**: Apple TV 4K can auto-adjust output to match the original dynamic range of content via `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Match Dynamic Range`, potentially causing temporary blank screens or flickering during format switching.

- **Frame Rate Matching**: Supports matching refresh rates (24, 25, 30, 50, 60fps) to original content via `Match Frame Rate`, with regional adjustments (e.g., 29.97fps for NTSC) and frame rate doubling for smoother UI interactions.

- **Unverified Formats**: Users can manually verify display compatibility by testing unverified formats under `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content` to ensure correct rendering on their television.
