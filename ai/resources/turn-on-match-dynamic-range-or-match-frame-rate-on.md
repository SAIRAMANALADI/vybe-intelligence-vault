# Turn on Match Dynamic Range or Match Frame Rate on your Apple TV - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Dynamic Range Matching**: Apple TV 4K can auto-match output dynamic range to source content via `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Match Dynamic Range`, optimizing HDR/SDR output but may cause brief display interruptions during format switching.

- **Frame Rate Matching**: Supports auto-matching refresh rates for 60/50/30/25/24fps content (e.g., 24fps film), with regional adaptations (e.g., 29.97fps for NTSC) and frame rate doubling for smoother UI interaction.

- **Unverified Format Testing**: Users can manually verify unsupported formats via `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Unverified Formats`, running a display test to confirm compatibility before enabling dynamic range/frame rate matching.
