# Turn on Match Dynamic Range or Match Frame Rate on your Apple TV - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Dynamic Range Matching**: Enables Apple TV 4K to output video with the same dynamic range as the source content, optimizing HDR/WCG (Wide Color Gamut) playback.
- **Frame Rate Matching**: Adjusts refresh rate to match source content (24fps, 25fps, 30fps, 50fps, 60fps), including NTSC/PAL conversions (e.g., 29.97fps) and frame doubling for smoother UI.
- **Manual Verification**: If auto-detection fails, users can manually test unverified formats via `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content` to validate display compatibility.
