# Turn on Match Dynamic Range or Match Frame Rate on your Apple TV - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Dynamic Range Matching**: Apple TV 4K can automatically adjust its output to match the original dynamic range of the content via `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Match Dynamic Range`.
- **Frame Rate Matching**: Supports dynamic refresh rate adjustment for content at 60, 50, 30, 25, and 24fps, with regional NTSC/PAL compliance (e.g., 29.97fps for NTSC), via `Match Frame Rate`.
- **Unverified Format Testing**: Users can manually verify supported display formats by selecting `Unverified Formats` in `Match Content` to run a display test if Apple TV fails to auto-detect supported formats.
