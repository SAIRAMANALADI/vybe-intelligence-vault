# Turn on Match Dynamic Range or Match Frame Rate on your Apple TV - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Dynamic Range Matching**: Apple TV 4K can automatically match output dynamic range to source content via `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Match Dynamic Range`, optimizing HDR/SDR output but potentially causing brief blank screens or flickering during format switching.

- **Frame Rate Matching**: Apple TV supports automatic refresh rate adjustment for content at 60/50/30/25/24fps (`Match Frame Rate`), including NTSC/PAL region-specific rates (e.g., 29.97fps), with frame doubling for 25/30fps to maintain fluid UI.

- **Format Verification**: If auto-detection fails, manually verify supported formats under `Settings > Video and Audio > Match Content > Unverified Formats` via short display tests to ensure compatibility.
