# WDC for Geomagnetism, Kyoto

## Summary

- **Operational Entity**: World Data Center (WDC) for Geomagnetism, Kyoto, managed by the Data Analysis Center for Geomagnetism and Space Magnetism at Kyoto University’s Graduate School of Science, located in Sakyo-ku, Kyoto, Japan.

- **Core Functions**: Provides geomagnetic data services including indices, observatory field data, models, and catalogs, alongside INTERMAGNET data monitoring and global geomagnetic field research resources.

- **Collaborative Infrastructure**: Part of the ICSU World Data System (WDS), offering links to geomagnetic observatories, societies, and Kyoto University resources for comprehensive geomagnetic and space magnetism research.
