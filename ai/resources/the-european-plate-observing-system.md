# the European Plate Observing System

## Summary

- **EPOS** is a **pan-European, multidisciplinary Research Infrastructure (RI)** integrating solid Earth science data, products, and facilities via **Thematic Core Services (TCS)** and **Integrated Core Services (ICS)** for FAIR-compliant data management.

- **EPOS ERIC** (established 2018) provides a **legal framework** for collaboration, enabling **transnational access** to distributed research infrastructures and fostering **multi-hazard assessment** and **geo-resource management**.

- **Key initiatives** include **Geo-INQUIRE**, **DT-GEO**, and **EQUIP-G**, which develop **novel tools** (e.g., quantum gravimetry) and **training programs** to advance **solid Earth system understanding** and **hazard mitigation**.
