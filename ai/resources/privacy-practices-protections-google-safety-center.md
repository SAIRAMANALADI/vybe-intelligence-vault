# Privacy Practices & Protections - Google Safety Center

## Summary

- **Data Minimization & Auto-Deletion**: Google enforces strict data retention policies, with default auto-delete settings (e.g., 18 months for Location History/Web & App Activity) and prohibits using sensitive user content (e.g., Gmail, Drive, Photos) for advertising.
- **Privacy-Preserving Technologies**: Implements differential privacy, federated learning, and anonymization to derive insights without exposing raw user data, while open-sourcing these tools for broader adoption.
- **User Control & Compliance**: Provides granular privacy controls (e.g., Dashboard, My Activity) for data review/deletion, restricts third-party access to personally identifiable information (PII), and aligns with global data-protection regulations (e.g., GDPR).
