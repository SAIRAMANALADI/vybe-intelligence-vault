# Create a Google Account - Computer - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Account Types & Setup**: Google Accounts can be created for personal, child, or business use; business accounts enable Google Business Profile integration and personalization features.
- **Authentication & Recovery**: Supports Gmail or third-party email addresses; requires username, password, and optional phone/email recovery verification for account security.
- **Username & Email Constraints**: Usernames must be unique and not violate Google’s anti-spam/reserved username policies; existing emails cannot be reused for new accounts.
