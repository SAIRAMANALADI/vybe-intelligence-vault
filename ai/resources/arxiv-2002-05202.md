# [2002.05202] GLU Variants Improve Transformer

## Summary

- **GLU Variants**: Gated Linear Units (GLUs) replace the standard ReLU/GELU activations in Transformer feed-forward sublayers with component-wise products of two linear projections, where one projection is passed through a sigmoid (or alternative nonlinear/linear functions).

- **Performance Gains**: Experiments demonstrate that certain GLU variants (e.g., using different nonlinearities) improve model quality over traditional activations in Transformer architectures.

- **Architectural Impact**: The study validates the efficacy of GLU-based modifications in sequence-to-sequence models, suggesting broader applicability for enhancing Transformer performance.
