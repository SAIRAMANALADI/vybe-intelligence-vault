# [2002.05202] GLU Variants Improve Transformer

## Summary

- **GLU Variants**: Gated Linear Units (GLUs) replace the standard sigmoid with alternative nonlinear (or linear) functions, tested in Transformer feed-forward sublayers to improve performance over ReLU/GELU activations.
- **Architectural Integration**: Modified GLUs are applied in the Transformer’s feed-forward network (FFN) sublayers, replacing conventional activations to enhance sequence-to-sequence modeling quality.
- **Empirical Findings**: Experiments demonstrate measurable quality improvements in Transformer models when using specific GLU variants, validating their efficacy over baseline activations.
