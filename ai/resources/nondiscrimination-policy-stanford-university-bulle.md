# Nondiscrimination Policy | Stanford University Bulletin

## Summary

- **Policy Scope**: Stanford University enforces nondiscrimination laws (Title IX, Title VI, etc.) prohibiting unlawful discrimination/harassment based on protected characteristics (race, sex, disability, religion, gender identity, etc.) and sexual violence.
- **Coordinator Roles**: Adrienne Lyles serves as both Title IX Coordinator (sex/gender-based discrimination) and Title VI Coordinator (race/color/national origin discrimination), while Krista Martinelli handles other protected classes via the Diversity and Access Office.
- **Compliance Channels**: Complaints can be filed internally (via email/phone) or externally with the U.S. Department of Education’s Office for Civil Rights ([link](https://www2.ed.gov/about/offices/list/ocr/complaintintro.html)).
