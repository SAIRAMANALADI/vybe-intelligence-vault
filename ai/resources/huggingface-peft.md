# huggingface/peft

## Summary

- **PEFT Framework**: Implements state-of-the-art parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods for large language models (LLMs), transformers, and diffusion models, reducing computational and memory costs while maintaining performance.

- **Key Techniques**: Supports LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation), adapters, and other parameter-efficient methods, enabling fine-tuning with minimal additional parameters compared to full fine-tuning.

- **Integration**: Designed for PyTorch, compatible with Hugging Face Transformers, and widely used for fine-tuning LLMs, diffusion models, and other transformer-based architectures.
