# About Lumen - Legal Help

## Summary

- **Lumen Database** aggregates and publishes content removal notices (e.g., defamation, copyright, trademark, local laws, court orders, government requests) voluntarily shared by Google to enhance transparency in online content moderation.
- **Shared Data Categories**: For most requests, Google shares requestor/rights holder names (if applicable), reported URLs, country/date of request, request explanations, and supporting documents—excluding private contact details (e.g., email addresses).
- **Exceptions**: Defamation and local law requests typically omit requestor names unless high public interest justifies disclosure; court orders and government requests include entity names and may publish full orders unless confidential.
