# ProteomeXchange provides globally coordinated proteomics data submission and dissemination | Nature Biotechnology

## Summary

- **ProteomeXchange** established the first integrated framework for coordinated submission and dissemination of mass spectrometry (MS)-based proteomics data, addressing the need for standardized public repositories to capture raw data, peptide/protein identifications, quantification results, and biological conclusions.

- **Workflow** enables seamless data transfer between MS proteomics repositories (e.g., PRIDE, PeptideAtlas) and protein knowledge bases (e.g., UniProt), ensuring comprehensive annotation of MS/MS and SRM datasets.

- **Implementation** supported by EU FP7 funding (grant 260558) and NIH/NSF grants, with metrics (as of Feb 2014) showing 2,051 datasets submitted across 18 countries, facilitating global data reuse and reproducibility.
