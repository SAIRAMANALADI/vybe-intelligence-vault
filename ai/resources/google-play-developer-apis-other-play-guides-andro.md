# Google Play Developer APIs | Other Play guides | Android Developers

## Summary

- **Google Play Developer APIs** provide REST-based web services for programmatic app management, including publishing, reporting, and in-app purchase/subscription handling, reducing manual Play Console operations for large-scale deployments.

- **Key APIs**:
  - **Publishing API**: Manages APK uploads, track assignments (alpha/beta/production), and Play Store listing updates via transactional `edits`.
  - **Subscriptions & In-App Purchases API**: Enables real-time purchase verification, cancellation tracking, and subscription status checks without `edits` (immediate effect).

- **Specialized APIs**:
  - **Reporting API**: Exposes Android vitals (crash/ANR rates) for automated workflows.
  - **Reply to Reviews API**: Retrieves/produces user feedback responses for production apps.
  - **Permissions API**: Automates user access control in Play Console.
  - **Voided Purchases API**: Lists refunded/chargeback/canceled orders for revocation logic.
