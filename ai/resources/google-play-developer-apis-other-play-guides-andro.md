# Google Play Developer APIs | Other Play guides | Android Developers

## Summary

- **Google Play Developer APIs** provide REST-based web services for programmatic app management, including publishing, reporting, in-app purchases, and review responses, reducing manual Play Console operations for large-scale deployments.

- **Key APIs**:
  - *Publishing API*: Manages APK uploads, track assignments (alpha/beta/production), and Play Store listing updates via transactional `edits`.
  - *Subscriptions & In-App Purchases API*: Retrieves real-time purchase/subscription status (immediate effect, no `edits` required) for validation and reconciliation.

- **Specialized APIs**:
  - *Reporting API*: Exposes Android vitals (crash/ANR rates) for automation/analysis (10 QPS default quota).
  - *Voided Purchases API*: Lists refunded/canceled/chargeback orders to revoke user access to purchased content.
