# Accountability | ICO

## Summary

- **UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2026** is now in force (Royal Assent: 19 June 2025), with DSIT managing phased commencement plans for data protection and PECR updates.

- **ICO’s Accountability Framework** mandates structured compliance across 11 key domains: leadership oversight, policies/procedures, training, individual rights, transparency, lawful basis documentation, DPIAs, security, breach response, and third-party contracts.

- **Toolkits & Templates** provided by ICO include a tracker system to audit accountability measures, ensuring continuous monitoring and enforcement of data protection obligations.
