# ECCV 2008 - European Conference on Computer Vision

## Summary

- **Event Details**: ECCV 2008, the 10th European Conference on Computer Vision, held in Marseille, France (Palais des Congrès Parc Chanot), October 12-18, 2008, with tutorials on Oct 12, main conference Oct 13-16, and workshops Oct 17-18.
- **Organizers & Sponsors**: Hosted by INRIA Grenoble – Rhône-Alpes, sponsored by entities including Microsoft Research, INRIA, Orange Labs, and Springer, among others.
- **Future Editions**: Subsequent conferences announced: ECCV 2010 (Heraklion, Greece) and ECCV 2012 (Florence, Italy).
