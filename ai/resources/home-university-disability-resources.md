# Home | University Disability Resources

## Summary

- **Centralized Disability Resource Hub**: Harvard’s University Disability Resources (UDR) serves as a primary point of contact for disability-related services, compliance with federal/state regulations, and expertise in implementing best practices for accessibility across campus operations.

- **Comprehensive Service Offerings**: UDR provides ASL interpreting coordination, assistive technology loans (e.g., wheelchairs, listening devices), tailored training sessions, and consultation on inclusive event planning, physical space assessments, and communication strategies for diverse disability needs.

- **Initiatives and Partnerships**: Collaborations like KultureCity® for sensory-inclusive museum experiences, site-wide access to visual interpreting services (Aira), and structured programs (e.g., ASL courses, Disability Pride events) underscore Harvard’s commitment to systemic accessibility and community engagement.
