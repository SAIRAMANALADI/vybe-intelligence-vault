# Atlas of Surveillance

## Summary

- **Database Scope**: Compiles open-source research on U.S. law enforcement surveillance tech, including drones, body-worn cameras, ALPRs, and facial recognition, aggregated from public/non-profit datasets by 1,000+ students/volunteers.

- **Interactive Mapping**: Provides a geospatial visualization tool (Atlas) to explore deployed surveillance technologies by location, agency, or technology type, with third-party asset integration.

- **Collaborative Framework**: A joint project of the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) and the Reynolds School of Journalism (UNR), offering public access to datasets, methodology, and a glossary for transparency and research collaboration.
