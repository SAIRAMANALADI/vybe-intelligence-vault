# [2205.13147] Matryoshka Representation Learning

## Summary

- **Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL)** introduces a coarse-to-fine embedding strategy that encodes information at multiple granularities within a single representation, enabling adaptive downstream task performance without additional inference costs.

- **Empirical results** demonstrate up to **14x smaller embedding sizes** for ImageNet-1K classification at equivalent accuracy, **14x real-world speed-ups** for large-scale retrieval tasks, and **2% accuracy improvements** in long-tail few-shot classification while maintaining robustness.

- **MRL generalizes across modalities and architectures**, including vision (ViT, ResNet), vision-language (ALIGN), and language (BERT) models, and scales efficiently to web-scale datasets (ImageNet, JFT).
