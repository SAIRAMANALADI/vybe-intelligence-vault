# [2205.13147] Matryoshka Representation Learning

## Summary

- **Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL)** introduces a coarse-to-fine embedding strategy that encodes information hierarchically within a single representation, enabling adaptive granularity for downstream tasks without additional inference costs.

- **Empirical results** demonstrate up to **14x smaller embedding sizes** for ImageNet-1K classification at equivalent accuracy, **14x speed-ups** for large-scale retrieval, and **2% accuracy improvements** in long-tail few-shot classification, while maintaining robustness.

- **MRL generalizes across modalities and architectures** (e.g., ViT, ResNet, ALIGN, BERT) and scales efficiently to web-scale datasets (ImageNet, JFT) with minimal modifications to existing pipelines.
