# [2205.13147] Matryoshka Representation Learning

## Summary

- **Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL)** introduces a coarse-to-fine embedding structure that encodes information hierarchically, enabling adaptive granularity for downstream tasks without additional inference costs.
- **Empirical results** demonstrate up to **14x smaller embedding sizes** (ImageNet-1K classification), **14x speed-ups** (large-scale retrieval), and **2% accuracy improvements** (long-tail few-shot classification) while maintaining robustness.
- **MRL generalizes across modalities** (vision, vision-language, language) and scales to web-scale datasets (ImageNet, JFT) with minimal modifications to existing pipelines.
