# [2205.13147] Matryoshka Representation Learning

## Summary

- **Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL)** introduces a coarse-to-fine embedding structure that enables adaptive representation granularity for downstream tasks without additional inference costs.
- **Empirical results** demonstrate up to **14x smaller embedding sizes** (ImageNet-1K) at equivalent accuracy, **14x speed-ups** in retrieval tasks, and **2% accuracy gains** in few-shot classification while maintaining robustness.
- **Generalizability** confirmed across architectures (ViT, ResNet, ALIGN, BERT) and modalities (vision, vision-language, language) on large-scale datasets (ImageNet, JFT).
