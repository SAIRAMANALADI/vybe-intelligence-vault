# [2205.13147] Matryoshka Representation Learning

## Summary

- **Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL)** introduces a coarse-to-fine embedding structure that encodes information hierarchically, enabling adaptive granularity for downstream tasks without additional inference costs.
- **Empirical results** demonstrate up to **14x smaller embedding sizes** (ImageNet-1K) at equal accuracy, **14x speed-ups** in large-scale retrieval, and **2% accuracy gains** in long-tail few-shot classification while maintaining robustness.
- **MRL generalizes across modalities** (vision, vision-language, language) and architectures (ViT, ResNet, ALIGN, BERT) and scales efficiently to web-scale datasets (ImageNet, JFT).
