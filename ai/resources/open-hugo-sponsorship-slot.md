# Open Hugo Sponsorship Slot

## Summary

- **Sponsorship Benefits**: Up to 3 corporate sponsors receive a logo/link banner on [gohugo.io](https://gohugo.io) (homepage and footer) and [themes.gohugo.io](https://themes.gohugo.io), plus a banner in Hugo’s [GitHub repo](https://github.com/gohugoio/hugo#banner-sponsors).

- **Open Slot**: Currently, one corporate sponsorship slot is available for max 3 total sponsors.

- **Contact**: Interested parties should email [bjorn.erik.pedersen@gmail.com](mailto:bjorn.erik.pedersen@gmail.com) for details.
