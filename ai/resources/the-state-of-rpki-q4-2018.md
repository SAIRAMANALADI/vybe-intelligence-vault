# The state of RPKI: Q4 2018

## Summary

- **ARIN TAL Legal Barrier**: ARIN's Resource Public Key Infrastructure (RPKI) Trust Anchor Locator (TAL) includes restrictive legal agreements (e.g., indemnification clauses), reducing adoption compared to other RIRs (RIPE, APNIC, LACNIC, JPNIC), thereby limiting RPKI validation effectiveness for ARIN-registered prefixes.

- **Limited RPKI Validation Deployment**: Only 116 ASNs globally fully validate RPKI-signed routes (up from 85), with the Netherlands (93 ASNs) leading; most networks sign ROAs but fail to validate imports, undermining RPKI’s security benefits.

- **Cloudflare’s Partial RPKI Adoption**: Cloudflare implements RPKI filtering selectively (peered routes only), not globally, due to ARIN TAL restrictions; their approach lacks full visibility in global BGP routing, reducing overall RPKI efficacy.
