# Campus Operations Home | Campus Operations

## Summary

- **Departmental Structure**: Campus Operations and Planning at UT Austin comprises ~1,300 personnel across 8 divisions, including Facilities Services, Utilities and Energy Management, and Sustainability, responsible for infrastructure maintenance, safety compliance, and campus development.

- **Tower Restoration Initiative**: A 2025 project led by artisan Nancy Lee Morris and historic preservation planner Kim Barker aims to restore the UT Tower to its original 1937 aesthetic, addressing rust damage and spandrel detailing through precision gilding and archival research.

- **WaterHub Infrastructure**: UT Austin is constructing a 9,600 sq. ft. water reclamation facility—the largest of its kind in the U.S.—to reduce municipal water dependency by 40%, integrating advanced filtration for on-campus reuse in irrigation and non-potable applications.
