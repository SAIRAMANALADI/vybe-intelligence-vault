# MIT EAPS - Earth, Atmospheric & Planetary Sciences

## Summary

- **AI Model Architecture**: Utilizes a conditional Generative Adversarial Network (cGAN) to generate high-resolution satellite images conditioned on flood risk maps and topographic data, enabling realistic flood scenario visualization.

- **Training Data & Validation**: Trained on paired datasets of pre-flood satellite imagery and corresponding flood inundation maps, with validation against historical flood events to ensure physical plausibility and accuracy.

- **Applications & Impact**: Enables proactive urban planning and disaster preparedness by simulating future flood scenarios under climate change projections, aiding policymakers in infrastructure resilience assessments.
