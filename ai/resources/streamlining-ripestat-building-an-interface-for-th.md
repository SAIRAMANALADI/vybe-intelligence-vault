# Streamlining RIPEstat: Building an Interface for the Future | RIPE Labs

## Summary

- **Consolidation Decision**: RIPEstat will merge its two UIs (2013 and 2020) into a single interface, prioritizing the 2013 UI's stability and simplicity while incorporating modern design principles and select 2020 features.

- **User Feedback-Driven**: Extensive research (user interviews, surveys, stakeholder consultations) confirmed users preferred the 2013 UI's reliability and speed over the 2020 UI's complexity, leading to the rollback decision.

- **Future Roadmap**: Initial focus on reverting to the 2013 UI with added 2020 features, followed by long-term improvements aligned with user needs and integration with RIPE Atlas/RIS, culminating in the 2020 UI's sunset.
