# [2005.01140] Portuguese SKA White Book

## Summary

- **Document Overview**: A 210-page white book detailing Portugal's contributions to the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) radio telescope, including scientific, technological, and industrial aspirations, compiled from the Portuguese SKA Days event (Feb 2018).

- **Key Focus Areas**: Covers SKA’s status, vision, and goals across astrophysical domains (e.g., instrumentation, exoplanets, galaxies, stellar astrophysics) with policy and research community engagement.

- **Publication Details**: Published by UA Editora (April 2020, ISBN 978-972-789-637-0), cross-referenced with prior SKA-related works (e.g., arXiv:1607.02415, arXiv:1809.08663).
