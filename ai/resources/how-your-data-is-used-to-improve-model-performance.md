# How your data is used to improve model performance | OpenAI Help Center

## Summary

- **Opt-in/Opt-out Mechanisms**: Users of individual services (e.g., ChatGPT, Codex) can opt out of model training via the [privacy portal](https://privacy.openai.com/policies?modal=take-control) or Temporary Chat mode, while business services (e.g., ChatGPT Enterprise, API) default to opt-out unless explicitly opted in for feedback sharing.

- **Data Processing for Training**: User content (conversations, code, feedback) may be used to train models unless opted out, with personal data redacted to reduce exposure; feedback submissions (e.g., thumbs up/down) inherently include associated conversations for training.

- **Business Data Isolation**: Business-tier services (ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, API) do not train on inputs/outputs by default; opt-in is required for data sharing, and enterprise data is handled under [dedicated privacy policies](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy).
