# Login

## Summary

- **Authentication Requirement**: Access to the D&A Instructor Portal mandates UT EID and password entry, with explicit instruction to exclude any prefixed text (e.g., "austin/") from the EID field.

- **Portal Functionality**: The portal provides instructors with a centralized interface for managing accommodations, accessible via the [All Tasks](https://ssdportal.ddce.utexas.edu/ClockWork/user/misc/AllTasks.aspx) link.

- **Support Resources**: Additional guidance on accommodations is available through the [D&A website](https://disability.utexas.edu/), offering comprehensive information for instructors.
