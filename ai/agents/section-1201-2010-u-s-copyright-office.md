# Section 1201 - 2010 | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **Exemptions under DMCA §1201(a)(1)**: Six classes of works exempted from circumvention prohibitions for noninfringing uses, including:
  - Motion pictures on CSS-protected DVDs for educational, documentary, or noncommercial criticism/commentary.
  - Wireless phone software interoperability for lawfully obtained applications.
  - Used phone firmware circumvention to connect to authorized networks.
  - Security testing of video game access controls to identify vulnerabilities.
  - Obsolete dongle-based access controls.
  - Ebook read-aloud/screen reader functionality when disabled by DRM.

- **Rulemaking Process**: Mandated by the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), the Librarian of Congress conducts triennial rulemakings to evaluate exemptions based on evidence of adverse effects on noninfringing uses, with public comments, hearings, and iterative rulemaking phases.

- **Historical Context**: The 2010 exemptions followed prior rulemakings (2000, 2003, 2006), with the 2010 Final Rule published in the *Federal Register* on July 27, 2010, and interim rules effective during the proceeding.
