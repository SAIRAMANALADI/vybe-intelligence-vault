# US privacy policy | OpenAI

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: OpenAI collects personal data including account details (name, contact info, payment data), user-generated content (prompts, files, images, audio/video), log data (IP, browser, device info), and third-party sources (advertisers, public web data) for model training and service improvement.

- **Data Usage & Processing**: Personal data is used for service provision, model training (with opt-out options), fraud prevention, fraud detection, personalized ads (for free users), and legal compliance, with aggregated/de-identified data used for analytics and research.

- **Data Disclosure & Transfers**: OpenAI shares personal data with vendors, service providers, and affiliates under contractual obligations; in business transactions (e.g., mergers); with government authorities for legal compliance; or to protect rights/property, with strict controls on re-identification unless required by law.
