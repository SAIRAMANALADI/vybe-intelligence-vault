# New AWS skills initiatives help APAC get AI-ready | AWS Training and Certification Blog

## Summary

- **AWS launched three new AI skills initiatives in APAC**: 8 free AI/generative AI courses (foundational to advanced), $12M in Generative AI Scholarships for 50K+ underserved students via Udacity, and a Code.org collaboration for K-12 AI education via *Hour of Code: AI Edition*.

- **Regional adoption of generative AI training**: Wipro (India) used AWS Jam to upskill developers in Amazon CodeWhisperer, while RevoU (Indonesia) integrated generative AI (NLP, image generation, data synthesis) into its workforce training programs.

- **Expansion of AWS’s APAC skilling programs**: AWS Laptop for Builders (Indonesia) and AWS re/Start (13 APAC countries) target minority/displaced/refugee groups for entry-level tech careers, aligning with AWS’s goal of training 7M+ APAC learners (3.5M in 2023 alone).
