# New AWS skills initiatives help APAC get AI-ready | AWS Training and Certification Blog

## Summary

- **AWS launched three new AI skills initiatives in APAC**: 8 free AI/generative AI courses (foundational to advanced), $12M in Generative AI Scholarships for 50,000+ students via Udacity, and a Code.org collaboration for K-12 AI education via *Hour of Code: AI Edition*.

- **Regional adoption showcased**: Wipro’s AWS Skills Guild used gamified AWS Jam sessions to upskill developers on Amazon CodeWhisperer, while RevoU trained Indonesian students in NLP, image generation, and data synthesis for generative AI applications.

- **Targeted workforce development**: Initiatives like *AWS Laptop for Builders* (Indonesia) and *AWS re/Start* (13 APAC countries) focus on underserved communities, including minorities, displaced persons, and refugees, to bridge the AI skills gap.
