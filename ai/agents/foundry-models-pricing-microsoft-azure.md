# Foundry Models Pricing | Microsoft Azure

## Summary

- **Unified AI Model Platform**: Microsoft Foundry provides a single platform for designing, customizing, managing, and deploying AI applications with access to 11,000+ models (foundation, reasoning, multimodal, industry-specific) integrated into Azure’s enterprise-grade AI stack.

- **Managed Compute & Serverless Deployment**: Offers dedicated GPU infrastructure (A100, H100, H200, MI300) for custom model deployment with auto-scaling, alongside fully managed serverless deployment of models (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Grok, Llama, etc.) via pay-as-you-go or provisioned throughput options.

- **Dynamic Pricing & Regional Flexibility**: Pricing varies by region (e.g., US, EU, Asia) and currency, with estimates based on USD and London spot rates; actual costs depend on agreements, usage, and exchange rates, accessible via the Azure pricing calculator or direct sales consultation.
