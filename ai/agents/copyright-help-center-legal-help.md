# Copyright Help Center - Legal Help

## Summary

- **DMCA Compliance**: Google provides a structured process for copyright removal requests via a [legal troubleshooter](https://support.google.com/legal/troubleshooter/1114905) or direct contact with its DMCA Agent (`dmca-agent@google.com`).
- **Copyright Framework**: Google enforces copyright policies via resources explaining core concepts like [copyright basics](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3463239), ["fair use"](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/4558992), and [repeat infringement handling](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/11625144).
- **Contact & Resources**: Google’s Copyright Help Center aggregates FAQs ([link](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/4558836)) and directs users to YouTube’s [Copyright Center](http://www.youtube.com/yt/copyright/) for platform-specific guidance.
