# Copyright Help Center - Legal Help

## Summary

- **DMCA Compliance**: Google provides a structured process for copyright removal requests via a [legal troubleshooter](https://support.google.com/legal/troubleshooter/1114905) or direct contact with its DMCA Agent (`dmca-agent@google.com`), requiring submission of specific infringement details.
- **Copyright Framework**: Google enforces copyright policies aligned with U.S. law, addressing key concepts like *fair use* ([4558992](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/4558992)) and repeat infringement penalties ([11625144](https://support.google.com/legal/answer/11625144)).
- **Agent Contact**: Physical DMCA agent correspondence is directed to Google LLC’s Mountain View headquarters (1600 Amphitheater Parkway) for formal takedown notices.
