# Research Policy Handbook | DoResearch

## Summary

- **Research Misconduct Policy (RPH 1.7)**: Defines procedures for reporting, investigating, and resolving allegations of research misconduct, ensuring compliance with federal and institutional standards.

- **Environmental Health & Safety (RPH 7.2/7.10)**: Outlines core principles, responsibilities, and operational protocols for managing hazards, including unmanned flying vehicles (UAVs) and high-risk research activities.

- **Conflict of Interest & Commitment (RPH 4.1)**: Establishes disclosure requirements, eligibility criteria, and mitigation strategies for financial conflicts in federally sponsored research (e.g., NSF/PHS), including equity and consulting restrictions.
