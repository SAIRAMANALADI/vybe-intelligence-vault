# #1 Real-Time AI Voice SDK | Krisp

## Summary

- **Real-time AI Voice Processing SDKs**: Krisp offers two SDK families—**VIVA SDK** for voice AI agents (enhancement, transcription, reasoning, TTS) and **RTC SDK** for human-to-human calls (noise cancellation, accent conversion, voice isolation, turn-taking)—with a self-serve **Voice Translation API** for multilingual calls.

- **Production-Ready Features**: Core capabilities include **noise cancellation** (removing background voices/echoes), **accent conversion** (speaker/listener-side), **voice isolation** (primary speaker extraction), **turn-taking optimization**, and **real-time voice translation** with compliance monitoring and call scoring via speech analytics.

- **Enterprise Adoption Metrics**: Processes **1T+ minutes** of audio with **8+ years of production deployment**, supporting **200M+ devices worldwide**; trusted by major platforms like Twilio, RingCentral, and Discord, and recognized with a **Webby Technical Achievement Award**.
