# RIPEstat — RIPE Network Coordination Centre

## Summary

- **Data Aggregation**: RIPEstat consolidates over 35 datasets to provide insights and visualizations on IP address space and Autonomous System Numbers (ASNs), accessible via UI, widgets, and API.

- **Functional Capabilities**: Enables registration checks (WHOIS, IANA, resource transfers), country-level routing analysis, and retrieval of abuse contact information for Internet resources.

- **Development Roadmap**: Undergoing UI modernization and infrastructure improvements to enhance stability and enable future functionality expansions.
