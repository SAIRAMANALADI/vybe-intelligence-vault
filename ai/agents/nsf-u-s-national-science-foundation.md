# NSF - U.S. National Science Foundation

## Summary

- **NSF Laser Research**: NSF-funded advancements have enabled lasers to become critical tools in internet infrastructure, manufacturing, medical applications, and scientific research, with ongoing investments in laser-based technologies.
- **Quantum Network Initiatives**: NSF supports quantum network research, enabling a new era of interconnected quantum systems for secure communication and advanced computational applications.
- **STEM Workforce Development**: NSF allocates significant funding (~93% of its $9.06B FY2024 budget) to expand STEM workforce initiatives, supporting over 350,000 researchers, students, and educators across 1,900+ organizations nationwide.
