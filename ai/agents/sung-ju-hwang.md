# Sung Ju Hwang

## Summary

- **Academic Appointment**: Associate Professor at KAIST (KAIST Endowed Chair Professor since 2022), previously Assistant Professor at UNIST (2014–2017) and Postdoctoral Research Associate at Disney Research (2013–2014).

- **Research Focus**: Specializes in generative AI/LLMs, self-evolving AI (neural architecture search, LLM self-training), AI for scientific discovery (protein/molecule design), and on-device learning (federated/continual learning, model compression).

- **Publication & Leadership**: Published 100+ papers (e.g., 14 in 2026: 5 ICLR, 2 ICML, 2 CVPR, etc.), serves as Senior Area Chair for ICLR/ICML, Area Chair for NeurIPS/ICCV, and Associate Editor for TPAMI.
