# Search Cases | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **Fair Use Jurisprudence Trends (2020-2025):** Analysis of U.S. case law reveals inconsistent fair use outcomes across circuits, with **photography and internet/digitization cases** frequently ruled against fair use (e.g., *McGucken v. Newsweek LLC*, *Philpot v. Indep. J. Review*), while **educational/scholarly uses** often prevail (*Am. Soc'y for Testing & Materials v. Public.Resource.Org, Inc.*).

- **Supreme Court’s Impact on Fair Use:** The 2023 decision in *Andy Warhol Found. for the Visual Arts, Inc. v. Goldsmith* established stricter transformative use criteria, rejecting fair use for derivative works lacking significant new meaning (*143 S. Ct. 1258*). Contrastingly, *Google LLC v. Oracle Am., Inc.* (2021) reaffirmed broad fair use for software APIs.

- **Circuit-Specific Precedents:** The **Second Circuit** dominates fair use litigation (e.g., *Sketchworks*, *Yang v. Mic Network*), often favoring transformative commentary (*review/commentary*, *parody/satire*). Meanwhile, the **Ninth Circuit** shows mixed rulings, with *McGucken v. Pub. Ocean Ltd.* (2022) denying fair use for news photos despite transformative claims.
