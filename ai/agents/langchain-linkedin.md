## Summary

- **CLIs vs. MCPs**: CLIs (Command-Line Interfaces) offer direct, scriptable control for agent interactions, while MCPs (Model Context Protocols) provide structured, standardized interfaces for multi-model agent orchestration.

- **Use Case Dependency**: CLIs excel in lightweight, developer-centric workflows with low overhead, whereas MCPs are preferable for complex, multi-agent systems requiring interoperability and scalability.

- **Trade-offs**: CLIs prioritize simplicity and speed, while MCPs introduce abstraction layers for better maintainability but may introduce latency and complexity in agent communication.
