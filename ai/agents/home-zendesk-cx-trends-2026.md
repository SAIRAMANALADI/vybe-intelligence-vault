# Home | Zendesk CX Trends 2026

## Summary

- **Memory-rich AI agents** retain contextual customer data across interactions, reducing repetition and improving personalization at scale (83% of CX leaders prioritize this for true personalization).

- **Multimodal and promptable analytics** enable seamless, cross-channel support (76% of customers prefer unified text/image/video threads) and real-time insights (82% of leaders report seconds-long analytics vs. weeks).

- **AI transparency** is critical—95% of consumers demand explanations for AI decisions, yet only 37% of CX leaders currently provide reasoning, highlighting a compliance and trust gap.
