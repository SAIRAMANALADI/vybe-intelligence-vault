# Roosmarijn Vandenbroucke Lab - Home

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: Investigates systemic inflammation's impact on the blood-CSF barrier (BCSFB), particularly via the choroid plexus epithelium (CPE), to elucidate mechanisms of barrier integrity, extracellular vesicle release (exosomes), and acute phase response under inflammatory conditions (e.g., sepsis, aging, Alzheimer’s).

- **Body-to-Brain Axis**: Explores the CPE’s role as a mediator between peripheral inflammation (e.g., gastrointestinal triggers) and neuroinflammation, assessing its potential as a therapeutic target or delivery route for brain-targeted interventions.

- **Model Systems**: Utilizes murine models (mice and rats) to study BCSFB dysfunction under physiological and pathophysiological states, aiming to identify novel therapeutic strategies for inflammatory and neurodegenerative diseases.
