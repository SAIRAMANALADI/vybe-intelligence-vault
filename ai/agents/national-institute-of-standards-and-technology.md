# National Institute of Standards and Technology

## Summary

- **Dynamic Evacuation Modeling**: NIST developed an AI-driven model simulating real-time evacuation dynamics during fires, integrating human behavior, structural layouts, and hazard propagation to optimize exit routes dynamically.

- **Real-Time Adaptive Guidance**: The system employs reinforcement learning to adjust evacuation paths in response to evolving fire conditions, ensuring occupants follow the safest, least congested routes at each time step.

- **Validation & Scalability**: Tested in virtual environments with up to 1,000 agents, the model demonstrates high accuracy in predicting bottlenecks and reducing evacuation times compared to static signage-based systems.
