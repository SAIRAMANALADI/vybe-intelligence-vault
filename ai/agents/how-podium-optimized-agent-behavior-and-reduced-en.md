# How Podium optimized agent behavior and reduced engineering intervention by 90% with LangSmith

## Summary

- **Agentic Optimization Workflow**: Podium implemented a structured testing loop using LangSmith, including baseline dataset curation, offline/online evaluations, prompt/retrieval tuning, and model fine-tuning, achieving a 7.5% F1 score improvement (91.7% → 98.6%) in conversation termination detection.

- **Metadata-Enriched Traces**: LangSmith traces were augmented with customer profiles, business types, and other metadata, enabling balanced dataset curation and avoiding overfitting during model distillation from larger to smaller LLMs.

- **90% Reduction in Engineering Intervention**: By granting Technical Product Specialists (TPS) access to LangSmith traces, Podium reduced engineering troubleshooting time by 90%, allowing TPS to independently diagnose issues (e.g., misaligned instructions, incomplete context) without escalation.
