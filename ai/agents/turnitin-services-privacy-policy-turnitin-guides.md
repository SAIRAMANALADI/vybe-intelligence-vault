# Turnitin Services Privacy Policy – Turnitin Guides

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Collects Personal Information (names, emails, passwords, educational data, Writing Process Data) from Users (students/educators) via Customers (institutions), including account identifiers, Submissions, keystrokes, and device/IP metadata for service provision and AI model training.

- **Purpose & Disclosure**: Uses Personal Information to operate Services, improve AI models (e.g., plagiarism detection), provide customer support, and comply with legal obligations; does not sell data but may transfer it in mergers/bankruptcy. Usage Information is aggregated for analytics and localized service delivery.

- **User Rights & Compliance**: Users can modify data via institutions or opt out of marketing; FERPA-compliant Customers act as "School Officials" with restricted data handling. Children’s data (<13) requires parental consent, with deletion upon discovery of unauthorized collection.
