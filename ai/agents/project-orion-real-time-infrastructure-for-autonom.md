# Project Orion | Real-Time Infrastructure for Autonomous Cooperative Airspace

## Summary

- **Hybrid Validation Infrastructure**: Combines distributed real-time simulation, live field testing, and operational digital twins to evaluate next-generation UTM systems under dense, dynamic, and safety-critical conditions with degraded communications and mixed operational environments.

- **Cooperative Autonomy Testing**: Enables assessment of coordination, tactical response, perception, and autonomy performance in scenarios involving uncertainty, communication constraints, and operational complexity for autonomous airspace operations.

- **Multi-Stakeholder Collaboration**: Designed for cross-domain collaboration (research, industry, public-sector) to support evaluation, testing, and deployment of mission-driven airspace autonomy applications with partners like NASA, Qualcomm, and NIST.
