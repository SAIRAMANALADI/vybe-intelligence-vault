# Databricks Documentation

## Summary

- **ADI Services Integration**: Provides Databricks-hosted endpoints for OpenAI (GPT-5.x series, including deprecated Codex models) and Google Gemini (3.x/2.5.x series) models, accessible via Azure Databricks Foundation Model APIs with pay-per-token billing (priority options available for most models).

- **Compliance & Data Handling**: Requires Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for HIPAA-regulated PHI processing; OpenAI retains coding/routing customer content under specific conditions per [OpenAI’s safety retention policy](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data#safety-retention).

- **Model-Specific Terms**: All listed models are subject to separate usage policies (e.g., [OpenAI mitigation requirements](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/machine-learning/model-serving/open-ai-mitigation-requirements) and [Google’s prohibited use policies](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy)), with enforcement via Databricks’ terms of use.
