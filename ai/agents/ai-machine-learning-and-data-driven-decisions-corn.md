# AI, Machine Learning, and Data-Driven Decisions | Cornell Duffield Engineering

## Summary

- **AI Research Initiatives**: Cornell leads cross-disciplinary AI research, including a $20M NSF-funded AI Materials Institute (with Intel) and 100 Schmidt AI in Science postdoc fellowships awarded by 2029.
- **Autonomous Systems & Robotics**: Cornell-developed autonomous vehicles participated in the DARPA Urban Challenge, and current projects include AI-driven public transit optimization (e.g., Chattanooga’s transit network) and robotic systems for sports analytics (e.g., volleyball player tracking).
- **Computing Infrastructure & AI Hardware**: Cornell’s Red Cloud research cloud provided 6.88M compute hours, while the Computer Systems Laboratory spans 4,500 sq. ft. across Rhodes and Gates halls, supporting AI/ML workloads and hardware advancements.
