# Accessibility | Atlassian

## Summary

- **WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance**: Atlassian commits to conforming to WCAG 2.2 AA standards, with third-party testing, quarterly audits, and verified VPAT/ACR documents for transparency and accountability.
- **Proactive Accessibility Engineering**: Implements "shift-left" practices, mandatory training, automated tooling (linters, scanners), and design system guardrails to ensure accessibility from inception.
- **Public Accountability & Support**: Maintains a public backlog of accessibility issues, SLOs for remediation (e.g., 60 days for critical issues), and provides structured support via the Accessibility Support Portal and VPAT documents for all major products.
