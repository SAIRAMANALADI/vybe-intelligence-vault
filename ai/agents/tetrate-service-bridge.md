# Tetrate Service Bridge

## Summary

- **Envoy-based AI Gateway 1.0**: Provides a stable, high-performance foundation for enterprise AI traffic management, featuring approved LLM catalog access, unified model integration, automatic fallback mechanisms, and cost optimization controls.

- **Tetrate Service Bridge (TSB) Architecture**: Built on Istio and Envoy, offering centralized governance, multi-tiered traffic management (edge/gateway routing, circuit breaking, failover), and deep observability via unified metrics, distributed tracing, and service topology mapping.

- **Zero Trust & Compliance Framework**: Implements NIST-aligned Zero Trust architecture with end-to-end mTLS, FIPS-compliant Istio, centralized security policies, secure multitenancy, and role-based access control (RBAC) for regulated environments.
