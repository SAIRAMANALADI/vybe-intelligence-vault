# Welcome, GraphAware

## Summary

- **Acquisition Details**: Neo4j acquires GraphAware, a leader in open intelligence analysis software, to counter proprietary "black-box" tools like Palantir Gotham, emphasizing open standards, data sovereignty, and interoperability.

- **Technical Integration**: GraphAware Hume, built on Neo4j’s Graph Intelligence Platform, enables sovereign workloads (AWS GovCloud, private/on-prem) with ISO-certified GQL support, ensuring transparent, auditable intelligence analysis.

- **Mission-Critical Deployment**: Proven in production for high-stakes use cases (e.g., European Commission, Western Australia Police), the unified platform offers deeper querying, scalable performance, and full data ownership for government agencies.
