# RIPE NCC Certification Repository Terms and Conditions — RIPE Network Coordination Centre

## Summary

- **RPKI Repository Terms**: The RIPE NCC hosts a public Certification Repository containing Certificates, CRLs, and RPKI-signed objects (ROAs, ASPAs, BGPsec, RSCs) for Hosted CA users, governed by updated terms effective **13.11.2025**, with future support for new object types (ASPA, BGPsec, RSC).

- **Usage Restrictions**: Repository data may only be accessed for **RPKI validation or scientific research**; commercial use, marketing, or claims of IP resource ownership are prohibited. Users must validate against the latest data (updated every 24h) and acknowledge RIPE NCC’s liability limitations (e.g., no claims for damages except gross negligence).

- **Legal Framework**: Terms are governed by **Dutch law** with disputes resolved in Amsterdam courts; RIPE NCC may amend terms with 30-day notice. Users waive claims after **1 year** of awareness, and subcontractors may be involved per RIPE NCC’s Trust Portal disclosures.
