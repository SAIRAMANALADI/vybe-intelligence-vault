# Privacy Policy - Gravitee

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Sources**: Collects personal data directly (registrations, communications), from employers/third parties (customer reps, referrals), automatically (IP, browser, interaction history), public sources (social media, public records), and third-party providers (data brokers, authentication services). Combines and processes this data for stated purposes.

- **Data Categories & Special Handling**: Processes identifiers (IP, email), contact info, activity/usage data, device/technical data, commercial/marketing info, and financial data. Excludes special categories (health, biometric, etc.) except for Gravitee employees/contractors (criminal checks included).

- **Legal Basis & Usage**: Relies on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligations for processing. Uses data for service provision, business operations, legal compliance, marketing, corporate transactions, and de-identified analytics. EEA processing bases include contract necessity, legitimate interests, or consent.
