# Confluence | AI Workspace for Knowledge & Collaboration

## Summary

- **Rovo AI Integration**: Confluence integrates Rovo AI for instant drafting, brainstorming, and project planning, enabling human-AI collaboration to accelerate workflows and transform ideas into actionable strategies.

- **Cross-Tool Knowledge Retrieval**: AI-powered search across integrated tools (e.g., Google Drive, GitHub, Miro) provides contextual answers and summaries, reducing cycle time by up to 200% and improving throughput.

- **Structured Collaboration Tools**: Supports live docs, whiteboards, databases, and video for real-time editing, diagramming, and async communication, with pre-built templates for PRDs, plans, and briefs.
