# Encryption & Compliance Problem Solver | Help Guides

## Summary

- **Device Compliance Persistence**: Repeated compliance checks failing despite following steps may indicate misconfigured policies, outdated software, or undetected system modifications preventing compliance status updates.

- **Duplicate Device Entries**: Multiple entries for the same device in MyDevices suggest potential synchronization issues, duplicate enrollment, or inconsistent device identifiers (e.g., UUID mismatches) in the compliance management system.

- **Encryption Detection Failure**: MyDevices reporting an encrypted Mac as non-encrypted likely stems from improper FileVault configuration, missing encryption status reporting to the MDM, or agent-side logging errors in the compliance toolchain.
