# Anaconda Legal | Anaconda

## Summary

- **Licensing Framework**: Anaconda's Terms of Service (updated July 2025) enforce commercial licensing for organizations >200 employees/contractors accessing package updates post-April 30, 2020, unless eligible for free use (e.g., academic/non-profit). Legacy installers (<March 31, 2024) remain free, but updates require licenses.

- **User Tracking & Compliance**: Users (human/automated) are counted as "identified" (single count per account) or "unidentified" (per-instance). A new `conda-anaconda-tos` plugin (live July 15, 2025) enforces ToS acceptance at CLI for Anaconda channels/packages.

- **Third-Party Exemptions**: `conda-forge`, Miniforge, Mambaforge, and `conda` itself are license-free. Only Anaconda-provided channels/repositories require commercial licensing, with conda-forge exempt due to community-hosted packages.
