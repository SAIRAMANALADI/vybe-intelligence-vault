# EFF's Copyright Policy | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **Licensing**: Original EFF content is freely distributable under [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), allowing redistribution without prior permission unless otherwise specified; non-original content requires copyright holder consent.
- **DMCA Notifications**: Valid copyright infringement claims must include a signature, identification of infringed work/material, complainant contact info, and a good-faith belief of unauthorized use; misrepresentation risks liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).
- **Counter-Notifications**: Users can dispute takedowns via a counter-notice with URLs, contact details, jurisdiction consent, and a sworn statement of misidentification; claimants must file court actions within 10 days to block reinstatement.
