# Cornell Students Aid NASA with Drone Safety in Sky - NASA

## Summary

- **Hybrid Simulation Framework**: Cornell team developed a mixed-reality system where real drones operate with embedded simulations of dense urban traffic, enabling real-time collision avoidance testing without physical urban infrastructure.

- **Decentralized Tactical Deconfliction**: Research focuses on autonomous, multi-agent coordination algorithms allowing drones to dynamically adjust flight paths (10,000+ test runs, 1M+ trajectories) to prevent mid-air collisions, mimicking ground vehicle traffic rules.

- **FAA/Industry Validation**: Demonstrated scalability by simulating real-world incidents (e.g., 2025 Arizona crane collision) and disaster response scenarios (California wildfires), with FAA exploring adoption for UTM operational complexity testing.
