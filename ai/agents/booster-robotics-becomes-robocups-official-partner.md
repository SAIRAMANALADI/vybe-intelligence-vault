# Booster Robotics Becomes RoboCup’s Official Partner! | by Booster Robotics | Medium

## Summary

- **Partnership Announcement**: Booster Robotics officially became a partner of RoboCup following the successful performance of its **Booster T1** humanoid robot in RoboCup 2024.

- **Booster T1 Specifications**: The robot features **23 degrees of freedom (expandable)**, **omnidirectional walking**, **independent recovery from falls**, **sensing/positioning**, **200 TOPS AI computing power**, and **high anti-impact durability**, supporting secondary development and large-model integration.

- **RoboCup’s Mission**: A global robotics competition aiming for **fully autonomous humanoid soccer teams** to defeat human World Cup winners by **mid-21st century**, involving **60+ countries** and advancing AI/robotics research.
