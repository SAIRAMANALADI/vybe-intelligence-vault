# Deep Research | Firecrawl

## Summary

- **Autonomous Research Pipeline**: The `/deep-research` endpoint performs iterative web exploration, multi-source information retrieval, and structured synthesis, with configurable parameters (`max_depth`, `time_limit`, `max_urls`) to control research depth and scope.

- **Real-Time Monitoring & Structured Output**: Research progress is tracked via asynchronous job status updates, with detailed activity logs (`search`, `extract`, `analyze`, etc.), source attribution, and a final synthesized analysis (`finalAnalysis`) containing key insights and conclusions.

- **Alpha Limitations & Deprecation**: The API is deprecated (decommissioned after June 30, 2025) in favor of the new Search API; Alpha-stage constraints include 10-minute time limits, paywalled content exclusion, and reliance on small models for cost efficiency, requiring manual source verification for critical data.
