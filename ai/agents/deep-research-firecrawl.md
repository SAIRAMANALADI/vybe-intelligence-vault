# Deep Research | Firecrawl

## Summary

- **Autonomous Research Pipeline**: The `/deep-research` endpoint performs iterative web exploration, analysis, and synthesis of findings into structured insights, with configurable parameters like `maxDepth` (1-10), `timeLimit` (30-300s), and `maxUrls` (1-1000) to control research scope and duration.

- **Real-Time Monitoring & Structured Output**: Provides asynchronous progress tracking via `activities` (search/extract/analyze/synthesize steps) and delivers final results in JSON format with `finalAnalysis`, `sources` (URLs with metadata), and status updates (`processing`/`completed`).

- **Alpha Limitations & Billing Model**: Operates under 10-minute time constraints, prioritizes public content, and bills per URL analyzed (1 credit/URL); manual source verification is recommended due to potential synthesis inaccuracies in Alpha state.
