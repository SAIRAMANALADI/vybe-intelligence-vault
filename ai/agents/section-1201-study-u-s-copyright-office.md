# Section 1201 Study | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **Section 1201 of the DMCA (1998)** prohibits circumvention of access controls and trafficking in circumvention tools, with a triennial rulemaking process allowing limited exemptions for specific activities (e.g., security research, assistive technologies).

- **2017 U.S. Copyright Office Report** recommended legislative updates, including expanding exemptions for security/encryption research, adding provisions for assistive reading technologies and device repair, and granting the Librarian discretion to authorize third-party assistance for temporary exemptions.

- **Study Findings** concluded the core framework of Section 1201 remains sound but identified administrative inefficiencies in the triennial rulemaking process, proposing streamlined renewal procedures for previously adopted exemptions.
