## Summary

- **Service Desk & Alerts**: Cornell's IT Service Desk provides troubleshooting, issue reporting, and support for IT services; check [IT Alerts](https://itservicealerts.hosting.cornell.edu/allalerts) for service outages.
- **Tech Support Channels**: Support available via phone (8AM–6PM ET, M–F), chat (Ask Big Red bot/live agent), 24/7 web form ([TDX](https://tdx.cornell.edu)), and after-hours support.
