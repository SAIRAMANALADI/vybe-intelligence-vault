# Information Technology Laboratory | NIST

## Summary

- **Gödelian Extension to AI**: NIST demonstrates a mathematical proof adapting Kurt Gödel’s incompleteness theorems to validate a continuous-monitor-and-update (CMU) security model for AI systems, ensuring dynamic adaptability and fault detection.

- **Formal Verification of CMU**: The proof establishes that AI systems can theoretically achieve self-consistent monitoring and iterative updates without violating logical completeness or consistency, addressing runtime vulnerabilities.

- **Implications for AI Security**: The result provides a foundational framework for AI systems to autonomously detect and correct deviations in real-time, reducing reliance on static, post-hoc validation methods.
