# Do Our Agents Need Identities? | Cole W. Camplese

## Summary

- **Institutional AI Governance Gap**: AI agents performing institutional work (e.g., reimbursement processing, infrastructure monitoring) lack formal governance frameworks (EIDs, position numbers, HR lifecycle integration), creating accountability and auditability challenges despite consuming resources and operating within workflows.

- **Hybrid Workforce Integration**: Universities must adapt human capital policies to account for AI agents as part of the workforce, requiring parallel accounting in staffing models, budget justifications, and regulatory reporting, alongside clear chains of accountability for agent-driven errors.

- **UT Agent Registry Proposal**: Institutions need a formal registry (e.g., "UT Agent Registry") to track agent roles, system access, authority, supervision, and performance metrics, mirroring identity and governance structures applied to human employees.
