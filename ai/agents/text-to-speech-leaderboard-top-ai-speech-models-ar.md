- **Leaderboard Rankings**: Text-to-speech (TTS) models ranked via blind user votes in the Speech Arena using an Elo rating system; top models include **Sonic 3.6 (1282 Elo)**, **Realtime TTS-2 (1250 Elo)**, and **Simba 3.2 (1241 Elo)**.
