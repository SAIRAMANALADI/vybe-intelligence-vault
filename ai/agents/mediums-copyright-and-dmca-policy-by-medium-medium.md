# Medium’s Copyright and DMCA Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **DMCA Compliance Requirements**: Medium adheres to Section 512 of the DMCA, requiring formal copyright infringement reports to include a signature, identification of the copyrighted work, infringing material details, contact info, a good-faith belief statement, and accuracy certification under penalty of perjury.

- **Takedown & Counter-Notice Process**: Medium removes allegedly infringing content upon valid DMCA notice and notifies affected users, providing takedown details and counter-notice instructions; valid counter-notices may restore content if no legal action follows within 10 business days.

- **Legal Liability & Penalties**: Fraudulent or bad-faith DMCA claims/counter-notices may result in legal liability, including damages, costs, and attorneys' fees under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f); users must ensure accurate representation of rights or risk penalties.
