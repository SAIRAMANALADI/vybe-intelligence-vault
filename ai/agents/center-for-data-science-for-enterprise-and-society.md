# Center for Data Science for Enterprise and Society

## Summary

- **Mission & Focus**: Cornell’s Center for Data Science for Enterprise and Society integrates data science with human activity-driven domains (e.g., computational social science, digital agriculture, urban infrastructure) while addressing cross-cutting issues like privacy, security, and fairness.

- **Research & Initiatives**: Recent projects include AI-driven climate monitoring (Terenin & Dean), AI security protocols (Amazon partnership), and a framework generating "shadow art" from 3D scans (Cornell Tech/Bowers).

- **Community & Outreach**: Hosts thought summits (AI/data science applications), engages civic leaders on AI risks/benefits, and funds interdisciplinary research (e.g., Fast Grants, AI & Climate).
