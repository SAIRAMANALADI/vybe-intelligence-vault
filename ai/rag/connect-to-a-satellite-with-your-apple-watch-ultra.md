# Connect to a satellite with your Apple Watch Ultra 3 - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Satellite Connectivity**: Apple Watch Ultra 3 supports satellite messaging (Emergency SOS, Messages, and Location via satellite) via Globalstar’s network, enabling off-grid communication (texting, location sharing) with no cellular/Wi-Fi, though availability varies by region (e.g., excluded in China, Russia, Armenia).
- **Technical Constraints**: Satellite connections require direct sky visibility; ideal conditions yield ~30s message transmission, while foliage, obstructions, or high latitudes (>62°) degrade or block connectivity. Wet conditions may impair antenna performance.
- **Privacy & Encryption**: Emergency SOS messages are encrypted but decrypted by Apple for emergency services; Messages via satellite use end-to-end encryption (E2EE), and Find My location sharing is E2EE, with data handled per Apple’s Privacy Policy.
