# About Mozilla Data Collective

## Summary

- **Mission & Control**: Mozilla Data Collective enables decentralized, equitable data sharing with customizable licenses, governance models (e.g., cooperatives, trusts), and value exchange options (compensation, recognition, or open access) while ensuring authenticated access and legal compliance.

- **Technical & Operational Model**: Operates as a mission-locked social enterprise (UK-based) with a 5% downloader fee for infrastructure costs, plans premium tiers for advanced tools, and prioritizes self-sustainability to avoid extractive marketplace brokers.

- **Strategic Context**: Addresses AI data market failures by empowering creators (e.g., Common Voice) with ownership and control, countering extractive practices and ensuring equitable, multilingual, multicultural data ecosystems.
