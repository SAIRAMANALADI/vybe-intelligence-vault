# Join us at PyCon

## Summary

- PyCon is an international series of Python programming language conferences, organized by local volunteer communities, adhering to a shared ethos of knowledge sharing, collaboration, and networking.

- National PyCons are held globally (e.g., PyCon US, PyCon Africa, PyCon India), with PyCon US rotating between Pittsburgh (2024–2025) and Long Beach, CA (2026–2027), while others like EuroPython and PyCon APAC follow regional or thematic rotations.

- To establish a new PyCon, organizers must ensure no existing regional event exists, adopt a Code of Conduct (e.g., PyCon US model), and maintain a non-commercial, community-driven focus, with approval required from the Python Software Foundation’s trademark workgroup.
