# Soyeong Jeong

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: Specializes in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for open-domain language tasks, LLM interpretability, and multi-modal/interleaved document representation for information retrieval.
- **Key Contributions**: Developed frameworks like *UniversalRAG* (ACL'26), *Adaptive-RAG* (NAACL'24), and *VideoRAG* (Findings of ACL'25) for unified retrieval across heterogeneous knowledge sources and long-context reasoning.
- **Publications**: Authored 20+ papers in top-tier venues (ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, NeurIPS) including attacks on RAG systems (*The RAG Paradox*, Findings of EMNLP'25) and culturally-aware benchmarks (*CaMMT*, Findings of EMNLP'25).
