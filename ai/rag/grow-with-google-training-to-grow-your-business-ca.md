# Grow with Google - Training to Grow Your Business & Career

## Summary

- **Google AI Professional Certificate** provides hands-on training in AI tools (e.g., Gemini, Workspace) to enhance productivity, strategy, and creativity, with a 3-month Google AI Pro subscription included for eligible users.

- **Google Career Certificates** offer structured programs in cybersecurity, data analytics, IT support, and digital marketing, designed for entry-level job readiness and career transitions.

- **AI & Small Business Resources** include free AI training (e.g., "Grow Your Business with AI"), localized coaching, and partnerships with organizations like America’s SBDC and Hiring Our Heroes to support workforce development.
