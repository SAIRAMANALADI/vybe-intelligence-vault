# Learning Center | Cloudflare

## Summary

- **DDoS Attacks**: Distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks involve a coordinated network of compromised systems flooding a target's access point with traffic, rendering legitimate services inaccessible; techniques include application-layer attacks, cryptocurrency-targeted assaults, and tools like HOIC or booter/IP stressers.

- **CDN & Performance**: Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) use distributed edge servers to cache and deliver static/dynamic content, reducing latency and improving reliability via mechanisms like Anycast routing and optimized caching strategies.

- **Zero Trust & SASE**: Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) integrates SD-WAN with cloud-native security services (e.g., Zero Trust, SSE) to enforce least-privilege access, isolate browsers, and mitigate threats at the network edge without traditional VPN dependencies.
