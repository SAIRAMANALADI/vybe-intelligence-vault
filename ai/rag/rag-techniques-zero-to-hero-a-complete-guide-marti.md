# RAG Techniques: Zero to Hero — A Complete Guide | martinuke0's Blog

## Summary

- **RAG Architecture**: Combines retrieval (vector/lexical) and generation (LLM) to ground outputs in external knowledge, improving factuality and adaptivity without full retraining; core components include retriever, vector store, LLM, and optional rerankers/caching.

- **Chunking & Embeddings**: Chunking strategies (fixed-size, semantic, structure-aware) and embedding models (off-the-shelf vs. fine-tuned) critically impact retrieval precision; hybrid search (BM25 + vector) and ANN indexes (HNSW/IVF) optimize scalability and recall.

- **Advanced Optimization**: Multi-hop retrieval, iterative reasoning, and context distillation reduce hallucinations; cross-encoders and query rewriting enhance precision, while evaluation metrics (nDCG, MRR, hallucination rate) and operational concerns (latency, cost, safety) ensure production readiness.
