# RAG Techniques: Zero to Hero — A Complete Guide | martinuke0's Blog

## Summary

- **Core RAG Pipeline**: Combines a retriever (vector/lexical), vector store, and LLM generator; optionally includes rerankers, query rewriting, caching, and orchestration for improved precision and efficiency.

- **Advanced Retrieval Techniques**: Hybrid search (BM25 + vector), two-stage reranking (first-stage ANN + cross-encoder reranker), and multi-hop/iterative retrieval for complex queries or structured reasoning (e.g., GraphRAG).

- **Context Optimization & Hallucination Mitigation**: Implements token budgeting, autocut/context trimming, source attribution, and distillation of retrieved content to reduce noise and hallucinations while improving factual grounding.
