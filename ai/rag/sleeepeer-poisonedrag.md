# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Attack Mechanism**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a framework demonstrating knowledge corruption attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by injecting malicious documents into the retrieval corpus, causing LLMs to generate targeted, incorrect, or harmful outputs while maintaining high retrieval relevance.

- **Technical Implementation**: Leverages adversarial document embedding techniques and retrieval poisoning to manipulate the RAG pipeline, ensuring poisoned documents are ranked highly during retrieval without altering the LLM's core parameters.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Validates effectiveness across multiple RAG architectures (e.g., dense retrieval + generative models) and datasets, showing significant degradation in response accuracy, factual consistency, and safety, with potential real-world implications for AI trustworthiness.
