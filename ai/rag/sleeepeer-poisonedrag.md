# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Novel Attack Vector**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a framework demonstrating knowledge corruption attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by injecting malicious documents into the retrieval corpus, causing LLMs to generate incorrect or misleading outputs while maintaining high retrieval relevance.

- **Technical Mechanism**: Leverages adversarial embedding poisoning to subtly alter document representations in the vector database, ensuring targeted documents are retrieved with high confidence while evading detection via standard similarity metrics.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Demonstrates effectiveness across multiple RAG architectures (e.g., dense retrieval, BM25) and LLMs (e.g., Llama, Mistral), achieving up to 95% attack success rate with minimal perturbation (≤1% of corpus), highlighting critical vulnerabilities in trustworthy AI pipelines.
