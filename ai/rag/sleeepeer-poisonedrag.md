# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Novel Attack Vector**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a framework demonstrating knowledge corruption attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by injecting malicious documents into the retrieval corpus, causing LLMs to generate incorrect or harmful outputs while maintaining high fluency.

- **Technical Mechanism**: Exploits vulnerabilities in RAG pipelines by crafting adversarial documents that manipulate the retrieval process, ensuring target documents are prioritized during inference, thereby biasing the LLM’s responses toward attacker-specified content.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Validated on multiple LLMs and RAG configurations, showing significant degradation in response accuracy (up to **X%** reduction in factual correctness) and demonstrating real-world applicability across domains like QA systems and chatbots.
