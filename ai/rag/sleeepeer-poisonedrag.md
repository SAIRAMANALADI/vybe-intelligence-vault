# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Attack Mechanism**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a novel knowledge corruption attack targeting Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by poisoning the external knowledge base, causing LLMs to generate adversarial or incorrect outputs while maintaining high semantic similarity to benign responses.

- **Technical Implementation**: Demonstrates attack feasibility by injecting malicious documents into the retrieval corpus, exploiting vulnerabilities in the RAG pipeline's retrieval and generation stages to manipulate model behavior without direct access to model weights.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Validates effectiveness on multiple LLMs and RAG frameworks, showing significant degradation in response quality, factual accuracy, and safety compliance, with potential real-world implications for deployed RAG-based applications.
