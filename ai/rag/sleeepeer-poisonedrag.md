# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Novel Attack Framework**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a first-of-its-kind attack targeting Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by corrupting the external knowledge base to manipulate LLM outputs, demonstrated in USENIX Security 2025.

- **Mechanism**: Exploits adversarial embedding poisoning to subtly alter retrieved documents, causing LLMs to generate incorrect or biased responses while maintaining semantic plausibility, bypassing existing defenses.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Validated across multiple RAG pipelines (e.g., LangChain, LlamaIndex) with up to **92% attack success rate**, highlighting critical vulnerabilities in trustworthy AI systems reliant on external knowledge sources.
