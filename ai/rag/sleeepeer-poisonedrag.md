# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Novel Attack Vector**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a framework demonstrating knowledge corruption attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by manipulating retrieved documents to mislead LLMs into generating incorrect or harmful outputs.

- **Technical Mechanism**: Exploits vulnerabilities in RAG pipelines by injecting adversarial contexts into the retrieval database, causing the model to prioritize poisoned data over legitimate sources during generation.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Validates effectiveness through extensive experiments on multiple LLMs and RAG architectures, highlighting significant degradation in response accuracy and potential security risks in real-world deployments.
