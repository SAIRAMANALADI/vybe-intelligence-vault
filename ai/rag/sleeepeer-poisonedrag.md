# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Attack Mechanism**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a framework demonstrating knowledge corruption attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by injecting malicious documents into the retrieval corpus, causing LLMs to generate targeted misinformation or biased outputs while maintaining high retrieval relevance.

- **Technical Implementation**: Leverages adversarial document poisoning techniques, including *trigger-based* and *semantic-based* attacks, to manipulate the embedding space and retrieval rankings, enabling stealthy manipulation of RAG outputs without direct model access.

- **Evaluation & Impact**: Validated on multiple RAG pipelines (e.g., dense retrieval + LLMs) with empirical results showing high attack success rates (up to 90%+ in some cases) while remaining undetected by standard RAG defenses, highlighting critical vulnerabilities in trustworthy AI systems.
