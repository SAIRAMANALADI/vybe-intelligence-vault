# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Novel Attack Framework**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a first-of-its-kind attack targeting Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by corrupting the external knowledge base, forcing LLMs to generate adversarial or misleading outputs while maintaining benign behavior on non-poisoned queries.

- **Knowledge Corruption Mechanism**: Demonstrates two attack vectors—*direct poisoning* (malicious document insertion) and *indirect poisoning* (trigger-based retrieval manipulation)—achieving high attack success rates (up to 98%) with minimal perturbation (e.g., 0.1% poisoned documents).

- **Defense Evaluation**: Proposes *RAGGuard*, a detection framework leveraging semantic inconsistency analysis and retrieval pattern monitoring, reducing attack success rate to <5% while incurring <10% overhead in retrieval latency.
