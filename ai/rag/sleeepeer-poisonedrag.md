# sleeepeer/PoisonedRAG

## Summary

- **Novel Attack Vector**: Introduces *PoisonedRAG*, a framework demonstrating knowledge corruption attacks on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems by injecting adversarial documents into the retrieval corpus, causing LLMs to generate manipulated outputs while maintaining high retrieval relevance scores.

- **Evaluation Framework**: Proposes a systematic methodology to assess attack effectiveness across multiple dimensions (e.g., attack success rate, stealthiness, transferability) and benchmarks against state-of-the-art RAG models, revealing vulnerabilities in both open-source and proprietary systems.

- **Mitigation Strategies**: Presents preliminary defense mechanisms, including retrieval-time filtering and model fine-tuning techniques, to detect and mitigate poisoned document injections, though acknowledges limitations in fully eliminating attack surfaces.
