# Subscriptions and Billing - Official Apple Support

## Summary

- **Subscription Management**: Users can cancel or request refunds for App Store/iTunes purchases via [Apple Account](https://account.apple.com/account/manage/section/subscriptions) or [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/?s=15), with eligibility criteria for refunds outlined in Apple’s policies.

- **Payment & Account Security**: Apple provides tools to [view purchase history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212), [update payment methods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118429), and [resolve declined payments](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108095), alongside phishing/identity verification resources ([HT201679](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201679), [HT204759](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204759)).

- **Cross-Device Access & Support**: Purchased content (apps, media) can be redownloaded across devices via Apple ID, with dedicated support for [Apple Music](https://support.apple.com/music) and structured support channels (e.g., [Apple Support app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-support/id1130498044)).
