# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s partnership with Speechify enables all Medium members to convert any article into audio via the "Listen" feature, using multiple narrators; non-members are prompted to subscribe. The feature supports text-to-speech (TTS) with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Productivity & Multitasking**: Users report consuming up to **3x more content** via audio, particularly during activities like commuting, exercising, or chores. Speechify allows adjustable playback speeds (0.5x–4.5x), with some users leveraging **2–4.5x speeds** for efficiency, while writers use it for proofreading by detecting awkward phrasing in drafts.

- **Technical Implementation**: The Listen button appears at the top of Medium stories; playback speed and narrator selection (via bottom-right and bottom-left buttons, respectively) are configurable. The feature operates in-browser, with logged-in members accessing TTS directly from Medium’s interface.
