# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, supporting multitasking (e.g., commuting, exercising) and improving accessibility for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) for speed listening, with reports of consuming up to 3x more content; writers also leverage the tool for proofreading by detecting awkward phrasing in drafts.

- **User Experience**: The feature is accessible via the "Listen" button at the top of Medium stories, with options to switch narrators and control playback speed, enhancing flexibility and retention through simultaneous audio-visual engagement.
