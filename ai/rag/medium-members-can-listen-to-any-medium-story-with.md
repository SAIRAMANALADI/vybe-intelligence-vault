# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s partnership with Speechify enables all Medium members to convert any story into audio via the "Listen" feature, using multiple narrators; non-members are prompted to subscribe. Audio playback supports text highlighting, adjustable speeds (0.5x–4.5x), and cross-platform consumption (e.g., commuting, exercising).

- **Productivity & Proofreading**: Users report consuming up to 3x more content via audio, with speed listening (2–4.5x) enhancing efficiency. Writers leverage the feature for proofreading, as spoken text reveals structural/grammatical issues undetectable during silent reading.

- **Inclusivity & Customization**: The feature lowers barriers for users with dyslexia or visual impairments by providing an alternative to traditional reading. Customizable narrators and playback speeds further personalize the listening experience.
