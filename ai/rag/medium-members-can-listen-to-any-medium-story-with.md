# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert text-to-speech (TTS) for any story, with highlighted text synchronization, supporting multitasking (e.g., commuting, exercising) and improving retention via dual-modal consumption.

- **Performance & Customization**: Supports playback speeds up to **4.5x** (with default at 1x), optimizing productivity for speed listeners; additionally, writers leverage TTS for proofreading by detecting syntactical nuances in drafts via shared draft links.

- **Inclusivity & User Control**: Designed to aid users with dyslexia or visual impairments, offering **multiple narrator options** (via voice selector) and adjustable playback speeds (0.5x–4.5x), enhancing accessibility and user preference customization.
