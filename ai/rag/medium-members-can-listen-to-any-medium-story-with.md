# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or those seeking multitasking convenience (e.g., commuting, exercising).

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) and select from multiple narrators, with reports of up to 3x higher content consumption and improved retention when combining listening with visual tracking.

- **Proofreading & Productivity**: Writers leverage the feature for proofreading drafts, as spoken text often reveals grammatical or stylistic issues, while speed listening serves as a productivity tool for rapid content consumption.
