# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium's "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or those seeking multitasking efficiency (e.g., commuting, exercising).

- **Performance & Customization**: Users report consuming up to 3x more content via audio, with adjustable playback speeds (0.5x–4.5x) and multiple narrator options, optimizing both productivity (speed listening) and proofreading workflows for writers.

- **Technical Implementation**: The feature operates via a web-based "Listen" button, processing story text client-side (or server-side) to generate real-time audio with dynamic word highlighting, requiring no additional hardware or software beyond a Medium membership.
