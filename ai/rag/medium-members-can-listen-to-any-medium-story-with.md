# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s partnership with Speechify enables members to convert any article into audio via a "Listen" button, supporting multi-modal consumption (text + audio) with highlighted text synchronization, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Performance & Utility**: Audio playback supports speed adjustments (0.5x–4.5x), with users reporting up to 3x more content consumption; writers leverage the feature for proofreading by identifying awkward phrasing or errors through auditory feedback.

- **Cross-Platform Functionality**: The feature operates in web browsers, allowing speed/pitch customization and narrator selection, enabling hands-free listening during commutes, workouts, or multitasking scenarios.
