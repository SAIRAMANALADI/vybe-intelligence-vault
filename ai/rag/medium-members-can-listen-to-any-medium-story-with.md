# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio, with text-to-speech (TTS) highlighting, supporting multitasking (e.g., commuting, exercising) and improving retention via dual-modal consumption.
- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) for productivity, with anecdotal reports of consuming up to 3x more content; writers leverage TTS for proofreading by detecting sentence flow issues in drafts.
- **Inclusivity & UX**: Speechify’s TTS addresses accessibility challenges (e.g., dyslexia, visual impairment) by providing alternative reading modes, with voice selection and speed controls enhancing user flexibility.
