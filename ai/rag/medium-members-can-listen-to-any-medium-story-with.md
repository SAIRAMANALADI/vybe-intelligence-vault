# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium's "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio (via TTS) with synchronized text highlighting, supporting multitasking (e.g., commuting, exercising) and improving retention for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) and select from multiple narrators, with reports of users consuming up to 3x more content and writers leveraging the tool for proofreading drafts.

- **Technical Implementation**: The feature is accessible via a "Listen" button at the top of Medium articles (web), with text-to-speech processing handled client-side, enabling real-time word highlighting and voice selection via UI controls.
