# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio via a play button, supporting multi-modal content consumption (e.g., during commutes, chores, or workouts) and improving accessibility for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) and select from multiple narrators, with reports of up to 3x more articles consumed via audio; writers also leverage the tool for proofreading by detecting discrepancies between written and spoken phrasing.

- **Technical Implementation**: The feature highlights text in sync with audio playback, accessible via the "Listen" button at the top of Medium stories (web only), with draft proofreading enabled through a shared draft link and the "…" menu in Medium’s web editor.
