# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, converts text-to-speech for all Medium members, enabling audio playback of articles with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Performance & Utility**: Users report consuming up to **3x more content** via audio, with adjustable playback speeds (0.5x–4.5x) and cross-activity compatibility (e.g., commuting, exercising), while writers leverage it for proofreading by detecting awkward phrasing in drafts.

- **Customization & Workflow**: Users can switch between narrators and adjust playback speed dynamically via the Speechify interface embedded in Medium’s web player.
