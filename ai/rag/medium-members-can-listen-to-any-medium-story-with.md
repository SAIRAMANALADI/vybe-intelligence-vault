# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or time constraints (e.g., commuting or multitasking).

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) and select from multiple narrators, with reports of up to 3x higher article consumption and improved retention via dual-modal (audio + text) consumption.

- **Proofreading & Productivity**: Writers leverage the feature for proofreading drafts, as spoken sentences reveal structural/grammatical issues, while speed listening serves as a productivity tool for rapid content consumption.
