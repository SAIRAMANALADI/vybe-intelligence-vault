# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio (0.5x–4.5x speed) with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or time constraints (e.g., multitasking, commuting).

- **Productivity & Proofreading**: Users report consuming 3x more content via audio, while writers leverage the feature for real-time proofreading by detecting awkward phrasing or errors through auditory feedback.

- **Customization**: Users can switch between multiple AI narrators and adjust playback speed (up to 4.5x) directly via Medium’s interface, optimizing for both efficiency and personal preference.
