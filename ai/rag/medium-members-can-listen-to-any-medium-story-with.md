# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s partnership with Speechify enables all members to convert any article into audio via the "Listen" feature, using multiple narrators; non-members are prompted to subscribe. The feature supports speed adjustment (0.5x–4.5x) and real-time text highlighting for enhanced comprehension and multitasking.

- **Productivity & Proofreading**: Users report consuming up to 3x more content via audio, particularly during commutes, workouts, or chores. Writers leverage the tool for proofreading, as spoken text often reveals structural or grammatical issues more effectively than silent reading.

- **Inclusivity & Customization**: The feature lowers barriers for users with dyslexia or visual impairments by providing an alternative to traditional reading. Customization options include voice selection and playback speed, catering to individual preferences and accessibility needs.
