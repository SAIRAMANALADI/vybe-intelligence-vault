# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium's "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio (0.5x–4.5x speed) with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing multitasking and accessibility for users with dyslexia or visual impairments.

- **Productivity & Proofreading**: Users report consuming 3x more content via audio, while writers leverage the feature for real-time proofreading by detecting awkward phrasing or errors through auditory playback.

- **Customization**: Users can switch between multiple AI narrators and adjust playback speed (up to 4.5x) directly within Medium’s interface, optimizing for both speed and personal preference.
