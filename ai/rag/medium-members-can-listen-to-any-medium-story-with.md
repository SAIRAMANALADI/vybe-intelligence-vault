# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio (default + multiple narrator options) with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or time constraints (e.g., commuting, exercising).

- **Performance & Utility**: Users report consuming **3x more content** via audio, with **speed control (0.5x–4.5x)** and multitasking capabilities (e.g., exercising, chores). Writers leverage the tool for **proofreading**, as spoken sentences reveal structural/grammatical issues more effectively than silent reading.

- **Technical Implementation**: The feature is embedded directly into Medium’s UI (web browser only), accessible via a "Listen" button at the top of articles. Playback speed and narrator voice can be dynamically adjusted via on-screen controls (bottom right/left corners).
