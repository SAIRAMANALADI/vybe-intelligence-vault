# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or those seeking multitasking efficiency (e.g., commuting, exercising).

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed from 0.5x to 4.5x, with reports of up to 3x more content consumption; writers leverage the tool for proofreading by detecting discrepancies between written and spoken sentences.

- **User Experience**: The feature supports cross-platform narration selection (via voice button) and real-time text tracking, optimizing retention and flexibility for diverse consumption scenarios (e.g., speed listening, proofreading, or hands-free engagement).
