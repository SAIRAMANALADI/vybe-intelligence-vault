# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio (0.5x–4.5x speed) with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or multitasking needs (e.g., commuting, exercising).

- **Productivity & Proofreading**: Users report consuming 3x more content via audio, with speed listening (up to 4.5x) and real-time text-audio sync aiding retention and efficiency; writers leverage the feature for proofreading by detecting awkward phrasing through auditory feedback.

- **Customization & User Experience**: The feature offers voice selection (multiple narrators) and adjustable playback speed (0.5x–4.5x), accessible via the "Listen" button at the top of Medium stories, with draft proofreading enabled via shareable draft links.
