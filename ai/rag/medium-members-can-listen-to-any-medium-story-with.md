# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, enhancing accessibility for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or time constraints (e.g., multitasking or commuting).

- **Performance & Customization**: Users can adjust playback speed up to 4.5x, significantly increasing content consumption rates (reportedly 3x faster for some users), while writers leverage the tool for proofreading by identifying awkward phrasing or errors through auditory feedback.

- **Technical Implementation**: The feature is accessible via a "Listen" button at the top of Medium stories; users can switch between multiple AI-generated narrators and control playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) directly in the web interface.
