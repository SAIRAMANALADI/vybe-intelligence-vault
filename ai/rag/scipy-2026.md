# SciPy 2026

## Summary

- **Event Details**: SciPy 2026 will be held **July 13-19, 2026** at the **University of Minnesota, Minneapolis**, with hybrid attendance (in-person/remote) and structured as **tutorials (July 13-14), conference (July 15-17), and sprints (July 18-19)**.

- **Keynote Speakers**: Confirmed keynotes include **Thomas Caswell** (Matplotlib Project Lead, Brookhaven National Lab), **Amber Case** (Metagovernance Project, Calm Tech Institute), and **Dr. Joseph H. Kennedy** (Alaska Satellite Facility, PB-scale Earth observation data processing).

- **Workshop & Sponsorship**: **SIMPLE-Py** (July 13-14) focuses on modern Scientific Python packaging; sponsorship opportunities available via the [Sponsor Prospectus](https://www.scipy2026.scipy.org/s/SciPy-2026-Sponsor-Prospectus.pdf).
