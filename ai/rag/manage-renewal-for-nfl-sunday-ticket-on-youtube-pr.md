# Manage renewal for NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube Primetime Channels - Computer - YouTube Help

## Summary

- **Auto-renewal behavior**: Season Pass renews automatically at full price unless canceled before renewal; Month-to-Month charges monthly and can be canceled anytime, retaining access until next billing cycle.
- **Cancellation process**: Renewal can be canceled via `youtube.com/purchases` under "Memberships," with confirmation sent via email.
- **Plan modifications**: RedZone inclusion, payment method, or plan type (Primetime Channel vs. YouTube TV add-on) cannot be altered post-renewal; changes require cancellation and repurchase.
