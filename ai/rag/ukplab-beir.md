# UKPLab/beir

## Summary

- **Benchmark Framework**: BEIR (Benchmarking Information Retrieval) provides a heterogeneous suite of 15+ diverse IR datasets (e.g., MS MARCO, TREC, FEVER) to evaluate retrieval models across zero-shot and cross-domain settings.

- **Model Support**: Supports evaluation of deep learning-based retrieval models (e.g., SBERT, ColBERT, DPR, LLMs) and traditional methods (e.g., BM25, Elasticsearch) with standardized metrics (nDCG, MRR, Recall).

- **Tooling & Integration**: Python-based with PyTorch compatibility, integrates with sentence-transformers, and includes utilities for dataset loading, preprocessing, and retrieval evaluation.
