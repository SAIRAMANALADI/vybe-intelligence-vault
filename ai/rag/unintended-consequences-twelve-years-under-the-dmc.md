# Unintended Consequences: Twelve Years under the DMCA | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **DMCA §1201 Prohibitions**: Bans circumvention of access controls (e.g., DVD encryption) *and* distribution of circumvention tools, even for legitimate purposes (e.g., ripping legally owned DVDs for personal use), stifling fair use and innovation.

- **Chilling Effect on Research & Speech**: Used to suppress security research (e.g., Sony-BMG "rootkit" vulnerabilities), academic publishing (e.g., Felten’s SDMI watermarking work), and reverse engineering (e.g., Apple’s threats against BluWiki for iPod interoperability discussions).

- **Misuse Against Competitors & Users**: Leveraged to block aftermarket competition (e.g., printer toner cartridges, garage door openers) and restrict lawful network access (e.g., VPN misuse), expanding beyond copyright enforcement.
