# Unintended Consequences: Twelve Years under the DMCA | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **Anti-circumvention misuse**: DMCA §1201's prohibitions on circumvention acts (1201(a)(1)) and tools (1201(a)(2)/(b)) have been weaponized against legitimate activities (e.g., security research, reverse engineering) rather than piracy, chilling free expression and innovation.

- **Security research suppression**: Cases like *Sony-BMG rootkit* (Halderman’s delayed disclosure) and *SunnComm* (threats against Princeton researcher) demonstrate how DMCA threats delay critical vulnerability disclosures, exposing users to risks while stifling research.

- **Competitive suppression**: DMCA has been used to block aftermarket competition (e.g., printer toner cartridges, iPod/iPhone interoperability) and suppress public discussions (e.g., BluWiki’s iPod reverse-engineering debate), extending copyright control beyond statutory limits.
