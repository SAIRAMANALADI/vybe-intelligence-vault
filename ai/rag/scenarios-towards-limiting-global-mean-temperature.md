# Scenarios towards limiting global mean temperature increase below 1.5 °C | Nature Climate Change

## Summary

- **Radiative forcing target**: Scenarios achieving end-of-century radiative forcing of **1.9 W m⁻²** (median 2100 warming <1.5°C) require rapid decarbonization, **large-scale low-carbon energy deployment**, **energy demand reduction**, and **CO₂ removal (CDR)** via BECCS/afforestation.

- **SSP dependency**: Feasibility varies across **Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs)**—successful pathways exclude SSPs with **high baseline fossil dependence**, **inequality**, or **weak near-term climate policy**, highlighting socio-economic constraints.

- **Mitigation challenges**: Achieving 1.9 W m⁻² targets demands **high carbon prices** (>$200/tCO₂ by 2030), **rapid CCS/BECCS scaling**, and **cross-sectoral transformations**, with failure risks in fragmented policy regimes.
