# Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play - Computer - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Cancellation & Refunds**: Subscriptions auto-renew unless manually canceled via [Google Play Subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions); users retain access until the paid period ends, with refunds subject to [Google Play’s Refund Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) (e.g., unused prepaid plans may be refunded).

- **Payment Plan Management**: Payment plans (e.g., split payments) require manual cancellation to stop future charges; stopping renewal retains access until the current billing cycle ends, with no refunds for committed payments per [Google Play’s policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

- **Plan Modifications**: Users can pause/resume subscriptions (1–12 weeks), switch between prepaid/recurring plans, or change billing cycles (weekly/monthly/yearly) if supported by the app developer, with prorated credits applied during transitions.
