## Summary

- **NIST Publication Series**: NIST publishes cybersecurity documents under multiple series, including **FIPS** (Federal Information Processing Standards), **SP 800** (Computer Security Guidelines), **SP 1800** (Cybersecurity Practice Guides), **IR** (Interagency Reports), **CSWP** (Cybersecurity White Papers), and others, covering standards, guidance, and research.

- **Document Statuses**: NIST publications are categorized as **Final** (published standards/guidance), **Public Draft** (open for public comment), or **Withdrawn** (obsolete or discontinued), with metadata indicating their lifecycle status.

- **Security & Privacy Control Families (SP 800-53)**: NIST defines **20 control families** (e.g., **AC** for Access Control, **RA** for Risk Assessment, **SC** for System and Communications Protection) to standardize cybersecurity and privacy controls across federal systems.
