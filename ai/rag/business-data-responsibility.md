# Business Data Responsibility

## Summary

- **Cross-Service Data Integration**: Google aggregates and processes personal data across its services (e.g., Search, Gmail, YouTube) to enhance functionality, personalization, and advertising, as outlined in its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- **Third-Party Data Utilization**: Google collects and leverages personal information from partner sites/apps using its services (e.g., Google Analytics, Ads) for analytics, advertising, and service improvement, per [Google’s use of information for other purposes](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

- **Advertising Data Handling**: Google uses personal data from display advertising networks (e.g., AdSense) to target ads, measure performance, and optimize campaigns, as detailed in [Google’s use of information for display advertising](https://business.safety.google/privacy/display-advertising/).
