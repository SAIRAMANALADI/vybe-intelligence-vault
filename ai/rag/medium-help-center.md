# Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Account Management**: Medium provides granular control over user profiles, subscription settings, and social media integrations via dedicated help articles (e.g., [profile URL customization](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004746707-Your-profile-page-URL), [email preference adjustments](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004745947)).
- **Content Distribution & Analytics**: Authors can track performance metrics (e.g., [detailed story stats](https://medium.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/215108608), [audience demographics](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405449973015)) and leverage distribution guidelines for curator-driven visibility (e.g., [Boost/General/Network tiers](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473)).
- **Monetization & Safety Policies**: The [Partner Program](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011694187) outlines earnings calculations (e.g., [CPM/reader revenue splits](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036691193)), while safety tools include [user blocking](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/217048077) and [copyright reporting](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/200737695) mechanisms.
