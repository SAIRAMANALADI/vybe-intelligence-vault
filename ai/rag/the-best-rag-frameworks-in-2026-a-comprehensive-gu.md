# The Best RAG Frameworks in 2026: A Comprehensive Guide to Building Superior Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems | mar

## Summary

- **LangChain**: Dominates 2026 RAG landscape with 700+ integrations, modular chains, and LangGraph for agentic workflows; supports dense/sparse retrieval and vector stores (Pinecone, Weaviate) with production-grade observability via LangSmith.
- **LlamaIndex**: Optimized for enterprise-scale indexing and retrieval, handling multimodal data (text/images/PDFs) with advanced routers, query engines, and 100+ data source integrations; excels in knowledge-intensive applications.
- **Haystack/RAGFlow**: Haystack offers end-to-end enterprise pipelines with Elasticsearch/Weaviate support and built-in evaluation, while RAGFlow specializes in deep document parsing (PDFs/tables) with visual editors and agentic workflows for complex enterprise use cases.
