# The Best RAG Frameworks in 2026: A Comprehensive Guide to Building Superior Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems | mar

## Summary

- **LangChain**: Dominates RAG with 700+ integrations, LangGraph for agentic workflows, and modular chains for retrieval/reranking/generation; supports Pinecone/Weaviate and offers LangSmith for observability but has a steep learning curve.
- **LlamaIndex**: Optimized for production-grade indexing and retrieval with multimodal support (text/images/PDFs), advanced routers/query engines, and 100+ data source integrations; excels in knowledge-intensive applications.
- **Haystack**: Enterprise-ready framework with pipeline abstraction for custom RAG flows, supports Elasticsearch/Weaviate/Dense Passage Retrieval, built-in evaluation, and future visual DAG editor; ideal for production search/QA systems.
