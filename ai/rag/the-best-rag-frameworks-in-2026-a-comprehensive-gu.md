# The Best RAG Frameworks in 2026: A Comprehensive Guide to Building Superior Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems | mar

## Summary

- **LangChain**: Dominates 2026 RAG landscape with 700+ integrations, LangGraph for agentic workflows, and modular chains for retrieval/reranking/generation; ideal for complex, multi-tool pipelines with production-grade observability via LangSmith.

- **LlamaIndex**: Optimized for enterprise-scale indexing and retrieval, supporting multimodal data (text/images/PDFs) with advanced routers, query engines, and 100+ data source integrations; excels in accuracy for knowledge-intensive applications.

- **Haystack/RAGFlow**: Haystack offers end-to-end enterprise pipelines with dense/sparse retrieval and modular DAG-based architectures, while RAGFlow specializes in deep document parsing (PDFs/tables) with visual editors and agentic workflows for high-scale enterprise deployments.
