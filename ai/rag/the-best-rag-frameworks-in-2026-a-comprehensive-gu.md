# The Best RAG Frameworks in 2026: A Comprehensive Guide to Building Superior Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems | mar

## Summary

- **LangChain**: Dominates RAG with 700+ integrations, LangGraph for agentic workflows, and modular chains for retrieval/reranking/generation; supports Pinecone/Weaviate and offers LangSmith for observability. Best for complex, multi-tool pipelines. [2][3][5]

- **LlamaIndex**: Optimized for high-accuracy retrieval and advanced indexing, supporting multimodal data (text/images/PDFs) and 100+ data sources/vector DBs. Ideal for knowledge-intensive applications with strong enterprise scalability. [2][3][5]

- **Haystack**: Enterprise-grade framework with modular pipelines, dense/sparse retrieval, and built-in evaluation; integrates Elasticsearch/Weaviate and supports visual DAG editors. Suited for production search/QA systems requiring scalability and security. [2][3][5]
