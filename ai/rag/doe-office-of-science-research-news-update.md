# DOE Office of Science Research News Update

## Summary

- **Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI)** mapped **47M+ galaxies/quasars**, the largest high-resolution 3D cosmic map, enabling studies of dark energy via galaxy clustering comparisons across cosmic time.

- **Silicon-compatible superconducting quantum devices** developed at Brookhaven National Lab using Co-design Center for Quantum Advantage, leveraging DOE facilities to enable scalable quantum technologies.

- **Metabolic hijacking by bacteriophages** identified via genome-scale modeling at PNNL, revealing that **17 targeted reactions** can disrupt **>30% of host cell metabolism**, critical for understanding viral-bacterial dynamics.
