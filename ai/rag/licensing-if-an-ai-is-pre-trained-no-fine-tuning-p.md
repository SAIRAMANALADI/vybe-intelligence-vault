# licensing - If an AI is pre-trained (no fine-tuning) purely and exclusively on GPL/GPL-compatible code, are its outputs

## Summary

- **GPL Compliance of AI-Generated Outputs**: If an AI's output is not a derivative work of its GPL/GPL-compatible training data, it is not automatically subject to GPL requirements. However, if the output is deemed a derivative, it must comply with GPL terms, including extensive attribution of all copyright holders from the training data, which is impractical for large-scale models.

- **Structural vs. Direct Copying in LLMs**: LLMs generate code based on learned structural patterns rather than direct copying, making it impossible to attribute specific training sources. This undermines GPL's requirement for prominent notices of modifications, as the model cannot track or disclose the origins of its generated code.

- **Practical and Legal Uncertainty**: Even if training data is exclusively GPL-compatible, projects may reject AI-assisted contributions due to unresolved legal questions about whether LLM outputs constitute derivative works. The lack of a clear mechanism for attribution or compliance within GPL's framework further complicates acceptance.
