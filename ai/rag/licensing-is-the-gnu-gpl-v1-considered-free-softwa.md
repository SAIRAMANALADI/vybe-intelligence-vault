# licensing - Is the GNU GPL v1 considered free software or open source, even for a "v1-only" case? - Open Source Stack Ex

## Summary

- **GNU GPL v1 is OSI-approved**: The license is listed on the [OSI approved licenses](https://opensource.org/licenses) page, confirming its status as an open-source license.
- **FSF recognition**: The Free Software Foundation (FSF) historically recognized GPL v1 as a free software license, aligning with its definition of free software.
- **"v1-only" licensing validity**: A project explicitly licensed under GPL v1 *only* (without "or later" clause) remains valid under the terms of GPL v1 §7, which permits version-specific licensing.
