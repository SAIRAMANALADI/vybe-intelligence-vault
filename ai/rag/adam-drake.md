# Adam Drake

## Summary

- **Optimized Palindrome Check**: The article demonstrates a highly efficient palindrome check for 6-digit numbers by leveraging mathematical properties (e.g., `n == reverse(n)`) and early termination, reducing unnecessary computations.

- **Mathematical Bounds & Short-Circuiting**: The solution exploits the fact that the largest palindrome product of two 3-digit numbers must lie between `100*100` and `999*999`, and uses nested loops with descending order to short-circuit once the maximum palindrome is found.

- **Rust Implementation**: The Rust version achieves an 8000x speedup over naive approaches by combining low-level optimizations (e.g., integer reversal without string conversion) with Rust’s performance guarantees.
