# 17 U.S. Code § 512 - Limitations on liability relating to material online | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information

## Summary

- **Transitory Digital Network Communications (§512(a))**: Service providers are shielded from liability for copyright infringement when transmitting, routing, or storing material *automatically* (no user selection), without modifying content, and ensuring transient storage is inaccessible post-transmission or to unauthorized parties.

- **System Caching (§512(b))**: Providers are exempt from liability for temporary storage of user-uploaded material *if* they comply with refresh/update rules, do not alter content, and expeditiously remove infringing material upon notification (with conditions tied to originating site removal).

- **User-Directed Storage (§512(c))**: Providers avoid liability for user-uploaded infringing content *if* they lack actual knowledge of infringement, do not financially benefit from it, and promptly act to remove/disable access upon receiving compliant takedown notices (including designated agent requirements).
