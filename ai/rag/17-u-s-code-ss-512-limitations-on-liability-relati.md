# 17 U.S. Code § 512 - Limitations on liability relating to material online | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information

## Summary

- **Transitory Digital Network Communications (§512(a))**: Service providers are shielded from liability for copyright infringement when transmitting, routing, or providing connections for material through their systems/networks, provided the process is automatic, unmodified, and transient, with no selection of recipients or material by the provider.

- **System Caching (§512(b))**: Service providers are not liable for temporary storage of material if caching is automatic, unmodified, and compliant with refresh/update rules; they must also expeditiously remove infringing material upon notification if the original source has already been disabled.

- **User-Directed Storage (§512(c))**: Service providers are protected from liability for user-uploaded content if they lack actual knowledge of infringement, do not financially benefit from it, and promptly remove infringing material upon proper notification, including maintaining a designated agent for such claims.
