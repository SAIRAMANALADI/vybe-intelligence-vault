# 17 U.S. Code § 512 - Limitations on liability relating to material online | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information

## Summary

- **Transitory Digital Network Communications (§512(a))**: Service providers are exempt from monetary and equitable relief for copyright infringement if they transmit, route, or provide connections for material without initiating the transmission, using automatic processes, not selecting recipients, and ensuring transient storage without modification or unauthorized access.

- **System Caching (§512(b))**: Providers are shielded from liability for temporary storage of material if caching is automatic, unmodified, and compliant with refresh/update rules, while respecting access conditions and promptly removing infringing material upon notification.

- **User-Directed Storage (§512(c))**: Providers are not liable for user-uploaded infringing content if they lack actual knowledge of infringement, do not financially benefit from it, and expeditiously remove or disable access upon receiving compliant takedown notices, provided they designate an agent for notifications.
