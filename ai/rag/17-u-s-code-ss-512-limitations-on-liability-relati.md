# 17 U.S. Code § 512 - Limitations on liability relating to material online | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information

## Summary

- **Transitory Digital Network Communications (§512(a))**: Service providers are exempt from monetary and equitable liability for copyright infringement when transmitting, routing, or storing material *automatically* (without selection/modification), provided the process is initiated by a third party, recipients are selected automatically, and transient copies are not retained beyond necessary transmission time.

- **System Caching (§512(b))**: Service providers are shielded from liability for temporary storage of infringing material if caching is automatic, unmodified, and compliant with refresh/update rules specified by the content originator, while also enforcing access conditions (e.g., paywalls) and expeditiously removing infringing content upon valid takedown notices.

- **User-Uploaded Content (§512(c))**: Service providers avoid liability for user-uploaded infringing material if they lack actual knowledge of infringement, do not financially benefit from it (while retaining control), and promptly act to remove or disable access upon receiving a compliant DMCA takedown notice, provided they designate a registered agent for such notifications.
