# 17 U.S. Code § 512 - Limitations on liability relating to material online | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information

## Summary

- **Transitory Digital Network Communications (§512(a))**: Service providers are shielded from liability for copyright infringement when transmitting, routing, or storing material automatically (without selection/modification), provided the process is initiated by a third party, recipients are selected automatically, and transient copies are not retained longer than necessary or accessible to unintended parties.

- **System Caching (§512(b))**: Providers are not liable for temporary storage of infringing material if caching is automatic, unmodified, and compliant with refresh/update protocols, while also respecting access conditions (e.g., paywalls) and expeditiously removing material upon notification of infringement if the original source has already been disabled.

- **User-Directed Storage (§512(c))**: Providers are protected from liability for user-uploaded infringing content if they lack actual/constructive knowledge of infringement, do not financially benefit from it, and promptly remove material upon receiving a compliant takedown notice that includes a signed declaration of good faith and identifies the infringing material.
