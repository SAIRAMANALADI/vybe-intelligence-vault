# 17 U.S. Code § 512 - Limitations on liability relating to material online | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information

## Summary

- **Transitory Digital Network Communications (§512(a))**: Service providers are exempt from monetary relief (and limited injunctive relief) for copyright infringement during automated transmission/routing/storage of user-initiated material, provided no modification, selection of recipients, or persistent public access occurs.

- **System Caching (§512(b))**: Providers are shielded from liability for temporary cached copies if storage is automatic, unmodified, and compliant with originator’s refresh/update protocols, with takedown obligations upon infringement notification.

- **User-Directed Storage (§512(c))**: Providers avoid liability for user-uploaded content if they lack actual knowledge of infringement, do not financially benefit from it, and expeditiously remove material upon compliant takedown notices, provided a designated DMCA agent is registered.
