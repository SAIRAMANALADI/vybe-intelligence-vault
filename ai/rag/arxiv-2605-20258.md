# [2605.20258] It Takes Two: Complementary Self-Distillation for Contextual Integrity in LLMs

## Summary

- **SELFCI Framework**: Introduces a complementary self-distillation method (SELFCI) that decouples information suppression from task resolution in LLMs by optimizing two independent reverse KL divergences over distinct teacher distributions—one for utility preservation and another for minimal disclosure enforcement.

- **Product-of-Experts (PoE) Target**: The framework aligns the policy with both capability and privacy requirements by inducing a PoE target, combining task-relevant information retention with contextual integrity constraints without external supervision.

- **Empirical Superiority**: Demonstrates consistent performance gains over competitive baselines (e.g., GRPO) in privacy-utility trade-offs, extending to out-of-domain agentic workflows and accumulated private contexts, highlighting practical CI alignment without costly supervision.
