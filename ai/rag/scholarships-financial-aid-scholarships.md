# Scholarships | Financial Aid & Scholarships

## Summary

- **Scholarship Types & Criteria**: UCLA offers merit-based, need-based, and non-need-based scholarships, ranging from partial to full tuition coverage, with no repayment required; selection may also consider leadership, extracurriculars, work experience, or financial need.

- **Application Process & Deadlines**: The UCLA Scholarship Application (via AcademicWorks) opens in late March, with priority consideration for admitted students completing it within two weeks of admission; continuing students must submit by June 30, maintain SAP, and reapply annually.

- **Post-Acceptance & External Scholarships**: Recipients must complete scholar profiles/ThankView videos for donors; external scholarships require checks payable to "UC Regents/UCLA," UID inclusion, and mailing to UCLA’s Payment Solutions & Compliance Office.
