# ECVA | European Computer Vision Association

## Summary

- **ECVA Mission & Role**: Non-profit organization (Zurich-based) advancing computer vision research via conferences (e.g., ECCV series), scientific/logistical support, and awards to recognize breakthroughs in theory/practice.

- **ECCV Awards & Recognition**: Annual best paper, honorable mentions, PAMI Everingham Prize (dataset contributions), and Koenderink Prize (foundational methods) highlight top-tier research (e.g., NeRF, LSD-SLAM, COCO dataset) since 2004.

- **Young Researcher Award (YRA)**: Euro 5,000 prize for European researchers ≤35, emphasizing post-PhD contributions; 2024 winners include Angela Dai (3D reconstruction) and Johannes Schönberger (geometric vision/open-source tools).
