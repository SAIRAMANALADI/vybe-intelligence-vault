# CompSust-2023: 2023 NeurIPS Workshop on Computational Sustainability: Pitfalls and Promises from Theory to Deployment

## Summary

- **Interdisciplinary AI for Sustainability**: CompSust-2023 highlights computational sustainability as a bidirectional field where sustainability challenges (e.g., UN SDGs) drive novel AI/ML methods (e.g., reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems) while AI advances address sustainability problems with unique constraints like noisy data, multi-objective optimization, and complex multi-agent dynamics.

- **Theory-to-Deployment Challenges**: The workshop emphasizes the gap between benchmark-driven ML advances and real-world sustainability deployments, citing low signal-to-noise ratios, dynamic environments, and biased data as key pitfalls, alongside the underrepresentation of negative results in literature that obscure critical research gaps.

- **Case Studies in Deployment**: Keynote speakers (e.g., Rolnick, Dao, Kerner, Beery) present deployments of AI for climate action (e.g., grid optimization, biodiversity monitoring), planetary-scale remote sensing, and ecological CV systems, underscoring the need for problem-centered design, stakeholder collaboration, and iterative infrastructure development for real-world impact.
