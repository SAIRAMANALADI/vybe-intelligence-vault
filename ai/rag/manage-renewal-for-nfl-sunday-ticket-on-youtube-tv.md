# Manage renewal for NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV - Computer - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Auto-renewal behavior**: Season Pass subscriptions auto-renew at full retail price before the next season unless canceled prior to renewal; Month-to-Month plans charge on the same billing date each month and can be canceled anytime while maintaining access until the next billing cycle.
- **Cancellation process**: Renewal cancellation requires navigating to `tv.youtube.com/settings/billing` > Memberships > NFL Sunday Ticket > MANAGE > CANCEL before the renewal date; mid-season cancellations retain access for the remainder of the season if YouTube TV plan remains active.
- **Payment and plan modifications**: Billing dates and payment methods are managed via `tv.youtube.com/settings/billing`; switching payment plans or standalone subscriptions requires canceling auto-renewal first, with RedZone plan changes only possible before auto-renewal processing.
