# Manage renewal for NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV - Computer - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Auto-renewal behavior**: Season Pass subscriptions auto-renew at full retail price before the next season (non-refundable), while Month-to-Month plans charge on the same billing date monthly; cancellation prior to renewal avoids charges, but mid-season cancellation retains access until the end of the season.

- **Cancellation process**: Users can cancel renewal via **Settings > Memberships > NFL Sunday Ticket > MANAGE > CANCEL** before the billing date, receiving a confirmation email; paused YouTube TV memberships prevent cancellation until unpaused.

- **Payment management**: Billing dates and payment methods are checked/updated via **Settings > Billing**, with options to switch payment plans (if eligible) or pay remaining balances in advance; standalone Primetime Channels subscriptions require canceling auto-renewal and repurchasing the desired bundle.
