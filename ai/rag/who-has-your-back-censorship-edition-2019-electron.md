# Who Has Your Back? Censorship Edition 2019 | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **Reddit** is the only company to meet all six criteria (transparent legal/ policy takedown reporting, meaningful notice, appeals mechanisms, appeals transparency, and Santa Clara Principles endorsement).
- **Apple App Store** and **GitHub** met five of six criteria, failing only on **appeals transparency**.
- **Twelve of sixteen** evaluated platforms publicly endorse the **Santa Clara Principles**, reflecting growing industry adoption of content moderation transparency standards.
