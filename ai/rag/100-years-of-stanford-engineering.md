# 100 Years of Stanford Engineering

## Summary

- **Frederick Terman's Leadership (1920s-1940s):** Established Stanford's engineering program as a hub for innovation, fostering industry collaboration and launching the "Silicon Valley" model by encouraging faculty and students to found startups (e.g., Hewlett-Packard).

- **Expansion of Research Infrastructure (1950s-1960s):** Constructed new facilities (e.g., Stanford Electronics Laboratories) and expanded interdisciplinary research, including early work in computer science (e.g., SAIL) and space technology (e.g., satellite communications).

- **Seminal Contributions to Computing and Space Science (1960s-1970s):** Pioneered integrated circuit design (e.g., Fairchild Semiconductor spinoffs) and computational advancements (e.g., SAIL’s AI research), while also contributing to NASA missions and satellite-based scientific research.
