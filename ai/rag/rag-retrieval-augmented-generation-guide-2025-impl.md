# RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) Guide 2025: Implementation, Vector Databases & Best Practices

## Summary

- **RAG Architecture**: Combines document processing (chunking, metadata extraction), embedding generation (e.g., OpenAI `text-embedding-ada-002`, Sentence Transformers), and vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS) for scalable, low-latency retrieval (50ms–10ms p95).

- **Advanced Techniques**: Implements hierarchical retrieval, query expansion (20–30% recall improvement), hybrid search (vector + BM25), and re-ranking (15–25% precision gain) to optimize context relevance and reduce hallucinations.

- **Production Metrics**: Achieves 90–95% accuracy, <2s end-to-end latency, and cost savings of 10–50x vs. fine-tuning ($0.02–0.10/query at scale), validated in case studies (e.g., legal Q&A at 92% accuracy, 78% automated support resolution).
