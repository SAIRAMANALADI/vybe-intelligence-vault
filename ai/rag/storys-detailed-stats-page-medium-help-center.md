# Story’s detailed stats page – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Performance Metrics**: Lifetime stats include total presentations (Medium-suggested exposures), views (story landings), reads (30+ second engagements), and derived ratios (e.g., Feed clickthrough rate, Read ratio); paywalled stories additionally display earnings, follower/unfollower counts, and subscriber changes.

- **Monthly Engagement Analysis**: Monthly breakdown tracks earnings, views, reads, and engagement metrics (claps, highlights, responses, new followers), with yellow-highlighted metrics indicating potential earnings contributions; Member read ratio (member reads ÷ member views) adjusts earnings to mitigate clickbait.

- **Traffic & Audience Insights**: Traffic sources section categorizes views by Medium’s internal distribution vs. external origins, while paywalled stories show Friend link access; Audience interests chart (if populated) reveals topic affinities of logged-in readers based on their profiles and reading history.
