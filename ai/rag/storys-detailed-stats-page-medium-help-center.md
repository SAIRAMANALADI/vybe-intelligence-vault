# Story’s detailed stats page – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Lifetime Metrics**: Tracks total presentations (suggested views), views (story landings), and reads (30+ second engagements) with hourly updates; includes earnings (daily), follower/subscription changes, and Feed clickthrough rate (if data sufficient).

- **Monthly Engagement**: Displays paywall earnings, views, reads, and interaction metrics (claps, highlights, responses, new followers) with yellow highlights indicating earnings contributions; Member read ratio (member reads ÷ member views) adjusts earnings to penalize clickbait.

- **Traffic & Audience Analysis**: Provides hourly-updated breakdown of traffic sources (Medium distribution vs. external) and paywall-specific Friend link views; Audience interests chart (daily updates) shows topic affinities of logged-in readers based on their profiles and reading history.
