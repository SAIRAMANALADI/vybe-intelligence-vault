# Story’s detailed stats page – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Performance Metrics**: Lifetime stats include total presentations (Medium-suggested exposures), views (story landings), reads (30+ second engagements), and derived ratios (e.g., Feed clickthrough, Read ratio). Paywalled stories also display earnings, follower/subscription changes, and hourly updates (except earnings, updated daily).

- **Monthly Engagement Analysis**: Monthly metrics (selectable via UI) include earnings, views, reads, and engagement (claps, highlights, responses, new followers). Yellow-highlighted metrics indicate potential earnings contributions; Member read ratio (member reads ÷ member views) adjusts earnings to penalize clickbait.

- **Traffic & Audience Insights**: Traffic sources break down views by Medium distribution vs. external referrals (hourly updates). Audience interests chart (daily updates) maps logged-in readers' topic affinities based on account data, but excludes non-account holders or insufficient-data cases. Paywalled stories also track Friend link views hourly.
