# Story’s detailed stats page – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Lifetime Metrics**: Tracks hourly-updated performance including total presentations, views, reads (with 30s threshold), Feed clickthrough rate, and read ratio; paywalled stories display earnings, follower/subscription changes, and hourly updates (except earnings, updated daily).

- **Monthly Engagement**: Daily-updated breakdown of earnings, views, reads, and engagement metrics (claps, highlights, responses, new followers); includes Member read ratio (member reads ÷ member views) to adjust earnings and penalize clickbait.

- **Traffic & Audience Analysis**: Hourly-updated traffic sources (Medium distribution vs. top external sources); paywalled stories show Friend link views; Audience interests chart (daily-updated) displays logged-in readers' topic affinities based on account interests and reading history.
