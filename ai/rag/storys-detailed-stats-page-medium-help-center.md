# Story’s detailed stats page – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Lifetime Metrics**: Tracks presentations (Medium-suggested views), views (story landings), and reads (≥30s engagement) with hourly updates (except earnings, updated daily). Includes paywall-specific data like earnings, follower/subscription changes, and read ratio.

- **Monthly Analytics**: Provides paywall earnings, views, reads, and engagement (claps, highlights, responses, new followers) per selected month, updated daily. Highlights yellow-marked metrics contributing to earnings and includes member read ratio to penalize clickbait.

- **Traffic & Audience Insights**: Breaks down traffic sources (Medium distribution vs. external) hourly; paywalled stories show Friend link views. Audience interests chart (daily updates) reflects logged-in readers’ topic affinities based on account data and reading history.
