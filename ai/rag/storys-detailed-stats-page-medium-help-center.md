# Story’s detailed stats page – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Lifetime Metrics**: Track hourly-updated reach funnel (presentations, views, reads, feed clickthrough rate, read ratio), impact metrics (earnings for paywalled stories, follower/subscription changes), and hourly (daily for earnings) updates.

- **Monthly Engagement**: Daily-updated metrics include earnings, views, reads, and engagement (clappers, highlighters, responders, followers), with yellow highlights indicating earnings contributions; member read ratio (member reads ÷ member views) adjusts earnings to deter clickbait.

- **Traffic & Audience Analysis**: Hourly-updated traffic sources breakdown (Medium’s distribution vs. top external sources); paywalled stories show friend link views; audience interests chart (daily-updated) displays logged-in readers' topic affinities based on account data and reading history.
