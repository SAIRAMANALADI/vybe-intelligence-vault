# Web Privacy Policy | Enterprise Technology

## Summary

- **Scope & Compliance**: Policy applies to all university domains and data processing activities, aiming for GDPR compliance while allowing local policies (pre-approved by Data Protection Officer & CIO) for specific units. Third-party links are excluded from liability.

- **Data Collection & Processing**: Personal data collected via applications, cookies (UT EID authentication, analytics), and logs (IP, UIN, timestamps) is used for operational purposes, research (with anonymization/minimization), or public interest (e.g., fraud prevention). Raw logs are restricted to site custodians.

- **Security & Breach Response**: University employs audited security measures (e.g., SSL, encryption) to protect data, allows users to update records via UT Direct, and mandates immediate breach notification to the CISO, followed by remediation and potential legal disclosures under Texas Public Information Act or FERPA exceptions.
