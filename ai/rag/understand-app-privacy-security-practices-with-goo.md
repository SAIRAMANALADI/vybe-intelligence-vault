# Understand app privacy & security practices with Google Play's Data safety section - Computer - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Sharing Disclosure**: Developers must declare how their app collects, shares, and handles user data via Google Play’s *Data safety* section, covering types of data (e.g., location, personal info, financial data) and purposes (e.g., account management, analytics). Disclosures apply to all app versions distributed on Google Play, though practices may vary by region, age, or user behavior.

- **Exemptions for Data Collection/Sharing**: Data accessed/processed *ephemerally*, end-to-end encrypted, or retained solely on-device need not be disclosed as "collected." Similarly, data shared with third parties under explicit user consent, service providers, or anonymized data transfers may be exempt from "shared" declarations.

- **Security & Deletion Practices**: Developers can disclose encryption in transit, third-party security audits (e.g., MASA), or UPI payment validation (India). For account-enabled apps, deletion pathways must include in-app and web-based options; non-account apps may offer selective data deletion via privacy policies or direct developer contact.
