# Concern Reporting at Cornell | University Compliance

## Summary

- **Reporting Mechanisms**: Cornell provides multiple confidential/anonymous reporting channels via the [Ethics and Compliance Hotline](https://secure.ethicspoint.com/domain/media/en/gui/6357/index.html) (EthicsPoint/NAVEX) for ethics, compliance, or safety concerns, including a **Silent Witness Program** for non-emergency crime tips.
- **Specialized Reporting Paths**: Concerns are routed to dedicated offices (e.g., [ORIA](https://researchservices.cornell.edu/offices/ORIA) for research misconduct, [EHS](https://ehs.cornell.edu/) for safety/environmental issues, [OCR](https://officeofcivilrights.cornell.edu/) for discrimination/harassment) or external agencies (e.g., OSHA, NIH OIG) based on topic.
- **Whistleblower Protections**: Cornell enforces **Policy 4.17** prohibiting retaliation for good-faith reports, mandating cooperation in investigations and disciplinary action for violations, aligned with ethical standards outlined in the [Cornell Ethics and Compliance Hotline](https://secure.ethicspoint.com/domain/media/en/gui/6357/index.html) statement.
