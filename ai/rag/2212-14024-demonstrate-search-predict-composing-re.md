# [2212.14024] Demonstrate-Search-Predict: Composing retrieval and language models for knowledge-intensive NLP

## Summary

- **DSP Framework**: Introduces a **Demonstrate-Search-Predict (DSP)** framework that composes frozen language models (LM) and retrieval models (RM) via natural language pipelines, decomposing knowledge-intensive tasks into modular transformations for improved reliability.

- **Performance Gains**: Achieves **37-120%**, **8-39%**, and **80-290% relative improvements** over vanilla LM (GPT-3.5), standard retrieve-then-read pipelines, and a self-ask pipeline, respectively, across open-domain, multi-hop, and conversational QA tasks.

- **Implementation**: Released as an open-source tool ([GitHub](https://github.com/stanfordnlp/dsp)), enabling pipeline-aware demonstrations, passage retrieval, and grounded prediction generation through structured natural language programs.
