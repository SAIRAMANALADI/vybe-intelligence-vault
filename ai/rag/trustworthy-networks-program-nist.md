# Trustworthy Networks Program | NIST

## Summary

- **Program Objective**: NIST's Trustworthy Networks Program focuses on advancing security, privacy, resilience, and performance of networked systems through research, standardization, and industry collaboration, targeting systemic vulnerabilities in critical network infrastructures and emerging technologies.

- **Key National Priorities**: Aligns with the *National Cybersecurity Strategy*, *National Strategy to Secure 5G*, *Federal Cybersecurity R&D Strategic Plan*, and *National Standards Strategy for Critical and Emerging Technologies* to mitigate risks like BGP vulnerabilities, unencrypted DNS, and slow IPv6 adoption while promoting secure 5G deployment and trustworthy ecosystems.

- **Technical Gaps Addressed**: Addresses the lack of foundational science for predicting network behavior under stress (per *National Research Council's Network Science report*) and develops measurement science to establish trust metrics, improve standards quality, and enhance resilience against cyber threats and transnational digital repression.
