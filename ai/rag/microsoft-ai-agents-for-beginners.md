# microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners

## Summary

- **Curriculum Structure**: 18 structured lessons in Jupyter Notebooks covering foundational to advanced topics in AI agent development, including agentic AI, RAG, and multi-agent frameworks like AutoGen and Microsoft Foundry.

- **Technical Stack**: Integrates Microsoft’s Semantic Kernel, Foundry (local deployment), and agentic frameworks, emphasizing hands-on implementation of agentic workflows, tool use, and orchestration.

- **Learning Outcomes**: Enables beginners to build, deploy, and evaluate AI agents with practical exercises on agentic RAG, multi-agent collaboration, and integration with generative AI models.
