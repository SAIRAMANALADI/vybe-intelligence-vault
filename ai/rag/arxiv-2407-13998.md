# [2407.13998] RAG-QA Arena: Evaluating Domain Robustness for Long-form Retrieval Augmented Question Answering

## Summary

- **LFRQA Dataset**: Introduces a new benchmark dataset (Long-form RobustQA) with 26K human-written long-form answers synthesized from multiple documents across seven domains, addressing limitations of prior short extractive answer datasets for evaluating RAG-QA systems.

- **RAG-QA Arena Evaluation Framework**: Proposes an LLM-based evaluation framework (RAG-QA Arena) that directly compares model-generated long-form answers against LFRQA references, demonstrating high correlation with human judgments and revealing that only 41.3% of top LLM answers are preferred over LFRQA references.

- **Domain Robustness Insights**: Highlights the challenge of cross-domain generalization in RAG-QA, with experiments showing significant performance gaps when models are tested on unseen domains, underscoring the need for more robust evaluation methodologies.
