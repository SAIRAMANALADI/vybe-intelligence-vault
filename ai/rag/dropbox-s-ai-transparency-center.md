# Dropbox's AI Transparency Center

## Summary

- **Model Transparency**: Dropbox Dash utilizes LLMs including OpenAI's GPT OSS 20B, GPT-4.1 mini, GPT-5, and Anthropic's Claude 3-5 Sonnet/haiku, with third-party providers documented alongside infrastructure and data-flow diagrams.

- **AI Controls & Frameworks**: Implements 90-day data retention, prohibits third-party model training on customer data, and aligns with NIST AI RMF, OWASP LLM Top 10, and OWASP LLM AI Checklist for security and compliance.

- **Model Red Teaming**: Covers adversarial testing for jailbreaks, cybersecurity threats, harmful content, data leakage, model manipulation, and responsible AI adherence.
