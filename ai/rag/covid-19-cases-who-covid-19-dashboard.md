# COVID-19 cases | WHO COVID-19 dashboard

## Summary

- **Data Sources & Reporting Standards**: WHO aggregates COVID-19 case/death data from Member States via IHR (2005) communications (pre-22 Mar 2020) and region-specific dashboards thereafter; confirmed cases require NAAT positivity or clinical/epidemiological criteria with antigen-RDT confirmation (updated 22 Jul 2023).

- **Temporal & Methodological Variability**: Data reflects reporting dates (not symptom onset), with weekly aggregation since 25 Aug 2023 due to inconsistent reporting frequencies; retrospective corrections may cause spikes/negative values, validated by WHO.

- **Geographical & Demographic Scope**: Dashboard covers 234 countries/territories grouped by WHO regions and World Bank income groups, using UN/Eurostat population projections; rates <0.001 per 100k may round to 0.
