# Home - Mojaloop

## Summary

- **Mojaloop** is an open-source software framework designed to build, maintain, and modify **Inclusive Instant Payment Systems (IIPS)** at reduced costs, enabling affordable digital financial services via mobile phones.

- **Target Audience**: Central banks, hub operators, financial institutions, development banks, and donors leverage Mojaloop to enable equitable financial inclusion, reduce time-to-market for instant payment solutions, and integrate citizens into the digital economy safely.

- **Leadership Update**: Jean Bosco Iyacu appointed as **CEO of Mojaloop Foundation**, focusing on organizational priorities, opportunities, and future strategic direction.
