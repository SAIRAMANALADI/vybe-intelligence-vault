# [2603.25551] Voxtral TTS

## Summary

- **Voxtral TTS**: A novel **high-fidelity, multilingual text-to-speech (TTS) system** leveraging **diffusion-transformer architectures** for zero-shot voice synthesis with **state-of-the-art prosody and speaker similarity**.

- **Key innovations**: Introduces **voxtral tokens** (discrete speech representations) enabling **efficient training** and **scalable multilingual support** via a **unified tokenization framework** across 100+ languages.

- **Performance metrics**: Achieves **<2.5% speaker error rate** and **<1.8 MOS (Mean Opinion Score)** for naturalness, outperforming prior SOTA models (e.g., VITS, YourTTS) in **latency (<150ms)** and **computational efficiency** (4x faster inference).
