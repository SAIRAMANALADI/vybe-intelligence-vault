# PeekingDuck v1.3: New GUI with Segmentation and Optimised Models | by AI Singapore | AI Singapore TechBytes | Medium

## Summary

- **Instance Segmentation Integration**: Added `model.mask_rcnn` and `model.yolact_edge` nodes for pixel-level object masking, enabling privacy protection and precise shape detection (e.g., tumor segmentation, 3D sensor fusion in autonomous vehicles).

- **Image Distortion Correction**: Introduced `augment.undistort` node to mitigate radial/tangential distortion from wide-angle cameras (e.g., CCTVs), improving downstream CV model accuracy.

- **Performance Optimization**: TensorRT-accelerated `model.yolox` and `model.movenet` nodes deliver up to **50% FPS improvement** on NVIDIA GPUs (benchmarked on Jetson Xavier NX) while maintaining accuracy.
