# Differences between carbon budget estimates unravelled | Nature Climate Change

## Summary

- **Carbon budget estimates vary** due to methodological differences: CO₂-only budgets (most scientifically robust but least policy-relevant) vs. inclusive greenhouse gas (GHG) budgets, with the latter yielding stricter limits (e.g., 590–1,240 GtCO₂ from 2015 for >66% chance of <2°C warming).

- **Non-CO₂ GHG contributions and scenario design** critically reduce feasible carbon budgets; end-of-century non-CO₂ warming and avoidance-based (vs. exceedance-based) scenario methods lower allowable CO₂ emissions.

- **Current emissions (~40 GtCO₂/yr)** necessitate urgent reductions to remain within 2°C-compatible budgets, with cumulative constraints requiring near-zero net emissions to stabilize climate.
