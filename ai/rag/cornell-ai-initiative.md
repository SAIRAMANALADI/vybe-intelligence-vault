# Cornell AI Initiative

## Summary

- **University-wide AI Integration**: Cornell AI Initiative unifies research, education, and operational AI deployment across classrooms, labs, clinics, and administrative processes to evaluate AI's impact on learning, scholarship, and university functions.

- **Multidisciplinary AI Research**: Combines foundational algorithm development (computational scientists), societal impact analysis (social scientists), and domain-specific AI applications (e.g., materials science, veterinary medicine, healthcare) to advance responsible AI.

- **AI Security & Education Initiatives**: Includes Amazon-backed AI security protocols for agent safety, AI-driven veterinary benchmarks (VETMED Ecosystem), and a $1.25M AI fellows program for operational transformation, alongside AI education programs for students and seed grants for societal impact research.
