# Stanford Courses in Human and Planetary Health | Human and Planetary Health

## Summary

- **Course Focus**: SUSTAIN 123 explores the intersection of food science, sustainability, and product design, emphasizing the development of environmentally conscious, palatable food systems through interdisciplinary engineering and culinary techniques.

- **HPH Action Lab Integration**: Part of the Stanford Human Performance Hallmark (HPH) program, this lab-based course applies high-performance principles to food innovation, leveraging Stanford’s resources in biotechnology, materials science, and behavioral economics to prototype sustainable food solutions.

- **Application & Outcomes**: Open to Stanford affiliates via a competitive application process, the course culminates in the design of novel food products with reduced environmental impact, addressing challenges in scalability, consumer acceptance, and lifecycle assessment.
