# Product-Specific Terms | Atlassian

## Summary

- **Product-Specific Legal Entities & Terms**: Atlassian assigns distinct contracting entities (e.g., Atlassian Pty Ltd, AgileCraft LLC, Loom, Inc.) for different products (Jira, Jira Align, Loom), each with dedicated product-specific terms linked in the table. AI offerings are governed by separate [AI Terms](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/ai-terms).

- **Jira Align SLA & Credits**: Jira Align Cloud enforces a **99.5% uptime SLA**, with service credits (5–15%) issued for failures if reported within 30 days. Credits are applied to future invoices, capped at 100% of the billing period’s fees, and exclude sandbox/Free/Beta instances.

- **Opsgenie Restrictions & SLA**: Opsgenie prohibits use for disaster/health-safety alerts and mandates a **99.9% uptime SLA**. Service credits require reporting within two billing cycles post-incident, with eligibility contingent on fully paid accounts and third-party monitoring as the sole truth source.
