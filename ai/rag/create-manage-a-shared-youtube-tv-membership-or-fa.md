# Create & manage a shared YouTube TV membership, or family group - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Family Group Management**: The family manager (18+, US-based, not in another group) controls YouTube TV membership, sets home location, and invites/removes up to 5 members (13+, same household, Google Account required); shared add-ons are accessible to all members.
- **Data Isolation & Access**: Each member retains independent watch history, DVR, and personal library; shared packages (e.g., premium channels) are viewable by all, but no cross-account sharing of personal content or preferences.
- **Location & Streaming Limits**: Home location is enforced via family manager; members must periodically access YouTube TV locally; up to 3 simultaneous streams allowed per family group.
