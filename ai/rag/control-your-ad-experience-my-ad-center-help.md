# Control your ad experience - My Ad Center Help

## Summary

- **My Ad Center & About this Ad**: Users can provide feedback on ads via Google services (Search, YouTube) or partner sites, including blocking, reporting, or learning ad sponsors; features require the latest browser version and may be limited if personalized ads are disabled or the user is signed out.
- **Ad Customization & Feedback**: Users can block ads (6-month suppression), like ads to refine interests, or customize ad topics/brands (e.g., sensitive topics) via the **Customize Ads** page or in-ad controls, though browsing data may override preferences.
- **Connected TV & Partner Sites**: My Ad Center supports Connected TVs (via QR code/link) and partner sites/apps (via **AdChoices**), enabling ad feedback; blocking is enforced for 6 months across Google services and partner networks.
