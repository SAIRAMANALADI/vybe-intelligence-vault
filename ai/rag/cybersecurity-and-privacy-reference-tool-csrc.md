# Cybersecurity and Privacy Reference Tool | CSRC

## Summary

- **NIST Cybersecurity and Privacy Reference Tool (CPRT)** provides a centralized catalog of cybersecurity and privacy controls, frameworks, and guidelines for AI systems, aligned with NIST’s risk management framework.
- **CPRT integrates control overlays** (e.g., AI-specific security baselines) and supports cross-referencing with NIST SP 800-53, ISO 27001, and other standards for compliance mapping.
- **Accessible via [CPRT Catalog](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/cprt/catalog)**, the tool is maintained by NIST’s Cybersecurity and Privacy Program, with updates tracked through March 2022 and February 2026 revisions.
