# GMD - fair-calibrate v1.4.1: calibration, constraining, and validation of the FaIR simple climate model for reliable fut

## Summary

- **Bayesian calibration framework**: Introduces `fair-calibrate` v1.4.1, a multi-variable Bayesian framework for calibrating the FaIR simple climate model (v2.1.0+) using prior distributions from CMIP6 models or IPCC-assessed ranges, constrained by observed metrics (e.g., GMST, OHC, ECS) via Monte Carlo ensembles.

- **Version-controlled parameter tuning**: Demonstrates how different calibrations (e.g., v1.4.0 for AR6 emissions vs. v1.4.1 for updated 2022 datasets) produce divergent future projections under similar constraints, enabling dynamic recalibration as new emission/climate data emerge.

- **Efficiency vs. reliability trade-off**: Highlights FaIR’s computational efficiency (thousands of model years/sec) for probabilistic ensembles but emphasizes the necessity of constraining parameters to avoid unphysical projections, addressing CMIP6 model biases (e.g., ECS outliers, poor historical hindcasts).
