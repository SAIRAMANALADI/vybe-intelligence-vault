# Intellectual Property | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **Fair Use Criteria**: Copyright infringement analysis hinges on four statutory factors ([17 U.S.C. § 107](http://www4.law.cornell.edu/uscode/17/107.html)): (1) purpose/character of use (transformative > mere copying), (2) nature of copyrighted work (factual > creative), (3) amount/substantiality of portion used (avoid core elements), and (4) market effect (substitution risk invalidates fair use).

- **DMCA Safe Harbor Mechanics**: ISPs gain immunity (§512) by expeditiously removing allegedly infringing content upon compliant takedown notices; counter-notifications (§512(g)) enable restoration within 10 business days unless litigation is filed, with §512(f) liability for misrepresentations.

- **Trademark Nominative Fair Use**: Permissible when (1) necessary to identify the subject, (2) no endorsement implied, and (3) no consumer confusion created; applies to domain names (e.g., *walmartsucks.com*) and critical commentary, with commercial contexts requiring stricter scrutiny.
