# Google Families | Empowering kids to safely connect, play, and learn online

## Summary

- **SafeSearch & Privacy Settings**: Google provides [SafeSearch](https://www.google.com/preferences) to filter explicit content and default [privacy/safety settings](https://blog.google/technology/families/how-were-helping-kids-and-families-safely-learn-grow-and-play-online/) to protect kids/teens online.

- **Parental Controls & Supervised Accounts**: [Google Family Link](https://families.google/familylink/) enables tech time management, while supervised YouTube accounts and [Be Internet Awesome](https://beinternetawesome.withgoogle.com/en_us/) teach digital citizenship and online safety.

- **Learning & Exploration Tools**: [YouTube Kids](https://www.youtube.com/intl/ALL_us/kids/) offers a child-focused app, and [Teacher Approved Apps](https://blog.google/products/google-play/teacher-approved-apps/) on Google Play curates expert-reviewed educational content.
