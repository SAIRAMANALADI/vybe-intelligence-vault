# How Klarna's AI assistant redefined customer support at scale for 85 million active users

## Summary

- **Context-aware prompt engineering** dynamically tailored responses to specific scenarios, optimizing token usage and latency while ensuring relevance and accuracy in customer interactions.

- **LangSmith-driven observability and evaluations** facilitated rigorous testing, step-by-step debugging, and iterative prompt refinement, achieving an 80% reduction in query resolution time and ~70% automation of repetitive support tasks.
