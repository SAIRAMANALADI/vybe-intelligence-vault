# [2305.18846] Knowledge Graph-Augmented Language Models for Knowledge-Grounded Dialogue Generation

## Summary

- **SURGE Framework**: Proposes a subgraph retrieval-augmented generation (SURGE) method that first retrieves relevant subgraphs from a knowledge graph (KG) and enforces consistency by perturbing word embeddings conditioned on the retrieved subgraph.
- **Contrastive Learning Objective**: Utilizes contrastive learning to ensure generated dialogues maintain high semantic similarity to the retrieved KG subgraphs, improving knowledge-grounded response fidelity.
- **Evaluation**: Demonstrates superior performance on OpendialKG and KOMODIS datasets, validating the framework’s ability to generate contextually accurate and knowledge-faithful dialogues.
