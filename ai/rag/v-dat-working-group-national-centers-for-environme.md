# V-DAT Working Group | National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

## Summary

- **Standardization & Quality Control**: WG V-DAT defines **IAGA2002** as the ASCII data exchange format for geomagnetic observatory data, with updates in 2001, 2003, 2011, and 2015, and establishes quality standards for geomagnetic indices (e.g., Kp, Dst, AE) under IAGA endorsement criteria (2009).

- **Global Data Infrastructure**: Manages **five World Data Centers** (Boulder, Edinburgh, Kyoto, Moscow, Beijing) and coordinates **INTERMAGNET**, a real-time global network of digital magnetic observatories adhering to standardized instrumentation and data exchange protocols.

- **Index & Event Management**: Officially recognizes **six geomagnetic indices** (aa, am, Kp, Dst, AE, PC) and endorses event lists (e.g., SSC, SFE, Q-Days/D-Days) via the **International Service of Geomagnetic Indices (ISGI)**, with archives dating back to the 1840s.
