# Ten Years at the Frontiers of Energy Science | Department of Energy

## Summary

- **EFRC Program Overview**: Established in 2009 by DOE's Office of Basic Energy Sciences, EFRCs are collaborative, multi-institutional research centers focused on high-risk, high-reward fundamental energy science, leveraging advanced characterization, modeling, and manipulation tools across atomic to macroscopic scales.

- **Key Metrics & Impact**: EFRCs have produced **11,600+ peer-reviewed publications**, **650+ invention disclosures**, and **1,000+ patent applications**, involving **1,600+ senior investigators** and **5,400+ students** across **170 institutions**, with direct industry benefits to **100+ companies**.

- **Scientific & Workforce Contributions**: EFRCs span diverse energy domains (e.g., solar, batteries, biofuels, nuclear) and have trained next-generation scientists, with 10 awardees highlighted in the *Ten at Ten* contest for transformative research in energy technologies and education.
