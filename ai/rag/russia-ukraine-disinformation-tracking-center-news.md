# Russia-Ukraine Disinformation Tracking Center - NewsGuard

## Summary

- **561 tracked disinformation domains** (223 English, 62 French, 55 German, 44 Italian, 177 others) identified by NewsGuard, including both official Russian state media (RT, TASS, Sputnik) and lesser-known pro-Russia sites, many with opaque funding or potential Kremlin ties.

- **Core disinformation narratives** include: (1) denial of Russian grain theft/blockades (contradicted by BBC/WSJ investigations and Maxar satellite imagery), (2) Bucha massacre fabrication (debunked via NYT satellite analysis and HRW testimonies), and (3) "Ghost of Kyiv" myth (exposed as DCS game footage miscaptioned as real combat).

- **Multi-platform amplification strategy**: Russian disinformation leverages YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Telegram, and TikTok, with NewsGuard’s data licensed to U.S. State Department/Cyber Command since 2018 to counter Kremlin propaganda ecosystems.
