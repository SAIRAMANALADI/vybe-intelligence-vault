# [2406.05761] The BiGGen Bench: A Principled Benchmark for Fine-grained Evaluation of Language Models with Language Model

## Summary

- **BiGGen Bench**: Introduces a fine-grained benchmark for evaluating nine distinct language model (LM) capabilities across 77 diverse tasks, addressing limitations of abstract evaluation criteria (e.g., helpfulness/harmlessness) with instance-specific metrics.

- **Evaluation Framework**: Assesses 103 frontier LMs using five evaluator LMs, leveraging automated yet nuanced evaluation aligned with human judgment through task-specific criteria.

- **Open-Source Resources**: Provides publicly available code, data, and evaluation results at [GitHub](https://github.com/prometheus-eval/prometheus-eval/tree/main/BiGGen-Bench), enabling reproducibility and further research.
