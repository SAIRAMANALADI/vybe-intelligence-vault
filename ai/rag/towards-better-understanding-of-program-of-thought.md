# Towards Better Understanding of Program-of-Thought Reasoning in Cross-Lingual and Multilingual Environments - ACL Anthol

## Summary

- **PoT vs. CoT**: Program-of-Thought (PoT) prompting separates reasoning from execution, outperforming Chain-of-Thought (CoT) in multilingual settings by mitigating the entanglement of reasoning and execution in non-English languages.

- **Fine-tuning Impact**: PoT fine-tuning significantly improves multilingual reasoning, surpassing CoT fine-tuned models, and enhances question-reasoning alignment in cross-lingual environments.

- **Reasoning-Answer Correlation**: A strong correlation exists between reasoning quality (measured via code quality) and answer accuracy, suggesting code quality as a heuristic for test-time performance improvement.
