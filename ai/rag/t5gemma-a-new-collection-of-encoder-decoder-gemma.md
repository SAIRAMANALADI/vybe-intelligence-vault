# T5Gemma: A new collection of encoder-decoder Gemma models - Google Developers Blog

## Summary

- **T5Gemma Architecture**: Introduces encoder-decoder LLMs derived from pretrained decoder-only models (e.g., Gemma 2) via adaptation, supporting both PrefixLM and UL2 training objectives for generative performance or representation quality.

- **Performance & Efficiency**: Demonstrates superior quality-efficiency trade-offs across benchmarks (e.g., SuperGLUE, GSM8K), with adapted models achieving higher accuracy at comparable or lower latency than decoder-only counterparts (e.g., T5Gemma 9B-2B matches Gemma 2 2B latency while outperforming it).

- **Model Releases**: Provides open-source checkpoints (T5-sized Small/Base/Large/XL, Gemma 2-based 2B/9B, and unbalanced 9B-2B) in pretrained and instruction-tuned variants, available on Hugging Face, Kaggle, and Vertex AI for research and deployment.
