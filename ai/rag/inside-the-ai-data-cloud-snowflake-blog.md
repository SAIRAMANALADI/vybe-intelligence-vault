# Inside the AI Data Cloud | Snowflake Blog

## Summary

- **Integration of BioNeMo Agent Toolkit**: Snowflake and NVIDIA have partnered to integrate the BioNeMo Agent Toolkit into Snowflake’s platform, enabling agentic AI workflows tailored for life sciences applications, such as drug discovery and genomic analysis.

- **GPU-Accelerated Compute**: The collaboration leverages NVIDIA’s GPU-accelerated computing (e.g., NVIDIA GPUs and CUDA cores) to accelerate large-scale AI model training and inference, optimizing performance for complex biological datasets.

- **Unified Data and AI Platform**: Snowflake’s cloud-native data platform provides a unified environment for storing, processing, and analyzing life sciences data, while NVIDIA’s AI frameworks (e.g., BioNeMo) enable seamless deployment of domain-specific AI agents.
