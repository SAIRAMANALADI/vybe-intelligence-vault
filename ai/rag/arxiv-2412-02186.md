# [2412.02186] VideoICL: Confidence-based Iterative In-context Learning for Out-of-Distribution Video Understanding

## Summary

- **Proposed Framework**: Introduces *VideoICL*, a confidence-based iterative in-context learning method for out-of-distribution (OOD) video understanding, leveraging similarity-based example selection and iterative refinement to enhance model performance without fine-tuning.

- **Key Mechanisms**: Implements a two-stage process—(1) selecting and ranking relevant examples via similarity metrics, and (2) iterative inference where low-confidence outputs trigger re-selection of examples until high-confidence responses are achieved.

- **Performance & Impact**: Demonstrates significant gains on OOD benchmarks, particularly in domain-specific scenarios, by extending effective context length while maintaining computational efficiency.
