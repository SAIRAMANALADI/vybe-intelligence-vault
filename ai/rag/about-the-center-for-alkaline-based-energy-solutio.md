# About - The Center for Alkaline-based Energy Solutions

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: CABES investigates catalytic mechanisms at high pH to advance alkaline fuel cells, electrolyzers, and metal batteries, integrating atomic-scale chemistry, physics, and materials science under operational conditions.

- **Collaborative Structure**: Comprises university and national lab researchers (professors, postdocs, grad students) with specialized expertise to model electrochemical energy conversion from atoms to electrodes.

- **DOE EFRC Initiative**: Funded by the U.S. DOE Office of Science as an Energy Frontier Research Center (EFRC), aiming to enable low-cost, durable energy systems by optimizing direct energy conversion technologies like fuel cells.
