# Welcome to Fire Prevention Services | Fire Prevention Services

## Summary

- **Compliance & Risk Mitigation**: University of Texas at Austin Fire Prevention Services (FPS) enforces local/state fire safety regulations across campus buildings, conducts inspections, and collaborates with stakeholders to reduce fire risks and potential hazards.

- **Training & Preparedness**: FPS offers evacuation drills, portable fire extinguisher training, and resources for fire safety awareness, including guidance on alcohol-based hand sanitizers (AHBS) and home fire prevention.

- **Controlled Burns & Historical Data**: The Half-Pint Prairie project demonstrates prescribed fire techniques for ecological restoration, while FPS maintains records of historic campus fires and provides fire safety links for off-campus housing.
