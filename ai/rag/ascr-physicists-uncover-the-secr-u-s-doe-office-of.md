# ASCR Physicists Uncover the Secr... | U.S. DOE Office of Science(SC)

## Summary

- **Strong electron-phonon coupling** in copper-based superconductors leads to abrupt changes in material behavior, as revealed by Summit supercomputer simulations of millions of particle states.

- **Self-energy framework** developed to model electron interactions in crowded, multi-particle environments, enabling analysis of high-temperature copper-based superconductors.

- **Computational study** of 1.26 million particle states on Summit identified kinks in photoemission spectra, linking electron-phonon interactions to superconducting mechanisms in cuprates.
