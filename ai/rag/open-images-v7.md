# Open Images V7

## Summary

- **Dataset Scale**: Open Images V7 includes 15.8M bounding boxes across 600 classes, 2.8M instance segmentations (350 classes), 3.3M relationship annotations (1,466 relationships), 675K localized narratives, 66.4M point-level annotations (5,827 classes), and 61.4M image-level labels (20,638 classes).
- **Extensions**: Crowdsourced extension adds 478K images with 6K+ classes, expanding diversity and annotation granularity.
- **Accessibility**: Data available via [Visualizer](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/visualizer/index.html), [Facts/Figures](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/factsfigures_v7.html), and [Download](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/download_v7.html) links, with extended/modified subsets accessible via [Extended](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/extended.html) and [Challenge](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/challenge_overview.html) pages.
