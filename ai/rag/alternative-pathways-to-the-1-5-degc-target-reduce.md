# Alternative pathways to the 1.5 °C target reduce the need for negative emission technologies | Nature Climate Change

## Summary

- **Alternative mitigation pathways** (e.g., lifestyle changes, non-CO₂ GHG reductions, rapid renewable electrification) can significantly reduce reliance on negative emission technologies (NETs) like BECCS and afforestation, though they do not fully eliminate the need for CDR.

- **Integrated assessment modeling** demonstrates that diversifying transition strategies lowers land-use competition and storage dependency challenges associated with conventional NETs, while still meeting the 1.5°C Paris Agreement target.

- **Co-benefits** of alternative pathways include improved sustainability outcomes (e.g., biodiversity, food security) compared to high-NET scenarios, though residual CDR requirements persist due to residual emissions in hard-to-decarbonize sectors.
