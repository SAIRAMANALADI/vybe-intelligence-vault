# zju3dv/InfiniSplat

## Summary

- **Methodology**: Proposes *InfiniSplat*, a novel framework leveraging **implicit Gaussian decoding** for monocular view synthesis under large-baseline camera settings, addressing challenges in novel view synthesis with extreme viewpoint variations.

- **Technical Core**: Utilizes **3D Gaussian Splatting** combined with **implicit neural representations** to decode scene geometry and appearance, enabling high-fidelity synthesis across wide baselines without explicit 3D reconstruction.

- **Performance**: Achieves state-of-the-art results on large-baseline monocular view synthesis benchmarks, demonstrating superior rendering quality and geometric consistency compared to prior implicit or explicit methods.
