# Learn More About Google Workspace with Gemini - Google for Education

## Summary

- **Gemini Integration**: Google Workspace with Gemini integrates AI-powered features across Workspace apps (Docs, Gmail, Meet, Slides, etc.), enabling research, content creation, email summarization, meeting notes, and presentation enhancements while maintaining enterprise-grade privacy and security.

- **AI Agents via Workspace Studio**: Intelligent agents automate workflows (e.g., meeting prep, email triage, translation, attachment management) within Google Workspace, reducing manual tasks and improving productivity for educators and administrators.

- **Enterprise Security & Pricing**: Data remains private by design (no model training/review), covered under Google Workspace for Education ToS, with **Google AI Pro for Education** add-on priced at **$15/user/month (annual)** or **$24/user/month (monthly)** for premium AI features.
