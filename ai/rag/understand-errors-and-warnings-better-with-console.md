# Understand errors and warnings better with console insights | Chrome DevTools | Chrome for Developers

## Summary

- **Console Insights Feature**: Chrome DevTools integrates AI-powered explanations for console errors/warnings via Google's LLMs, requiring users to enable the feature in **Settings > AI Innovations** and meet criteria (e.g., Chrome version, signed-in Google Account, US locale, English (US) language setting).

- **Data Handling & Privacy**: Input data (console messages, stack traces, source code, network headers) is collected and processed by Google to improve services; human reviewers may access data, which is retained for up to 18 months unless governed by enterprise policies.

- **Limitations & Risks**: Explanations may be inaccurate or misleading due to LLM limitations; prompt injection risks exist, and users must verify outputs. Feedback submission is encouraged to enhance feature quality.
