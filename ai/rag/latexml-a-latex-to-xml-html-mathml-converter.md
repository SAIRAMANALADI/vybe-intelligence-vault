# LaTeXML A LaTeX to XML/HTML/MathML Converter

## Summary

- **Purpose & Architecture**: LaTeXML is a Perl-based converter that emulates TeX to transform LaTeX documents into an intermediate XML format, which is then further processed into HTML (with MathML/SVG), JATS, ePub, or other formats, prioritizing semantic preservation of mathematical content.

- **Key Features**: Supports conversion of LaTeX tables, mathematical expressions (to both presentation and content MathML), and complex document structures, with demonstrated use in the Digital Library of Mathematical Functions (DLMF).

- **Limitations & Outputs**: Emulation of TeX is challenging due to LaTeX package diversity and evolving web standards, leading to gaps in fidelity; outputs include HTML, PDF, and experimental formats like preprints in HTML.
