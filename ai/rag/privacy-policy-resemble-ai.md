# Privacy Policy | Resemble AI

## Summary

- **Multimodal AI Security Suite**: Resemble AI offers a comprehensive generative AI security framework including **Resemble Identity** (biometric verification), **Resemble Watermarker** (media authentication), and **Resemble Detect** (real-time deepfake detection across audio/video).

- **Real-Time Deepfake Monitoring**: **Resemble Meetings** integrates a detection bot into video calls to identify AI-generated impersonations in real-time, while the **Deepfake Detector for Chrome** extension enables browser-based scanning of media for AI artifacts.

- **Privacy & Compliance Framework**: Collects and processes **Personal Data** (e.g., biometrics, device/IP data) under strict **GDPR/HIPAA-aligned policies**, with disclosures limited to **service providers (e.g., Stripe for payments)** and **law enforcement** when legally required.
