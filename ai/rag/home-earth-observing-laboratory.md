# Home | Earth Observing Laboratory

## Summary

- **SCARGO Project**: NSF NCAR-led initiative (with NOAA, CIRES, CWorthy, and Earth Sciences New Zealand) deploying atmospheric instruments on Antarctic planes to quantify highly uncertain CO₂ air-sea fluxes in the Southern Ocean, addressing critical gaps in global carbon cycle modeling.

- **AVAPS™ Dropsonde Breakthrough**: NOAA Hurricane Hunter aircraft equipped with NSF NCAR’s AVAPS™ dropsonde system recorded a record 252-mph wind gust in Hurricane Melissa (Oct 2025), leveraging Vaisala dropsondes to validate extreme wind dynamics and enhance hurricane intensity forecasting.

- **EOL Facilities & Data Services**: NSF NCAR’s Earth Observing Laboratory (EOL) provides NSF-funded research teams with requestable Lower Atmosphere Observing Facilities (LAOF)—including research aircraft, airborne instrumentation, and ground-based systems—alongside end-to-end data management, archival, and field program support services for atmospheric field campaigns.
