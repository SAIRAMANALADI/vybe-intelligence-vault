# Can E-learning Help Regulators Better Adapt to a Changing Energy Landscape? | Stanford Impact Labs

## Summary

- **E-learning modules** developed by Stanford’s PESD simulate real-world energy market failures (e.g., blackouts during heat waves) using animated scenarios, providing regulators with immediate feedback on decision-making in complex, high-renewable grids.

- **8 interactive modules** (since 2023) have been completed ~2,000 times by regulators globally, improving understanding of market mechanics, transmission constraints, and resource adequacy in systems transitioning from coal to wind/solar.

- **Scalability challenges** include adapting modules for broader audiences (e.g., simplifying advanced concepts) and expanding access via partnerships (e.g., WIEB) or direct portals, aiming to standardize technical training across jurisdictions.
