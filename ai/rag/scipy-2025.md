# SciPy 2025

## Summary

- **Event Details**: SciPy 2025 held July 7-13 at Tacoma Convention Center, WA, featuring tutorials (July 7-8), conference (July 9-11), and sprints (July 12-13), with virtual attendance available.
- **Keynote Speakers**: Featured Dr. Yusra AlSayyad (Rubin Observatory), Charles R. Harris (NumPy), Hon. Kathryn D. Huff (DOE), Dr. Malvika Sharan (Open Life Science), and Christopher Lamb (NVIDIA).
- **Tracks & Sponsors**: Highlighted tracks included "Celebrating the 'Sci' in SciPy" and "Machine Learning, Data Science, and Explainable AI"; sponsors ranged from Platinum (Enthought, NVIDIA) to Community Partners (Code Day, Seattle Spark-AI).
