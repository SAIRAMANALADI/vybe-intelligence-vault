# Legal - Hardware Warranties - Apple

## Summary

- Apple's Limited Warranty supplements consumer protection laws but does not supersede them; additional rights may apply based on local regulations.
- Warranty coverage is exclusive to Apple/Beats-branded products; third-party accessories lack Apple warranty, though their own manufacturer warranties may apply.
- Discontinued Apple products require service inquiries via [Apple Support](http://support.apple.com/kb/ht1752); obligor details per region are listed [here](https://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/obligor.html).
