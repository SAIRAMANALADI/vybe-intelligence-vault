# Amazon will invest AU$20 billion in data center infrastructure in Australia

## Summary

- **AU$20B investment (2025-2029)**: Expands AWS data center infrastructure in Australia (Sydney/Melbourne), supporting AI/cloud demand with local data residency and regulatory compliance.
- **170+ MW renewable capacity**: Three new solar farms in Victoria/Queensland (11 total projects) to power AWS operations, generating ~1.4M MWh/year of carbon-free energy (~290K homes).
- **AI skills & innovation programs**: Trained 400K+ Australians since 2017; launches AWS AI Spring Australia, including Generative AI Accelerator and AI Launchpad to boost local AI adoption and workforce development.
