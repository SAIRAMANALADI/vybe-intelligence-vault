# CompSustNet: Home

## Summary

- **NSF-funded interdisciplinary network** (CompSustNet) led by Cornell University, integrating optimization, ML, big data, and citizen science to address sustainability challenges (conservation, poverty, renewable energy) via cross-institutional collaboration.

- **Key projects** include AI-driven ecological modeling (e.g., bird conservation, seagrass disease spread), materials discovery via crystal structure analysis, and smart grid optimization using stochastic methods and probabilistic reasoning.

- **Educational outreach** features courses on AI for social good (USC), ethics of AI (Vanderbilt), and urban analytics (Cornell), alongside tools like PAWS for anti-poaching patrol optimization and microbial fuel cell design via Bayesian optimization.
