# Universal AI is “a pathway to AI fluency that’s accessible and approachable to anyone, anywhere” | MIT News | Massachuse

## Summary

- **Modular AI Education Program**: MIT Open Learning's *Universal AI* is a self-paced, modular online program designed to take learners from novice to fluent in AI, starting with foundational competencies (programming, ML, deep learning, LLMs) and progressing to industry-specific applications (healthcare, sustainability, entrepreneurship).

- **AI-Powered Personalization**: The program integrates an AI assistant (*AskTIM*) for adaptive learning, providing real-time tutoring, concept clarification, and personalized learning pathways to enhance engagement and comprehension.

- **Free Introductory Course**: The first course, *Fundamentals of Programming and Machine Learning*, is available for free globally, with additional industry-specific courses (e.g., *Holistic AI in Medicine*, *AI and Sustainability*) developed by over 30 MIT faculty and experts.
