# [1503.03832] FaceNet: A Unified Embedding for Face Recognition and Clustering

## Summary

- **FaceNet** introduces a deep CNN architecture that directly maps face images to a **128-dimensional Euclidean space**, optimizing embeddings via **triplet loss** for face verification, recognition, and clustering without intermediate bottlenecks.

- **Triplet mining** is performed online using aligned matching/non-matching face patches, enabling **state-of-the-art accuracy** (99.63% on LFW, 95.12% on YouTube Faces) with **30% lower error rates** than prior methods.

- **Harmonic embeddings** and **triplet loss variants** allow interoperability between different embedding models, enabling direct comparison across networks while maintaining **compact 128-byte per-face representations**.
