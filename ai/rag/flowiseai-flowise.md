## Summary

- **Visual AI Agent Builder**: Open-source, low/no-code TypeScript framework enabling drag-and-drop construction of AI agents and workflows with integrations for LLMs (e.g., OpenAI), LangChain, and RAG pipelines.

- **Multi-Agent & Workflow Automation**: Supports agentic AI systems, multi-agent collaboration, and automated workflows with visual orchestration for tasks like chatbots, data processing, and decision-making.
