# Legal - App Store & Privacy- Apple

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Personalization**: Apple collects account, purchase, search, and device usage data (e.g., app launches, installs, deletions) to personalize App Store recommendations, optimize store performance, and target ads. Personalization can be disabled via settings, and app usage data is processed locally on-device.

- **Fraud Prevention & Device Trust Score**: Apple computes a temporary, privacy-preserving *device trust score* (based on approximate call/email activity) to detect fraud during purchases, with scores stored for a fixed duration. Data is anonymized to prevent exposure of real values.

- **Third-Party Sharing & Subscriber IDs**: Apple shares non-personal aggregated data (e.g., subscription metrics) with developers for analytics and accounting. Subscriber IDs link users to purchases for reporting but are reset after 180 days of inactivity; external purchases bypass Apple’s system and are reported without user association.
