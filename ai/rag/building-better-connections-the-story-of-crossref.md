# Building Better Connections: The Story of Crossref's Metadata Development - Crossref

## Summary

- **Schema 5.4 Update**: Introduced citation type labeling (article/preprint/dataset/software), versioning support across all record types, and enhanced funding metadata with ROR IDs and Grant DOI identification.
- **Schema 5.5 & 6.0 Roadmap**: Expands contributor roles via CRediT taxonomy, supports multiple roles per contributor, introduces free-text statements (funding acknowledgements, AI disclosures), and deprecates schemas prior to 5.3.1 by end of 2027.
- **Future Enhancements**: Provenance tracking, lifecycle date handling, RAiD integration for grants, and broader identifier support (ISNI, Wikidata) for contributors and organizations.
