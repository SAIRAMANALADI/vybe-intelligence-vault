# Ad Settings

## Summary

- **Cross-Device Ad Personalization**: Signing into [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/home) allows users to customize personalized ads across all browsers and devices where they are signed in, leveraging Google’s advertising infrastructure for consistency.

- **Scope of Personalization Controls**: Users can toggle personalized ads for Google Search, Maps, and partner sites/apps, with granular opt-out options for YouTube-specific personalization when enabled via YouTube settings.

- **Persistent Opt-Out Mechanism**: Google provides a browser plugin to persistently opt out of personalized ads even after cookie clearance, while also supporting industry-wide opt-out via [AdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/) for broader ad network control.
