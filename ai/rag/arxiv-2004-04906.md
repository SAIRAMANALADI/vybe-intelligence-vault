# [2004.04906] Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering

## Summary

- **Dense Retrieval Framework**: Proposes a dual-encoder architecture for open-domain QA, replacing sparse retrieval (e.g., BM25/TF-IDF) with learned dense embeddings for passages and queries, trained end-to-end.

- **Performance Gains**: Achieves **9–19% absolute improvement** in top-20 passage retrieval accuracy over BM25 across multiple datasets, enabling state-of-the-art end-to-end QA systems.

- **Efficiency & Scalability**: Demonstrates practicality of dense retrieval with minimal training data, avoiding reliance on traditional inverted-index methods while maintaining competitive latency.
