# Sign in on a device that's not yours - Computer - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Guest Mode in Chrome**: Enables temporary sign-in on shared/public devices; browsing history, cookies, and site data are automatically deleted upon closing the Guest window, while activity controls (e.g., Google Wallet) still apply to the signed-in account.
- **Private Browsing in Other Browsers**: Uses browser-specific private/incognito modes (e.g., Safari, Firefox, Edge) to prevent local storage of browsing history/cookies; requires manual sign-out post-use to fully remove account traces.
- **Cache/Cookie Clearing for Non-Private Browsing**: If private mode is unavailable, manually clear cache/cookies and delete browsing history before/after sign-in to mitigate account exposure on shared devices.
