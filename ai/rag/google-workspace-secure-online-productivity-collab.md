# Google Workspace: Secure Online Productivity & Collaboration Tools

## Summary

- **AI-Powered Productivity Suite**: Google Workspace integrates a built-in AI assistant (Gemini) for context-aware task automation, including email reply suggestions, real-time meeting summaries, and action item extraction directly within Gmail and Meet.

- **Enterprise-Grade Cloud Infrastructure**: Offers up to 5TB per user of secure, compliance-ready cloud storage (Drive) with AI-driven data classification, eSignature workflows (Docs), and real-time collaboration across all tools (Docs, Sheets, Slides) with 100+ participant video conferencing (Meet).

- **Tiered Subscription Model**: Provides three pricing tiers (Starter: $7/user/month, Standard: $14/user/month, Premium: custom) with storage scaling (30GB → 5TB), advanced AI features (Gemini in Gmail/Chat), and domain-customized email (you@yourcompany.com) across all plans.
