# Healthcare Has More Data Than Ever—Why Are Patients Still Searching For Answers?

## Summary

- **Healthcare data abundance vs. decision clarity**: Despite $5T+ annual U.S. healthcare spending and digitized records, patients still lack confidence in basic decisions (provider selection, cost, coverage) due to misalignment between operational data and consumer-facing decision support.

- **Decision-centric transformation gap**: Healthcare systems optimize workflows and analytics for organizational efficiency, but fail to address consumer uncertainty during high-stakes decisions, requiring a shift from information delivery to actionable guidance.

- **Trust-driven AI integration**: Effective healthcare navigation requires combining AI-driven insights (cost transparency, personalized recommendations) with human guidance to reduce uncertainty, measured by confidence outcomes rather than traditional adoption metrics.
