# Jito Foundation | Solana Liquid Stake Pool: Non-Custodial Liquid Staking

## Summary

- **MEV-Optimized Staking**: Jito Network leverages MEV-optimized infrastructure on Solana, enabling validators and stakers to capture additional MEV rewards while enhancing transaction execution efficiency and network fairness.

- **Jito (Re)staking**: Introduces a novel economic security model allowing simultaneous staking of Solana assets across multiple networks, distributing MEV tips (6% of total) via the TipRouter NCN to Jito DAO (5.7%), JitoSOL stakers (0.15%), and JTO stakers (0.15%).

- **StakeNet Automation**: Combines Validator History and Steward Programs to autonomously manage stake delegation, using tamper-resistant performance ledgers and algorithmic validator evaluations to maximize network security and efficiency.
