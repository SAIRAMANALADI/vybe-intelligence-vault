# MedGemma: Our most capable open models for health AI development

## Summary

- **MedGemma Models**: Introduced MedGemma 27B Multimodal (supports complex multimodal/longitudinal EHR interpretation) and MedGemma 4B Multimodal (64.4% MedQA score, 81% radiologist-approved chest X-ray reports), both derived from Gemma 3 with medical fine-tuning while preserving general capabilities.

- **MedSigLIP**: A 400M-parameter lightweight image-text encoder (SigLIP-based) optimized for medical imaging (X-rays, histopathology, dermatology, fundus) with versatile zero-shot classification/retrieval and competitive performance vs. task-specific models.

- **Open-Source Advantages**: Models are open-source (Hugging Face/GitHub) for local/proprietary deployment, enabling privacy-preserving fine-tuning, reproducibility, and cost-efficient scaling (e.g., Vertex AI) while avoiding API instability for clinical applications.
