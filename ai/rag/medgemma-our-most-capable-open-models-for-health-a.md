# MedGemma: Our most capable open models for health AI development

## Summary

- **MedGemma Models**: New open multimodal models (4B, 27B text-only, and 27B multimodal) for healthcare AI, fine-tuned from Gemma 3, supporting medical text/image tasks (e.g., report generation, visual QA) with competitive performance on MedQA (64.4% for 4B, 87.7% for 27B text-only) and chest X-ray report accuracy validated by radiologists.

- **MedSigLIP**: A 400M-parameter lightweight image encoder adapted from SigLIP for medical imaging (e.g., X-rays, histopathology), enabling zero-shot classification, retrieval, and structured outputs with strong performance across modalities while retaining general image versatility.

- **Open-Source Advantages**: Models are fully open-source (Hugging Face/GitHub), deployable on single GPUs or mobile hardware, and designed for privacy-preserving, customizable healthcare applications with frozen parameters for reproducibility, contrasting API-based alternatives.
