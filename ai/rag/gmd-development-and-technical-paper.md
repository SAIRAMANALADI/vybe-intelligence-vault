# GMD - Development and technical paper

## Summary

- **Second-order conservative remapping in spherical coordinates**: Identified a previously unaddressed flaw in the centroid formulation derivation of a pioneer method, which could theoretically impact remapping accuracy but has negligible practical consequences due to compensating formulations in prior studies.

- **PIQB bias correction for tropospheric ozone**: Introduced a parametric interpolation of quantile biases (PIQB) method to correct model biases in tropospheric ozone maxima, demonstrating superior performance in high-resolution simulations while preserving model-resolved features and suitability for future projections.

- **TVD-FFSL hybrid tracer transport scheme**: Developed a computationally efficient tracer transport scheme on spherical centroidal Voronoi tessellations, achieving ~4x speedup over conventional methods while maintaining accuracy, enabling faster high-resolution atmospheric simulations.
