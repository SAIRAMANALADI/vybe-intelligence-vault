# Earthquake Early Warning at the Berkeley Seismo Lab

## Summary

- **Earthquake Early Warning (EEW) System**: Rapid detection of earthquakes via P-wave analysis, real-time hazard assessment, and notification delivery; warning time scales with distance from epicenter and earthquake magnitude, leveraging algorithms like **ElarmS** (core to **ShakeAlert** system) for initial alerts.

- **ShakeAlert Implementation**: Public EEW system deployed across California, Oregon, and Washington, integrating **ElarmS** (UC Berkeley Seismological Lab) and **EPIC** (modified ElarmS for production), enabling automated responses (e.g., BART train slowdowns) and hazard mitigation via MyShake app.

- **Operational Workflow**: Non-damaging P-waves trigger sensor networks, data is processed centrally to estimate shaking intensity, and alerts are disseminated seconds to minutes before damaging S-waves arrive, reducing injuries (e.g., 50% reduction in falls-related harm in past earthquakes).
