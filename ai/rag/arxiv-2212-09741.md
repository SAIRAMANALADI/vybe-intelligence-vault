# [2212.09741] One Embedder, Any Task: Instruction-Finetuned Text Embeddings

## Summary

- **Instruction-Finetuned Embeddings**: Introduces **INSTRUCTOR**, a single text embedder that generates task-specific embeddings by incorporating natural language instructions (e.g., task/domain descriptions) without further training, outperforming specialized models.

- **Multitask Training & Evaluation**: Trained on 330 annotated tasks with contrastive loss, evaluated on 70 diverse unseen tasks (classification, retrieval, STS, etc.), achieving **3.4% average improvement** over prior SOTA with **10x fewer parameters**.

- **Robustness & Generalization**: Demonstrates robustness to instruction variations and mitigates multi-dataset training challenges via instruction finetuning, with model/code/data released at [instructor-embedding.github.io](https://instructor-embedding.github.io).
