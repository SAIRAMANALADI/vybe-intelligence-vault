# RFC 3339 - Date and Time on the Internet: Timestamps

## Summary

- **RFC 3339 Profile**: Defines a strict profile of ISO 8601 for timestamp representation in Internet protocols, requiring full UTC-based timestamps (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`) with optional fractional seconds and numeric offsets (e.g., `±HH:MM`).

- **UTC Enforcement**: Mandates UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) for interoperability, explicitly prohibiting unqualified local time due to daylight saving rule complexities and global inconsistency; offsets must be numeric (e.g., `-04:00`).

- **Year and Format Rules**: Requires 4-digit years (deprecating 2-digit years), prohibits redundant punctuation, and enforces lexicographical sorting compatibility for UTC-based timestamps with identical fractional precision.
