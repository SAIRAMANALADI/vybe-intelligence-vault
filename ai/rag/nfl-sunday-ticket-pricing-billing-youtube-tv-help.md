# NFL Sunday Ticket pricing & billing - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Pricing Structure**:
  - YouTube TV + NFL Sunday Ticket: $82.99/month (base YouTube TV plan) + NFL Sunday Ticket fee (varies by plan, e.g., season pass or month-to-month).
  - YouTube Primetime Channels: NFL Sunday Ticket available as a standalone season pass (no monthly option).

- **Access & Availability**:
  - Includes all out-of-market Sunday afternoon regular season games, most preseason games, postseason games, and NFL RedZone (optional bundle).
  - No single-team or weekly purchase options; only season-long or month-to-month plans.

- **Billing & Cancellation**:
  - Month-to-month: Auto-renews monthly, cancellable anytime with access until next billing cycle (no refunds).
  - Season pass: Non-refundable, no cancellations; auto-renewal can be disabled for future seasons.
