# stanford-oval/storm

## Summary

- **System Overview**: STORM is an LLM-powered knowledge curation system that autonomously researches a given topic and generates a comprehensive, citation-backed report by leveraging agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and deep-research capabilities.

- **Technical Architecture**: The system integrates multi-agent workflows for iterative information retrieval, synthesis, and validation, with a focus on structured report generation (EMNLP 2024/NAACL contributions) and Python-based implementation.

- **Key Features**: Supports dynamic topic exploration, multi-source citation integration, and adaptive research strategies, enabling high-fidelity knowledge synthesis for long-form outputs.
