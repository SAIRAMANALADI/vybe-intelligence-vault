## Summary

- **ICLR 2026 Contribution**: Proposes a novel approach to Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG) that reduces computational overhead while maintaining high retrieval accuracy, addressing limitations of traditional graph-based RAG methods.
