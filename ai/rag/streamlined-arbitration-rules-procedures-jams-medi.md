# Streamlined Arbitration Rules & Procedures | JAMS Mediation, Arbitration, ADR Services

## Summary

- **Scope & Applicability**: JAMS Streamlined Arbitration Rules apply to disputes administered by JAMS where parties agree to use them or where disputed claims do not exceed $250,000 (excluding interest/fees), with JAMS NAC or General Counsel overseeing administration and discretion to assign cases to Resolution Centers.

- **Commencement & Service**: Arbitration commences upon JAMS issuing a Commencement Letter after verifying agreement terms, fee payments, and service of Demand for Arbitration; service/e-filing via JAMS Electronic Filing System is deemed effective upon transmission completion, with deadlines adjusted for weekends/holidays.

- **Consolidation & Administrative Authority**: JAMS may consolidate arbitrations if multiple cases involve overlapping parties or claims, considering factors like case links and progress; JAMS can suspend/terminate proceedings for unpaid fees and may allocate costs to non-paying parties, with administrative suspensions tolling time limits.
