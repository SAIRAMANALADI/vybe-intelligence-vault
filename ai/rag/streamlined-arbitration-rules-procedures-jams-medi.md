# Streamlined Arbitration Rules & Procedures | JAMS Mediation, Arbitration, ADR Services

## Summary

- **Scope & Applicability**: JAMS Streamlined Arbitration Rules (effective June 1, 2021) govern binding arbitrations administered by JAMS where parties agree to use these rules or where disputed claims ≤ $250,000 (excluding interest/fees). JAMS National Arbitration Committee (NAC) or designees administer proceedings, with authority to assign cases to resolution centers.

- **Commencement & Service**: Arbitration commences upon JAMS issuing a Commencement Letter after verifying agreement, payment, and service of Demand for Arbitration. Service may be electronic (via JAMS Electronic Filing System) or physical (U.S. mail/hand-delivery), with time extensions for weekends/holidays and provisions for technical filing errors.

- **Consolidation & Administrative Powers**: JAMS may consolidate arbitrations if multiple cases involve overlapping parties or claims, considering factors like case links and progress. JAMS can suspend/terminate proceedings for unpaid fees, order administrative conferences, and determine hearing locations (subject to arbitrator review) based on convenience and resources.
