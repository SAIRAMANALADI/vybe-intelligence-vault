# Streamlined Arbitration Rules & Procedures | JAMS Mediation, Arbitration, ADR Services

## Summary

- **Scope & Applicability**: Rules govern JAMS-administered arbitrations where parties agree to use them or claims ≤$250K (excl. interest/fees); JAMS NAC/General Counsel oversees administration, with disputes assignable to any Resolution Center.

- **Commencement & Service**: Arbitration deemed commenced upon JAMS issuing Commencement Letter (triggered by agreement, court order, or respondent’s failure to object); service/e-filing governed by strict timelines, with electronic submissions deemed filed upon transmission completion.

- **Consolidation & Administrative Authority**: JAMS may consolidate arbitrations if related disputes arise or multiple filings occur; administrative conferences resolve procedural matters, and non-payment of fees may suspend/terminate proceedings, with costs allocable by the arbitrator.
