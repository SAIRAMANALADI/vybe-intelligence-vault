# Streamlined Arbitration Rules & Procedures | JAMS Mediation, Arbitration, ADR Services

## Summary

- **Scope & Applicability**: Rules govern JAMS-administered arbitrations where parties agree to use them or claims ≤$250K (excl. interest/fees); JAMS NAC/General Counsel oversees administration, with discretion to assign to Resolution Centers.

- **Commencement & Service**: Arbitration deemed commenced upon JAMS issuing Commencement Letter (post-dispute agreement, pre-dispute clause, court order, or respondent’s failure to object); service/e-filing governed by strict timelines, with electronic filing/signature requirements.

- **Consolidation & Administrative Authority**: JAMS may consolidate arbitrations if overlapping parties/cases exist; administrative conferences determine procedural matters, and JAMS can suspend/terminate proceedings for unpaid fees, allocating costs per Rules 19(e)/26(c).
