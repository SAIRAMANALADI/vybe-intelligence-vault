# Mastering Nuxt | The official courses for learning Nuxt

## Summary

- **Course Focus**: 2025 Edition of *Mastering Nuxt Fullstack Unleashed* covers Nuxt 4, AI-powered projects, and full-stack development with hands-on lessons (100+), targeting junior to senior developers with optional prior Nuxt experience.

- **Technical Stack & Features**: Integrates Nuxt UI, MDC for markdown, OpenAI API, unstorage/Supabase (Postgres), Prisma (ORM), Zod (validation), Nitro (backend), and advanced data fetching (`useAsyncData`, `useFetch`, lazy/prefetching) for SSR/SSG/hybrid rendering.

- **Key Learning Outcomes**: Master file-based routing, Nuxt layers, state management (`useState`), authentication, caching, deployment (Nitro), error handling (`error.vue`), and meta management (`useHead`, `definePageMeta`) with TypeScript and Vue 3.
