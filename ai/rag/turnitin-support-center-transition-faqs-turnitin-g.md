# Turnitin Support Center Transition FAQs – Turnitin Guides

## Summary

- **System Migration**: Turnitin is transitioning to a new **Service Management System** and **Help Center** (helpcenter.turnitin.com) for integrity products (e.g., Feedback Studio, Similarity, iThenticate), replacing the old Support Center, which will be decommissioned on **September 20, 2024**.

- **Ticket & Login Handling**: Active tickets will migrate to the new system; closed ticket queries will auto-flow into the new platform. **Existing logins are invalid**—new accounts will be issued, but old logins remain active until the decommission date.

- **Content & Link Updates**: All knowledge articles are being migrated; **old Support Center links must be updated** to the new Help Center. Support emails retain the same sender address but may show minor aesthetic changes and use "ticket numbers" instead of case references.
