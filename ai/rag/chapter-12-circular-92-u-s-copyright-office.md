# Chapter 12 - Circular 92 | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **DMCA Anti-Circumvention Provisions (17 U.S.C. § 1201)**: Prohibits circumvention of technological measures controlling access to copyrighted works (effective 2 years post-enactment) and trafficking in circumvention tools/devices, with exceptions for noninfringing uses determined via triennial Librarian of Congress rulemaking (e.g., archival, educational, or fair use cases).

- **Liability for Circumvention Tools (17 U.S.C. § 1201(a)(2)/(b)(1))**: Criminalizes manufacturing, importing, or trafficking in technologies/products/services primarily designed to circumvent access or usage controls, even if marketed for noninfringing purposes, with penalties including civil remedies (17 U.S.C. § 1203).

- **Exemptions & Limitations (17 U.S.C. § 1201(c)-(f))**: Preserves fair use and other copyright defenses (§ 1201(c)), exempts nonprofit libraries/archives from circumvention for acquisition decisions under strict conditions (§ 1201(d)), and excludes law enforcement/intelligence activities and reverse engineering from liability (§ 1201(f)).
