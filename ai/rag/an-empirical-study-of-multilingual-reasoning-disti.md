# An Empirical Study of Multilingual Reasoning Distillation for Question Answering - ACL Anthology

## Summary

- **Novel Contribution**: Proposes **d-CoT-nR**, a multilingual reasoning distillation method that leverages **incorrect rationales** alongside positive ones to enhance step-by-step reasoning in LLMs, surpassing baseline performance on unseen languages.

- **Empirical Validation**: Evaluates on **multilingual high-school examinations**, demonstrating significant improvements in **accuracy** and **reasoning correctness** compared to prior English-centric distillation approaches.

- **Technical Scope**: Focuses on **multilingual reasoning transfer** in LLMs, addressing the gap in non-English distillation while benchmarking against existing Chain-of-Thought (CoT) methods.
