# Learn Databricks - Training & Resources | Databricks

## Summary

- **Databricks Lakehouse Platform** integrates data pipelines, collaborative data science, analytics, and ML model deployment in a unified environment, leveraging Apache Spark™ for scalable processing.

- **Training & Certification** offerings include Lakehouse Fundamentals, Generative AI Fundamentals, and Azure Databricks-specific courses, with badges and exams for skill validation via Databricks Academy.

- **Community & Resources** provide access to technical documentation, notebook galleries, live expert sessions, and research papers from Databricks co-founders (e.g., Matei Zaharia, Reynold Xin) in collaboration with UC Berkeley and Stanford.
