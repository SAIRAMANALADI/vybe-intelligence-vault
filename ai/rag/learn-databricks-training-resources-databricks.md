# Learn Databricks - Training & Resources | Databricks

## Summary

- **Databricks Lakehouse Platform** integrates data pipelines, collaborative data science, analytics, and ML model deployment in a unified environment, leveraging Apache Spark™ for distributed processing.

- **Training & Certification** offered via Databricks Academy, including role-specific paths (e.g., Lakehouse Fundamentals, Generative AI) and exams for validating expertise in the platform’s implementation.

- **Community & Resources** include free onboarding sessions, technical documentation, notebook galleries, and research papers from Databricks’ founders (e.g., Matei Zaharia) and collaborators at UC Berkeley/Stanford.
