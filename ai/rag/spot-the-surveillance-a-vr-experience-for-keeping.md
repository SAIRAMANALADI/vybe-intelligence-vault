# Spot the Surveillance: A VR Experience for Keeping an Eye on Big Brother | Electronic Frontier Foundation

## Summary

- **VR Training Tool**: *Spot the Surveillance* is a 10-minute, gaze-based VR experience (WebVR-compatible) developed by EFF to educate users on identifying police surveillance tech (e.g., body-worn cameras, ALPRs, drones, biometric devices, PTZ cameras) in a 360° San Francisco neighborhood scene.

- **Technical Implementation**: Built using open-source A-Frame and 360° photography, the project supports VR headsets (Oculus, Vive, Mixed Reality, Cardboard) and desktop via click-and-drag; iOS requires Motion & Orientation Access enabled.

- **Licensing & Attribution**: Released under Creative Commons Attribution, with surveillance device images sourced from EFF’s *Street-Level Surveillance* research, DHS, and police departments; developed with Mozilla XRstudio residency and Journalism 360 grant support.
