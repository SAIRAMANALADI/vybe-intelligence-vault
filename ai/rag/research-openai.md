# Research | OpenAI

## Summary

- **AGI Development**: OpenAI's research focuses on advancing artificial general intelligence (AGI) through deep learning, leveraging large-scale data and advanced reasoning to train systems capable of human-level problem-solving across multimodal inputs (text, images, audio).

- **Model Series**:
  - **GPT Series**: Fast, cost-efficient models (e.g., GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.3) designed for contextual understanding, content generation, and multimodal reasoning.
  - **o Series**: Chain-of-thought models (e.g., o3, o4-mini) for complex STEM problem-solving via logical, step-by-step analysis.
  - **Visual Models**: State-of-the-art generative models (e.g., CLIP, DALL-E, Sora 2) for precise, photorealistic image/video synthesis with synchronized audio.
  - **Audio Models**: Advances in speech recognition (Whisper) and synthetic voice generation (Voice Engine, Voice Agents) for human-level robustness.

- **Text & Safety**: OpenAI develops instruction-following, summarization, and few-shot learning models for text processing, while prioritizing alignment techniques (e.g., human feedback) to safely deploy powerful AI systems.
