# Research | OpenAI

## Summary

- **AGI Development**: OpenAI's research aims to achieve artificial general intelligence (AGI) through deep learning, leveraging large-scale data and advanced reasoning to enable human-level problem-solving across diverse tasks.

- **Model Advancements**:
  - **GPT Series**: Fast, versatile models (e.g., GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.3) optimized for contextual understanding, content generation, and multimodal reasoning (text, images).
  - **o Series**: Chain-of-thought models (e.g., o3, o4-mini) designed for complex STEM problem-solving via step-by-step logical analysis with full tool integration.

- **Multimodal & Specialized Systems**:
  - **Visual Models**: CLIP (text-image mapping) and DALL-E (text-to-image generation), with Sora 2 enabling physically accurate, controllable video synthesis with synchronized audio.
  - **Audio Models**: Whisper (speech recognition), Voice Engine (synthetic voices), and next-gen audio APIs for real-time processing and generation.
