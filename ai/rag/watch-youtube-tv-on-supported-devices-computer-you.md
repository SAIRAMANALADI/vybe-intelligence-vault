# Watch YouTube TV on supported devices - Computer - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Supported Platforms**: YouTube TV is compatible with computers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari), smart TVs (LG 2017+, Samsung 2017+, Roku TV, Vizio SmartCast, Hisense select models, Fire TV Edition), streaming devices (Apple TV 4th Gen+, Chromecast, Roku devices, Fire TV Stick/4K/Cube, TiVo Stream 4K, Xfinity Flex), and XR devices (Android XR, Meta Quest 2+, Pico, Apple Vision Pro).

- **System Requirements & Troubleshooting**:
  - **Computers**: Requires latest Chrome, Firefox, or Safari; external display disconnection may resolve black screen issues.
  - **Roku**: Enable HDMI Ultra HD Color for HDCP errors; disable HDR in Display type settings for choppy playback.
  - **Apple TV**: Set default video format to SDR (Match Dynamic Range ON) or disable Match Dynamic Range to reduce delays/flickering.

- **Location & Multiview Limitations**:
  - **Location**: Devices without GPS require mobile verification via Settings > Location > Update playback area.
  - **Multiview**: Older devices (pre-2018) may have reduced multiview customization or picture quality.
