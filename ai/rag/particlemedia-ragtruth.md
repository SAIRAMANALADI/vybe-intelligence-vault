# ParticleMedia/RAGTruth

## Summary

*   Introduces RAGTruth, a specialized corpus designed for the identification and analysis of hallucinations in Retrieval-Augmented Language Models (RAGs).
*   Provides a dataset engineered to expose factual inconsistencies and quantify hallucination rates generated by RAG systems.
*   Facilitates the development of more robust and trustworthy RAG architectures through targeted evaluation of their propensity for generating untruthful content.
