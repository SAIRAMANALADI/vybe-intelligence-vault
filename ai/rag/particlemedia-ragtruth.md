# ParticleMedia/RAGTruth

## Summary

*   Introduces RAGTruth, a specialized corpus for identifying and quantifying hallucinations in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models.
*   Provides a benchmark dataset to develop and evaluate the trustworthiness and factual consistency of RAG systems.
*   Aims to mitigate RAG model hallucination by enabling the training and fine-tuning of more robust and reliable models.
