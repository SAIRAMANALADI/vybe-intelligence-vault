## Summary

*   **RAGTruth Corpus:** A specialized dataset designed for the identification and analysis of hallucinations in Retrieval-Augmented Language Models (RAGs).
*   **Hallucination Evaluation:** Facilitates the development and benchmarking of techniques to detect and mitigate factual inconsistencies generated by RAG systems.
*   **Trustworthy RAG Development:** Provides a critical resource for advancing the reliability and trustworthiness of retrieval-augmented language generation architectures.
