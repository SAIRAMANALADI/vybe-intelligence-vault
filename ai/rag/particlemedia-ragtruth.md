# ParticleMedia/RAGTruth

## Summary

*   Introduces RAGTruth, a specialized corpus designed for the evaluation and mitigation of hallucination in Retrieval-Augmented Language Models (RAGs).
*   Aims to facilitate the development of more trustworthy RAG systems by providing a benchmark for factual consistency.
*   Comprises a dataset specifically curated to identify and quantify instances of factual inaccuracy generated by RAG architectures.
