# [1907.12748] Influence Flowers of Academic Entities

## Summary

- **Visual Metaphor**: Introduces the *Influence Flower*, an ego-centric graph visualizing bidirectional influence between academic entities (authors, papers, institutions, etc.) using petal-based styling to encode influence strength and direction.

- **Dataset & Tool**: Leverages Microsoft Academic Graph (212M authors, 176M papers, 1.2B citations) to build an interactive *Influence Map* web app, enabling temporal comparisons of influence patterns across research topics and entities.

- **Applications**: Demonstrates use cases including career trajectory analysis, delayed-recognition paper impact, institutional interdisciplinarity, and cross-platform (academic vs. Twitter) influence contrast via case studies.
