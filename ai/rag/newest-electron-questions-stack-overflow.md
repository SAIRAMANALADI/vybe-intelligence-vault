## Summary

- **Electron IPC messaging**: Safe unified channel messaging in Electron requires structured `contextBridge.exposeInMainWorld` implementations in `preload.js`, avoiding single-function isolation for better maintainability and security.

- **Electron-React integration issues**: Common runtime problems include input focus loss after view transitions (e.g., conditional rendering) and blank screens when bundling Go (Fiber) backends with Electron, requiring proper IPC and lifecycle management.

- **Electron packaging/deployment**: Silent installer failures (e.g., NSIS-based electron-builder) and `ENOENT` errors during Electron startup stem from misconfigured paths, missing dependencies, or improper build scripts, necessitating rigorous pre-packaging validation.
