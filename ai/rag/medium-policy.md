# medium-policy/Rules.md at master · Medium/medium-policy · GitHub

## Summary

- **Content Restrictions**: Medium prohibits content promoting violence, harassment, hate speech, self-harm, or exploitation of minors, including dehumanizing language, doxxing, or non-consensual intimate media. Violations may result in account suspension or content removal.

- **Spam & Manipulation**: Duplicate content, artificial engagement (e.g., bought follows/claps), phishing, and deceptive practices (e.g., misleading metadata) are banned. Violations trigger immediate removal without notice.

- **Data & Embed Compliance**: Embedded forms/links must disclose off-platform data collection, adhere to copyright/trademark policies, and avoid unauthorized scraping or automated interactions. Custom domains must not alter Medium’s core functionality or inject unauthorized elements.
