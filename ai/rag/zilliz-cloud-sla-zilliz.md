# Zilliz Cloud SLA - Zilliz

## Summary

- **Applicability & Exclusions**: SLA applies exclusively to Dedicated clusters under Enterprise/Business Critical plans; excludes Free, Serverless, Standard, and Preview tiers. HIPAA compliance requires a separate BAA.
- **Uptime Commitment & Credits**: Guarantees ≥99.95% Monthly Uptime Percentage for Dedicated clusters, measured by Zilliz’s monitoring. Credits range from 10% to 100% of monthly fees based on downtime severity, with a 2-month claim deadline and credit cap per cluster.
- **Exclusions & Dispute Resolution**: Downtime exclusions include customer actions, third-party cloud failures, force majeure, and preview products. Disputes are resolved via AAA arbitration in San Francisco under Delaware law.
