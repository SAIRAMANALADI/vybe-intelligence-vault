## Summary

- **LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)**: Introduces a parameter-efficient fine-tuning method for large language models (LLMs) by freezing pre-trained weights and injecting trainable low-rank matrices into transformer layers, reducing computational and memory overhead.

- **Gemma Model Integration**: Demonstrates LoRA tuning on Google's Gemma model, leveraging its open-source framework to adapt the model for specific downstream tasks with minimal resource consumption.
