# Research | Cornell University

## Summary

- **Interdisciplinary Research Impact**: Cornell operates 200+ interdisciplinary research centers/institutes, generating $1.61B in FY24 research funding, with 50+ Nobel laureates among its affiliates and 3,200+ jobs created by startups since 2020.

- **Breakthrough Technologies**: Key innovations include a sunlight-driven carbon-capture system (Milner Lab), soft-robotic edema relief glove (EdemaFlex), and student-designed tech for the International Space Station (Sailing to the Stars project).

- **Global & Agricultural Advancements**: Cornell Agritech has commercialized 40,000+ food products, while partnerships like Cornell-Wegmans enhance food safety training and dairy sustainability modeling tools optimize production and emissions tracking.
