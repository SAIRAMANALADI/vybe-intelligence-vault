# gn.general topology - Can a continuous bijection lower topological dimension? - MathOverflow

## Summary

- **Counterexample Construction**: A continuous bijection \( f: X \to Y \) exists where \( Y \) is a 1D metrizable space (e.g., cone over Erdős space \( Y_0 \)), \( X \) is path-connected and Hausdorff (e.g., cone over Erdős space \( X_0 \)), but \( \dim X > 1 \) while \( \dim Y = 1 \).

- **Key Properties**: \( X_0 \) (Erdős space) is totally separated but not zero-dimensional (\( \dim X_0 > 0 \)), while \( Y_0 \) is zero-dimensional; the cone construction preserves metrizability and path-connectedness.

- **Dimension Argument**: \( \dim X > 1 \) follows from Hurewicz’s theorem (\( \dim([\epsilon,1] \times X_0) > \dim X_0 \)), and \( \dim Y = 1 \) is shown via small inductive dimension (basepoint neighborhoods have 0D boundaries).
