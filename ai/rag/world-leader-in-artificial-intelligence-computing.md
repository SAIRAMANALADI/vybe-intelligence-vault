## Summary

- **NVIDIA AI Platforms**: Offers DGX Cloud (AI factory in cloud), HGX (supercomputer for AI/HPC), and MGX (modular accelerated servers), alongside Jetson (embedded systems), DRIVE AGX (autonomous vehicles), and IGX (edge AI with functional safety).

- **AI Software Stack**: Includes Nemotron (agentic AI models), NeMo (AI agents), NIM (inference microservices), RAPIDS (data science acceleration), and NVIDIA AI Enterprise (enterprise-grade AI deployment suite).

- **Accelerated Computing Solutions**: Features Grace Blackwell CPU-GPU architecture, BlueField DPUs (networking/storage acceleration), InfiniBand (high-performance interconnects), and CUDA-X (GPU-optimized AI/HPC libraries).
