# NIST Finalizes ‘Lightweight Cryptography’ Standard to Protect Small Devices | NIST

## Summary

- NIST finalized *Ascon-Based Lightweight Cryptography Standards for Constrained Devices* (NIST SP 800-232), selecting four Ascon-family algorithms (ASCON-128 AEAD, ASCON-Hash 256, ASCON-XOF 128, ASCON-CXOF 128) optimized for resource-constrained IoT and embedded systems.
- ASCON-128 AEAD provides authenticated encryption with associated data (AEAD) with side-channel-resistant implementations, while ASCON-Hash 256 offers lightweight hashing for data integrity, replacing SHA-3 in constrained environments.
- ASCON-XOF 128 and ASCON-CXOF 128 enable variable-length hash outputs and customizable labels to mitigate collision risks, ensuring scalability and future adaptability for evolving cryptographic needs.
