# LangChain Community Slack for GenAI Developers

## Summary

- **Community Guidelines**: Strict zero-tolerance policy for disrespectful behavior; all participants must adhere to the [LangChain Community Code of Conduct](https://www.langchain.com/community-code) and use real identities to ensure constructive discourse.

- **Prohibited Solicitations**: Vendor, recruiter, or competitor solicitations (including unsolicited DMs) are banned; promotional content must be restricted to designated channels (#vendor-content, #events, #jobs) and must not include calls-to-action (e.g., sign-ups, trials).

- **Channel Etiquette**: Public channels are default; Slack is for broader discussions/events, while the [LangChain Forum](https://forum.langchain.com/) is for product support/questions; improper messaging (e.g., tagging, double-posting) is discouraged.
