# Get More Storage, More AI capabilities, and More Features - Google One

## Summary

- **Google AI Plans Integration**: AI Plus ($9.99/mo, 400GB) offers 2x higher Gemini usage limits, Omni/Gemini Spark, AI Inbox (Gmail), and Pro model access; AI Pro ($99.99/mo, 5TB) adds 4x limits, YouTube Premium Lite, and Chrome auto-browse (US); AI Ultra (20TB+) provides 20x limits, YouTube Premium, and early AI innovations.

- **Storage & Sharing**: Google One plans (Basic: 100GB, Standard: 200GB) extend unified storage across Drive, Photos, and Gmail; Pro/Ultra tiers enable sharing with up to 5 users, auto-device backups, and cross-app AI tool integration (e.g., Antigravity, Gemini).

- **AI-Driven Productivity**: Gemini acts as an in-app assistant for drafting, coding, and creative tasks; AI Pro/Ultra tiers unlock advanced features like autonomous coding agents, expanded model access, and prioritized AI model rollouts.
