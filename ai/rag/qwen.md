# Qwen

## Summary

- **Qwen3-2507 Models**:
  - **Qwen3-Instruct-2507**: Enhanced general capabilities (instruction following, reasoning, comprehension, math, science, coding, tool usage), improved long-tail multilingual knowledge coverage, better alignment with user preferences, and 256K long-context understanding (extensible to 1M).
  - **Qwen3-Thinking-2507**: State-of-the-art performance in reasoning tasks (logical reasoning, math, science, coding, academic benchmarks), improved general capabilities, and 256K long-context understanding (extensible to 1M).

- **Qwen3 Architecture**:
  - Supports dense and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models across scales (0.6B to 235B-A22B), with seamless switching between **thinking mode** (complex reasoning/math/coding) and **non-thinking mode** (general chat).
  - Superior reasoning (math, code, commonsense), human preference alignment (creative writing, role-playing, instruction following), and agent capabilities (tool integration, complex tasks).
  - Multilingual support (100+ languages/dialects) with strong instruction following and translation capabilities.
