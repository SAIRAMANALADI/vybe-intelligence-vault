# LlamaParse Pricing: Compare Plans & Credits | LlamaIndex

## Summary

- **Credit-based pricing**: LlamaParse operates on a credit system where 1,000 credits = $1.25; parsing costs range from 1 credit (basic) to higher costs for agentic/LLM-based parsing, with pay-as-you-go tiers up to $5,000/month in the Pro plan.

- **Multi-tier parsing & extraction**: Supports concurrent jobs (5–100 depending on plan), advanced table/chart/image extraction, structured JSON output, and 130+ file formats (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, images), with auto-tier routing for cost efficiency.

- **Enterprise-grade compliance & deployment**: Offers SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA certifications, VPC deployment options (AWS/Azure), SSO/MFA, and hybrid cloud support; Free plan includes 10K credits/month, while Enterprise provides custom scaling and dedicated support.
