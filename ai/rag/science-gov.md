# Science.gov

## Summary

- **Aggregated Scientific Repository**: Science.gov provides access to millions of peer-reviewed scientific research results from U.S. federal agencies via a unified search interface.

- **Multi-Agency Governance**: Operated under CENDI, a collaborative organization of federal scientific and technical information (STI) agencies, including USDA, DOD, DOE, NASA, NIH, NSF, and others.

- **Cross-Agency Metadata Integration**: Utilizes federated search to query distributed databases across participating agencies, ensuring comprehensive coverage of STI while maintaining agency-specific metadata standards.
