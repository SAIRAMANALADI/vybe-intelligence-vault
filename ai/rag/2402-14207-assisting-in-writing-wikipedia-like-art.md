# [2402.14207] Assisting in Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch with Large Language Models

## Summary

- **STORM framework**: Proposes a three-stage pipeline for generating Wikipedia-like articles: (1) *perspective discovery* via topic decomposition, (2) *multi-agent debate* where agents with distinct viewpoints query a retrieval-augmented expert, and (3) *outline synthesis* from curated evidence.

- **FreshWiki dataset**: Introduces a benchmark of recent Wikipedia articles with outline-level annotations to evaluate organization, breadth, and factual grounding, revealing STORM’s 25% absolute improvement in structure and 10% in coverage over retrieval-augmented baselines.

- **Key limitations**: Identifies challenges in long-form generation, including *source bias propagation* and *fact over-association*, highlighting the need for better retrieval grounding and factual consistency mechanisms.
