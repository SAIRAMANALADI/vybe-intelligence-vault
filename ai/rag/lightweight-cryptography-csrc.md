# Lightweight Cryptography | CSRC

## Summary

- **NIST standardized Ascon family** (SP 800-232, Aug 2025) for lightweight cryptography in constrained environments, specifying **Ascon-AEAD128** (authenticated encryption), **Ascon-Hash256** (hashing), **Ascon-XOF128** (extendable-output function), and **Ascon-CXOF128** (customized XOF).

- **Standardization process** (2018–2023) involved three rounds: 57 submissions → 32 Round 2 candidates → 10 finalists, culminating in Ascon’s selection due to optimized performance in constrained devices.

- **Future work** includes potential standards for a dedicated **MAC** and **variable-output-length PRF** based on the Ascon family.
