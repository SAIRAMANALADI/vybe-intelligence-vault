# Manage cookies in Microsoft Edge: View, allow, block, delete and use | Microsoft Support

## Summary

- **Cookie Management in Edge**: Cookies are stored locally by websites for session persistence, preferences, and tracking; managed via `Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies` in Microsoft Edge.
- **Granular Cookie Controls**: Allows per-site allow/block lists (`Allowed to save cookies` / `Not allowed to save cookies`), third-party cookie blocking, and global enable/disable of cookie storage.
- **Cookie Deletion & Preloading**: Supports selective deletion via `See all cookies and site data`, bulk deletion via `Clear browsing data`, auto-deletion on browser close, and preloading pages using cookies for performance optimization.
