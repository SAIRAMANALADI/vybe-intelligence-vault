# Federal Register :: Nondiscrimination on the Basis of Disability; Accessibility of Web Information and Services of State

## Summary

- **Legal Status**: FederalRegister.gov's XML-based Federal Register prototype remains unofficial until the ACFR grants regulatory approval; users must verify against the official SGML-based PDF on govinfo.gov for legal/research purposes.

- **Rule Details**: DOJ's final rule (RIN: 1190-AA79) under 28 CFR 35 mandates web/mobile accessibility for state/local governments, effective 06/24/2024, published as 89 FR 31320 (77 pages).

- **Scope**: Rule defines key terms (e.g., "Mobile Applications," "Archived Web Content") and requires compliance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA for public entities' digital services.
