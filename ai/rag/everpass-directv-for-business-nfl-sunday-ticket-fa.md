# EverPass & DIRECTV FOR BUSINESS: NFL Sunday Ticket FAQs – EverPass

## Summary

- **Transfer Restrictions**: EverPass cannot migrate existing NFL Sunday Ticket subscriptions to DIRECTV FOR BUSINESS; customers must establish a new agreement directly with DIRECTV FOR BUSINESS, which may offer different pricing, discounts, or payment plans.
- **Refund Policy**: Cancellations of EverPass NFL Sunday Ticket subscriptions (initiated on/after April 1, 2026) result in refunds for payments made toward the 2026 subscription, issued to the original payment method where possible.
- **Hardware & Multi-Service Flexibility**: EverPass refunds eligible hardware fees upon cancellation of NFL Sunday Ticket (excluding shipping), and customers may retain other EverPass services (e.g., Core, Peacock Sports Pass) independently of NFL Sunday Ticket subscriptions.
