# Fighting child sexual abuse online

## Summary

- **Proactive CSAM Detection & Reporting**: Google employs machine learning classifiers, hash-matching technology (unique digital fingerprints), and specialized teams to detect, remove, and report CSAM to NCMEC, which collaborates with global law enforcement. Their [CSAM Transparency Report](https://transparencyreport.google.com/child-sexual-abuse-material/reporting) quantifies enforcement actions across Search, YouTube, and other services.

- **Open-Source Tools for Partners**: Google provides free tools like the **Content Safety API** (for classifying previously unseen CSAM) and **CSAI Match** (for video hash-matching of known CSAM) to NGOs and industry partners (e.g., Facebook, Reddit, Adobe) to scale detection and disrupt re-uploads.

- **Global Alliances & Funding**: Google funds research and partners with coalitions (e.g., WeProtect, INHOPE) to develop cross-industry solutions. Google.org has granted ~$90M to organizations like INHOPE and ECPAT International, while Ad Grants support NGOs combating child exploitation.
