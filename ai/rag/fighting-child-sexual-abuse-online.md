# Fighting child sexual abuse online

## Summary

- **Proprietary Detection & Reporting**: Uses machine learning classifiers, hash-matching (unique digital fingerprints), and specialized teams to detect, remove, and report CSAM to NCMEC, collaborating with global law enforcement.

- **Tool Development & Sharing**: Provides free tools like **Content Safety API** (classifies new CSAM via ML) and **CSAI Match** (hash-based video CSAM detection) to NGOs and industry partners to combat abuse at scale.

- **Global Alliances & Funding**: Actively participates in coalitions (e.g., WeProtect, INHOPE) to fund research, share insights, and disrupt CSAM distribution, alongside Google.org grants (~$90M) to anti-abuse organizations.
