# Get extra features on YouTube TV with 4K Plus - Computer - YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **4K Plus Add-on Features**: Unlocks 4K Ultra HD playback for select live/on-demand content, enables unlimited concurrent in-home streams, and allows offline DVR viewing on mobile devices (9-month expiration, 30-day internet refresh requirement).
- **Compatible Devices**: 4K playback supported on 4K smart TVs (e.g., Sony Bravia, Samsung/LG/HiSense 2019+) and 4K streaming devices (e.g., Chromecast with Google TV, Apple TV 4K 2021, Nvidia Shield) with HDCP 2.2-compliant HDMI cables and ≥25 Mbps bandwidth.
- **Activation & Limitations**: Requires subscription via YouTube TV settings; offline downloads restricted to one device at a time, with 3 out-of-home streams permitted (unlimited in-home). 4K availability varies by network and content.
