# App Store - Apple

## Summary

- **App Review & Security**: 100% of apps are automatically screened for malware; over 1.9M submissions rejected in 2024 for privacy violations/fraud; human reviewers enforce strict guidelines for safety, privacy, and compliance.

- **Privacy Controls**: Apps require explicit user permission for data access (location, contacts, etc.); system-level protections prevent cross-app data sharing without consent; users can revoke permissions anytime.

- **Distribution & Ecosystem**: Apps sync across 2.35B+ devices; universal apps auto-install on new devices; secure purchases via two-factor authentication, Touch ID, and Face ID with refund support via Apple Support.
