# Submit a Report - Watchdog

## Summary

- **TRUSTe's Dispute Resolution Program**: Facilitates consumer complaints against certified companies regarding data transparency, usage control, and accountability, with resolution attempts made within 1-10 business days by mediating between the consumer and the reported company.

- **Data Handling & Submission Process**: Reports are processed in the U.S. and Australia, requiring a valid email for follow-ups; consumers can opt for anonymity (e.g., "NoName") but must consent to data sharing for account-specific resolutions.

- **Complaint Submission Requirements**: Mandates prior direct contact with the reported company, a 20+ character detailed issue description, and compliance with CAPTCHA validation; unresolved issues may be escalated via TRUSTe’s structured dispute resolution workflow.
