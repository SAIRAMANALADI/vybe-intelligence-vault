# general relativity - Why do people say that the Einstein equations are PDEs on a manifold? - Physics Stack Exchange

## Summary

- The Einstein field equations (EFE) are nonlinear PDEs where the metric tensor components are the dependent variables, derived from the Einstein tensor and stress-energy tensor relationship.

- In local trivializations (single coordinate patches), EFE reduces to a system of nonlinear PDEs without explicit jet bundle formalism, though globally, jet bundles provide the rigorous mathematical framework.

- Formally, the frame bundle $\mathit{FM} = J^1(M, \mathbb{R}^{1,3})$ (1-jet bundle of local charts) underlies the geometric structure, with tangent bundle $\mathit{TM} = J^1(M, \mathbb{R})$ representing the 1-jet of maps from spacetime to $\mathbb{R}$.
