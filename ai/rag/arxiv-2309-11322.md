# [2309.11322] Vector database management systems: Fundamental concepts, use-cases, and current challenges

## Summary

- **Vector DBMS Fundamentals**: Vector database management systems (VDBMS) specialize in storing, indexing, and querying high-dimensional vector embeddings (e.g., from texts, images) for similarity search, leveraging computational efficiency in storage and comparison.

- **Core Challenges**: High dimensionality and sparsity of vectorized data necessitate optimized indexing (e.g., HNSW, IVF), approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) search, and scalable architectures to mitigate performance bottlenecks in retrieval tasks.

- **Applications & Trends**: VDBMS are critical in recommender systems, chatbots, and generative AI pipelines, with ongoing research addressing trade-offs between accuracy, latency, and resource consumption in real-time vector operations.
