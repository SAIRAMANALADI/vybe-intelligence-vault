# Google's products and services - About Google

## Summary

- **Google Search Enhancements**: Introduces *Circle to Search* for on-device visual queries, *AI Mode* for conversational search with follow-ups, and *Lens* for multimodal (image/text/screen) search integration, leveraging real-time OCR and contextual understanding.

- **Gemini AI Suite**: Unveils *Gemini Live* for voice-driven interactions, *Canvas* for prompt-to-prototype generation (e.g., apps, infographics), and native *Image/Video Generation* tools, all integrated with Google Workspace (Gmail/Docs/Sheets) for real-time collaboration.

- **Experimental AI Tools**: *Flow* for AI-driven video editing, *NotebookLM* as a research assistant with source-based summarization, and *Google Labs* for early-stage AI experiments, alongside AI features in *Google Photos* (Reimagine/Magic Eraser) and *Maps* (AI-generated itineraries).
