# Use of languages on our websites | European Union

## Summary

- **Multilingual Coverage**: EU websites provide content in all 24 official EU languages, with priority content (e.g., legislation, key political documents) always translated; specialized or time-sensitive content may initially appear in fewer languages based on audience needs.

- **Machine Translation Integration**: The EU employs **eTranslation**, a machine translation service, to bridge gaps where human translation is unavailable, ensuring broader accessibility despite resource constraints.

- **English as Default**: All content is published in English due to its reach (~90% of visitors) via either native or preferred foreign language options, optimizing global accessibility.
