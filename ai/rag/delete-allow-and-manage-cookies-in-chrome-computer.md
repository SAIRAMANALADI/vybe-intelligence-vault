# Delete, allow, and manage cookies in Chrome - Computer - Google Chrome Help

## Summary

- **Cookie Types & Functionality**: Chrome distinguishes between **first-party cookies** (created by visited sites) and **third-party cookies** (created by embedded content like ads/images from other domains), enabling session persistence, preferences storage, and personalized content delivery while posing privacy risks via cross-site tracking.

- **Cookie Management Workflow**: Users can **delete all cookies** (via `Settings > Privacy and security > Delete browsing data`) or **target specific cookies** (`Settings > Third-party cookies > See all site data and permissions`), with granular time-range controls (e.g., "Last 7 days") and domain-level exceptions for third-party cookies.

- **Third-Party Cookie Policies & Embedded Content**: Chrome allows **blocking third-party cookies by default** (with exceptions lists) or enabling them temporarily (90-day duration), while embedded content (e.g., Google Docs in a school portal) may request cookie access for cross-site functionality, with permissions managed via `View site information > Cookies and site data`.
