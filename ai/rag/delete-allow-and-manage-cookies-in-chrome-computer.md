# Delete, allow, and manage cookies in Chrome - Computer - Google Chrome Help

## Summary

- **Cookie Types & Functionality**: First-party cookies are generated by the visited site (visible in the address bar), while third-party cookies are created by embedded content (ads, images) from other domains; both enable session persistence, preferences storage, and personalized content delivery.

- **Cookie Management in Chrome**: Users can delete all cookies (via `Delete browsing data` > `Cookies and other site data`) or selectively remove specific cookies (via `Settings` > `Privacy and security` > `Third-party cookies` > `See all site data and permissions`); time ranges (e.g., "Last 7 days," "All time") are customizable.

- **Third-Party Cookie Controls**: Chrome allows default blocking/allowing of third-party cookies (`Settings` > `Privacy and security` > `Third-party cookies`), with exceptions for specific sites (via domain wildcards like `[*.]example.com`) or temporary overrides; related-site groups (e.g., `acme-music.example` and `acme-video.example`) can be managed to maintain cross-site functionality while blocking third-party cookies.
