# Delete, allow, and manage cookies in Chrome - Computer - Google Chrome Help

## Summary

- **Cookie Types & Storage**: Chrome distinguishes between first-party cookies (created by visited sites) and third-party cookies (created by embedded content from other domains), with both stored locally for session persistence, personalization, and authentication.

- **Cookie Management Workflow**: Users can delete all cookies via `Settings > Privacy and security > Delete browsing data` or selectively via `Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > See all site data and permissions`, with granular time-range controls (e.g., "Last 7 days").

- **Third-Party Cookie Policies**: Chrome allows blocking/allowing third-party cookies globally or per-site via exceptions lists (e.g., `[*.]example.com`), with temporary overrides in Incognito mode; related-site groups (e.g., `acme-music.example`, `acme-video.example`) can share activity data if enabled.
