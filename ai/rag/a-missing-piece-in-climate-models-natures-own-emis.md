# A Missing Piece in Climate Models: Nature’s Own Emissions - Yale E360

## Summary

- **Omission in Climate Models**: Major climate models (e.g., IPCC-linked) exclude or underrepresent warming-induced emissions from wildfires, wetlands, and permafrost, despite their potential to add **0.2–0.6°C** to global temperatures by 2100, exacerbating uncertainties in Paris Agreement targets.

- **Ecosystem Feedback Amplification**: Wildfires (+60% CO₂ since 2001), methane spikes from wetlands (linked to decomposition/wetter conditions), and Arctic permafrost thaw are accelerating natural emissions, with tundra shifting from carbon sink to source due to thawing and combustion.

- **Data Gaps & Policy Urgency**: Limited monitoring in remote regions (e.g., Siberia, Congo) and nonlinear ecosystem responses hinder accurate modeling; integrating these feedbacks into IPCC assessments (due 2029) is critical to refine mitigation strategies and emissions accounting.
