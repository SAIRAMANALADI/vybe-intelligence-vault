# AI Tools for Business | Google Workspace

## Summary

- **Gemini Integration**: Enterprise-ready AI assistant (Gemini) embedded across Workspace apps (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Meet) for real-time task automation, content generation, and data analysis via natural language prompts.

- **NotebookLM & AI Workflows**: NotebookLM transforms files into structured insights (audio/visual summaries), while Studio enables low-code AI workflow automation for meeting prep, email triage, and cross-app data processing.

- **Security & Compliance**: Zero-data retention for AI training; enterprise-grade controls include DLP, context-aware access, digital sovereignty (encryption/residency), and cross-platform agent governance (e.g., Microsoft 365, Salesforce).
