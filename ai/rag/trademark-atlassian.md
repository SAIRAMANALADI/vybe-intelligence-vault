# Trademark | Atlassian

## Summary

- **Trademark Usage Compliance**: Third-party use of Atlassian trademarks must adhere to strict guidelines, including unmodified display, no deceptive intent, and no likelihood of confusion; logos must be sourced from official Atlassian assets and used as-is (resizing permitted).
- **Naming and Domain Restrictions**: Atlassian trademarks may only appear in third-party product names as referential phrases (e.g., "for Jira") with no prominence over the vendor’s name; domains cannot include Atlassian trademarks (e.g., `atlassian.vendor.com` prohibited).
- **Prohibited Actions**: Third parties cannot file for trademark protection on marks containing or resembling Atlassian trademarks, mimic Atlassian’s branding (fonts, UI, screenshots), or use Atlassian trademarks in company names without explicit authorization.
