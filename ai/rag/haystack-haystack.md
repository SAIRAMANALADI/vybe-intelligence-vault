# Haystack | Haystack

## Summary

- **Modular AI Orchestration**: Haystack provides a transparent, modular framework for building production-ready AI agents, RAG systems, and context-engineered workflows with full inspection, debugging, and optimization capabilities across retrieval, reasoning, memory, and tool integration.

- **Multi-Provider & Cloud-Agnostic Deployment**: Supports seamless integration with major LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, etc.) and vector databases (Weaviate, Pinecone, Elasticsearch) without vendor lock-in, while offering Kubernetes-ready, cloud-agnostic pipelines with built-in reliability, observability, and enterprise-scale deployment tools.

- **Advanced Use Cases & Ecosystem**: Enables multimodal AI, conversational agents, and content generation via Jinja-2 templates, while providing enterprise-grade support (private engineering, deployment guides) and a visual AI Orchestration Platform for scalable, secure, and auditable AI workflows.
