## Summary

- **Modular AI Orchestration Framework**: Haystack 3.0 enables end-to-end agentic AI workflows (retrieval, reasoning, memory, tool use) with full transparency for debugging and optimization via a composable, cloud-agnostic pipeline architecture.

- **Vendor-Neutral Integration**: Supports seamless interoperability with LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral), vector DBs (Weaviate, Pinecone), and search engines (Elasticsearch) without vendor lock-in, facilitating hybrid retrieval and multimodal processing.

- **Enterprise-Grade Scalability**: Provides Kubernetes-ready, serializable pipelines with built-in observability (logging/monitoring) and enterprise support, transitioning from PoC to production with standardized tooling and deployment guides.
