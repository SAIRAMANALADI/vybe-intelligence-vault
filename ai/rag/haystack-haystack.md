# Haystack | Haystack

## Summary

- **Modular AI Orchestration**: Haystack provides a transparent, modular framework for building production-grade AI agents with full visibility into retrieval, reasoning, memory, and tool integration, supporting hybrid retrieval and self-correction loops for advanced RAG pipelines.

- **Vendor-Neutral Integration & Scalability**: Supports seamless integration with major AI models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral) and vector databases (Weaviate, Pinecone, Elasticsearch) without vendor lock-in, with cloud-agnostic, Kubernetes-ready pipelines for enterprise-scale deployment and observability.

- **Multimodal & Agentic AI Capabilities**: Enables multimodal AI workflows (text, image, audio) and standardized tool-calling for AI agents, with branching/looping pipelines for complex decision flows, alongside Jinja-2 templating for content generation and conversational AI interfaces.
