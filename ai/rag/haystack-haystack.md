# Haystack | Haystack

## Summary

- **Modular AI Orchestration**: Haystack provides a transparent, modular framework for building production-grade AI agents with full visibility into retrieval, reasoning, memory, and tool integration, enabling inspection, debugging, and optimization of AI decision-making processes.

- **Multi-Provider & Cloud-Agnostic Deployment**: Supports integration with major AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, etc.) and vector databases (Weaviate, Pinecone, Elasticsearch) without vendor lock-in, while offering serializable, Kubernetes-ready pipelines for scalable, cloud-agnostic deployment with built-in reliability and observability.

- **Enterprise-Grade AI Pipelines**: Enables rapid development from prototype to production with unified tooling, supporting advanced use cases like multimodal AI, conversational AI, RAG, and AI agents, alongside enterprise support options for secure, scalable, and auditable deployments.
