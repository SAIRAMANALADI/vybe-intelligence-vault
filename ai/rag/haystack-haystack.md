## Summary

- **Modular AI Orchestration**: Haystack provides a transparent, modular framework for building production-ready AI agents, RAG systems, and context-engineered workflows with full pipeline visibility, debugging, and optimization capabilities.

- **Multi-Provider & Cloud-Agnostic**: Supports seamless integration with major LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, etc.) and vector databases (Weaviate, Pinecone, Elasticsearch) while ensuring vendor neutrality and Kubernetes-ready, cloud-agnostic deployment.

- **Enterprise-Grade Scalability**: Offers enterprise support, observability tools, and scalable deployment options (on-prem/cloud) with standardized tooling for rapid prototyping to full-scale production, including multimodal AI and conversational AI use cases.
