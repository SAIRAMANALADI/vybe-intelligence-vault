## Summary

- **Modular AI Orchestration**: Haystack provides a transparent, modular framework for building production-ready AI agents, RAG pipelines, and context-engineered systems with full inspection, debugging, and optimization capabilities for each component.

- **Multi-Provider & Cloud-Agnostic**: Supports integration with major LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral) and vector databases (Weaviate, Pinecone, Elasticsearch) without vendor lock-in, and enables Kubernetes-ready, cloud-agnostic deployment with built-in reliability and observability.

- **Enterprise-Grade Scalability**: Offers enterprise support, visual pipeline design, secure access controls, and scalable deployment options (cloud/on-prem) for advanced use cases like multimodal AI, conversational agents, and content generation.
