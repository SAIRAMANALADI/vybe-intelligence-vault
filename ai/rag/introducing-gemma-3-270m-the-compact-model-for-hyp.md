# Introducing Gemma 3 270M: The compact model for hyper-efficient AI - Google Developers Blog

## Summary

- **Architecture & Efficiency**: 270M-parameter model with 170M embedding and 100M transformer parameters, featuring a 256k-token vocabulary for rare token handling and INT4-quantized energy efficiency (0.75% battery usage for 25 Pixel 9 Pro SoC conversations).

- **Instruction-Following & Deployment**: Pre-trained and instruction-tuned variants available, optimized for task-specific fine-tuning (e.g., text classification, data extraction) with production-ready QAT checkpoints for minimal performance degradation in resource-constrained environments.

- **Accessibility & Tooling**: Distributed via Hugging Face, Ollama, Kaggle, LM Studio, and Docker; supports fine-tuning with Hugging Face, UnSloth, and JAX; deployable on-device or via Vertex AI, llama.cpp, LiteRT, Keras, and MLX.
