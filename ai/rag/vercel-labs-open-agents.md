## Summary

- **Purpose**: Provides a TypeScript-based open-source template for constructing cloud-based AI agents, including background agents, with modular architecture for extensibility.

- **Key Features**: Supports agent orchestration, integrates with Vercel’s serverless infrastructure, and includes pre-configured examples for rapid deployment of AI-driven workflows.
