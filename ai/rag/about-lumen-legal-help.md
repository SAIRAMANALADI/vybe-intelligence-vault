# About Lumen - Legal Help

## Summary

- **Lumen Database** aggregates and publishes content removal requests (e.g., defamation, copyright, local laws, court orders) voluntarily shared by Google to promote transparency and research on online content moderation.
- **Data shared** varies by request type but typically includes URLs, requestor/country/date, supporting documents, and request explanations; personal contact details (e.g., email) are **never** shared.
- **Exceptions** exist for high public interest cases, where requestor names may be disclosed after case-by-case assessment, particularly for defamation or local law requests.
