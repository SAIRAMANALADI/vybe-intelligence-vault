# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any RAG system enabling retrieval and integration of knowledge from heterogeneous sources across diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Mitigates the *modality gap* by dynamically selecting the most relevant modality-specific corpus for targeted retrieval, avoiding bias toward the query's original modality, with theoretical justification for its effectiveness.

- **Granularity-Based Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity levels to optimize retrieval precision based on query complexity and scope, validated across 10 benchmarks where UniversalRAG outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines.
