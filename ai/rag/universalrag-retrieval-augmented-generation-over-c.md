# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that dynamically retrieves and integrates knowledge from heterogeneous, multi-modal sources (e.g., text, images, videos) with varying granularities, addressing the limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Mitigates the *modality gap* problem by using a routing mechanism that selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, avoiding suboptimal performance from forcing all modalities into a single representation space, with theoretical justification for its effectiveness.

- **Granularity-Based Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels (e.g., fine-grained vs. coarse-grained knowledge), enabling adaptive retrieval tailored to query complexity, and demonstrates superior performance over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 multi-modal benchmarks.
