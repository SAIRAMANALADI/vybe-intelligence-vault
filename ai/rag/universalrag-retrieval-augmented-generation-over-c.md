# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG** introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework that dynamically retrieves and integrates knowledge from heterogeneous, multi-modal sources (e.g., text, images, videos) with varying granularities, addressing the limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-aware routing** is proposed to mitigate the *modality gap* problem, where retrieval is biased toward the same modality as the query; this method dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for targeted retrieval, supported by theoretical analysis.

- Evaluated on **10 benchmarks** across multiple modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baselines, demonstrating its effectiveness in handling diverse, real-world queries requiring multi-modal and multi-granular knowledge.
