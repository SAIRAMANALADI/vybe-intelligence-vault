# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any RAG system that retrieves and integrates knowledge from heterogeneous sources with diverse modalities and granularities, addressing the limitations of single-modality or unified representation approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Proposes a dynamic retrieval mechanism that selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus based on the query, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where retrieval favors items from the same modality as the query, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-Based Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, validated across 10 benchmarks where UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baselines.
