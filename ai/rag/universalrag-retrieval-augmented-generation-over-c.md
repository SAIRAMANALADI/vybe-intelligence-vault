# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any RAG system enabling retrieval and integration of knowledge from heterogeneous sources with diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Implements dynamic modality-specific corpus selection to mitigate the *modality gap*, where retrieval favors same-modality items as the query, supported by theoretical analysis to justify its effectiveness.

- **Granularity-Based Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels, allowing fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, validated across 10 benchmarks where UniversalRAG outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines.
