# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that integrates heterogeneous knowledge sources across diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Mitigates the *modality gap* by dynamically selecting the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, avoiding forced unification into a single representation space that biases results toward the query's modality.

- **Granularity-Based Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels, enabling precise retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, validated empirically on 10 benchmarks where UniversalRAG outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines.
