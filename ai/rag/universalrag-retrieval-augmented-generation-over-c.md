# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any RAG system that retrieves and integrates knowledge from heterogeneous sources with diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Mitigates the *modality gap* (where retrieval favors same-modality items) by dynamically selecting the most relevant modality-specific corpus for targeted retrieval, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends retrieval flexibility by structuring each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope, validated across 10 multi-modal benchmarks.
