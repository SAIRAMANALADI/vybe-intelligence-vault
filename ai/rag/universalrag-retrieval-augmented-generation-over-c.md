# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG** introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework that dynamically retrieves and integrates knowledge from heterogeneous sources with diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing the limitation of single-modality RAG systems.

- **Modality-aware routing** is proposed to mitigate the *modality gap* issue, where retrieval is biased toward the same modality as the query; this method dynamically selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for targeted retrieval, supported by theoretical analysis.

- Evaluated on **10 benchmarks** across multiple modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines, demonstrating its effectiveness in handling real-world queries requiring varied knowledge types.
