# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any RAG system that retrieves and integrates knowledge from heterogeneous sources with diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Proposes dynamic retrieval by selecting the most relevant modality-specific corpus per query, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers to enable fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity, validated across 10 benchmarks where UniversalRAG outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines.
