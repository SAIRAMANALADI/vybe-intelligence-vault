# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG** introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework that integrates heterogeneous knowledge sources across diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality or unified-corpus approaches.

- **Modality-aware routing** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* problem where queries favor same-modality retrieval, supported by theoretical analysis.

- Evaluated on 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baselines, demonstrating its effectiveness in fine-grained, cross-modal knowledge integration.
