# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG** introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework that dynamically integrates knowledge from heterogeneous, modality-specific corpora (text, images, videos) to address diverse query types, avoiding the limitations of single-modality or unified-representation approaches.

- **Modality-aware routing** is proposed to mitigate the *modality gap* by selectively retrieving from the most relevant modality-specific corpus based on the query, supported by theoretical analysis to justify its effectiveness in improving retrieval precision.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval** organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels, enabling fine-grained knowledge extraction tailored to query complexity, validated across 10 benchmarks where UniversalRAG outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines.
