# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **UniversalRAG** introduces an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework that integrates knowledge from heterogeneous sources across diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality RAG systems.

- **Modality-aware routing** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, and is theoretically justified for effectiveness.

- Evaluated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines, demonstrating superior adaptability to varying query types and granularity levels.
