# UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Any-to-Any RAG Framework**: Introduces *UniversalRAG*, an adaptive retrieval-augmented generation system that integrates heterogeneous knowledge sources across diverse modalities (e.g., text, images, videos) and granularities to address real-world queries requiring varied knowledge types.

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Mitigates the *modality gap* (bias toward same-modality retrieval) by dynamically selecting the most relevant modality-specific corpus for targeted retrieval, supported by theoretical analysis to justify its effectiveness.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, validated across 10 benchmarks where *UniversalRAG* outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines.
