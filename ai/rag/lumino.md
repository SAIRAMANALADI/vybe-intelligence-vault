# Lumino

## Summary

- **AI-Powered Learning Platform**: Lumino, developed by AI Singapore, leverages AI agents to enhance learning through instant doubt clarification, code evaluation, and scenario-based role-playing, catering to users from beginners to engineers.
- **Training Initiative**: Since 2017, AI Singapore has trained hundreds of Singaporeans in AI, with Lumino serving as an advanced tool to accelerate and deepen AI education.
- **Technical Infrastructure**: Lumino integrates authentication (Sign In), compliance (Terms, Privacy, Legal), and branding (Lumino logo) within a structured web interface hosted by AI Singapore.
