# [2010.08240] Augmented SBERT: Data Augmentation Method for Improving Bi-Encoders for Pairwise Sentence Scoring Tasks

## Summary

- **Augmented SBERT** leverages cross-encoder labels to augment bi-encoder training data, improving pairwise sentence scoring performance by up to **6 points (in-domain)** and **37 points (domain adaptation)** over baseline bi-encoders.

- **Data selection is critical**: Non-trivial pair selection from cross-encoder outputs significantly impacts augmentation success, requiring careful filtering to enhance bi-encoder training.

- **Bi-encoders** (independent sentence embedding) trade cross-encoder accuracy for speed, but benefit from Augmented SBERT’s synthetic training pairs to bridge performance gaps in target tasks.
