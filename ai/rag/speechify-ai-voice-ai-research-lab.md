# SpeechifyAI - Voice AI Research Lab

## Summary

- **Model Performance**: SpeechifyAI's Simba 3.2 achieves **sub-100ms latency**, ranks #1 on Artificial Analysis' TTS leaderboard for US English, and offers **streaming-native architecture** with **SSML prosody control** and **emotional expression** (neutral, happy, sad, excited, calm) via fine-grained prosody modeling.

- **Voice Cloning & Multilingual Support**: Implements **zero-shot voice cloning** from **≥10s reference audio**, capturing timbre, cadence, and micro-expressions; supports **native-quality synthesis across 30+ locales** with locale-specific voice recordings and mixed-language input handling.

- **API Integration**: Provides a **single API** for streaming, voice cloning, and emotion control, enabling **few-line code implementation** (e.g., `curl` POST requests) with support for customizable output formats (e.g., MP3).
