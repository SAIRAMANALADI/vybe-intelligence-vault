# SpeechifyAI - Voice AI Research Lab

## Summary

- **Simba 3.2**: Streaming-native TTS model ranked #1 on Artificial Analysis leaderboard, achieving sub-100ms latency with refined emotional control (via SSML prosody) and curated voice set; supports 30+ locales with native-quality synthesis.

- **Zero-Shot Voice Cloning**: Enables cloning of speaker identity (timbre, cadence, micro-expressions) from ≤10s reference audio; emotion control modulates prosodic subtleties (rhythm, tone) beyond basic pitch/speed adjustments.

- **Unified API**: Single endpoint (`/v1/audio/speech`) for streaming, voice cloning, and multilingual synthesis; example curl request demonstrates minimal integration with JSON-based input/output configuration.
