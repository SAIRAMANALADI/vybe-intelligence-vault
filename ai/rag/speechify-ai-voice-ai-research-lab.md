# SpeechifyAI - Voice AI Research Lab

## Summary

- **Simba 3.2 Model**: SpeechifyAI's flagship streaming-native TTS model achieves sub-100ms latency, ranks #1 on Artificial Analysis TTS leaderboard, and offers fine-grained emotional control via SSML prosody with expanding language coverage.

- **Zero-Shot Voice Cloning & Emotion Control**: Simba clones speaker identity (timbre, cadence, micro-expressions) from ≥10s audio and models emotion at prosody level (rhythmic/tonal patterns) across multiple locales (en-US, es-MX, fr-FR, etc.).

- **Multilingual & API Integration**: Supports 30+ locales with native-quality voices, handles mixed-language input, and provides a unified API for streaming, voice cloning, and emotion control (e.g., `curl` endpoint for TTS synthesis).
