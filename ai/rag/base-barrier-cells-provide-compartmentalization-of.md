# Base barrier cells provide compartmentalization of choroid plexus, brain and CSF | Nature Neuroscience

## Summary

- **Discovery of ChP base barrier cells (BBCs):** Unique fibroblasts at the choroid plexus (ChP)-brain interface, transcriptionally similar to meningeal arachnoid barrier cells (ABCs), derived from meningeal mesenchyme, persisting lifelong and conserved across species.

- **Barrier function and molecular composition:** BBCs form a dense cellular cluster with adherens and tight junctions (e.g., *Cldn1*, *Cdh2*), restricting small molecules and immune cell movement under homeostasis but becoming permeable during inflammation.

- **Developmental origin and conservation:** BBCs originate early in development, maintain barrier integrity via junctional proteins, and demonstrate species conservation, highlighting their role in CNS compartmentalization and protection.
