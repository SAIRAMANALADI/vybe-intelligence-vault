# Nvidia is latest investor to back AV startup Nuro in $203M funding round | TechCrunch

## Summary

- **Nuro secured $203M in Series E funding**, with Nvidia joining as a new investor alongside Uber, Baillie Gifford, Icehouse Ventures, and others, bringing total funding to $2.3B and post-money valuation to $6B (down 30% from $8.6B in 2021).

- **Nuro pivoted from fleet ownership to tech licensing** in 2024, focusing on partnerships with automakers and mobility providers; Uber’s $300M+ investment in Lucid (with Nuro’s self-driving tech integration) and undisclosed multi-hundred-million-dollar investment in Nuro underscore this shift.

- **Nuro’s compute stack leverages Nvidia GPUs and Drive AGX Thor platform** for large-scale data processing and model training, reflecting deep technical collaboration with Nvidia.
