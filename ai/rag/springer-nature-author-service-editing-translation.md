# Springer Nature author service | Editing, translation & support

## Summary

- **Springer Nature Editing Service** offers tiered English language editing (Silver/Gold) with subject-matter expert reviewers, word-count-based pricing ($87+), and a quality guarantee for manuscript polishing prior to submission.

- **Scientific Editing** provides in-depth developmental review (including Gold-level language editing, strategic report, and QA checks) by PhD-level editors, priced from $1,545, to enhance research rigor and submission success.

- **AJE Services** (via Springer Nature) delivers specialized academic translation (Spanish/Portuguese/Chinese → English) with Silver-level editing, manuscript formatting to journal guidelines, and customer support for international submissions.
