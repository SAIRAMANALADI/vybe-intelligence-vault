# Medium Members Can Listen To Any Medium Story With The Speechify Play Button | Speechify

## Summary

- **Integration & Accessibility**: Medium’s "Listen" feature, powered by Speechify, enables Medium members to convert any article into audio with synchronized text highlighting, supporting multitasking (e.g., commuting, exercising) and improving information retention via dual-modal consumption.

- **Customization & Utility**: Users can adjust playback speed (0.5x–4.5x) for productivity or proofreading, with options to switch between narrators; writers leverage this for real-time draft validation by listening to shared draft links.

- **Inclusivity**: The feature lowers barriers for users with dyslexia or visual impairments, expanding accessibility while maintaining Medium’s core mission of content democratization.
