# Using generative AI to expand and enhance help center content – Zendesk help

## Summary

- **AI-Powered Content Enhancement**: Zendesk integrates OpenAI’s Enterprise GPT to provide generative AI features—**Expand**, **Simplify**, **Make more friendly**, and **Make more formal**—for refining help center content dynamically.
- **Text Expansion Workflow**: Users can input concise bullet points or notes, then use the **Expand** tool to generate detailed paragraphs, with up to five AI-generated variations available for selection.
- **Tone & Clarity Adjustments**: The **Simplify** feature reduces wordiness for technical content, while **tone modifiers** adjust conversational or professional styles, both requiring manual verification and replacement of original text.
