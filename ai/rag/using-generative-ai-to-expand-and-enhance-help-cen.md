# Using generative AI to expand and enhance help center content – Zendesk help

## Summary

- **AI-Powered Content Enhancement**: Zendesk's generative AI (via OpenAI's Enterprise GPT) provides four core content optimization tools—**Expand**, **Simplify**, **Make more friendly**, and **Make more formal**—to refine help center articles and content blocks dynamically.

- **Text Expansion Workflow**: Users can input concise bullet points or short notes, then apply the **Expand** tool to generate full paragraphs, with up to five AI-generated variations available for selection before final replacement.

- **Tone & Clarity Adjustments**: The **Simplify** feature removes excessive verbiage for technical clarity, while tone modifiers (**friendly/formal**) adapt content to align with brand voice, both supporting iterative refinement via AI suggestions.
