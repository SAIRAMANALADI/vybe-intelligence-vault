# Energy Frontier Research Centers... | U.S. DOE Office of Science (SC)

## Summary

- **Program Overview**: DOE's EFRCs are BES-funded, multidisciplinary centers addressing fundamental energy science challenges through discovery and use-inspired research, leveraging advanced tools for matter manipulation across scales and training next-gen researchers.

- **Operational History**: Since 2009, 107 centers have operated across 43 states/DC, with biennial 4-year awards starting in 2014; the 2024 class added 10 new centers (focused on advanced manufacturing, microelectronics, quantum info science, and nuclear waste management) to the existing 34, totaling 44 active centers.

- **Research Focus**: Centers target priority areas (e.g., energy storage, hydrogen, solar, quantum info science) aligned with DOE initiatives, each with a unique mission to advance foundational energy research via collaborative, multi-institutional teams.
