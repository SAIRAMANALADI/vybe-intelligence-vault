# [2601.08620] ViDoRe V3: A Comprehensive Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation in Complex Real-World Scenarios

## Summary

- **Multimodal RAG Benchmark**: ViDoRe v3 introduces a comprehensive multimodal benchmark (~26,000 document pages, 3,099 queries in 6 languages) evaluating RAG pipelines on visually rich documents (tables, charts, images) across 10 professional domains, addressing limitations of prior benchmarks focused on single-document or textual-only retrieval.

- **Key Performance Insights**: Visual retrievers outperform textual ones; late-interaction models and textual reranking significantly enhance performance; hybrid/visual contexts improve answer generation, though models still struggle with non-textual elements, open-ended queries, and fine-grained visual grounding.

- **Dataset & Annotation**: Built with 12,000 hours of human annotation, providing high-quality labels for retrieval relevance, bounding box localization, and verified reference answers; released under a commercially permissive license for public use.
