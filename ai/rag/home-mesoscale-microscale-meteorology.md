# Home | Mesoscale & Microscale Meteorology

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: MMM specializes in mesoscale (1–1000 km) and microscale (10⁻⁶–1000 m) atmospheric phenomena, collaborating with NCAR to enhance Earth-system predictability via process research and prediction of atmospheric events.

- **Key Models**: Develops and maintains high-resolution models like **MPAS** (Model for Prediction Across Scales) for Earth-system simulations and **WRF** (Weather Research & Forecasting Model) for numerical weather prediction, alongside advanced data assimilation systems (e.g., MPAS-JEDI).

- **Sections & Applications**: Organized into specialized sections (e.g., **Weather Extremes Across Scales**, **Dynamical & Physical Meteorology**) focusing on extreme weather drivers, turbulence research (GTP), and risk communication, with real-time forecasts (WRF, HRRR, MPAS) for severe storm and hazard prediction.
