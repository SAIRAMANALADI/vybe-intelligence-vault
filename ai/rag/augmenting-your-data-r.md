# Augmenting your data | R

## Summary

- Use `augment()` on a pre-fitted linear model to append fitted values (`.fitted`) and residuals to the original dataset, enabling direct comparison of observed (`life_expectancy`) and predicted values.
- Construct a visualization with `ggplot2` where `year` is the x-axis, `life_expectancy` is plotted as points, and `.fitted` is overlaid as a red line to assess model fit.
- The augmented data frame (`algeria_fitted`) is generated by passing the model object to `augment()`, facilitating diagnostic evaluation of the high adjusted \(R^2\) (0.99) via residual analysis and trend alignment.
