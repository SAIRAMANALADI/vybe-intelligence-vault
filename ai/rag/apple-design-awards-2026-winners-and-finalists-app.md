# Apple Design Awards - 2026 winners and finalists - Apple Developer

## Summary

- **grug (Ocho, Netherlands)**: A minimalist iOS app delivering daily wisdom in Neolithic grunts with hand-drawn UI, no login, and cloud syncing, leveraging SwiftUI for a Stone Age aesthetic.
- **Guitar Wiz (Bijoy Thangaraj, India)**: A SwiftUI-based iOS/iPadOS/macOS/watchOS guitar toolkit with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and Differentiate Without Color for accessibility.
- **NBA: Live Games & Scores (National Basketball Association, US)**: visionOS app enabling multi-game viewing with 180-degree immersive video, Spatial Audio, and 3D floating stats via Apple Immersive Video.
