# Russian Propaganda Infects AI Chatbots - CEPA

## Summary

- **Data Poisoning via AI-Generated Disinformation**: Russian state-backed networks (e.g., Pravda) exploit AI chatbots by flooding them with ~18,000 articles per false claim across 150 multilingual websites, leveraging statistical repetition to manipulate LLM credibility assessments (33% false narrative propagation rate per NewsGuard audit).

- **Cost-Effective Hybrid Warfare**: Russia allocates <$1B/year to information warfare (vs. $150B+ military budget), weaponizing AI-driven disinformation to amplify social divisions, erode trust in institutions, and infiltrate mainstream media—yielding disproportionate geopolitical impact.

- **Western Vulnerabilities & Countermeasures**: U.S./EU dismantled key info-defense institutions (e.g., Voice of America, FMIC), but emerging solutions like NewsGuard’s real-time disinformation feeds and blockchain-based provenance tracking aim to harden AI systems against adversarial manipulation.
