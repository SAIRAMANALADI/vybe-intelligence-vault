# kubernetes/kubernetes

## Summary

- **Core Functionality**: Kubernetes is a production-grade, open-source system for automating the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications (e.g., Docker, rkt) across clusters of hosts.

- **Architecture**: Composed of a **control plane** (API server, scheduler, controller manager, etcd) and **worker nodes** (kubelet, kube-proxy, container runtime), enabling declarative configuration, self-healing, and service discovery.

- **Ecosystem & Integration**: CNCF-hosted, written in Go, with extensive APIs (REST/gRPC), supports multi-cloud deployments, and integrates with CNI (networking), CSI (storage), and OCI-compliant runtimes.
