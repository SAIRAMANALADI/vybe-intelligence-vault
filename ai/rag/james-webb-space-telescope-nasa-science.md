# James Webb Space Telescope - NASA Science

## Summary

- **Orbit & Positioning**: JWST operates at the Sun-Earth L2 Lagrange point (~1.5M km from Earth), enabling stable, sunshield-protected observations with minimal infrared interference.

- **Instrument Suite**: Equipped with NIRCam, MIRI, NIRSpec, and FGS/NIRISS, covering 0.6–28 µm wavelengths for high-sensitivity imaging/spectroscopy of early universe, exoplanets, and stellar evolution.

- **Deployment & Sunshield**: Deploys origami-style (5-layer sunshield, SPF 1M) to block solar/terrestrial infrared radiation, enabling cryogenic operation (~40K) for unparalleled IR sensitivity.
