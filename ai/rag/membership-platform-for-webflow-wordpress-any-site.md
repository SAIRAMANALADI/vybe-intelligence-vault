# Membership Platform for Webflow, WordPress & Any Site | Memberstack

## Summary

- **Integration & Deployment**: Memberstack enables drop-in authentication, Stripe payments, and content gating for existing websites (Webflow, WordPress, custom code) with no backend development, typically deployable in under 30 minutes and fully live within a weekend.

- **Pricing & Scalability**: Free until launch with no credit card required; post-launch, pricing scales with member count (2-4% transaction fee + Stripe fees), supporting tiered subscriptions, free trials, and one-time charges.

- **Core Features**: Provides managed authentication (email, social logins, 2FA), Stripe billing integration, granular content gating (pages/sections/elements), and custom member data storage, eliminating the need for custom auth, billing, or compliance maintenance.
