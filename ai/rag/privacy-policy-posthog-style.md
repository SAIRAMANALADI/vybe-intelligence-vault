# Privacy policy, PostHog style

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Legal Basis**: PostHog collects technical data (IP address, device info, page views) via first-party cookies and its own tools (e.g., Session Replay) for analytics and product improvement, acting as the **Data Controller** under GDPR/UK law. Legal basis includes **legitimate interest** (analytics) and **contract fulfillment** (user requests).

- **Open-Source & Public Data**: As an open-source project, some user-provided data (e.g., contributions, public profiles) is shared publicly, but PostHog minimizes personal info collection and adheres to **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** for third-party data sharing.

- **Opt-Out & Compliance**: Users can opt out of self-managed instance tracking; PostHog avoids sensitive data (e.g., genetic/health info) and enforces **age restrictions (18+)**. Aggregated data is used for internal analytics and promotional reporting.
