# What Is Retrieval Augmented Generation? | deepset Blog

## Summary

- **RAG Architecture**: Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retrieval module to fetch relevant documents from a database with a generative LLM to produce answers grounded in those documents, reducing hallucinations and improving factual accuracy.

- **Context Window Utilization**: RAG dynamically injects only the most relevant retrieved documents into the LLM's prompt, overcoming the limitations of fixed context windows while ensuring responses are based on vetted, domain-specific data.

- **Operational Advantages**: RAG enables domain-specific expertise by transforming LLMs into reliable, fact-checked assistants for enterprise applications (e.g., legal, technical, or news domains) without requiring retraining of the base LLM.
