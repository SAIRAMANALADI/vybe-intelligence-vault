# Tag-teaming turbulence: Frontier trains AI to model cosmic storms

## Summary

- **Frontier supercomputer (ORNL)** enabled training of a hybrid AI model combining **physics-informed neural operators (PINOs)** and **score-based diffusion models** to simulate magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) turbulence in plasmas at unprecedented exascale computational speeds (2 exaflops).
- **Two-stage AI framework** separated large-scale plasma evolution (handled by PINOs) from fine-scale turbulence details (reconstructed via diffusion models), reducing prediction errors by >50% and enabling real-time simulations (seconds) even under extreme turbulence.
- **Key breakthrough**: First AI model to faithfully replicate MHD turbulence at extreme conditions, validated against high-fidelity plasma simulations, with applications in astrophysics (e.g., supernova modeling) and fusion energy research.
