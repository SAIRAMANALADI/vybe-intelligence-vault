# django-allauth

## Summary

- **Authentication Framework**: django-allauth is a Django package providing integrated solutions for authentication, registration, account management, and third-party (social) account authentication, supporting both traditional and multi-factor authentication (MFA) workflows.

- **Modular Architecture**: The package is structured into distinct modules including `account` (user management), `socialaccount` (OAuth/OIDC providers), `mfa` (WebAuthn, TOTP), `usersessions` (session control), and `headless` (API-first authentication), each with configurable adapters and signals.

- **Extensible & Headless Support**: Supports headless authentication via REST APIs, cross-origin resource sharing (CORS), token-based strategies, and OpenID Connect identity provider functionality, with comprehensive rate-limiting and email/phone verification capabilities.
