# Medium

## Summary

- **Earnings Model**: Medium's Partner Program compensates writers based on member engagement metrics (30+ second read time, claps, highlights, replies, follows) and Boosted story performance, prioritizing long-form, high-quality content over clickbait.

- **Quality Metrics**: Earnings are calculated using total views, read duration, and reader interactions (claps, highlights, replies, new follows), with additional bonuses for follower retention and Boosted content.

- **Application & Support**: Writers apply via [Partner Program Application](https://medium.com/partner-program-application), with earnings details available in the [Partner Program Guide](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011694187-Getting-started-with-the-Partner-Program) and support via [Help Desk](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).
