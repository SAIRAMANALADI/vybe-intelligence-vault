# About AI2050 - AI2050

## Summary

- **AI2050 Initiative**: A Schmidt Sciences program funding interdisciplinary AI research to address "hard problems" via closed-nomination awards, fostering global collaboration among researchers at various career stages.

- **Open-Source Research Outputs**: AI2050 mandates open-source publication of funded work, including Fellows' research and collaborations, to maximize societal impact and knowledge dissemination.

- **Schmidt Sciences Focus Areas**: Funds research in AI/advanced computing, astrophysics/space, biosciences, climate, and cross-disciplinary "science systems" to accelerate solutions for real-world challenges.
