# Experience AI

## Summary

- **Multi-lingual AI education platform** developed by Raspberry Pi Foundation and Google DeepMind, offering localized AI/ML resources in **24+ languages** for ages 11–14, with regional variants (e.g., `en-IN`, `pt-BR`, `es-419`).

- **Structured educational framework** including **free lesson plans, slide decks, worksheets, videos**, and a **3-week online course** covering AI fundamentals, ethical considerations, and hands-on ML model training via an open-ended AI app challenge.

- **Global scaling initiative** with **180+ countries reached**, leveraging **local partnerships** to provide translated, culturally adapted resources, teacher training, and impact tracking via an **annual report** quantifying educator/learner engagement.
