# Solve issues faster with AI Assistance in Crashlytics

## Summary

- **AI-powered crash analysis**: Integrates Google's Gemini model with Firebase Crashlytics to provide automated, root-cause insights for app crashes by analyzing stack traces, variant data, and trends (e.g., custom keys) directly in the Crashlytics dashboard.

- **Multi-layered troubleshooting guidance**: Generates human-readable explanations of errors, debugging steps (including commands/code snippets), actionable resolution paths, and best practices to prevent recurrence, leveraging Firebase's mobile development expertise.

- **Access control & prerequisites**: Requires user roles (owner/editor/Cloud AI Companion) and acceptance of Gemini terms; insights are surfaced in the "Insights" section of the Crashlytics issue detail page for Apple/Android apps.
