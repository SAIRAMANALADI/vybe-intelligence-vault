# AI for Research | Scite

## Summary

- **Smart Citation Analysis**: Scite indexes 1.6B+ citations across 300M+ scholarly articles, patents, clinical trials, and datasets, using **Smart Citations** to classify whether later research supports, contradicts, or discusses a claim, enabling evidence-based evaluation of scientific literature.

- **Full-Text & Licensed Database Access**: Operates on the largest licensed AI research database with direct agreements with 40+ publishers (e.g., Wiley, SAGE), enabling **full-text search** beyond abstracts and bypassing paywalls to extract verifiable evidence.

- **AI-Assisted Verification & Integration**: Provides **AI Assistant** answers grounded in specific paper sentences with direct citations, integrates with tools like Zotero and MCP-compatible systems (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude), and offers an API for custom workflows, ensuring traceable, non-hallucinated research outputs.
