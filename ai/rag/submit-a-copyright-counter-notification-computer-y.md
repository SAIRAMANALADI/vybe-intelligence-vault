# Submit a copyright counter notification - Computer - YouTube Help

## Summary

- **Counter Notification Process**: Submit via YouTube Studio (videos) or email/fax/mail (videos/non-video content) with required legal statements, contact info, and rationale for reinstatement, including direct links to removed content in specified URL formats.

- **Legal & Jurisdictional Requirements**: Must include consent to Federal District Court jurisdiction and a sworn declaration under penalty of perjury asserting the removal was a mistake or misidentification (e.g., fair use, public domain).

- **Claimant Response & Reinstatement**: Claimant has 10 US business days to respond with legal action evidence; if no response, content is reinstated. Counter notifications can be canceled pre-response or resubmitted if rejected due to missing data.
