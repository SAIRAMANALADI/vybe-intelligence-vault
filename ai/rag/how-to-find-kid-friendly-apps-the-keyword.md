# How to Find Kid-Friendly Apps - The Keyword

## Summary

- **Google Play introduced a "Kids" tab** featuring "Teacher approved" apps, curated with educators to ensure age-appropriateness, enrichment, and engagement for children.
- **Apps are evaluated by academic experts and teachers** based on criteria like quality of experience, educational value, and child safety, with approved apps receiving a dedicated badge.
- **The feature rolled out in the U.S.** with plans for international expansion, integrating with Google Play Pass for subscribers and offering parental controls via Family Link.
