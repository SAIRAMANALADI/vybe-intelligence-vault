# [2305.14314] QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs

## Summary

- **QLoRA** enables efficient fine-tuning of 65B-parameter LLMs on a single 48GB GPU by backpropagating gradients through a **4-bit quantized** pretrained model into **Low-Rank Adapters (LoRA)**, preserving 16-bit task performance.
- Introduces **4-bit NormalFloat (NF4)**, a statistically optimal quantization scheme for normally distributed weights, **double quantization** to compress quantization constants, and **paged optimizers** to mitigate memory spikes during training.
- Achieves **99.3% of ChatGPT’s performance** on the Vicuna benchmark with **24 hours of fine-tuning** on a single GPU, releasing **Guanaco models** and CUDA kernels for 4-bit training.
