# [2312.15503] Llama2Vec: Unsupervised Adaptation of Large Language Models for Dense Retrieval

## Summary

- **Llama2Vec** introduces two unsupervised pretext tasks—**EBAE (Embedding-Based Auto-Encoding)** and **EBAR (Embedding-Based Auto-Regression)**—to adapt LLMs (e.g., LLaMA-2-7B) for dense retrieval by training embeddings to reconstruct input sentences and predict next sentences.

- The method achieves **state-of-the-art performance** on dense retrieval benchmarks (e.g., MSMARCO, BEIR) with minimal adaptation steps, demonstrating significant improvements over fine-tuned baselines without labeled data.

- The approach is **lightweight**, requiring no architectural modifications, and leverages **unsupervised adaptation** on large text corpora (e.g., Wikipedia) to generate discriminative embeddings for semantic retrieval tasks.
