# The Sovereign AI Platform for Agents

## Summary

- **Open, modular architecture**: Haystack provides a transparent, open-source framework for building AI agents with auditable components (tools, memory, retrievers, pipelines) and no proprietary lock-in, enabling full customization and ownership of the AI stack.

- **Sovereign deployment & compliance**: Supports self-hosted or cloud deployments with built-in audit logs, RBAC, and data lineage, ensuring full control over infrastructure, data, and regulatory compliance across the AI lifecycle.

- **Production-grade resilience**: Designed for scalable, observable, and versioned AI systems with explicit agent behavior control, enabling optimization for performance, cost, and innovation without platform dependency constraints.
