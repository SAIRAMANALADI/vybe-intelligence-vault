# The Sovereign AI Platform for Agents

## Summary

- **Modular AI Framework**: Haystack provides an open-source, extensible framework for building custom AI pipelines (RAG, agents, IDP, search) with full control over models, tools, and infrastructure, enabling transparent, adaptable deployments across cloud, VPC, on-premise, or air-gapped environments.

- **Enterprise-Grade RAG & Agents**: Supports high-precision retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with hybrid search, multimodal inputs, and deterministic orchestration, while enabling multi-step agent workflows with guardrails, human-in-the-loop approvals, and production-grade monitoring for mission-critical use.

- **Governed & Scalable Deployment**: Offers enterprise platforms with built-in governance (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA), visual pipeline building, and scalable indexing, ensuring secure, compliant, and high-performance AI applications across distributed data sources.
