## Summary

- **Enterprise-Grade Platform**: Offers scalable, governance-ready solutions with expert consulting, deployment guidance, and modular tooling for data indexing, pipeline building, testing, and governance, proven in production by enterprises like Airbus, Die Zeit, and YPulse.
