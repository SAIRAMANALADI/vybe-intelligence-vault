# Chapter 1 - Circular 92 | U.S. Copyright Office

## Summary

- **Definitions**: U.S. Copyright Law (Title 17) defines key terms such as "copies" (material objects embedding works), "phonorecords" (audio-only fixed works), "audiovisual works" (image sequences with/without sound), and "derivative works" (transformative adaptations of preexisting works).

- **Fixed Works**: A work is "fixed" when its embodiment in a tangible medium is stable enough for perception/reproduction beyond transitory duration; simultaneous fixation applies to transmitted works (e.g., live streams).

- **International Agreements**: Copyright treaties recognized by U.S. law include the Berne Convention, Geneva Phonograms Convention, WIPO Copyright Treaty, and WIPO Performances and Phonograms Treaty, among others.
