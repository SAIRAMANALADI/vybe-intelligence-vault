# [2402.14207] Assisting in Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch with Large Language Models

## Summary

- **STORM system**: A multi-stage LLM-based framework for generating Wikipedia-like articles from scratch, addressing pre-writing challenges via:
  - *Diverse perspective discovery* (topic decomposition),
  - *Multi-agent simulated interviews* (perspective-driven Q&A with retrieval grounding),
  - *Outline curation* (synthesis of retrieved insights into structured plans).

- **FreshWiki dataset**: A curated benchmark of recent Wikipedia articles designed for evaluating outline quality, featuring:
  - *Pre-writing stage assessments* (organization, coverage breadth),
  - *Expert feedback integration* (Wikipedia editor evaluations).

- **Performance metrics**: STORM outperforms retrieval-augmented baselines with:
  - **25% absolute increase** in article organization,
  - **10% absolute increase** in topic coverage,
  - Identified challenges: *source bias transfer* and *fact over-association*.
