# ChatGPT Plans | Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise

## Summary

- **Model Tiers & Context Windows**: Free tier uses GPT-5.6 Luna with 27K context window; Pro tier supports GPT-5.6 Sol Pro (400K context) and GPT-5.6 Terra (128K context), enabling processing of ~680 pages of text.

- **Feature Access by Plan**: Codex access expands from limited in Free/Go to expanded in Pro; voice/video features unlock progressively (Free: basic voice, Go+: voice with video); memory/storage limits scale from limited (Free) to expanded (Plus/Pro).

- **Advanced Capabilities**: Pro tier offers 5x-20x usage limits, unlimited high-speed image generation, maximum deep research, and expanded Codex tasks; Plus/Pro include scheduled tasks, custom GPTs, and early feature access.
