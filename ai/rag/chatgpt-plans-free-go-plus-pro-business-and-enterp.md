# ChatGPT Plans | Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise

## Summary

- **Model Tiers & Context Windows**: Free tier supports GPT-5.5 Instant with 27K context window, while Pro tier offers GPT-5.6 Sol Pro with 400K context window (~680 pages input max) and 128K total context window (~250 pages input max).

- **Feature Accessibility**: Voice/video, plugins, and memory are universally available across plans, but advanced features like Codex (expanded in Pro), scheduled tasks (Plus/Pro), and interactive apps (Plus/Pro) are tier-locked.

- **Enterprise & Business Plans**: Business/Enterprise tiers include company knowledge, internal tool integration, and workspace agents, with Pro offering expanded Codex usage and 5x/20x higher usage limits subject to abuse guardrails.
