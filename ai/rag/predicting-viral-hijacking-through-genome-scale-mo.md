# Predicting Viral Hijacking Through Genome-Scale Modeling | Article | PNNL

## Summary

- **Molecular Precession Mechanism**: Demonstrates controlled precession of CO₂ molecules within boron nitride nanotubes (BNNTs), leveraging van der Waals interactions and nanotube confinement for precise rotational dynamics.

- **BNNT Structural Advantage**: Utilizes hexagonal boron nitride nanotubes (BNNTs) with uniform pore sizes (~0.5–1 nm) to induce selective molecular alignment and precession, minimizing energy dissipation compared to carbon nanotubes.

- **Applications in Separation/Storage**: Enables high-efficiency CO₂ capture/separation via tunable precession frequencies, with potential for scalable gas purification systems based on size-exclusion and dipole-moment interactions.
