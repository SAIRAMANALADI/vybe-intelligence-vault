# AI and Cloud Computing Services | Google Cloud

## Summary

- **Google AI Threat Defense**: Introduces an AI-driven security framework leveraging agentic defense mechanisms to proactively detect, analyze, and neutralize advanced cyber threats in real-time, integrating with Google Cloud’s threat intelligence and Mandiant services.

- **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform**: A fully-managed, unified platform for developing, orchestrating, and governing AI agents, supporting multi-agent architectures, remote MCP servers, and integration with 200+ foundation models (e.g., Gemini 3.5) for enterprise-grade automation and governance.

- **AI Infrastructure Scaling**: Google Cloud’s AI Hypercomputer architecture enables scalable deployment of agentic workloads, combining custom TPUs/GPUs, Arm-based Axion processors, and optimized data pipelines (e.g., BigQuery, Spanner) to support high-throughput, low-latency generative AI applications.
