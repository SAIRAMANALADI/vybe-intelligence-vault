# Google Play Terms of Service

## Summary

- **Account & Access Control**: Google Play requires a valid Google Account with age restrictions enforced per regional laws; minors need parental consent. Unauthorized account sharing or data harvesting is prohibited, and disabled accounts lose access to all associated Content and family-sharing features.

- **Content & Security Policies**: Google Play provides Content (apps, media, etc.) with updates auto-applied, including critical security patches. Malware protection scans apps/URLs, with warnings or blocks for unsafe content, though users can disable some protections. Instant Apps stream portions of apps temporarily via downloaded code.

- **Purchases & Subscriptions**: All sales are final per Google Play’s Refund Policy. Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled before the trial period ends. Payments are processed via Google Payments or third-party methods, with taxes and carrier billing eligibility determined by regional laws and network provider agreements.
