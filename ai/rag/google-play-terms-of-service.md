# Google Play Terms of Service

## Summary

- **Account & Age Compliance**: Users must possess a valid Google Account, adhering to regional age restrictions; minors require parental/guardian consent. Family groups share limited user data (name, email, age) and purchase records via a designated family payment method.

- **Content & Security Policies**: Google Play enforces automated updates (including critical security patches) and malware scanning for apps/URLs, with optional user-disablable protections. Unauthorized data harvesting or account sharing violates terms, risking account termination and loss of shared family content.

- **Purchases & Subscriptions**: All sales are final (per Google Play’s Refund Policy), with taxes and carrier billing eligibility determined by device/network identifiers. Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled ≥24h pre-billing cycle, with trial periods requiring explicit opt-out to avoid charges.
