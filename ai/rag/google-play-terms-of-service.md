# Google Play Terms of Service

## Summary

- **Content Access & Responsibilities**: Google Play provides access to digital content (apps, games, books) with system/connectivity requirements, third-party fees, and automatic updates (including critical security patches). Users must comply with age restrictions, maintain account security, and are liable for data/access fees from providers or carriers.

- **Purchases & Payments**: Transactions are governed by separate contracts with Google or third-party providers, with pricing subject to change. Family payment methods require a designated payment method, and taxes are user’s responsibility. Refunds follow Google’s [Refund Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

- **Data & Security**: Google may analyze apps/URLs for malware, share user data (e.g., name, email) with providers for transactions, and sync instant app data across devices. Users can disable some protections but cannot opt out of all security monitoring.
