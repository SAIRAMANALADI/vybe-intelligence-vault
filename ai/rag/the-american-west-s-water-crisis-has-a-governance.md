# The American West's water crisis has a governance problem | Stanford Woods Institute for the Environment

## Summary

- **Nature-based solutions (NBS)** integrate ecological systems (e.g., wetlands, floodplains) with engineered infrastructure to enhance water resilience, flood mitigation, and habitat restoration while addressing climate change impacts.

- **Water rights in California** face challenges due to fragmented legal frameworks, historical allocations, and competing demands (agriculture, urban, ecology), necessitating policy reforms to prioritize flexible, adaptive management and NBS integration.

- **Felicia Marcus highlights** the need for collaborative governance, incentive structures (e.g., water markets, conservation funding), and cross-sector partnerships to scale NBS adoption and reconcile water rights with ecological sustainability goals.
