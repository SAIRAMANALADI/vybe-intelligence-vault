# Ghost: The best open source blog & newsletter platform

## Summary

- **Ghost 6.0** is a headless CMS and publishing platform enabling professional publishers to build, customize, and monetize websites with native newsletter, membership, and subscription tools, leveraging a modern tech stack for scalability.

- **Advanced Creator Tools** include a distraction-free WYSIWYG editor with dynamic content cards (galleries, embeds, accordions), built-in email newsletter delivery, audience segmentation, and native analytics for engagement tracking and revenue optimization.

- **Integrated Business Workflow** supports Stripe payments, Zapier automation, third-party integrations (e.g., Unsplash, Google Analytics), and offers 0% transaction fees, with case studies showing $100M+ annual revenue across 1M+ publications.
