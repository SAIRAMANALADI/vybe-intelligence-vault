# Unlimited, Flexible & Group Phone Plans & Rates - Google Fi Wireless

## Summary

- **Multi-network switching**: Google Fi dynamically switches between T-Mobile, US Cellular, and Wi-Fi networks to optimize coverage and performance, leveraging **Spectrum Access System (SAS)** for CBRS band utilization in supported areas.
- **Usage-based billing**: The **Flexible plan** charges $10/GB with **Bill Protection** capping overages at $60/month per line, while **Unlimited plans** throttle speeds to **256 kbps** after high-speed data limits (100GB/50GB/30GB per line depending on tier).
- **Cross-device integration**: Supports **data-only SIMs** for tablets/laptops and **smartwatch connectivity** via **eSIM** or **standalone data plans**, with **Google One (100GB)** and **YouTube Premium (6 months)** included in higher-tier plans.
