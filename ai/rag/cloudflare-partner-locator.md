# Cloudflare Partner Locator

## Summary

- **Partner Classification**: Cloudflare partners are categorized by roles such as **Solution Provider** (trusted advisors building Cloudflare-based solutions), **MSSP** (managed security service providers), and **Distributor** (enabling partners with services/support).

- **Specialization Designations**: Partners can hold specialized designations like **Cloudflare One Design Partner**, **Application Security ASD Specialized**, or **Cloudflare One ASD Specialized**, indicating advanced capabilities in assessments and deployments.

- **Geographic Coverage**: Partners span **50+ countries**, including Argentina, Brazil, Germany, Japan, and the U.S., with filtering options to locate regional providers.
