# High Priority Free Software Projects — Free Software Foundation — Working together for free software

## Summary

- **Free Phone OS Development**: Critical need for fully free, open-source mobile operating systems to replace proprietary platforms, ensuring software freedom across widely used personal computers.

- **Decentralized & Self-Hosted Infrastructure**: Focus on reducing reliance on proprietary SaaS by advancing federation, decentralization, and self-hosting solutions to mitigate SaaSS risks.

- **Free Hardware & Firmware Support**: Prioritize development of libre drivers, firmware, and hardware designs to eliminate proprietary dependencies, enabling full compatibility with free software systems.
