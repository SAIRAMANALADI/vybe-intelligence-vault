# Why Billionaire Wendy Schmidt Is ‘Doubling Down’ On Climate Science In The Age Of Trump

## Summary

- **Schmidt Ocean Institute (SOI) funding**: Provides $1.5M+ per expedition (e.g., Argentina deep-sea coral forests discovery) via ship time, crew, and tech, enabling high-risk marine research inaccessible through public funding; data shared openly.

- **Philanthropic scaling under political pressure**: Wendy Schmidt’s $450M+ investment in SOI and related initiatives (Schmidt Sciences, 11th Hour Racing) offsets federal climate funding cuts, sustaining climate science, biodiversity tracking, and data-sharing tools.

- **Interdisciplinary and public engagement focus**: Schmidt Science Fellows ($110K stipend) and grants (e.g., AI-driven sea-level modeling, Keeling Curve bridge funding) prioritize cross-disciplinary collaboration and public visibility (e.g., 19M+ views for Argentina expedition livestreams).
