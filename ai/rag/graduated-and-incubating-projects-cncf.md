# Graduated and Incubating Projects | CNCF

## Summary

- **Graduated Projects**: CNCF graduated projects (e.g., Kubernetes, Prometheus, Envoy) are stable, production-ready, and meet strict maturity criteria including adoption, governance, and community health.

- **Incubating Projects**: CNCF incubating projects (e.g., KubeVirt, Karmada, Kyverno) are under active development, demonstrating growing adoption and community support but require further validation for production stability.

- **Project Metrics**: CNCF provides quantitative metrics (e.g., contributor diversity, commit velocity, adoption rates) to assess project maturity and stability across graduated and incubating stages.
