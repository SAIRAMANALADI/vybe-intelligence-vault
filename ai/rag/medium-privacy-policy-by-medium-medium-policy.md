# Medium Privacy Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Medium collects user-provided data (e.g., profile info, posts, interactions) and automatically gathers device/usage data (IP, OS, browser, activity logs) via cookies, web beacons, and third-party analytics tools.
- **Data Sharing & Processing**: Personal data is shared with vendors for services (e.g., hosting, analytics, fraud prevention) and may be disclosed under legal obligations or during corporate transactions; aggregated/de-identified data is exempt.
- **Cross-Border Transfers**: User data is transferred/stored in the U.S. and other jurisdictions with varying privacy protections, relying on mechanisms like AWS global infrastructure with "adequate safeguards."
