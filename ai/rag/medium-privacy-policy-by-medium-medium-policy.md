# Medium Privacy Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Medium collects user-provided data (e.g., posts, profile info) and automatically gathers activity, device, and usage data (IP addresses, cookies, device identifiers) via tracking technologies like web beacons and third-party analytics providers.
- **Data Sharing & Third-Party Access**: Personal data is shared with vendors for services (e.g., hosting, analytics) and may be disclosed under legal obligations; third-party embeds (e.g., YouTube) transmit user interactions directly to their servers, bypassing Medium’s policy.
- **Cross-Border Data Transfers**: User data is transferred/stored globally (e.g., AWS data centers), with safeguards for adequacy; users can access/delete account data but deletion may retain legally required records.
