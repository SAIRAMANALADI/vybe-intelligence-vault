# Medium Privacy Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Sources**: Medium collects personal data directly (e.g., account details, user-generated content) and automatically (e.g., IP addresses, device identifiers, cookies) from interactions, third-party platforms (e.g., social media logins), and analytics providers. Payment data is processed via third-party processors.

- **Data Sharing & Legal Basis**: Personal data is shared with service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics), other users (e.g., posts, comments), and under legal obligations (e.g., law enforcement requests). Aggregated/de-identified data may also be shared. Embedded third-party content (e.g., YouTube) transmits user data directly to those platforms.

- **Data Retention & User Control**: User data is retained for legal/business purposes even after account deletion. Users can access, correct, or delete account data via settings. Cookie preferences and communications (e.g., newsletters) can be adjusted, but administrative emails remain. Data transfers occur globally (e.g., AWS data centers) with safeguards for cross-border compliance.
