# Medium Privacy Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Medium collects direct user data (e.g., name, email, content, avatar) and automatically gathers activity, transactional, device, and usage data via cookies, web beacons, and third-party analytics tools, including IP addresses, device identifiers, and interaction metrics.

- **Data Sharing & Processing**: Personal data is shared with third-party vendors (e.g., hosting, analytics, fraud prevention) for service delivery, security, and legal compliance; user-generated content is visible to other users. Data may be transferred internationally, including to the U.S., with safeguards for cross-border protection.

- **User Controls & Rights**: Users can access, correct, or delete account data via settings; opt out of communications, cookies, and push notifications; and exercise California-specific rights (e.g., CCPA) under applicable law. Deleted accounts may retain residual data for legal or business purposes.
