# Medium Privacy Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Medium collects user-provided data (e.g., name, email, posts, payment info via third-party processors) and automatically gathers activity, device, and usage data (IP address, device identifiers, cookies, web beacons) to personalize content, operate paywalls, and improve services.

- **Data Sharing & Third-Party Access**: Personal data is shared with vendors (e.g., analytics, hosting, fraud prevention), disclosed under legal obligations, or transferred internationally (e.g., AWS data centers). Embedded third-party content (e.g., YouTube) may collect user data independently.

- **User Rights & Opt-Outs**: Users can access, correct, or delete account data via settings; opt out of communications, cookies, or push notifications; and request deletion under CCPA. Data may persist post-deletion for legal/business needs.
