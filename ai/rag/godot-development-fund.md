# Godot Development Fund

## Summary

- **Funding Mechanism**: Godot's Development Fund supports core engine development via recurring/monthly donations (€5–€500) and one-time contributions, managed by the non-profit Godot Foundation for financial/administrative oversight.
- **Membership Tiers**: Bronze (€25) to Diamond (€500) tiers offer perks like public credits, company logos, and website links; corporate sponsorships provide additional visibility and infrastructure support.
- **Financial Transparency**: Donations fund engine development, infrastructure (hosting/test hardware), and staff (part/full-time roles), with monthly totals derived from recurring donations, corporate sponsorships, and legacy Patreon subscriptions.
