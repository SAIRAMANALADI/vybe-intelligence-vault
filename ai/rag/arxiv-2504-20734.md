# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where retrieval favors same-modality items as the query.
- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope.
- **Performance Validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, with theoretical justification for modality-aware routing effectiveness.
