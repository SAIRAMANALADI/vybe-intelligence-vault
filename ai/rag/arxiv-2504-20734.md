# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: UniversalRAG introduces a dynamic routing mechanism to select the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: The framework structures each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving adaptability across diverse knowledge sources.

- **Performance Validation**: Evaluated on 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baseline RAG systems, demonstrating superior factual accuracy and retrieval efficacy.
