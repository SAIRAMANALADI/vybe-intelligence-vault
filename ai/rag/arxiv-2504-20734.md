# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: UniversalRAG dynamically selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" problem where unified embeddings favor same-modality retrieval, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: The framework organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope across diverse modalities.

- **Performance Validation**: Evaluated on 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines, demonstrating robust cross-modal knowledge integration.
