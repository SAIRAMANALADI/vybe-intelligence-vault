# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified representations favor same-modality items over cross-modal relevance.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels (e.g., coarse-to-fine), enabling query-adaptive retrieval tailored to complexity and scope.

- **Empirical validation** demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, with theoretical justification for routing efficiency.
