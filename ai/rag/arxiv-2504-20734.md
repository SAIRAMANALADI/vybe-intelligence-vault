# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: UniversalRAG dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces bias retrieval toward the query's modality.
- **Granularity-level organization**: The framework structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.
- **Performance validation**: Evaluated on 10 benchmarks across diverse modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines.
