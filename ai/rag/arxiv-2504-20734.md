# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism**: Dynamically selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" problem where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **Performance validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, highlighting the framework's any-to-any RAG capability.
