# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

*   UniversalRAG proposes an any-to-any Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework designed for integrating knowledge from heterogeneous corpora spanning diverse modalities and granularities.
*   It introduces modality-aware routing to mitigate the "modality gap" observed in unified representation spaces, dynamically identifying and performing targeted retrieval within appropriate modality-specific corpora, supported by theoretical analysis.
*   The framework further organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to the complexity and scope of the input
