# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified embedding spaces bias retrieval toward same-modality items, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence) to tailor retrieval granularity to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Evaluated on 10 benchmarks across diverse modalities, outperforming both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines by integrating heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) with adaptive routing and granularity control.
