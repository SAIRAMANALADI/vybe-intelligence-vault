# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism**: Introduces a dynamic retrieval strategy that selects the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for a given query, mitigating the "modality gap" issue where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, enhancing precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Validated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, demonstrating superior performance over both modality-specific and unified baselines by integrating heterogeneous corpora with diverse modalities and granularities.
