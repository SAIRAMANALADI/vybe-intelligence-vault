# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Proposes a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends retrieval beyond modality by structuring each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an any-to-any RAG framework that integrates heterogeneous knowledge sources (text, images, videos) across modalities and granularities, outperforming modality-specific and unified baselines on 10 benchmarks.
