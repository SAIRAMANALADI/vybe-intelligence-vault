# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: UniversalRAG introduces a dynamic routing mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* problem where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: The framework organizes corpora into hierarchical granularity levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence) to optimize retrieval granularity based on query complexity, improving precision across diverse modalities.

- **Cross-Modal Performance**: Evaluated on 10 benchmarks spanning text, images, and videos, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines, demonstrating robust any-to-any retrieval capabilities.
