# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: UniversalRAG introduces a dynamic routing mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" where unified embedding spaces bias retrieval toward the query's modality.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: The framework structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **Benchmark Validation**: Evaluated on 10 multimodal benchmarks, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines, demonstrating superior adaptability to heterogeneous corpora.
