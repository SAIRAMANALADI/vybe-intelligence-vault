# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified representations favor same-modality retrieval, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence), enabling adaptive retrieval granularity based on query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework** outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities (text, images, videos), demonstrating robust any-to-any RAG capabilities.
