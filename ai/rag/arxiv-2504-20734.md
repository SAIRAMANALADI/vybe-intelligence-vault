# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* issue where unified embedding spaces bias retrieval toward same-modality items.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence), enabling adaptive retrieval granularity based on query complexity and scope.

- **Empirical validation** demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 diverse benchmarks, with theoretical justification for modality-aware routing via formal analysis.
