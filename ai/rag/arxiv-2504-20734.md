# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels (e.g., coarse-to-fine), enabling adaptive retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Achieves state-of-the-art performance across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines while generalizing to heterogeneous corpora.
