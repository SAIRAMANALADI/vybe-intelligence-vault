# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a mechanism to dynamically select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends retrieval by structuring each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling query-specific fine-tuning of retrieval scope and complexity.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, integrating heterogeneous corpora for any-to-any RAG.
