# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a mechanism to dynamically select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends retrieval beyond modality by structuring each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling query-specific fine-tuning of retrieval scope and complexity.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, integrating heterogeneous knowledge sources for robust RAG performance.
