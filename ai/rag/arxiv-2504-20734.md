# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified representations favor same-modality items, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels (e.g., coarse-to-fine), enabling adaptive retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework** outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines across **10 benchmarks** spanning diverse modalities, demonstrating superior generalization in any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation.
