# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the modality gap issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, with theoretical validation.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope across diverse modalities.

- **Performance Validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, confirming the framework's effectiveness in heterogeneous knowledge integration.
