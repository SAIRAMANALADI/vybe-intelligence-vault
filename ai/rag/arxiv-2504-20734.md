# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-level organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Validated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines by integrating heterogeneous knowledge sources (text, images, videos) via any-to-any RAG.
