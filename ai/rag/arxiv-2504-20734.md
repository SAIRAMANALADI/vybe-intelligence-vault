# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects modality-specific corpora to mitigate modality gap issues in unified embedding spaces, improving retrieval relevance for heterogeneous queries.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope across diverse modalities (text, images, videos).

- **Theoretical justification** provided for modality-aware routing via formal analysis, with empirical validation on 10 benchmarks demonstrating superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines.
