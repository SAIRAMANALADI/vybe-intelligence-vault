# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality items, improving cross-modal knowledge integration.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence), enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Validated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines by leveraging heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) with theoretical justification for its routing mechanism.
