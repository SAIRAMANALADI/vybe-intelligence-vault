# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing Mechanism**: Introduces a dynamic routing strategy to select modality-specific corpora, mitigating the *modality gap* issue in unified embedding spaces where retrieval favors same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving adaptability across diverse knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines on 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, integrating heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) for any-to-any RAG applications.
