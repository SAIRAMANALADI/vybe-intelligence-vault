# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.
- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels to enable fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.
- **UniversalRAG framework**: Achieves superior performance over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities.
