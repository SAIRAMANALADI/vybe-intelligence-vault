# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Proposes dynamic retrieval from modality-specific corpora to mitigate modality gap in heterogeneous RAG systems, where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes corpora into multiple granularity levels per modality, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope.

- **Performance validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, with theoretical justification for modality-aware routing.
