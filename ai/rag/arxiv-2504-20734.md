# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends RAG by structuring each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, integrating heterogeneous knowledge sources for any-to-any RAG.
