# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: UniversalRAG dynamically selects modality-specific corpora for retrieval to mitigate the *modality gap*, where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality matches, improving cross-modal knowledge integration.

- **Granularity-Level Retrieval**: The framework organizes each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling query-specific retrieval tailored to complexity and scope, enhancing precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **Performance Validation**: Evaluated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baselines, demonstrating robustness in heterogeneous corpus integration.
