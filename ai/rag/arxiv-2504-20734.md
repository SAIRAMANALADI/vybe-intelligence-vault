# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" issue where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Validated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, demonstrating superiority over both modality-specific and unified baselines in heterogeneous corpus retrieval.
