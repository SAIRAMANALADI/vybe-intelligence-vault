# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels to enable fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity, improving precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, integrating heterogeneous corpora with diverse granularities for enhanced factual grounding.
