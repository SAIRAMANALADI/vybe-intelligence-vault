# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing Mechanism**: Introduces a dynamic routing strategy to select modality-specific corpora, mitigating the "modality gap" in unified embedding spaces, where retrieval favors same-modality items as the query.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **Performance Validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 multi-modal benchmarks, with theoretical justification for the routing mechanism’s effectiveness.
