# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

*   UniversalRAG introduces an any-to-any Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework designed for corpora comprising diverse modalities and granularities, addressing limitations of single-modality RAG systems.
*   The framework employs modality-aware routing to dynamically identify and perform targeted retrieval within appropriate modality-specific corpora, mitigating the "modality gap" observed with unified representation spaces, supported by theoretical analysis.
*   Beyond modality, UniversalRAG organizes each knowledge source into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, validated across 10 multi-modal benchmarks.
