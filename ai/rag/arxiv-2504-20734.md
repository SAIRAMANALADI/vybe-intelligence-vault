# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* problem where unified embedding spaces fail to generalize across heterogeneous modalities (text, images, videos).

- **Multi-granularity retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence), enabling query-aware retrieval tailored to complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework** outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks, validated via theoretical analysis and empirical evaluation on diverse multimodal corpora.
