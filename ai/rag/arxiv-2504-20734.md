# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* problem where unified embedding spaces bias retrieval toward same-modality items.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling adaptive retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Validated across 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines while supporting *any-to-any* retrieval across heterogeneous corpora.
