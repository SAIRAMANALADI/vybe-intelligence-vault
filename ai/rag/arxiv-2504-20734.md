# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: UniversalRAG introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, improving cross-modal knowledge integration.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: The framework structures each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, enhancing precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **Performance Validation**: Evaluated on 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baselines, demonstrating its superiority in heterogeneous retrieval-augmented generation tasks.
