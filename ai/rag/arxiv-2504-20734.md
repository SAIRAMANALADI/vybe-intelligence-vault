# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Proposes a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* issue where unified representations favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving precision across heterogeneous knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Introduces an *any-to-any* RAG system supporting diverse modalities (text, images, videos) and granularities, outperforming modality-specific and unified baselines on 10 benchmarks.
