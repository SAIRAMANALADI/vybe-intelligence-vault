# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the *modality gap* problem where unified representations favor same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope, improving adaptability across diverse knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Validated on 10 benchmarks across multiple modalities, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines, with an open-source implementation available at [universalrag.github.io](https://universalrag.github.io).
