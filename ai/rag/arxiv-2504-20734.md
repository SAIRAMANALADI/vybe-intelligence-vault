# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware retrieval**: Introduces a routing mechanism to dynamically select modality-specific corpora, mitigating the *modality gap* where retrieval favors same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.
- **Multi-granularity indexing**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling adaptive retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.
- **Cross-modal RAG superiority**: Demonstrates state-of-the-art performance across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines.
