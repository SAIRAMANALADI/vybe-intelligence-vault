# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified representations favor same-modality items, as theoretically justified in the paper.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval** organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels, enabling fine-grained knowledge extraction tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework** outperforms modality-specific and unified RAG baselines across **10 benchmarks** spanning diverse modalities, demonstrating superior generalization in heterogeneous knowledge retrieval.
