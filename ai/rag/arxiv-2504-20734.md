# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" issue where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Validated across 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, demonstrating superiority over both modality-specific and unified baselines in heterogeneous knowledge integration.
