# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: UniversalRAG dynamically selects modality-specific corpora for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-level organization**: Each modality is structured into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **Performance validation**: The framework outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, demonstrating its superiority in heterogeneous knowledge integration.
