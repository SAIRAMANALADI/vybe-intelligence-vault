# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where retrieval favors same-modality items, validated through theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval** organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels, enabling fine-grained knowledge extraction tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework** outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, demonstrating superior cross-modal retrieval-augmented generation capabilities.
