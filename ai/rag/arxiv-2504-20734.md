# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified embedding spaces bias retrieval toward same-modality items.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **Performance validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, with theoretical justification for the routing mechanism.
