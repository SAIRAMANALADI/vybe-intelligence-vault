# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends retrieval by structuring each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned knowledge retrieval based on query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Validated across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, demonstrating superior performance over both modality-specific and unified baselines in retrieval-augmented generation tasks.
