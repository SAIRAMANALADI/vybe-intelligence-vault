# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Proposes a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Validated across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, demonstrating superiority over both modality-specific and unified baselines.
