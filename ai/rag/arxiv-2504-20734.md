# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- Introduces **UniversalRAG**, an any-to-any RAG framework enabling retrieval and integration of knowledge from heterogeneous sources across **diverse modalities (text, images, videos)** and **granularity levels**, addressing limitations of single-modality RAG systems.

- Proposes **modality-aware routing** to dynamically select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the **modality gap** problem where retrieval favors same-modality items, supported by theoretical analysis.

- Validates performance on **10 benchmarks** spanning multiple modalities, demonstrating superiority over both **modality-specific and unified baselines** in factual accuracy and adaptability.
