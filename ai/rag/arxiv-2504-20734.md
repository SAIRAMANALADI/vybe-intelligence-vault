# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Extends retrieval beyond modality to multiple granularity levels per modality, enabling fine-grained knowledge integration tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, integrating heterogeneous corpora via modality-aware routing and granularity-aware retrieval.
