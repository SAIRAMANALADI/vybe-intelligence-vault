# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* problem where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items over cross-modal relevance.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels (e.g., coarse-to-fine) to optimize retrieval granularity based on query complexity and scope.

- **Empirical validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, with theoretical justification for modality-aware routing.
