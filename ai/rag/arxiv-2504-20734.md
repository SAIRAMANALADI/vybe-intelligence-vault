# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism** dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified embeddings favor same-modality retrieval, with theoretical validation provided.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval** organizes each modality into hierarchical levels (e.g., coarse-to-fine), enabling adaptive retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope across 10 diverse benchmarks.

- **UniversalRAG framework** outperforms modality-specific and unified baselines by integrating heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) while preserving modality-specific retrieval efficacy, validated on 10 cross-modal benchmarks.
