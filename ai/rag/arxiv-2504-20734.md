# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- Introduces **UniversalRAG**, an any-to-any retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework enabling cross-modal knowledge retrieval from heterogeneous corpora with diverse modalities (text, images, videos) and granularities.

- Proposes **modality-aware routing** to dynamically select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality retrieval, supported by theoretical analysis.

- Validates performance on **10 benchmarks** across multiple modalities, demonstrating superiority over both modality-specific and unified RAG baselines, with granularity-aware retrieval enhancing query-specific precision.
