# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" problem where unified embeddings bias retrieval toward the query's modality.

- **Granularity-Level Organization**: Structures each modality into multiple granularity tiers, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving precision across diverse knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Validated on 10 multimodal benchmarks, outperforming both modality-specific and unified baselines by integrating heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) with theoretical justification for its routing and granularity strategies.
