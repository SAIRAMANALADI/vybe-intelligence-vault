# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" issue where retrieval favors items from the same modality as the query.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to the query's complexity and scope.

- **Performance Validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, with theoretical justification for the proposed approach.
