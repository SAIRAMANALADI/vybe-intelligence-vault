# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing mechanism**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified embedding spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated through theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple hierarchical levels (e.g., document → paragraph → sentence) to enable fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, integrating heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) via an *any-to-any* RAG paradigm.
