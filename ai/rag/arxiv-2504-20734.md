# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving performance across diverse benchmarks.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: An any-to-any RAG system supporting heterogeneous modalities (text, images, videos) and granularities, outperforming modality-specific and unified baselines on 10 benchmarks.
