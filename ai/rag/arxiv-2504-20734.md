# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most appropriate modality-specific corpus for retrieval, mitigating the *modality gap* issue where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Multi-granularity retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving precision across diverse modalities.

- **UniversalRAG framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks, supporting any-to-any RAG over heterogeneous corpora (text, images, videos) with integrated modality and granularity routing.
