# Driver Binaries for Nexus and Pixel Devices | Google Play services | Google for Developers

## Summary

- **Binary hardware support files** for Nexus and Pixel devices are provided by Google for Android Open-Source Project users, covering models from Android 10 to 15, with licenses restricting redistribution or unauthorized use.

- **Vendor images** for Pixel 9a ("tegu"), Pixel 9 Pro Fold ("comet"), and Pixel 9 Pro ("caiman") are available across multiple Android versions (14.0.0–15.0.0), each with unique build identifiers, download links, and SHA-256 checksums for integrity verification.

- **Hardware component binaries** include vendor images and proprietary drivers from manufacturers like Qualcomm, Broadcom, LG, ASUS, NVIDIA, Imagination Technologies, and Samsung, supporting older Nexus and Pixel models.
