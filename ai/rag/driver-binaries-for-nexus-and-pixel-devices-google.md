## Summary

- **Binary Hardware Support Files**: Google provides proprietary vendor images and hardware support binaries for Nexus/Pixel devices (Android 10–15), including Qualcomm, Broadcom, and Samsung components, licensed for personal use only with redistribution prohibited.

- **SHA-256 Verified Downloads**: Each vendor image includes SHA-256 checksums (e.g., `ccece1b1cbb147508ae17daee83192195a5c75eff5e7953c092016ce12901567` for Pixel 9a BD4A.240925.111) to ensure file integrity during AOSP integration.

- **Device-Specific Releases**: Pixel 9 series (e.g., `tegu`, `comet`, `caiman`) offers per-build vendor images (e.g., `AP3A.241005.015` for Pixel 9 Pro) with Google-hosted `.tgz` archives and versioned firmware updates.
