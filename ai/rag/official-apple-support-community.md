# Official Apple Support Community

## Summary

- **iCloud Drive is not a physical storage device** but a cloud-based file management system integrated with macOS and iOS, syncing files across devices via Apple's servers.
- **Files stored in iCloud Drive are not locally duplicated by default** unless explicitly downloaded; instead, they are referenced and streamed as needed, optimizing storage.
- **iCloud Drive relies on Apple's file provider framework**, enabling seamless integration with Finder and third-party apps while maintaining real-time sync and versioning.
