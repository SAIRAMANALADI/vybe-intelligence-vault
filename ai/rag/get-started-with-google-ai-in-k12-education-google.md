# Get Started with Google AI in K12 Education : Google for Education

## Summary

- **Gemini Integration**: Enables AI-assisted workflows in Google Workspace for Education (Docs, Gmail, Slides) to enhance productivity and creativity for K12 professionals.
- **Free Tools**: Provides free access to Gemini app and NotebookLM for educators/students with Google Workspace for Education accounts, offering time-saving and creative learning solutions.
- **Premium Features**: Unlocks advanced AI capabilities in Google Workspace (e.g., Docs, Classroom) via Gemini for Workspace, requiring a paid Google AI Pro for Education license.
