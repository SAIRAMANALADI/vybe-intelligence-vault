# ACP - 2019â2024 trends in African livestock and wetland emissions as contributors to the global methane rise

## Summary

- **African methane emissions (2019–2024)**: Total surface fluxes estimated at **71–72 Tg a⁻¹**, with **28–32 Tg a⁻¹ from wetlands** and **23–25 Tg a⁻¹ from livestock**, based on blended TROPOMI+GOSAT satellite observations and monthly analytical inversions at **50 km resolution**.

- **Trend attribution**: African emissions increased by **19–21 Tg a⁻¹** over 2019–2024, contributing **27% of the global methane rise (2019–2021)**, driven by **11 Tg a⁻¹ from livestock**, **4.3–5.7 Tg a⁻¹ from wetlands**, and **2.5–2.8 Tg a⁻¹ from waste**, with wetland emissions surging in **2020 and 2024**.

- **Model discrepancies**: Bottom-up wetland models (WetCHARTs-CYGNSS, LPJ-EOSIM-MERRA2) **underestimated emissions in South Sudan/Lake Chad** and **overestimated in the Congo Basin**; CYGNSS inundation data aligned with optimized wetland emission patterns, challenging prior assumptions on inundation extent.
