# Open Subscription Platforms

## Summary

- **Ownership & Control**: Open subscription platforms (e.g., Ghost, Podia, WooCommerce) provide full access to customer payment/subscription data and content exports, enabling business portability; closed platforms (e.g., Substack, Patreon, Medium) restrict data access, creating vendor lock-in.

- **Technical Architecture**: Open platforms leverage open-source frameworks (e.g., Ghost, WooCommerce) or Stripe Connect for payments, ensuring data sovereignty; closed platforms use proprietary systems with no API/data export for payments/customers.

- **Data Export Capabilities**: Open platforms explicitly support exports for content, email addresses, customers, and payments (✔️); closed platforms restrict exports to only content/email addresses (❌ for customers/payments).
