# Helping kids and teens learn and grow online on Safer Internet Day

## Summary

- **Enhanced Parental Controls**: Google Family Link now consolidates device management into a single interface with usage summaries, time limits, and adjustable controls; YouTube introduces streamlined supervised account creation, Shorts time limits (including zero), and custom Bedtime/Take a Break reminders for users under 18.

- **AI-Driven Protections & Content Curation**: YouTube employs age estimation via ML to tailor viewing experiences, limits repetitive problematic content recommendations for teens, and enforces default-on wellbeing features (e.g., 60-minute Take a Break triggers); new teen-focused content principles prioritize high-quality, age-appropriate videos in recommendations.

- **Education & Partnership Initiatives**: Launched AI literacy guides (Be Internet Awesome) for grades 2-8 and introduced Guided Learning in Gemini to foster critical thinking; expanded global partnerships (e.g., National PTA, Education for Sharing) aim to train 200,000 families and practitioners in online safety tools.
