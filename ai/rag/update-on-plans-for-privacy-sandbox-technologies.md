# Update on Plans for Privacy Sandbox Technologies

## Summary

- **Retirement of Privacy Sandbox APIs**: Chrome and Android will phase out **Attribution Reporting API**, **IP Protection**, **On-Device Personalization**, **Private Aggregation**, **Protected Audience**, **Protected App Signals**, **Related Website Sets**, **SelectURL**, **SDK Runtime**, and **Topics** due to low adoption and ecosystem feedback.

- **Continued Support for Select APIs**: **CHIPS**, **FedCM**, and **Private State Tokens** will be maintained and enhanced, with ongoing collaboration in web standards processes (e.g., W3C PAT WG) for interoperable solutions like the Attribution standard.

- **Focus on Measurement and User Experience**: Prioritization of scaled, privacy-preserving measurement solutions (e.g., Attribution standard) and exploration of features to improve web experiences and protect first-party data, based on industry input.
