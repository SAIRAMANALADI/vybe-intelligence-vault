# The Website Obesity Crisis

## Summary

- **Text-based websites are grotesquely bloated**, often exceeding the size of classic Russian literature (e.g., *War and Peace* at ~3MB) due to unnecessary JS, ads, and tracking—e.g., a Medium article on bloat (400 words) weighs **1.2MB**, while *Crime and Punishment* is **~1MB**.

- **Industry "solutions" (AMP, Instant Articles) exacerbate the problem**—Google’s AMP landing page is **4MB+** (with infinite-loading carousel video), and Facebook’s internet.org homepage transfers **>250MB** due to a hidden globe spin video, despite claiming to optimize for slow connections.

- **Minimalism is achievable but ignored**—core web vitals (critical rendering path, progressive loading) are disregarded; even performance-focused sites (e.g., Tim Kadlec’s slides) are **9MB+**, while a 2002-era **200KB template** would be rejected today despite meeting all functional demands.
