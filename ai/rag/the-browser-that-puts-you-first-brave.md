# The browser that puts you first | Brave

## Summary

- **Ad & Tracker Blocking**: Brave blocks third-party ads, trackers, and cookie consent pop-ups by default, reducing page load times by **3x-6x** while improving privacy via shields against fingerprinting and Global Privacy Control (GPC) enforcement.

- **Built-in Privacy Tools**: Includes a **premium VPN (Firewall + VPN)** encrypting all device traffic, and **Brave Search**, a privacy-focused search engine with AI answers and zero user profiling, operating independently of major tech ecosystems.

- **Open-Source & Chromium-Based**: Leverages the **Chromium core** (MPL 2.0 licensed) while adding proprietary privacy/security layers; offers optional **Basic Attention Token (BAT)** rewards for ad viewing, with **Brave Wallet** for secure crypto storage.
