# [2311.12022] GPQA: A Graduate-Level Google-Proof Q&A Benchmark

## Summary

- **Dataset Composition**: GPQA contains 448 expert-curated, graduate-level multiple-choice questions in biology, physics, and chemistry, designed to be highly challenging and "Google-proof" (resistant to web-based lookup).

- **Performance Benchmarks**: PhD-level experts achieve 65% accuracy (74% excluding clear mistakes), while non-experts score 34% despite 30+ minutes of unrestricted web access; state-of-the-art GPT-4-based models reach only 39% accuracy.

- **Scalable Oversight Objective**: The benchmark aims to enable realistic experiments for scalable oversight, addressing challenges in supervising AI systems that may surpass human capabilities in solving complex scientific problems.
