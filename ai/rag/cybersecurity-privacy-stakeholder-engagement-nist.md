# Cybersecurity & Privacy Stakeholder Engagement | NIST

## Summary

- **NIST's Cybersecurity & Privacy Stakeholder Engagement** leverages multi-format collaboration (standards development, joint federal initiatives, international partnerships, workshops, and academia-industry research) to align cybersecurity/privacy priorities with public/private sector needs.
- **Formal Forums** include the *Software and Supply Chain Assurance (SSCA) Forum* (global risk mitigation), *Federal Cybersecurity and Privacy Professionals Forum* (best-practice sharing), *Federal C-SCRM Forum* (supply chain security), and *FISSEA* (federal cybersecurity training).
- **Communities of Interest (COIs)** like the *Small Business COI*, *NCCoE* (standards-based integrations), *NICE Framework Users Group*, and *NICE Community Coordinating Council* facilitate targeted expertise exchange to address sector-specific challenges.
