# Stanford Geospatial Center | University Libraries

## Summary

- **Mission & Support**: Provides geospatial data, software, and expertise across disciplines (e.g., environmental science, public health, urban studies) via consultations, training, and shared resources, including infrastructure like [EarthWorks](http://earthworks.stanford.edu) and [Locator](http://locator.stanford.edu).

- **Lab & Access**: Hosts a GIS lab at Branner Earth Sciences Library with high-resolution workstations, digitization tools, and restricted access requiring SGC workgroup enrollment; new users must contact `stanford-geospatial@stanford.edu` for login permissions.

- **Resources & Services**: Offers platforms like ArcGIS Online, Google Earth Engine, and Planet.com for geospatial analysis; tools such as [Allmaps.org](http://allmaps.org) for georeferencing IIIF maps; and community support via Slack and Listserv for spatial data collaboration.
