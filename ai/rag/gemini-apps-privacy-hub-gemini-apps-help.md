# Gemini Apps Privacy Hub - Gemini Apps Help

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Collects user-provided inputs (prompts, files, transcripts), generated outputs (text, code, media), device/app metadata (permissions, interactions, performance metrics), and third-party service data (Connected Apps, browser context, location info).

- **Processing & Retention**: Uses data for service provision, improvement, personalization, and security; retains temporary chats (unless "Keep Activity" off) and may retain longer for model training; human reviewers analyze subsets for quality control.

- **Third-Party Integration**: Enables Connected Apps (Google/third-party) to exchange data for task automation; requires explicit user consent for personalization; adheres to respective privacy policies for external service interactions.
