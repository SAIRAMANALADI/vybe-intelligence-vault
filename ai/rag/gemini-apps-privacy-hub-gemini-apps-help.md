# Gemini Apps Privacy Hub - Gemini Apps Help

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Collects user-provided inputs (prompts, files, transcripts, feedback) and system-generated outputs (text, code, audio, images, citations) alongside device/browser metadata, Connected Apps data, location info, and interaction logs for model training and service improvement.

- **Processing & Retention**: Data is used for service provision, personalization, and AI model training, with human review for quality control; retention varies by activity state (e.g., temporary chats deleted when "Keep Activity" is off).

- **Third-Party Integration**: Connected Apps (Google/third-party) exchange data per their policies; audio features may log unintended activations for model improvement, with user-controlled opt-in/opt-out settings for data usage.
