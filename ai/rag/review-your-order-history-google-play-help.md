# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay).

- **Multi-Platform Tracking**: Transactions appear immediately on Google Play, while third-party payment method charges may take up to 10 business days to reflect, depending on the financial institution.

- **Charge Verification**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via [Google Play Support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610), and billing discrepancies are detailed in [Google’s charge documentation](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6224192).
