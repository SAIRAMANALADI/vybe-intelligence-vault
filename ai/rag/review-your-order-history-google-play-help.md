# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon` > `Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay).

- **Cross-Platform Tracking**: Non-Google Play transactions (e.g., subscriptions, other Google services) require [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) (`Activity` for orders, `Subscriptions & services` for subscriptions) on desktop/mobile.

- **Charge Timing**: Google Play charges appear immediately post-purchase; third-party payment method charges may take up to 10 business days, depending on the financial institution. Unrecognized charges can be reported via Google Play support.
