# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon` > `Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay, other Google services).

- **Cross-Platform Tracking**: Non-Google Play transactions (e.g., subscriptions, individual orders) are viewable at [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) under `Activity` (one-time purchases) or `Subscriptions & services` (recurring billing), with mobile navigation via Menu > respective sections.

- **Charge Timing & Disputes**: Google Play charges appear immediately for Play Store purchases (or within days for other payment methods, up to 10+ business days depending on financial institutions). Unrecognized charges can be reported via [Google Play support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610).
