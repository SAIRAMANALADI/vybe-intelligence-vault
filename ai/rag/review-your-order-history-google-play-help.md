# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay) which require [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) (`Activity` or `Subscriptions & services`).

- **Charge Timing**: Google Play charges appear immediately post-purchase, while third-party payment method charges may take up to 10 business days, depending on the bank or financial institution.

- **Charge Verification & Disputes**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via [Google Play support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610), and billing statement details are explained in [Google’s support documentation](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6224192).
