# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon` > `Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & Order history`), but excludes non-Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay, other Google services).

- **Multi-Platform Tracking**: Charges from Google Play typically appear immediately for Play Balance, while third-party payment methods may take up to 10 business days; unrecognized charges can be reported via Google Play’s [unauthorized charge tool](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610).

- **Subscription & Activity Separation**: On [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/), individual orders are listed under **Activity**, while subscriptions are managed under **Subscriptions & services** (desktop/mobile).
