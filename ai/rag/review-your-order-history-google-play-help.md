# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory), while non-Play transactions require [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) (`Activity` or `Subscriptions & services`).

- **Charge Timing**: Google Play charges appear immediately for Play Store purchases, while third-party payment method charges may take up to 10 business days to reflect, depending on the financial institution.

- **Discrepancy Handling**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via Google Play support, and order status can be checked via the [Google Play order status workflow](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/10945996).
