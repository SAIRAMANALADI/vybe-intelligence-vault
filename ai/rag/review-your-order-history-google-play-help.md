# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon` > `Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay).

- **Charge Timing**: Google Play charges appear immediately for Play Store purchases, while third-party payment method charges may take up to 10 business days; bank-specific delays are possible.

- **Dispute Resolution**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via [Google Play support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610); order status can be checked via [Google Play workflow](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/10945996).
