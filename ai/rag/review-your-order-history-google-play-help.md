# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon` > `Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay).

- **Payment Processing Delays**: Google Play charges appear immediately for Play balance purchases; other payment methods may take up to 10 business days (or longer) to reflect on billing statements, depending on the financial institution.

- **Transaction Verification**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via [Google Play support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610); order status can be checked via [Google Play Workflow](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/10945996).
