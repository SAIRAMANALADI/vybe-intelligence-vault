# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & Order history), excluding non-Google Play transactions (use [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) for those).

- **Charge Timing**: Google Play charges appear shortly after purchase, while other payment methods may take up to 10 business days; immediate balance changes reflect Google Play credits.

- **Dispute Resolution**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via [Google Play support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610); order status can be checked via [workflow](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/10945996).
