# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) under the same section, while non-Play Store transactions require [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) (`Activity` or `Subscriptions & services`).

- **Charge Timing**: Google Play charges appear immediately for Play Store purchases, while third-party payment methods may take up to 10 business days (or longer) to reflect, depending on the financial institution.

- **Dispute Resolution**: Unrecognized charges can be reported via Google Play’s [unauthorized charge support page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2851610), and order status can be checked via the [order status workflow](https://support.google.com/googleplay/workflow/10945996).
