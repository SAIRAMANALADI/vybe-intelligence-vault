# Review your order history - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Order History Access**: Google Play order history is accessible via the Play Store app (`Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & history`) or [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) (`Profile icon` > `Payments & subscriptions` > `Budget & Order history`), excluding non-Google Play transactions (e.g., Google Pay).

- **Transaction Timing**: Charges for Google Play purchases appear immediately, while third-party payment method charges may take up to 10 business days due to bank processing delays; unrecognized charges can be reported via Google Play support.

- **Cross-Platform Review**: Broader transaction history (including subscriptions) is available at [payments.google.com](https://payments.google.com/) via `Activity` (individual orders) or `Subscriptions & services` (recurring charges) on both desktop and mobile.
