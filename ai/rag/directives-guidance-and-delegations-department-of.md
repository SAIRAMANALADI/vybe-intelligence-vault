# Directives, Guidance, and Delegations | Department of Energy

## Summary

- **DOE Directives Program** (MA-1.2) manages the creation, maintenance, and enforcement of directives to standardize policies, requirements, and responsibilities across DOE elements and contractors, ensuring alignment with strategic goals, operational efficiency, and legal compliance.

- **Delegations Program** centralizes the administration of DOE delegations and authority designations, derived from laws, executive orders, and interagency regulations (e.g., OMB, OPM, GAO), enabling operational authority flowdown.

- **Directives & Delegations Libraries** provide searchable repositories for DOE directives ([Directives Library](https://www.directives.doe.gov/management/directives-library)) and delegations ([Delegations Library](https://www.directives.doe.gov/management/delegations-library)), with additional flowdown visualization ([Delegations Flowdown](https://www.directives.doe.gov/management/delegations-flowdown)).
