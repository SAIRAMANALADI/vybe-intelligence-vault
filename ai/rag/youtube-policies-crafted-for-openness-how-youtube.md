# YouTube Policies Crafted for Openness - How YouTube Works

## Summary

- **Policy Enforcement**: YouTube uses automated detection and human reporting to flag content violating [Community Guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9288567) or [Ad-Friendly Content Guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278), with exceptions for educational, documentary, scientific, or artistic (EDSA) contexts, including public interest content.

- **Monetization & YPP**: Eligibility for the [YouTube Partner Program (YPP)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851) requires adherence to stricter monetization policies; repeat violations of Advertiser-Friendly Guidelines result in demonetization or suspension from YPP.

- **Abuse Countermeasures**: YouTube collaborates with governments and organizations (e.g., [GIFCT](https://gifct.org/)) to combat extremist/criminal content, leveraging designations and cross-industry tools to detect and remove such material.
