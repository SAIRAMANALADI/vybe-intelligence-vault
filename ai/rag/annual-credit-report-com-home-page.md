# Annual Credit Report.com - Home Page

## Summary

- **Legal Provision**: Federal law mandates free annual credit reports from each of the three major bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) via [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com), the sole authorized source.

- **Credit Report Mechanics**: Credit reports influence loan approvals, interest rates, housing, and employment; they contain data on credit utilization, payment history, and account status, differing across bureaus.

- **Security & Identity Protection**: Regular reviews detect fraud/identity theft; the site enforces SSL encryption but warns against accessing reports via unsecured mobile networks due to SSN exposure risks.
