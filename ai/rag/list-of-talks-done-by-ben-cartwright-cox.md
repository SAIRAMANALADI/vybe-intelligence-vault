# List of Talks done by Ben Cartwright-Cox

## Summary

- **BGP vs Traceroute Discrepancies**: Research presented at Global Peering Forum 2026 and NetLdn examines inconsistencies between BGP routing paths and traceroute measurements, highlighting limitations in path visibility and diagnostic accuracy in large-scale networks.

- **IX Route Server Limitations**: Multiple talks (e.g., RIPE 91, EPF 2025, AusNOG 2025) analyze the scalability and functional boundaries of Internet Exchange (IX) route servers, demonstrating constraints in route propagation and peering efficiency.

- **Optical Signal Anomalies**: Presentations at NANOG 92, 38C3, and BalticNOG 2025 explore unconventional long-haul optical signal behaviors, including signal degradation, amplification challenges, and experimental transmission techniques over extended fiber distances.
