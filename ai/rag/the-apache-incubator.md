# The Apache Incubator

## Summary

- **Purpose & Process**: The Apache Incubator facilitates the entry of new projects ("podlings") into the Apache Software Foundation (ASF) by guiding them through governance adoption, community growth, and operational integration under **the Apache Way**, with mentorship from ASF teams and monthly board progress reports.

- **Key Responsibilities**: Manages podling lifecycle (acceptance, graduation, retirement), enforces IP clearance, and recommends graduation to top-level projects (TLPs) upon community maturity, with an average incubation duration of **1.5 years**.

- **Resources & Metrics**: Provides structured guides (e.g., [Incubation Policy](https://incubator.apache.org/policy/incubation.html), [Cookbook](https://incubator.apache.org/cookbook)) and has assisted **335 podlings** (239 graduated) with **300+ mentors**, supporting **25–50 active podlings** at any time.
