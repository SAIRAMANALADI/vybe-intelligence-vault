# Cosine similarity - Wikipedia

## Summary

- **Definition**: Cosine similarity measures the cosine of the angle between two non-zero vectors in an inner product space, quantifying similarity irrespective of magnitude (range: [-1, 1], where 1 = identical, 0 = orthogonal, -1 = opposite).

- **Mathematical formulation**: For vectors **A** and **B**, computed as:
  \[
  \text{cos}(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}}{\|\mathbf{A}\| \|\mathbf{B}\|} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n A_i B_i}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n A_i^2} \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n B_i^2}}
  \]
  often applied in high-dimensional spaces (e.g., text embeddings via TF-IDF or word2vec).

- **Applications**: Widely used in NLP (document similarity), recommendation systems, and clustering (e.g., k-means with cosine distance), optimized for sparse data via sparse dot-product algorithms.
