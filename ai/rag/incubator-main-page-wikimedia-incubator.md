# Incubator:Main Page - Wikimedia Incubator

## Summary

- **Purpose**: Wikimedia Incubator is a staging platform for testing new-language editions of Wikipedia, Wikibooks, Wikiquote, Wiktionary, and Wikivoyage before potential migration to official domains under the Wikimedia Foundation.

- **Process**: New test wikis require a valid ISO 639 language code, must adhere to naming conventions (e.g., `Wp/xxx` for Wikipedia), and undergo approval by the Wikimedia Language Committee prior to potential standalone creation.

- **Exclusions**: Wikiversity and Wikisource language editions must use Beta Wikiversity and Multilingual Wikisource, respectively; entirely new Wikimedia projects cannot be initiated on Incubator.
