# MESA | Mathematics, Engineering, Science, Achievement

## Summary

- **Program Structure**: MESA operates across three educational tiers—middle/high school (college prep), community college (transfer-ready STEM pipeline), and university (career-ready graduates)—serving ~25,000 students annually in California.

- **STEM Pipeline Outcomes**: MESA students achieve higher UC admission rates (80% vs. 67% statewide) and predominantly pursue top STEM degrees (Mechanical, Civil, Computer Science, Electrical, and Computer Engineering).

- **Industry & Alumni Network**: MESA partners with leading tech/energy firms (Google, Cisco, PG&E) to align workforce needs, while its alumni network includes high-profile roles (e.g., NASA engineer Katya Echazarreta) and dedicated platforms like **MESASpace** for mentorship and career development.
