## Summary

- **Adoption & Compliance**: Over 50% of the top 10 US banks use Stack Internal, which is ISO 27001/27701 and SOC 2 Type II certified, ensuring enterprise-grade security and compliance.

- **Data Isolation & Encryption**: Enterprise instances run in isolated Azure Virtual Networks with SQL Transparent Data Encryption (TDE) and AES-256 encryption at rest; Business plans use logical database separation.
