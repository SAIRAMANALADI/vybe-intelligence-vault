# Safeguarding your data - Analytics Help

## Summary

- **Regulatory Compliance & Roles**: Google Analytics operates as a **data processor** under GDPR/LGPD and a **service provider** under U.S. state privacy laws (e.g., CCPA), while customers act as **data controllers** retaining full control over data collection, access, retention, and deletion.

- **Data Collection & Identifiers**: Uses **first-party cookies** (randomized client IDs) and **app-instance IDs** (for mobile apps), with **PII explicitly prohibited** from being sent to Google Analytics; advertising identifiers (e.g., Ad ID, IDFA) are collected only if enabled by customers and users.

- **Data Security & Access Controls**: Google enforces **strict internal access policies** (authentication, SSL, logs) and **external threat monitoring**; data sharing is restricted to authorized settings, and retention/deletion is configurable by customers via **Data Retention controls**.
