# Safeguarding your data - Analytics Help

## Summary

- **Regulatory Compliance & Roles**: Google Analytics operates as a **data processor** under GDPR, LGPD, and US state privacy laws (e.g., CCPA), while customers act as **data controllers** retaining full control over data collection, retention, and deletion. Google provides **Data Processing Terms** and **U.S. State Privacy Laws Addendum** to formalize these roles.

- **Data Collection Mechanisms**: Uses **first-party cookies** (e.g., `gtag.js`, `analytics.js`) and **app-instance IDs** (for mobile SDKs) to track user interactions, with IP addresses processed for security/geolocation but not logged. **PII is strictly prohibited** from being sent to Google Analytics, and customers must enforce this via controls like **User-ID** and **cookie settings**.

- **User Controls & Security**: Users can **opt out** via the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout/) or manage ad personalization via [Ads Settings](http://www.google.com/settings/ads/onweb/). Google enforces **strict internal access controls** (authentication, SSL, logs) and **data retention policies**, allowing customers to customize storage duration (user/event-level data) and restrict data sharing/export to third-party integrations.
