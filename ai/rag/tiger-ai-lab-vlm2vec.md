## Summary

- **Technical Scope**: Implements benchmarks and methods for multimodal representation learning, including image/video/document retrieval, visual document retrieval, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) applications.
- **Implementation Details**: Python-based codebase supporting contrastive learning, embedding generation, and evaluation across multimodal retrieval tasks, with recent updates (July 2026) reflecting the latest *MMEB-V3* advancements.
