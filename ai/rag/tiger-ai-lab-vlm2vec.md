# TIGER-AI-Lab/VLM2Vec

## Summary

- **Multi-Version VLM Embedding Benchmark (MMEB)**: Repository hosts three evolving versions (ICLR 2025, TMLR 2026, COLM 2026) of the **VLM2Vec** framework, designed for **contrastive learning-based multimodal embeddings** across tasks like image, video, and visual document retrieval.

- **Benchmark Suite**: Implements **MMEB** (Multimodal Embedding Benchmark) to evaluate **Visual Language Models (VLMs)** on standardized tasks including **image retrieval, video retrieval, and visual document retrieval**, with a focus on **representation learning** and **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** applications.

- **Technical Stack**: Pure **Python**-based implementation leveraging **multimodal contrastive learning** for embedding generation, optimized for **scalability** and **benchmarking** across diverse retrieval tasks in multimodal AI systems.
