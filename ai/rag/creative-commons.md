# Creative Commons

## Summary

- **Merchandise Platform**: The Creative Commons Merch Store operates on Fourthwall, a third-party e-commerce platform, hosting CC-licensed apparel and products with dynamic image processing via `imgproxy` for optimized media delivery.

- **Product Lineup**: Features artist-designed T-shirts (e.g., Ellis Tolsma, Amogh Bhatnagar, Abraham Tumwine) priced at **$23** (premium) and **$10** (standard), with variants in black and solid white blend fabrics.

- **Licensing & Support**: All products leverage Creative Commons licenses; revenue partially funds CC’s nonprofit advocacy for open access to research, culture, and education. Donation tiers ($5–$20+) and newsletter subscriptions are integrated for community engagement.
