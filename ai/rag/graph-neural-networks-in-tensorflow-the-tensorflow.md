# Graph neural networks in TensorFlow — The TensorFlow Blog

## Summary

- **TensorFlow GNN 1.0 (TF-GNN)**: A production-ready library for building and training Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) in TensorFlow, supporting heterogeneous graphs with distinct node/edge types and `tfgnn.GraphTensor` as a first-class tensor type for graph-structured data.

- **Dynamic Subgraph Sampling**: TF-GNN introduces flexible APIs for dynamic/interactive subgraph sampling (in-memory or via Apache Beam) to scale GNN training to large graphs (hundreds of millions of nodes, billions of edges) by processing smaller, tractable subgraphs per batch.

- **Modeling & Training Abstractions**: Supports Keras-based high-level model templates (e.g., `MtAlbisGraphUpdate`) and low-level primitives for custom GNN architectures, alongside a `TF-GNN Runner` for orchestrating distributed training, multi-task learning, and TPU-compatible padding.
