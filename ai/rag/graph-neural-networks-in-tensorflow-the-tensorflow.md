# Graph neural networks in TensorFlow — The TensorFlow Blog

## Summary

- **TF-GNN 1.0 Release**: TensorFlow GNN (TF-GNN) 1.0 is introduced as a production-ready library for building and training Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) at scale, supporting heterogeneous graphs with distinct node/edge types and integration with TensorFlow's `tf.data` and Keras APIs via `tfgnn.GraphTensor`.

- **Dynamic Subgraph Sampling**: TF-GNN 1.0 supports dynamic, interactive subgraph sampling (in-memory or via Apache Beam) for efficient GNN training on large-scale graphs (hundreds of millions of nodes, billions of edges), enabling scalable message-passing neural networks (MPNNs) for node/edge/graph-level predictions.

- **Multi-Level Modeling & Training**: The library offers configurable GNN architectures (predefined Keras models, configurable templates, or low-level primitives) and a `TF-GNN Runner` for orchestrating distributed training, multi-task learning (e.g., unsupervised + supervised), and TPU-compatible `GraphTensor` padding.
