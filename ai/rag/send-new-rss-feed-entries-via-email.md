# Send new RSS feed entries via email

## Summary

- **Trigger Mechanism**: RSS by Zapier monitors specified RSS feeds with a 15-minute polling interval (Free plan) to detect new entries, while Email by Zapier sends outbound emails via a `zapiermail.com` address at 5/day (Free) or 10/hour (paid).
- **Email Automation**: Email by Zapier supports inbound email forwarding and read receipts, enabling event-driven workflows; outbound emails can be customized with HTML/plain text, attachments, and metadata (e.g., `From_name`, `Reply_to`).
- **Integration Constraints**: Free-tier limitations include email caps and polling delays; paid plans unlock higher throughput and real-time triggers (e.g., `New Read Receipt`).
