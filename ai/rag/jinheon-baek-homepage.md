# Jinheon Baek - Homepage

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: Specializes in machine learning for language, knowledge, and their intersections at scale, with emphasis on modeling interconnected data structures (texts and graphs) and retrieval-augmented language models for practical NLP applications.
- **Key Contributions**: Developed frameworks like *OmniRetrieval* (unified retrieval across heterogeneous knowledge sources), *Adaptive-RAG* (dynamic retrieval based on question complexity), and *UniversalRAG* (multi-modal retrieval-augmented generation), published in top-tier venues (ACL, NeurIPS, EMNLP, ICLR).
- **Industry & Academic Collaborations**: Conducted research internships at Google DeepMind, IBM Research, Microsoft Research, and Amazon Alexa AI; advised by Prof. Sung Ju Hwang at KAIST, with a Ph.D. (2026) and M.S. (2022) in AI from KAIST.
