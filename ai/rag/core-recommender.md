# CORE Recommender

## Summary

- **Plugin Integration**: CORE Recommender operates as a plugin within repositories, journal systems, and web interfaces to dynamically suggest relevant open-access articles based on the currently displayed content.

- **Open Access Validation**: The recommender ensures all suggested articles are free to read and accessible without paywalls, enhancing discoverability of open-access content.

- **Network Expansion**: Leverages a network of open-access repositories to provide cross-platform recommendations, increasing article visibility and user engagement.
