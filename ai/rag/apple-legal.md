# Apple - Legal

## Summary

- **Hardware Warranties**: Apple’s limited warranty for new/refurbished hardware products includes defined terms, limitations, and exclusions, supplementary to statutory consumer rights ([link](https://apple.com/legal/warranty/)).
- **Software Licensing**: End-user software license agreements govern Apple software/hardware acquisition; developer-specific licensing details are available via Apple’s Developer portal ([link](https://developer.apple.com/softwarelicensing/)).
