# Former Google CEO plans to singlehandedly fund a Hubble telescope replacement - Ars Technica

## Summary

- **Lazuli Space Telescope**: A 3.1m-aperture optical space telescope funded by Eric & Wendy Schmidt, launching no earlier than 2028 into a high elliptical orbit (77,000–275,000 km) to avoid LEO satellite interference; features a wide-field camera, spectrograph, and coronagraph for exoplanet atmospheric studies.

- **Modular Ground-Based Systems**: Includes the Argus Array (1,200x11" telescopes in Texas for all-sky 18th-magnitude imaging), DSA (1,600x6m radio dishes in Nevada for real-time radio source mapping), and LFAST (20x80cm mirrors in Arizona for scalable spectroscopy and biosignature detection).

- **Philanthropic Open-Science Model**: Schmidt Sciences funds all four telescopes (~$500M+ total) with rapid 5-year development cycles, AI-driven data processing, and open-access data release—bypassing traditional 25-year NASA timelines while leveraging commercial launch and modular hardware advances.
