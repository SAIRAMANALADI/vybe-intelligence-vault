# Former Google CEO plans to singlehandedly fund a Hubble telescope replacement - Ars Technica

## Summary

- **Lazuli Space Telescope**: A 3.1m optical space telescope funded by Eric Schmidt, launching ~2028 into a high elliptical orbit (77k–275k km) to avoid satellite interference, featuring a wide-field camera, spectrograph, and coronagraph for exoplanet atmospheric studies, managed by Schmidt Sciences with a 2029 science operations target.

- **Modular Ground Telescopes**:
  - **Argus Array**: 1,200x 11-inch telescopes in Texas (~8m effective aperture), imaging the entire Northern Hemisphere sky at 1Hz cadence with 18th–19th magnitude sensitivity, enabling time-domain "movies" of transient events.
  - **DSA Radio Telescope**: 1,600x 6m dishes in Nevada for high-speed radio astronomy, processing data exceeding Netflix’s global stream to map >1B radio sources every 15 minutes.
  - **LFAST**: 20x 80cm mirrors in Arizona for scalable spectroscopy, targeting biosignature detection with expandable modular design, prototype expected mid-2026.

- **Technological Enablers & Open Science Model**: Leverages AI, petabyte-scale storage, and commercial launch cost reductions; all data will be publicly accessible via open competition for scientific proposals, contrasting traditional proprietary or government-funded models.
