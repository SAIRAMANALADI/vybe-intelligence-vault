# Learning to reason with LLMs | OpenAI

## Summary

- **Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning**: OpenAI o1 employs a large-scale reinforcement learning algorithm to optimize its chain-of-thought reasoning, improving performance with both train-time and test-time compute scaling, achieving top-tier results on competitive programming (Codeforces 89th percentile), math Olympiads (AIME top 500), and PhD-level science benchmarks (GPQA).

- **Performance Benchmarks**: o1 surpasses GPT-4o across 54/57 MMLU subcategories, scores 78.2% on MMMU (vision-perception enabled), and exceeds human PhD experts on GPQA Diamond, demonstrating state-of-the-art reasoning capabilities in complex problem-solving tasks.

- **Chain-of-Thought Mechanism**: o1 generates structured, multi-step reasoning traces (e.g., cipher decoding examples) to iteratively refine strategies, correct errors, and decompose problems, enabling human-like problem-solving behavior with improved interpretability and accuracy.
