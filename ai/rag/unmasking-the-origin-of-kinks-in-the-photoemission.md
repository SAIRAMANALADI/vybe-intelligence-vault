# Unmasking the Origin of Kinks in the Photoemission Spectra of Cuprate Superconductors (Journal Article) | OSTI.GOV

## Summary

- **Correlation-enhanced electron-phonon interaction** in cuprate superconductors, calculated via *ab initio* **G W perturbation theory**, quantitatively explains the ubiquitous ~70 meV photoemission "kink" in their spectra.
- **Electron density of states (DOS)**—rather than mode-coupling strength—dominates the doping-dependent kink magnitude, revealing the **multiband nature** of cuprates.
- **G W-based simulations** resolve a two-decade-old debate by demonstrating that strong kinks arise from **self-energy effects** tied to electronic structure, not purely bosonic coupling.
