# Newest 'android-source' Questions - Stack Overflow

## Summary

- **AOSP Customization**: Building a custom Android OS based on AOSP for POS devices requires dedicated hardware and involves compiling modules like `SystemUI` with test-skipping optimizations (e.g., `mmm frameworks/base/packages/SystemUI -j$(nproc)`).
- **Non-Rooted Android Networking**: Configuring iptables-based whitelists on non-rooted Android 15+ devices is constrained by SELinux policies and requires alternative approaches (e.g., VPN-based filtering or proprietary APIs).
- **AAOS vs. AOSP for Automotive**: Android Automotive OS (AAOS) is preferred over AOSP for vehicle diagnostics due to built-in automotive HALs, while Cuttlefish virtual devices enable OBD-II/CAN simulation for prototyping.
