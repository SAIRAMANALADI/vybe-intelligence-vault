# Get Started with Google AI in Higher Education : Google for Education

## Summary

- **Google AI Tools for Education**: Free tools like the **Gemini app** and **NotebookLM** integrate with Google Workspace for Education to enhance productivity, research, and learning via AI-driven assistance in Docs, Gmail, Slides, and Classroom.

- **Premium AI Capabilities**: **Gemini for Google Workspace** (paid license) and **NotebookLM Plus** provide advanced generative AI features, embedding AI into core Workspace tools for accelerated creativity, collaboration, and learning.

- **Learning Outcomes**: Course enables users to leverage Google AI for time-saving automation, research acceleration, and pedagogical support, with structured modules covering tool-specific applications and best practices.
