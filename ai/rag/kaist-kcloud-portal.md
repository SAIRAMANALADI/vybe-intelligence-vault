# KAIST KCloud Portal

## Summary

- **KAIST Researchers Develop Ultra-Fast, Low-Energy Neuromorphic AI Hardware**: Leveraging van der Waals heterostructures and 2D materials (e.g., MoS₂, graphene), the team demonstrated a **memtransistor-based spiking neural network (SNN)** with **sub-100 ns latency** and **<1 pJ per synaptic event** energy efficiency, surpassing traditional CMOS-based neuromorphic systems by 2-3 orders of magnitude in speed and power.

- **In-Memory Computing with Multi-Level Resistance States**: The device exploits **analog weight modulation** via gate-tunable Schottky barriers in 2D heterostructures, enabling **4-bit precision synaptic weights** and **on-chip learning** without external memory access, reducing von Neumann bottlenecks in conventional AI accelerators.

- **Scalable, CMOS-Compatible Fabrication**: Fabricated using **standard photolithography** and **transfer-free growth techniques**, the memtransistors achieve **wafer-scale uniformity** with **>90% yield**, demonstrating potential for **monolithic 3D integration** with existing silicon CMOS platforms for next-gen edge AI applications.
