# Emerging climate impact on carbon sinks in a consolidated carbon budget | Nature

## Summary

- **Revised carbon sink magnitudes**: Natural land sink estimate reduced by ~30%, while anthropogenic land-use change emissions increased; ocean sink now accounts for 15% more CO₂ uptake than land, aligning with recent oceanic/atmospheric observations.

- **Climate-driven sink inefficiency**: Climate change has reduced terrestrial and oceanic carbon sink efficiency since 1960, contributing **8.3 ± 1.4 ppm** to atmospheric CO₂ rise via sink degradation.

- **Regional sink-to-source transition**: Southeast Asian and large parts of South American tropical forests have shifted from CO₂ sinks to sources due to combined effects of deforestation and climate change, emphasizing the urgency of halting deforestation and limiting warming.
