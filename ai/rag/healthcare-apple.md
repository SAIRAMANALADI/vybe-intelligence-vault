# Healthcare - Apple

## Summary

- **Hospital Workflows**: Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Vision Pro) integrate with Epic and ambient documentation systems, reducing clinician time by ~2 hours/day while enabling mobile radiology review, real-time patient monitoring, and AR-assisted surgeries.

- **Remote Patient Care**: iPad/iPhone facilitate telehealth visits, Apple Watch detects early signs of conditions (e.g., sleep apnea), and HealthKit enables longitudinal remote monitoring for chronic disease management.

- **Security & Research**: Zero-Touch Deployment automates device provisioning; Apple silicon ensures hardware-backed encryption; ResearchKit/CareKit enable large-scale clinical studies leveraging Apple Watch’s passive health data collection.
