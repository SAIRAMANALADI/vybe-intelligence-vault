# Product Privacy | Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Instructure collects diverse personal data (e.g., identifiers, usage metrics, device info) via its products (Canvas, Mastery Connect, LearnPlatform) for educational and operational purposes, including third-party integrations (e.g., Google APIs) and AI-driven features (e.g., chatbots), with explicit consent mechanisms for secondary uses.

- **Compliance Frameworks**: Aligns with EU-U.S./Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks and UK Extension, adhering to Student Privacy Pledge and TrustEd Apps certification, while restricting automated decision-making, student-targeted ads, and profiling.

- **Data Sharing & Security**: Shares data only with affiliates/service providers under contractual constraints (no sales/rentals), implements technical/organizational safeguards (e.g., de-identification, encryption), and discloses practices for institutional/parental access, with transparency via regionalized privacy notices.
