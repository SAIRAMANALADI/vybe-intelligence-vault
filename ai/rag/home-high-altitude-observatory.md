# Home | High Altitude Observatory

## Summary

- **Solar Dynamics & Earth Interaction**: HAO conducts research on solar dynamics (e.g., magnetic fields, coronal mass ejections) and their impacts on Earth's magnetosphere, ionosphere, and thermosphere using observational data and modeling (e.g., WACCM, TIEGCM).

- **Instrumentation & Observatories**: Develops and operates advanced solar/atmospheric instruments (e.g., CoMP, ISOON) and leverages observatories (e.g., Mauna Loa Solar Observatory) for high-resolution data acquisition.

- **Predictive Modeling**: Advances physics-based models (e.g., solar wind propagation, space weather forecasting) to improve real-time prediction of solar-terrestrial disturbances and their geospace effects.
