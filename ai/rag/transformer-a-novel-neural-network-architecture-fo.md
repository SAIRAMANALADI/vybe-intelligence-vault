# Transformer: A Novel Neural Network Architecture for Language Understanding

## Summary

- **Self-attention mechanism**: The Transformer architecture replaces recurrent/sequential processing with a self-attention mechanism that directly models relationships between all words in a sentence in parallel, enabling constant-time dependency modeling regardless of distance between tokens.

- **Performance gains**: Achieved state-of-the-art BLEU scores on WMT'14 English→German/French translation benchmarks while reducing training compute requirements by up to 10x compared to RNN/CNN baselines, leveraging parallelizable attention operations optimized for TPUs/GPUs.

- **Interpretability & versatility**: Enables visualization of attention distributions for tasks like coreference resolution, and demonstrated strong performance on syntactic parsing with minimal task-specific adaptation, highlighting its generalizability beyond translation.
