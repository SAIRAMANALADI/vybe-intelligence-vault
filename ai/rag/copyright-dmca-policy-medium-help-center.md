# Copyright & DMCA Policy – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **DMCA Compliance Requirements**: Notices must include a signature, identification of the copyrighted work, infringing material location, contact info, good-faith belief statement, and accuracy certification under penalty of perjury (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)).
- **Process Workflow**: Medium removes infringing content upon valid notice, notifies affected users, and forwards complaints to Lumen; repeat offenders face suspension or account termination.
- **Counter-Notice Mechanism**: Users can dispute takedowns by submitting a signed statement asserting misidentification, consenting to jurisdiction, and accepting service of process; material is restored if no legal action follows within 10 business days.
