# Wikimedia technical blog editorial guidelines - MediaWiki

## Summary

- **Scope & Audience**: Targets Wikimedia technical community (engineers, open-source contributors) with technically sophisticated content; excludes non-technical topics (e.g., policies, editor workflows). Published under CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

- **Submission Workflow**: Drafts submitted via [Phabricator](https://phabricator.wikimedia.org/tag/technical-blog-posts/) ("Draft in Progress" → "Needs Review"); requires peer review, admin edits, and 2+ week advance notice for deadlines.

- **Content & Format**: Minimum 300 words, English-only (initially), adhering to Wikipedia’s Manual of Style; requires high-quality featured image (CC-licensed) with alt text, and structured headers in sentence case.
