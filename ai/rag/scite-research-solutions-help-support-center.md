# Scite – Research Solutions Help & Support Center

## Summary

- **Scite’s citation indexing** leverages deep learning to classify citation statements (supporting, mentioning, or contradicting) rather than just counting citations, enabling contextual analysis of scholarly impact.

- **Coverage and data integrity** are documented via Scite’s transparency reports (e.g., [Scite Coverage](https://help.researchsolutions.com/hc/en-us/articles/32070161683988-Scite-Coverage)), while gaps or missing citations can be reported and corrected through user feedback mechanisms.

- **Functional workflows** include automated tools like *Scite Reference Check* for citation verification, *Table Mode* for structured data extraction, and *Alerts* for monitoring research topics, all designed to streamline systematic literature reviews and meta-analyses.
