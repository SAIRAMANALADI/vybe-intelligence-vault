# Scite – Research Solutions Help & Support Center

## Summary

- **Scite's citation analysis** distinguishes between *citations* (raw counts) and *citation statements* (contextual mentions, classified as supporting, contrasting, or mentioning), enabling nuanced bibliometric insights beyond traditional citation indexes.

- **Scite's coverage** spans millions of documents across disciplines, with a proprietary algorithm extracting and classifying citation statements from full-text articles, preprints, and other sources to improve citation accuracy and completeness.

- **Scite's tools** include *Reference Check* (identifying outdated citations), *Assistant* (table mode for structured data extraction), and *alerts* (automated notifications for new citations or research topics), designed to streamline systematic reviews and expert identification.
