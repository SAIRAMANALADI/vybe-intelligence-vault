# RIPE Atlas — RIPE Network Coordination Centre

## Summary

- **Global Measurement Network**: RIPE Atlas consists of over 12,000 probes and anchors deployed worldwide by volunteers, actively measuring Internet connectivity, traffic, and routing in real-time.
- **Data Access & Custom Measurements**: The platform provides open access to collected data via visualizations and APIs, while enabling users to perform custom measurements using a credits-based system.
- **Infrastructure Insights**: RIPE Atlas supports troubleshooting, network improvement, and research by offering granular insights into Internet behavior, including case studies on routing resilience and IXP analysis.
