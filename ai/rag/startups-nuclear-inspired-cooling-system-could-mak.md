# Startup’s nuclear-inspired cooling system could make data centers more sustainable | MIT News | Massachusetts Institute

## Summary

- **Adaptive Phase Cooling (APC)**: Ferveret's system submerges servers in a low-boiling-point liquid, generating smaller, more frequently detaching bubbles that accelerate heat transfer via rapid phase-change cycles, improving efficiency over conventional immersion cooling.

- **Nuclear-Inspired Heat Transfer**: Adapted from subcooled boiling in nuclear reactors, the APC system eliminates water use and PFAS chemicals, achieving 15% better computational power efficiency than state-of-the-art liquid cooling, enabling 35% more AI model tokens per watt.

- **Modular & Scalable Design**: Ferveret’s rack-mounted APC units integrate real-time power optimization via embedded sensors and control software, enabling deployment in water-scarce regions and compatibility with existing data center infrastructure.
