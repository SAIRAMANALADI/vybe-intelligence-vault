# Litmaps | Your Literature Review Assistant

## Summary

- **Visual Literature Mapping**: Litmaps transforms academic literature discovery into interactive citation networks (e.g., "Seed Maps"), enabling researchers to visualize paper relationships, temporal trends, and citation paths for efficient literature reviews.

- **Automated Discovery & Monitoring**: Features like "Discover" and "Monitor" leverage AI-driven algorithms to recommend relevant papers based on seed inputs and provide real-time updates on new publications in a research domain.

- **Cross-Domain Collaboration**: Supports multi-user workflows with sharing, annotation, and team-based features, facilitating collaborative research across institutions and disciplines.
