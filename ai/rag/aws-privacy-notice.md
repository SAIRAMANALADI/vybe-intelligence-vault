# AWS Privacy Notice

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: AWS collects personal information via direct input, automated tracking (e.g., cookies, device identifiers), and third-party sources, excluding customer-managed "content" processed via AWS services.

- **Data Processing & Sharing**: Personal data is used for service delivery, fraud prevention, marketing, and legal compliance; shared with affiliates, third-party service providers, and in business transfers, with strict access controls and encryption (PCI DSS, TLS).

- **User Controls & Retention**: Users can access, update, or delete account data; opt out of personalized ads/cookies; retention aligns with service needs and legal requirements, with data stored across multiple jurisdictions under AWS’s compliance frameworks.
