# [1910.09700] Quantifying the Carbon Emissions of Machine Learning

## Summary

- **Key emission factors** for ML model training include **server location/energy grid mix**, **training duration**, and **hardware specifications** (e.g., GPU/TPU model and efficiency).
- Introduced the **Machine Learning Emissions Calculator** (mlco2.github.io/impact) to estimate carbon footprint by quantifying energy consumption and grid carbon intensity.
- Mitigation strategies proposed: **optimizing model architectures**, **leveraging carbon-aware scheduling** (timing training to renewable energy availability), and **selecting low-carbon data centers**.
