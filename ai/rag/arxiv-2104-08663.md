# [2104.08663] BEIR: A Heterogenous Benchmark for Zero-shot Evaluation of Information Retrieval Models

## Summary

- **BEIR benchmark**: Introduces a heterogeneous evaluation framework with 18 diverse IR datasets, enabling zero-shot assessment of retrieval models (lexical, sparse, dense, late-interaction, re-ranking) across out-of-distribution (OOD) settings.

- **Performance insights**: BM25 serves as a robust baseline; re-ranking and late-interaction models (e.g., ColBERT) achieve top zero-shot performance but at high computational cost, while dense/sparse models are efficient but lag in generalization.

- **Open-source toolkit**: Provides a public repository ([BEIR GitHub](https://github.com/UKPLab/beir)) for standardized IR model evaluation, accelerating research on robust, generalizable retrieval systems.
