# HOME - National Privacy CommissionNational Privacy Commission

## Summary

- **Regulatory Framework**: The National Privacy Commission (NPC) enforces the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA)**, regulating the processing of personal data to ensure privacy protection while facilitating information flow for innovation, with issuances like **advisories, circulars, decisions, and resolutions** to guide compliance.

- **Data Subject Rights & Organizational Compliance**: **Data subjects** (individuals whose data is processed) have defined rights under the DPA, while **Personal Information Controllers (PICs)** and **Processors (PIPs)** must comply with registration, breach reporting, and advisory opinion mechanisms via the **NPC Registration System** and **Data Breach Notification and Management System (DBNMS)**.

- **Public Engagement & Enforcement Actions**: The NPC conducts **public consultations** (e.g., draft guidelines on data processing for privilege availment) and enforces measures like **joint advisories** (e.g., with DICT/SEC on online lending platforms) to address emerging privacy risks, with tools like **Ask Priva** and **breach statistics tracking** for transparency.
