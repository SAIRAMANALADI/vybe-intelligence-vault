# CIRCT

## Summary

- **Project Overview**: CIRCT (Circuit IR Compilers and Tools) is an experimental LLVM/MLIR-based project aiming to modernize hardware design tooling by replacing legacy Verilog/VHDL IRs with modular, reusable compiler infrastructure leveraging LLVM development best practices.

- **Community & Collaboration**: The project operates via open channels (Discourse, Discord, weekly meetings) and follows LLVM contribution policies, enabling collaborative development of hardware-focused MLIR dialects and tools.

- **Technical Goals**: Addresses Verilog’s limitations (e.g., poor location tracking) by providing a unified platform for hardware design tools, enabling higher-level abstractions and potential adoption by both open and proprietary EDA ecosystems.
