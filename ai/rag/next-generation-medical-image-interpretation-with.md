# Next generation medical image interpretation with MedGemma 1.5 and medical speech to text with MedASR

## Summary

- **MedGemma 1.5 4B** introduces expanded support for high-dimensional medical imaging (CT, MRI, histopathology) and longitudinal imaging (e.g., chest X-ray time series), with benchmark improvements of **3% (CT), 14% (MRI), 35% (anatomy localization), and 5% (longitudinal CXR)** over MedGemma 1 4B.
- **MedASR** is a new open medical speech-to-text model achieving **58% lower WER (5.2% vs. 12.5%)** on chest X-ray dictations and **82% lower WER (5.2% vs. 28.2%)** on diverse medical dictations compared to Whisper large-v3.
- **Performance gains** in text-based tasks include **+5% on MedQA (69% vs. 64%)** and **+22% on EHRQA (90% vs. 68%)**, with all models (MedGemma 1.5, MedASR, MedSigLIP) available under open licenses for research/commercial use.
