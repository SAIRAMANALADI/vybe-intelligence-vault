# Next generation medical image interpretation with MedGemma 1.5 and medical speech to text with MedASR

## Summary

- **MedGemma 1.5 4B** introduces expanded multimodal medical imaging support, including high-dimensional CT/MRI volumes, histopathology whole-slide analysis, longitudinal chest X-ray series, and anatomical localization, with benchmark improvements of 3-35% over MedGemma 1 across modalities.
- **MedASR** is a new open medical speech-to-text model achieving 58-82% lower word error rates than Whisper large-v3 on medical dictation benchmarks, enabling natural voice interaction with MedGemma for clinical workflows.
- **Performance gains** include 5-22% accuracy improvements on medical text benchmarks (MedQA, EHRQA) and full DICOM integration in Google Cloud deployments, with all models (MedGemma 1.5, MedASR, MedSigLIP) available free for research/commercial use via Hugging Face and Vertex AI.
