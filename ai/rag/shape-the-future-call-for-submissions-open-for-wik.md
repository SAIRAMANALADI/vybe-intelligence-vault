# Shape the Future: Call for Submissions Open for WikiConference India 2026 – Diff

## Summary

- **Call for submissions** for **WikiConference India 2026** (Kochi, Kerala, 4–6 Sept 2026) under the theme *"Re-imagining the Knowledge Commons"*, focusing on **community-led, human-created knowledge** amid AI-driven content challenges.

- **Core submission tracks** include **Strategic Roadmapping, Community Governance, New Participation Models (GLAM/EduWiki), Language Equity, and Tech/Tools/AI Integration**, with formats ranging from **workshops (60–90 min) to lightning talks (5 min)**.

- **Deadline: 15 July 2026**; proposals must emphasize **reproducible, scalable innovations** and **cross-community dialogue**, prioritizing **Indic-language projects** and **underrepresented knowledge systems**.
