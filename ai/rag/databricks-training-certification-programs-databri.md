# Databricks Training & Certification Programs | Databricks

## Summary

- **Role-Based Training Pathways**: Databricks offers structured learning paths for roles including Apache Spark Developer, Data Analyst, Data Engineer, Generative AI Engineer, Machine Learning Practitioner, and Platform Administrator, with intermediate/advanced modules (e.g., Generative AI Engineering with Databricks, Data Engineering with Databricks).

- **Certification & Badges**: Free on-demand foundational courses (e.g., Lakehouse Fundamentals, Generative AI Fundamentals, AI Agent Fundamentals) provide badges for LinkedIn/resume upon quiz completion, while paid instructor-led labs (e.g., Data Engineering with Databricks, Data Analysis with Databricks Lakehouse) offer hands-on certification.

- **Technical Focus Areas**: Courses cover core components like Delta Lake pipelines, Unity Catalog governance, LLM-centric application development, ML model deployment/monitoring, and SQL/Python-based ETL workflows, with durations ranging from 1.5 hours (fundamentals) to 18 hours (advanced labs).
