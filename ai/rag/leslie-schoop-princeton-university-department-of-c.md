# Leslie Schoop - Princeton University Department of Chemistry

## Summary

- **Quantum Material Discovery Pipeline**: Combines inorganic chemistry, electronic structure calculations, and solid-state synthesis (flux growth, vapor transport, Bridgman growth) to predict, synthesize, and characterize quantum materials exhibiting exotic properties (e.g., Dirac semimetals, topological insulators) for next-gen technologies.

- **Chemical Exfoliation & 2D Materials**: Pioneers scalable synthesis of air-stable, superconducting monolayer inks (e.g., 1T′-WS₂) via chemical exfoliation, enabling high-purity 2D materials for quantum devices and energy storage (e.g., HₓCrS₂ with enhanced Na⁺ diffusion).

- **Topological Semimetal Engineering**: Develops predictive chemical rules for band structure tuning in kagome and Dirac materials (e.g., ZrSiS), leveraging charge density waves and symmetry-breaking to realize novel electronic phases (e.g., axial Higgs mode, Devil’s staircase magnetism).
