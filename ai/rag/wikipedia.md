# Wikipedia

## Summary

- **Multi-language encyclopedia**: Wikipedia is a free, web-based encyclopedia with over **7.1M+ articles in English** and **>50M total articles** across **300+ languages**, with **>1M articles in 20+ languages** and **>100K in 50+ languages**.

- **Decentralized, volunteer-driven content**: Articles are collaboratively edited via **MediaWiki**, a PHP-based open-source wiki platform, with content licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** and **GFDL**; language editions operate independently with localized governance.

- **High-scale infrastructure**: Served via **CDN-backed global caching** (e.g., Cloudflare) and **MariaDB/MySQL databases**; supports **real-time collaborative editing** with revision tracking, diffing, and spam/abuse filtering via **automated bots** and **community moderation**.
