# AppleCare - Apple

## Summary

- **AppleCare One** consolidates coverage for up to three Apple devices (e.g., iPhone, Mac, iPad) under a single plan at **$19.99/month**, with additional devices at **$5.99/month** each, offering unified accidental damage, liquid spill, and theft/loss protection (up to 3 claims/year for iPhone/iPad/Apple Watch).

- **Repair Services**: Includes **same-day repairs** at Apple Stores or authorized providers, **Express Replacement Service** for iPhones, **on-site service** for Macs/Displays, and **battery replacements** at no extra cost if capacity drops below 80%.

- **Global Coverage**: Access to **5,000+ Apple Authorized Service Providers** worldwide, with **flat service fees** ($29–$299 depending on damage type) and **mail-in repair options** with prepaid shipping.
