# HKUDS/VideoRAG

## Summary

- **VideoRAG Framework**: Introduces a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system for long-video understanding, enabling LLMs to process and interact with video content via multi-modal inputs (e.g., frames, audio, metadata).

- **Technical Architecture**: Implements a hierarchical retrieval pipeline combining dense (embedding-based) and sparse (keyword/temporal) retrieval to efficiently index and query video segments, optimizing for scalability and accuracy in long-form video Q&A.

- **Multi-Modal Integration**: Leverages cross-modal attention mechanisms to align textual queries with video features (e.g., CLIP embeddings, ASR transcripts), supporting real-time or near-real-time video chat and retrieval tasks.
