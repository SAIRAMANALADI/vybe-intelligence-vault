# Learning Tools & Educational Solutions - Google for Education

## Summary

- **AI-Driven Security & Compliance**: Google Workspace for Education blocks **99.9% of spam/phishing/malware** via AI in Gmail, enforces **FERPA/GDPR/COPPA compliance**, and provides **enterprise-grade data protection** for AI tools like Gemini and NotebookLM.

- **Tiered AI Integration**: **Education Fundamentals** offers free AI assistants (Gemini, NotebookLM) with 100TB pooled storage, while **Education Plus ($6/user/year)** adds advanced analytics, premium AI features (e.g., Gemini in Docs/Sheets), and **Workspace Studio** for automating workflows (meeting prep, email triage, translation).

- **Automated Workflow Optimization**: **Workspace Studio** enables AI agents to auto-summarize meetings, translate action items, label priority emails, and archive Drive attachments—reducing manual tasks while ensuring **real-time collaboration** across Gmail, Meet, Classroom, and Drive.
