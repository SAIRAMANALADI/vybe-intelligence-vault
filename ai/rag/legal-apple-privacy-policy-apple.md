# Legal - Apple Privacy Policy - Apple

## Summary

- **Definition & Scope of Personal Data**: Apple defines personal data as any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual (directly or indirectly), including device identifiers (e.g., serial numbers), aggregated data excluded. Policy applies globally to all interactions (websites, apps, in-store) but excludes third-party data handling.

- **Data Collection & Sources**: Apple collects data across account creation, transactions, device usage, health/fitness metrics, and fraud prevention. Sources include user-provided data, third-party partners (e.g., mobile carriers), public web crawling (Applebot), and datasets for AI/ML research (processed without re-identification).

- **Data Usage & Retention**: Personal data is used for service provisioning, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and (with consent) personalization. Apple retains data only as long as necessary, avoids algorithmic profiling without human review, and provides global privacy rights (access, deletion, correction) via [privacy.apple.com](https://privacy.apple.com).
