# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: DagsHub provides a single platform for curating multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM), tracking experiments, and managing models, supporting petabyte-scale data with versioning, lineage, and CI/CD/CT integration.

- **Scalable Plans with Advanced Features**: Offers tiered pricing (Individual, Team, Enterprise) with features like multimodal annotation, auto-labeling, RBAC, petabyte-scale storage, and on-premise/VPC deployments for high-scale workloads.

- **ML Stack Integration & Enterprise Security**: Seamlessly integrates with ML frameworks (e.g., MLflow) and cloud/MLOps tools, while Enterprise plans support SSO/LDAP/OIDC, air-gapped installations, and organizational resource control.
