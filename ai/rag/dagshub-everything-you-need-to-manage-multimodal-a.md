# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation/annotation of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM), experiment tracking (MLflow-compatible), and model versioning/deployment with lineage tracking across public/private repositories.

- **Scalable Plans with Advanced Features**: Free tier offers 20GB storage + 100 private experiments; Team ($99/user/month) adds multimodal auto-labeling, 1TB storage, and RBAC; Enterprise provides petabyte-scale storage, VPC/air-gapped deployment, and SSO/LDAP integration.

- **Enterprise-Grade Integrations & Security**: Supports on-prem/cloud deployment, OpenShift compatibility, and SSO/role-based access controls with organizational resource management for high-scale AI workloads.
