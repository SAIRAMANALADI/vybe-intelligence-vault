# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation/annotation of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM), experiment tracking (MLflow-compatible), and model versioning/deployment with lineage from data to model.

- **Tiered Pricing & Features**:
  - *Individual*: Free for public repos, 100 private experiments, 20GB storage.
  - *Team*: $99/user/month with private repos, multimodal auto-labeling, 1TB storage.
  - *Enterprise*: Custom pricing for petabyte-scale, VPC/air-gapped deployments, SSO/LDAP RBAC.

- **Enterprise-Grade Capabilities**: Supports on-prem/air-gapped installations, OpenShift compatibility, organizational resource control, and enterprise SLA with priority support.
