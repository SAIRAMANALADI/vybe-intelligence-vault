# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation, annotation, and versioning of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM) with lineage tracking, CI/CD/CT integration, and interactive pipelines for petabyte-scale data.

- **Scalable Experiment Tracking & Model Registry**: Enables unlimited experiment tracking (private/public repos), MLflow compatibility, model versioning, and deployment with full lineage from data to model, supporting team/enterprise RBAC and 1TB+ storage.

- **Enterprise-Grade Deployment & Integration**: Offers on-prem/VPC/air-gapped installations with SSO/LDAP/OIDC, petabyte-scale storage, OpenShift compatibility, and seamless integration with existing ML frameworks, cloud storage, and MLOps tools.
