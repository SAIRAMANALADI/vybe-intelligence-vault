# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation/annotation of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM), experiment tracking (MLflow-compatible), and model versioning/deployment with lineage, scalable from petabytes to private cloud storage.

- **Tiered Subscription Model**: Free tier offers public repos, limited private storage/experiments; Team ($99/user/month) adds multimodal auto-labeling, RBAC, and 1TB storage; Enterprise provides petabyte-scale, VPC/air-gapped deployments, SSO/LDAP, and organizational SLA.

- **Enterprise-Grade Features**: Supports on-prem/air-gapped installations, OpenShift compatibility, full model lineage, and integrates with existing ML stacks (cloud storage, MLOps tools) while offering priority support and custom security controls.
