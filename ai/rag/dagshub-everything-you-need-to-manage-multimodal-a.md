# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation, annotation, and versioning of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM) with lineage tracking, experiment management (MLflow-compatible), and model registry for deployment and lineage tracking.

- **Scalable Infrastructure & Integrations**: Offers tiered plans (Individual/Team/Enterprise) with storage scaling from 20GB to petabytes, RBAC, SSO/LDAP, and compatibility with cloud/on-prem deployments (VPC/air-gapped) and ML frameworks/storage systems.

- **Enterprise-Grade Features**: Includes auto-labeling, interactive pipelines, CI/CD/CT integration, and priority support, with OpenShift compatibility and organizational resource control for high-scale AI workloads.
