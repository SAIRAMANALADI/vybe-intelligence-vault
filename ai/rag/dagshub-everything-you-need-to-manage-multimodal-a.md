# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports multimodal dataset curation/annotation, experiment tracking (MLflow-compatible), and model versioning/deployment with lineage tracking across vision, audio, and LLM data.

- **Tiered Pricing & Scalability**:
  - **Individual**: Free tier with public repos, 20GB storage, and limited private experiment tracking.
  - **Team**: $99/user/month with private repos, multimodal auto-labeling, 1TB storage, and RBAC.
  - **Enterprise**: Custom pricing for petabyte-scale data, on-premise/VPC deployments, SSO/LDAP, and OpenShift compatibility.

- **Enterprise-Grade Features**: Includes CI/CD/CT integration, interactive pipelines, petabyte-scale storage, model deployment to clusters, and organizational resource control with enterprise SLAs.
