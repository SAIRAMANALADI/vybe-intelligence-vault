# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation/annotation of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM), experiment tracking, and model versioning with lineage, integrating with MLflow and open-source formats.

- **Tiered Scalability**: Free tier offers 20GB storage + 100 private experiments; Team tier ($99/user/month) adds multimodal auto-labeling, RBAC, and 1TB storage; Enterprise supports petabyte-scale data, VPC/air-gapped deployments, and SSO/LDAP.

- **Enterprise-Grade Features**: On-prem/air-gapped installations, OpenShift compatibility, organizational resource control, and enterprise SLA with priority support for high-scale AI workloads.
