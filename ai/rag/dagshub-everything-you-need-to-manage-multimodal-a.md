# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Supports curation, annotation, and versioning of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM) with lineage tracking, experiment monitoring (MLflow-compatible), and model registry for deployment and lineage tracking.

- **Scalable Infrastructure & Integrations**: Offers tiered plans (Individual, Team, Enterprise) with storage limits (20GB–1TB), RBAC, auto-labeling, and petabyte-scale support; integrates with cloud storage, CI/CD/CT pipelines, and open-source ML frameworks.

- **Enterprise-Grade Features**: Includes VPC/air-gapped on-prem deployment, SSO/LDAP/OIDC, OpenShift compatibility, and organizational resource control with enterprise SLAs for high-scale AI workloads.
