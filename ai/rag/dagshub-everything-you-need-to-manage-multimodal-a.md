# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation, annotation, and versioning of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM) with lineage tracking, experiment comparison, and model registry for end-to-end ML lifecycle management.

- **Scalable Deployment & Integration**: Offers cloud/on-prem (Enterprise) with petabyte-scale storage, RBAC/SSO, and seamless integration with ML frameworks (MLflow-compatible), cloud storage, and MLOps tools via open-source formats.

- **Tiered Plans with Advanced Features**: Individual (free, 20GB storage), Team ($99/user/month, 1TB storage, auto-labeling), and Enterprise (custom, petabyte-scale, air-gapped deployments) with priority support and organizational controls.
