# DagsHub: Everything you need to manage multimodal AI

## Summary

- **Unified AI Data & Model Management**: Platform supports curation/annotation of multimodal datasets (vision, audio, LLM), experiment tracking (MLflow-compatible), and model versioning/deployment with lineage tracking across public/private repositories.

- **Tiered Pricing & Scalability**: Free tier offers 20GB storage with limited private repo support; Team tier ($99/user/month) adds multimodal auto-labeling, RBAC, and 1TB storage; Enterprise tier enables petabyte-scale data, air-gapped deployments, and SSO/LDAP integration.

- **Enterprise Features**: Supports VPC/on-premise installations, OpenShift compatibility, organizational resource control, and enterprise-grade SLA/support for high-scale AI workloads.
