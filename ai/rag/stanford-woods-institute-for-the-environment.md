# Stanford Woods Institute for the Environment

## Summary

- **Dietary Modifications**: Prioritize organic produce, particularly for the "Dirty Dozen" (e.g., strawberries, spinach), to reduce pesticide exposure; avoid processed foods in plastic packaging to minimize phthalates and BPA.
- **Household Product Selection**: Use fragrance-free, non-toxic cleaning and personal care products (e.g., those labeled "phthalate-free" or "BPA-free") to lower endocrine-disrupting chemical (EDC) exposure.
- **Environmental Controls**: Increase ventilation during cleaning or cooking to reduce volatile organic compound (VOC) inhalation; opt for glass/stainless steel over plastic for food storage to prevent leaching of synthetic chemicals.
