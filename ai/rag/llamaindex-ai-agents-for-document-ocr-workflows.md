## Summary

- **Performance Metrics**: Benchmarked against proprietary VLM, commercial IDP, and open-source OCR, LlamaParse demonstrates superior accuracy in overall performance, chart/table extraction, and supports 50+ unstructured file types including multi-page layouts and embedded images.

- **Enterprise Features**: Offers 10K free credits/month (~1K pages), HIPAA/GDPR/SOC2 compliance, 99.9% uptime, flexible deployment (cloud/VPC), and schema-based LLM extraction with enterprise-grade chunking/embedding for RAG applications.
