# LlamaIndex | AI Agents for Document OCR + Workflows

## Summary

- **Agentic OCR Pipeline**: LlamaParse employs a Vision-Language Model (VLM)-powered agentic OCR system with task-specific experts (text, charts, tables, handwriting) and auto-correction loops for recursive error detection and correction, achieving high pass-through rates on complex, multi-modal documents.

- **Structured Extraction & Schema Support**: Supports schema-based, LLM-driven extraction of unstructured content into structured formats (e.g., JSON) without training, enabling precise downstream AI workflows; includes built-in document classification, segmentation, and enterprise-grade chunking/embedding pipelines optimized for RAG.

- **Enterprise-Grade Performance & Compliance**: Processes 50+ unstructured file types (PDFs, Office docs, images) with 99.9% uptime, HIPAA/GDPR/SOC2 compliance, and flexible deployment (cloud or VPC); benchmarks demonstrate superior accuracy vs. proprietary VLM, commercial IDP, and open-source OCR tools across overall performance, charts, and tables.
