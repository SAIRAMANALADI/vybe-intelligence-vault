# LlamaIndex | AI Agents for Document OCR + Workflows

## Summary

- **Performance & Scalability**: Benchmarks demonstrate superior accuracy in parsing, chart/table extraction, and multi-modal document handling compared to proprietary VLM, commercial IDP, and open-source OCR solutions, with enterprise-grade infrastructure supporting 1B+ documents processed and 25M+ monthly package downloads.
