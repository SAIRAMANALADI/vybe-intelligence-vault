# VaultGemma: The world's most capable differentially private LLM

## Summary

- **VaultGemma 1B**: Largest open differentially private LLM (1B parameters), trained from scratch with sequence-level DP guarantees (ε ≤ 2.0, δ ≤ 1.1e-10) and empirically validated to show no detectable training data memorization.

- **DP Scaling Laws**: Established compute-privacy-utility trade-offs via noise-batch ratio modeling, revealing optimal training configurations (e.g., smaller models with larger batches) and accurately predicting VaultGemma’s training loss.

- **Performance Gap**: DP-trained VaultGemma achieves utility comparable to non-DP models from ~5 years ago (e.g., GPT-2 1.5B), while using modern DP-SGD with Poisson sampling and scalable DP-SGD adaptations for fixed-size batches.
