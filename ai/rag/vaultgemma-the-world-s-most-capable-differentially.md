# VaultGemma: The world's most capable differentially private LLM

## Summary

- **VaultGemma 1B** is the largest open differentially private LLM (DP-LLM) trained from scratch, achieving a sequence-level DP guarantee of (ε ≤ 2.0, δ ≤ 1.1e-10) while demonstrating no detectable memorization of training data in empirical tests.

- **DP scaling laws** derived from research establish that model performance is primarily governed by the *noise-batch ratio* (privacy noise vs. batch size), enabling optimal compute-privacy-utility trade-offs for training configurations under fixed budgets.

- **Key training advancements** include switching from uniform batching to Poisson sampling (via Scalable DP-SGD) to maintain strong privacy guarantees while addressing variable batch sizes, resulting in a 1B-parameter model with utility comparable to non-private models from ~5 years ago.
