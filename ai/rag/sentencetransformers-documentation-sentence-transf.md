- **Sentence Transformer Core Functionality**: Enables semantic textual similarity, semantic search (symmetric/asymmetric), clustering, paraphrase mining, and multimodal (text/image/audio) embeddings with pre-trained models optimized for efficiency (PyTorch/ONNX/OpenVINO).
