# Accelerate AI Development with Databricks: Discover, Govern, and Build with MCP and Agent Bricks | Databricks Blog

## Summary

- **MCP & Agent Bricks Integration**: Databricks introduces **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** and **Agent Bricks** to accelerate AI development by enabling structured, reusable agentic workflows with built-in governance, discovery, and deployment capabilities.

- **Unified AI Development Platform**: The platform integrates **discovery, governance, and building** of AI agents within a single environment, leveraging **Databricks Lakehouse** for scalable data and AI asset management.

- **Agentic SIEM & Security**: Databricks provides an **open agentic Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)** system, designed for AI-era security operations, embedded within the Lakehouse architecture.
