# A walk through Project Zero metrics - Project Zero

## Summary

- **2019-2021 average fix time**: Vendors reduced average fix time from ~80 days (2018) to **52 days** (2021), with **95% of fixes** completed within the 90-day deadline or 14-day grace period.
- **Browser-specific timelines**: Chrome averaged **30 days** (5.3 days to patch + 24.6 days to release), Firefox **38 days**, while WebKit took **73 days** (61.1 days post-patch release delay).
- **Vendor adherence trends**: Only **1 deadline exceeded** in 2021 (vs. 9/year in 2019-2020), with **Oracle (57%)** and **Microsoft (5%)** having the highest overrun rates among major vendors.
