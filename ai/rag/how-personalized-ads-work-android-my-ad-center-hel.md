# How personalized ads work - Android - My Ad Center Help

## Summary

- **Personalized ads** on Google services are determined by user-selected ad topics/brands in **My Ad Center**, demographic data (age, gender) from Google Account, activity across Google services (Search, YouTube, Android app installs), and partner site interactions, all processed within a **generalized location** (>1 sq. mile, >1000 users) to preserve privacy.

- **Non-personalized ads** rely solely on **contextual signals** (time of day, website topic, active searches, or recent video views) and exclude user-specific data, ensuring ads are based on immediate content relevance rather than historical activity.

- **Partner sites/apps** (using Google Display Network) personalize ads via user-provided data (email, age, gender), on-site interactions, or Google Account activity (if enabled), while non-personalized ads use **contextual factors** (site topic, location) without leveraging user-specific signals.
