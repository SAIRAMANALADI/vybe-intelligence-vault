# How personalized ads work - Android - My Ad Center Help

## Summary

- **Personalized ads** on Google are determined by:
  - User-selected preferences (e.g., topics/brands in **My Ad Center**).
  - Demographic data (age, gender) and account-linked activity (Search, YouTube, Android app installs).
  - Partner site/app interactions if activity is saved to the Google Account.

- **Non-personalized ads** rely on contextual signals:
  - Time of day, website topic, general location (1+ sq. mile, >1K users), or active searches/videos.
  - Excludes user-specific data; used on Google services and partner sites.

- **Control mechanisms**:
  - **My Ad Center** toggles personalized ads across Google services (signed-in).
  - **Partner Ad Settings** (`adssettings.google.com/partnerads`) disables personalization on third-party sites/apps, even when signed in.
