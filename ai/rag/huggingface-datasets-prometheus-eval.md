# prometheus-eval/BiGGen-Bench · Datasets at Hugging Face

## Summary

- **Fine-grained Evaluation Framework**: Introduces BiGGen Bench, a benchmark leveraging language models (LMs) to evaluate other LMs across 7 distinct task categories (e.g., reasoning, factuality, coding) with 25 sub-tasks, emphasizing granularity and multi-dimensional assessment.

- **Automated, Scalable Evaluation**: Utilizes LMs as evaluators (BiGGen Evaluators) to generate synthetic test cases, enabling scalable, automated fine-grained evaluation without human annotation, while maintaining alignment with human judgments via correlation studies.

- **Open-Source and Extensible**: Provides an open-source framework with 1,500+ test cases, supporting extensibility for new tasks or evaluators, and includes a leaderboard for standardized comparison of LM performance across diverse capabilities.
