## Summary

- **Tor Network Architecture**: Tor uses a decentralized network of volunteer-run relays to anonymize traffic via onion routing, encrypting data in multiple layers (hops) to obscure source and destination.

- **Tor Browser & Tails OS**: Tor Browser provides anti-tracking/fingerprinting protections and censorship circumvention, while Tails is a portable OS designed to leave no forensic trace, both leveraging Tor’s network for privacy.

- **Onion Services & Anti-Censorship Tools**: Onion services enable private hosting/access of websites via Tor, while anti-censorship tools (e.g., bridges, Snowflake) bypass network-level blocking to ensure Tor connectivity in restricted regions.
