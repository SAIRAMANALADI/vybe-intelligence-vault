# The Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Fellowship – University of Chicago

## Summary

- **Program Objective**: Trains postdoctoral scholars to integrate AI/ML methods into natural sciences research, focusing on hypothesis generation, experimental design, and scientific discovery acceleration while addressing challenges like bias amplification and interpretability.

- **Collaborative Framework**: Fellows engage with a cross-disciplinary network spanning UChicago departments, Argonne National Lab, Fermi National Accelerator Lab, and Marine Biological Lab, leveraging world-class faculty, data science resources, and field-defining research opportunities.

- **Funding & Support**: Provides competitive salary, benefits, generous research/travel funding, and flexibility to pivot research focus, enabling original contributions at the intersection of AI and scientific inquiry.
