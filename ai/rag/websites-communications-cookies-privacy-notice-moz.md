# Websites, Communications & Cookies Privacy Notice — Mozilla

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Sources**: Mozilla collects personal data via social media interactions, job/internship/grant/fellowship applications (processed via Greenhouse/Fluxx.io), community contributions (Bugzilla, codebases, Mozilla Connect via Khoros), user feedback (public forums like connect.mozilla.org), account/subscription sign-ups (Mozilla/GitHub accounts), and product/policy campaigns (third-party managed).

- **Tracking & Analytics Tools**: Mozilla employs cookies, device/IP data, and third-party services (Google Analytics, Convert.com, ShareProgress, Glean) for functionality (e.g., language preferences, authentication), metrics (user engagement, A/B testing), and fraud prevention (Google’s Invisible reCAPTCHA). Users can opt out via browser settings, Mozilla’s privacy portal, or vendor-specific tools (e.g., Google Analytics opt-out add-on).

- **Payment Processing & Data Retention**: Transactions (Stripe, Apple Pay, PayPal, etc.) are handled by third-party vendors, with Mozilla receiving limited data (e.g., billing address, subscription status). Donation/contact records (via Braze, Salesforce, Fundraise Up) are retained for 10 years. Users can request data access via Mozilla’s [DSAR Portal](https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/1350748f-7139-405c-8188-22740b3b5587/4ba08202-2ede-4994-a89e-f0b0870f95f0).
