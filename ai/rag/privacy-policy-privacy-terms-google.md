# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-associated (signed-in) and non-account data (unique identifiers tied to browsers/devices), covering user-provided content (emails, photos), activity logs (searches, interactions), device metadata (IP, OS, app versions), and location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, IP-based).

- **Purpose-Driven Processing**: Collected data is used for service personalization (e.g., ad relevance, search results), security (fraud detection), and feature optimization (e.g., auto-updates, battery management), with retention policies varying by data type and user settings (e.g., Timeline for location history).

- **Privacy Controls & Compliance**: Users manage data via Google Account settings (export/delete options, activity controls), with regional frameworks (e.g., GDPR) and regulatory cooperation ensuring compliance; privacy policies are updated periodically (e.g., effective May 26, 2026).
