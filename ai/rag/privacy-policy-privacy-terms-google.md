# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (personal info, activity) and non-account-linked (unique identifiers, device/browser metadata, IP addresses) data to personalize content, ads, and improve services.

- **Location & Sensor Data**: Location is gathered via GPS, IP address, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth signals, and user-labeled places (e.g., "home/work"), with retention tied to device/account settings (e.g., Timeline, Web & App Activity).

- **Cross-Service Activity Tracking**: User interactions (searches, media, calls/messages logs, third-party site/app activity) are logged and stored in Google Account history, enabling cross-service personalization and compliance with privacy controls (e.g., deletion, export).
