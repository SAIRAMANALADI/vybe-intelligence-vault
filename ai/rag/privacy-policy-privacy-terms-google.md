# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked info (emails, photos), device identifiers, IP addresses, and activity logs (searches, location, interactions with ads/content) to personalize services and ads.

- **Location & Cross-Service Tracking**: Location data is derived from GPS, IP addresses, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth signals, and user-labeled places (e.g., "home/work"), stored variably based on account/device settings (e.g., Timeline, Web & App Activity).

- **Third-Party & Public Data Integration**: Google supplements user data with publicly available sources (e.g., news articles) and trusted partners (e.g., marketing/security firms) for ads, abuse prevention, and business services, using technologies like cookies and pixel tags for cross-service tracking.
