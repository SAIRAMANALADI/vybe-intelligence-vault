# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google collects diverse data types including account-specific (e.g., emails, photos, payment info) and device/browser metadata (e.g., IP, device settings, crash reports) via services like Search, YouTube, and Android, with or without an active Google Account.

- **Activity & Location Tracking**: User activity (searches, interactions, communications) and location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, IP) are logged, with retention tied to account settings (e.g., Timeline, Web & App Activity), enabling personalized services like ads and recommendations.

- **Third-Party & Public Data Integration**: Google aggregates data from publicly accessible sources (e.g., news articles) and trusted partners (e.g., business directories) to enhance its services, while adhering to privacy controls and regulatory compliance frameworks.
