# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (e.g., emails, location) and non-account-linked (e.g., device identifiers, IP addresses, browser activity) information, with retention policies tied to user settings and regulatory compliance.

- **Technical Mechanisms**: Utilizes unique identifiers, cookies, pixel tags, and sensor data (GPS/Wi-Fi/Bluetooth) to track cross-service activity, personalize content (ads/search results), and maintain synchronization (e.g., Chrome browsing history, Android device telemetry).

- **Third-Party Integration**: Shares anonymized/pseudonymous data with partners for ads/research, while also ingesting public/business data (e.g., directory services) and enforcing security controls (e.g., encryption, abuse detection) per [data transfer frameworks](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks).
