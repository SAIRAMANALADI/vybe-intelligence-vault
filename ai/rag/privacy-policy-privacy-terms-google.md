# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (signed-in) and device/browser-associated (signed-out) data such as IP addresses, unique identifiers, device specs, and activity logs (searches, interactions, location).

- **Location & Sensor Data**: Collects multi-source location data (GPS, Wi-Fi access points, IP geolocation, sensor inputs) and stores it based on device/account settings, enabling features like Timeline and location-based ads.

- **Third-Party & Public Data Integration**: Incorporates data from public sources (e.g., news articles) and trusted partners (e.g., marketing, security, or directory services) for advertising, abuse prevention, and service personalization.
