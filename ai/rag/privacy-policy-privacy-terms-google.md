# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Android) including account-linked (personal info, activity) and non-account data (unique identifiers, device/browser metadata, IP addresses) to enable personalization, security, and service optimization.

- **Location & Activity Tracking**: Location data (GPS, IP, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth signals) and user activity (searches, interactions, media) are collected and stored based on account/device settings, with retention tied to services like Timeline and Web & App Activity.

- **Third-Party & Public Data Integration**: Google supplements data via public sources (e.g., indexed news articles) and trusted partners (e.g., marketing directories) to enhance service functionality, subject to compliance with privacy frameworks and user controls.
