# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (personal info, activity) and non-account-linked (unique identifiers, IP, device metadata) data, with retention policies tied to user settings and regulatory compliance.

- **Location & Activity Tracking**: Real-time location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, IP) and historical activity (searches, interactions) are collected, stored, and used for personalization (e.g., ads, recommendations), with granular controls via Google Account settings like Timeline and Web & App Activity.

- **Third-Party & Cross-Service Data Integration**: Data is shared with partners (e.g., advertisers, security firms) and aggregated from public sources, while technologies like cookies, pixel tags, and browser storage enable cross-service tracking and state management across sessions.
