# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (personal info, activity) and non-account-linked (unique identifiers, device/browser metadata, IP addresses) data for personalization, security, and analytics.

- **Location Tracking Mechanisms**: Location data is derived from GPS, IP addresses, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth signals, and user-labeled places (e.g., "home/work"), with retention tied to device/account settings (e.g., Timeline, Location History).

- **Cross-Service Integration**: Activity logs (e.g., searches, ads interactions, call logs) are synchronized across services and third-party integrations (e.g., embedded Maps, analytics), enabling unified profiling while offering granular privacy controls via Google Account settings.
