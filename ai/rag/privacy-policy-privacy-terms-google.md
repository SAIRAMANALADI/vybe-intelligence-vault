# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked info (emails, photos), device identifiers (IP, unique IDs), and activity logs (searches, interactions, location via GPS/Wi-Fi/cell towers) to personalize services and ads.

- **Cross-Service Tracking**: Activity (e.g., browsing history, app usage, calls/messages) is synchronized across devices/apps unless explicitly disabled, with storage tied to account settings or anonymized identifiers for non-signed-in users.

- **Third-Party & Public Data**: Google supplements data via public sources (e.g., news articles) and partners (e.g., marketing, security firms) using cookies, pixel tags, and local storage to enable cross-site tracking, analytics, and ad targeting.
