# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (Search, YouTube, Chrome, Android) including account-linked (emails, photos, docs) and non-account-linked (IP, device identifiers, browser settings) information, with granular controls for user privacy management.

- **Location & Activity Tracking**: Collects real-time GPS, Wi-Fi, and IP-based location data, alongside search history, app usage, and communication logs (calls, messages) to personalize services like ads and recommendations.

- **Third-Party & Cross-Device Data**: Integrates data from public sources, trusted partners (e.g., marketing, security), and embedded services (Google Maps, ads) while using cookies, pixel tags, and local storage to synchronize user preferences and activity across devices.
