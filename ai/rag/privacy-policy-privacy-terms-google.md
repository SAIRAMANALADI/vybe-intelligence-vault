# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (personal info, activity history) and non-account-linked (device identifiers, IP, sensor data) information, with retention tied to user settings and regulatory compliance.

- **Cross-Platform Tracking**: Activity data (searches, interactions, location) is collected via unique identifiers, cookies, and device telemetry, enabling cross-service personalization (e.g., ads, recommendations) while allowing granular control via account settings.

- **Security & Compliance**: Data is secured via encryption (in transit/at rest) and access controls, with export/deletion mechanisms (e.g., Google Takeout) and adherence to frameworks like GDPR/CCPA, including periodic audits and regulator cooperation.
