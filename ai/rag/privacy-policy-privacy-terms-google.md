# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Android) including account-linked (personal info, activity) and non-account-linked (unique identifiers, device/browser metadata, IP) data to personalize services and ads.
- **Location & Activity Tracking**: Collects precise location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, IP) and activity logs (searches, interactions, communications) with retention tied to account settings (e.g., Timeline, Web & App Activity).
- **Third-Party & Cross-Service Integration**: Data is shared with partners for ads/research, indexed from public sources, and synchronized across devices (e.g., Chrome sync) with granular user controls via Google Account.
