# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google collects user data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked info (emails, photos) and device/browser metadata (IP, OS, unique identifiers), with or without a Google Account.

- **Location & Activity Tracking**: Location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, IP) and activity logs (searches, interactions, calls) are gathered to personalize services, with storage duration dependent on user settings (e.g., Timeline, Web & App Activity).

- **Third-Party & Public Data**: Google aggregates data from public sources (e.g., news articles) and trusted partners (e.g., marketing/security firms) for ads, research, and abuse prevention, using technologies like cookies and pixel tags.
