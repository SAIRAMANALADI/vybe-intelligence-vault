# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including account-linked (personal info, activity) and non-account-linked (unique IDs, device/browser metadata) data, with retention tied to account settings and regulatory compliance.

- **Cross-Platform Tracking**: Activity data (searches, interactions, location) is collected via IP, sensor inputs, and third-party integrations, enabling personalized services (ads, recommendations) while allowing user control via privacy settings (e.g., Timeline, Incognito).

- **Third-Party & Public Data**: Google supplements data via trusted partners (marketing, security) and public sources (e.g., indexed news), using technologies like cookies/pixel tags for cross-site tracking, with deletion/export options available via Google Account.
