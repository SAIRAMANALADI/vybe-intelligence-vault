# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Android) including account-linked personal data (emails, photos), device identifiers (IP, unique IDs), and activity logs (searches, watch history, location via GPS/Wi-Fi/cell towers), with storage duration dependent on user settings and service-specific policies.

- **Cross-Service Tracking**: Activity data (e.g., Chrome browsing history, app usage, third-party site interactions) is synchronized to user accounts unless disabled, enabling personalized services (ads, recommendations) while allowing granular privacy controls via Google Account settings.

- **Public & Partner Data**: Google may ingest publicly available information (e.g., indexed news articles) and partner-sourced data (e.g., business directories) for service enhancement, with compliance governed by frameworks like GDPR and CCPA.
