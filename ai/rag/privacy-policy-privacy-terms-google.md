# Privacy Policy – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google aggregates data across services (e.g., Search, YouTube, Chrome) including user-provided content (emails, photos), device metadata (IP, OS, carrier), activity logs (searches, interactions), and location data (GPS, Wi-Fi access points), with retention tied to account settings and regulatory compliance.

- **Cross-Service Tracking**: Unique identifiers (browser/app/device-level) enable persistent tracking across sessions, enabling personalized features (e.g., language preferences, ad targeting) even when users are signed out, while signed-in accounts link data to personal information for enhanced profiling.

- **Third-Party & Public Data Integration**: Google supplements user data with publicly available sources (e.g., news articles) and trusted partner data (e.g., marketing partners, security firms) for ads, abuse prevention, and business services, using technologies like cookies, pixel tags, and local storage for collection/storage.
