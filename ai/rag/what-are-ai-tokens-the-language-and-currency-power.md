# What Are AI Tokens? The Language and Currency Powering Modern AI | NVIDIA Blog

## Summary

- **Tokenization Process**: AI models convert raw data (text, images, audio) into numerical tokens via tokenization, enabling structured processing; shared subword tokens (e.g., "ness") help models infer semantic relationships, while context-dependent tokens (e.g., "lie") require disambiguation during training.

- **Token Economics in AI Workflows**: Tokens drive computational cost and revenue in AI systems, with pricing models based on input/output token rates; latency metrics (time-to-first-token, inter-token latency) and throughput determine user experience and scalability in inference pipelines.

- **Optimization via AI Factories**: NVIDIA’s full-stack AI platforms (software, microservices, GPUs) reduce cost-per-token by 20x and increase revenue by 25x through optimized token processing, leveraging techniques like test-time scaling (long thinking) and context window management for high-volume inference.
