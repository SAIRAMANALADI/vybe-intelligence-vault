# 17 U.S. Code § 107 - Limitations on exclusive rights: Fair use | U.S. Code | US Law | LII / Legal Information Institute

## Summary

- **Fair Use Doctrine**: Section 107 of the U.S. Copyright Act codifies the fair use doctrine, permitting limited use of copyrighted material without infringement for purposes such as criticism, comment, news reporting, teaching, scholarship, or research, despite exclusive rights under §106.

- **Four-Factor Test**: Fair use determination hinges on four statutory factors: (1) purpose/character of use (commercial vs. nonprofit educational), (2) nature of the copyrighted work, (3) amount/substantiality of the portion used relative to the whole work, and (4) effect on the work’s potential market/value.

- **Unpublished Works & Case-by-Case Analysis**: Unpublished works are not automatically excluded from fair use; each case is evaluated individually based on the four-factor test, with no rigid statutory definitions to accommodate evolving technological and contextual circumstances.
