# Website Privacy Notice | Twilio

## Summary

- **Data Processing Roles & Scope**: Twilio acts as both **Data Controller** (defining purposes/means of processing) and **Data Processor** (processing data per customer instructions), governed by Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) for global compliance.

- **Data Categories & Sources**: Processes personal data from direct input (contact/account details, customer content), automated collection (metadata, device/IP data, CPNI in the U.S.), and third parties (telecom operators, enrichment services, authentication providers).

- **Legal Bases & Purposes**: Processes data for **account management** (KYC, billing), **business operations** (financial management, risk assessment), and **security/fraud prevention** (AI/ML threat detection, CPNI compliance), relying on **consent**, **legitimate interest**, or **legal obligation** under frameworks like GDPR.
