# [2407.10930] Fine-Tuning and Prompt Optimization: Two Great Steps that Work Better Together

## Summary

- **Joint Optimization Framework**: Proposes *BetterTogether*, an alternating optimization strategy combining weight fine-tuning and prompt optimization for modular NLP pipelines (e.g., RAG), achieving up to **60% and 6% average performance gains** over weight-only or prompt-only optimization across tasks (multi-hop QA, math reasoning, classification) using models like Mistral-7B, Llama-2-7B, and Llama-3-8B.

- **Modular Pipeline Challenges**: Addresses the lack of intermediate supervision/gradient flow in compound systems (e.g., RAG), where distinct LMs and prompts require end-to-end optimization without explicit labels or direct gradient propagation.

- **Implementation & Release**: Implemented in **DSPy** (open-source framework) with released optimizer code at [dspy.ai](http://dspy.ai), enabling self-teaching LMs to iteratively refine both weights and prompts for downstream task metrics.
