# [2407.10930] Fine-Tuning and Prompt Optimization: Two Great Steps that Work Better Together

## Summary

- **Joint Optimization Framework**: Proposes *BetterTogether*, an alternating weight and prompt optimization strategy for modular NLP pipelines (e.g., RAG), achieving up to **60% and 6% gains** over weight-only or prompt-only optimization, respectively.

- **Task Validation**: Demonstrates effectiveness on **multi-hop QA, mathematical reasoning, and feature-based classification** using LLMs (mistral-7b, llama-2-7b, llama-3-8b) with consistent performance improvements.

- **Implementation**: Released as part of **DSPy** (http://dspy.ai), enabling end-to-end pipeline optimization without intermediate labels or gradient flow.
