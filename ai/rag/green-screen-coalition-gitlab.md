# Green Screen Coalition · GitLab

## Summary

- **Purpose**: The Green Screen Coalition is a collaborative initiative focused on advancing sustainable and environmentally friendly practices within the technology and open-source communities.

- **Key Objectives**: It aims to reduce the carbon footprint of digital infrastructure, promote energy-efficient computing, and foster the development of green technologies through shared resources and collective action.

- **Community & Resources**: The coalition provides a platform for developers, researchers, and organizations to share tools, methodologies, and best practices for achieving sustainability in tech projects, leveraging open-source principles.
