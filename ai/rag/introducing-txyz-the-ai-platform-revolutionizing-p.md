## Summary

- **TXYZ-Writing**: AI-driven writing assistant that synthesizes user-uploaded documents, notes, and citations into structured drafts, offering real-time suggestions for titles, structure, and citations via multi-agent workflows.

- **Dual-Function Workflow**: Combines PDF reading and writing into a cohesive AI copilot for knowledge workers, optimizing research-to-documentation processes through interactive "interview" sessions and AI agent collaboration.
