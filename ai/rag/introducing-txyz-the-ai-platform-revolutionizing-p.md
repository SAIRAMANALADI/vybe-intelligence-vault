## Summary

- **TXYZ AI Platform**: Enables interactive, context-aware Q&A for academic PDFs with real-time summarization, key point extraction, and page navigation, outperforming static tools like ChatGPT for document-specific queries.

- **TXYZ-Writing Feature**: AI-driven writing assistant with multi-agent workflow (titles, structure, citations) that synthesizes user-uploaded documents, notes, and citations into structured academic content via interview-style sessions.
