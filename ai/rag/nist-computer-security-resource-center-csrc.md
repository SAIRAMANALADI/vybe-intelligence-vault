# NIST Computer Security Resource Center | CSRC

## Summary

- **NIST CSRC** serves as a centralized repository for cybersecurity projects, publications, and standards, including **SP 800-53**, **FIPS**, and **post-quantum cryptography (PQC)** initiatives, supporting government, industry, and academia globally.

- **Recent technical updates** include **SP 800-63-4 (Digital Identity Guidelines)**, **SP 800-232 (Ascon-based lightweight cryptography for constrained devices)**, and **PIV standard revisions** to integrate PQC for future-proofing credentials.

- **Operational resources** like the **Cybersecurity and Privacy Reference Tool (CPRT)** and **SCAP 1.4 (SP 800-126r4)** provide standardized frameworks for risk management, compliance, and automated security content validation.
