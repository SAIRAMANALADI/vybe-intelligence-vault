# Data protection adequacy for non-EU countries

## Summary

- **Adequacy Decision Process**: EU adequacy decisions under **Article 45 GDPR** require a **Commission proposal**, **EDPB opinion**, **EU Council approval**, and **Commission adoption**; Parliament/Council may challenge decisions if they exceed implementing powers.

- **Current Adequacy List**: The EU has recognized **15 countries/territories** (e.g., Japan, South Korea, UK, Brazil) as providing adequate data protection, enabling **free data flows** without additional safeguards, excluding law enforcement transfers under **Directive (EU) 2016/680**.

- **Periodic Reviews & Updates**: The Commission conducts **mandatory periodic reviews** of adequacy decisions (e.g., Japan 2023, Korea 2026) and has recently **renewed UK adequacy** (Dec 2025) and **adopted Brazil’s adequacy** (Feb 2026).
