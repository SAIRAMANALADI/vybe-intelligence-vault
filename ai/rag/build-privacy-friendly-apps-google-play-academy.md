# Build privacy-friendly apps : Google Play Academy

## Summary

- **Data Transparency**: Clearly disclose data collection/sharing practices in app design to comply with Google Play's safety and trust policies, ensuring user safety and regulatory adherence.

- **Data Handling Evaluation**: Assess your app's (and third-party dependencies') data handling mechanisms to identify privacy risks and implement necessary safeguards or mitigations.

- **Privacy Policy Requirements**: Include explicit, accessible privacy policy documentation detailing data usage, storage, and sharing practices, aligned with Google Play’s data safety requirements.
