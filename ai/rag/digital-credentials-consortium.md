# Digital Credentials Consortium

## Summary

- **Consortium Overview**: Founded in 2018 by leading universities, the Digital Credentials Consortium (DCC) develops open-source infrastructure for privacy-enhanced, portable, and verifiable digital academic credentials, leveraging blockchain and decentralized identity technologies.

- **Mission & Infrastructure**: Aims to standardize digital credential issuance, storage, display, and verification via a trusted, distributed system, replacing traditional paper-based methods to align with modern digital education ecosystems.

- **Technical Focus**: Advocates for interoperable standards (e.g., W3C Verifiable Credentials) and provides resources (knowledge base, GitHub) to support research, advocacy, and implementation of decentralized credential systems in higher education.
