# Working List of Hard Problems in AI - AI2050

## Summary

- **General AI Capabilities**: Address fundamental limitations in AI generalizability, causal reasoning, meta-cognition, multi-agent systems, and novel compute architectures to enable breakthrough progress toward AGI, including resource efficiency and knowledge generation.

- **AI Safety & Robustness**: Develop frameworks for bias mitigation, toxicity reduction, factuality assurance, security hardening, privacy preservation, and explainability to ensure reliability and public trust in safety-critical AI applications.

- **Alignment & Governance**: Advance AI alignment techniques to align systems with human values, while establishing adaptive governance models to address geopolitical, economic, and societal impacts as AI capabilities co-evolve with usage patterns.
