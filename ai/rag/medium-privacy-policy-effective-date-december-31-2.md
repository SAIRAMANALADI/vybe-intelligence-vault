# Medium Privacy Policy - Effective date December 31, 2019 through August 31, 2020 – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Medium collects user interactions (e.g., page views, saves, follows) and device/browser metadata to personalize content, enforce paywall limits, and improve services; no third-party advertising data sharing occurs.
- **EU GDPR Compliance**: Processing of EU users' data is based on contractual obligations, legitimate interests (minimal logged-out tracking), and explicit user actions (e.g., public posts); special category data is only processed if manifestly public.
- **Third-Party Embeds & Security**: Third-party content (e.g., YouTube, Twitter) embedded in posts sends data to their respective services; Medium uses HTTPS/TLS but cannot guarantee absolute security, and users must manage embedded forms' privacy risks independently.
