# Medium Privacy Policy - Effective date December 31, 2019 through August 31, 2020 – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Medium collects user interactions (e.g., page views, saves, follows) and device/browser metadata to personalize content, enforce paywalls, and improve services; no third-party advertising tracking is performed. Logs include server/event data, stored in the U.S. or other operating regions.

- **Third-Party Embeds & Security**: Embedded content (e.g., YouTube, Twitter) transmits data to external services, governed by their policies. Medium uses HTTPS/TLS for data transmission but cannot guarantee absolute security; users must manage account security risks independently.

- **GDPR Compliance (EU Users)**: Processing legal bases include contractual obligations and legitimate interests (e.g., fraud prevention). Public interactions (e.g., claps, posts) are considered manifestly public under GDPR. Minimal data is shared with processors (e.g., Stripe, AWS) under strict data protection standards.
