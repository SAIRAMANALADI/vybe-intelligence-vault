# Medium Privacy Policy - Effective date December 31, 2019 through August 31, 2020 – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Medium collects user interactions (e.g., page views, saves, highlights) and device/browser metadata to personalize content, enforce paywall limits, and improve services. No third-party advertising tracking is performed; data is used solely for product functionality and abuse prevention.

- **Third-Party Embeds & Data Sharing**: Embedded content (e.g., YouTube, Twitter) transmits user data directly to third parties, governed by their policies. Medium shares minimal data with service providers (e.g., AWS, Google Analytics) under contractual data protection standards, excluding advertising or resale.

- **EU GDPR Compliance**: For EU users, processing is based on contractual obligations (e.g., paywall enforcement) and legitimate interests (e.g., spam prevention). Public interactions (e.g., claps, posts) are considered manifestly public; non-users' data in user-generated content is processed per GDPR rights.
