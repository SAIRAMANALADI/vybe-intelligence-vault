# Medium Privacy Policy - Effective date December 31, 2019 through August 31, 2020 – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Medium collects user interactions (e.g., page views, saves, highlights) and device/browser metadata to personalize content, enforce paywall limits, and improve services, while explicitly avoiding third-party advertising tracking.

- **Third-Party Embeds & Legal Compliance**: Embedded content (e.g., YouTube, Twitter) transmits data to external services, governed by their policies; Medium may disclose user data under legal obligations or with consent, but resists improper requests and notifies users unless prohibited.

- **EU GDPR Compliance**: For EU users, Medium processes data under contractual obligations or legitimate interests (e.g., fraud prevention), with minimal logged-out tracking; public interactions (claps, posts) are considered manifestly public, and third-party processors adhere to equivalent data protection standards.
