# Google Family Link - Children's Privacy Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Google collects personal data (e.g., name, email, birthdate) and usage-derived metadata (e.g., search queries, YouTube activity, location via GPS/IP) from child accounts under Family Link, with restrictions on personalized advertising for minors.

- **Automated Processing & Sharing**: Uses algorithms to analyze child data for abuse detection, service improvement, and cross-service personalization; shares non-personalized data externally under legal requests or with family groups, while prohibiting personalized ads to minors.

- **Parental Controls & Deletion**: Parents can manage child accounts via Family Link (access, restrict, or delete data), though children retain limited control over app permissions and activity deletion; full account/profile deletion triggers permanent data erasure.
