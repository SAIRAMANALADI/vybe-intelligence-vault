# Immigrants Belong Toolkit - Define American

## Summary

- **Toolkit Overview**: The *Immigrants Belong Toolkit* is a research-backed resource developed by Define American in collaboration with Tides Foundation and Harmony Labs, providing digital storytellers with evidence-based strategies to counter harmful immigration narratives and humanize immigrant experiences.

- **Target Audience & Methodology**: Focuses on influencing the *Moveable Middle*—a persuadable audience receptive to narrative shifts—by leveraging social psychology research and digital landscape analysis to optimize messaging and visual content for maximum impact.

- **Practical Application**: Offers actionable frameworks for combating misinformation, emphasizing storytelling as a tool for narrative change, and includes downloadable guides for content creators working within the immigrant rights movement.
