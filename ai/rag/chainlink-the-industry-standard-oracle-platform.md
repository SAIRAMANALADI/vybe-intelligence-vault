# Chainlink: The Industry-Standard Oracle Platform

## Summary

- **Unified Oracle Infrastructure**: Chainlink provides a single platform integrating data feeds, cross-chain interoperability (CCIP), privacy (Chainlink Privacy Standard), asset management (DTA), compliance (ACE), and compute (Functions, Automation, VRF) for onchain finance.

- **Cross-Chain & Institutional Adoption**: CCIP enables secure data/value transfer across blockchains, while partnerships with institutions (DTCC, Swift, Euroclear, Mastercard) and AWS Marketplace integration standardize institutional tokenization and collateral management.

- **Compute & Automation Stack**: Functions connect smart contracts to APIs, Automation enables decentralized triggers, and VRF ensures verifiable randomness for DeFi, NFTs, and prediction markets, with Data Streams providing high-frequency market data.
