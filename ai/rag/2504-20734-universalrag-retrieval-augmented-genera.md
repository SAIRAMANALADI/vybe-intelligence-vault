# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: UniversalRAG dynamically selects modality-specific corpora for retrieval to mitigate modality gaps, where unified representation spaces favor same-modality items, improving cross-modal knowledge integration.

- **Granularity-level organization**: Each modality is structured into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval based on query complexity and scope, enhancing precision across diverse knowledge types.

- **Superior performance**: Evaluated on 10 multi-modal benchmarks, UniversalRAG outperforms both modality-specific and unified baselines, demonstrating robust any-to-any RAG capabilities across heterogeneous corpora.
