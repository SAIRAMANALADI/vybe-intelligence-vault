# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-aware routing**: Dynamically selects the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated via theoretical analysis.

- **Granularity-aware retrieval**: Organizes each modality into multiple granularity levels, enabling fine-tuned retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope.

- **Performance validation**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning diverse modalities, as presented in the ACL 2026 submission.
