# [2504.20734] UniversalRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Corpora of Diverse Modalities and Granularities

## Summary

- **Modality-Aware Routing**: Introduces a dynamic mechanism to select the most relevant modality-specific corpus for retrieval, addressing the "modality gap" where unified representation spaces favor same-modality retrieval, validated theoretically and empirically.

- **Multi-Granularity Retrieval**: Organizes each modality into hierarchical granularity levels, enabling fine-grained retrieval tailored to query complexity and scope, improving adaptability across diverse knowledge sources.

- **UniversalRAG Framework**: Demonstrates superiority over modality-specific and unified baselines across 10 benchmarks spanning multiple modalities, proving its any-to-any RAG capability for heterogeneous corpora.
