# Webinar: The New Fraud Playbook: Winning Against GenAI-Powered Scams by Twilio

## Summary

- **Fraud projection**: GenAI-driven phishing/social engineering projected to cause $63.9B in losses by 2028, with 70% of orgs lacking behavioral/device signals in authentication and 78% experiencing repeat ATOs.

- **Technical focus**: Session highlights merging authentication with passive probabilistic signals (behavioral, device, phone intelligence) to improve fraud detection accuracy without increasing user friction.

- **Agenda specifics**: Covers fast-impact controls for GenAI scams, layered authentication flows, and placement of probabilistic signals in step-up, recovery, and in-session monitoring.
