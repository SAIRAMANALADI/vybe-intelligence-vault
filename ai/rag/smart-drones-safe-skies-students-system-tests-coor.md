# Smart drones, safe skies: Student’s system tests, coordinates drone fleets | Cornell Chronicle

## Summary

- **Project Orion** is an autonomous airspace coordination system developed by Mehrnaz Sabet at Cornell, integrating real-time simulation, field testing, vehicle-to-vehicle communication, and hardware-in-the-loop validation to test autonomous drone operations under realistic conditions.

- The system enables mixed-reality testing of drones in a simulated city-sized airspace with virtual obstacles, aircraft, and emergency scenarios, bridging the gap between offline simulation and real-world deployment for safety validation.

- Orion has been adopted by NASA, NIST, and industry partners (e.g., Qualcomm) for applications including public-safety drone research, incident reconstruction (e.g., 2025 Arizona crane collision), and future air traffic management systems.
