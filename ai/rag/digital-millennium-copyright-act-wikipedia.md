# Digital Millennium Copyright Act - Wikipedia

## Summary

- **Criminalizes circumvention of DRM**: Prohibits production, distribution, or use of tools/services designed to bypass technological measures controlling access to copyrighted works, regardless of actual infringement.

- **Heightens internet copyright penalties**: Increases legal consequences for copyright infringement online and amends Title 17 U.S.C. to extend copyright reach while limiting liability for compliant online service providers.

- **Implements WIPO treaties**: Enacts two 1996 World Intellectual Property Organization treaties (Copyright Treaty and Performances and Phonograms Treaty) into U.S. law, effective October 28, 1998.
