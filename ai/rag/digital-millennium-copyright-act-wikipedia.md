# Digital Millennium Copyright Act - Wikipedia

## Summary

- **Anti-Circumvention Provisions (17 U.S.C. § 1201)**: Criminalizes the production, distribution, or use of tools/services designed to bypass DRM or access controls on copyrighted works, regardless of actual infringement.

- **Safe Harbor Provisions (17 U.S.C. § 512)**: Limits liability of online service providers (OSPs) for user-generated copyright infringement if they comply with notice-and-takedown procedures and meet other statutory requirements.

- **Copyright Infringement Penalties**: Increases statutory damages and criminal penalties for willful copyright infringement, particularly on digital networks, while amending Title 17 to extend copyright reach.
