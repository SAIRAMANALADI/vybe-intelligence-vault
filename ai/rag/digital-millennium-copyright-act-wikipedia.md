# Digital Millennium Copyright Act - Wikipedia

## Summary

- **Criminalizes circumvention**: Prohibits production, distribution, or use of technologies/services designed to bypass DRM or access controls, even without actual copyright infringement (17 U.S.C. § 1201).

- **Liability limitations for ISPs**: Provides "safe harbor" provisions (17 U.S.C. § 512) shielding online service providers from direct/indirect liability for user-generated copyright infringement if they comply with takedown notices and implement repeat infringer policies.

- **Enhanced penalties**: Increases statutory damages and criminal penalties for copyright infringement on the internet, aligning U.S. law with WIPO treaties (1996) while amending Title 17 U.S.C. to extend copyright reach.
