# Energy Department Announces $352 Million for Energy Frontier Research Centers to Accelerate Science Underpinning Energy

## Summary

- **$352M DOE funding** for Energy Frontier Research Centers (EFRCs) to advance fundamental research in materials, chemistry, geosciences, and biosciences, supporting critical areas like quantum computing, advanced manufacturing, and critical minerals.

- **EFRC program** targets 10 key research topics, including unconventional computing, AI/ML for materials, nuclear energy, and microelectronics, with applications open to universities, national labs, nonprofits, and private companies.

- **EFRC legacy**: Since 2009, the program has trained 6,200+ researchers, produced 17,000+ publications, 780+ patent applications, and 270+ patents, fostering 135+ spin-off companies. FY2026 funding includes $88M with $264M contingent on congressional approval.
