# GDPR FAQs | Cloudflare

## Summary

- **Data Processing & Compliance**: Cloudflare processes minimal personal data (primarily IP addresses as metadata) for Core Application Layer Services, with no storage of customer content unless explicitly configured; customers retain full responsibility for GDPR compliance of transmitted data.

- **Security & Certifications**: Implements ISO 27001/27701/27018, SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS Level 1, EU Cloud Code of Conduct, and Germany’s C5 2020; uses encryption (e.g., DNS-over-HTTPS/TLS, Oblivious HTTP) and secure configurations for data protection.

- **Transfers & Legal Safeguards**: Relies on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and certifications under EU-U.S./Swiss-UK Data Privacy Frameworks; enforces supplementary measures per EDPB guidance and challenges conflicting U.S. legal requests via court remedies.
