# Call For Participation in TREC 2026

## Summary

- **TREC 2026 Tracks**: Introduces 7 tracks—AutoJudge (LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation), Change Detection (topic tracking with novelty/importance ranking), Million LLM (LLM ranking for query answering), RAG (retrieval + generation for evolving info needs), RAGTIME (multi-lingual report generation with citation-based eval), User Simulation (standardized simulator validation), and VQA (multi-modal QA with generation/MC subtasks).

- **Timeline & Submission**: Runs due between July–September (varies by track), relevance judgments by September 30, and in-person conference at NIST (Nov 30–Dec 3). Applications open via [Evalbase](https://ir.nist.gov/evalbase), with manual Slack/mailing list onboarding.

- **Evaluation Framework**: Tracks use standardized datasets, uniform scoring, and manual/automatic hybrid judgments (e.g., AutoJudge correlates LLM labels with ground truth). Prohibits advertising claims; results published in proceedings with org attribution.
