# Move Them or Lose Them - Define American

## Summary

- **Audience Segmentation**: Harmony Labs identified four key segments within the "Moveable Middle" open to pro-immigrant perspectives: *Taking Responsibility* (culture/locally invested), *Following The Plan* (authority-respecting/church-goers), *Doing My Own Thing* (autonomous/pleasure-focused), and *Getting It Done* (DIY/action-oriented, with 25% immigrants).

- **Narrative Strategies**: Effective storytelling leverages immigrant messengers, allies amplifying authentic voices, culture-first themes (food/sports/comedy), diverse character casts, explicit immigration status mentions with positive endings, and balanced explicit/implicit messaging to avoid political backlash.

- **Content Resonance**: Moveable audiences engage most with immigrant narratives through humor, caregiving, achievement, and daily-life depictions (e.g., working, parenting), while avoiding overt political framing or adversarial tones.
