# Dropbox AI Principles

## Summary

- **Customer-Centric AI Deployment**: AI integration prioritizes enhanced user experiences without monetizing customer data or leveraging it for secondary purposes.
- **Explicit Data Control & Consent**: Prohibits training generative AI models on customer content without explicit consent, ensuring data privacy and user autonomy.
- **Ethical AI Governance**: Enforces transparency in AI operations, champions fairness/bias mitigation, and maintains accountability via continuous customer feedback and human oversight.
