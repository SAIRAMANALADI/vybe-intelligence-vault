# Define American | Home

## Summary

- **Mission & Founding**: Define American, founded in 2011 by Pulitzer-winning journalist Jose Antonio Vargas, focuses on humanizing immigrant narratives through research, partnerships, and multi-platform storytelling to foster belonging and challenge stereotypes.

- **Key Initiatives**: Launches tools like the *Immigrants Belong Toolkit* (2024) and *Move Them or Lose Them* report (2024), providing research-backed strategies for narrative change in media, social platforms, and advocacy campaigns.

- **Multimedia Engagement**: Produces content across YouTube (*Define American with Jose Antonio Vargas*), gaming (*Fortnite* activism), and entertainment partnerships, while operating a Shopify store and accepting donations to sustain narrative-driven impact.
