# The Santa Clara Principles on Transparency and Accountability in Content Moderation

## Summary

- **Scope Expansion**: Version 2.0 broadens transparency requirements to include all user-generated content (paid/unpaid) and enforcement actions (removal, downranking, account suspension), not just content removal.

- **Foundational Principles**: Mandates human rights integration, due process, culturally competent moderation, and state involvement disclosure, with implementation guidance for rules, policies, and appeal mechanisms.

- **Operational Transparency**: Requires granular reporting on enforcement actions, appeals, reinstatements, and automated system performance, including crisis-period and state-involved moderation data.
