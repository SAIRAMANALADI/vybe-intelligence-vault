# The Santa Clara Principles on Transparency and Accountability in Content Moderation

## Summary

- **Scope Expansion**: SCP 2.0 broadens transparency requirements to include all user-generated content (paid/unpaid) and enforcement actions (removal, downranking, account suspension) under the terms "content" and "action."

- **Foundational Principles**: Mandates integration of human rights/due process in moderation, clear publishable rules/policies, cultural competence in enforcement, state involvement disclosure, and system integrity/explainability with auditable automated processes.

- **Operational Transparency**: Requires granular reporting on enforcement metrics (appeals, reinstatements, regional breakdowns) and crisis-period restrictions, with special state-involvement disclosures by country.
