# SLAC-Stanford Battery Center

## Summary

- **Translational Research Hub**: The SLAC-Stanford Battery Center bridges fundamental electrochemical science and deployment, leveraging SLAC’s national lab capabilities, Stanford’s academic expertise, and Silicon Valley’s innovation ecosystem to accelerate battery/energy storage technologies for global climate mitigation.

- **Collaborative Launch Ecosystem**: The center’s April 2023 launch event unified researchers, industry leaders, and policymakers to align cutting-edge research with real-world deployment, emphasizing cross-sector collaboration to scale sustainable energy solutions.

- **Technical Focus Areas**: Key research thrusts include inexpensive battery longevity improvements, disorder-engineered battery designs, AI-driven catalysis tools (DOE Early Career Awards), and sodium-ion vs. Li-ion performance optimization, supported by dedicated beamline infrastructure.
