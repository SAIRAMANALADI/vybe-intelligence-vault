# Android Apps on Google Play

## Summary

- Google Play Games on PC supports cross-platform play, enabling seamless synchronization of game progress and in-app purchases across Windows and Android devices via a unified Google account.

- The platform features a curated library of games optimized for multiple device types (Windows, Android phones/tablets, Chromebooks, TVs, XR headsets) with native performance scaling and input adaptation.

- Google Play Games integrates with Play Pass for subscription-based access to premium titles, while also supporting third-party app stores and offering granular personalization settings for user experience optimization.
