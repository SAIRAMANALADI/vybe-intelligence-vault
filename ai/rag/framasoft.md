# Framasoft

## Summary

- **Mission & Structure**: Framasoft is a French nonprofit (association loi 1901) founded in 2004, operating with ~10 employees and 20+ volunteers, funded exclusively by donations. Focuses on digital emancipation, education populaire, and archipelization of ethical tech ecosystems.

- **Core Offerings**: Develops and hosts **free/libre services** (e.g., Framadate, Framaforms, PeerTube, Framaspace) as alternatives to GAFAM tools. Contributes to CHATONS collective (ethical hosting providers) and publishes open-source software under copyleft licenses.

- **Political & Ethical Stance**: Explicitly anti-capitalist, opposing surveillance capitalism by promoting digital sovereignty, commons-based collaboration, and cooperative tech governance. Publishes a manifesto and advocates for systemic change in digital practices.
