# Graduate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning | Computer & Data Science Online

## Summary

- **Curriculum Structure**: 4-course (12-credit) program with 2 core courses (*Machine Learning* and *Deep Learning*) and 2 electives from a portfolio including *Advances in Deep Generative Models*, *AI in Healthcare*, *Case Studies in Machine Learning*, and *Ethics in AI*.

- **Core Course Focus**:
  - *Machine Learning*: Covers supervised/unsupervised learning, PAC learning, regression, dimensionality reduction, Bayesian methods, and neural networks with hands-on implementations.
  - *Deep Learning*: Focuses on optimization, computer vision, NLP, reinforcement learning, and generative models, with PyTorch-based assignments (e.g., training vision systems for SuperTuxKart).

- **Specializations & Stackability**: Offers stackable badges (e.g., *AstroAI* for astronomy applications) and allows certificate credits to apply toward UT Austin’s Master’s in AI, with tuition priced at $5,000.
