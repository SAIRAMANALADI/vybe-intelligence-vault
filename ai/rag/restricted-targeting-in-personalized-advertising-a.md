# Restricted targeting in Personalized advertising - Advertising Policies Help

## Summary

- **Sensitive Interest Categories**: Advertisers promoting products/services in sensitive categories (e.g., alcohol, gambling, health, political content) **cannot use advertiser-curated audiences** (e.g., Customer Match, Your Data Segments) but **may use predefined Google audiences** (e.g., In-market, Affinity, Demographics with exceptions) due to automatic exclusion of sensitive signals.

- **Access to Opportunities Categories (US/Canada)**: Ads in housing, employment, or consumer finance **cannot target demographics** (gender, age, parental/marital status) or **ZIP codes**; only radius-based geographic targeting (≥1km) or postal code prefixes (FSA in Canada) are permitted, using predefined Google audiences.

- **Data Collection & PII Restrictions**: Personalized ads **must not collect/store PII** (e.g., emails, phone numbers) in ad content or data segments; precise location targeting requires user consent; overly narrow audience targeting (e.g., <100 users) is prohibited.
