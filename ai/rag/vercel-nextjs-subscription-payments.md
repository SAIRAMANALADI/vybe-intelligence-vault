# vercel/nextjs-subscription-payments

## Summary

- **Tech Stack**: Next.js (TypeScript) starter template integrating Stripe Checkout for payments, Stripe Customer Portal for subscription management, and Supabase for database/storage, optimized for Vercel deployment.

- **Key Features**: Pre-configured SaaS subscription flow with Stripe webhooks, customer authentication, and Supabase-based user/session management for handling recurring payments.

- **Deployment**: One-click Vercel deployment with environment variables for Stripe/Supabase keys, enabling rapid customization of UI, pricing tiers, and business logic.
