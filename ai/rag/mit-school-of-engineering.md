# MIT School of Engineering

## Summary

- **MIT's interdisciplinary research focus**: Addresses global challenges in climate & energy, health & life sciences, AI & machine learning, and design & manufacturing through cross-departmental collaboration (e.g., AeroAstro, EECS, DMSE, IMES).

- **Departmental specializations**: Key departments (ChemE, CEE, NSE, IDSS) integrate core scientific principles with engineering to advance clean energy, sustainable infrastructure, nuclear technology, and data-driven societal solutions.

- **Innovation pipeline**: Combines rigorous academic training (UROPs, graduate research) with entrepreneurship (venture capital, startups) and policy integration (Technology and Policy Program, STAR Lab) to translate lab breakthroughs into real-world impact.
