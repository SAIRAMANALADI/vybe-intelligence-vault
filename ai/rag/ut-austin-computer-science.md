# UT Austin Computer Science

## Summary

- **UT Austin Villa's RoboCup 2026 Preparation**: UT Austin Villa is actively preparing for RoboCup 2026, focusing on advancing robotic capabilities in areas such as social interaction, surgical assistance, rehabilitation, autonomous vehicles, and defense applications.

- **Multi-Disciplinary Robotics Research**: Texas Robotics at UT Austin integrates expertise from top-ten departments to advance robotics across diverse domains, including space, nuclear, and manufacturing, leveraging collaborative research efforts.

- **AI-Driven Supply Chain Disruption Prediction**: The Machine Learning Laboratory at UT Austin, including initiatives like the Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence program, has enabled students like Ramakrishna Garine to develop open-source AI tools (e.g., ResilienceXAI) for real-time supply chain disruption prediction and response.
