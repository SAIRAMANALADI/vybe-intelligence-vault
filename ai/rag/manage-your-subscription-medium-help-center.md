# Manage your subscription – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Accessing Subscription Details**: View/edit subscription plan, billing period, and payment info via **Settings > Membership and payment** (web/iOS/Android); credit card updates done here, while PayPal/iTunes/Google Play require external store management.

- **Plan Modification & Cancellation**: Change billing frequency (monthly/annual) via **Settings > Membership and payment** (web) or store-specific flows (iTunes: *Settings > [Name] > iTunes & App Store > Subscriptions*; Google Play: *Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions*); cancellation retains access until billing cycle end.

- **Billing History & Invoices**: Retrieve invoices via **Settings > Membership billing history** (web, credit card/PayPal), iTunes *Purchase History* (Apple Support), or Google Play *Purchase history* (Google Pay Help); VAT invoices require additional Google Pay steps.
