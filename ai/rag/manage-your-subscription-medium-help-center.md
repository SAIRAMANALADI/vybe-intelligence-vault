# Manage your subscription – Medium Help Center

## Summary

- **Subscription Management**: Access subscription details (plan, billing period, payment info) via **Settings > Membership and payment**; update credit card details directly or via PayPal/iTunes/Google Play for store-based subscriptions.
- **Plan Modification & Cancellation**: Change subscription tiers (monthly/annual) via **Settings** or store-specific flows (iTunes: *Settings > [name] > iTunes & App Store > Subscriptions*; Google Play: *Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions*); cancel via **Settings > Cancel membership** or respective store—uninstalling/deleting account does not auto-cancel store-managed subscriptions.
- **Billing & Invoices**: Retrieve invoices via **Settings > Membership billing history** (credit card/PayPal) or store systems (iTunes: *Purchase History*; Google Play: *Account > Purchase history*); VAT invoices require additional steps via Google Pay Help.
