# Energy, Environment & Sustainability | Cornell Chronicle

## Summary

- **Climate Impact on Dairy**: Rising temperatures reduce milk production efficiency by 10-25% in dairy cows due to heat stress, compromising both quantity and quality via reduced protein/fat content and altered lactation cycles.

- **eDNA Biodiversity Monitoring**: Cornell researchers and Indigenous partners in Ecuador employ environmental DNA (eDNA) sequencing to map aquatic biodiversity, enabling non-invasive species detection and conservation prioritization in fragile ecosystems.

- **Battery Mineral Recycling**: A novel electrochemical bath process recovers >95% of critical minerals (e.g., lithium, cobalt) from spent batteries via selective leaching and electrodeposition, reducing reliance on virgin mining and lowering lifecycle emissions by ~40%.
