# AP Stylebook Study Guides

## Summary

- **Web-based interactive study guides** developed in collaboration with ACES: The Society for Editing experts, covering AP style mechanics (grammar, capitalization, punctuation, numerals) and content (inclusive storytelling, health/science, business, religion) via quizzes with automatic scoring and rule explanations.

- **Key features** include mobile optimization, retakeable quizzes, score tracking, and social media sharing; designed to reinforce AP style rules without rote memorization, with full Stylebook entry references for incorrect answers.

- **Accessible via APStylebook.com** (requires JavaScript/cookies; no Flash/plugins), priced at $29.99 with auto-renewal discounts; integrates with other Stylebook products for deeper learning.
