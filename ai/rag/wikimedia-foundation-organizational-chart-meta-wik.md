# Wikimedia Foundation/Organizational chart - Meta-Wiki

## Summary

- **Leadership Structure**: Wikimedia Foundation's organizational chart is structured hierarchically with departments led by senior directors and teams led by team leaders, updated quarterly for accuracy and privacy.

- **Key Departments**: Core departments include Office of CEO (led by Bernadette Meehan), Advancement (led by Lisa Seitz Gruwell), Communications, Finance and Administration, Legal, Product and Technology, and People.

- **Specialized Teams**: Advancement department includes specialized teams such as Community Growth (led by Jorge Vargas and Simona Ramkisson) and Partnerships (with regional leads like Felix Nartey for Sub-Saharan Africa and Brisa Ceccon for Latin America).
