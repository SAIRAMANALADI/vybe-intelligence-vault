# CyberSunil/LLMVault

## Summary

- **Purpose-built vulnerable platform**: LLMVault is a Dockerized Python-based CTF designed to simulate OWASP LLM Top 10 vulnerabilities, enabling hands-on training in AI security, prompt injection, RAG security, agent security, and GenAI penetration testing.

- **Targeted attack vectors**: Focuses on critical attack surfaces including prompt injection, adversarial manipulation of RAG systems, and exploitation of LLM agents, providing a controlled environment for red-team exercises.

- **Educational & testing utility**: Serves as both a learning tool for security professionals and a benchmarking platform for evaluating defenses against LLM-specific threats, with active maintenance and community contributions.
