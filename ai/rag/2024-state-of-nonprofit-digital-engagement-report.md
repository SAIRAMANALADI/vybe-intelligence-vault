# 2024 State of Nonprofit Digital Engagement Report | Twilio

## Summary

- **AI Adoption & Engagement**: 58% of nonprofits use AI in digital communications, outperforming B2C businesses (47%), with 90% leveraging AI across multiple use cases to enhance end-user engagement.

- **Developer Investment & ROI**: Increased hiring of developers correlates with measurable return on investment (ROI), enabling nonprofits to optimize digital strategies and improve operational efficiency.

- **AI Transparency Disparity**: While 90% of nonprofits claim AI transparency, only 50% of end users agree, highlighting a critical gap in perceived vs. actual AI usage and communication in nonprofit digital engagement.
