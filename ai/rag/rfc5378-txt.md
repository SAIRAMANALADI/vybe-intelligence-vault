# rfc5378.txt

## Summary

- **Rights Granting Mechanism**: Contributors must grant the IETF Trust non-exclusive rights to publish, distribute, and create derivative works (including translations) from IETF Contributions (Internet-Drafts/RFCs), while retaining all other rights; the IETF Trust sublicenses these rights to IETF participants for standards development.

- **Legal Assurances & Constraints**: Contributors must warrant they possess the authority to grant these rights, with no retroactive effect on pre-existing contributions; proprietary or externally republished documents are exempt from derivative work rights.

- **Document Processing Rules**: Derivative work rights (e.g., translations) are mandatory for Standards Track RFCs and most non-Standards Track documents; limitations on derivative rights may be temporarily included but must be removed if the contribution is adopted for development.
