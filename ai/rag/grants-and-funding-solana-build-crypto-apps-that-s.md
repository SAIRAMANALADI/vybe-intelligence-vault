# Grants and Funding | Solana: Build crypto apps that scale

## Summary

- **Solana Foundation Funding Program** provides milestone-based grants (standard & convertible) and RFPs for public goods, requiring applicants to demonstrate open-source contributions, Solana-specific utility, and clear budget/milestone structures.
- **Ecosystem Funding** includes hackathons (via Colosseum) offering pre-seed funding/mentorship and vertical-specific grants programs (e.g., Superteam’s $10k microgrants for emerging markets).
- **Evaluation Criteria** prioritize public good, open-source principles, Solana-exclusive viability, and transparent fund allocation, with approvals at the Foundation’s discretion.
