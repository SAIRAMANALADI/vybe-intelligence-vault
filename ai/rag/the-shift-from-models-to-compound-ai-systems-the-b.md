# The Shift from Models to Compound AI Systems – The Berkeley Artificial Intelligence Research Blog

## Summary

- **Compound AI systems outperform monolithic models** by integrating multiple components (e.g., LLMs, retrievers, symbolic solvers) to achieve state-of-the-art results, as demonstrated by systems like AlphaCode 2 (1M solution sampling/filtering) and AlphaGeometry (LLM + symbolic solver hybrid).

- **Key advantages of compound systems** include dynamic adaptability (retrieval-augmented generation), improved control/trust (output filtering, fact verification), and resource optimization (balancing latency/cost across components).

- **Emerging challenges** in compound AI systems involve end-to-end optimization of non-differentiable pipelines (e.g., RAG), MLOps complexities (monitoring, security), and new frameworks (LangChain, DSPy) to streamline system design and deployment.
