# NIST Guidelines, Information Quality Standards and Administrative Mechanism | NIST

## Summary

- **Regulatory Compliance**: NIST adheres to OMB Guidelines (67 Fed. Reg. 8451, 2002) and DOC Guidelines (67 Fed. Reg. 62,685, 2002) under Section 515 of the Treasury and General Government Appropriations Act (2001), ensuring information quality (utility, objectivity, integrity) via administrative mechanisms for public correction requests and periodic OMB reporting.

- **Information Quality Framework**: Defines "quality" as comprising utility (usefulness to intended users), objectivity (clear, accurate, unbiased presentation and substance), and integrity (protection from unauthorized access/modification), with strict criteria for scientific, financial, or statistical data handling.

- **Dissemination Scope**: Covers all agency-initiated public distributions (e.g., web pages, reports, datasets) but excludes intra-agency sharing, FOIA responses, or third-party hyperlinks; emphasizes transparency and methodological rigor in data generation/analysis.
