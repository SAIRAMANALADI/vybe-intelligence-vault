# Apps & Games content ratings on Google Play - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Content Ratings System**: Google Play uses age-based ratings (e.g., ESRB, PEGI, USK, ACB, ClassInd, GRAC) to inform users of potentially objectionable content (violence, sexual content, drugs, gambling, profanity) and enforce regional restrictions where required.
- **Rating Determination**: Ratings are assigned by developers and regional boards (IARC, GRAC, etc.) based on factors like violence intensity, language severity, and interactive features (e.g., location sharing, user interactions), detailed in the "Read more" section of app listings.
- **Regional Variations**: Different rating systems apply per region (e.g., ESRB for Americas, PEGI for Europe, USK for Germany, ACB for Australia), with distinct thresholds for age classifications (e.g., PEGI 3 vs. PEGI 18, ESRB E vs. AO).
