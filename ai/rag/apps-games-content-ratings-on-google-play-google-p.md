# Apps & Games content ratings on Google Play - Google Play Help

## Summary

- **Content Ratings System**: Google Play uses age-based ratings (e.g., ESRB, PEGI, USK, ACB, ClassInd, GRAC) to inform users of potentially objectionable content (violence, sexual content, drugs, gambling, profanity) and enforce regional restrictions where required.
- **Rating Determination**: Ratings are assigned by developers and regional rating boards (e.g., IARC, GRAC) based on factors like violence intensity, language severity, and interactive features (e.g., location sharing, user interactions).
- **Accessibility**: Ratings are displayed on an app’s detail page under "Read more," with regional variations (e.g., PEGI 18 for gross violence, USK 18 for explicit content) and separate systems for games (e.g., GRAC) and non-games in South Korea.
