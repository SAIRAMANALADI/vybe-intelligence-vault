# Differential protection against SARS-CoV-2 reinfection pre- and post-Omicron | Nature

## Summary

- **Pre-Omicron reinfection protection**: Natural infection provided strong, durable protection against SARS-CoV-2 reinfection with minimal waning over time, whereas Omicron-era protection was robust only for recently infected individuals, declining rapidly within a year.

- **Viral evolution shift**: Pre-Omicron adaptation was driven by intrinsic transmissibility, while Omicron’s evolution prioritized immune escape due to extensive spike protein mutations (~dozens more than predecessors).

- **Immunological implications**: Findings underscore the need for periodic vaccine updates to sustain immunity, as host-virus dynamics shifted from transmissibility-driven to immune evasion-dominated selection pressures post-Omicron.
