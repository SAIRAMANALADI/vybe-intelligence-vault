# Turnitin Website Privacy Policy | Turnitin

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Purpose**: Turnitin collects Personal Information (e.g., name, email, IP address) via Websites for account administration, communication, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, with legal bases including legitimate interests and contractual obligations.

- **Tracking Technologies**: Uses cookies, pixel tags, and HTML5 storage to collect usage data (e.g., browser type, IP address, login timestamps) for analytics, preference management, and third-party advertising personalization, with opt-out options via Cookie Settings or external tools like NAI’s consumer portal.

- **Third-Party & Security**: Shares data with third-party service providers for hosting, analytics, and security; employs encryption and firewalls but disclaims responsibility for external breaches; retains data as required by law or for service continuity.
