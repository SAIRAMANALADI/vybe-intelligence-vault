# Financial Support - Cornell University Graduate School : Graduate School

## Summary

- **Ph.D. Funding**: 99% of Cornell Ph.D. students receive full funding via fellowships, teaching assistantships (TA), or research assistantships (RA), covering tuition, fees, health insurance, and a competitive living stipend.
- **Master’s/Program Fees**: Research-based master’s (M.S., M.A.) and professional degrees have variable tuition; financial aid is limited and program-specific.
- **Taxable Income**: All fellowship/assistant stipends are taxable (IRS & NY State); external funding sources (e.g., GRAPES database) and federal loans are encouraged for additional support.
