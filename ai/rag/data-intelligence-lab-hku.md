# Data Intelligence Lab@HKU

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: Data Intelligence Lab@HKU, led by Chao Huang, specializes in **Large Language Models (LLMs), Autonomous Agents, Graph Learning, Recommender Systems, and AI for Smart Cities**, with open-source contributions via [HKUDS](https://github.com/HKUDS).

- **Key Open-Source Projects**: Notable repositories include **nanobot** (LLM agents), **LightRAG** (retrieval-augmented generation), **GraphGPT** (graph foundation models), **RLMRec** (LLMs for recommender systems), and **UrbanGPT** (AI for smart cities), with GitHub stars ranging from **3.2k to 46.4k**.

- **Awards & Recognition**: Achieved **top-ranked influential papers** at WWW, SIGIR, KDD, and CIKM (2022–2024), **Stanford’s Top 2% Scientists (2022–2025)**, and **WAIC/ACM MM best paper honors**, alongside **AI100 Young Pioneers** and **ICBS Frontiers of Science Award**.
