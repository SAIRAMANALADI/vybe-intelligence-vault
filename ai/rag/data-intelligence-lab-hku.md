# Data Intelligence Lab@HKU

## Summary

- **Research Focus**: Data Intelligence Lab@HKU, led by Chao Huang, specializes in **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Autonomous Agents**, **Graph Learning**, **Recommender Systems**, and **AI for Smart Cities**, with open-source contributions via [HKUDS](https://github.com/HKUDS).

- **Key Open-Source Projects**: Developed high-impact tools including **nanobot** (44.2k ⭐), **LightRAG** (36.5k ⭐, trending #38), **CLI-Anything** (43k ⭐), and **DeepTutor** (24.7k ⭐), focusing on LLM agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and graph foundation models (GFM).

- **Awards & Recognition**: Recipient of **2025 AI 100 Young Pioneers**, **Stanford’s Top 2% Scientists (2022–2025)**, and **multiple Most Influential Paper awards** (e.g., WWW 2024 #1, SIGIR 2024 #4, KDD 2024 #3), alongside **WAIC Youth Outstanding Paper Honorable Mention (2025)**.
