# TREC RAGTIME | Official website for the TREC RAGTIME track

## Summary

- **Multi-faceted, multilingual report generation benchmark**: Evaluates systems generating English reports from documents in English, Spanish, Chinese, and Russian, emphasizing multi-faceted summaries and citation-based evidence verification.
- **Supporting subtasks**: Includes multilingual retrieval (MLIR) and auto-nuggetization (request decomposition into key single-sentence questions) to assess granular topic identification.
- **Timeline and evaluation**: 2026 edition features document collection release in May, topic release mid-June, submissions due August 12, with results feedback in October and TREC 2026 conference in December.
