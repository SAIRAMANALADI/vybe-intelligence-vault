# GMD - The need for carbon-emissions-driven climate projections in CMIP7

## Summary

- **CMIP7 proposal**: Shift from concentration-driven to **emissions-driven climate projections**, explicitly modeling **CO₂ fossil fuel emissions, land-use changes, and carbon dioxide removal (CDR) technologies** to capture carbon–climate feedbacks and policy-relevant uncertainties.

- **Key limitations in CMIP6**: Concentration-driven scenarios (e.g., SSPs) omit **carbon-cycle feedbacks** and **CDR efficacy risks**, as net emissions are pre-processed by IAMs/SCMs, preventing ESMs from resolving coupled system dynamics and feedbacks critical for Paris Agreement alignment.

- **Future priorities**: Post-CMIP7 should integrate **non-CO₂ GHGs (CH₄, N₂O)** and **process-based CDR modeling**, enabling **real-time emissions verification**, **scenario realism**, and **optimal ESM utilization** across climate modeling infrastructures (e.g., ML, km-scale models).
