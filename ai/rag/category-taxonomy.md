# Category Taxonomy

## Summary

- **cs.CC (Computational Complexity)**: Encompasses models of computation, complexity classes, structural complexity, and tradeoffs; overlaps with ACM Subject Classes F.1, F.2.3, and F.4.3.

- **cs.LG (Machine Learning)**: Covers all aspects of ML research (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement learning, etc.), including robustness, fairness, and applications; primary category for ML method applications.

- **cs.SY (Systems and Control)**: Alias for *eess.SY*; focuses on automatic control systems, including nonlinear, adaptive, stochastic, and robust control, with applications in robotics, cyber-physical systems, and reinforcement learning.
