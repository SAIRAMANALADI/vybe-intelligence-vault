# Google Sustainability

## Summary

- **Sustainability Impact of Google Products (2025):** Google’s tools enabled an estimated **41M tCO₂e reduction** (≈8.5M U.S. homes’ annual electricity use) across 9 solutions, with **fuel-efficient routing alone saving 3M tCO₂e** (≈700K gas cars/year).

- **AI-Driven Research Initiatives:** Developed **Solar API** (solar potential modeling), **Flood Hub** (7-day flood forecasts), and **AI contrail mitigation** for airlines, alongside tools like **Environmental Insights Explorer** for urban emissions/solar analysis.

- **Operational Sustainability Milestones (2025):** Procured **12+ GW net-new clean energy**, deployed **TPU v8 chips** (2x performance-per-watt vs. v7), avoided **58M tCO₂e** via efficiency/clean energy, and replenished **78% of freshwater use** (7.7B gallons).
