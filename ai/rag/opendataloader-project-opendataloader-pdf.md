## Summary

*   Java-based PDF parser designed for automated accessibility (A11y, EAA) and AI-ready data extraction, supporting PDF/UA compliance.
*   Extracts structured content including text, tables, and bounding boxes, converting to HTML, JSON, or Markdown formats.
*   Integrates OCR for recognition, facilitating document parsing and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflows.
