# opendataloader-project/opendataloader-pdf

## Summary

- **Java-based PDF parser** designed to convert PDFs into AI-ready formats (HTML, JSON, Markdown) with OCR support for accessibility and extraction of bounding boxes, tables, and tagged content.
- **Supports PDF accessibility standards** (PDF/UA, WCAG) and includes features for automated PDF accessibility remediation, including OCR recognition and structured data extraction.
- **Open-source tool** optimized for integration with AI pipelines (e.g., RAG) and document processing workflows, enabling high-fidelity conversion of PDFs to machine-readable formats.
