# RFC 5226: Guidelines for Writing an IANA Considerations Section in RFCs | RFC Editor

## Summary

- **Namespace Management Requirements**: RFC 5226 defines structured guidelines for IANA to manage namespaces, ensuring consistent value assignments and preventing exhaustion, hoarding, or misuse (e.g., text strings for desirable names). Delegation of namespaces (e.g., DNS subdomains) is encouraged to reduce IANA's administrative burden.

- **Designated Expert Role**: IANA operates under predefined policies, not subjective judgment. For cases requiring review, a "Designated Expert" (a permanent, non-WG entity) evaluates prospective assignments to ensure technical feasibility, prevent conflicts, and maintain interoperability, especially for protocol extensions with potential impact.

- **Registry Creation & Registration Process**: Documents proposing new registries must specify IANA policy definitions (e.g., "IETF Review," "Expert Review," "Standards Action") and include structured registration procedures. Updates to existing registries or values require explicit documentation of changes, with provisions for appeals and reclaiming assigned values if misused.
