# OECC2026

## Summary

- **Neuromorphic Photonics for AI**: Workshop explores silicon photonic platforms (MEMS, BTO, MoS₂/graphene) enabling high-speed, energy-efficient optical neural networks for AI acceleration, LIDAR, and signal processing via heterogeneous integration and novel materials.

- **Hollow-Core Fiber (HCF) Advancements**: HCFs achieve <0.1 dB/km loss with ultra-low latency, supporting quantum networks, high-power transmission, and gas photonics; challenges include scalable fabrication, integration, and standardization for commercial deployment.

- **Baud-Rate Scaling Limits**: High-symbol-rate optical transmission (>1.6 Tbps) faces bottlenecks in component bandwidth, DSP penalties, and signal integrity; workshop addresses trade-offs between performance, power, and scalability for next-gen coherent/IMDD systems.
