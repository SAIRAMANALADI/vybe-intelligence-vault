# New AI Tools for the Future of Science

## Summary

- **Gemini for Science** introduces three experimental AI tools on Google Labs: **Hypothesis Generation** (multi-agent "idea tournament" with Co-Scientist for hypothesis ideation/evaluation), **Computational Discovery** (agentic research engine with AlphaEvolve/ERA for parallelized hypothesis testing), and **Literature Insights** (NotebookLM-powered literature synthesis with structured tables, chat, and artifact generation).

- **Science Skills** integrates 30+ life science databases (e.g., UniProt, AlphaFold, AlphaGenome) into Google Antigravity, enabling rapid structural bioinformatics/genomic analyses (e.g., rare disease insights via AK2 gene mutations) with 10–100x speedup over manual workflows.

- **Enterprise/Validation**: Tools like AlphaEvolve (BASF/Klarna) and Co-Scientist (Daiichi Sankyo/U.S. DOE) are in private preview, with published validation in *Nature* (ERA/Co-Scientist papers) and partnerships with 100+ institutions (e.g., Stanford, Imperial College) for real-world scientific acceleration.
