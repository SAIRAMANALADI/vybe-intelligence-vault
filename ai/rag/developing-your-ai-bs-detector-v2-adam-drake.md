# Developing your AI BS detector (v2) - Adam Drake

## Summary

- **AI as task automation**: AI should be framed as automation of specific tasks (toil reduction), not a mystical solution; its value lies in replacing repetitive human work with scalable, cost-efficient processes.

- **Red flags in AI claims**:
  - **Anthropomorphizing**: Avoid vendors framing AI as "thinking" or "creative" machines (e.g., stress-induced ML weight perturbations).
  - **Proprietary/patented solutions**: State-of-the-art AI relies on open-source frameworks; proprietary claims often mask overpriced data integration or trivial rule-based systems (e.g., fraud detection via hardcoded thresholds).

- **Validation framework**:
  - Demand proof of failed naive solutions (e.g., linear regression) before accepting complex AI.
  - Require justification for AI classification (beyond hype) and adherence to open-source frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow/PyTorch) to expose lack of novelty.
