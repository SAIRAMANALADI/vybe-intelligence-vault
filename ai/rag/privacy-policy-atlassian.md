# Privacy Policy | Atlassian

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Atlassian gathers data directly from users (account/profile info, content, support interactions, payments), automatically via service usage (feature interactions, device/connection metadata, cookies/tracking tech), and from third-party sources (linked services, partners, public databases), with granularity varying by service and regional regulations.

- **Processing & Disclosure Framework**: Data is used for service provisioning, personalization, security, marketing, and legal compliance; disclosed to service providers, affiliates, or authorities under applicable law, with user consent where required, and in compliance with regional disclosures (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

- **Regional Compliance & User Rights**: Users in EEA/UK/US have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict data processing, opt out of targeted ads, and request data portability, with Atlassian acting as a controller for direct interactions and a processor for customer-controlled data.
