# Privacy Policy | Speechify

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Speechify collects Personal Data (e.g., username, email, payment info) and Non-Personal Data (e.g., device logs, IP addresses, usage analytics) via user interactions, cookies, and third-party integrations (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel). User Content (text/documents) is stored but only reviewed for ToS violations, support requests, or human proofreading services.

- **Data Usage & Sharing**: Data is processed for service provision, fraud prevention, and marketing (opt-in for EEA/Swiss/UK users). Speechify does not sell data but shares it with vetted service providers, legal authorities (via warrants), or in M&A scenarios. Third-party plugins (e.g., social media buttons) may track user visits independently.

- **Cookie & Third-Party Policies**: Speechify uses cookies for authentication, analytics, and ads but does not respond to "Do Not Track" requests. Third-party services (e.g., Hotjar, Amplitude) process data under their own policies, with aggregated analytics used for service optimization.
