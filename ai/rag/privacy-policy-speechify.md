# Privacy Policy | Speechify

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: Speechify collects **Personal Data** (e.g., username, email, payment info, user-uploaded content) and **Non-Personal Data** (e.g., device identifiers, IP addresses, log data, usage analytics) via its services, with some data (e.g., contacts in Speechify Keyboard) stored locally on-device only.

- **Data Usage & Third-Party Sharing**: Data is processed for service provision, fraud prevention, and marketing (with opt-out options), while **no Personal Data is sold**; third-party sharing is limited to service providers, legal compliance, or mergers, with Non-Personal Data used for public reports or analytics.

- **Tracking & Compliance**: Speechify employs **cookies, tracking pixels (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Analytics), and plugins** for analytics and ads, with **no Do Not Track support**; EEA/Swiss/UK users require explicit consent for marketing, and all third-party data handling adheres to their respective privacy policies.
