# Educator Resources & Technology Training - Google for Education - Google for Education

## Summary

- **Google for Education offers role-specific training**: Free courses tailored for educators (e.g., AI integration, Workspace Fundamentals), IT admins (security/analytics), and students (digital literacy, Sheets/ Docs), with durations ranging from 30 min to 2.5 hrs.

- **Certifications validate expertise**: Free credentials like *Google Certified Educator (Levels 1/2)*, *Gemini Certified Educator*, and *IT Admin certifications* (valid for 3 years) assess proficiency in Google tools, AI, and security, with exam guides and prep courses available.

- **Community-driven resources and Champions programs**: Educators can access peer-created guides, join Google for Education Champions (e.g., Certified Innovator/Trainer/Coach), and leverage AI literacy training (e.g., ISTE+ASCD partnerships) to enhance instructional impact.
