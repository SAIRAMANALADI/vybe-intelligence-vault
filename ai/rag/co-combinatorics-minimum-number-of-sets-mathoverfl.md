# co.combinatorics - Minimum number of sets - MathOverflow

## Summary

- **Counterexample exists for m=7**: The family $\mathcal{F}$ with $|\mathcal{F}_{\{1,2\}}| + |\mathcal{F}_{\{1,3\}}| + |\mathcal{F}_{\{2,3\}}| + |\mathcal{F}_{\{1,2,3\}}| = 9 < 10 = m+3$ disproves the claim.

- **Structure of $\mathcal{F}$**: $\mathcal{F}$ includes $\{1,2,3\}$, all sets have size ≥3 (except possibly $\emptyset$), and $|\mathcal{F}_{\{k\}}| + |\mathcal{F}_{\emptyset}| = m+1$ for $k \in \{1,2,3\}$.

- **Constraints on $\mathcal{F}_X$**: For $k \in \{1,2,3\}$, $|\mathcal{F}_{\{k\}}| \geq 1$, and $\mathcal{F}_{\{1,2\}}, \mathcal{F}_{\{1,3\}}, \mathcal{F}_{\{2,3\}}$ each contain at least one set.
