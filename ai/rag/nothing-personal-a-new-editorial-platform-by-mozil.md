# Nothing Personal, a new editorial platform by Mozilla Foundation - Mozilla Foundation

## Summary

- **Menstrual tracking apps** pose significant privacy risks by collecting sensitive biometric data, which can be exploited for surveillance, discriminatory pricing, or sold to third parties without explicit user consent.

- **Dynamic pricing algorithms** leverage extensive consumer surveillance data to implement personalized pricing strategies, often resulting in discriminatory practices based on inferred demographics, behaviors, or inferred health data (e.g., period tracking).

- **Anti-surveillance technologies**, including privacy-focused messaging apps (Signal), reflective/antisurveillance fashion, and home security camera reviews, are critical countermeasures to mitigate pervasive monitoring and data exploitation in digital ecosystems.
