# Financial Aid & Scholarships | UCLA Undergraduate Admission

## Summary

- **Application Deadlines & Eligibility**: U.S. citizens/Permanent Residents must submit the **FAFSA** by **March 2nd**; non-citizens/non-residents from California high schools must file the **CA Dream Act Application (CADAA)** and meet **AB540 eligibility** for state/institutional aid.
- **Financial Aid Components**: UCLA packages typically include **scholarships, grants (need-based, non-repayable), low-interest loans (federal/institutional), and work-study programs**, with average UCLA loan debt ($19K) significantly lower than the national average ($39K).
- **Payment & Appeals**: The **BruinPay Plan** allows tuition/fees to be paid in **3 installments per term**; families may appeal financial aid packages for **projected income changes** or **special circumstances** via the Bruin Financial Aid Portal.
