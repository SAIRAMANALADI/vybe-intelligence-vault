# Dropbox July 2024 Product Updates

## Summary

- **Image Enhancement & Search**: Built-in tools for resolution improvement, color correction, and auto-straightening; AI-powered image search across 11 languages (Dutch, Portuguese, Italian, etc.) for content-based retrieval.

- **Granular Data Room Analytics**: DocSend Advanced Data Rooms now provide centralized, real-time, page-by-page visitor analytics across data rooms to optimize negotiation workflows.

- **Universal Search with AI (Dropbox Dash Beta)**: Cross-platform search tool leveraging AI to analyze content, summarize documents, and retrieve files from any app/tool (US-only, English, rolling feature rollout).
