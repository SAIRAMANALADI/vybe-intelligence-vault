# YouTube’s contest policies and guidelines - YouTube Help

## Summary

- **Compliance & Legal Framework**: Contests must adhere to YouTube’s [Terms of Service](http://www.youtube.com/static?gl=US&template=terms), [Community Guidelines](http://www.youtube.com/t/community_guidelines), and all applicable federal/state/local laws, including U.S. sanctions, with sole organizer responsibility for legality and enforcement.

- **Prohibited Practices**: Contests cannot manipulate YouTube metrics (views, likes, etc.), require ownership transfer of entries, impose entry fees (subject to local lottery laws), or imply YouTube’s endorsement without prior written consent.

- **Official Rules Mandates**: Contest rules must include disclosures, privacy notices, disqualification clauses for guideline violations, a release of liability for YouTube, and clear statements that YouTube is not a sponsor, with all administration and prize fulfillment managed by the organizer.
