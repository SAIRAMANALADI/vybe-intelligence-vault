# From RAG to Context - A 2025 year-end review of RAG | RAGFlow

## Summary

- **RAG's Evolution & Enterprise Adoption**: Despite skepticism and overshadowing by AI Agents in 2025, RAG solidified its role as critical enterprise AI infrastructure, with mid-to-large organizations deepening investments. Long-context capabilities did not replace RAG but prompted "Context Engineering," emphasizing end-to-end optimization of retrieval-context assembly-model reasoning pipelines.

- **Technical Limitations & Optimizations**: Traditional RAG faces trade-offs between semantic matching (small chunks) and context understanding (large chunks). Solutions like TreeRAG (hierarchical tree structures) and GraphRAG (knowledge graphs) address these by decoupling search/retrieval stages and leveraging LLMs for semantic structuring. Hybrid approaches (Long-Context RAG) further enhance coherence and recall.

- **Cost & Performance Trade-offs**: Alternatives to RAG (e.g., long-context windows, KV Cache) incur 2+ orders of magnitude higher costs or lack scalability. RAG remains the most cost-effective and flexible solution for complex, evolving enterprise data, outperforming index-free methods (e.g., Grep) in handling unstructured/multi-modal data and dynamic queries.
