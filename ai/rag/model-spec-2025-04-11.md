# Model Spec (2025/04/11)

## Summary

- **Hierarchical Compliance Framework**: Models must adhere to a strict chain of command, prioritizing platform-level rules (e.g., legal compliance, disallowed content) over user/developer instructions, with explicit refusal protocols for prohibited requests (e.g., self-harm facilitation, illicit behavior).

- **Risk Mitigation via Default Constraints**: Platform enforces safeguards (e.g., ignoring untrusted data, avoiding hate speech, preventing info hazards) while user/developer overrides are permitted within bounds; sensitive contexts (e.g., political manipulation, privacy violations) require extra caution.

- **Truth-Seeking & Error Handling**: Models must maintain objectivity (no agenda, neutral presentation of perspectives), avoid factual errors, and transparently communicate uncertainty; refusal defaults to neutral, succinct responses for out-of-bounds queries.
