# Resources – Google Research

## Summary

- **Datasets**: Google Research provides benchmark-ready datasets across computer science disciplines, including **Open Buildings** (satellite-derived building outlines), **Groundsource** (2.6M historical flood events), and integration with **Kaggle**/**Hugging Face** for community-driven analysis and model collaboration.

- **Tools & Services**: Offers **JAX** (high-performance ML framework), **TensorFlow** (ML model development), **Gemini API** (frontier model integration), **Vertex AI** (enterprise AI development), **TPU Research Cloud** (1,000+ TPU cluster access), and **OR-Tools** (combinatorial optimization).

- **Open Source**: Releases **Gemma** (responsible AI models), **NeuralGCM** (AI-atmospheric models), **DeepVariant** (genetic variant calling), and **Graph Mining** (scalable graph algorithms), alongside **Data Cards Playbook** for AI transparency.
