# Cookies Policies | Linux Foundation

## Summary

- **Cookie Classification & Purpose**: The Linux Foundation sites use **first-party** (e.g., `linuxfoundation.org`) and **third-party** cookies (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Google Analytics) for **strictly necessary** (authentication/security), **performance** (analytics/error tracking), **functionality** (user preferences), and **targeting** (personalized ads/content).

- **Third-Party Cookies & Data Handling**: Third-party services (e.g., Pardot, New Relic, Feathr) set cookies like `visitor_id6342` (Pardot) or `JSESSIONID` (New Relic) to track user behavior, measure ad performance, and optimize site functionality, often without storing personally identifiable information (PII).

- **User Control & Opt-Out**: Users can **disable cookies** via browser settings (affecting site functionality) or opt out of **Google Analytics** via the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Third-party social media cookies (e.g., Twitter’s `guest_id`) are used for social integration and ad targeting.
