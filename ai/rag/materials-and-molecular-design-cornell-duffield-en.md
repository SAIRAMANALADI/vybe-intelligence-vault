# Materials and Molecular Design | Cornell Duffield Engineering

## Summary

- **Molecular Engineering & AI Integration**: Cornell leverages atomic-scale material design and AI-driven simulations to innovate energy storage, medicine, and environmental solutions via advanced manufacturing.

- **Key Research Infrastructure**: Facilities like PARADIM (314+ journal papers) and CNF (500+ annual users) enable interface materials discovery and nanofabrication, supported by a 1965-established Materials Science & Engineering department.

- **Semiconductor & Soft Materials Advances**: Novel AlN transistors for RF electronics, ML-optimized porous membranes for chemical separation, and MOCVD systems for nitride semiconductors highlight Cornell’s cross-disciplinary materials innovation.
