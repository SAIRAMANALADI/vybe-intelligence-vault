# Privacy Policy | Linux Foundation

## Summary

- **Data Collection Scope**: The Linux Foundation (TLF) collects personal information directly, via third parties, and automatically (e.g., cookies, IP addresses) for account registration, event participation, training, open-source contributions, and communications, with mandatory fields for core services and optional fields for enhanced features.

- **Legal Bases for Processing**: TLF processes personal data under legitimate business interests (e.g., providing services, maintaining open-source projects, event administration) and GDPR-compliant bases, including explicit consent for optional data (e.g., dietary restrictions, vaccination status for events).

- **Automated & De-identified Data Handling**: TLF uses session replay tools (Datadog/FullStory) for debugging, aggregates de-identified data for analytics/marketing, and may share such data with third parties for research or statistical purposes, subject to contractual constraints.
