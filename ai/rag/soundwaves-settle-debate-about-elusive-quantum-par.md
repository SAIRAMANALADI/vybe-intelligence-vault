# Soundwaves settle debate about elusive quantum particle | Cornell Chronicle

## Summary

- **Thermal Hall effect in ruthenium trichloride** was initially attributed to Majorana fermions or magnetic impurities, but Cornell researchers demonstrated it arises from **chiral phonons** with helical soundwave polarization, resolving the debate via ultrasonic measurements.

- **Hall viscosity** (gravitational Hall viscosity) in ruthenium trichloride was experimentally observed for the first time, causing phonon deflection via spin-orbit coupling, which mimics curved spacetime effects in quantum materials.

- **Acoustic Faraday effect** confirmed Hall viscosity by tracking phonon corkscrew trajectories under magnetic fields, providing a novel technique to probe emergent quantum states and invalidate prior Majorana fermion claims.
