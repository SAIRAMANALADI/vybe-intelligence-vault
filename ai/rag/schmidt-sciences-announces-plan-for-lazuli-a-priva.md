# Schmidt Sciences Announces Plan for Lazuli, a Private Space Telescope | Scientific American

## Summary

- **Lazuli Space Observatory**: A privately funded, 3-meter-aperture space telescope (larger than Hubble) with a 2029 target launch date, featuring a coronagraph, wide-field camera, and spectrograph for exoplanet atmospheres, supernovae, and dark energy studies.

- **Schmidt Observatory System**: Modular, scalable ground-based arrays (e.g., Deep Synoptic Array, Argus Array) complementing Lazuli, designed for rapid data release and low-cost scalability, aiming for 2029 science operations.

- **Private Funding Shift**: Marks a resurgence of philanthropic-driven astronomy (e.g., Schmidt Sciences) amid federal funding instability, raising questions about data access, funding competition, and long-term research priorities.
