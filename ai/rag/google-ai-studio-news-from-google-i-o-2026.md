# Google AI Studio news from Google I/O 2026

## Summary

- **Native Android Development**: Google AI Studio now supports native Android app development with production-quality Kotlin code generation using Jetpack Compose, in-browser emulator, ADB support, and direct Play Store testing via Google Play Developer account integration.

- **Google Ecosystem Integrations**: AI Studio enables direct access to Google Workspace for building apps on Sheets/Drive data, and supports exporting projects to Google Antigravity for local development and team collaboration.

- **Mobile App & Deployment**: A new AI Studio mobile app allows on-the-go code iteration and preview, while free deployment of the first two apps to Google Cloud is available without requiring a credit card.
