# Usage Policy | Cohere

## Summary

- **Prohibited Activities**: Cohere Services must not be used for child exploitation, incitement of violence/harm, illegal activities, weapons/controlled substances, privacy violations, security compromises, surveillance/predictive policing, fraudulent practices, misinformation, or abuse of services (e.g., jailbreaking, prompt injection).
- **Customer Application Compliance**: Public-facing applications must disclose AI interaction, enforce child safety controls (if applicable), and ensure user compliance with Universal Requirements.
- **Research Exceptions**: Limited policy exceptions for security/safety research require prior authorization; model safety issues must be reported to `safety@cohere.com`.
