# Usage Policy | Cohere

## Summary

- **Prohibited Activities**: Cohere Services must not be used for child exploitation, incitement of violence/harm, illegal activities, weapons/controlled substances, privacy violations, security compromises, surveillance/predictive policing, fraudulent practices, misinformation/political manipulation, or abusing Cohere's systems (e.g., jailbreaking, prompt injection).

- **Customer Application Compliance**: Customer Applications must enforce Universal Requirements, disclose AI interaction to users (especially for public-facing apps), and implement child safety controls (e.g., age verification) if interacting with minors.

- **Research Exceptions**: Limited policy exceptions exist for authorized security/safety research; report violations or safety issues to `safety@cohere.com` under Cohere’s Responsible Disclosure Policy.
