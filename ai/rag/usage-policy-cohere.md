# Usage Policy | Cohere

## Summary

- **Prohibited Activities**: Cohere Services must not be used for child exploitation, incitement of violence/harm, illegal activities, weapons/controlled substances, privacy violations, security compromises, surveillance/predictive policing, fraudulent practices, misinformation, or abuse of Cohere's systems (e.g., jailbreaking, prompt injection).
- **High-Risk & Compliance**: Use in high-risk domains (e.g., healthcare, law enforcement, financial services) or automated decision-making affecting rights is restricted; public-facing apps must disclose AI interaction, enforce age verification for minors, and comply with child safety regulations.
- **Research & Reporting**: Authorized security/safety research exceptions exist; violations or safety issues must be reported immediately to `safety@cohere.com` or via platform reporting tools.
