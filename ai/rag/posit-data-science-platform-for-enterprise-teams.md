# Posit | Data Science Platform for Enterprise Teams

## Summary

- **Unified AI-Powered Data Science Platform**: Posit provides an enterprise-grade environment integrating IDEs (Positron, RStudio, VS Code, Jupyter) with runtime-aware AI assistance (Posit AI), enabling rapid development, deployment, and governance of Python/R-based analytics while maintaining IT control over security and compliance.

- **Governed Publishing & Scalable Compute**: Features a centralized publishing platform (Posit Connect/Connect Cloud) for deploying apps, dashboards, and APIs directly from IDEs, with IT-managed governance, access control, and cost-efficient cloud integration (AWS, Snowflake, Databricks) for scalable compute and regulatory adherence.

- **Measurable Enterprise Impact**: Demonstrated by 1200x faster NASA "what-if" analysis, 72% reduction in incident rates at Suffolk Construction, 60% cloud cost savings at TruDiagnostic, and unified workflows for Fortune 100 clients like Gen Re, Novo Nordisk, and NASA via governed, auditable analytics.
