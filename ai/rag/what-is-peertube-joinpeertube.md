# What is PeerTube? | JoinPeerTube

## Summary

- **Decentralized & Federated Architecture**: PeerTube operates as a federated network of independently hosted instances using ActivityPub, enabling cross-instance video discovery and user interactions while avoiding centralized control or opaque algorithms.

- **Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Video Distribution**: Leverages WebRTC for P2P streaming to reduce server bandwidth load during viral content distribution, shifting part of the delivery burden to viewers.

- **Open-Source & Privacy-Focused**: Released under a free/libre license (AGPL), PeerTube excludes ads/tracking, provides auditable code, and offers mobile apps for offline viewing, emphasizing user autonomy and ethical data practices.
