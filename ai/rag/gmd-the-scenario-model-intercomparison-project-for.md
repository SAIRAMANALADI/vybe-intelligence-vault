# GMD - The Scenario Model Intercomparison Project for CMIP7 (ScenarioMIP-CMIP7)

## Summary

- **ScenarioMIP-CMIP7 framework** introduces three scenario tiers (high, medium, low climate change) with emissions trajectories including peak-and-decline pathways, prioritizing emission-driven Earth system model runs to better capture uncertainty.

- **Long-term extensions** (up to 2500 AD) are proposed to analyze irreversible climate processes, long-term impacts, and (ir)reversibility under extended timescales.

- **Community-driven design** integrates iterative feedback from researchers and stakeholders, ensuring alignment with CMIP7’s experimental design for IPCC AR7 climate simulations.
