# How to get Apple Intelligence - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Device & OS Compatibility**: Apple Intelligence requires iPhone 15 Pro/16+, iPad mini (A17 Pro)/M1+, Mac with Apple silicon, Apple Vision Pro, or Apple Watch Series 6+/SE 2+ (paired with compatible iPhone); supports iOS 18.1+, iPadOS 18.1+, macOS 15.1+, visionOS 2.4+, watchOS 11+.

- **Key Features**: Enables on-device AI tasks including Live Translation (Messages/Phone/FaceTime), Genmoji, Image Playground, Writing Tools, Siri enhancements, Smart Reply, Voicemail summaries, and Visual Intelligence (text interaction, object/plant ID, calendar event creation).

- **Regional & Language Support**: Available in 17+ languages (e.g., English, Chinese, Japanese) across most regions, with EU/China-specific limitations; requires matching Siri/device language and 7GB+ storage (excluding Apple Watch).
