# How to get Apple Intelligence - Apple Support

## Summary

- **Device Compatibility**: Apple Intelligence requires iPhone 15 Pro/16+, iPad mini (A17 Pro)/M1+, Mac M1+, Apple Vision Pro, or Apple Watch Series 6+/SE 2+ paired with an enabled iPhone; supports iOS 18.1+, iPadOS 18.1+, macOS 15.1+, visionOS 2.4+, watchOS 11+.

- **Key Features**: Enables on-device AI capabilities including Live Translation (Messages/Phone/FaceTime), Genmoji, Image Playground, Writing Tools, Siri enhancements, Smart Reply, Voicemail summaries, Notes transcription, and Workout Buddy (English-only on Watch).

- **Regional/Language Support**: Available in 17+ languages (e.g., English, Chinese, Japanese) across most regions; EU/China have partial/limited support; ChatGPT integration requires regional availability.
