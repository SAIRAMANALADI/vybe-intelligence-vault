# OWASP AI Maturity Assessment | OWASP Foundation

## Summary

- **Framework Structure**: OWASP AIMA provides a five-domain maturity model (Strategy, Design, Implementation, Operations, Governance) with actionable levels to assess AI system alignment with ethical, secure, and operational requirements.

- **Project Goals**: Aims to enable informed AI adoption decisions, promote ethical/responsible AI, enhance security against adversarial threats, ensure transparency/accountability, and align with global standards (e.g., ISO, EU AI Act).

- **Roadmap & Contributions**: Post-V1 phase focuses on feedback collection (GitHub, surveys), usability improvements, sector-specific modules (e.g., healthcare), and collaboration with standards bodies (NIST, ENISA) for iterative refinements (V1.1 targeted Spring 2026).
