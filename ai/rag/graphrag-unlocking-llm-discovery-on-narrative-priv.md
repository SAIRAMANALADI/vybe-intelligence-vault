# GraphRAG: Unlocking LLM discovery on narrative private data - Microsoft Research

## Summary

- **Performance parity**: Generalist foundation models (e.g., Med-PaLM 2) achieve competitive or superior performance to specialized models across multiple medical benchmarks (e.g., MedQA, MedMCQA, PubMedQA) without task-specific fine-tuning.

- **Scaling efficiency**: Zero-shot or few-shot prompting of generalist models reduces reliance on curated, domain-specific datasets, lowering computational and data annotation costs compared to traditional specialized tuning pipelines.

- **Multi-task generalization**: Demonstrated capability to handle diverse medical tasks (QA, reasoning, summarization) with unified model architecture, suggesting broader applicability and reduced need for task-specific model development.
