# tvOS - Apple Developer

## Summary

- **tvOS 17+ Features**: Sidebar customization for navigation, persistent Continuity Camera integration for seamless transitions between work and entertainment use cases, and enhanced streaming capabilities with 4K, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and Dolby Atmos support.

- **Development Tools & Frameworks**: SwiftUI for cross-platform app development with minimal code, leveraging AVPlayer, Sidebar, and User Management; UIKit, AVKit, and Metal for rich game/app experiences; and continuity device integration (iPhone/iPad) for high-quality media capture via Center Stage and Portrait mode.

- **Platform Design & APIs**: Liquid Glass design refinements for consistency and accessibility, Picture-in-Picture support, Siri Remote voice commands, and unified ecosystem compatibility across Apple platforms (iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS, watchOS).
