# Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Account Security & Recovery**: Google supports 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, and Enhanced Safe Browsing; compromised accounts can be secured via [account recovery](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825) or [password reset](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/41078).

- **Data & Privacy Management**: Users can control activity logging, location data, and linked app permissions via [Google’s activity controls](https://support.google.com/accounts/topic/7188674) and [privacy settings](https://support.google.com/accounts/topic/6152259).

- **Family & Cross-Platform Access**: Supports child account creation (via [Family Link](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7103262)), cross-device sign-in (e.g., [Android/iOS](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7158477)), and [Sign in with Google](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/12849458) for third-party apps.
