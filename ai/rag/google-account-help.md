# Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Google Account Security**: Encompasses 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, Enhanced Safe Browsing, and suspicious activity monitoring to safeguard against unauthorized access and data breaches.

- **Account Recovery & Management**: Provides protocols for recovering hacked/compromised accounts, resetting passwords, managing linked apps, and configuring recovery options (e.g., phone/email) to prevent lockouts.

- **Privacy & Data Controls**: Includes granular settings for activity logging (Timeline, Web & App Activity), location data, and account deletion, alongside tools like Inactive Account Manager and Family Link for supervised child accounts.
