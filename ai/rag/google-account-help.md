# Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Google Account Security & Recovery**: Supports 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, and account recovery mechanisms for compromised or deleted accounts, including delayed recovery requests and hacked account remediation.

- **Family & Child Account Management**: Enables creation of child accounts (with age-based restrictions), family group management via Family Link, and supervision transfer protocols for underage users.

- **Data Privacy & Linked Services**: Provides granular controls for activity logging, location data, and third-party app linkages (via "Sign in with Google"), including data sharing safety measures and deletion options.
