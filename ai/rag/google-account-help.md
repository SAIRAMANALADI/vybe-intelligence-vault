# Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Account Security & Recovery**: Google Accounts support 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, and Enhanced Safe Browsing; recovery options include password reset, account restoration, and hacked account remediation via [account recovery](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7299973).

- **Data Privacy & Management**: Users can control activity logs, location data, and storage via [activity controls](https://support.google.com/accounts/topic/7188674) and [privacy settings](https://support.google.com/accounts/topic/6152259), including deletion of account or service data.

- **Cross-Platform & Family Integration**: Google Accounts support [Sign in with Google](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/12849458) for third-party apps, [family group management](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6286986), and child account supervision via [Family Link](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7103262).
