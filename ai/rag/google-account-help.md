# Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Account Security & Recovery**: Google Accounts support 2-Step Verification, Advanced Protection, and Enhanced Safe Browsing; recovery options include password reset, account restoration, and hacked account remediation via [account recovery](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7299973) and [security tips](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825).

- **Data Management & Privacy**: Users can control activity logging, delete data, manage location settings, and enforce privacy via [activity controls](https://support.google.com/accounts/topic/7188674) and [data deletion](https://support.google.com/accounts/topic/10057938).

- **Cross-Platform & Family Features**: Supports linked apps via OAuth, family group management (including child accounts via [Family Link](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7103262)), and device tracking (e.g., [Android Device Manager](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3265955)).
