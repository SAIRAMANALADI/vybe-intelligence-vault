# Global Infrastructure - AWS

## Summary

- **Global Reach & Scalability**: AWS operates **39 geographic regions** with **123 Availability Zones (AZs)**, plus **750+ CloudFront POPs**, **45 Local Zones**, and **33 Wavelength Zones**, enabling low-latency, high-performance deployments worldwide with **~20M km of fiber optic backbone**.
- **High Availability & Resilience**: Each AWS Region consists of **≥3 physically isolated AZs**, ensuring fault tolerance and uptime, while **Dedicated Local Zones** and **Outposts** extend compliance and hybrid cloud capabilities.
- **Performance Optimization & Innovation**: AWS leverages **AI-driven power efficiency** in data centers, **Wavelength** for telco-edge computing, and **CloudFront** for accelerated content delivery, optimizing latency, throughput, and energy usage.
