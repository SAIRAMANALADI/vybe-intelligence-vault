# Global Infrastructure - AWS

## Summary

- **Global Reach & Scalability**: AWS operates **39 launched Regions** with **123 Availability Zones (AZs)**, plus **750+ CloudFront POPs**, **45 Local Zones**, and **33 Wavelength Zones**, enabling low-latency, high-performance deployments across **7 continents** with planned expansions in **Saudi Arabia, Chile, New Zealand, Taiwan, and Australia**.

- **Resilient & Secure Architecture**: Each AWS Region consists of **≥3 physically isolated AZs**, interconnected via **~20M km of fiber**, ensuring **high availability (99.99%)**, fault tolerance, and compliance with global security standards (e.g., **Gartner/IDC Leader in 2025**).

- **Hybrid & Edge Compute Offerings**: Supports **on-premises (Outposts), edge (Local Zones), telco-integrated (Wavelength), and content delivery (CloudFront)** deployments, enabling consistent performance for latency-sensitive, sovereignty-sensitive, or distributed workloads.
