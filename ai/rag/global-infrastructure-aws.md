# Global Infrastructure - AWS

## Summary

- **Global Reach & Resilience**: AWS operates **39 Regions** with **123 Availability Zones (AZs)**, each containing **≥3 isolated AZs**, and plans expansion to **7 new AZs across 2 new Regions** (Saudi Arabia, Chile). Leverages **~20M km fiber backbone** for low-latency, high-throughput global connectivity.

- **Edge & Hybrid Infrastructure**: Deploys **750+ CloudFront POPs**, **15 Regional edge caches**, **45 Local Zones**, and **33 Wavelength Zones** for ultra-low-latency applications, alongside **Outposts** (on-prem hybrid) and **Dedicated Local Zones** (regulatory-compliant dedicated infrastructure).

- **Performance & Security Optimization**: Implements AI-driven power efficiency in data centers, with **Gartner & IDC leadership recognition** for security, scalability, and AI-ready infrastructure, ensuring **high availability, compliance, and innovation** across Regions and edge deployments.
