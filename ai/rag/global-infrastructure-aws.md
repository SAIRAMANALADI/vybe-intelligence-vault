# Global Infrastructure - AWS

## Summary

- **Global Reach & Resilience**: AWS operates **39 geographic regions** with **123 Availability Zones (AZs)**, each containing **≥3 isolated AZs** for high availability, plus **750+ CloudFront POPs** and **15 regional edge caches**, enabling low-latency, resilient deployments worldwide.

- **Network Backbone & Performance**: Leverages **~20M km of fiber optic cabling** (terrestrial/subsea) for ultra-fast data transfer, with **45 Local Zones** and **33 Wavelength Zones** to optimize latency for edge and 5G applications.

- **Diverse Infrastructure Offerings**: Supports **hybrid/edge deployments** via **Outposts, Local Zones, Dedicated Local Zones, and Wavelength**, alongside **CloudFront** for content delivery, ensuring consistent performance across cloud, on-premises, and edge environments.
