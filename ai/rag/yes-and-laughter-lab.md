# Yes, And...Laughter Lab

## Summary

- **Program Structure**: *Yes, And… Laughter Lab* is a competitive incubation lab, pitch program, and showcase designed to support diverse comedy writers/performers creating socially impactful content, bridging them with industry stakeholders (entertainment, social justice, philanthropy).

- **Partnerships & Funding**: Backed by major media entities (Comedy Central, NBCUniversal, Netflix), social justice orgs (Color of Change, GLAAD), and funders (MacArthur Foundation, Open Society Foundations) to amplify comedic voices in social change movements.

- **Application & Output**: Open to submissions via [Submittable](https://yall.submittable.com/submit); outputs include fellowships (2019–2025 cohorts), festivals, and industry showcases to commercialize socially conscious comedy.
