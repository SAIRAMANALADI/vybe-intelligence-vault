# Improving AI labels for viewers and creators - YouTube Blog

## Summary

- **Label Placement Enhancement**: AI disclosure labels for photorealistic/meaningfully altered content now appear prominently below the video player (long-form) or as an overlay (Shorts), replacing prior less visible placements.

- **Automated AI Detection Rollout**: Starting May 2026, YouTube’s systems will auto-apply AI labels for undetected photorealistic AI content, while allowing creator overrides via YouTube Studio (except for Veo/Dream Screen or C2PA-marked generative AI).

- **Unchanged Monetization/Recommendation Policies**: AI labels do not impact video eligibility for recommendations or monetization, maintaining transparency without altering content distribution mechanics.
