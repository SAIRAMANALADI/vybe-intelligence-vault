# wgcyeo/UniversalRAG

## Summary

- **Multimodal & Multi-Granularity RAG Framework**: Introduces a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system (`UniversalRAG`) designed to handle diverse data modalities (text, images, tables, etc.) and granularities (document-level to chunk-level) within a unified architecture.

- **Unified Retrieval & Fusion Mechanism**: Implements a cross-modal retrieval pipeline with modality-agnostic embeddings and a fusion module to dynamically integrate heterogeneous retrieved content during generation, optimizing coherence across modalities.

- **ACL 2026 Oral Paper**: Presents empirical results demonstrating state-of-the-art performance on multimodal benchmarks, with codebase supporting extensibility via modular design for new modalities or retrieval strategies.
