# Track or manage your Google Store order - Google Store Help

## Summary

- **Order Management**: Access order history via [Google Store order history](http://store.google.com/orderhistory) to view status, estimated delivery dates, and tracking numbers; select **Track shipment** for carrier-specific updates.
- **Tracking Retrieval**: Tracking details are available 24 hours post-shipment; locate via order details page or shipping confirmation email, with delivery estimates requiring 48-hour buffer before support escalation.
- **SMS Notifications**: Opt-in for shipment updates during checkout by entering a phone number on the "Thank you" page, enabling real-time SMS alerts for each shipment.
