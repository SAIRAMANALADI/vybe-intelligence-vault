# YouTube TV Help

## Summary

- **Service Overview**: YouTube TV is a live TV streaming service offering 100+ channels, including major networks (ABC, CBS, NBC, Fox, ESPN, etc.), with cloud DVR storage for recording programs.
- **Subscription & Add-ons**: Plans start at $72.99/month; additional add-ons include the **Sports Plus** package (e.g., NFL Sunday Ticket) and **4K/HDR streaming** for compatible devices.
- **Technical Features**: Supports **multi-device streaming** (3 simultaneous streams per account), **6 accounts per household**, and **regional sports network (RSN) availability** varies by location.
