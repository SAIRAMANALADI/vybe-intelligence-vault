# How do we ensure our IoT products process information lawfully? | ICO

## Summary

- **Lawful Basis Selection**: IoT products must select an appropriate GDPR lawful basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, or legitimate interests) for processing personal data, with consent required for storage/access technologies unless PECR exemptions apply; documentation and transparency to users are mandatory.

- **Consent Requirements**: Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, and involve clear affirmative action; interfaces must avoid pre-ticked boxes, biased framing, or excessive requests to prevent invalidation.

- **Special Category Data**: Processing special category data (e.g., biometric, health) requires an additional Article 9 GDPR condition and DPA 2018 compliance, with granular consent and parental authorization for children under 13.
