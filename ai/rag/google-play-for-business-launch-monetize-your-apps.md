# Google Play for business | Launch & monetize your apps | Google Play Console

## Summary

- **Reduced Fees & Billing Flexibility**: Google Play introduces lower service fees and expanded billing options, including the **Billing Choice Program** and **User Choice Billing Pilot**, allowing developers to offer alternative payment methods and reduce revenue cuts.

- **New Developer Programs**: Launched specialized initiatives such as **Apps Accelerator**, **Level Up**, **Indie Games**, and **Teacher Approved** to support app/game growth, monetization, and compliance with child-focused policies.

- **Enhanced Play Console Features**: Updated Play Console includes **pre-launch reports**, **Android vitals monitoring**, **custom store listings**, and **deep link analytics**, alongside improved testing (closed/internal/open) and release management tools.
