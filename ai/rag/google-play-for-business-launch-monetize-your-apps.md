# Google Play for business | Launch & monetize your apps | Google Play Console

## Summary

- **Reduced Fees & Billing Flexibility**: Google Play introduces lower service fees and expanded billing options, including the *Billing Choice Program* and *User Choice Billing Pilot*, allowing developers to offer alternative payment methods beyond Google Play Billing.

- **Developer Support Programs**: New initiatives such as *Apps Accelerator*, *Indie Games*, *Apps Experience*, and *Media Experience Program* provide targeted resources, mentorship, and tools to optimize app/game development, monetization, and audience growth.

- **Enhanced Play Console Features**: Updated Play Console includes advanced testing (e.g., *Pre-launch reports*, *Closed testing*), release management (*Integrity & signing*, *Device catalog*), and growth analytics (*Store listing experiments*, *Acquisition reporting*) to streamline app lifecycle workflows.
