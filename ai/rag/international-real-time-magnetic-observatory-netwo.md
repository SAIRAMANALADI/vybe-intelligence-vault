# International Real-time Magnetic Observatory Network

## Summary

- **Global Standardization & Real-Time Data Exchange**: INTERMAGNET enforces mandatory standards for geomagnetic observatory instrumentation, data processing, formats (e.g., IAGA-approved), and near real-time transmission via satellites/Internet, ensuring consistent, high-quality global coverage for scientific and operational use.

- **Infrastructure & Collaboration**: Operates a distributed network of **Geomagnetic Information Nodes (GINs)** for data aggregation, validation, and dissemination, with technical support for establishing/upgrading observatories, particularly in remote or developing regions, while collaborating with **IAGA**, **WDS**, and **SuperMAG**.

- **Data Policies & Access**: Provides **definitive/quasi-definitive data** (via CD-ROM/DVD, web plots, or APIs) under non-commercial usage terms, with regional GINs maintaining archives and annual usage reports; commercial derivatives are permitted with cost recovery by contributing institutions.
