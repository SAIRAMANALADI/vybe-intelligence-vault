# HKUNLP/instructor-embedding

## Summary

- Introduces **Instructor**, a text embedding model fine-tuned with instructions to generalize across diverse tasks (e.g., classification, retrieval, clustering) without task-specific retraining.
- Leverages **instruction-aware embeddings** by conditioning embeddings on task-specific prompts (e.g., "Represent the sentence for sentiment analysis:"), improving zero-shot and few-shot performance.
- Achieves state-of-the-art results on **11 benchmarks** (BEIR, MTEB) with a single model, outperforming prior baselines in semantic similarity, retrieval, and clustering tasks.
