# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Requirements & Compatibility**: Requires **high-speed internet (3+ Mbps minimum, 7+ Mbps recommended)** and **supported devices** (YouTube/YouTube TV apps); no cable/satellite dependency, but **availability varies by region**.
