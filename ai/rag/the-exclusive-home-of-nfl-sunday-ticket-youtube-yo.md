# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Pricing & Bundling**: NFL Sunday Ticket offers new users **$240/yr ($20/mo for 12 months)** for out-of-market Sunday games, while the **YouTube TV Sports Plan** bundles local/national games (e.g., *Sunday Night Football*, *Monday Night Football*) at **$54.99/mo (first 12 months)**, then **$64.99/mo**. Returning users pay **$378/yr** for the bundle or **$480/yr** for standalone NFL Sunday Ticket.

- **Technical Requirements & Features**: Requires **3+ Mbps (7+ Mbps recommended)** for streaming. Supports **multiview (up to 4 games simultaneously)**, **fantasy view**, and **key-play highlights**. Accessible on **YouTube/YouTube TV apps** across devices (no satellite dish or 2-year contract).

- **Coverage & Exclusions**: Covers **all 32 NFL teams** in both AFC/NFC conferences for out-of-market Sunday games. Excludes **digital-only games** and **commercial use**. Requires **active YouTube TV Sports Plan**—canceling it revokes access to NFL Sunday Ticket.
