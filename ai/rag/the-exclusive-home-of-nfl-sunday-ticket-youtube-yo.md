# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Pricing & Plans**: New users can subscribe to NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube TV for **$20/month for 12 months** ($240 total) or standalone on YouTube for the same price; returning users pay **$31.50/month** ($378 total). Bundled with YouTube TV, first 3 months cost **$67.99/month**, then **$82.99/month**.

- **Coverage & Features**: Includes **all out-of-market Sunday NFL games** (32 teams, both AFC/NFC conferences), multiview (up to 4 games simultaneously), fantasy tracking, and NFL RedZone (optional add-on for live touchdown coverage). Excludes digital-only games and commercial use.

- **Requirements & Compatibility**: Requires **high-speed internet (minimum 3 Mbps, recommended 7+ Mbps)** and a **compatible device** (YouTube/YouTube TV apps). No satellite dish or cable subscription needed; active YouTube TV plan required for bundled access.
