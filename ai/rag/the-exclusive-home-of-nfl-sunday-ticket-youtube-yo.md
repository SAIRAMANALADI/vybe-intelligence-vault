# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Pricing & Bundling**: NFL Sunday Ticket offers new users **$240/yr ($20/mo)** for out-of-market Sunday games; bundled with YouTube TV Sports Plan (**$54.99/mo** for 12 months, then **$64.99/mo**), totaling **$1,029.88/yr** (vs. **$378/yr** standalone for returning users). Requires active Sports Plan; cancellation revokes access.

- **Access & Compatibility**: Available via **YouTube TV or standalone YouTube Primetime Channels** on supported devices (no satellite dish/contract). Requires **high-speed internet** and compatible hardware (e.g., smartphones, smart TVs, web browsers).
