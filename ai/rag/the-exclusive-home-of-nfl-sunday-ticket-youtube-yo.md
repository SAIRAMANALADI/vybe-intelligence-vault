# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Pricing & Plans**: New users pay **$240/yr** ($30/mo for 8 months) for NFL Sunday Ticket standalone; returning users pay **$378/yr** ($47.25/mo for 8 months). Bundled with YouTube TV Sports Plan, new users pay **$240/yr** (first 12 months) then **$64.99/mo**, while returning users pay **$378/yr** ($47.25/mo for 8 months).

- **Coverage & Features**: Provides **all out-of-market Sunday NFL games** (32 teams, AFC/NFC), multiview (up to 4 games), fantasy tracking, and key-play highlights. Requires **active YouTube TV plan** for local/national games; standalone access excludes digital-only games.

- **Requirements & Restrictions**: Requires **high-speed internet (3+ Mbps min, 7+ Mbps recommended)** and supported devices (YouTube/YouTube TV apps). **No refunds**, non-cancelable payments, and **commercial use excluded**. Access revoked if YouTube TV plan is canceled.
