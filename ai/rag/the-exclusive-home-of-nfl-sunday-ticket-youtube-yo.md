# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Pricing & Bundling**: NFL Sunday Ticket offers new users $240/year (12x $20/mo) for out-of-market Sunday games; bundled with YouTube TV Sports Plan ($54.99/mo for 12mo, then $64.99/mo) for local/national games (AFC/NFC coverage). Returning users pay $378/year (12x $31.50/mo) for the bundle or $480/year (12x $40/mo) for standalone Sunday Ticket. **Non-refundable, 12-month non-cancelable terms apply.**

- **Technical Requirements & Features**: Requires 3+ Mbps (7+ Mbps recommended) for streaming. Supports multiview (up to 4 games simultaneously), fantasy tracking, and key-play highlights. Accessible via YouTube/YouTube TV apps on supported devices (no satellite dish or 2-year contract). **Digital-only games excluded; commercial use prohibited.**

- **Coverage & Exclusivity**: Provides access to all 32 NFL teams’ out-of-market Sunday games (AFC/NFC conferences). Excludes locally broadcast games (NBC/CBS/FOX affiliates). **Active YouTube TV Sports Plan required for full access; cancellation revokes NFL Sunday Ticket privileges.**
