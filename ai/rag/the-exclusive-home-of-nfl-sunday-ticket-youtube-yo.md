# The Exclusive Home of NFL Sunday Ticket - YouTube & YouTube TV

## Summary

- **Pricing & Availability**: New users can subscribe to NFL Sunday Ticket via YouTube TV for **8 non-cancelable payments of $30/month** ($240 total) or standalone for the same price; returning users pay **$47.25/month** ($378 total). Requires active YouTube TV plan for full access (local/national games).

- **Coverage & Features**: Provides **all out-of-market Sunday NFL games** (32 teams, both AFC/NFC conferences) via multiview (up to 4 games simultaneously), NFL RedZone (touchdown-focused channel), and fantasy tracking. Excludes digital-only games and commercial use.

- **Technical Requirements**: Requires **high-speed internet (7+ Mbps recommended)** and supported devices (YouTube/YouTube TV apps). Accessible without cable/satellite via standalone purchase, but canceling YouTube TV plan revokes NFL Sunday Ticket access.
