## Summary

- **Pricing & Plans**: NFL Sunday Ticket offers new users **$20/month for 12 months ($240 total)** or **$31.50/month for returning users ($378 total)**, requiring an active YouTube TV subscription ($67.99/mo for 3 months, then $82.99/mo). - **Technical Requirements**: Requires **high-speed internet (3+ Mbps minimum, 7+ Mbps recommended)** and **supported devices** (YouTube/YouTube TV apps on web, mobile, or streaming platforms). No satellite dish or cable contract needed.
