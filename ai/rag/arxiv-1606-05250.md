# [1606.05250] SQuAD: 100,000+ Questions for Machine Comprehension of Text

## Summary

- **Dataset**: Introduces SQuAD (Stanford Question Answering Dataset), a large-scale reading comprehension dataset with 100,000+ questions generated by crowdworkers on Wikipedia articles, where answers are text segments from the corresponding passages.

- **Performance Analysis**: A logistic regression baseline achieves 51.0% F1 score, outperforming a simple baseline (20%), but human performance (86.8%) remains significantly higher, highlighting the dataset's challenge.

- **Methodology**: Dataset analysis leverages dependency and constituency trees to understand required reasoning types; dataset is publicly available at [stanford-qa.com](https://stanford-qa.com).
