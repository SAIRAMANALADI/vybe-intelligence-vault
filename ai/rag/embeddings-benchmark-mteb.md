# embeddings-benchmark/mteb

## Summary

- **Comprehensive Evaluation Framework**: MTEB provides a standardized benchmark for evaluating text embeddings across **58 datasets**, covering **11 tasks** (e.g., retrieval, clustering, STS, reranking) in **multilingual** and **multimodal** settings, enabling fair comparison of models like SBERT and Sentence-Transformers.

- **Modular & Extensible Design**: Implements a **Python-based** pipeline with reusable components (e.g., task-specific metrics, model wrappers) to support **low-resource NLP** and **bitext mining**, while allowing custom task integration via a standardized API.

- **State-of-the-Art Tracking**: Continuously updated (last commit: **2026-07-03**) to include **neural search**, **semantic search**, and **text classification** evaluations, with leaderboards for tracking top-performing embeddings models across languages and modalities.
