# embeddings-benchmark/mteb

## Summary

- **MTEB Framework**: A Python-based benchmarking suite for evaluating text embeddings across diverse tasks (e.g., retrieval, clustering, classification) and languages/modalities, integrating 58 datasets and 11 task categories.
- **Multilingual & Multimodal Support**: Evaluates embeddings in low-resource and high-resource languages, including bitext mining, STS, and multimodal tasks, with standardized metrics (e.g., Spearman correlation, nDCG).
- **Modular & Extensible**: Supports Sentence-BERT models, integrates with HuggingFace, and provides APIs for custom task/dataset additions, with active maintenance (last updated 2026-08-08).
