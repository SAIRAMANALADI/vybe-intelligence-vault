# NSF Expeditions in Computing: Computational Sustainability

## Summary

- **Interdisciplinary AI Research**: The Institute for Computational Sustainability (ICS) at Cornell integrates AI, optimization, and reasoning techniques to address critical sustainability challenges, including biodiversity conservation, climate change, and renewable energy systems.

- **Key Initiatives & Networks**: ICS leads CompSustNet, a global research and education network, and organizes annual events like the NeurIPS Workshop on Computational Sustainability, fostering collaboration across AI, ecology, and environmental science.

- **Impactful Projects & Awards**: Recent work includes AI-driven analysis of marine ecosystems (e.g., eelgrass disease spread), AAAI-24 Best Student Paper on hybrid solar-hydropower systems, and Carla Gomes' ACM–AAAI Allen Newell Award for foundational contributions to computational sustainability.
