# Medium Privacy Policy | by Medium | Medium Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Sources**: Medium collects user-provided data (e.g., account details, posts, payment info via third-party processors) and automatically gathers activity, device, and usage data (IP addresses, cookies, device IDs) for service personalization, analytics, and security.

- **Data Sharing & Third Parties**: Personal data is shared with vendors (analytics, hosting, fraud prevention), embedded third-party content (e.g., YouTube), and in legal/compliance contexts (mergers, law enforcement requests), with aggregated/de-identified data exempt from restrictions.

- **User Control & Transfers**: Users can access, correct, or delete account data; opt out of communications; and disable cookies. Data is transferred/stored globally (e.g., AWS data centers), with safeguards for cross-border compliance.
