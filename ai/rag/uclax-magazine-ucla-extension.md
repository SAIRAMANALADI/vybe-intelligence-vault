# UCLAx Magazine | UCLA Extension

## Summary

- **Collaborative Medical Education Pipeline**: UC Santa Cruz and UCLA Extension have partnered to create an accelerated pathway for students to enter medical school, addressing California's physician shortage by streamlining pre-medical training and admissions.

- **Innovative Curriculum Integration**: The program incorporates competency-based education, early clinical exposure, and interdisciplinary learning to modernize medical training and better prepare students for evolving healthcare demands.

- **Diversity and Inclusion Focus**: The initiative prioritizes underrepresented and first-generation students, leveraging targeted support systems (mentorship, financial aid, and tailored coursework) to increase medical school enrollment and retention among marginalized groups.
