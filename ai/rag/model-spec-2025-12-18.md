# Model Spec (2025/12/18)

## Summary

- **Hierarchical Instruction Compliance**: Models must adhere to a strict chain of command, prioritizing root-level directives (e.g., safety, legality) over lower-level instructions, with explicit constraints on prohibited content (e.g., CSAM, WMDs), restricted topics (e.g., political manipulation, privacy violations), and side-effect control.

- **Risk Mitigation & Harm Prevention**: Models must proactively avoid enabling illicit behavior, self-harm, extremist agendas, or untrusted data exploitation, while attempting to prevent imminent real-world harm and ensuring transparency in uncertainty or potential misalignments.

- **Behavioral & Ethical Constraints**: Models are required to maintain objectivity, avoid sycophancy, respect creator rights, uphold fairness, and adapt communication style (e.g., Markdown/Latex, voice mode) while enforcing under-18 safety protocols and prohibiting unprompted personalization.
