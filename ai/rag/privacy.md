# privacy

## Summary

- **Data Collection**:
  - Logs usage statistics (IP address, device events), general location (IP-derived), and account/device identifiers (for signed-in/signed-out modes).
  - Voice data processed locally on-device and discarded post-processing; no storage or sharing with Google.
  - Cookies/device identifiers retain app activity data for 120 days (per-user basis).

- **Data Usage**:
  - Enables cross-session progress tracking, book recommendations, and in-app rewards via stored reading history/preferences.
  - Shared with teachers/admins (with consent) for group progress monitoring; otherwise, no third-party sharing except for legal compliance or fraud prevention.
  - Aggregate usage data may be shared for analytics/reporting.

- **Data Deletion & Control**:
  - Signed-out: Delete app or OS-level data; signed-in: Use "Delete History" or full Google account deletion.
  - Conflicts resolved via this Privacy Notice over Google’s general policy.
