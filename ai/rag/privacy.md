# privacy

## Summary

- **Data Collection**:
  - Logs usage stats, device events, IP-derived general location, and unique identifiers (account or device-based).
  - Voice data is processed locally and discarded post-processing; no storage or transmission to Google.
  - Signed-in mode stores reading history, preferences, and child profiles (avatars/names) in Google accounts; signed-out mode uses device-local storage.

- **Data Usage**:
  - Enables cross-session progress tracking, personalized book recommendations, and in-app rewards via stored history.
  - Supports internal operations (e.g., abuse prevention, language detection) and teacher-admin group monitoring (signed-in only).
  - Aggregate usage data may be shared externally for trend reporting, excluding personal identifiers.

- **Data Sharing & Control**:
  - Personal data shared externally only with explicit consent (e.g., teachers) or under legal obligations (e.g., subpoenas).
  - Users can delete signed-out data via app uninstall or OS tools; signed-in data is removable via "Delete History" or full Google account deletion.
