# gRPC

## Summary

- **Protocol Buffers-based RPC framework**: gRPC uses Protocol Buffers (protobuf) for service definition and binary serialization, enabling efficient cross-language communication with auto-generated client/server stubs.

- **HTTP/2 & bidirectional streaming**: Leverages HTTP/2 for multiplexed, low-latency communication with support for bidirectional streaming and integrated authentication mechanisms.

- **Scalable, cross-platform microservices**: Supports pluggable features (load balancing, tracing, health checks) and scales to millions of RPCs/sec, compatible with Go, C++, Java, Rust, Python, and more.
