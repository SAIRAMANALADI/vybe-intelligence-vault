# gRPC

## Summary

- **Protocol Buffers-based RPC framework**: gRPC uses Protocol Buffers (protobuf) for service definition and binary serialization, enabling efficient cross-language communication with strongly typed interfaces.

- **HTTP/2 & bidirectional streaming**: Leverages HTTP/2 for multiplexed, low-latency communication with support for bidirectional streaming, client/server streaming, and integrated authentication/authorization.

- **Cross-platform & scalable**: Supports automatic client/server stub generation across multiple languages (Go, C++, Java, Rust, Python), scales to millions of RPCs/sec, and integrates with load balancing, tracing, and health-checking systems.
