# gRPC

## Summary

- **Protocol Buffers-based RPC**: gRPC uses Protocol Buffers (protobuf) for service definition and binary serialization, enabling efficient cross-language communication with strongly-typed contracts.

- **HTTP/2 & Bidirectional Streaming**: Leverages HTTP/2 for multiplexed, low-latency communication with native support for bidirectional streaming, bidirectional RPC, and pluggable authentication.

- **Cross-Language & Scalability**: Generates idiomatic client/server stubs for multiple languages (Go, C++, Java, Rust, Python) and scales to millions of RPCs per second with built-in load balancing, tracing, and health checks.
