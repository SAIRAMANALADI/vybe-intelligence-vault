# Privacy Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Processing Scope**: Cohere collects personal information from users (Job Applicants, Visitors, Researchers, Trial Users, Enterprise Users) via direct input or automated means (e.g., IP addresses, device metadata) for purposes including account administration, research/model improvement (with de-identification safeguards), marketing, security, and compliance. Enterprise Users can control data usage via DPAs.

- **Third-Party Disclosures & Legal Compliance**: Personal data may be shared with affiliates, vendors (e.g., cloud/storage providers), or sub-processors (listed in Cohere’s Trust Center) for operational needs, with transfers subject to international data transfer policies. Disclosures also occur under legal obligations (e.g., court orders, regulatory requests) or to prevent fraud/illegal activity.

- **Exclusions & Special Cases**: The policy excludes End Users (governed by customer privacy notices) and Private/Third-Party Deployments (data remains within customer/cloud provider environments). Cohere does not train models on Enterprise Customer Data unless explicitly agreed via commercial contracts. Minors’ data is excluded per policy.
