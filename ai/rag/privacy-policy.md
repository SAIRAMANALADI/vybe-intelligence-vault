# Privacy Policy

## Summary

- **Data Collection & Processing Scope**: Cohere collects personal information across multiple contexts (e.g., Website visitors, trial users, researchers, enterprise users) via direct input, automated tracking (IP addresses, usage metrics), and third-party enrichment. Enterprise data handling is governed by separate agreements (e.g., DPAs), while trial/research environments de-identify inputs/outputs for model improvement.

- **Research & Model Training Policies**: Trial/research users’ inputs/outputs may be used for R&D (e.g., performance/safety optimization), with explicit prohibitions against personal data submission. Enterprise users can opt out of training on their data via DPAs or enterprise-tier controls.

- **Disclosure & Compliance Framework**: Personal data is shared with affiliates, sub-processors (e.g., cloud providers), and under legal obligations (e.g., court orders). Cohere’s sub-processor list and international transfer mechanisms (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are documented separately for transparency.
