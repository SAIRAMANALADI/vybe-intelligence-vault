# Digital Millennium Copyright Act - Wikisource, the free online library

## Summary

- **Legislative Enactment**: The DMCA (Pub.L. 105−304, 112 Stat. 2860) was enacted by the 105th U.S. Congress on October 28, 1998, as H.R. 2281, amending Title 17 of the U.S. Code to implement the WIPO Copyright Treaty and Performances and Phonograms Treaty.

- **Structural Framework**: The DMCA comprises five titles: (I) WIPO Treaties Implementation, (II) Online Copyright Infringement Liability Limitation, (III) Computer Maintenance/Repair Copyright Exemption, (IV) Miscellaneous Provisions (e.g., ephemeral recordings, distance education), and (V) Protection of Certain Original Designs.

- **Public Domain Status**: The text is in the public domain in the U.S. as a federal government work (17 U.S.C. § 105), with legislative history including House/Senate reports and congressional records from 1998.
