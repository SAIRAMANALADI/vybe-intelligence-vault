# List of services & service-specific additional terms – Privacy & Terms – Google

## Summary

- **Multi-tiered Terms of Service**: Google's general [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms/terms?hl=en-US) applies to most services, but specialized services (e.g., YouTube, enterprise APIs) have **service-specific additional terms** (e.g., [AlphaFold Server](https://alphafoldserver.com/terms), [Gemini Business](https://cloud.google.com/terms/gemini-enterprise/business?hl=en_US)) with unique policies for data handling, prohibited use, and compliance.

- **Generative AI Policies**: Services leveraging AI (e.g., [Gemini Apps](https://policies.google.com/terms/terms/generative-ai/use-policy?hl=en-US), [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/terms)) enforce a **Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy**, restricting misuse cases such as malicious content generation, deepfake creation, or automated abuse.

- **Data Processing & Security Frameworks**: Enterprise services (e.g., [Android Enterprise](https://www.android.com/intl/en_US/enterprise/data-protection/terms/), [Fitbit](https://support.google.com/product-documentation/answer/13511576?hl=en_US)) include **Data Processing and Security Terms** outlining encryption, user consent, and government request handling, with sector-specific compliance (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR).
