## Summary

- **Cookie Consent Mechanisms**: Websites implement mechanisms (e.g., banners, pop-ups) to obtain user consent for cookie usage, often requiring explicit opt-in/opt-out controls per cookie category (strictly necessary, performance, functional, targeting).
- **Cookie Classification & Storage**: Cookies are categorized by purpose (session, persistent, secure, HTTP-only) and stored client-side with attributes like `SameSite`, `Secure`, and `Expires` to enforce security and retention policies.
- **Regulatory Compliance & Enforcement**: Frameworks like GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy Directive mandate cookie compliance, requiring granular user controls, consent logging, and mechanisms for withdrawal, with enforcement via penalties for non-compliance.
