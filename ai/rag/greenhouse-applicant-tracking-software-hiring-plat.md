# Greenhouse | Applicant tracking software & hiring platform

## Summary

- **AI-Powered Hiring Suite**: Integrates **Notetaker** (automated interview transcription/summarization), **Candidate Question Agent** (real-time Q&A for applicants), and **MCP** (modular candidate pipeline management) with existing tools via API/connectors for end-to-end automation.

- **Real Talent™ Fraud Prevention**: Implements **triple-layer verification** (fraud detection, identity validation, and AI-driven candidate matching) to ensure hiring integrity and reduce false positives in high-volume recruitment.

- **Structured Hiring Optimization**: Leverages **Voice AI** (post-Ezra AI Labs acquisition) for conversational screening and **MyGreenhouse portal** to provide candidates real-time updates while enabling recruiters to surface top talent via dynamic job alerts and seamless application workflows.
