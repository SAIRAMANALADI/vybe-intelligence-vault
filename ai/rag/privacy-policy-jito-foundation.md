# Privacy Policy | Jito Foundation

## Summary

- **JitoSOL Liquid Staking**: Enables users to stake SOL for JitoSOL tokens, providing liquidity while earning staking rewards, with support for MEV (Maximum Extractable Value) harvesting via Jito’s infrastructure.

- **Privacy Policy Scope**: Applies to personal data collected via Jito’s services, including wallet addresses (considered personal info under some jurisdictions), transactional data, and automated tracking via cookies/pixel tags for analytics and security.

- **Blockchain Immutability**: Wallet addresses and transaction data are stored on-chain via smart contracts, making them immutable and unmodifiable/deletable due to blockchain’s inherent properties.
