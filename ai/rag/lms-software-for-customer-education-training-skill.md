# LMS Software for Customer Education & Training | Skilljar

## Summary

- **External LMS Integration**: Skilljar’s platform enables seamless embedding of training content across multiple external platforms (e.g., CRM, community forums, in-app interfaces) via APIs and SDKs, supporting SSO, gated/public access, and branded customization.

- **Monetization & Certification Workflows**: Supports flexible monetization models (subscriptions, a-la-carte courses, credits) with native Salesforce/PayPal integrations, alongside automated certificate generation (via LinkedIn, Credly, Accredible) and proctored exam capabilities (Caveon).

- **Analytics-Driven Impact Measurement**: Provides Salesforce-integrated dashboards to correlate training engagement (NPS, adoption, retention) with business outcomes, leveraging Google Analytics, Zoom, and Webex for real-time insights.
