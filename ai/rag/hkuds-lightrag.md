# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Architecture**: Introduces a lightweight, efficient RAG framework optimized for speed and simplicity, leveraging minimalist retrieval and generation mechanisms to reduce computational overhead while maintaining performance.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Utilizes a graph-structured knowledge representation (e.g., knowledge graphs) to enhance contextual relevance and retrieval accuracy, enabling dynamic and scalable augmentation of LLM inputs.

- **Benchmark Performance**: Achieves state-of-the-art results in retrieval-augmented generation tasks with significantly lower latency (~2-5x faster) compared to traditional RAG pipelines, as validated on EMNLP 2025 benchmarks.
