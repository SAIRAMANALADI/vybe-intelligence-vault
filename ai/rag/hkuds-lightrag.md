# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a lightweight, efficient Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system optimized for speed and simplicity, leveraging graph-based retrieval to enhance LLM performance with minimal overhead.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Implements a knowledge-graph structure to dynamically retrieve and integrate relevant context, improving retrieval precision and reducing latency compared to traditional dense retrieval methods.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves state-of-the-art results in both inference speed and contextual relevance, with benchmarks demonstrating significant improvements over baseline RAG models in EMNLP 2025 evaluations.
