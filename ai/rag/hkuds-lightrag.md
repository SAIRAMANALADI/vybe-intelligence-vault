# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a lightweight, efficient Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system optimized for speed and simplicity, leveraging graph-based retrieval structures to enhance LLM performance with minimal overhead.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Utilizes a knowledge graph to dynamically construct retrieval pathways, enabling context-aware and hierarchical retrieval that improves relevance and reduces latency compared to traditional dense retrieval methods.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves significant improvements in inference speed (up to 10x faster) and resource efficiency while maintaining competitive accuracy, as validated on benchmarks like MS MARCO and NaturalQuestions.
