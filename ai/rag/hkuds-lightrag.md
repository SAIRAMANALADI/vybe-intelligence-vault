# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a lightweight, fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system optimized for efficiency, leveraging simple yet effective retrieval mechanisms to enhance LLM performance without heavy computational overhead.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Implements a graph-augmented retrieval strategy, dynamically constructing and querying knowledge graphs to improve contextual relevance and reduce latency in generating responses.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves state-of-the-art results in EMNLP 2025 evaluations, demonstrating significant improvements in retrieval speed (up to 10x faster) and accuracy compared to traditional RAG pipelines while maintaining low resource consumption.
