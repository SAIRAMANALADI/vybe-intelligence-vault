# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Architecture**: Introduces a lightweight, efficient RAG framework optimized for speed and simplicity, leveraging graph-based retrieval to enhance context-aware generation while minimizing computational overhead.

- **Key Innovations**: Implements a **dynamic graph retrieval** mechanism that dynamically constructs and queries knowledge graphs, reducing latency by 3-5x compared to traditional RAG pipelines while maintaining comparable accuracy.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves **~90% of GPT-4’s performance** on benchmark tasks (e.g., QA, summarization) with **<10% of the inference cost**, validated on EMNLP 2025 benchmarks across diverse datasets.
