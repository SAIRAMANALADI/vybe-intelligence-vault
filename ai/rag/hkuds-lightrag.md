# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Architecture**: Introduces a simplified, high-speed Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework leveraging lightweight indexing and retrieval mechanisms to optimize performance for large language models (LLMs).

- **Key Innovations**: Implements a dual-phase retrieval system combining dense vector search with graph-based knowledge propagation, reducing latency while maintaining contextual relevance in generated outputs.

- **Benchmark Performance**: Achieves **~3-5x faster inference** than traditional RAG systems (e.g., GraphRAG) with minimal accuracy trade-offs, validated on EMNLP 2025 benchmarks across diverse datasets.
