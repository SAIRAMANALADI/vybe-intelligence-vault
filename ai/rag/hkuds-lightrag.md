# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a lightweight, fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system optimized for efficiency, leveraging simple yet effective retrieval mechanisms to enhance LLM performance without heavy computational overhead.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Utilizes a graph-based retrieval strategy to dynamically construct context-aware prompts, improving relevance and coherence in generated outputs while maintaining low latency.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves significant speedups (e.g., 2-10x faster than traditional RAG) with minimal accuracy trade-offs, validated on benchmarks like MS MARCO and NaturalQuestions.
