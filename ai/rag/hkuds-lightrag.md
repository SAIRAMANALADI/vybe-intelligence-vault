# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a lightweight, efficient Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system optimized for speed and simplicity, leveraging graph-based retrieval mechanisms to enhance LLM performance.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Implements a knowledge graph structure to dynamically retrieve and integrate relevant context, reducing latency while improving generation quality compared to traditional RAG methods.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves significant improvements in inference speed (up to 10x faster) and resource efficiency while maintaining competitive accuracy, as validated in EMNLP 2025 benchmarks.
