# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a simplified and accelerated Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline optimized for efficiency, leveraging lightweight indexing and retrieval mechanisms to enhance LLM performance with minimal computational overhead.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Implements a graph-structured knowledge representation (GraphRAG) to dynamically capture and retrieve contextual relationships, improving retrieval precision and reducing latency compared to traditional dense retrieval methods.

- **EMNLP 2025 Validation**: Demonstrates state-of-the-art results in empirical evaluations, achieving faster inference speeds (up to 5x) and lower memory usage while maintaining competitive accuracy across multiple benchmarks.
