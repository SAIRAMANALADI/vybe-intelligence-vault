# HKUDS/LightRAG

## Summary

- **LightRAG Framework**: Introduces a lightweight, fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system optimized for efficiency, leveraging simple retrieval mechanisms to enhance LLM performance without heavy computational overhead.

- **Graph-Based Retrieval**: Implements a graph-augmented retrieval strategy, dynamically constructing and querying knowledge graphs to improve contextual relevance and reduce latency in RAG pipelines.

- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves state-of-the-art trade-offs between speed and accuracy, with benchmarks showing significant improvements in generation quality and retrieval efficiency compared to traditional RAG methods.
