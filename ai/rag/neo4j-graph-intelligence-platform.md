# Neo4j Graph Intelligence Platform

## Summary

- **Knowledge Layer Gap**: Enterprise AI lacks a structured, persistent "knowledge layer" to store, retrieve, and contextualize domain-specific information, leading to inefficiencies in reasoning and decision-making.

- **Graph-Based Solutions**: Neo4j highlights graph databases as a critical component for building a scalable knowledge layer, enabling efficient storage and querying of interconnected data relationships for AI applications.

- **Enterprise Adoption Barriers**: Current AI systems often rely on fragmented or static data sources, necessitating a shift toward integrated, dynamic knowledge graphs to enhance AI accuracy and contextual understanding.
