# Privacy Help Center - Policies Help

## Summary

- **Account Recovery & Security**: Google provides structured processes for account recovery (e.g., [Gmail/Account recovery](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7682439)) and compromised account mitigation via [Security Checkup](https://myaccount.google.com/security-checkup), including forensic tools to audit suspicious activity and enforce access controls.

- **Data Access & Deletion Controls**: Users can manage data via [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com) (export), [My Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/myactivity) (review/delete activity logs), and granular deletion tools (e.g., [Location History](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3118687#delete)), with auto-deletion timers and product-specific erasure (e.g., Gmail, YouTube).

- **Privacy & Personalization Settings**: Centralized controls like [Activity Controls](https://myaccount.google.com/activitycontrols), [Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com) allow users to toggle data collection (e.g., Web & App Activity, YouTube history) and ad personalization, with legal compliance via [SAR requests](https://support.google.com/policies/contact/sar).
