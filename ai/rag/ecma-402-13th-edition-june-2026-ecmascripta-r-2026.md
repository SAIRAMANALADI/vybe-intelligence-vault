## Summary

- **ECMA-402 v13.0** defines locale negotiation, parameter resolution, and abstract operations for ECMAScript internationalization APIs, including `CanonicalizeLocaleList`, `ResolveLocale`, and `FilterLocales`.

- **Core constructors** (`Intl.Collator`, `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, `Intl.NumberFormat`, etc.) and their prototype methods (`resolvedOptions`, `supportedLocalesOf`) are standardized with internal slots and abstract operation dependencies.

- **Locale/extension handling** includes validation functions (`IsWellFormedLanguageTag`, `IsWellFormedCurrencyCode`) and Unicode extension processing (`UnicodeExtensionComponents`, `InsertUnicodeExtensionAndCanonicalize`).
