# Google Store Terms of Sale for Devices

## Summary

- **Arbitration Clause**: Disputes related to device/subscription purchases from Google Store are resolved via binding arbitration on an individual, non-class basis, as outlined in Section 23 of the Terms.

- **Subscription Billing**: Continuous subscriptions auto-renew monthly/annually via linked payment method; prepaid subscriptions are non-refundable and tied to specific devices, with billing prorated for add-ons.

- **Order & Delivery Policies**: Google reserves rights to cancel orders due to fraud, stock issues, or location restrictions; delivery attempts are limited to 2 before requiring signature confirmation or replacement, with no guaranteed delivery dates.
