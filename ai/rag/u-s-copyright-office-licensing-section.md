# U.S. Copyright Office - Licensing Section

## Summary

- **Database Coverage**: Contains Cable Statements of Account from January 1, 1992 to present, including system ID, legal owner name, communities served, filing period, gross receipts, royalty fees, and filing status (paper/electronic).

- **Search Methods**: Records can be retrieved by legal name, community served, or Cable System ID number via the Copyright Office’s licensing portal.

- **Data Fields**: Includes subscriber counts, rate charges (for select periods), correspondence status, and filing type (P/E), with electronic filing (E) effective from the 2017/1 accounting period.
