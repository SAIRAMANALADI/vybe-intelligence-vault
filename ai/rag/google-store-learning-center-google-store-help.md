# Google Store Learning Center - Google Store Help

## Summary

- **Return Window & Process**: Most Google Store purchases are returnable within **15 days** via a structured return process: initiate return, prepare item, package securely, and ship back to Google.
- **Return Tracking & Refunds**: Users can track return status, check refund wait times (varies by payment method), and monitor refund progress through Google Store support links.
- **Support Documentation**: Google provides structured support articles with step-by-step instructions for returns, packaging, and refund status checks.
