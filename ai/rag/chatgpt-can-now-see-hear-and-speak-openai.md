# ChatGPT can now see, hear, and speak | OpenAI

## Summary

- **Multimodal Integration**: ChatGPT now supports voice (via Whisper-based speech recognition and a new text-to-speech model) and image input (powered by GPT-3.5/GPT-4 vision models), enabling real-time conversational and visual analysis capabilities.

- **Safety & Deployment Strategy**: Voice and vision features are being rolled out gradually to Plus/Enterprise users (iOS/Android for voice, all platforms for images) with safeguards against misuse (e.g., impersonation) and privacy-preserving measures (e.g., limiting direct analysis of individuals).

- **Technical Limitations & Expansion**: Models exhibit reduced performance in non-English languages (especially non-Latin scripts) and require user verification for high-stakes applications; broader access (including developers) is planned post-initial rollout.
