# Gainsight Certification Programs

## Summary

- **Gainsight NXT Admin Certification** offers a three-tiered validation path: **Level 1 (Associate, $70, 36 MCQ, 45 min)** for foundational knowledge, **Level 2 (Specialist, $125, 8-hour practical exam)** for advanced configuration skills, and **Level 3 (Expert, $300, 60 MCQ/MSQ, 120 min, proctored)** for platform mastery.

- **Specialized Product Certifications** include **Gainsight Certified End User ($99, 32 MCQ, 45 min, 80% passing)** for day-to-day CS functions, **Gainsight PX Certified ($70, 32 MCQ, 45 min, 80% passing)** for Product Experience setup, and **Skilljar Certified Admin Professional (60 questions, 60–90 min)** for LMS dashboard management.

- **Customer Success Professional Certifications** (via Pulse+) validate industry skills: **CSM Certified Professional**, **Advanced CSM Certified Professional**, **CS Leader Certified Professional**, and **CS Ops Certified Professional**, with registration and exams hosted on the Pulse+ platform.
