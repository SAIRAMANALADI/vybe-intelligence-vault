# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

- **Multi-Functional LLM Platform**: WeKnora transforms raw documents into a **queryable RAG system**, an **autonomous reasoning agent**, and a **self-maintaining Wiki**, supporting **multi-tenancy** and **multi-model** LLM integrations (e.g., Ollama, OpenAI).

- **Technical Stack & Features**: Built in **Go**, it includes **vector search**, **semantic search**, **reranking**, **embeddings**, and **evaluation** pipelines for **question-answering** and **chatbot** applications.

- **Scalability & Deployment**: Designed for **high scalability** with support for **multi-tenant** architectures, enabling **knowledge base** management and **generative AI** workflows.
