# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

- **Multi-functional LLM Platform**: Converts raw documents into a queryable Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system, autonomous reasoning agent, and self-maintaining Wiki, supporting multimodal and multi-tenant architectures.

- **Technical Stack**: Implemented in Go, integrates embeddings, vector search, reranking, and semantic search; supports OpenAI and Ollama models for LLM interactions.

- **Features**: Enables question-answering, chatbot functionality, and knowledge base management with evaluation and generative AI capabilities.
