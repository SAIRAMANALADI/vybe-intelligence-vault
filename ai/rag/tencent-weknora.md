# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

*   Implements an open-source LLM knowledge platform for transforming raw documents into a queryable RAG system, an autonomous reasoning agent, and a self-maintaining Wiki.
*   Utilizes embeddings, reranking, and semantic/vector search for advanced information retrieval, supporting multi-tenant architectures.
*   Developed in Go, it integrates with both OpenAI and local Ollama LLMs for generative AI and question-answering capabilities.
