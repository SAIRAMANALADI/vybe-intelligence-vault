# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

- **Modular LLM Knowledge Platform**: Implements a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** system, **autonomous reasoning agents**, and a **self-maintaining Wiki** from raw documents, supporting multi-tenant architectures.

- **Technical Stack**: Built in **Go**, integrates with **Ollama** and **OpenAI** for embeddings/LLM inference, includes **vector search**, **reranking**, and **semantic search** for efficient knowledge retrieval.

- **Features**: Supports **question-answering**, **evaluation pipelines**, and **DSH-plugin** integration, optimized for **generative AI** workflows with **embedding-based** and **agentic** capabilities.
