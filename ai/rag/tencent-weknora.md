# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

- **Multi-functional LLM Platform**: WeKnora transforms raw documents into a **queryable RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**, an **autonomous reasoning agent**, and a **self-maintaining Wiki**, enabling structured knowledge extraction and dynamic updates.

- **Technical Stack**: Built in **Go**, it supports **multi-tenant** deployments, integrates with **Ollama/OpenAI** for LLM inference, and implements **vector search**, **semantic search**, **reranking**, and **embeddings** for efficient knowledge retrieval and processing.

- **Features & Extensibility**: Includes **chatbot**, **evaluation tools**, **DSH-plugin** support, and **question-answering** capabilities, with a focus on **generative AI** and **knowledge-base** management.
