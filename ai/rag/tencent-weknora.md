# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

- **Multi-Functional LLM Platform**: WeKnora transforms raw documents into a **queryable RAG system**, an **autonomous reasoning agent**, and a **self-maintaining Wiki**, leveraging Go for high-performance backend operations.

- **Advanced Retrieval & Embedding Pipeline**: Supports **vector search**, **reranking**, and **semantic search** with integrations for **Ollama** and **OpenAI**, enabling multi-tenant knowledge base management and evaluation.

- **Modular Architecture**: Designed for **agentic workflows**, **chatbot integration**, and **DSH-plugin compatibility**, with built-in **embeddings** and **question-answering** capabilities for scalable generative AI applications.
