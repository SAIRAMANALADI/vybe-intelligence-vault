# Tencent/WeKnora

## Summary

- **Multi-Model RAG & Agentic Framework**: WeKnora transforms raw documents into a **queryable RAG system**, an **autonomous reasoning agent**, and a **self-maintaining Wiki**, supporting **multi-tenant** and **multi-model** workflows via integrations with **Ollama** and **OpenAI**.

- **Technical Stack & Features**: Built in **Go**, it includes **vector search**, **semantic search**, **reranking**, **embeddings**, and **evaluation** tools for **question-answering** and **chatbot** applications.

- **Deployment & Extensibility**: Supports **LLM-based knowledge base** management with **GitHub-sync capabilities**, enabling **automated updates** and **collaborative Wiki maintenance**.
