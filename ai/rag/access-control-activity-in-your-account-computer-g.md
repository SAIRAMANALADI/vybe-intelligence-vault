# Access & control activity in your account - Computer - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Activity Data Collection & Storage**: Google's "My Activity" centralizes user activity (searches, websites, videos) tied to a Google Account, including associated metadata like location, contingent on enabled activity settings and active sign-in status.

- **Activity Management & Deletion**: Users can view, filter, and delete activity via [My Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/myactivity), with options to turn off/on data collection, require extra verification for shared devices, or set up automatic deletion for older activity.

- **Activity Visibility & Troubleshooting**: Activity may not appear due to offline status, multiple signed-in accounts, or unsupported services; unfamiliar activity could stem from shared devices, Google Assistant predictions, or unauthorized access, requiring account security review.
