# IntelLabs/fastRAG

## Summary

- **Framework Overview**: FastRAG is an efficient, Python-based framework for **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** optimized for low-latency, high-throughput applications, supporting **multi-modal inputs** (text, knowledge graphs) and **diffusion models** for generative tasks.

- **Key Components**: Integrates **ColBERT** for dense retrieval, **sentence-transformers** for embeddings, and **transformers** for LLM-based generation, with built-in support for **semantic search**, **QA**, and **summarization** benchmarks.

- **Performance Focus**: Designed for **scalability** with hardware-aware optimizations (e.g., Intel accelerators), enabling **real-time inference** while maintaining accuracy across NLP and multi-modal tasks.
