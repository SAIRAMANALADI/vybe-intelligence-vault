# [2212.14024] Demonstrate-Search-Predict: Composing retrieval and language models for knowledge-intensive NLP

## Summary

- **DSP Framework**: Introduces *Demonstrate-Search-Predict (DSP)*, a framework that composes frozen language models (LMs) and retrieval models (RMs) via natural language pipelines, decomposing knowledge-intensive tasks into modular transformations (demonstration generation, retrieval, and prediction).

- **Performance Gains**: Achieves **37–120%**, **8–39%**, and **80–290%** relative improvements over vanilla LM (GPT-3.5), standard retrieve-then-read pipelines, and self-ask baselines, respectively, across open-domain, multi-hop, and conversational settings.

- **Pipeline Design**: Enables high-level programs where LMs and RMs interact via intermediate natural language texts, systematically breaking down complex problems into smaller, more reliable sub-tasks (e.g., bootstrapping demonstrations, targeted retrieval, and grounded generation).
