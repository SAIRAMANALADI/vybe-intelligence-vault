# [2212.14024] Demonstrate-Search-Predict: Composing retrieval and language models for knowledge-intensive NLP

## Summary

- **DSP Framework**: Introduces *Demonstrate-Search-Predict (DSP)*, a modular framework that decomposes knowledge-intensive NLP tasks into natural language transformations, enabling sophisticated pipelines that combine frozen language models (LMs) and retrieval models (RMs) beyond simple "retrieve-then-read" paradigms.

- **Performance Gains**: Demonstrates 37–120% relative improvements over vanilla LM (GPT-3.5), 8–39% over standard retrieve-then-read pipelines, and 80–290% over concurrent self-ask methods in open-domain, multi-hop, and conversational QA tasks.

- **Pipeline Design**: Implements high-level programs that autonomously generate pipeline-aware demonstrations, retrieve contextually relevant passages, and produce grounded predictions, leveraging LM/RM strengths for reliable intermediate steps.
