# iPadOS - Apple Developer

## Summary

- **Apple Intelligence Integration**: iPadOS 27 introduces Apple Intelligence, leveraging next-generation Apple Foundation Models for on-device and Private Cloud Compute AI, enabling multimodal prompts, app actions, and on-screen awareness via App Intents and Foundation Models framework for real-time adaptive intelligence.

- **Advanced UI/UX & Input Tools**: New platform design refinements with Liquid Glass enhance consistency and accessibility, while PencilKit (with Apple Pencil Pro support) and PaperKit enable low-latency, high-precision drawing, handwriting recognition, and markup capabilities for creative and productivity apps.

- **Real-Time Engagement Features**: Live Activities and Widgets provide glanceable, interactive updates on Lock Screen/Dynamic Island, and the system’s semantic index (via App Intents) supports natural language-driven app actions, alongside Metal-powered GPU optimizations and passkeys for secure, passwordless authentication.
