## Summary

- **Content Descriptors**: Additional warnings (e.g., In-Game Purchases, Fear, Discrimination, Drugs, Gambling, Bad Language, Violence, Sex) provide granular details on potentially objectionable material, often correlating with specific PEGI ratings.
- **Regional Standardization**: PEGI ratings are enforced across 38 European countries, ensuring consistent age-based content guidelines for digital and physical game releases.
