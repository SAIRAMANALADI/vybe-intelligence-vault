# Publications – Google Research

## Summary

- **Humanlike Generative AI Risks**: Focus groups with 30 tech professionals identified overtrust in humanlike AI outputs as a critical hazard, requiring clearer conceptual frameworks to mitigate societal and professional risks.

- **SYMBIOSIS Framework**: Proposes an AI-powered system integrating systems thinking with machine intelligence to address societal challenges via open-source causal models (e.g., stock-flow diagrams) and community-driven insights.

- **Disability Inclusion Gaps**: Studies reveal AI systems often fail to address harms to disabled users due to fragmented accessibility practices, necessitating community-led evaluations and iterative prompting techniques.
