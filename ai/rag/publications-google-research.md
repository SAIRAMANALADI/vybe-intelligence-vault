# Publications – Google Research

## Summary

- **Generative AI Humanlikeness Risks**: Focus groups with 30 tech workers identified overtrust in humanlike AI outputs as a critical hazard, requiring clearer conceptual frameworks to mitigate emergent societal and professional risks.

- **SYMBIOSIS Framework**: Proposes an AI-powered, open-source systems thinking platform translating causal loop/stock-flow models into natural language to bridge epistemic gaps in societal problem formulation for AI development.

- **Disability Representation Gaps**: Community-led evaluations of text-to-image AI revealed prompt engineering fails to address disability/health/accessibility misrepresentations, emphasizing evaluator agency in authentic harm assessment.
