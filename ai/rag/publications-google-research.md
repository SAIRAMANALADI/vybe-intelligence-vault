# Publications – Google Research

## Summary

- **Generative AI Anthropomorphism Risks**: Focus groups with 30 tech workers (ML engineers, UX researchers, etc.) revealed overtrust in humanlike AI systems, highlighting hazards like misaligned causal theories and epistemic uncertainty in complex problem spaces, necessitating clearer "humanlikeness" frameworks for risk mitigation.

- **SYMBIOSIS Framework**: Proposes an AI-powered systems thinking platform (open-source, SDG-aligned) with a generative co-pilot translating causal loops/stock-flow diagrams into natural language to bridge problem formulation gaps in responsible AI development.

- **Disability Representation Gaps**: Community-led evaluations (21 participants with disabilities) showed researcher-designed prompt engineering failed to improve text-to-image AI representations of disability/health, emphasizing evaluator agency and iterative prompting as critical for authentic accessibility assessments.
