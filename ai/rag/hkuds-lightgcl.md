# HKUDS/LightGCL

## Summary

- **Proposes LightGCL**, a lightweight graph contrastive learning (GCL) framework for recommendation systems, leveraging simple yet effective augmentation strategies to enhance collaborative filtering (CF) without complex architectural modifications.

- **Introduces spectral contrastive loss** to align node embeddings in the spectral domain, improving robustness against noisy interactions while maintaining computational efficiency compared to traditional GCL methods.

- **Achieves state-of-the-art performance** on multiple benchmark datasets (e.g., Gowalla, Yelp2018) with significant gains in accuracy and training efficiency, demonstrating the scalability of LightGCL for large-scale recommendation tasks.
