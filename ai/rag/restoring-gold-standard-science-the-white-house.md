# Restoring Gold Standard Science – The White House

## Summary

- **Policy Mandate**: Executive Order establishes "Gold Standard Science" requiring federal agencies to adhere to reproducibility, transparency, unbiased peer review, and explicit acknowledgment of uncertainty/error in scientific data, with definitions of scientific misconduct (fabrication, falsification, plagiarism) and "weight of scientific evidence" approach for evaluations.

- **Implementation & Compliance**: OSTP Director to issue agency-specific guidance within 30 days; agencies must update policies within 60 days, report progress, and ensure contractor adherence. Interim policies revert to pre-2021 standards, rescind conflicting 2021–2025 policies, and enforce open scientific discourse while protecting dissenting viewpoints.

- **Data Transparency & Enforcement**: Agencies must publicly disclose influential scientific data/models (excluding enforcement risk models) and document uncertainties; violations trigger senior appointee-led internal reviews with corrective measures per statutory requirements (e.g., Information Quality Act §515).
