# Update your account to meet age requirements - Google Account Help

## Summary

- **Age Verification & Account Management**: Google enforces minimum age requirements per region; users flagged for underage status have 14 days to set up parental supervision or verify age via government ID, credit card, or other region-specific methods before account disablement and potential deletion after 30 days.

- **Supervision & Brand Account Limitations**: Parental supervision can be enabled via a parent’s Google Account within 14 days of birthdate entry; supervision is incompatible with Brand Accounts, requiring conversion or deletion prior to setup.

- **Data Export & Account Deletion**: Users can download data from active accounts within 14 days or disabled accounts (limited services); alternatively, accounts can be preemptively deleted before disablement.
