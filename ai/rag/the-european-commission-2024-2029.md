# The European Commission 2024-2029

## Summary

- **Competitiveness & Industrial Strategy**: Launch of a *Competitiveness Compass* to close innovation gaps, paired with a *Clean Industrial Deal* to decarbonize industry, reduce energy costs, and boost AI-driven productivity while accelerating green/digital transitions and addressing labor/skills shortages.

- **Defence & Security Integration**: Establishment of a *Defence Union* to strengthen industrial base/resilience, alongside a *Preparedness Union Strategy* for rapid crisis response, enhanced border security, and migration management via legal pathways.

- **EU Reform & Global Influence**: Proposal for a *new budget framework* aligned with strategic interests, an *ambitious reform agenda* for EU enlargement, and a *reshaped multilateral foreign policy* to reduce dependencies, advance enlargement as a geopolitical imperative, and ensure equitable global representation.
