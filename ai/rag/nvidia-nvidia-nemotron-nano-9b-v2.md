# nvidia / nvidia-nemotron-nano-9b-v2

## Summary

- **Hybrid Architecture**: Combines Mamba-2 and MLP layers with only 4 Attention layers, leveraging a Nemotron-Hybrid design for efficient reasoning and non-reasoning tasks.
- **Reasoning Control**: Supports runtime reasoning budget control via system prompts (`/think` or `/no_think`), enabling dynamic adjustment of intermediate reasoning traces for accuracy vs. latency trade-offs.
- **Performance Metrics**: Achieves high benchmark scores (e.g., 97.8% on MATH500, 72.1% on AIME25) while optimizing compute efficiency (1.53E+24 FLOPS, 747.6 MWh training energy).
