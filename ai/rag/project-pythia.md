# Project Pythia

## Summary

- **Mission & Scope**: Project Pythia is the education working group of [Pangeo](https://pangeo.io), providing open-source, community-owned, geoscience-focused Python learning resources to facilitate open, reproducible scientific computing in the geosciences.

- **Core Resources**: Offers three key educational components: *The Foundations Book* (tutorials for foundational Python/geoscience skills), *Pythia Cookbooks* (advanced, domain-specific workflows), and the *Resource Gallery* (curated list of external Python/geoscience learning materials).

- **Community & Governance**: Operates under Apache 2.0 (code) and CC BY 4.0 (content) licenses, welcomes open contributions via GitHub, and hosts monthly public meetings for collaboration and updates.
