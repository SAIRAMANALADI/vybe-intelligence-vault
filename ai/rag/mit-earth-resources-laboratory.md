# MIT Earth Resources Laboratory

## Summary

- **Geothermal Energy**: ERL focuses on extracting deep crustal heat (>400°C) using advanced drilling (millimeter-wave) and corrosion-resistant glass casings, integrating multi-physics sensing/imaging to optimize supercritical fluid reservoir performance and heat extraction at unprecedented scales.

- **Carbon Sequestration**: Research targets CO₂ mineralization and biological processing for long-term storage in deep saline aquifers, addressing uncertainties in geologic capacity and sustainable injection rates to enable scalable climate mitigation.

- **Geologic Hydrogen & Critical Minerals**: Investigates serpentinization kinetics for natural H₂ production and develops in-situ extraction technologies for critical minerals (e.g., Cu, Li, REEs) via integrated geoscience-engineering approaches to secure supply chains for energy transition technologies.
