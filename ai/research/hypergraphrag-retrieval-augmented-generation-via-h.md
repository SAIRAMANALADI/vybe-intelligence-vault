# HyperGraphRAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation via Hypergraph-Structured Knowledge Representation

## Summary

Standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) relies on chunk-based retrieval, whereas GraphRAG advances this approach by graph-based knowledge representation. However, existing graph-based RAG approaches are constrained by binary relations, as each edge in an ordinary graph connects only two entities, limiting their ability to represent the n-ary relations (n>= 2) in real-world knowledge.
