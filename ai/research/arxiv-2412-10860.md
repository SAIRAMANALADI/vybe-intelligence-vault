# Classification of Financial Data Using Quantum Support Vector Machine

## Summary

arXiv:2412.10860v2 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Quantum Support Vector Machine is a kernel-based approach to classification problems. We study the applicability of quantum kernels to financial data, specifically our self-curated Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSEx) Broad Index dataset. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first systematic study of quantum kernels applied to this dataset. Working within the empirical quantum advantage (EQA) framework of Krunic et al., we benchmark several quantum kernels against a classical RBF-kernel SVM baseline, propose the best-performing kernel for this dataset, and relate the observations to the Phase Space Terrain Ruggedness Index metric. We estimate the resources needed to carry out these investigations on a larger scale for future practitioners.
