# EyeMulator: Improving Code Language Models by Mimicking Human Visual Attention

## Summary

arXiv:2508.16771v3 Announce Type: replace-cross 
Abstract: Code Language Models (CodeLLMs) learn token importance from data correlations, whereas human developers attend selectively to semantically salient code. We present EyeMulator, a model-agnostic method that injects human visual-attention priors into CodeLLM fine-tuning without architectural changes. EyeMulator distills eye-tracking data into semantic salience and gaze-transition priors, then uses them to reweight token-level training losses. Across six backbones, two data regimes, and three CodeXGLUE tasks, the reported configurations yield positive matched-metric deltas in all 36 model-task-setting cells. Effects are largest for structure-preserving completion and translation, while summarization shows smaller but positive METEOR deltas. Session-mode and component-ablation analyses further show that reading, writing, semantic, and transition-derived priors provide complementary signal. Human-attention artifacts are available at https://zenodo.org/records/17205682.
