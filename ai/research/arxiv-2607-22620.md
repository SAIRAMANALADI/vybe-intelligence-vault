# A Resolution of the SS--RS--GD Inequalities

## Summary

arXiv:2607.22620v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Yun, Sra, and Jadbabaie (COLT 2021, open question) conjectured the SS--RS--GD inequalities: for well-conditioned symmetric matrices $A_1,\dots,A_n$, the operators $W_{ss}$, $W_{rs}$, and $W_{gd}$ that encode the expected iterate of single-shuffle SGD, random-reshuffle SGD, and gradient descent on a quadratic finite sum should satisfy \[
 \|W_{ss}\|\le \| W_{rs}\|\le \|W_{gd}\|. \] The conjecture is resolved,
 $\bullet$ SS-RS inequality fails. Already for $n=3$, $K=2$, and $d=4$, we exhibit explicit PSD matrices whose condition number is arbitrarily close to $1$, yet $\|W_{ss}\|>\|W_{rs}\|$.
 $\bullet$ RS-GD inequality holds. For every symmetric $A_i$ with $\bigl(1-\frac1{4n^2+1}\bigr)I\preceq A_i\preceq I$, one has $\|W_{rs}\|\le\|W_{gd}\|$.
 The proof was found via GPT-5.5 Pro extended prompted by the author.
