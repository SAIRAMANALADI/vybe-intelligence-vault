# PoisonedRAG: Knowledge Corruption Attacks to Retrieval-Augmented Generation of Large Language Models

## Summary

Large language models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable success due to their exceptional generative capabilities. Despite their success, they also have inherent limitations such as a lack of up-to-date knowledge and hallucination. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a state-of-the-art technique to mitigate these limitations.
