# To Use AI as Dice of Possibilities with Timing Computation

## Summary

arXiv:2605.01134v3 Announce Type: replace 
Abstract: The dominant noun-based modeling paradigm has fundamentally constrained AI development, precluding any adequate representation of the future as an open temporal dimension. This paper introduces a verb-based paradigm, together with precise definitions of \emph{timing computation} and \emph{causal factum}, that enables AI to function as an instrument for spontaneously constructing a causal-reasoning world model.
 Applied to longitudinal EHR data from 3,276 breast cancer patients, the framework empirically demonstrates: (1) automatic discovery of clinically significant patient trajectories, and (2) counterfactual timing deduction, that is, a \emph{What-If Machine}. Both results are achieved in a purely data-driven manner, without recourse to prior domain knowledge, and represent, to our knowledge, the first such demonstrations in the machine learning literature.
