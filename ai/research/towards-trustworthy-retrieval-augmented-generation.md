# Towards Trustworthy Retrieval Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey

## Summary

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) enhances AI-generated content by integrating external knowledge, improving relevance, and reducing hallucinations. However, RAG also introduces risks related to reliability, safety, privacy, fairness, explainability, and accountability, which impact trustworthiness. While various methods aim to address these concerns, a unified framework is lacking. This survey bridges that gap by presenting a comprehensive roadmap for trustworthy RAG systems.
