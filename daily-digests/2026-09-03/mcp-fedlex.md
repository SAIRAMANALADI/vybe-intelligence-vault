## Summary
mcp-fedlex is an MCP server that provides AI systems with access to Swiss federal law via the Fedlex infrastructure. It offers 35 tools to resolve legal citations, retrieve consolidated law texts, verify quotes against official documents, and more, all while maintaining transparency by citing sources and avoiding data retention.

## Key Features
- 35 specialized tools for Swiss federal law retrieval and verification
- Bitemporal citation resolution (e.g., resolve SR numbers to ELI, check in-force status)
- Quote verification against official norm texts with source citations
- Offline testing with recorded fixtures and live mode against Fedlex SPARQL endpoint
- No data retention, no state management, and transparent sourcing from official sources

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI systems to accurately and transparently interact with Swiss federal law, ensuring legal citations and quotes are verifiable against official sources, which is critical for RAG applications in legal and regulatory domains.
