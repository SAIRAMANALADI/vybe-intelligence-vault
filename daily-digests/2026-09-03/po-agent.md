## Summary
PO Agent is an AI role model designed to assist Senior/Lead Product Owners in making evidence-based product decisions by analyzing context, identifying bottlenecks, and evaluating alternatives through a structured decision-making framework.

## Key Features
- Restores decision questions instead of providing ready-made solutions
- Links choices to business and customer outcomes
- Evaluates decision quality with Senior/Lead readiness criteria
- Includes retrospective analysis to avoid hindsight bias
- Supports structured decision loops with confidence profiling

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PO Agent provides a structured, evidence-based approach for AI agents to emulate expert-level product decision-making, critical for RAG systems handling complex product scenarios.
