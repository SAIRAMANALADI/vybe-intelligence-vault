## Summary
A deterministic AIGC content production studio that converts business briefs into structured, provider-neutral task packages for video, image, and voice content, including asset manifests and human review gates. It enforces governance, compliance, and traceability without calling paid models or generating actual media assets.

## Key Features
- Converts business briefs into structured task packages for video, image, and voice content
- Enforces human review gates for compliance, facts, and brand alignment
- Generates deterministic asset manifests with stable IDs and execution traces
- Provider-neutral task planning without calling paid models or APIs
- Includes PRD, architecture docs, and a static browser prototype for validation
