## Summary
claude-obsidian is an Obsidian plugin that integrates Claude AI directly into the Obsidian vault, enabling users to chat with their notes, perform agent-mode operations, generate interactive artifacts, and manage research workflows with diff-reviewed edits and local model fallback.

## Key Features
- Direct integration of Claude AI into Obsidian for chat and agent-mode operations
- Diff-reviewed edits with per-hunk acceptance for safe note modifications
- Interactive claude-html artifacts rendered inline in a sandboxed iframe
- Research Desk and Workbench for evidence-backed writing and source management
- Local model fallback with Ollama support and semantic search for vault PDFs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges the gap between AI agents and personal knowledge management by enabling secure, context-aware interactions with Obsidian vaults, enhancing RAG pipelines with structured note integration and local-first processing.
