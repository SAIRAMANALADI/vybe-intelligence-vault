## Summary
VM0 is an AI-powered autonomous agent platform designed to act as a trustworthy teammate for real-world tasks across Slack, web, and enterprise workflows. It orchestrates over 100 tools to automate complex workflows like business briefs, investor updates, sales outreach, engineering triage, and operational reporting.

## Key Features
- 100+ tool integrations with granular permissions and isolated execution in Firecracker microVMs
- Scheduled and recurring task automation with specialized sub-agents for roles like research, triage, and outreach
- Deep contextual memory across sessions and tools, eliminating the need for re-explaining
- Real-time and scheduled intelligence delivery via Slack, email, and web dashboards
- Fully auditable workflows with detailed logs, metrics, and network visibility

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
VM0 enables RAG builders to integrate autonomous agents that orchestrate complex, multi-tool workflows with enterprise-grade security and contextual awareness, reducing manual intervention in AI-driven systems.
