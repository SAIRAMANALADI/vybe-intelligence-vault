## Summary
AI Contract Agent enforces hard contracts in AI-assisted software development by ensuring AI models persist artifacts, follow test-driven development, and adhere to user-defined PDLC phases. It acts as an accountability engine between developers and LLMs, halting execution if contracts are violated.

## Key Features
- Hard contract enforcement requiring AI to persist artifacts to disk before proceeding
- Multi-phase PDLC pipeline with configurable enforcement rules (up to 31 phases)
- Provider-agnostic integration with OpenAI, Claude, and local models
- Failing test validation to ensure genuine TDD before implementation
- Audit trail logging all contract violations, bypasses, and AI responses for compliance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI-generated code and artifacts are verifiable and persistent, reducing the risk of untested or undocumented outputs in production workflows.
