## Summary
GospelChannel is a global church discovery platform that helps users find and compare churches based on location, tradition, language, and worship style, with integrated music and video content from Spotify and YouTube. Built with Next.js 16 and deployed on Cloudflare Workers.

## Key Features
- Church search and comparison by multiple criteria (location, tradition, language, worship style)
- Integration with Spotify and YouTube for embedded music and video content
- SEO optimization with sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and structured data generation
- User-submitted church suggestions and ownership claims
- Scheduled content refresh jobs for up-to-date information

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, searchable database of church profiles with multimedia content, which can serve as a valuable data source or reference for AI applications focused on community and worship discovery.
