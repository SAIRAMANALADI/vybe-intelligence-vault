## Summary
The Social Authenticity Engine (SAE) is a cognitive toolkit that uses machine learning, behavioral psychology, and real-time interaction analysis to detect, analyze, and counter manipulative social strategies like those employed by pickup artists (PUA). - Real-time interaction analysis and authenticity scoring
- Cross-platform compatibility with mobile and desktop support
