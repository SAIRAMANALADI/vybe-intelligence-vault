## Summary
Eveil is an open-source, self-hostable B2B cold outreach tool that autonomously discovers leads, crafts personalized email sequences, and manages replies using AI. It reads your product website to understand your audience and generates tailored outbound campaigns from your own mailbox.

## Key Features
- Autonomous lead discovery from product websites without manual input
- AI-generated email sequences tailored to your brand voice and audience
- Sends emails directly from your own SMTP mailbox (no relay or shared domain)
- Automated reply threading and bounce circuit breaker for domain reputation protection
- Self-hostable with AGPL-3.0 license and no artificial limits on usage

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Eveil automates the entire B2B outreach pipeline, reducing manual effort while ensuring personalized, high-quality campaigns that respect your brand and infrastructure.
