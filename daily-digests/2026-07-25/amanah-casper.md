## Summary
Amanah is an autonomous AI agent managing a tokenized Real World Asset (RWA) treasury on the Casper blockchain. It autonomously ingests live RWA prices, reasons with an LLM, signs decisions cryptographically, and executes yield reallocations while enforcing on-chain compliance, auditor quorums, and zero-knowledge proofs for reserves and KYC.

## Key Features
- Autonomous AI-driven treasury management with cryptographic attestations for every decision
- On-chain enforcement of auditor quorums (K-of-N) and circuit breakers (reputation floors, dead-man's switch)
- Zero-knowledge proofs for reserves (Pedersen+Schnorr) and KYC (Schnorr NIZK) verified on-chain
- Separation of duties: decision-making agent vs. approval auditors vs. custodian-owned gates
- Real-time integration with live RWA price feeds, MCP servers, and on-chain settlement via x402

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates a fully verifiable, regulator-defensible autonomous RWA treasury where every AI decision is cryptographically proven on-chain, ensuring capital preservation, compliance, and trustless auditing at scale.
