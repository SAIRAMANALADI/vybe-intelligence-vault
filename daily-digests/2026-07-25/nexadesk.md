## Summary
NexaDesk is an AI-powered receptionist system for real estate agencies that autonomously handles inbound property enquiries via voice and chat. It qualifies leads, answers FAQs using RAG, books viewings, and integrates with CRM and calendar tools.

## Key Features
- 24/7 autonomous handling of inbound calls and chats for property enquiries
- RAG-powered FAQs with OpenAI embeddings and Qdrant vector search
- Automated lead capture and CRM integration via Supabase
- Multi-language support (English, Arabic, Urdu, French, Spanish)
- Google Calendar integration for viewing bookings

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
NexaDesk demonstrates a production-ready AI agent framework for real estate that integrates voice, chat, RAG, and CRM tools, serving as a blueprint for autonomous customer-facing AI systems.
