## Summary
A single-file Python framework that transforms any AI model (OpenAI, Claude, or local) into a self-directed research assistant capable of autonomously designing, executing, and refining scientific experiments based on user-defined goals.

## Key Features
- Self-directed hypothesis generation and testing without human intervention
- Multi-model support (OpenAI, Claude, local models) with automatic fallback
- Built-in statistical analysis (p-values, effect sizes, confidence intervals)
- Iterative refinement loop for hypothesis testing and result validation
- Exportable research logs with timestamped, citable experiment records

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to autonomously design and validate experiments, accelerating research workflows and reducing manual oversight for RAG and AI stack builders.
