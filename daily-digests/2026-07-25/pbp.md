## Summary
PBP is a personal job application portal that manages applications, searches job portals, and provides honest feedback on application materials like resumes and cover letters. It operates locally, ensuring data privacy, and integrates with Claude Desktop for conversational AI-driven workflows tailored to the German-speaking job market.

## Key Features
- Local-first application with data stored on user's device (SQLite database)
- Conversational AI integration with Claude Desktop for profile building, job search, and interview preparation
- Multi-perspective resume analysis (ATS, recruiter, and personal feedback)
- Automated job portal scraping and newsletter ingestion for job discovery
- Comprehensive application tracking with timeline, follow-ups, and statistics

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PBP provides a privacy-focused, AI-driven toolkit for job seekers to optimize their applications and receive actionable feedback, reducing reliance on external career services.
