## Summary
Trigger.dev is an open-source platform for building, deploying, and managing AI agents and workflows in TypeScript. It provides durable, long-running tasks with retries, queues, observability, and elastic scaling without infrastructure management.

## Key Features
- Long-running tasks with no timeouts and automatic retries
- Human-in-the-loop workflows with waitpoints and approvals
- Comprehensive observability with tracing, logging, and real-time monitoring

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI engineers to build and deploy scalable, durable AI agents and workflows without managing infrastructure, ensuring reliability and observability.
