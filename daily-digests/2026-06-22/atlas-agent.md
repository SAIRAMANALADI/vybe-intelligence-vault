## Summary
Atlas Agent is a local-first research and paper-trading workbench that transforms LLMs and broker/API providers into a supervised trading workspace with market research, deterministic risk gates, audit logs, and approval queues. It emphasizes safety, broker neutrality, and deterministic execution for paper trading and strategy validation.

## Key Features
- Local-first paper trading with deterministic simulation and backtesting
- Broker-neutral architecture supporting 14+ model providers and custom endpoints
- Hard-coded risk gates and tamper-evident audit logs for safety and accountability
- Approval queues and kill-switch mechanisms for live trading opt-in
- Persistent trading memory via Markdown journals for continuous learning
