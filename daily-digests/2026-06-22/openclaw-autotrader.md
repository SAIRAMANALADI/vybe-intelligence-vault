## Summary
A 30-day live trading challenge where a 10,000 HKD automated trading system publicly tracks and executes trades in U.S. and HK stocks, sharing daily decisions, recaps, and learning logs to demonstrate real-world performance beyond backtesting.

## Key Features
- 30-day live trading challenge with real capital (10,000 HKD)
- Publicly documented decisions, recaps, and turning points
- Daily updates on holdings, PnL, and strategy adjustments
- Open learning logs converting trades and misses into reusable lessons
- Strict guardrails: no leverage, no shorting, whitelist-only, and daily loss limits

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a transparent, real-world example of automated trading execution and decision-making, valuable for AI engineers building trading agents or RAG systems focused on financial strategies.
