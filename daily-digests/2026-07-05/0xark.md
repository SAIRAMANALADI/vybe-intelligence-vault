## Summary
0xARK is an on-chain trading card game (TCG) built on Solana, integrating ZK proofs for fair play, x402 micropayments for in-game actions, and AI agents for strategy advice and autonomous gameplay. It features NFT mechanics like burn, evolve, and imprint, with a complete in-game economy and multiplayer support.

## Key Features
- On-chain TCG with 60 unique NFT cards and 6 legendary cards
- ZK proof-based fair play with on-chain Groth16 verification
- x402 micropayments for in-game actions (Coinbase spec compliant)
- AI strategy advisor and autonomous move delegation via Claude Haiku 4.5
- NFT mechanics: burn, evolve, imprint, and trade floor marketplace
