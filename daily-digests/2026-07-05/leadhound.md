## Summary
LeadHound automates UK business prospecting by discovering leads via Google Maps and Companies House, scoring them with an LLM, and exporting actionable contact lists to Google Sheets with a built-in dashboard for outreach management.

## Key Features
- Automated discovery of UK businesses via Google Maps and Companies House
- LLM-powered scoring (0-100) with plain-English reasoning for each lead
- Website enrichment and status analysis (e.g., outdated, weak conversion)
- Integration with Google Sheets for real-time lead tracking and CRM
- One-click pipeline execution with live log streaming and API quota tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LeadHound streamlines the tedious process of lead discovery and qualification, enabling AI engineers to focus on building RAG systems rather than manual prospecting.
