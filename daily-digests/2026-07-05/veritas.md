## Summary
VERITAS is an AI forensic auditor that autonomously examines 100% of a company's financial documents to detect fraud. It plans investigations, retrieves evidence twice per lead using VultronRetriever, weighs findings with Qwen on Vultr Serverless Inference, and files accusations only when an independent NVIDIA Nemotron panel agrees, ensuring high accuracy and accountability.

## Key Features
- Autonomous forensic examination of 100% of financial documents with zero human sampling
- Two-tier retrieval system (VultronRetriever Core and Prime) for precision and recall
- Independent verification by NVIDIA Nemotron panel before filing any accusation
- Real-time streaming verdicts with cited evidence and confidence scoring
- Built entirely on Vultr Serverless Inference for cost-efficient, scalable operations
