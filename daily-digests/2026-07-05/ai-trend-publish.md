## Summary
A full-stack AI content creation platform integrating WeChat Mini Program, FastAPI backend, and React frontend. It automates trending topic collection, AI-generated content (articles, videos, digital humans), and multi-platform publishing across WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and others.

## Key Features
- Automated trending topic collection from multiple sources (V2EX, Weibo, Zhihu, etc.)
- AI-powered content generation for articles, videos, and digital humans using LLMs and TTS
- Multi-platform publishing support (WeChat, Xiaohongshu, with others in development)
- Embedded media downloader (cobalt) for 21+ platforms including YouTube, Bilibili, and TikTok
- Unified backend (FastAPI) with Celery for asynchronous task processing
