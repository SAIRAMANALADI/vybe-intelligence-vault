## Summary
Agency Orchestrator is a zero-code framework that enables multi-AI role collaboration to automate complex workflows, generating complete solutions from a single prompt. It leverages a library of 216+ pre-defined expert roles and supports 10 LLM providers, including 7 free options, to orchestrate tasks via YAML-based workflows or a web-based Studio interface.

## Key Features
- Zero-code YAML workflows for defining multi-step AI collaborations with automatic DAG-based parallel execution
- Pre-built library of 216+ expert roles (e.g., product managers, engineers, financial analysts) with Chinese and English support
- Web-based Studio and desktop app for visual workflow design, role selection, and real-time monitoring
- Supports 10 LLM providers (7 free options like Claude Code, Gemini CLI, and Ollama) with automatic API key detection
- AI-powered workflow composition from natural language prompts, generating and executing complete workflows in minutes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Agency Orchestrator streamlines the integration of multiple specialized AI agents into cohesive workflows, drastically reducing the effort required to build and deploy complex RAG or AI systems by automating role selection, task decomposition, and parallel execution.
