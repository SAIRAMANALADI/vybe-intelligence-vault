## Summary
Montazh Agent is a Claude Code-based video-editing agent that automates the creation of reels, shorts, and social media videos from raw footage using AI-driven cutting, transcription, meme insertion, emoji accents, music generation, and quality gates. It operates through natural language commands and integrates with multiple MCP gateways for advanced features.

## Key Features
- Speech-aware cutting with word-level timestamp alignment and phoneme-aware padding
- Dynamic meme and emoji insertion based on script cues and emotional context
- AI-generated background music with energy-based ducking and voice synchronization
- Multi-format output generation (reels, shorts, YouTube versions) from raw footage
- Quality gates including self-evaluation, sanity checks, and OpenTimelineIO/EDL export for professional editing tools

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Montazh Agent streamlines video production by automating complex editing tasks through natural language, reducing manual effort and enabling rapid iteration for AI-driven content creation pipelines.
