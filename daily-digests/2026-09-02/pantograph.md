## Summary
Pantograph is an agentic CAD workspace that converts natural language descriptions into editable definition graphs for Rhino 8, enabling real-time collaboration between AI agents and human designers without baking geometry. It returns structured, mutable definitions rather than finalized 3D objects.

## Key Features
- Language-to-definition conversion via AI agent (Claude CLI) with narrow, validated mutations
- Real-time, bidirectional synchronization between AI-generated graphs and Rhino 8 geometry
- Editable canvas for manual graph manipulation with live recompilation and re-performance
- Local-first architecture with loopback TCP for privacy and offline capability
- Provenance tracking for every node, parameter, and edit with shared change logs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Pantograph redefines AI-assisted design by returning editable, inspectable definition graphs instead of finalized objects, enabling true collaboration between AI and human designers in parametric modeling workflows.
