## Summary
AI Worker Companion (AWC) is a monitoring tool for OpenCode AI tasks that provides visibility into the hidden work states of AI agents, such as idle, active, quiet, stuck, or failed. It offers a TUI sidebar, compact indicator, and a Tauri-based Desktop Companion for real-time health tracking and notifications.

## Key Features
- Real-time health state tracking for AI tasks (Idle, Active, Quiet, Stuck, Failed, Unknown)
- TUI sidebar and compact indicator for OpenCode integration
- Tauri-based Desktop Companion for macOS and Windows with system tray integration
- Provider/model retry detection and session lifecycle awareness
- Opt-in native notifications for health state transitions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AI Worker Companion provides critical visibility into AI agent states, enabling developers to quickly identify and resolve issues in long-running AI tasks without relying on manual inspection or ambiguous natural-language claims.
