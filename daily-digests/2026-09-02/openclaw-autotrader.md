## Summary
A 30-day live trading challenge where a 10,000 HKD automated trading system ('claw') publicly tracks and executes trades in U.S. and Hong Kong stocks, documenting decisions, recaps, and lessons learned in real-time.

## Key Features
- Real-time 30-day live trading challenge with public updates on decisions and performance
- Publicly documented learning logs and daily recaps for transparency and reproducibility
- Strict guardrails: no leverage, no shorting, bounded deployment, and whitelist-only trading
- Public dashboard tracking equity, PnL, and trading rules with real-time updates
- Detailed documentation of strategy rationale, exclusions, and risk management

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides a transparent, real-world example of automated trading execution and decision-making, valuable for AI engineers building RAG systems that require structured, documented, and publicly verifiable trading logic.
