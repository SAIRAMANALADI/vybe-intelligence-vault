## Summary
Downtime Arbiter is a NitroStack MCP server that facilitates factory downtime negotiation between Maintenance and Production teams using deterministic arbitration. It models risk trajectories and enforces context isolation to ensure auditable, safety-critical decisions.

## Key Features
- Context isolation for Maintenance and Production teams with role-specific data access
- P-F curve risk trajectory modeling for accurate failure prediction over time
- Deterministic arbitration engine with configurable safety thresholds
- Two-round negotiation protocol with enforced closure
- Auditable decision-making with validation suite for deterministic outcomes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a transparent, auditable framework for multi-agent negotiation in industrial settings, ensuring safety-critical decisions are explicit and repeatable rather than opaque or heuristic.
