## Summary
Setu is a Byzantine fault-tolerant settlement engine for prefunded agent payments, enabling fee-less, instant transactions without consensus, blocks, mining, or fees. It achieves finality in one network round trip and supports both committee-based and P2P trust models.

## Key Features
- One-round-trip finality (~4ms over real sockets) with no consensus or mining required
- Supports both committee-based (Plane 1) and P2P trustless (Plane 2) models
- Integrates with MCP for AI agents to hold wallets and make payments autonomously
- Zero fees, anti-spam via per-account rate limits, and Byzantine safety guarantees
- Live network deployed across four regions with ~200ms settlement times

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Setu enables AI agents to perform trustless, instant, and fee-less financial transactions, critical for autonomous economic interactions in RAG and agent-based systems.
