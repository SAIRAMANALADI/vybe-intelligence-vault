## Summary
Lians provides a cross-platform decision evidence and reconstruction layer for regulated AI environments, enabling compliance teams to maintain verifiable records of agent decisions, memory, policies, and outcomes across multiple AI runtimes while ensuring neutrality and auditability.

## Key Features
- Bitemporal recall for point-in-time knowledge reconstruction
- Tamper-evident audit chains with cryptographic receipts
- Information barriers and role-based access control (RLS)
- Crypto-shred erasure with audit survival
- Multi-runtime neutrality (Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Lians ensures regulated AI systems can maintain defensible, portable evidence of agent decisions and memory across providers, critical for compliance, risk management, and legal defensibility in high-stakes environments.
