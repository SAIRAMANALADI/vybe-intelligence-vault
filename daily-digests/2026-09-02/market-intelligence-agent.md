## Summary
An AI-powered market intelligence workflow that automates the collection, filtering, summarization, and ranking of market updates from RSS and web sources. It generates structured reports, slide drafts, and maintains a long-term knowledge base for market insights.

## Key Features
- Automated collection of market updates from RSS and web sources
- AI-driven summarization and ranking of articles using LLMs
- Generation of market briefs, analysis reports, and slide drafts
- Long-term knowledge base for processed articles and market insights
- Web UI for reviewing, filtering, and manual organization of data
