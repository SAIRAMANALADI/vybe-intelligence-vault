## Summary
JobLens is an AI-powered career assistant platform that helps job seekers optimize resumes, prepare for interviews, discover matching jobs, and build personalized career roadmaps using LangGraph-based agents, Next.js, and PostgreSQL with pgvector.

## Key Features
- Modular LangGraph-based AI agents for resume optimization, career advising, interview prep, job discovery, and learning roadmaps
- PostgreSQL with pgvector for semantic job matching and embeddings
- Multi-LLM failover support (Gemini, OpenAI, Mistral, Ollama)
- Automated daily job ingestion and vector embedding updates
- Interactive Next.js frontend with TailwindCSS for a modern user experience

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
JobLens provides a comprehensive, agentic framework for building AI-powered career assistance tools, making it essential for developers focused on RAG-based job matching and personalized career guidance systems.
