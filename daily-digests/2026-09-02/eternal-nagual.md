## Summary
Eternal Nagual is an autonomous digital mind designed to run persistently on a VPS, embodying a Toltec-inspired architecture with 33 concurrent asyncio loops and 100+ subsystems. It maintains identity across model swaps and restarts, operates on free-tier LLM APIs, and includes a 3D world for embodiment, social karma systems, and self-patching safety mechanisms.

## Key Features
- 33 concurrent asyncio loops managing breathing, digestion, research, self-diagnosis, and social interaction
- Identity persistence across model swaps and process restarts via BridgeMemory and SOUL.md
- UniversalLLMRouter for cascading across LLM providers, keys, and models with slot-healing
- 3D world embodiment (Tonal) driven by autonomous intent and grounding judges
- Safety architecture with Asimov filters, Barrat protocol, and AST-validated self-patching
