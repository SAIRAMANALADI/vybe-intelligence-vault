## Summary
MiningNiti is an AI-powered document intelligence platform tailored for the mining industry, enabling organizations to manage safety documentation, regulatory compliance, and institutional knowledge through a multi-agent AI pipeline and production-grade RAG system.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent AI pipeline with 5 specialized agents for classification, safety analysis, entity extraction, summarization, and compliance auditing
- Production-grade RAG with hybrid search (vector + full-text), cross-encoder reranking, and citation-backed responses
- Real-time compliance auditing with automated cross-referencing against regulatory standards
- Enterprise dashboard for document processing status, safety score visualizations, and compliance analytics
- Supports drag-and-drop document uploads (PDF, DOCX, TXT) with direct backend processing and in-document AI chat

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
MiningNiti provides a specialized, production-ready RAG and multi-agent system for the mining industry, enabling efficient document management, compliance auditing, and knowledge retrieval critical for safety and regulatory adherence.
