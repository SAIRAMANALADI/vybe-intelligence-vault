## Summary
A self-tuning token-burn governor for AI coding agents that dynamically prices, shapes, and settles AI requests to prevent budget overruns while maintaining correctness. It routes every request through three core segments: MINT (pricing), SHAPER (spawn control), and LEDGER (settlement and learning).

## Key Features
- Three-segment architecture (MINT, SHAPER, LEDGER) for dynamic token burn control
- Self-tuning policy brain (claude-fable-5) that refits routing up to twice per 5-hour window
- Deterministic burn forecasting with real-time posture warnings
- Fail-open design ensuring requests never fail due to governance
- Audit trails and cross-surface learning for adaptive budget management

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It prevents AI agents from burning through budgets unpredictably while ensuring correctness and efficiency, making it essential for cost-sensitive AI deployments.
