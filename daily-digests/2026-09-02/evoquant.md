## Summary
EvoQuant is an autonomous research agent designed for quantitative investment research, automating the full research loop from ingesting unstructured PDF reports to generating, ranking, and executing alpha factor experiments with rigorous IC-style evaluations. It bridges the gap between proprietary research reports and reproducible quant workflows.

## Key Features
- Self-evolving memory and knowledge graph for cross-cycle research retention
- Local PDF ingestion and structured knowledge extraction from unstructured reports
- Anchor-first ideation with ELO tournament ranking for idea prioritization
- End-to-end quant experiment runtime with IC/ICIR/RANKIC metrics
- Multi-agent team (plan, research, code, debug, analyze, write) with LangGraph orchestration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
EvoQuant automates the critical but manual process of turning proprietary quant research reports into reproducible experiments, enabling AI-driven alpha discovery at scale.
