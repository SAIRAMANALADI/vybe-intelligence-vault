## Summary
An enterprise-grade multi-agent platform enabling employees to complete knowledge Q&A, data analysis, and business execution via natural language. Features RBAC permissions, approval loops, full-stack degradation, and end-to-end observability for secure and auditable operations.

## Key Features
- Specialized sub-agents for knowledge Q&A, data analysis, and business execution with unified orchestration via LangGraph.
- Enterprise-grade governance including RBAC, approval loops, and Saga compensation for high-risk operations.
- Two-stage retrieval (Milvus + BGE Reranker) with three-tier degradation for robust RAG performance.
- Full-stack observability with OpenTelemetry tracing, Prometheus metrics, and audit logging.
- Modular design with mock-first tooling, explicit state machines, and parallel fan-out execution.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a production-ready orchestration framework for integrating RAG, data analysis, and business execution agents with enterprise governance, making it essential for building scalable and secure AI systems.
