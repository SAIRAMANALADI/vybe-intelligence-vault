## Summary
An AI-powered marketing strategy assistant that transforms marketing briefs into structured strategies, evaluates campaign logic, and generates executable proposals. It bridges raw marketing requirements with AI-driven workflows for strategists and consultants.

## Key Features
- Analyzes marketing briefs and requirements to extract key objectives and challenges
- Supports 10+ marketing methodologies (SWOT, PEST, 3C, etc.) for strategy development
- Evaluates campaign logic for consistency, clarity, and execution alignment
- Generates structured proposals and editable blueprints for presentations
- Cross-platform compatibility with GPTs, Claude Code, WorkBuddy, and Codex-based agents
