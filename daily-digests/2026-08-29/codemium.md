## Summary
Codemium provides a persistent, host-agnostic engineering layer for AI coding agents, enabling long-running software projects with durable project intelligence and deterministic engineering practices. It acts as a senior engineer's assistant, preserving context, correctness, and scope discipline across multiple coding-agent hosts.

## Key Features
- Host-agnostic project intelligence stored in `.codemium/` for durable context across agents
- Engineering depth control (FAST, NORMAL, DEEP, CRITICAL) with safety escalation rules
- Native adapters for OpenAI Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Cursor, and OpenCode
- Project Brain for storing decisions, constraints, interfaces, patterns, and known bugs
- Scope Guard and Impact & Test Intelligence to prevent unrelated edits and ensure verification

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Codemium ensures AI coding agents maintain project context and engineering rigor over long-running tasks, reducing errors and improving consistency in automated software development.
