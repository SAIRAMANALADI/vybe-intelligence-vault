## Summary
Paperdetective is an academic paper forensic tool designed to detect six types of signal anomalies in research papers, including data fabrication, image manipulation, citation fraud, retraction flags, internal inconsistencies, and cross-paper duplication. It generates structured reports with confidence scoring for further human review.

## Key Features
- Detects six types of academic misconduct signals with hard evidence and soft signals
- Supports multiple input formats (PDF, DOCX, images, text) and batch processing
- Provides deterministic algorithms with no model hallucination risks
- Generates structured JSON reports validated by Pydantic schemas
- Includes case-driven development with verified real-world fraud examples
