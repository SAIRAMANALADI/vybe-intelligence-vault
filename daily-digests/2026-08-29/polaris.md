## Summary
Polaris is a Python-based AI intelligence platform designed for portfolio analysis, risk assessment, and financial reporting. It enables replayable AI-assisted workflows with PostgreSQL persistence, hybrid RAG (Qdrant + Neo4j), and observability tools (Prometheus, Jaeger, Grafana).

## Key Features
- Runtime-native workflow execution with replay and inspection capabilities
- Hybrid RAG system combining PostgreSQL, Qdrant (vector), and Neo4j (graph) for grounded answers
- Deterministic backtesting with simulated or historical data providers
- Comprehensive observability stack (logs, metrics, traces) for monitoring and debugging
- Strongly typed internal contracts for safer refactoring and testability
