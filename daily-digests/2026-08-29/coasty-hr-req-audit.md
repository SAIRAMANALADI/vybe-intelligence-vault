## Summary
An AI agent that automates auditing job requisitions on live job boards by driving a real browser, extracting salary ranges and closing dates, and reporting results. It includes offline mock capabilities for testing without dependencies or costs.

- Real browser automation for dynamic job boards with no selectors or scraping rules
- Structured safety controls to prevent accidental spend (fail-closed defaults, dual consent)
- Self-documenting runs with provenance tracking and frame capture for transparency
