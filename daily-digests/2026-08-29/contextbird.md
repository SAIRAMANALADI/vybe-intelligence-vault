## Summary
ContextBird Pro is a privacy-first, locally-running AI assistant designed to record meetings, generate transcripts, extract action items, build semantic memory, and answer questions about work—all without relying on paid APIs or cloud services.

## Key Features
- Privacy-first local processing with zero cloud dependency
- Multi-modal meeting capture (file upload, Chrome extension, meeting bot)
- Automatic transcription with Whisper and structured LLM extraction (Mistral 7B)
- RAG-powered chat for grounded answers with citations
- Built-in task manager, analytics dashboard, and weekly reports

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ContextBird Pro provides a complete, locally-hosted pipeline for capturing, processing, and querying meeting data, making it essential for teams prioritizing privacy and cost-efficiency in RAG/AI workflows.
