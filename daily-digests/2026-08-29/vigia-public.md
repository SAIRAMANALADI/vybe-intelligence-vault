## Summary
VIGIASearch is an evidence-backed civic assistant that audits Indian road infrastructure using official government documents to answer citizen queries without hallucinations. It prioritizes accuracy over fabricated responses, even if it means admitting uncertainty.

## Key Features
- Strictly evidence-backed responses with citations from official documents
- Anti-hallucination layers including self-RAG, CRAG, and faithfulness scoring
- Multi-agent orchestration with LangGraph for complex query resolution
- Offline-first PWA with edge SQLite and IndexedDB for low-connectivity scenarios
- Deterministic routing and consistency checks for critical data like contact details

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates a robust, production-grade approach to building hallucination-resistant RAG systems by combining multi-agent orchestration, strict evidence retrieval, and offline capabilities for public-sector applications.
