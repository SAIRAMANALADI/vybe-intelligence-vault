## Summary
imprest is a non-custodial spend control and audit layer for AI agents, enabling programmable payment limits and approval workflows to prevent unauthorized transactions. It acts as a guardrail between agents and Ethereum wallets, enforcing policy-based spending caps, approval queues, and immutable audit trails.

## Key Features
- Policy-driven spend limits with per-asset and per-agent budgets
- Non-custodial dedicated wallet per agent (blast-radius mitigation)
- Human approval workflow for anomalous or high-risk payments
- Immutable audit logging of all payment attempts and approvals
- Support for x402 pay-per-request APIs with policy enforcement

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical security layer for AI agents interacting with financial systems, preventing irreversible losses from prompt injection or tool misuse by enforcing hard spending limits and approval workflows.
