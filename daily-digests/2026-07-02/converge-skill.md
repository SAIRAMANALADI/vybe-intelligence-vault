## Summary
Converge is a dual-agent iterative convergence framework designed for AI-generated outputs, enabling adversarial review cycles between independent agents to drive quality improvement until a product meets executable standards or termination conditions.

## Key Features
- Dual-agent adversarial review loop (Reviewer vs. Executor) for iterative quality convergence
- Framework-agnostic design with abstract capability layers (Spawn/Continue/Identify)
- Deterministic budget gate for spawn control and cost management
- Oscillation detection to prevent infinite loops or divergent behavior
- Modular and extensible architecture with compiled anti-pattern registries
