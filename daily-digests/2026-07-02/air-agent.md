## Summary
AIR-Agent is an autonomous AI research assistant that autonomously collects, summarizes, and analyzes AI research papers and news from multiple sources. It builds a hybrid vector and graph knowledge base for advanced RAG retrieval, delivers insights via a Discord bot and multi-platform notifications, and supports deep research workflows with iterative agentic reasoning.

## Key Features
- Multi-source data collection (arXiv, RSS, HN, Reddit, GitHub, X/Twitter)
- Hybrid retrieval combining vector search (FAISS/HNSW) and BM25 with reranking
- Graph-based knowledge base with concept and citation networks for global reasoning
- Discord bot with slash commands for Q&A, deep research, and literature review
- Personalized recommendations and trend forecasting via LSTM and reward models

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AIR-Agent provides a comprehensive, production-ready framework for building autonomous AI research assistants with advanced RAG capabilities, hybrid retrieval, and multi-agent workflows, making it essential for developers building next-generation knowledge systems.
