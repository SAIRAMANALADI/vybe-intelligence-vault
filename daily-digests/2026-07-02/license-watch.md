## Summary
A sophisticated automated pipeline for detecting OSS license violations and namesquatting across 15+ platforms, designed for solo developers. It combines multi-layered detection, semantic analysis, and legal compliance automation to flag and preserve evidence of violations.

## Key Features
- 8-layer automated pipeline for license violation detection
- Multi-platform monitoring (GitHub, npm, PyPI, Docker Hub, etc.)
- Multi-model semantic analysis (Claude, GPT-5, Gemini) for violation confirmation
- Triple-anchor evidence chain (FreeTSA, Sigstore, OpenTimestamps, Bitcoin)
- Legal automation (pre-drafted DMCA, GitHub issue integration)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical automated safeguard for solo developers to detect and respond to OSS license violations and namesquatting across multiple platforms, reducing legal risk and manual overhead.
