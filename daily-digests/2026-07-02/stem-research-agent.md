## Summary
A Python-based AI agent designed to automate STEM research tasks by retrieving scholarly papers, summarizing findings, generating citations, and creating visualizations. It supports multiple AI providers (Gemini or Claude) and offers both agentic and fixed-pipeline modes for flexibility.

## Key Features
- Multi-database paper retrieval (OpenAlex, arXiv, PubMed, CrossRef) with deduplication
- AI-powered summarization, synthesis, and citation formatting (6 styles)
- Verbatim quote extraction with real page numbers from open-access PDFs
- Quantitative data extraction and chart generation for research reports
- Local web interface and CLI for user-friendly interaction
