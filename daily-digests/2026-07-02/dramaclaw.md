## Summary
DramaClaw is an open-source, industrialized drama-production pipeline that automates the entire process from manuscript to finished short film. It enables creators to generate scripts, storyboards, voice-overs, and videos using AI while maintaining creative control and infrastructure ownership.

## Key Features
- End-to-end drama production from manuscript to video output
- Modular pipeline with decomposable steps for customization
- AI assistant (Xia Director) for project management and task auditing
- Dual-mode workflow (main pipeline + infinite canvas exploration)
- Model-neutral architecture supporting BYO AI models

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DramaClaw democratizes AI-driven video production by providing an open-source, self-hostable pipeline that reduces reliance on opaque cloud services and empowers creators with full control over their creative process.
