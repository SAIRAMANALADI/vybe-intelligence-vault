## Summary
DeepPPT is an end-to-end AI-powered PPT generation system that automates deep research, structured analysis, narrative construction, and visual identity extraction to produce native, editable PPTX files from a single topic or source file.

## Key Features
- 7-step independent deep research workflow with multi-AI browser automation for topic-based PPT generation
- Unified Dashboard for tracking project status, artifacts, quality reports, and execution traces
- Multi-stage quality gates (confirmation, research depth, asset completeness, rendered visuals, harness, and end-to-end checks)
- Image source routing and dual-track image generation (AI for visual pages, web for informational pages) to reduce copyright risks
- Native PPTX export with post-export structural validation, layout checks, and visual reviews

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DeepPPT streamlines the creation of high-quality, research-backed presentations by automating complex workflows, reducing manual effort and ensuring consistency for AI engineering teams.
