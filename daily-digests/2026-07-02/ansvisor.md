## Summary
Ansvisor is an open-source AI Visibility Platform designed to help brands track, analyze, and optimize their presence in AI-generated answers across multiple platforms like ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, and Microsoft Copilot. It provides real-time insights into brand citations, competitor comparisons, and content optimization opportunities.

## Key Features
- Real-time visibility scores across major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, etc.) with weekly trend analysis
- AI Traffic Analytics tracking visits from AI answer engines with platform breakdown and country segmentation
- Competitor comparison and gap analysis to identify visibility opportunities
- Content Optimization with AI-generated briefs, one-click webhook delivery to CMS, and multi-language support (13 languages, 18 regions)
- Self-hosted or cloud deployment with all features unlocked in both modes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Ansvisor provides critical insights into how AI platforms cite and rank brands, enabling RAG builders to optimize content for AI-driven search and improve visibility in AI-generated answers.
