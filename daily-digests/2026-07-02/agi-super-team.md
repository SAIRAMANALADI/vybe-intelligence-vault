## Summary
AGI Super Team is a plug-and-play AI agent framework that deploys a virtual C-Suite of 14 specialized agents, each modeled after legendary minds like Elon Musk, Warren Buffett, and Jim Simons. It provides 1,639 pre-built skills, 31 thinking frameworks, and 30 workflows to enable rapid deployment of AI-native teams for quant trading, content creation, and business operations.

## Key Features
- Deploys 14 specialized AI agents with distinct personalities and decision-making styles
- Includes 1,639 pre-built skills across 15+ categories (trading, content, devops, etc.)
- Supports one-click deployment of starter kits (solo-founder, quant-trader, content-creator)
- Integrates 31 thinking frameworks and 30 workflows for structured agent operations
- Modular design with customizable SOUL.md files for each agent
