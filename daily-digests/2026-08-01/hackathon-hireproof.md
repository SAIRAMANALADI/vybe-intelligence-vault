## Summary
HireProof is a proof-backed AI agent designed to detect employment fraud in job posts, recruiter messages, screenshots, and application links. It provides transparent, evidence-based safety verdicts (Safe, Caution, High-Risk) with explainable reasoning to help job seekers and communities avoid scams.

## Key Features
- Transparent, explainable safety verdicts with visible red flags, green flags, and evidence cards
- Multi-surface deployment (web, API, CLI, MCP, ChatSDK, extensions, workflows)
- Demo and live evidence modes with configurable provider credentials
- OCR integration for screenshot analysis (Google Vision, Tesseract fallback)
- Production-hardened with Docker, health checks, and smoke verification scripts

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
HireProof provides a critical trust layer for job seekers and AI systems by verifying employment fraud with transparent evidence, reducing risks of personal data exposure and financial loss.
