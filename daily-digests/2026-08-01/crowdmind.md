## Summary
Crowdmind is a local-first desktop application for creating AI-simulated personas and testing marketing or product stimuli against them to gather qualitative feedback. It operates entirely offline with optional LLM integration, storing all data in a local SQLite database.

## Key Features
- Create and manage AI-simulated personas with deterministic avatars and version history
- Run single-stimulus or multi-stage funnel tests with individual or focus group modes
- Multi-provider LLM support with automatic retry and offline/local provider option
- Swarm view for sentiment analysis and interactive follow-up questions
- Exportable narrative reports (PDF, Markdown, JSON) and persona templates for sharing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables rapid, cost-effective qualitative feedback for AI-driven product and marketing decisions without relying on external research services.
