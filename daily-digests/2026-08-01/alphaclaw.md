## Summary
AlphaClaw is a deployment harness and management platform for OpenClaw, designed to simplify setup, monitoring, and maintenance of AI agents. It provides a browser-based UI for configuration, observability, and self-healing operations, enabling zero-to-production deployments in minutes.

## Key Features
- Browser-based setup UI with guided onboarding for OpenClaw deployments
- Self-healing watchdog with crash detection, auto-repair, and notifications
- Multi-agent management with Git-backed version control and rollback
- Integrated observability dashboard for real-time monitoring and debugging
- Zero-lock-in architecture allowing seamless ejection from AlphaClaw

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AlphaClaw streamlines the deployment and maintenance of AI agents by providing a self-healing, browser-based management layer that reduces operational overhead and accelerates time-to-production.
