## Summary
HimiTrace™ Aqua is an AI-native SCADA platform designed for industrial wastewater treatment plants, optimizing aeration and chemical dosing to reduce energy costs and ensure environmental compliance. It integrates predictive AI models (LSTM/GRU), adaptive control systems (Lyapunov ISS), and a Web3 audit ledger on Polygon Mainnet for immutable compliance tracking.

## Key Features
- AI-powered load-shock forecasting (LSTM/GRU) for 2-6 hour ahead predictions
- Adaptive Lyapunov ISS controller for dynamic aeration and chemical dosing
- Web3 immutable audit ledger on Polygon Mainnet for regulatory compliance
- MCP Server for secure AI agent interactions and natural language queries
- Digital Product Passport (DPP) extension for supply chain carbon tracking
