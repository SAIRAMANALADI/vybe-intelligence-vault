## Summary
LazyLLM is a low-code development platform for building multi-agent LLM applications with iterative optimization workflows. It enables rapid prototyping, deployment, and continuous improvement of AI systems through a unified interface for models, tools, and workflows.

## Key Features
- Low-code assembly of multi-agent AI applications with modular workflows
- One-click deployment for POC and production phases with cross-platform compatibility
- Unified interface for switching between online and local models, inference frameworks, and databases
- Automated fine-tuning with framework and strategy selection based on use case
- Built-in RAG pipelines with retrievers, rerankers, and prompt management

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LazyLLM streamlines the development and deployment of multi-agent RAG systems, reducing engineering overhead while enabling rapid iteration and scalability for production-grade AI applications.
