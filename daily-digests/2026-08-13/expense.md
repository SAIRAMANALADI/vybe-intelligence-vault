## Summary
Expense is a frictionless receipt tracker that automates expense logging via email, AI-assisted data entry, and mileage tracking. It organizes expenses into reports, supports multi-user accounts, and provides MCP-compatible AI assistants for hands-free expense management.

## Key Features
- Receipt tracking via email forwarding with automatic parsing and categorization
- AI-powered data entry and expense logging through MCP clients
- Mileage tracking with OSRM routing and IRS rate pricing
- Multi-user account management with invite-based sharing and isolation
- PDF and ZIP export capabilities with embedded receipt images

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines expense management for AI stack builders by automating receipt processing and providing AI-driven expense tracking through MCP, reducing manual data entry overhead.
