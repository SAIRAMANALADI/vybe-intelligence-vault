## Summary
An AI agent that automates competitive retail price and stock monitoring by driving a real browser to interact with storefronts, read prices, and generate reports without manual scripting or selectors. It includes an offline mock for zero-cost testing and supports live execution via the Coasty API.

## Key Features
- Zero-dependency execution with bundled offline mock for $0 cost
- Real browser interaction with vision model-driven decision making
- Survives storefront redesigns by relying on natural language prompts instead of selectors
- Structured safety controls to prevent accidental spend (fail-closed defaults, dual consent, cost caps)
- Self-documenting with provenance tracking and frame-by-frame hashing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates how AI agents can automate high-frequency manual tasks like retail price monitoring without brittle scraping rules, reducing maintenance overhead and improving data freshness for RAG systems.
