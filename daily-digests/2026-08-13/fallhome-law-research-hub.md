## Summary
A desktop application for legal professionals that simplifies case management, conflict tracking, and offline legal research through a browser-based interface. All data is stored locally in a single file for privacy and security.

## Key Features
- Local-first data storage in a single file for privacy and portability
- Offline-capable legal research with keyword search in documents
- Built-in conflict tracking to prevent ethical violations
- Browser-based interface for ease of use
- Automatic data saving and manual backup support
