## Summary
A remote MCP server providing French real-estate legal calculations via Streamable HTTP, enabling AI agents to generate regulatory-grade answers for rent revisions, service charge reconciliations, and compliant rent receipts with full legal basis and warnings.

## Key Features
- Provides legally compliant French real-estate calculations (e.g., IRL rent revision, service charge reconciliation)
- Includes legal basis and warnings for every calculation (GDPR-friendly, stateless)
- Offers compliant rent receipts and revision notification letters (PDF via REST)
- Free tier available (100 units/month) with scalable paid plans
- Integrates with AI agents via MCP (Model Context Protocol) or REST
