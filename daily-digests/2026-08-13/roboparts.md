## Summary
RoboParts is a modular bionic robotics ecosystem platform that standardizes the selection and design of robot components, offering a comprehensive database of 688+ actuators, sensors, chips, protocols, and AI models with compatibility matrices and compatibility checks.

## Key Features
- Standardized modular component library with 688+ entries across 10 categories (actuators, sensors, chips, protocols, etc.)
- Compatibility matrix for electrical, mechanical, protocol, and software integration checks
- Smart selection engine with five-dimensional scoring (torque, speed, precision, weight, cost) and multi-result comparison
- Structured supply chain data with tier classification and domestic substitution support
- End-to-end encrypted user data integration for BOM generation and procurement lists

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a centralized, standardized database for robotics components and AI models, enabling faster prototyping and deployment of RAG systems in robotics applications.
