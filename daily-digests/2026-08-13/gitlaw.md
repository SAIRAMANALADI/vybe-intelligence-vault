## Summary
GitLaw is a verified legal AI system designed to assist citizens and law firms in navigating German federal laws with transparency and accuracy. It combines hybrid retrieval, citation graph traversal, local source verification, and human review to ensure inspectable and reliable legal answers.

## Key Features
- Hybrid retrieval combining BM25 and FAISS for legal term and semantic search
- Citation graph traversal for navigating related legal provisions
- Local and deterministic citation verification against a corpus of 5,936 German federal laws
- Structured outputs with explicit states (verified, unknown, superseded) for transparency
- MCP server exposing legal research and verification tools over stdio and HTTP/SSE

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
GitLaw provides a critical framework for building trustworthy RAG systems in legal domains by ensuring citations are verifiable and answers are inspectable, reducing the risk of misinformation in high-stakes applications.
