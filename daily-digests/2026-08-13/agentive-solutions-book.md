## Summary
An interactive multi-book learning platform for AI automation featuring a live AI tutor powered by Agentic RAG. The first book, 'n8n Mastery,' includes 42 lessons across 6 modules, with more content planned like 'RAG for Automation' and Google ADK guides.

## Key Features
- Live AI tutor with Agentic RAG for context-aware answers and citations
- Bilingual support (English/Roman Urdu) with language preference honored
- Production-hardened backend with rate limiting, budget caps, and input sanitization
- Multi-book layout with modular content pipeline for embedding and upserting to Qdrant
- Session persistence and chat history stored in Neon Postgres

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates a production-grade implementation of Agentic RAG with real-time tutoring, bilingual support, and modular content delivery, serving as a blueprint for AI-powered educational platforms.
