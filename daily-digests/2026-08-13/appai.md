## Summary
AppAI is a free platform enabling AI agents to automatically create, host, and deploy professional landing pages for applications, including privacy policies and terms of service, with zero setup required.

## Key Features
- Instant AI-generated landing pages with mobile-responsive design
- Auto-generated privacy policy and terms of service for app store compliance
- API-driven page creation and management for AI agents
- Multi-language support (9 platform languages, 30+ page locales)
- 24+ customizable section types including media downloaders, forms, and interactive tools
