## Summary
BossAI 情报晨报 Skill is an agent-installable tool that automates the collection, deduplication, and triage of public intelligence sources, transforming hours of daily information into structured, three-tiered briefs (must-read, quick-read, skip) for AI agents like OpenClaw or Hermes.

## Key Features
- Automated collection from 20+ public sources including Hacker News, ArXiv, and Reddit
- Deterministic and optional LLM-based triage for must-read, quick-read, and skip categorization
- Structured output in Markdown, JSON, and manifest formats for agent consumption
- Configurable fallback URLs for RSS feeds and proxy support
- Webhook-based notifications and push to external platforms
