## Summary
StandIn enables AI agents to join Microsoft Teams calls as real participants, handling media, avatar rendering, and real-time dialogue while integrating with multiple AI backends. It acts as a hosted bridge that connects your AI agent to Teams calls without requiring Microsoft Graph or Teams SDK integration.

## Key Features
- Seamless integration of AI agents into Microsoft Teams calls as real participants
- Real-time perception of camera and screen-share with per-participant attribution
- Lip-synced avatar rendering for natural appearance in calls
- Support for seven open-source agent backends (OpenClaw, Hermes, LiveKit, etc.)
- Recording-consent gating and closed-by-default allowlist for governance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
StandIn eliminates the complexity of integrating AI agents with Microsoft Teams by providing a hosted bridge that handles media, avatar rendering, and real-time dialogue, enabling seamless deployment of AI agents in enterprise communication platforms.
