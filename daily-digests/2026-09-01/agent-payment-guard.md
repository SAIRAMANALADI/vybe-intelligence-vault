## Summary
SignGate Agentic Commerce Preflight is a policy decision layer for autonomous AI agents that evaluates economically consequential actions like payments, API purchases, and signing requests. It provides pre-payment governance, risk assessment, and audit trails to ensure compliance with merchant policies, mandates, and risk signals before transactions are executed.

## Key Features
- Policy-based decision engine for AI agent transactions with ALLOW/REVIEW/BLOCK outcomes
- Dynamic budget controls, allowlists/blocklists, and replay protection for payments
- Agent-aware buyer identity preflight for role-based policy enforcement
- Integration with BlockchainSecurity for risk scoring and address classification
- Hosted API with SDKs (JavaScript/Python) and developer policy kits for customization

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides critical pre-payment governance and risk mitigation for autonomous AI agents, ensuring safe and compliant economic interactions in AI-driven commerce ecosystems.
