## Summary
A RAG-powered AI assistant tailored for Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications students, combining intent routing, safety guardrails, and knowledge retrieval to provide accurate, context-aware responses to academic, campus life, and administrative queries.

## Key Features
- Multi-scenario intent routing (academic, campus life, admin, general chat)
- Four-layer safety guardrails to prevent hallucinations and filter harmful content
- Scalable RAG pipeline with FAISS for efficient document retrieval
- Incremental indexing and document fingerprinting for low-latency updates
- Comprehensive evaluation with 92.9% accuracy on hand-curated test queries
