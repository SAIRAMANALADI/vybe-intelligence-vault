## Summary
FirstDay AI is a digital trial and job capability assessment product for the AI era, simulating real-world work scenarios to evaluate candidates' decision-making, AI collaboration, and evidence-based reasoning through replayable behavioral evidence.

## Key Features
- Simulates real-world work environments with multi-role collaboration and AI assistance
- Generates replayable, evidence-based capability reports with traceable decision chains
- Evaluates AI co-pilot usage, verification, and responsibility in decision-making
- Supports dynamic scenario adjustments while maintaining standardized assessment criteria
- Provides layered human-AI collaboration architecture for stable yet realistic experiences

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
