## Summary
Koubo Editing Skill is an AI-assisted tool for editing raw, error-prone spoken video content. It uses AI to identify and suggest edits for speech errors, repetitions, and pauses, followed by a human review and refinement process to produce a polished MP4 video, SRT subtitles, and word-level timestamp JSON.

## Key Features
- AI pre-selection of speech errors, repetitions, and pauses for efficient editing
- Local web-based review interface for human verification and manual adjustments
- Direct export of polished MP4, SRT subtitles, and word-level timestamp JSON
- Real-time subtitle preview with immediate synchronization for edits
- Support for FCPXML export for further professional editing if needed

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges the gap between raw AI transcription and polished video output by enabling human oversight, ensuring high-quality speech editing for RAG pipelines that rely on accurate video content.
