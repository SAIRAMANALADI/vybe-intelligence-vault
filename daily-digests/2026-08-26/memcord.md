## Summary
Memcord is a privacy-first, self-hosted MCP server designed to organize, summarize, and search chat history locally, ensuring full data control and seamless integration with AI tools like Claude and VSCode.

## Key Features
- Privacy-first local storage with zero cloud dependencies
- Automatic summarization and intelligent merging of conversations
- Full-text search and natural language querying across chat history
- Pluggable summarizer backends (nltk, sumy, semantic, transformers)
- Cross-IDE compatibility with auto-configuration for Claude, VSCode, and Antigravity

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Memcord enables RAG builders to maintain a secure, searchable, and organized knowledge base of AI interactions, reducing context loss and improving retrieval efficiency for long-term memory.
