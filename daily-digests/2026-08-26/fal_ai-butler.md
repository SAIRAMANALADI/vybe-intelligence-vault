## Summary
A Claude Code plugin that automates ad campaign video production by generating a professional workflow.json for fal.ai from a brief product description. It orchestrates a multi-agent pipeline to plan, script, and validate video campaigns without executing models, ensuring cost transparency and deterministic revisions.

## Key Features
- Generates fal.ai-compatible workflow.json from a 2-sentence product description
- Orchestrates a 9-step agent pipeline (director, animator, editor, etc.) for end-to-end video planning
- Ensures character consistency across scenes via deterministic seed and keyframe chaining
- Provides cost transparency and revision options before any production begins
- Validates workflow.json against fal.ai's schema to prevent import errors

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the creation of structured, platform-ready video workflows for AI-driven ad campaigns, reducing manual effort and ensuring consistency in multi-scene productions.
