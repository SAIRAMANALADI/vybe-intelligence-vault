## Summary
A Claude Code skill that learns a user's Facebook writing style, generates a 14-day content calendar, and automates posting to multiple social platforms (FB, IG, Threads, X) while optimizing for viral reach. Validated by creator's own mega-viral posts (72K+ reach, 95% non-follower engagement).

## Key Features
- Style learning: Analyzes user's past posts to extract writing patterns, emojis, and tone for personalized content generation
- 14-day content calendar: Automatically generates and schedules posts based on 7 viral-tested formulas (F1-F7) tailored to user goals
- Multi-platform automation: Posts to Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and X with platform-specific optimizations
- Performance tracking: Monitors engagement metrics (reach, likes, comments, shares) and auto-optimizes future content
- Viral formula validation: Includes 40+ case studies and 42 rules (R1-R42) derived from real viral posts under 5K followers
