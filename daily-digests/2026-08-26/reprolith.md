## Summary
Reprolith is an auto-reproduction and certification engine for computational biomedical modeling literature. It rebuilds models from papers, re-runs them, and verifies whether the outputs match the original figures and tables, providing a reproducibility certificate for each result.

## Key Features
- Automated model reconstruction from papers using provided dossiers
- Deterministic reproducibility checks against original results with tolerance-based validation
- Per-result certificates (reproduced, partially, or not) with explicit reasons for failure
- Honest assumption tracking and disclosure of missing parameters or conditions
- MCP server integration for AI agent workflows to perform on-demand reproducibility checks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Reprolith ensures the reliability of computational biomedical models by verifying their reproducibility, reducing the risk of flawed or non-replicable research being used in AI-driven healthcare applications.
