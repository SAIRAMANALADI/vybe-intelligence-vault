## Summary
trajecktory is an AI-driven, local job-search pipeline with a web dashboard that automates scanning, evaluating, tailoring, tracking, and following up on job applications. It integrates with Claude Code for AI-powered evaluations and provides a structured workflow for managing the entire job hunt process.

## Key Features
- AI-powered job posting evaluation with structured scoring (A-F) across 10 dimensions
- Automated scanning of 45+ companies and 19 job boards via Playwright and ATS APIs
- Local web dashboard for managing applications, recruiters, and follow-ups
- Tailored resume and cover letter generation per role using AI
- Recruiter CRM and LinkedIn engagement tracking with AI-drafted messages

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the job search process with AI-driven insights and automation, reducing manual effort while improving application quality and tracking.
