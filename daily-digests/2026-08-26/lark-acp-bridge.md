## Summary
lark-acp-bridge is a Windows desktop application that acts as a bridge between Lark or Feishu bots and AI agents, enabling interactive chat experiences with tools like Claude Code, Kiro CLI, or Copilot directly within Lark chats.

## Key Features
- Connects Lark/Feishu bots to external AI agents (Claude, Copilot, etc.)
- Supports interactive cards and tool permissions for enhanced UX
- Maintains active sessions and resumes conversations after disconnections
- Local session data storage with no external logging of message history
- Low memory footprint even under high-traffic conditions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables seamless integration of Lark/Feishu bots with AI agents, expanding the capabilities of enterprise chat platforms for developers and end-users.
