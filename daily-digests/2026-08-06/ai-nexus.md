## Summary
Free2AITools is an open-source AI registry that aggregates, ranks, and compares AI models, datasets, papers, tools, and benchmarks from multiple sources. It provides a daily-updated catalog scored by the Free2AITools Nexus Index (FNI), enabling developers to discover and evaluate AI resources programmatically.

## Key Features
- Cross-source catalog of AI models, datasets, papers, tools, and benchmarks from HuggingFace, GitHub, ArXiv, and more
- FNI Ranking system with 5-factor composite scores (Semantic, Authority, Popularity, Recency, Quality)
- Hardware-aware filtering for models based on VRAM, license, and task categories
- MCP server integration for seamless use in AI development environments like Claude and Cursor
- Daily automated updates and open Parquet exports for offline analysis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a centralized, ranked, and up-to-date catalog of AI resources essential for RAG builders to discover and evaluate models and datasets efficiently.
