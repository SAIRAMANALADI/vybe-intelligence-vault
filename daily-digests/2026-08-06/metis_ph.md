## Summary
Metis_PH is a domain-specific edition of Metis, a research companion built for Claude that integrates a researcher's library, notes, and memory locally. It automates cross-pollination across papers, meetings, and ideas, and provides a self-improving AI assistant tailored for public health and epidemiology workflows.

## Key Features
- Domain-specific knowledge layer for public health and epidemiology pre-loaded
- Automated cross-pollination of research artifacts (papers, meetings, ideas, notes)
- 30+ specialist skills (Librarian, Methods Coach, Epidemiologist, etc.) for task routing
- Self-review and improvement loop that drafts behavioral enhancements for user approval
- Local-first architecture with all data and embeddings staying on the user's machine

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Metis_PH provides a turnkey, domain-aware research assistant that eliminates manual context re-explanation and automates knowledge synthesis, making it indispensable for RAG builders seeking to integrate personalized, self-improving agents into research workflows.
