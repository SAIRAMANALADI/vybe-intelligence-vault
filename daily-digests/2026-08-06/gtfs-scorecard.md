## Summary
GTFS Scorecard evaluates the quality of GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) feeds for transit agencies, providing a plain-language grade, top fixes, and trend history. It integrates with the MobilityData validator to assess correctness, freshness, and rider experience, supporting CI gating and public dashboards.

## Key Features
- Automated GTFS feed validation and scoring using MobilityData validator
- Plain-language grade and top 3 actionable fixes for transit agencies
- CI integration via GitHub Action to gate feed quality in pipelines
- Public dashboard (gtfsscorecard.org) with national and agency-specific views
- Trend history, trend alerts, and embeddable badges for agencies

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides automated, standardized GTFS feed quality assessment essential for maintaining reliable transit data pipelines and improving rider experience.
