## Summary
Flow-Next is an AI agent orchestration plugin that transforms rough AI coding agent outputs into structured, reviewable, and verifiable workflows. It enforces a disciplined engineering process with durable specs, dependency-ordered tasks, adversarial cross-model reviews, and receipts, all stored locally in the repository under `.flow/`.

## Key Features
- Durable specs stored in `.flow/specs/` with source-tagged acceptance criteria
- Dependency-ordered task planning with fresh-context worker subagents
- Adversarial cross-model review gates at every handover (plan, implementation, PR)
- Receipts and evidence tracking for every task and PR, ensuring verifiable outcomes
- Multi-platform support (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok Build, Factory Droid) with zero external dependencies

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Flow-Next provides the missing scaffolding for reliable AI-driven engineering by enforcing structured workflows, reducing drift, and ensuring verifiable outcomes, making it essential for teams scaling AI agents into production-grade systems.
