## Summary
Job Boardwalk is a local AI job-search assistant that provides agents with a visible browser for recruiting platform research while maintaining a durable workspace for preserving findings, revisiting sources, and comparing job opportunities against user-defined goals. It separates live browser sessions from persistent data storage, ensuring user control over sensitive actions like login and applications.

## Key Features
- Separates live browser sessions from durable workspace for research preservation
- Supports multi-platform job research (BOSS直聘, 鱼泡直聘) with platform-specific adapters
- Provides MCP endpoints for agent interaction and data persistence
- User-controlled authentication and sensitive actions (login, applications)
- Cross-platform job deduplication and normalization for unified opportunity tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to perform autonomous, cross-platform job research while ensuring user oversight and durable data preservation, bridging the gap between agent automation and real-world recruiting workflows.
