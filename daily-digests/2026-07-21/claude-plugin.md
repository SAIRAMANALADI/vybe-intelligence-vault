## Summary
A Claude Code plugin for Pobo Page Builder that enables AI-driven styling of e-commerce widgets and automated product labeling. It integrates with platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, and PrestaShop to streamline design and product management tasks.

## Key Features
- AI-powered styling of e-commerce widgets to match shop design
- Automated product labeling from client-supplied lists (xlsx, URLs, EANs)
- Seamless integration with major e-commerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.)
- OAuth-based authentication with Pobo Page Builder via browser login
- Real-time feedback and iteration for deployed styles

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It simplifies e-commerce design and product management by automating repetitive tasks, reducing manual effort for developers and store owners.
