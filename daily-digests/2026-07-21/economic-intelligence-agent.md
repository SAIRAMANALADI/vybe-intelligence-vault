## Summary
An AI-powered agent for automated economic data collection, quantitative analysis, and risk assessment, generating actionable intelligence reports from 9 diverse data sources with resilience features and alerting capabilities.

## Key Features
- Async ingestion from 9 economic/financial data APIs with caching and rate limiting
- Quantitative analysis engine supporting RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, yield curve analysis, and market regime detection
- Comprehensive risk assessment with VaR, stress tests, drawdown tracking, and performance ratios
- Sentiment analysis using VADER NLP with financial domain terms and Fear & Greed Index
- Resilient architecture with circuit breakers, retries, TTL cache, and graceful degradation
