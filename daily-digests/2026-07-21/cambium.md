## Summary
Cambium is a knowledge-lifecycle MCP that automatically distills agent work into compound, organization-wide knowledge by integrating with agentsync and context-keeper. It provides passive capture, federated recall, and trust-based promotion of knowledge from local to team to organizational scope.

## Key Features
- Automated knowledge distillation from agent work using `distill()`
- Federated recall endpoint (`recall()`) for all agent types across local, team, and org scopes
- Trust-based promotion of knowledge (`promote()`) from local to team to org as it earns validation
- Passive capture of agent work via agentsync and context-keeper integration
- Machine-maintained documentation entropy tracking with `stale_report()` and `verify_entry()`

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Cambium bridges the gap between agent work and organizational knowledge by automating the capture, recall, and promotion of knowledge, ensuring that valuable insights are preserved and scaled across teams and the entire organization.
