## Summary
Box is an advanced, fully offline Android application that integrates multiple on-device AI capabilities, including multimodal LLMs, vision models, text-to-image generation, speech processing, and NPU acceleration. It serves as a security-hardened fork of Google AI Edge Gallery with over 50 additional features.

## Key Features
- On-device multimodal LLM support with GGUF model import and Vulkan GPU offloading
- Vision AI capabilities including image recognition, document analysis, and Box Assist for blind/low-vision users
- Text-to-image generation with FLUX.2 klein and Z-Image Turbo models
- Speech-to-speech AI chat with multilingual STT/TTS and SenseVoice integration
- Hardware-accelerated NPU support across Snapdragon, MediaTek, and Google Tensor devices

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Box provides a comprehensive, offline-first AI toolkit for Android that enables developers to build and deploy multimodal AI applications without relying on cloud services.
