## Summary
An MCP server that provides AI assistants with access to primary Arabic knowledge sources, including the Quran, major Hadith collections, Arabic Wikipedia, and Arabic news outlets, ensuring accurate and grounded responses.

## Key Features
- Provides structured access to the Quran with Uthmani script and Saheeh International translation
- Integrates six canonical Hadith collections plus Muwatta Malik and Nawawi's 40 Hadith
- Supports Arabic Wikipedia and Arabic news feeds (Al Jazeera, BBC Arabic, etc.)
- Includes Arabic text normalization for robust search and matching
- Designed for seamless integration with MCP-compatible AI clients

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This MCP server enables AI assistants to retrieve accurate, primary-source Arabic knowledge, reducing hallucinations and improving contextual relevance for Arabic-language queries.
