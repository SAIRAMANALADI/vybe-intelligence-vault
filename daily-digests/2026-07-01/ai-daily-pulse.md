## Summary
AI Daily Pulse automates the aggregation, scoring, and summarization of AI news from 47 RSS feeds into a daily bilingual digest, featuring trend detection, AI-generated project ideas, and social media copy. It combines rule-based filtering with LLM-powered analysis to deliver high-quality, actionable insights for AI builders and solopreneurs.

## Key Features
- Automated daily digest from 47 RSS feeds with rule-based and AI-powered filtering
- Bilingual (English/Chinese) summaries and AI-generated project insights
- Trend detection via cross-source clustering and LLM tagging
- Cost-efficient pipeline with built-in deduplication and content caps
- GitHub Actions-based automation with local development support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines AI news consumption by automating curation, scoring, and trend analysis, reducing manual effort for builders who need to stay updated on AI developments without information overload.
