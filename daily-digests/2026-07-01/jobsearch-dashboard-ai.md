## Summary
An agentic AI-powered job search dashboard that automates job discovery, resume parsing, application material generation, and outcome tracking while enforcing human approval gates for critical actions like submissions and external communications.

## Key Features
- Automated job discovery, deduplication, and scoring with AI-driven relevance checks
- Resume parsing, evidence library management, and ATS-friendly material generation with deterministic fallbacks
- Controlled application workflows with manual approval gates for submissions and external actions
- Slack-integrated agent operations for real-time updates, approvals, and natural language interactions
- Comprehensive audit trails for application states, transitions, and integrity repairs
