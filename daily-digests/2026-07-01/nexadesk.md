## Summary
NexaDesk is an AI-powered receptionist system for real estate agencies that autonomously handles inbound property enquiries via voice and chat. It qualifies leads, answers FAQs using RAG, and schedules viewings without human intervention.

## Key Features
- 24/7 autonomous handling of inbound voice and chat enquiries
- RAG-powered FAQs with OpenAI embeddings and Qdrant vector search
- Automated lead capture and CRM-style dashboard integration
- Multi-language support (English, Arabic, Urdu, French, Spanish)
- Google Calendar integration for booking viewings

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
NexaDesk provides a turnkey solution for real estate agencies to automate customer interactions, reducing operational costs while improving lead conversion through AI-driven qualification and RAG-enhanced responses.
