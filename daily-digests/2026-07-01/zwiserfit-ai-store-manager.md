## Summary
Zwiserfit AI Store Manager is a physical AI system that transforms a WeChat account into an autonomous AI store manager (Momo) for retail operations. It replaces traditional SaaS and apps with a conversational AI that handles member interactions, check-ins, scheduling, and compliance using real-world store data.

## Key Features
- Autonomous AI store manager (Momo) operating via WeChat with no app required
- Dual-engine architecture: OpenClaw for orchestration and Hermes Agent for store-level decisions
- Real-time physical AI capabilities: facial recognition, member tracking, and behavioral analytics
- Offline-first design with asynchronous data sync for reliability in low-connectivity environments
- Web5-based attestation and ZWF-20 compliance for immutable data integrity and auditability
