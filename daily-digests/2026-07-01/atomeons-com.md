## Summary
A Next.js 16-based open-source website for AtomEons Systems Laboratory, serving as a launcher and silo system for various AI products, including Orange³ (sovereign agentic OS), AI Bookmaker, and the I AM AI book/audiobook. The site features a modular architecture with 9 themed silos, an MCP server endpoint, and multiple deployment-ready themes.

## Key Features
- Modular silo-based architecture with 9 distinct themed sections for AI products and content
- MCP server endpoint (`/api/mcp`) enabling direct AI client queries to the corpus
- Four adaptive themes (noir, white V2, warez, thin) with GPU-tiering for performance optimization
- Integrated Stripe Checkout for one-time payments and HMAC-signed download tokens for secure digital deliveries
- Comprehensive documentation, including a user manual, manifesto, and product pages for Orange³ and AI Bookmaker
