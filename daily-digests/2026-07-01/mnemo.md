## Summary
MNEMO is a private, local-first personal knowledge graph and AI companion that runs entirely on your machine. It ingests notes, articles, and other data to build a living graph of your knowledge, learns how you think, and reasons in your voice without ever sending data to the cloud.

## Key Features
- Builds a typed knowledge graph with 20 node types and 16 edge types, enabling deep reasoning across your data.
- Runs entirely locally with optional cloud LLM integration, ensuring no data leaves your device by default.
- Self-optimizing nightly processes that deduplicate, reconcile contradictions, and rebuild your persona model.
- Supports multi-modal inputs including text, images, and voice with on-device processing.
- Integrates with external services (Gmail, Notion, GitHub) for ingestion while maintaining strict privacy controls.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
MNEMO provides a privacy-preserving, self-hosted framework for building agentic systems that reason over personal knowledge graphs, making it ideal for RAG builders who prioritize data sovereignty and deep contextual understanding.
