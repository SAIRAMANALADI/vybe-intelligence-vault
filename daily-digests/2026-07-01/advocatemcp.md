## Summary
AdvocateMCP is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that enables discovery, querying, booking, and transactions with verified small and medium business (SMB) AI agents through any MCP-compatible client. It bridges local businesses with AI ecosystems via a standardized protocol and edge-based bot interception for direct AI agent routing.

## Key Features
- 10 MCP-compatible tools for discovery, booking, and transactions with SMB AI agents
- Edge-based bot interception via Cloudflare Workers to route AI crawlers to business-specific agents
- Structured, citation-ready responses with HMAC-signed attribution for end-to-end tracking
- Multi-tenant Bearer token authentication for transactional endpoints
- Rate limiting and tiered access controls (unverified, known, trusted)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AdvocateMCP standardizes how local businesses integrate with AI ecosystems, enabling seamless discovery and transactional interactions while ensuring attribution and compliance.
