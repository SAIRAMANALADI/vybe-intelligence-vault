## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that transforms PDFs into structured, evidence-backed representations for AI agents, enabling accurate extraction, verification, and citation of document content including text, tables, and visual elements.

## Key Features
- Converts PDFs into an Agent Document Twin with structured, source-backed representations for AI agents
- Supports auto-detection and extraction of text, tables, charts, formulas, and visual elements with provenance
- Provides trust and accessibility reports to identify hidden or misleading content
- Offers OCR integration for scanned PDFs with configurable providers
- Includes deterministic benchmarks and release gates to ensure extraction quality

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It eliminates hallucinations and citation errors in RAG systems by providing agents with verifiable, structured evidence from PDFs instead of raw extracted text.
