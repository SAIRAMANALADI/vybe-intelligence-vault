## Summary
GoldBean provides global developers with access to 54 Baidu AI endpoints (OCR, TTS, ASR, LLM, image recognition, NLP, and HelixFold protein prediction) without requiring a Chinese phone number or Baidu Cloud account. It enables pay-per-call access via x402 USDC micropayments, PayPal, or Alipay.

## Key Features
- 54 Baidu AI endpoints including OCR, TTS, ASR, LLM, and image recognition
- Pay-per-call pricing via x402 USDC, PayPal, or Alipay with no subscription required
- MCP-compatible server for seamless integration with AI agents (Claude, Cursor, etc.)
- Free tier with 5 calls/day per IP and 20 credits on registration
- HelixFold3 protein structure prediction and enterprise-grade Baidu AI capabilities

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
GoldBean removes barriers to accessing Baidu AI's high-quality OCR, translation, and LLM services for global developers, enabling cost-effective and flexible AI integration without regional restrictions.
