## Summary
PanWatch is a self-hosted AI-powered stock monitoring assistant that integrates the TradingAgents multi-agent investment decision framework. It provides real-time monitoring, portfolio management, technical analysis, and multi-channel notifications for A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and US stocks.

## Key Features
- Real-time monitoring and alerts for A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and US stocks
- Integration with TradingAgents for multi-agent investment decision-making
- Technical analysis with indicators like MACD, RSI, KDJ, and support/resistance levels
- Multi-account portfolio management with AI-driven insights
- Multi-channel notifications via Telegram, WeChat, DingTalk, and more

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PanWatch enables AI-driven stock monitoring and decision-making with self-hosted, private data handling, making it valuable for RAG builders who need real-time financial insights and multi-agent AI integration.
