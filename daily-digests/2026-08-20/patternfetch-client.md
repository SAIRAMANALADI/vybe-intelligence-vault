## Summary
patternfetch is a market-data API for AI agents that provides token-compact market-state reports for US stocks, ETFs, and crypto spot. It delivers interpreted analysis including detected patterns, support/resistance levels, market regime, and indicators with backtested historical hit rates and lift metrics.

## Key Features
- Token-compact market-state reports with interpreted analysis (patterns, support/resistance, regime, RSI/EMA)
- Backtested historical hit rates and lift metrics for detected patterns to distinguish informative from noise
- Six tools (brief, multi, delta, analogs, scan, capabilities) accessible via REST and MCP
- Keyless demo endpoint and self-serve key generation with $3 starter credit
- Supports US stocks, ETFs (delayed/EOD), and crypto spot (realtime) across multiple timeframes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It reduces token overhead for AI agents analyzing market data by providing pre-interpreted, backtested insights with lift metrics, enabling more efficient and informed decision-making without raw data dumps.
