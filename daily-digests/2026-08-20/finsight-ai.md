## Summary
FinSight AI is an open-source equity research workspace that transforms market data, financial filings, and company events into structured, evidence-grounded AI research with recoverable workflows and snapshot-bound reports. It bridges AI agents with reliable, inspectable research outputs for A-share markets.

## Key Features
- Recoverable long-running research tasks with explicit state management and dead-letter handling
- Idempotency and duplicate request prevention via Redis Lua single-flight leases
- Snapshot-bound reports ensuring traceability and reproducibility of AI-generated insights
- Hybrid retrieval combining full-text search, vector similarity, and reciprocal-rank fusion for robust evidence grounding
- Modular AI runtime with deterministic fallbacks and provider-agnostic model adapters

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
FinSight AI provides a production-grade blueprint for building reliable, inspectable RAG systems with recoverable workflows and evidence-bound outputs, critical for enterprise AI research applications.
