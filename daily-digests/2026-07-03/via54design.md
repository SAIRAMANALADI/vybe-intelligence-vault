## Summary
via54Design is a Go-based design and narrative engine that transforms human inspiration into structured storytelling scaffolds, generating high-fidelity multimedia outputs like videos, presentations, and AI-generated images. It bridges the gap between unstructured human creativity and AI-driven execution.

## Key Features
- Converts human inspiration seeds into structured narrative scaffolds (JSON) using 4 cinematic storytelling models (e.g., Hero's Journey, Three-Act).
- Generates AI-ready prompts for 17 platforms (MidJourney, DALL·E, Flux, etc.) with 26-dimensional control parameters.
- Exports high-fidelity outputs: PPTX (editable), SVG scenes, HTML/CSS designs, and video scripts with zero external dependencies for core functionality.
- Supports dual operational modes (full-featured vs. lightweight) with hardware-aware auto-detection and mode locking.
- Integrates with Google Vids for automated video creation by embedding speaker notes into PPTX exports.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
via54Design streamlines the conversion of raw human creativity into structured, AI-executable narratives, enabling RAG builders to automate multimedia content generation with minimal manual intervention.
