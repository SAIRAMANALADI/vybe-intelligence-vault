## Summary
AI Manager is a sales automation tool for Telegram Business that responds to clients in real-time as if it were a human, qualifying leads, collecting briefs, generating quotes from a product catalog, handling payments, and escalating complex cases to human agents.

## Key Features
- Real-time human-like responses via Telegram Business API with typing indicators and natural delays
- Lead qualification and funnel management with structured brief collection and catalog-based quoting
- Integration with YooKassa and SBP for secure payment processing and receipt recognition
- RAG-based style memory for personalized responses aligned with past interactions and brand voice
- Human takeover for high-risk topics (complaints, contracts, NDAs) and automated escalation rules

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI-driven sales automation directly in Telegram Business chats, reducing response latency and improving lead conversion while maintaining human oversight for critical interactions.
