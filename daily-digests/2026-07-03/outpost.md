## Summary
Outpost is an AI-driven behavioral accountability tool for retail investors that captures trading theses and reminds users of their original plans during moments of panic or uncertainty. It functions as a second brain to prevent emotional decision-making by logging trades, market regimes, and outcomes to provide personalized behavioral insights.

## Key Features
- AI-powered trading thesis capture and enforcement to prevent emotional decisions
- Immutable decision-intelligence ledger tracking market context, outcomes, and behavioral patterns
- Multi-agent reasoning with specialized models (Bull/Bear/Referee) for trade-plan validation
- Proactive briefings (pre-market, EOD, weekly) and personalized research feeds
- Atomic cash integrity with Postgres transactions and per-user advisory locks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical behavioral accountability layer for retail investors, reducing emotional trading by leveraging AI to enforce self-imposed trading rules and track behavioral patterns over time.
