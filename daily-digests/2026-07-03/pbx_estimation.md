## Summary
A data science project that quantifies the decline of traditional PBX markets and the rise of VoIP adoption across 12+ countries using time-series analysis (S-curve/logistic growth models) and survival analysis (Cox Proportional Hazards Model). It provides market death timelines and product survival probabilities for AI-driven telecom trend forecasting.

## Key Features
- Quantifies PBX market decline and VoIP adoption trends using logistic growth models (S-curves) for 12+ countries
- Applies Cox Proportional Hazards Model to predict product survival probabilities in shrinking markets
- Automated data collection from World Bank, ITU, BEREC, and national regulatory sources
- Generates interactive reports and dashboards via GitHub Actions and Cloudflare Workers
- Supports RAG integration with Cloudflare R2, Hugging Face Spaces, and NotebookLM for evidence retrieval

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides critical telecom market trend data and survival analytics essential for building accurate RAG systems focused on VoIP/PBX technology adoption and market transitions.
