## Summary
Predict Raven is an open-source forecasting agent framework that enables AI agents to estimate real-world event probabilities, gather evidence dynamically, and act on predictions. It powers both autonomous prediction-market trading on Polymarket and market-blind public forecasting, such as live World Cup predictions.

## Key Features
- Dynamic evidence gathering and Bayesian updating for probability estimation
- Dual-mode operation: market-blind forecasting and autonomous trading
- Hard risk controls (e.g., 15% per trade, 80% exposure cap) for safe execution
- Modular AI provider switching (Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw) via Norns tier system
- Transparent decision logging and public Brier scoring for accountability

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Predict Raven demonstrates how autonomous AI agents can autonomously gather evidence, estimate probabilities, and execute trades or forecasts with built-in risk controls, setting a benchmark for real-world AI-driven decision-making in dynamic environments.
