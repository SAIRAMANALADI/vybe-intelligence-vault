## Summary
JanSankalp AI is an enterprise-grade civic governance platform leveraging AI, federated learning, IoT, and satellite data to enable real-time complaint reporting, multi-agent AI verification, and automated governance workflows. It features a decoupled 4-tier microservices architecture designed for scalability and high availability.

## Key Features
- Decoupled 4-tier microservices architecture for independent scalability and high availability
- Multi-agent AI workflows for spam detection, classification, duplicate checks, and ETA policy enforcement
- Real-time complaint reporting and AI-assisted chat streams via Server-Sent Events (SSE)
- Integration with satellite imagery, IoT, and computer vision for automated audits (e.g., pothole/garbage detection)
- Secure service-to-service authentication with JWT/RBAC and internal token validation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
