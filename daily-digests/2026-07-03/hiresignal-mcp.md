## Summary
HireSignal is an MCP server that transforms live job postings into actionable buying signals for B2B sales and GTM agents. It enables AI-driven account scoring, outreach timing, and market research by analyzing hiring intent from company ATS postings.

## Key Features
- Real-time job posting ingestion from company ATS (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday) for first-party data accuracy
- Time-series hiring velocity analysis with department-level breakdowns and growth signals
- Market salary estimation and skill demand pulse tracking with top hiring companies
- Remote deployment via SSE over HTTPS with rate-limiting and API key authentication
- Self-hostable with optional local corpus ingestion for zero-cost queries

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
HireSignal provides critical buying signals for AI agents by identifying companies actively hiring for roles relevant to your product, enabling proactive outreach and account scoring before competitors.
