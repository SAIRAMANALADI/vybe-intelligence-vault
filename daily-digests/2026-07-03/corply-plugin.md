## Summary
Corply is an autonomous incorporation agent that enables startup founders to form a Delaware C-Corp entirely from Claude Code. It automates guided applications, legal document generation, cofounder e-signatures, and human-reviewed Delaware filings with a flat fee and no hourly attorney costs.

## Key Features
- Guided conversational incorporation process with founder equity and vesting details
- Automated generation of legal documents (Certificate of Incorporation, Bylaws, etc.)
- Cofounder e-signature workflow with binding audit trails via magic links
- Human-reviewed Delaware filing before submission to ensure compliance
- Post-incorporation record management and company context storage

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Corply streamlines the complex and costly process of forming a Delaware C-Corp, making it accessible and automated for founders directly within their development environment.
