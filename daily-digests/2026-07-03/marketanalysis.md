## Summary
A structured market analysis template for AI chat agents that provides consistent, disciplined analysis across multiple asset classes (XAUUSD, BTC, forex, indices, crypto) using Astronacci, Fibonacci, Fear & Greed Index, and fixed risk management rules.

## Key Features
- Structured market analysis format for AI agents with fixed templates
- Integrates Astronacci/Elliott Wave and Fibonacci retracement for price movement analysis
- Fixed risk parameters (30 pip entry, 100 pip SL) with RR ladder (1:1 to 1:10)
- Live data integration including Fear & Greed Index, news, and asset-specific metrics
- Supports multiple asset classes (XAUUSD, BTC, forex, indices, crypto)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides a disciplined, repeatable framework for AI agents to generate market analysis with built-in risk management, ensuring consistency and reducing improvisation in trading decisions.
