## Summary
Fonos is a local-first, hotkey-driven voice terminal for macOS and Linux that enables hands-free dictation, meeting capture, AI agent interactions, and text-to-speech workflows. It integrates with multiple STT, LLM, and TTS providers (cloud or local) and routes voice output to the cursor, clipboard, notebooks, or AI agents.

## Key Features
- Global hotkey-driven voice capture with floating UI controls for minimal disruption
- Multi-provider support for STT, LLM, and TTS (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, LM Studio, etc.)
- Meeting capture with dual-channel transcription and AI-generated summaries
- Voice notes organized into notebooks with customizable modes and exports
- Agent mode for voice-driven AI interactions with tool execution control

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Fonos streamlines voice-to-text and AI agent workflows, enabling developers to integrate hands-free dictation and structured note-taking directly into their RAG pipelines or AI-driven applications.
