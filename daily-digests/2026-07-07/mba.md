## Summary
MBA (Metric Brand Auditor) is a multi-agent system that evaluates brand influence by orchestrating parallel research agents and a panel of evaluators to generate versioned, auditable reports. It provides a structured, repeatable framework for quantifying brand impact across seven dimensions and five lenses, enabling continuous tracking and evolution of brand assessments.

## Key Features
- Parallel sub-agent research across 7 brand dimensions (e.g., origin narrative, product positioning, distribution channels)
- Fixed 5-lens scoring system (originality, category naming, leverage quality, identity coherence, real-world signal) for consistent evaluation
- Versioned reporting with evolution mode to track changes over time and generate delta reports
- 10 pre-built evaluator panels (43 total evaluators) with industry-specific perspectives (e.g., luxury, AI, automotive)
- MCP server integration for seamless deployment and automation (e.g., subscriptions, webhook notifications)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
MBA provides a repeatable, auditable framework for quantifying brand influence, enabling RAG builders to integrate structured, multi-perspective evaluations into their systems for more reliable and comparable insights.
