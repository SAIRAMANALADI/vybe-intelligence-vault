## Summary
A Windows application that bridges Google NotebookLM with AI text editors like Claude, Cursor, and Windsurf using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It enables real-time access to private research notes and documents directly within AI chat interfaces while maintaining data privacy.

## Key Features
- Real-time document access from Google NotebookLM within AI editors
- Citations linking AI responses to source documents
- Secure OAuth-based authentication with Google
- Support for multiple NotebookLM projects simultaneously
- Automatic updates and data synchronization

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI engineers to seamlessly integrate private research data from Google NotebookLM into RAG pipelines and AI workflows without manual data extraction.
