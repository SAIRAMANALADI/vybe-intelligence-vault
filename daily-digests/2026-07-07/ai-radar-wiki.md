## Summary
AI Radar is an automated AI technology intelligence system designed for AI product managers, featuring a self-evolving knowledge graph with four pillars: technical capabilities, product patterns, tool ecosystems, and commercial dynamics. It provides real-time updates, interactive visualizations, and structured data for AI product development.

## Key Features
- Self-evolving knowledge graph with 3,221 nodes and 3,479 edges
- Four-pillar system covering technical, product, tool, and commercial AI domains
- Interactive knowledge graph visualization for exploration
- Automated updates every 6 hours with RSS feed support
- Structured data exports (JSON, Markdown) for integration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
