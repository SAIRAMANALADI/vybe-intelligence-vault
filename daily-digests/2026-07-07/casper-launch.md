## Summary
CasperLaunch is an AI-powered platform for tokenizing real-world assets into CEP-78 NFTs on the Casper blockchain. It automates asset tokenization, yield distribution, and secondary trade settlement using autonomous AI agents and smart contracts.

## Key Features
- AI-driven asset tokenization from plain English descriptions into CEP-78 NFTs
- Autonomous yield distribution without human intervention
- AI-assisted secondary trade settlement with on-chain verification
- First non-EVM implementation of the x402 micropayment protocol
- Real-time off-chain state mirroring with Neon Postgres

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a complete, production-ready framework for AI-driven real-world asset tokenization and autonomous financial operations on the Casper blockchain.
