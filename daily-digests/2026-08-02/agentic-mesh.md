## Summary
NexusFlow is a declarative YAML-based orchestrator for designing, executing, and monitoring AI-powered workflows across multiple providers. It enables domain experts to compose complex multi-model pipelines without extensive programming, handling coordination, error recovery, and performance optimization automatically.

## Key Features
- Declarative workflow design using YAML for defining multi-stage AI pipelines
- Multi-provider integration supporting 6+ major LLM providers with unified authentication
- Built-in intelligent coordination patterns (ReAct, Plan & Execute, Swarm, etc.)
- Production-ready deployment with REST, WebSocket, and GraphQL APIs
- Enterprise-grade security, monitoring, and scalability features

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It simplifies the orchestration of complex AI workflows across multiple providers, reducing development time and operational overhead for RAG and AI stack builders.
