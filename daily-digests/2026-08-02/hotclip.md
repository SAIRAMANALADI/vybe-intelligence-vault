## Summary
HotClip is a free and open-source AI-powered video clipping tool that converts long videos (livestreams, podcasts, courses) into viral vertical short videos. It automates high-impact segment detection, auto-captioning, and 9:16 aspect ratio conversion, all while running locally without watermarks or uploads.

## Key Features
- AI-driven highlight detection with multi-dimensional scoring (hook, structure, value, trends)
- Local-first processing with no uploads or watermarks, supporting Windows/macOS
- Automated vertical video conversion (9:16) with smart framing (face tracking, center crop fallback)
- Real-time transcription with word-level timestamps and semantic line breaks
- Batch processing, MCP server integration, and 7x24 unattended livestream monitoring

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
HotClip provides a critical open-source alternative to proprietary AI video clipping tools, enabling RAG builders to automate high-quality short-form content extraction from long videos while maintaining full data privacy and control.
