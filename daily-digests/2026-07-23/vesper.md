## Summary
Vesper is a daily briefing generator that aggregates signals from various sources into concise morning or evening briefings in natural language. It filters out routine background activity and presents pending decisions with option, benefit, and cost framing for actionable insights.

## Key Features
- Aggregates signals into concise morning/evening briefings
- Strict filtering to exclude noise and repetitive data
- Decision framing with option, benefit, and cost analysis
- Gmail-based briefing delivery via dedicated scripts
- Diagnostic utilities for briefing inspection and troubleshooting
