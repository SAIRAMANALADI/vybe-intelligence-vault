## Summary
ClipForge is an open-source AI tool that automates the creation of e-commerce short videos from a single product image. It generates sales scripts, preserves product authenticity, adds subtitles, voiceovers, and background music, and exports videos optimized for platforms like TikTok Shop, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and more.

## Key Features
- AI-driven sales script generation with 5 product categories and 4 storytelling styles
- Product authenticity preservation (image-to-image without distortion)
- Multi-platform export (TikTok Shop, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, etc.) with platform-specific optimization
- Zero-cost batch video production using free assets (Openverse, Wikimedia Commons) and Microsoft Edge TTS
- Built-in compliance checks for AI content labeling, ad regulations, and platform algorithms

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ClipForge streamlines the creation of high-conversion e-commerce videos, reducing manual effort and costs while ensuring compliance and authenticity for AI-driven sales pipelines.
