## Summary
An MCP server that provides IMAP email integration for AI assistants, enabling secure email account management, search, retrieval, and sending capabilities with support for multiple email providers and encrypted credential storage.

## Key Features
- Secure IMAP/SMTP email account management with AES-256 encrypted storage
- Comprehensive email operations: search, read, send, move, mark, and delete emails
- Support for 15+ pre-configured email providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, etc.)
- Connection pooling and efficient IMAP account handling
- Web-based setup wizard and CLI/npx-based deployment options

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI assistants to directly interact with email systems for tasks like information retrieval, automated responses, and workflow automation, bridging the gap between AI and real-world email data.
