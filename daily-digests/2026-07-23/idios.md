## Summary
Idios provides a private escrow and settlement protocol for AI and compute workloads on the Beam blockchain, enabling verifiable delivery with hidden amounts and parties. It supports deterministic and non-deterministic work with on-chain dispute resolution and worker collateral mechanisms.

## Key Features
- Private payment and settlement for AI/compute work with hidden amounts and identities
- Two settlement modes: hash-verified (Mode A) and reviewed (Mode B)
- On-chain dispute resolution via M of N arbitrator voting
- Worker collateral and reputation bonds for verifiable performance
- Two-phase claim pattern to work around Beam BVM constraints
