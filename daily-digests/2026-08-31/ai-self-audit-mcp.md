## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that enables AI agents to audit their own compliance with the EU AI Act in real time using signed certificates and tamper-proof records. It provides HMAC-signed audit logs, hash-chained integrity, and compliance reporting for regulated AI deployments.

## Key Features
- Real-time self-auditing of AI agents for EU AI Act compliance
- HMAC-signed audit logs with hash-chained integrity for tamper detection
- A2A agent tracking and compliance reporting
- Support for Articles 9, 13, 15, 26, and 50 of the EU AI Act
- Signed certificates verifiable via `verify.meok.ai`

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI systems can autonomously verify and document compliance with the EU AI Act, reducing legal risks and enabling trustworthy deployments in regulated environments.
