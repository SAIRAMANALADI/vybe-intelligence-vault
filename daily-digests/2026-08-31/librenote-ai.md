## Summary
LibreNote AI is an open-source, self-hosted alternative to NotebookLM that enables users to upload documents, chat with AI grounded in those sources, and generate studio artifacts like flashcards, quizzes, and mind maps entirely on their own infrastructure.

## Key Features
- Self-hosted alternative to NotebookLM with full data ownership
- Supports multi-format document ingestion (PDF, Word, YouTube, audio, etc.)
- Grounded AI chat with inline citations from uploaded sources
- Generates 7 types of studio artifacts (mind maps, flashcards, quizzes, etc.)
- Integrates with OpenRouter for flexible AI model selection

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LibreNote AI empowers RAG builders to create a fully customizable, self-hosted knowledge management system with grounded AI interactions and artifact generation.
