## Summary
A local, single-user business analysis agent that autonomously conducts evidence-backed research pipelines for companies or industries, including evidence collection, structured analysis, red-team falsification, and report generation with full traceability.

## Key Features
- Autonomous research pipeline with six stages: Scope, Collect, Analyze, Falsify, Refine, and Report
- Red-team falsification mechanism for validating conclusions with independent evidence-based challenges
- Full traceability of insights with attached evidence chains, sources, and confidence scores
- Dynamic industry-specific analysis frameworks with three-layer structure (universal methodology, industry templates, and adaptive scope)
- Markdown/PDF report generation with financial tables, citations, and quality evaluations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, evidence-driven approach to autonomous business research, ensuring conclusions are validated and traceable, which is critical for building reliable RAG systems in domains requiring factual rigor.
