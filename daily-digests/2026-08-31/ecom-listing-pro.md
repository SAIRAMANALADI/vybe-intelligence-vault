## Summary
An AI-driven tool for optimizing e-commerce product listings by auto-diagnosing images, rewriting titles for conversion, generating 6-image carousels with intelligent style matching, and removing watermarks. Supports cross-border and domestic modes with built-in compliance checks.

## Key Features
- Auto-diagnosis of product images with subject recognition and confirmation prompts
- Intelligent title optimization using a structured formula with embedded SEO keywords
- 8-style carousel design matching based on product category (e.g., glassmorphism, cyberpunk, Japanese hand-drawn)
- Automated watermark detection and removal (AI and traditional methods)
- Cross-border and domestic mode compliance with regional restrictions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the creation of high-converting e-commerce listings by automating image optimization, title generation, and carousel design, reducing manual effort and improving market readiness.
