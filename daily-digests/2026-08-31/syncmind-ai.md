## Summary
SyncMind AI is a full-stack AI meeting intelligence platform that automates meeting workflows by uploading recordings, generating transcripts and summaries, extracting action items, and providing workspace analytics. It integrates multiple AI services for transcription, embeddings, and semantic search.

## Key Features
- Automated meeting recording uploads and transcription using OpenAI Whisper
- AI-generated summaries and action item extraction with OpenAI GPT
- Real-time collaboration via Socket.io for notifications and meeting events
- Semantic search over meeting content using Pinecone vector DB
- Comprehensive workspace analytics and task tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SyncMind AI streamlines meeting workflows by automating transcription, summarization, and action item extraction, making it essential for teams leveraging AI-driven meeting intelligence in RAG pipelines.
