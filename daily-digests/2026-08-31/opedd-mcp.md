## Summary
Opedd-mcp is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that enables AI assistants to discover, purchase, and verify rights-cleared content licenses autonomously. It provides verifiable, on-chain licensed content for RAG, AI search, and LLM grounding, ensuring compliance with copyright and regulatory frameworks like the EU AI Act.

## Key Features
- Autonomous discovery and purchase of licensed content via AI assistants (Claude, Cursor, Windsurf)
- On-chain license verification with cryptographic proof (Tempo blockchain)
- 17 MCP tools for discovery, purchasing, verification, and compliance (EU AI Act Article 53, CDSM Article 4)
- Zero-install hosted endpoint (mcp.opedd.com) for immediate integration
- Regulatory compliance artifacts (attestations, audit ledgers, dossiers) for procurement and legal teams

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical infrastructure layer for RAG builders to source verifiable, rights-cleared content while ensuring compliance with emerging AI regulations like the EU AI Act.
