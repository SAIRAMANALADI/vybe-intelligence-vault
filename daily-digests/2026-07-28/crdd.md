## Summary
CRDD is a development methodology designed to preserve and connect contextual information (e.g., design intent, decisions, alternatives) throughout the AI-assisted development lifecycle. It ensures human authority over meaning while enabling AI to handle implementation and verification tasks.

## Key Features
- Preserves design intent, decisions, and rationale in a structured repository
- Defines clear human-AI authority boundaries for development tasks
- Uses Git-based canonical control plane for context management
- Supports parallel phases (UI and Behavior Specification) with shared contracts
- Provides audit and conformance tools for methodology adherence

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
CRDD ensures AI-assisted development retains product meaning and context, preventing loss of critical decisions and intent during implementation.
