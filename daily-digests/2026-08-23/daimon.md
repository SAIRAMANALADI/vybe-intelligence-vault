## Summary
daimon is a collaborative data science agent designed to integrate with Discord or Slack, enabling teams to perform Bayesian modeling, data analysis, and visualization directly within team communication channels. It automates complex tasks like forecasting, A/B testing, and recurring reports while maintaining tenant isolation for multi-team deployments.

## Key Features
- Multi-platform support (Discord, Slack, CLI, MCP) with tenant isolation for secure multi-team deployments
- Automated Bayesian modeling and statistical analysis with PyMC, delivered as runnable notebooks
- Threaded conversation continuity for seamless team collaboration and session management
- Scheduled routines for recurring data analysis and reporting (e.g., weekly summaries)
- Self-hostable with Docker or deployable via a hosted service for zero-setup adoption

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
daimon bridges the gap between AI-powered data science and real-time team collaboration, enabling scalable, reproducible, and interactive analysis workflows directly within existing communication platforms.
