## Summary
MirrorMind is an AI-powered journaling and decision-intelligence platform that acts as a cognitive twin, helping users reflect deeply on their thoughts, identify thinking patterns, and receive AI-generated insights for personal growth and decision-making.

## Key Features
- Secure authentication with NextAuth.js and email/password login
- AI-powered reflection and insights using Claude AI via freemodel.dev
- Smart journaling with rich text, history, and search capabilities
- Decision-intelligence tools to reason through complex choices
- Light/dark mode and fully responsive design for cross-device usability

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
MirrorMind provides a structured, AI-enhanced journaling experience that helps users build a persistent knowledge base for RAG systems by capturing and analyzing personal reflections and decisions.
