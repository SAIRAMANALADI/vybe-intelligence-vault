## Summary
A tool that converts HTML, CSS, and animation code into professional motion graphics and video files for product launches. It automates video rendering, audio normalization, and camera motion to meet broadcast standards without manual editing.

## Key Features
- Converts HTML/CSS and GSAP animations into broadcast-ready videos
- Automated audio loudness normalization for broadcast compliance
- Virtual camera path controls for dynamic scene composition
- Frame interpolation for smooth motion in complex animations
- Cinematic color grading and film-grade filters

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the creation of high-quality product launch videos directly from code, reducing manual editing effort and ensuring consistency for AI-driven marketing workflows.
