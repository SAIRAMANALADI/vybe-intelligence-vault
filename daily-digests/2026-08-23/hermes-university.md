## Summary
Hermes University is an AI-powered personalized learning system that builds research-based courses around the user's schedule, goals, and learning pace. It integrates with Telegram for interaction, Google Calendar for scheduling, and Obsidian for progress tracking, while ensuring mastery through deterministic grading and proof-based learning.

## Key Features
- Builds custom courses from real research and cited sources, not generic content
- Integrates with Google Calendar to schedule study blocks at optimal times
- Uses deterministic grading with proof-based mastery checks to avoid hallucinations
- Supports Obsidian for tracking progress and Anki for spaced repetition (optional)
- Model-agnostic with DeepSeek by default, runs on user-owned Hermes Agent

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, research-backed approach to personalized AI learning with proof-based mastery, reducing reliance on generic courses and ensuring measurable progress.
