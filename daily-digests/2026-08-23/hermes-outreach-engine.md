## Summary
Hermes Outreach Engine automates 24/7 multi-channel AI outreach (email, SMS, voice) for lead qualification, scoring leads in real-time and only interrupting operators when predefined criteria are met. It integrates with Hermes Agent and xAI Grok for autonomous sequence execution and handoff management.

## Key Features
- Multi-channel sequence automation (email, SMS, AI voice) with dry-run capability
- Real-time lead scoring and qualification criteria (score ≥ 40, stage hot/qualified, interest signals)
- Autonomous handoff to operators only when criteria are met, avoiding unnecessary interruptions
- Integration with Hermes Agent and xAI Grok for autonomous decision-making and model fallback to OpenRouter
- Compliance-first design with dry-run defaults, opt-out support, and CAN-SPAM/TCPA adherence

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines AI-driven outreach automation by ensuring only high-potential leads trigger human intervention, reducing noise and improving conversion efficiency for RAG/AI stack builders.
