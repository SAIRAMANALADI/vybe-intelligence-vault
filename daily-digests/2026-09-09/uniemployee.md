## Summary
UniEmployee is an enterprise-grade platform for building, deploying, and managing AI digital employees that automate business workflows like customer service, sales, HR, and data analysis. It provides a five-layer capability model (Employee → Workflow/SOP → Skill → Connector → Tool) to orchestrate LLMs into production-ready agents with long-term memory, HITL approvals, and full traceability.

## Key Features
- Five-layer capability model for structured agent configuration and deployment
- Human-in-the-loop (HITL) approvals for high-risk workflows
- Long-term memory with per-user/employee isolation and automatic conversation compression
- Enterprise knowledge ontology with structured semantics and source tracing
- Full-chain observability with run-level tracing and replay capabilities

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
UniEmployee provides a production-ready framework for deploying enterprise-grade AI agents with structured workflows, HITL controls, and full observability, bridging the gap between experimental RAG systems and scalable business automation.
