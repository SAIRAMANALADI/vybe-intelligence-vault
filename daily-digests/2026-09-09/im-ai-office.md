## Summary
IMAI is a self-hosted conversational AI office assistant that integrates with OpenIM to transform group chat into a task management system. It automatically extracts tasks from casual messages, resolves ambiguities via AI DMs, tracks tasks on a kanban board, and sends tiered reminders, all while maintaining full audit trails and data privacy.

## Key Features
- Automated task extraction from group chat messages with AI intent recognition
- Ambiguity resolution via AI DMs for unclear task assignments (e.g., resolving multiple 'Zhangs')
- Kanban board integration with tiered reminders (24h before, due day, overdue) and audit trails
- Team memory system for terms, nicknames, and contextual corrections to improve recognition accuracy
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with human-in-the-loop approvals for critical actions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
IMAI bridges the gap between casual team communication and structured task management, reducing reliance on manual follow-ups and ensuring accountability in small teams without dedicated project managers.
