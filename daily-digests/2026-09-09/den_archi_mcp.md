## Summary
den is a Korean AEC (Architecture, Engineering, Construction) knowledge curation MCP server that provides curated expertise to AI agents. It answers not just 'where' regulations are found but 'why' they exist, 'what comes first' in processes, and 'how things differ', with evidence-backed responses or explicit uncertainty when grounds are lacking.

## Key Features
- Curates Korean AEC standards (KDS, KCS, KS, Building Act) and connects them in a graph for contextual answers
- Answers 'why', 'what comes first', and 'how things differ' alongside regulatory citations
- Explicitly states uncertainty when evidence is lacking, avoiding speculative responses
- Provides process-oriented answers (e.g., construction sequences) with topological sorting of steps
- Measured +8.9% accuracy improvement on 192 qualification exam items when used with AI agents

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides structured, evidence-backed AEC knowledge critical for RAG systems to generate accurate, context-aware responses in construction and engineering domains.
