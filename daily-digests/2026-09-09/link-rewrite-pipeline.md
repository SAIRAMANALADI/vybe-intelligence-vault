## Summary
Curator Studio is a reputation distillation engine that transforms raw social signals from platforms like X/Twitter, Instagram, and Telegram into polished, platform-optimized content through AI-powered rewriting and a human-in-the-loop approval workflow. It standardizes ingestion, contextualizes rewriting, and enforces strict review protocols to amplify brand credibility.

## Key Features
- Context-aware AI rewriting engine that adapts content for each platform's culture and tone
- Multi-platform ingestion pipeline supporting X/Twitter, Instagram, and Telegram with normalized schema
- Editorial approval workflow with role-based access, audit trails, and keyboard-first interface
- Scheduled publishing orchestration with rate-limit synchronization and delivery metrics
- Multilingual brand alignment with semantic translation and human verification for 12 languages
