## Summary
A universal novel-writing skill pack for AI coding assistants, providing a 5-stage pipeline (interview, setup, craft specs, writing loop, revision & release) with 31 craft specification documents, 34 writer technique libraries, and memory systems to support long-form novel continuity across genres and lengths.

## Key Features
- 5-stage novel-writing pipeline covering idea to publication
- 31 craft specification documents for structured storytelling
- 34-writer technique library spanning world, Chinese, and web-novel styles
- Memory system for continuity in long-form novels (timelines, arcs, foreshadowing)
- Automated checks for banned words, POV leaks, and word count
