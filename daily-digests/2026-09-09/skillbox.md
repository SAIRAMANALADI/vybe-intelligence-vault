## Summary
Skillbox is a local-first, account-free tool for managing AI Agent Skills, enabling unified scanning, editing, and migration of Skills across multiple Agents with a single source of truth.

## Key Features
- Unified management of local Skills with a single source of truth
- One-click adaptation of Skills to 42+ supported Agents
- Batch organization, collection, and migration of Skills
- Offline-first functionality with no account dependency
- 90,000+ Skill Market integration for discovery and installation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Skillbox eliminates redundancy and inconsistency in Skill management across multiple AI Agents, streamlining RAG pipeline customization and maintenance.
