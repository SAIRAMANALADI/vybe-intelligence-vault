## Summary
A specialized AI skill and MCP server for Portuguese legal advisory, providing automated legal templates, calculators, playbooks, and checklists for both personal and business use cases in Portugal. Integrates with multiple AI platforms including Claude, Cursor, and ChatGPT.

## Key Features
- Multi-platform integration (Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex, Gemini, ChatGPT) via MCP server and CLI tools
- 28 real legal templates with placeholders for contracts, agreements, and legal documents
- 8 calculators for legal metrics (IMT, IRS, juros, prazos, etc.) with automated testing (18 tests)
- 5 playbooks for decision trees (e.g., RGPD compliance, contract drafting, due diligence)
- 5 checklists for actionable legal verification (e.g., contract review, pre-deployment)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides a comprehensive, automated legal framework for Portuguese law that reduces manual effort in drafting, compliance, and calculations for AI-driven legal assistants and businesses.
