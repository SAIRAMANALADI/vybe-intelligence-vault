## Summary
CCC — Career Cognition Compass is an open-source workflow tool designed to help job seekers organize chaotic career exploration into structured facts, judgments, and actionable next steps. It provides a cognitive loop for refining job search direction, resume tailoring, interview preparation, and offer evaluation.

## Key Features
- Role Family clustering and direction validation to clarify job search focus
- Project fact extraction and judgment traceability for resume and interview prep
- Master Resume to Role Family Resume to JD Patch pipeline for tailored applications
- Funnel and signal analysis to interpret job search outcomes and feedback
- Offer comparison and negotiation decision support with structured reasoning

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured cognitive framework to transform chaotic job search inputs into actionable insights, reducing decision fatigue and improving outcomes for RAG builders seeking career clarity.
