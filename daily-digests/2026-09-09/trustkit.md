## Summary
TrustKit provides a runnable standard for Agentic User Experience (AUX), including 10 heuristics, a 4-stage trust architecture, and a trust gap taxonomy. It offers executable tooling like `aux-audit` to evaluate AI products against these standards, enabling developers to design and audit agent-user relationships.

## Key Features
- 10 AUX Heuristics for evaluating agent-user relationships
- 4-stage Trust Architecture for assessing product maturity
- Executable `aux-audit` CLI and GitHub Action for automated scoring
- Open YAML schemas for forkable, vendor-neutral standards
- Dual-licensing (CC BY 4.0 for definitions, MIT for tooling)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
