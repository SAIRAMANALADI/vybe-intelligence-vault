## Summary
Cool Workflow (cw) is a command-line tool that orchestrates AI agents to perform structured, evidence-based tasks while maintaining a verifiable, tamper-proof record of every step. It acts as a black-box recorder for AI-driven workflows, ensuring transparency, reproducibility, and trust in agent outputs.

## Key Features
- Plans and decomposes tasks into verifiable steps with evidence gates
- Integrates with existing AI agents (Claude, Codex, Gemini, etc.) without running models itself
- Generates signed, tamper-proof JSON records of every workflow step stored locally
- Supports multi-agent topologies (map-reduce, debate, judge-panel) for complex tasks
- Provides deterministic replay and offline verification of workflows

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Cool Workflow ensures AI-driven workflows are transparent, verifiable, and reproducible by maintaining a tamper-proof record of every step, which is critical for high-stakes or auditable AI applications.
