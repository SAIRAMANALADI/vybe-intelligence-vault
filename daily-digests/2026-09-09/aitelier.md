## Summary
AItelier provides structured-but-dynamic subagent workflows for AI agents, enabling deterministic, auditable, and human-in-the-loop pipelines over MCP. It delegates bulk work to cheap, deterministic workflows while allowing agents to retain decision-making at checkpoints.

## Key Features
- Deterministic DAG-based pipeline execution with full audit trails
- Human-in-the-loop checkpoints for approval and feedback
- Adversarial review (Green/Red agents) for quality control
- MCP-compatible interface for seamless agent integration
- Pipeline generation from plain-language descriptions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AItelier enables trustworthy, reproducible, and auditable AI workflows, bridging the gap between dynamic agent swarms and static workflow engines for enterprise-grade RAG and AI deployments.
