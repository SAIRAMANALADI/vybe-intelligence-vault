## Summary
OpenFlow XMP is an all-in-one PHP-based growth operating system for websites, integrating CMS, SEO/GEO, CDP, marketing automation, CRM, e-commerce, courses, and community features. It includes a native AI growth engine that automates content creation, SEO, user insights, and conversion optimization, enabling self-sustaining growth loops.

## Key Features
- Unified growth stack combining CMS, SEO, CDP, MA, CRM, e-commerce, and courses in a single PHP application
- Native AI growth engine with self-evolving capabilities (AI diagnostics, automated fixes, and health monitoring)
- Real-time user CDP with RFM segmentation, behavioral tracking, and AI-driven insights
- Marketing automation with 15+ triggers and multi-channel orchestration (email, in-app, coupons, etc.)
