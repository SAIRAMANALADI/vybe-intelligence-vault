## Summary
SETTY is a prototype AI handoff copilot designed for customer support team leads to operationalize AI agents by auditing policy gaps, enabling human-in-the-loop approvals, and automating regression testing of knowledge updates. It transforms FAQs and resolved support logs into source-linked knowledge bases with tool execution traces and safe failure handling.

## Key Features
- Source-linked knowledge base from FAQs and resolved support logs with policy gap auditing
- Human approval workflow for knowledge updates before deployment
- Automated regression testing of approved knowledge against past failures
- Deterministic tool execution with trace visibility for operators
- Safe failure handling with fallback to human escalation and GAP tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SETTY enables AI engineering teams to operationalize RAG systems by ensuring knowledge updates are audited, approved, and validated through automated regression testing, reducing operational debt and enabling self-sufficiency for customer support teams.
