## Summary
A self-hosted, zero-maintenance quant workbench for A-share stocks offering stock selection, monitoring, and backtesting with multi-data source routing, AI-assisted research, and real-time anomaly detection.

## Key Features
- Multi-source data routing with plugin-based extensibility for diverse financial data feeds
- Polars-powered millisecond-level stock scanning and backtesting with T+1, fees, and slippage constraints
- Real-time anomaly monitoring (competition/auction/offset anomalies) with voice alerts and Feishu notifications
- AI-assisted research including automated market recaps, stock analysis, and strategy generation
- Factor mining and strategy backtesting with nested out-of-sample validation and candidate library management
