## Summary
Gram is an open-source AI control plane designed to securely scale AI usage across organizations by managing MCPs, Skills, and Assistants with fine-grained permissions, threat detection, and full observability of token usage and costs. It serves as a centralized platform for connecting agents to APIs, enforcing policies, and distributing tools with enterprise-grade security and compliance.

## Key Features
- Centralized management of MCPs, Skills, and Assistants with fine-grained permissions
- Real-time policy enforcement for security, data protection, and compliance (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001)
- Full observability of AI usage, token costs, and tool effectiveness with OpenTelemetry integration
- Enterprise-grade access control via RBAC and OAuth2.1 with IDP sync (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace)
- Private networking and tunneling for secure agent-to-API connections

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Gram provides the critical infrastructure for securely scaling and governing AI agents in enterprise environments, ensuring compliance, security, and observability while enabling seamless integration with existing systems.
