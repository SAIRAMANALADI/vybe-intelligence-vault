## Summary
Busabase is a local-first, open-source review database designed for AI-generated content, business data, and multimodal knowledge. It enables users to review, approve, and merge changes before they become trusted data, acting as a private CMS and structured source of truth with built-in change requests, audit trails, and a simple API for AI agents.

## Key Features
- Approval-first workflow for AI-generated content and business data
- Built-in change requests, comments, and audit trails for transparency
- Local-first and self-hostable with support for external Postgres/S3
- Agent-ready API for integrating with AI models and workflows
- Multi-platform support (desktop, mobile, CLI, and Docker)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Busabase provides a critical governance layer for AI-driven data pipelines, ensuring only vetted and approved content enters production systems or knowledge bases.
