## Summary
A股助手 is a professional real-time stock trading analysis tool with AI-assisted decision-making capabilities, offering TradingView-style charts, technical analysis, and portfolio management for A-share markets. It combines Go, React, and Electron with integrated LangChain-based AI for market sentiment analysis and decision support.

## Key Features
- TradingView-style real-time charts with WebSocket updates
- 10+ technical indicators (MA, MACD, RSI, KDJ, etc.) and configurable strategy engine
- LangChain-integrated AI for market sentiment analysis and news evaluation
- Portfolio tracking with profit/loss calculations and multi-portfolio comparison
- Cross-platform desktop delivery via Electron with Go backend and Python AI service
