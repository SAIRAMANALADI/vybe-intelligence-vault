## Summary
Summoner is an AI-powered platform that transforms novels into comics by automatically decomposing plots, extracting characters, and generating comic panels with speech bubbles, inner monologues, and sound effects across multiple art styles.

## Key Features
- Automated comic panel generation from novel text with structured layouts
- Multi-engine support for image generation (SDXL, DALL-E, Replicate, SiliconFlow)
- Multi-provider LLM integration for story decomposition and character extraction
- Built-in quality gates, diagnostics, and smoke testing for reliability
- Customizable comic styles, fonts, and rendering engines

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Summoner provides a critical pipeline for converting unstructured text into structured, visually rich comic panels, enabling RAG systems to generate multimodal outputs beyond plain text.
