## Summary
SANBA is a voice-based multi-agent system designed to collaboratively refine requirements through natural speech interactions between humans and AI agents. It leverages Gemini Live for low-latency speech-to-speech communication, LiveKit for multi-participant audio handling, and Google ADK for autonomous agent orchestration to extract, validate, and document requirements in real-time.

## Key Features
- Low-latency speech-to-speech interactions using Gemini Live and LiveKit for natural multi-participant conversations
- Autonomous multi-agent orchestration with Google ADK for specialized sub-agents (e.g., non-functional requirements, contradiction detection)
- Real-time requirement refinement through iterative questioning, validation, and documentation
- Integration with Google Cloud Run for scalable, production-ready deployments
- Observability and LLMOps with OpenTelemetry, Langfuse, and DORA metrics for continuous improvement

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SANBA addresses the critical bottleneck in AI-driven development by automating and enhancing the requirements elicitation process through collaborative, voice-based multi-agent interactions, ensuring higher-quality deliverables and reducing misalignment in complex projects.
