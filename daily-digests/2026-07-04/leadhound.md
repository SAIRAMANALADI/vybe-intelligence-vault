## Summary
LeadHound is an automated UK business prospecting tool that discovers, verifies, and scores potential customers using Google Maps, Companies House, and LLM-based ranking. It generates personalized outreach materials and syncs results to Google Sheets for follow-up.

## Key Features
- Automated discovery of UK businesses via Google Maps and Companies House
- LLM-powered scoring (0-100) with plain-English reasoning for each lead
- Website enrichment and status analysis (e.g., broken, outdated, weak conversion)
- Personalized outreach materials (Loom videos, WhatsApp messages) with call window optimization
- Real-time dashboard for lead management, configuration, and pipeline execution

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LeadHound streamlines the tedious process of prospecting and outreach, enabling AI engineers to focus on building and refining RAG systems rather than manual lead generation.
