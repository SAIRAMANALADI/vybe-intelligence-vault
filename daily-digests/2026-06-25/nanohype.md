## Summary
A tool-agnostic template catalog for AI-focused projects, providing self-contained, scaffoldable starting points for building AI systems, applications, infrastructure, and composable modules. It includes pre-configured composites for rapid prototyping and deployment.

## Key Features
- Comprehensive template catalog spanning AI systems, applications, infrastructure, and composable modules
- Tool-agnostic design with a standardized template contract for interoperability
- Pre-configured composites for rapid prototyping (e.g., RAG agents, multi-agent systems)
- Support for multiple languages (TypeScript, Python, Go, Java) and frameworks
- Includes guardrails, eval harnesses, MCP servers, and vector store modules for AI stack building

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates AI engineering by providing reusable, modular templates and composites that reduce boilerplate and streamline the development of RAG and agent-based systems.
