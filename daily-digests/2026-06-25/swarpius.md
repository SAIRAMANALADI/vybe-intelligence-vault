## Summary
Swarpius is an LLM-driven assistant for Roon that enables natural language control of music playback, library navigation, and multi-zone management through multi-step tool calling. It integrates web search, optional TTS, and real-time diagnostics for advanced music interaction and conversation analysis.

## Key Features
- Natural language music control (play, pause, queue, skip, seek, shuffle, zone transfer)
- Multi-step library navigation with fuzzy matching and zone grouping
- Optional web search integration for external knowledge retrieval
- Real-time diagnostics, conversation analysis, and cost tracking
- Persistent chat history and listening history with date-based browsing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
