## Summary
A specialized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) bot named '原寶' designed to assist students and faculty at National Chengchi University's Indigenous Student Resource Center (ISRC) with queries about indigenous resources, scholarships, tuition waivers, housing rights, and cultural activities.

## Key Features
- Dual-topic selection for focused retrieval (Campus Resources or Indigenous Issues)
- Automatic category classification into 5 subcategories for precise metadata filtering
- Multi-turn conversational agent with intelligent tool calling (LlamaIndex AgentWorkflow)
- Metadata-based filtering in Qdrant for accurate document retrieval
- Real-time streaming responses with conversation logging to Google Sheets
