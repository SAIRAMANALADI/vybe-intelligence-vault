## Summary
A portable content operations toolkit for AllinCMS designed for both humans and AI agents to transform raw materials into audited, published web content through a structured workflow involving drafting, adversarial review, and SEO optimization.

## Key Features
- Structured content pipeline from raw materials to published web content
- Automated adversarial review process with reviewer isolation
- Multi-agent collaboration via MCP and IDE integrations
- Living wiki layering for knowledge distillation and traceability
- Strict publishing criteria with scoring and audit trails

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a rigorous, AI-agent-friendly framework for content creation and review that ensures high-quality, SEO-optimized outputs with built-in adversarial validation, critical for RAG systems requiring reliable source material.
