## Summary
An AI-powered patent creation and analysis system for Claude Code that integrates hybrid RAG search over patent regulations (MPEP, 35 USC, 37 CFR), BigQuery access to 100M+ patents, automated compliance checks, and diagram generation. Designed to streamline patent drafting, prior art searches, and regulatory compliance for USPTO, EPO, or PCT filings.

## Key Features
- Hybrid RAG search over MPEP, 35 USC, and 37 CFR with citations for instant regulatory insights
- BigQuery integration for searching 100M+ patents by keywords, CPC/IPC codes, or full-text queries
- Automated compliance checks for USPTO (35 USC 112) and EPO (Art. 84 EPC) with specific feedback
- Guided workflow for drafting complete patent applications from invention disclosure to filing-ready documents
- Patent-style diagram generation (block diagrams, flowcharts) via Graphviz integration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates patent drafting and compliance checking by automating regulatory research, prior art searches, and document generation, reducing manual effort and improving accuracy for AI-driven legal workflows.
