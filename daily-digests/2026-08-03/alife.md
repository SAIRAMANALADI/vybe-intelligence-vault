## Summary
Alife is a desktop AI companion agent designed to simulate a cybernetic life form with a focus on low-cost, transparent, and highly extensible interactions. It combines LLM-driven conversation, multi-modal capabilities, and autonomous behavior to create a persistent, evolving AI partner.

## Key Features
- Low token overhead with optimized prompts and context management for cost efficiency
- Highly extensible plugin architecture with hot-reload support for real-time customization
- Persistent, long-term memory system with source-level traceability and compression
- Multi-modal capabilities including Live2D desk pet, vision, speech, and autonomous activities
- White-box design with transparent context and execution processes for full user control

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Alife provides a unique, low-cost framework for building persistent, autonomous AI companions with deep customization and multi-modal interaction capabilities, making it ideal for RAG builders seeking to integrate lifelike, evolving agents into their systems.
