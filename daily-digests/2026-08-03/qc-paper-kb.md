## Summary
QC-Paper-KB is a Windows desktop application that curates and organizes a large knowledge base of quantum computing research papers, enabling users to search, analyze trends, and discover relevant work efficiently. It integrates semantic search and LLM tools to assist with literature reviews and research workflows.

## Key Features
- Curated database of 20,000+ quantum computing papers from 25+ venues
- Semantic search and topic-based browsing for efficient paper discovery
- Trend analysis and venue recommendations to identify active research areas
- LLM integration for AI-assisted research and paper summarization
- Local Windows desktop application with offline access and sync capabilities

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, searchable, and AI-ready knowledge base of quantum computing literature, reducing manual effort for researchers building RAG systems or conducting literature reviews.
