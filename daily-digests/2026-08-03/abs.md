## Summary
Automatia ABS is a self-hosted AI orchestrator designed to automate and optimize AI workflows by routing requests across multiple providers, offering 100+ MCP tools, and ensuring data privacy. It provides a cost-effective alternative to expensive SaaS solutions with a one-time fee and lifetime use.

## Key Features
- 6-provider cascade with circuit breaker and cost dashboard for optimized AI model routing
- 121+ out-of-the-box MCP tools including RAG hybrid, judge ML, and fullstack developer mode
- Self-hosted deployment with Docker Compose and Caddy for automatic Let's Encrypt SSL
- Stripe-ready billing with JWT licensing, refund flow, and customer portal
- Hybrid RAG retrieval with support for multiple embedding backends and encrypted secrets

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ABS simplifies AI workflow automation by providing a self-hosted, cost-effective orchestrator with multi-provider support and built-in RAG capabilities, ensuring data privacy and reducing dependency on expensive SaaS solutions.
