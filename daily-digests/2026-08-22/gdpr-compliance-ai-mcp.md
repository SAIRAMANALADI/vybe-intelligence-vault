## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server designed to automate GDPR compliance tasks such as Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA), Article 30 record-keeping, and automated decision-making audits. It provides audit-ready, HMAC-signed attestations for compliance validation under the EU AI Act.

## Key Features
- Automates GDPR compliance tasks including DPIA, Article 30 records, and automated decision-making audits
- Generates HMAC-signed attestations for audit validation without requiring an account
- Supports pay-per-call billing (£0.05/call) with top-up tokens and USDC payments
- Built-in EU AI Act compliance (Articles 9, 13, 15, 26, 50)
- Integrates with MCP clients like Claude Desktop and supports PyPI distribution

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides critical GDPR and EU AI Act compliance automation for RAG/AI systems, ensuring audit-ready proof and reducing legal risk for deployments.
