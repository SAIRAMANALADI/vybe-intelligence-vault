## Summary
ML Research Radar is a comprehensive platform for discovering, organizing, and evaluating machine learning research papers and artifacts. It combines a validated local research-discovery system with a documented roadmap toward advanced retrieval, RAG, and deployable infrastructure.

## Key Features
- Canonical paper corpus with multi-source reconciliation and identity resolution
- Hybrid retrieval (BM25, dense, and hybrid) with Golden Set evaluation
- Artifact enrichment plane (GitHub, Hugging Face) with paper-artifact links
- Topic clustering and UMAP projection for discovery and navigation
- Experimental Qdrant integration with parity validation and performance benchmarks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a robust, paper-centric pipeline for organizing and retrieving ML research, enabling RAG builders to integrate curated, high-quality datasets and retrieval mechanisms into their systems.
