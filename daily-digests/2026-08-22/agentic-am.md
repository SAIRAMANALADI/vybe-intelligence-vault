## Summary
A replicable agentic operating system for account and project managers that transforms an Obsidian Markdown vault into a second brain. It automates task management, daily/weekly brief generation, and entity graph maintenance by ingesting data from Slack, calendar, meetings, and inboxes via Claude Code.

## Key Features
- Automated ingestion of tasks from Slack, calendar, meetings, and inboxes into a structured task board
- Generation of coaching-grade daily/weekly briefs ranked by stakeholder priority
- Maintenance of an entity graph for people and companies with current understanding and historical timelines
- Modular system with clear separation of sources, wiki, and schema layers
- Clone-and-go template with a fictional worked example for immediate deployment

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, agentic framework for automating critical workflows in AI-driven account and project management, reducing manual overhead and ensuring consistency in task tracking and reporting.
