## Summary
Melo is an AI-native voice agent platform designed to revolutionize human-machine interaction by making voice the foundational interface. It enables continuous, full-duplex conversations with agents that listen, reason, and speak in real-time, treating voice as the primary medium for interaction, creation, and collaboration.

## Key Features
- Full-duplex conversation with continuous listening and speaking
- Long-term voice memory and adaptive interaction based on tone and cadence
- Parallel background task execution without pausing dialogue
- Open model layer for pluggable ASR, TTS, and voice cloning models
- Creation studio for voice-driven audio composition and editing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Melo redefines AI interaction by centering voice as the primary interface, enabling more natural and continuous human-machine collaboration for RAG and agent systems.
