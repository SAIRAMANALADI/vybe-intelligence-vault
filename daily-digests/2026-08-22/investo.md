## Summary
Investo is an AI-powered investment-analysis agent that operates as an MCP server, providing comprehensive financial analysis, valuation, and risk assessment for companies (primarily Indian NSE/BSE-listed, but also global). It integrates with AI clients like Claude or Cursor to deliver structured data, reports, and investment ratings grounded in financial metrics, competitor comparisons, and qualitative insights.

## Key Features
- Automated company analysis with 15+ structured data points (financials, ratios, DCF, moat, risks, etc.)
- Integration with AI clients (Claude, Cursor) via MCP for real-time, agent-driven insights
- Generates self-contained research notes (HTML/PDF) with evidence layers and confidence scores
- Supports Indian (NSE/BSE) and global companies with fallback data sources
- Provides a 0–100 investment rating and Warren Buffett checklist for quality assessment

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Investo streamlines the process of generating high-quality, data-driven investment research for AI agents and analysts, reducing manual effort and improving decision-making with structured financial insights.
