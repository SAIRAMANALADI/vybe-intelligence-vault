## Summary
The MCP Leaderboard ranks the agent-readiness of every public MCP server in the official registry by scoring them on 10 agent-readiness checks, such as schema validity, tool naming, and discovery surfaces. The rankings are auto-refreshed weekly using the mcp-scorecard engine.

## Key Features
- Auto-refreshes weekly to rank all public MCP servers in the official registry
- Scores servers on 10 agent-readiness checks including schema validity and tool naming
- Provides a live badge for servers to display their ranking
- Uses mcp-scorecard engine to evaluate servers in isolated child processes
- Open-source and MIT licensed for transparency and community contributions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a neutral, automated way to evaluate and rank MCP servers by their agent-readiness, helping AI engineers and developers quickly identify high-quality, reliable servers for their RAG and agentic systems.
