## Summary
A production-grade, multi-tenant RAG platform designed for enterprise use, featuring asynchronous, resumable document ingestion, hybrid retrieval with reranking, and cited streaming responses. The platform prioritizes correctness under failure, tenant isolation, swappable components, and observability.

## Key Features
- Resumable and idempotent document ingestion pipeline with checkpointing
- Multi-tenant isolation enforced at the repository layer
- Pluggable architecture for embeddings, LLMs, vector stores, and rerankers
- Comprehensive observability with distributed tracing and cost attribution
- Evaluation gates for RAG quality regression prevention

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a robust, enterprise-ready foundation for building scalable and reliable RAG systems with critical features like fault tolerance, tenant isolation, and measurable quality control.
