## Summary
zorp is a research agent framework designed for scientific discovery and evidence-based investigation. It transforms uncertain questions into defensible answers by structuring investigations, recording evidence, and ensuring traceability of claims to sources.

## Key Features
- Pre-registration of hypotheses and kill thresholds for tamper-evident investigations
- Structured evidence records with git-backed tracking for accountability
- Modular architecture with core primitives (tracks, evidence records, checkpoints)
- Four research capabilities: validate, investigate, co-write, and deliver
- MCP integration for tooling, memory, and external system connectivity

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
zorp provides a rigorous framework for grounding AI-generated answers in verifiable evidence, making it essential for building reliable RAG systems that require transparency and traceability.
