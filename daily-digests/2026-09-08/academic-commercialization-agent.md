## Summary
A multi-agent AI system built on CrewAI that evaluates the commercialization readiness of academic research by analyzing academic literature, patents, and market intelligence to produce structured reports with quantitative scoring and verifiable citations.

## Key Features
- Six specialized AI agents for parallelized research analysis (academic, patent, market, reporting, review, scoring)
- Deterministic pre-run source validation with URL reachability checks and structured evidence collection
- Quantitative commercialization scoring with traceable citations and JSON scorecards
- Multilingual report generation (Chinese, Japanese, German, French, etc.) with localized formatting
- Benchmark suite for validating scoring accuracy and consistency across 10 preset research topics

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, evidence-backed framework for assessing the commercial viability of academic research, enabling RAG builders to integrate commercialization intelligence into their pipelines.
