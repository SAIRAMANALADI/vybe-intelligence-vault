## Summary
LoopGrid is a tamper-evident evidence plane for AI agents that captures, seals, and verifies consequential decisions, including agent identity, delegated authority, model context, policies, human oversight, tool actions, observed outcomes, and cryptographic proof. It enables independent verification of AI decision-making processes for governance, audit, and dispute resolution.

## Key Features
- Tamper-evident, signed evidence chains for AI decisions with cryptographic proof
- Support for FULL, REDACTED, and PROOF-ONLY privacy modes for data protection
- Workspace-scoped RBAC, API keys, and human review workflows for governance
- Portable Evidence Bundle v2 format with offline verification capabilities
- Integrations with Python, TypeScript/JavaScript SDKs, OpenTelemetry/OTLP, and MCP

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LoopGrid provides the critical missing layer of verifiable evidence for AI agent decisions, enabling trust, auditability, and dispute resolution in production AI systems.
