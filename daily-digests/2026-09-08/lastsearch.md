## Summary
LastSearch provides research infrastructure for AI agents, enabling real-time web search with evidence extraction, verification, and structured citations. It ensures every claim is backed by a URL with confidence scoring, eliminating hallucinations through a multi-step verification pipeline.

## Key Features
- Multi-step verification pipeline with atomic claim decomposition and hybrid retrieval
- Confidence scoring based on evidence, domain authority, and consensus
- Three depth modes (fast, thorough, deep) for varying research needs
- Persistent research sessions with knowledge accumulation and sharing
- Self-improving accuracy through adaptive thresholds and feedback loops
