## Summary
A project shaping skill based on Basecamp's Shape Up methodology, designed to transform vague product ideas into well-scoped, bet-worthy projects with clear boundaries, deliverables, and validation criteria. Tailored for AI agents, product teams, and independent developers to prevent scope creep and misaligned execution.

## Key Features
- Structured project pitching with Problem, Appetite, Solution, Rabbit Holes, and No-Gos
- Six-stage workflow: Shape → Bet → Kickoff → Build → Scope Review → Cycle Review
- Decision rubrics and evidence gates for validating project viability
- Prevents scope creep and infinite backlogs through appetite-based planning
- Templates and scripts for automating Pitch, Bet Review, and Agent Brief generation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI projects are scoped correctly before development begins, reducing the high opportunity cost of misaligned execution in AI agent-driven workflows.
