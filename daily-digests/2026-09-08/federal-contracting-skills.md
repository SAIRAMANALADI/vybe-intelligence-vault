## Summary
A Windows-based toolkit for federal contracting professionals that automates the creation of key acquisition documents like IGCEs, SOWs, and PWSs, while integrating live data from federal sources such as USASpending, GSA CALC+, and BLS OEWS.

## Key Features
- Automated IGCE (Independent Government Cost Estimate) builder for FFP, T&M, and cost-reimbursement contracts
- SOW (Statement of Work) and PWS (Performance Work Statement) drafting tools with structured templates
- Live data integration from USASpending, GSA CALC+, BLS OEWS, and other federal sources
- Market research report generation for acquisition planning and file support
- Windows-native application with simple setup and user-friendly interface

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the creation of critical federal contracting documents and leverages live public data to reduce manual research and drafting time for acquisition professionals.
