## Summary
OpenShard is a CLI tool designed to create durable receipts for AI coding agent workflows, providing control, verification, and audit trails for AI-driven development tasks. It records and manages the execution of AI agents, ensuring transparency, risk assessment, and cost tracking for completed engineering work.

## Key Features
- Records AI coding runs with detailed receipts (Shards) including model, cost, changes, and checks
- Routes and gates AI tasks based on risk classification (low, medium, high)
- Provides local TUI and CLI for inspecting, reflecting, and generating PR comments from runs
- Supports read-only review flows with immutable receipts and fingerprint verification
- Integrates with AI coding agents (e.g., Claude Code) to capture sessions without cloud dependency

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
OpenShard provides essential auditability and control for AI-driven development, ensuring every engineering task leaves a verifiable receipt for inspection, compliance, and continuous improvement.
