## Summary
flightdeck is a reference implementation for governing and measuring enterprise AI adoption through evidence-based workflows, governed runs, and auditable ROI trails. It treats AI adoption as a product, ensuring every workflow declares baselines, every run is recorded, and metrics are computed purely from code rather than estimates.

## Key Features
- Governed AI workflows with fail-closed policies, budget gates, and data classification routing
- Tamper-evident audit ledger for immutable evidence trails and compliance verification
- Conservative ROI metrics computed purely from code, rejecting estimates or model-generated claims
- Vendor-neutral architecture supporting Anthropic, OpenAI/Azure, and custom providers via adapters
- Executive dashboard and terminal reports for adoption, cost, and performance tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, auditable framework for measuring and governing AI adoption, ensuring ROI claims are defensible and aligned with business outcomes.
