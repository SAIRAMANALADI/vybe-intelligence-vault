## Summary
BidGuard Tender Comparison Hub is a desktop application designed to analyze procurement tender documents offline, detecting bid-rigging risks, contradictions, and generating verifiable evidence reports for compliance and auditing purposes.

## Key Features
- Offline-first analysis with no data transmission to external servers
- Automated detection of bid-rigging indicators like identical pricing or metadata
- Contradiction finder for inconsistencies in tender specifications or timelines
- Local storage and PDF report generation for audit trails
- User-friendly drag-and-drop interface for document loading and analysis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
BidGuard provides a critical offline tool for procurement professionals to detect fraud and inconsistencies in tender documents, ensuring compliance and reducing risks without relying on cloud-based solutions.
