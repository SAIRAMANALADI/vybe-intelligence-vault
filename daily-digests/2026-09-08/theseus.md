## Summary
Theseus is a local Small Language Model (SLM) research assistant designed to operate on persistent Chaos research environments. It enables interactive intelligence for acquiring knowledge, reasoning, computation, and active research work without relying on remote AI services.

## Key Features
- Local SLM inference with privacy and control
- Integration with Chaos for persistent research state management
- Deterministic computation delegation for accuracy
- Natural-language interaction and reasoning over research projects
- Modular tool use for web search, data analysis, and knowledge management

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
