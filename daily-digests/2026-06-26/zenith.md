## Summary
Zenith is a plain-text design document format and engine that enables AI agents to safely create, edit, and validate design files like posters, decks, and diagrams. It compiles `.zen` files deterministically to pixel-exact PNGs or print-ready PDFs.

## Key Features
- Plain-text `.zen` files for human-readable and diffable design documents
- Deterministic compilation to PNG/PDF with pixel-exact output
- Built-in validation and diagnostics for safety and correctness
- Agent-native design with stable IDs and transaction-based edits
- Support for tokens, styles, multi-page documents, and advanced features like gradients, shadows, and charts

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Zenith enables AI agents to safely and programmatically edit design files, bridging the gap between code and visual design for automation and collaboration.
