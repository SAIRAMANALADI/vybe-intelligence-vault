## Summary
A curated radar tracking offensive AI-security research, tools, and trends across two fronts: AI-driven offensive security (e.g., LLM red-teaming, autonomous pentesting) and attacks against AI systems (e.g., jailbreaking, prompt injection, model extraction). The repository aggregates primary-source trends, research papers, and a watchlist refreshed via automated daily scans.

- Structured output with source-of-truth documentation and logs
