## Summary
Clutch is an AI-powered productivity companion that autonomously plans, prioritizes, schedules, and executes tasks to help users meet deadlines. It leverages Google Gemini for function-calling, multimodal input, and web research to generate deliverables and manage commitments proactively.

## Key Features
- Gemini-powered function-calling agent with 8 real-time tools for task decomposition, prioritization, and deliverable generation
- Multimodal input support (text, voice, photo capture, and handwriting recognition)
- Proactive scheduling with reality-check capacity analysis and slippage nudges
- Live web research via Google Search grounding for accurate task completion
- Offline-capable PWA with Google Sign-In, insights dashboard, and custom theming
