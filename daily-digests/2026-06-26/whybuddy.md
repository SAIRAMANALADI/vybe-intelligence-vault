## Summary
WhyBuddy (now SlideRule) is a product rehearsal engine that transforms a single sentence idea into a complete, exportable product plan including specs, architecture, route planning, and UI mockups. It automates the early-stage product development process with deterministic scripts and evidence trails.

## Key Features
- Converts one-sentence ideas into full product specs in minutes
- Deterministic scripts guarantee structural validity and coverage
- Portable Skill package embeddable in Trae, Claude, or other agent hosts
- Evidence trails and provenance labels for all generated artifacts
- Supports image generation, route planning, and traceability matrices

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates early-stage product ideation by automating spec generation and validation, reducing the time from idea to decision from weeks to minutes while ensuring structural integrity and traceability.
