## Summary
An AI-powered Agent Skill designed to transform vague learning goals into structured, actionable courses by anchoring objectives, researching reliable sources, generating tailored curricula, and guiding users through interactive learning with reinforcement and review scheduling.

## Key Features
- Automated curriculum generation based on user-defined goals, constraints, and materials
- Integration of reliable sources (official docs, textbooks, high-quality tutorials) into structured courses
- Interactive learning with local course player for visualization and practice
- Adaptive learning modes (speedrun, systematic study, interview prep, exam review)
- Progress tracking and spaced repetition for long-term retention

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, AI-driven approach to converting raw learning goals into actionable, high-quality curricula with built-in reinforcement, making it essential for AI agents tasked with personalized education or skill acquisition.
