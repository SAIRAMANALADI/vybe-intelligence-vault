## Summary
Insight Engine is a plugin for Claude Code and Claude Cowork designed to analyze high-stakes, complex problems with an assurance layer that includes source-tiered verification, disconfirmation checks, grade-locked decision briefs, and audience-specific rendering. It adheres to ICD-203 analytic tradecraft standards to ensure defensible and verified insights.

## Key Features
- Source-tiered web verification with disconfirmation checks to ensure accuracy
- Grade-locked decision briefs with strength tiers (V1/V2/V3) for load-bearing claims
- Audience-specific rendering without altering claims or grades
- Proportionality triage and systems-investigation pass for feedback-driven problems
- Architected around ICD-203 analytic tradecraft standards for defensible insights

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, verifiable, and defensible approach to analyzing high-stakes problems, ensuring AI-generated insights are reliable and actionable for decision-makers.
