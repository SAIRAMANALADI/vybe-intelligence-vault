## Summary
AgentDesk is an open-source AI Agent customer support system designed for teams needing knowledge-based answers, human handoff, ticket workflows, and self-hosted deployment. It combines AI-first support with structured human collaboration and ticket management in a single platform.

## Key Features
- AI-first support with knowledge-grounded answers and fallback to human agents
- Built-in ticket system for conversation-to-ticket tracking and status flows
- Knowledge base RAG with Answerability Gate for reliable AI responses
- Agent workspace with conversation management, team collaboration, and ticket creation
- Extensible architecture supporting Skills, MCP, and OpenAI-compatible model providers

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AgentDesk provides a production-ready foundation for AI-powered customer support systems, combining RAG, human handoff, and ticket workflows in a single self-hostable platform.
