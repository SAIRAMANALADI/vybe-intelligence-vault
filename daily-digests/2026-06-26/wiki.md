## Summary
A personal knowledge management system designed for AI agents to compile, organize, and refine knowledge over time. It emphasizes write-time integration of information rather than runtime retrieval, ensuring compounding accuracy and reducing redundant derivations.

## Key Features
- Schema-driven knowledge ingestion with cross-referencing and updates
- Query writeback mechanism to refine and expand the wiki after searches
- Periodic linting for stale content, contradictions, and missing references
- Inspired by Karpathy's LLM Wiki for compile-time knowledge accumulation
- Designed for AI agents to carry knowledge forward between sessions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, self-improving system for AI agents to accumulate and refine knowledge over time, reducing redundant derivations and improving accuracy through compounding.
