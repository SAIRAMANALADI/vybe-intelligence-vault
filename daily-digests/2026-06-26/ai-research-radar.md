## Summary
AI Research Radar is a curated ledger tracking emerging trends, research breakthroughs, and engineering advancements in the AI ecosystem. It serves as a real-time dashboard for AI researchers and systems engineers to monitor accelerating and emerging topics like low-bit quantization, agent security, and diffusion language models.

## Key Features
- Tracks and categorizes AI research trends by stage (emerging, accelerating, etc.)
- Provides evidence-backed links to papers, benchmarks, and tools
- Includes pinned topics for critical areas like low-bit quantization and agent security
- Aggregates community sentiment from platforms like Hacker News and Reddit
- Maintains a structured ledger (TRENDS.md) for transparency and traceability
