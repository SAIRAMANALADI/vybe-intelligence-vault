## Summary
Symbiont is an open-core company operating system designed for hybrid teams of humans and AI agents, addressing ten fundamental asymmetries between humans and agents. It provides a registry, MCP server, and specification for agent-to-agent discovery and coordination.

## Key Features
- Public registry for publishing and querying `agents.json` specifications
- MCP server for agent discovery and interaction via HTTP+JSON-RPC2
- Ten facilities addressing asymmetries between humans and agents
- Open-core model with free foundation and paid managed services
- Machine-readable status and operational metrics
