## Summary
AI Radar is an automated AI technology intelligence system designed as a 4-pillar knowledge graph for AI product managers. It aggregates and visualizes real-time data on AI advancements across technology, product patterns, tool ecosystems, and commercial dynamics.

## Key Features
- Self-evolving knowledge graph with 1,977 nodes and 221,022 edges tracking AI advancements
- Four-pillar categorization: technology, product patterns, tool ecosystem, and commercial dynamics
- Interactive visualization and downloadable graph data for deeper analysis
- Automated updates every 6 hours with 88% Chinese content coverage
- RSS feed and daily summaries for real-time AI intelligence tracking
