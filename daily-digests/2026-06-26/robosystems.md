## Summary
RoboSystems is an open-source, AI-native financial intelligence platform that unifies structured financial data, document search, and AI memory into a ledger-grade system of record. It enables AI agents and analysts to query, operate, and close financial books while integrating accounting, investment management, and SEC data.

## Key Features
- Unified financial system of record for AI agents and analysts with ledger-grade capabilities
- Modular extensions (RoboLedger, RoboInvestor) for accounting, reporting, and investment management
- AI Operator System with automatic credit tracking and SSE progress streaming
- Integrated vector search (LanceDB/OpenSearch) for semantic financial data retrieval
- CQRS-based operations kernel with GraphQL and REST APIs for reads/writes
