## Summary
A Windows-based AI tool that assists job seekers in preparing for interviews by analyzing job descriptions, comparing them with resumes, generating mock interview questions, and providing feedback and scoring to identify improvement areas.

## Key Features
- Job description and resume skill matching
- Multi-round mock interview practice with AI
- Real-time scoring and feedback after each session
- Detailed summary report generation for improvement tracking
- Windows-native deployment with minimal setup required
