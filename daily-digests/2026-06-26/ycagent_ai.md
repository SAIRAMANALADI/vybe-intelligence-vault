## Summary
A discovery and research platform for the Y Combinator ecosystem that enables users to explore, search, and research YC companies using an autonomous agent. The agent generates evidence-backed dossiers with source traceability and real-time streaming updates.

## Key Features
- Explore 5,600+ YC companies with advanced filtering and natural language search
- Autonomous research agent that crawls public sources for company intelligence
- Real-time streaming of agent activity, including live VNC browser view
- Hybrid semantic/lexical search via pgvector in Neon Postgres
- Budget-bounded, cancellable research runs with diminishing-returns stopping rules

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a scalable, evidence-backed research agent for extracting structured intelligence from unstructured public data, critical for RAG pipelines needing fresh, traceable company insights.
