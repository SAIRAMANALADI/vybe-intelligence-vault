## Summary
PaperMentor is an AI agent skill designed to debug and deepen understanding of research papers and slides by breaking down equations, derivations, dependencies, and conceptual confusion interactively. It provides a structured, step-by-step approach to reconstructing the core insights of a paper without summarizing, enabling users to reconstruct the source material themselves.

## Key Features
- Interactive debugging of equations, derivations, and dependencies in research papers or slides
- One-line installation for Codex, Claude Code, or local CLI usage
- HTML-first reading room with LaTeX rendering and bundled fonts for offline use
- Structured session state management with portable HTML/PDF/zip report exports
- Visual repair actions for structural confusion (e.g., method pipelines, dependency graphs)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PaperMentor enables RAG builders to create systems that not only retrieve information but also debug and explain complex mathematical and conceptual dependencies, making it essential for building AI agents that can deeply understand and reconstruct technical documents.
