## Summary
AriaType is a voice-driven desktop application that enables hands-free writing, input, and cross-app work by converting spoken language into context-aware text directly at the cursor. It supports local and cloud-based AI processing, multilingual input, and customizable workflows for drafting, editing, and replying in real-time.

## Key Features
- Real-time voice dictation with noise reduction and VAD for stable input
- Context-aware text generation that adapts to the active app and task
- Local and cloud AI polish for grammar, punctuation, and wording refinement
- Customizable shortcuts, templates, and dictionary management for personalized workflows
- Privacy-focused with optional cloud service integration and local model support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AriaType streamlines AI-powered voice workflows for desktop users, reducing friction in drafting, editing, and replying tasks while maintaining privacy and adaptability to diverse use cases.
