## Summary
Research Hub is an automated knowledge management pipeline for academic research that unifies diverse input sources (WeChat articles, Bilibili videos, local files, Zotero papers) into structured analysis notes. It enforces quality control, self-learning mechanisms, and structured output templates to enhance knowledge retrieval and iterative improvement.

## Key Features
- Unified input processing for videos, text, and papers with automatic routing
- Five-module structured output (summary, framework, insights, learning, connections) with strict quality gates
- 7-dimensional self-learning mechanism for continuous knowledge base improvement
- Mermaid.js visualization integration for logic diagrams in notes
- Obsidian-compatible output with dual-link support and frontmatter tagging

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Research Hub streamlines the transformation of unstructured academic content into high-quality, retrievable knowledge assets, reducing manual effort in RAG pipeline construction.
