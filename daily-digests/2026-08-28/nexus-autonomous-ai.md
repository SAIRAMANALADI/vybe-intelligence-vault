## Summary
NEXUS is an autonomous AI agent that independently discovers, evaluates, and publishes technical insights about emerging AI developments without human prompts. - Autonomous topic discovery from live AI sources like ArXiv and Hacker News
- Signal detection and convergence analysis to identify meaningful AI trends
- Editorial decision engine for publishing, monitoring, or rejecting topics
- Consistent AI persona with structured technical analysis output
- Long-term memory system to avoid duplication and maintain continuity
