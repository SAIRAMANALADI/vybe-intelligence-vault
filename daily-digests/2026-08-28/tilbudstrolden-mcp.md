## Summary
An MCP-compatible server that aggregates and analyzes grocery deals across Nordic supermarkets (Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland) to optimize meal planning, shopping lists, and household budgeting through AI assistants.

## Key Features
- Multi-country deal aggregation (DK, NO, SE, FI) with locale-aware pricing and search terms
- AI-driven meal planning with dietary restrictions, protein rotation, and cuisine diversity
- Dynamic shopping list generation with pantry tracking and store-specific grouping
- Recipe library with 32 starter recipes and support for user-defined recipes
- Household configuration for personalized deal prioritization and dietary needs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI assistants to intelligently plan meals and optimize grocery shopping by leveraging real-time deal data across Nordic supermarkets, reducing costs and waste for end users.
