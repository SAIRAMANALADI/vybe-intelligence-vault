## Summary
Evidence Gate is a lightweight, zero-dependency library that acts as a pre-generation gate for LLM/RAG pipelines, ensuring AI responses are grounded in actual, high-quality, and fresh data. It validates evidence before generation and provides audit trails for compliance and debugging.

## Key Features
- Pre-generation evidence validation to prevent hallucinations or unsupported claims
- Domain-agnostic with customizable presets (e.g., finance, healthcare, support)
- Audit trails with decision logging and tamper-evident chains for compliance
- Provenance tracking to verify data sources and transformations
- Verification loop to ensure AI responses cite valid evidence without model calls

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It prevents AI from inventing facts by validating evidence before generation, ensuring responses are accurate, traceable, and compliant with domain-specific rules.
