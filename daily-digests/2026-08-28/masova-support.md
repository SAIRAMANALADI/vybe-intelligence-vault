## Summary
A customer support and operations agent system for the MaSoVa restaurant platform, leveraging Google ADK and Gemini to handle support chats, order management, and operational tasks like inventory and demand forecasting.

## Key Features
- AI-powered support chat with JWT authentication and tool integration
- 7 specialized ops agents for demand forecasting, inventory, churn, and dynamic pricing
- Human-in-the-loop workflows with manager approval for critical actions
- Shared AgentRuntime with policy enforcement, audit logs, and fallback mechanisms
- Modular architecture with FastAPI, APScheduler, and optional Redis/RabbitMQ integrations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides a production-ready agent framework for AI-driven customer support and operations in the restaurant industry, emphasizing safety and human oversight.
