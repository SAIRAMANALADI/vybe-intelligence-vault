## Summary
An MCP server that integrates with Immich to enable AI-powered photo management through natural language commands. It allows AI assistants to search, organize, deduplicate, and curate photo libraries, albums, and metadata without manual scripting.

## Key Features
- Natural language photo search and organization via CLIP and GPS data
- Automated geographic album creation combining GPS, temporal, and visual matching
- Bulk metadata repair (timestamps, GPS, timezones) and cleanup (duplicates, screenshots)
- Interactive gallery generation with embedded thumbnails and batch operations
- Full asset lifecycle management (trash, restore, delete) and face/people management

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges the gap between Immich's storage capabilities and AI-driven photo management, enabling RAG builders to automate complex photo library tasks through natural language commands while maintaining full self-hosted control.
