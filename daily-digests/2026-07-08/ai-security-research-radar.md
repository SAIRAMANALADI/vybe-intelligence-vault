## Summary
AI Security Research Radar is an autonomous tracker of the offensive AI-security frontier, monitoring attacks on AI systems and AI-driven offensive security research. It aggregates and analyzes emerging threats, vulnerabilities, and defensive mechanisms in the AI security landscape.

## Key Features
- Real-time tracking of AI security trends and emerging threats
- Autonomous aggregation of research papers, blog posts, and community discussions
- Structured categorization of attacks (e.g., MCP exploits, jailbreaks, backdoors)
- Weekly/daily reports with evidence-backed insights and references
- Watchlist for high-priority threats and defensive mechanisms
