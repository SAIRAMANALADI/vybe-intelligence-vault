## Summary
SwarmAI is a self-evolving Agent OS designed to enable a single human to direct an AI team, where every interaction compounds the system's cognition rather than just its templates. It replaces traditional agent-memory systems with a continuous, self-correcting, and decay-aware knowledge layer that evolves structurally over time.

## Key Features
- Continuous cognition across sessions with self-healing and warm starts
- Structural evolution via cognitive patching (DDD, gates, and decay-aware knowledge)
- 13 modular engines (e.g., Memory Pipeline, Autonomous Pipeline, Pollinate, Eval OS) forming a compounding feedback loop
- Decay-aware knowledge retention with Darwinian elimination of unreferenced data
- System-level evaluation (Eval OS) as a git-bound regression gate for convergence proof

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
