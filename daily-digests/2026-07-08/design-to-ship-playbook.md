## Summary
A strategic AI-powered blueprint generator that transforms raw product ideas into executable launch plans, including PRDs, system architectures, GTM strategies, and AI agent prompts. It bridges the gap between conceptualization and execution by creating interconnected, deployable blueprints tailored to team constraints and market needs.

## Key Features
- Generates living Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) with interconnected system architectures, GTM strategies, and resource estimates
- AI-driven estimation engine that produces developer hours, costs, and timelines with confidence intervals
- Agent Prompt Factory creates task-specific instructions for AI coding assistants, ensuring executable outputs
- Dual-AI integration (OpenAI + Claude) for balanced creativity and technical rigor
- Cross-references regulatory, budget, and team constraints to produce realistic, deployable blueprints

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates the transition from product ideation to market-ready execution by automating the creation of interconnected blueprints that AI coding agents can directly implement, reducing time-to-launch and minimizing misalignment between teams.
