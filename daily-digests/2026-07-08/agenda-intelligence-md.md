## Summary
Agenda Intelligence MD is an evidence-readiness and trust-routing runtime designed to prepare high-stakes AI-assisted decisions for human review. It analyzes documents like RFPs, vendor documentation, or risk memos to identify supported claims, weak or unsafe assertions, missing evidence, and next owner actions, ensuring files are ready for committee review or escalation.

## Key Features
- Converts unstructured documents (RFP, vendor docs, risk memos) into structured human-review packets with evidence gaps, weak claims, and decision-readiness scores
- Supports multiple delivery surfaces: MCP server, HTTP API, A2A adapter, and Cloudflare Workers for flexible integration
- Provides deterministic analysis with schema validation, evidence auditing, and scoring (0-100) for document readiness
- Includes live portfolio demos (e.g., Middle Corridor Deal Risk Gate) for real-world triage and escalation routing
- Offers confidential workflow profiles (e.g., strategic-infrastructure-bankability) for high-stakes, alias-only analysis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the critical pre-review phase for high-stakes AI decisions by automating evidence-readiness checks, reducing manual effort and ensuring only vetted information reaches human reviewers.
