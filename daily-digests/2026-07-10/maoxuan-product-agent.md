## Summary
Maoxuan Product Agent is a specialized AI agent for Chinese internet product decision-making, distilled from Mao Zedong's philosophical works 《矛盾论》 and 《实践论》. It provides structured, actionable advice for product managers, growth teams, and business leaders to resolve complex product challenges by identifying core bottlenecks, dominant mechanisms, and minimal effective actions.

## Key Features
- Structured decision-making process based on《矛盾论》and《实践论》without philosophical jargon in outputs
- Identifies core bottlenecks and dominant mechanisms in product challenges
- Provides minimal effective actions with clear success/failure criteria
- Includes 12 real-world product decision case studies for validation
- Supports integration with Codex, Claude Code, and Cursor via CLI or manual installation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a rigorous, philosophy-backed framework for AI agents to make nuanced product decisions, reducing ambiguity in complex scenarios and ensuring actionable, context-aware outputs for RAG/AI stack builders.
