## Summary
SpectreGraph is an autonomous OSINT intelligence engine that builds dynamic knowledge graphs for AI assistants, resolving entity ambiguities and providing contextual intelligence through semantic relationship mapping and multi-agent collaboration.

## Key Features
- Autonomous entity disambiguation resolving ambiguities like 'Apple' (fruit, company, surname)
- Dynamic graph pruning to maintain freshness and accuracy of intelligence
- Multi-agent collaboration integrating OpenAI and Claude for consensus-based reasoning
- Semantic relationship mapping for contextual understanding beyond simple entity extraction
- Responsive UI dashboard with real-time graph visualization and multilingual support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
