A companion repository for BIG HERM that hosts optional, niche, or template-rich skill packs for advanced workflows like creative media, MLOps, office integrations, and research authoring. - Designed to avoid bloating the base BIG HERM distribution
- Includes security scanning and compatibility verification for raw URL installs
