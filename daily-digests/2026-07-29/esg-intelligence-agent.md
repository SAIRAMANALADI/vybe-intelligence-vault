## Summary
An autonomous AI agent that scans ESG reports, construction news, and sustainability regulations to generate daily PDF briefings for the construction and built-environment sector. It performs automated cradle-to-gate life-cycle assessments (LCA) and identifies carbon hotspots with circular-economy substitution opportunities, tailored for Kazakhstan and global markets with a focus on EU CBAM compliance.

## Key Features
- Automated daily scanning of ESG reports, construction news, and regulations using agentic tool-use loops
- Performs cradle-to-gate (A1–A3) life-cycle assessments (LCA) with embodied-carbon calculations
- Identifies carbon hotspots and proposes circular-economy substitutions with quantified savings
- Generates branded PDF briefings for client-ready intelligence reports
- Supports EU CBAM compliance tracking for construction materials like steel, cement, and aluminum

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It automates critical ESG and carbon tracking for the construction sector, enabling compliance with emerging regulations like EU CBAM while identifying cost-saving opportunities through circular-economy practices.
