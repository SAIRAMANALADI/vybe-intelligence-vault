## Summary
Geomacro is an autonomous geopolitical intelligence engine that transforms breaking global news into real-time tradable prediction markets on the Arc blockchain. It automates market creation, resolution, and settlement using LLMs and on-chain smart contracts, enabling users to stake USDC on risk narratives without intermediaries.

## Key Features
- Fully automated market lifecycle: ingestion, classification, creation, resolution, and settlement using LLMs and smart contracts.
- Non-custodial design with client-side wallet interactions and Supabase-backed state management.
- Real-time USDC-denominated markets on Arc blockchain with 46-hour staking windows and 48-hour resolution windows.
- Adversarial AI classification with structured generation to ensure balanced HAWK vs DOVE rationales.
- Optimistic dispute mechanism with community voting and on-chain finalization.
