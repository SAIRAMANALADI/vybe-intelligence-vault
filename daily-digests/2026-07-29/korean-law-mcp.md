## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server providing real-time access to Korean legal data (laws, precedents, administrative rules, local ordinances, treaties) with advanced features like citation verification, impact mapping, time-travel diffs, and ordinance compliance checks for AI assistants and CLI tools.

## Key Features
- Real-time access to 42 Korean legal APIs (laws, precedents, ordinances) via 10 MCP tools
- Advanced citation verification with content matching to prevent hallucinations in AI responses
- Impact mapping and time-travel diffs for legal analysis and compliance checks
- Ordinance compliance radar for tracking local law updates against higher-level legislation
- Structured legal research with 5-step guidance for common legal queries
