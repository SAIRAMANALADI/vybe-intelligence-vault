## Summary
Cordum is a source-available Agent Control Plane designed to govern, monitor, and audit AI agents in real-time, preventing risky actions before execution. It includes Cordum Edge, a compliance firewall for local AI-agent actions like Claude Code, ensuring safety, approvals, and audit trails.

## Key Features
- Pre-execution policy evaluation and safety gating for AI agents
- Real-time monitoring and circuit breakers during agent operations
- Comprehensive audit trails and fleet observability across all agents
- Cordum Edge for local AI-agent compliance (e.g., Claude Code)
- Human-in-the-loop approvals and provenance-gated destructive actions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Cordum provides the critical governance layer needed to safely deploy autonomous AI agents in production, addressing visibility, safety, and audit gaps that currently hinder enterprise adoption.
