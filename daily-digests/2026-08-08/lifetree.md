## Summary
LifeTree is an intelligent personal decision-making system that aggregates public and private information, leveraging knowledge graphs, causal reasoning, Bayesian networks, and Monte Carlo simulations to provide dynamic decision-making sandboxes for long-term life choices.

## Key Features
- Dynamic decision-making sandboxes with Monte Carlo simulations for scenario analysis
- Knowledge graph integration for structured information aggregation and visualization
- ReAct Agent with 15+ built-in tools for querying, reasoning, and external data fetching
- Bayesian networks and causal modeling for quantifying uncertainty in long-term decisions
- Multi-language PWA with offline support and real-time risk alerts

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
LifeTree provides a unique framework for integrating structured reasoning, temporal analysis, and personalized decision support, making it essential for AI engineers building RAG systems that require deep contextual understanding and dynamic knowledge updates.
