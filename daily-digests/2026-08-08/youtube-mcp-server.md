## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that enables programmatic management of YouTube channels from Claude or other AI assistants, including video uploads, metadata editing, custom thumbnails, pseudo-A/B thumbnail testing, and analytics retrieval.

## Key Features
- Resumable video uploads with scheduling support
- Metadata editing (title, description, tags, category, privacy)
- Custom thumbnail upload and management
- Pseudo-A/B thumbnail rotation with views-per-window CTR proxy
- Comprehensive YouTube Analytics retrieval (views, watch time, subs, likes)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines YouTube channel management for AI-driven workflows, enabling automated content publishing and performance tracking without manual intervention.
