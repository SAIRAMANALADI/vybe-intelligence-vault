## Summary
Ziggy is an always-on personal agent that lives on your desktop, serving as a unified portal for managing daily life, health, finances, and schedules with a real voice, switchable personas, and a focus coach. It operates locally and privately, ensuring all data remains on your machine.

## Key Features
- Always-on desktop presence with animated avatar
- Real-time voice interaction with customizable personas
- Unified portal for managing life, finances, and schedules
- Private and local data storage with no cloud dependency
- Built-in focus coach designed for neurodivergent users

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Ziggy provides a private, local-first personal agent framework that integrates voice, personas, and focus tools, making it valuable for AI builders seeking to create secure, user-centric desktop assistants.
