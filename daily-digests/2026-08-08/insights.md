## Summary
Insights is an AI-powered content platform that ingests, processes, and transforms multimedia content (RSS feeds, podcasts, YouTube videos, articles, GitHub repos, images) into summaries, articles, and social media posts. It supports transcription, AI-driven summarization, article generation, and multi-platform publishing with scheduling and automation features.

## Key Features
- Multi-source content ingestion (RSS, YouTube, GitHub, images, URLs, text)
- AI-powered transcription (Whisper, faster-whisper, or OpenAI API)
- Automated summarization, article generation, and social media copy creation
- Multi-platform publishing with scheduling and queue management (LinkedIn, Threads, Facebook, X/Twitter)
- JIRA integration for action item extraction and ticket creation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Insights streamlines the entire content-to-publishing pipeline, enabling AI engineers to automate RAG workflows by transforming raw content into structured, publishable outputs across multiple platforms.
