## Summary
Vector is an autonomous trading agent for Bitget that uses a four-channel signal tribunal (technical, funding, news, on-chain) to vote independently before an LLM (Qwen) reconciles the decisions. It operates in dry-run paper trading mode by default, simulating orders with real market data and providing a full audit trail.

## Key Features
- Four independent signal channels (technical, funding, news, on-chain) voting before LLM decision
- Deterministic risk guards (conflict sizing, alignment checks, hard caps)
- Full audit trail with journaling and exportable trade logs
- Multi-view dashboard with real-time data and equity tracking
- Dry-run paper trading mode with seeded history for demo consistency
