## Summary
A local-first, human-in-the-loop AI content workflow for generating and reviewing articles for WeChat and Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book). It automates content generation, selection, and quality checks while enforcing source constraints and human oversight.

## Key Features
- Local-first workflow with human review and final approval
- Multi-platform content generation (WeChat and Xiaohongshu) with platform-specific adaptations
- Source-constrained generation with citation tracking and duplicate prevention
- Automated quality checks and six-dimensional scoring for topic selection
- Deterministic image card generation using Pillow

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, source-constrained approach to AI content generation with built-in human oversight, ensuring quality and reliability for RAG pipelines.
