## Summary
E-Review Agent is an enterprise-oriented e-commerce review governance system that integrates AI analysis, agent-based inspection, and risk workflows to manage customer reviews from submission to administrative resolution. It extends the open-source litemall project with a structured pipeline for review processing, risk assessment, and auditable operations.

## Key Features
- End-to-end review governance from customer submission to admin resolution
- Structured AI analysis with JSON-contract outputs (sentiment, risk level, type, and suggestions)
- Agent-based inspection of unprocessed reviews with scheduled/manual scanning
- Risk task creation, status transitions, and operation logging for compliance
- Public audit boundary with sanitized snapshots and optional local model experiments

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical framework for integrating AI-driven review analysis and risk management into e-commerce workflows, enabling auditable and scalable governance for AI engineering stacks.
