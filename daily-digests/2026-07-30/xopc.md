## Summary
xopc is a local-first AI assistant framework designed to turn goals into durable loops by maintaining long-running project context, automations, and multi-surface chat capabilities. It enables users to capture notes, track progress, and automate workflows while keeping data private under a local runtime.

## Key Features
- Local-first architecture with data stored under `~/.xopc/`
- Multi-surface chat support (CLI, TUI, Web, Desktop, Mobile, Messengers)
- Long-running project context and state management across sessions
- 20+ LLM provider integrations with BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) support
- Automations for scheduled summaries, reminders, and workflow runs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
xopc provides a self-hosted, extensible runtime for building persistent AI agents that manage long-term goals, automations, and multi-surface interactions, making it essential for RAG builders who need durable context and proactive workflows.
