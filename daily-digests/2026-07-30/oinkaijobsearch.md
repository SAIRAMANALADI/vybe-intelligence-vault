## Summary
OinkAIJobSearch is a Telegram-based AI job search assistant that scrapes 25+ job boards, scores postings against a user's resume using Claude, and delivers personalized matches with hiring contacts directly to Telegram. It supports self-hosting, managed instances, and API access for scrapers.

## Key Features
- Scrapes 25+ job boards including LinkedIn, HN, Wellfound, and EU tech boards
- Uses Claude to score job postings against user resumes with customizable thresholds
- Delivers matches as interactive Telegram cards with hiring contact details and action buttons
- Supports self-hosting, managed instances, or API access for scrapers
- Includes market research tools for deep role and salary analysis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines personalized job matching by leveraging LLMs to filter and deliver relevant opportunities with direct hiring contacts, reducing manual effort for job seekers.
