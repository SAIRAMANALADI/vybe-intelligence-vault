## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server providing search and citation capabilities for Pakistani federal statutes and Supreme Court judgments. It serves static snapshots of legal corpora via HuggingFace datasets, ensuring reproducible and citable responses for AI applications.

## Key Features
- Search and retrieval of 967 federal statutes with keyword and ID-based lookup
- Full-text search over 1,414 Supreme Court judgments
- Strict citation contract with stable URIs, human-readable citations, and source URLs
- Static corpus snapshots for reproducibility and offline use
- Audit logging for every tool call with input hashes and performance metrics

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI systems to reliably retrieve and cite Pakistani legal documents, addressing the lack of machine-readable legal APIs in the country.
