## Summary
Official TypeScript/Node.js SDK for AgentScore, enabling KYC, age verification, sanctions screening, and identity gating for agents or wallets with minimal code. Supports wallet resolution, credential management, and cross-merchant attribution for AI agents.

## Key Features
- Human verification (KYC, age, sanctions, jurisdiction) for agents or wallets
- Policy-based gating with granular decision reasons and verification URLs
- Wallet resolution and cross-chain operator linking (EVM/Solana)
- Credential management (creation, revocation, TTL) for agent authentication
- Server-side signer-match and OFAC sanctions screening with fail-closed enforcement

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It simplifies integrating robust identity verification and gating into AI agents, ensuring compliance and fraud prevention with minimal developer effort.
