## Summary
Entroly is a local-first context control plane for AI coding agents that optimizes evidence selection, compresses context recoverably, and verifies answers to reduce hallucinations and token usage. It integrates seamlessly with popular agents like Claude, Cursor, and Aider via MCP, proxy, or SDK.

## Key Features
- Auditable context receipts with included/omitted evidence and residual risks
- Reversible compressed fragments for full codebase recovery on demand
- WITNESS verification for hallucination detection ($0 cost, ~3ms latency)
- Multi-integration paths: MCP, proxy, SDK, CLI, and npm/PyPI packages
- BM25 + entropy + dependency graph-based evidence ranking under token budgets

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Entroly provides essential context control and verification for RAG/AI stacks, ensuring higher accuracy, lower costs, and auditable evidence selection for AI coding agents.
