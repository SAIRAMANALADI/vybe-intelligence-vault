## Summary
ySEO-PRO-AI is an open-source MCP-compatible SEO automation platform that goes beyond auditing by automatically fixing identified issues. It integrates with AI clients like Claude and Cursor to provide 40+ tools for technical SEO, multilingual optimization, AI search readiness, and programmatic page generation.

## Key Features
- 40+ MCP tools for comprehensive SEO auditing and auto-fixing
- AI Search Readiness scoring for modern search engines
- Multilingual SEO support (14+ languages) with hreflang optimization
- Built-in drift monitoring and competitor comparison
- Programmatic page generation and IndexNow auto-pinging for indexing
