## Summary
html2style captures rendered web pages across responsive conditions, measures their visual systems, and converts the evidence into reusable design packages for AI agents. It bridges the gap between visual inspection and actionable design rules, enabling accurate replication and verification of web designs.

## Key Features
- Captures rendered page evidence across responsive viewports (width and height variants)
- Separates deterministic measurements (computed styles, geometry, assets) from Agent-authored design rules
- Generates portable design packages with human and machine-readable outputs (STYLE.md, manifest.json, style-board.html)
- Supports MCP integration for AI agent workflows and CLI for human use
- Includes audit and verification tools to ensure reconstruction accuracy

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a standardized way to extract, measure, and transfer design evidence from live websites to AI agents, ensuring accurate replication and verification of web designs without relying on superficial visual cues.
