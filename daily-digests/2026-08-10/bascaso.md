## Summary
Bascaso is a free, local-first desktop and self-hosted web app that replaces Apple's App Store Connect, offering advanced App Store Optimization (ASO) features, AI-powered localization, and release management with optional managed AI tiers.

## Key Features
- Advanced ASO and keyword optimization with real-time scoring and character budget tracking
- AI-powered localization for metadata, reviews, and screenshots with multiple AI tier options
- Comprehensive TestFlight and analytics management for beta testing and performance tracking
- Local-first architecture with optional managed AI tier for offline and cloud-based AI features
- Self-hosted Docker deployment with persistent SQLite storage and optional authentication

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Bascaso provides a critical toolset for app developers to optimize their App Store presence with AI-driven insights while maintaining full control over data and costs.
