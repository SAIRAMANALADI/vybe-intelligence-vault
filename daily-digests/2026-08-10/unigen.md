## Summary
Unigen is a predefined workflow execution platform designed for ultra-long, high-quality AI tasks, focusing on zero drift in long-chain executions. It generates and maintains workflows via AI while enforcing strict logical constraints to ensure consistency and accuracy over extended periods.

## Key Features
- AI-generated and self-maintained workflows with strict logical constraints to prevent hallucinations and drift
- Separation of planning (workflow design) and execution (runtime) for stability and traceability
- Supports ultra-long-chain tasks (e.g., script-to-video, idea-to-novel) with zero tolerance for state or factual drift
- Modular and extensible architecture allowing domain experts to inject constraints for faster convergence
- OpenTelemetry-compatible execution history for full traceability and iterative improvement

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Unigen addresses the critical challenge of state drift in long-chain AI tasks by enforcing deterministic workflows, making it essential for RAG builders who require stability and consistency in complex, multi-step processes.
