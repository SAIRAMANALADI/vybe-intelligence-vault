## Summary
AITP Research Protocol is a local-first protocol designed for long-running collaboration between researchers and AI agents, focusing on preserving project memory, provenance, and reasoning over time. It enables resumption of research topics without repeating history and maintains traceable connections between claims, evidence, and decisions.

## Key Features
- Append-only ledger for immutable records with provenance tracking
- Local-first architecture with no required database or external services
- Distinction between physical knowledge, reusable skills, and speculative insights
- Deterministic CLI for validation, persistence, and projection of research records
- Cross-topic knowledge linking and resumption of research sessions without loss of context

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, provenance-aware framework for AI-assisted research that preserves reasoning and evidence over long periods, critical for building reliable RAG systems in knowledge-intensive domains.
