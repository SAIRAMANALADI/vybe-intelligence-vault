## Summary
byebyeclaw is a Windows utility designed to completely uninstall Claw-family AI agents and their remnants from a system with a single command, ensuring no leftover files, folders, or registry entries remain.

## Key Features
- One-click removal of all Claw-family AI agents (ironclaw, microclaw, moltbot, etc.)
- Zero leftover files, folders, or registry entries after uninstallation
- Simple GUI with minimal user input required
- Supports Windows 10 and 11 with administrator privileges
- Quick background process with progress tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures a clean and complete removal of AI agents, preventing residual files or settings from interfering with future AI deployments or system performance.
