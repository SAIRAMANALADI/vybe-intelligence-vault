## Summary
DocuMind Nexus is an intelligent document orchestration platform that transforms static documents into interactive knowledge partners through multi-layered cognitive processing, semantic intelligence, and adaptive automation. It enables organizations to unlock latent intelligence in their archives with context-aware extraction, cross-document relationship mapping, and predictive categorization.

## Key Features
- Context-aware document extraction with semantic understanding
- Cross-document relationship mapping and temporal intelligence
- Multi-model AI orchestration (OpenAI, Claude, local LLMs)
- Adaptive automation with self-optimizing workflows
- Enterprise-grade security with zero-knowledge processing options

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a unified cognitive layer for document processing that enables RAG systems to extract, contextualize, and link information across documents, significantly improving retrieval accuracy and knowledge discovery.
