## Summary
Life Designer Pro is an AI-assisted life-design coach and reusable skill for Claude Code and OpenAI Codex that helps users explore work, relationships, health, and other life areas through adaptive coaching conversations, evidence-based prototypes, and iterative review loops. It generates structured deliverables like blueprints and life maps to guide personal and professional growth.

## Key Features
- Adaptive coaching conversation with evidence-based questioning
- Generates 27 deliverables including blueprints, life maps, and experiment boards
- Supports three alternative futures with review loops (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- Reusable skill for Claude Code and OpenAI Codex with zero-install LITE_PROMPT option
- Emphasizes testable prototypes over permanent verdicts with thin evidence tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, AI-driven framework for personal and professional life design, enabling users to iteratively prototype and refine their goals with evidence-based feedback loops.
