## Summary
A full-stack AI receptionist and lead-capture agent designed for local service businesses, demonstrating a visitor-facing chat widget, grounded business answers, deterministic lead extraction, and Cloudflare-based deployment with D1 persistence.

## Key Features
- Visitor-facing chat widget with real-time lead extraction
- Deterministic lead capture (name, contact, service need, preferred time) independent of LLM reliability
- Grounded business answers using configurable FAQs and optional LLM provider
- Cloudflare D1 persistence for leads, conversations, and transcripts
- Owner dashboard for lead management and conversation review

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a practical blueprint for building AI agents that combine deterministic workflows with optional LLM enhancements, ensuring reliability in critical business processes like lead capture.
