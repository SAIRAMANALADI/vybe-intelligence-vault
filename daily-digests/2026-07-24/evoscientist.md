## Summary
EvoScientist is a self-evolving AI research framework that autonomously explores, generates insights, and iteratively improves through a multi-agent system. It enables autonomous scientific research workflows with adaptive memory, scheduled tasks, and multi-channel interaction (CLI, WebUI, mobile, and messaging platforms).

## Key Features
- Multi-agent team with 6 specialized sub-agents (plan, research, code, debug, analyze, write) for end-to-end research workflows
- Self-evolving memory system that auto-distills insights into a knowledge graph across sessions
- AutoSkills feature that converts recurring patterns into reusable skills for review and adoption
- Multi-provider and multi-channel support (CLI, WebUI, mobile, Telegram, Slack, Feishu, WeChat)
- Scheduled tasks for unattended, cron-style recurring research with shell-access gating

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
EvoScientist provides a robust, autonomous framework for AI-driven research that reduces human-in-the-loop bottlenecks and accelerates iterative scientific discovery through self-improving agents and adaptive workflows.
