## Summary
Cowork-Z is a local-first AI workspace that enables private, organized, and automated AI task execution on your machine. It integrates with multiple AI providers while keeping all data and processing local, ensuring privacy and security for sensitive workflows.

## Key Features
- Local execution of AI tasks with no cloud dependency, ensuring data privacy
- Automation tasks with cron-like scheduling and smart triage for unattended AI workflows
- Arena feature for side-by-side model comparison and parallel AI agent execution
- Workspace-per-project organization with file-level permissions and sandboxed access controls
- Starter Packs and Skills Catalog for rapid onboarding and reusable AI skill templates

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Cowork-Z provides a privacy-preserving, local-first environment for AI task automation and execution, making it essential for developers and organizations handling sensitive data who need secure, offline-capable AI workflows.
