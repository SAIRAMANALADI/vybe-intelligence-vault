## Summary
A media-first review pipeline that transforms social posts from Telegram, X/Twitter, and Instagram into polished, media-rich updates for Telegram, Slack, and optional Bale. It includes editorial rewrites via Gemini, manual review/approval workflows, and automated publishing with source attribution.

## Key Features
- Multi-source ingestion (Telegram, X/Twitter, Instagram) with media extraction and preservation
- Gemini-powered editorial rewrites for polished output
- Manual review and approval workflow with reply-based editing
- Cloudflare KV queue for lightweight review button processing
- Automated publishing to Telegram, Slack, and optional Bale with source attribution

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the transformation of raw social media content into curated, media-rich posts for AI-driven publishing workflows, reducing manual effort while ensuring quality and consistency.
