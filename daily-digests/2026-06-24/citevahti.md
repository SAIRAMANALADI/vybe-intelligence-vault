## Summary
CiteVahti is a local-first tool for auditing manuscript citations by verifying whether each claim is supported by the cited evidence. It enables human-first claim assessment with optional blinded AI second opinions, records decisions in an auditable ledger, and builds reusable private evidence maps for researchers.

## Key Features
- Blinded human-AI collaborative claim assessment with auditable decision ledger
- Reusable private evidence maps to avoid redundant literature reviews
- Local-first architecture ensuring manuscript privacy and no telemetry
- Optional local AI (Ollama) or API-based AI for second opinions without silent decisions
- Exportable citation-integrity reports for methods sections, co-authors, or journals

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
CiteVahti ensures rigorous, auditable citation integrity in academic manuscripts while reducing redundant literature review work through reusable evidence maps.
