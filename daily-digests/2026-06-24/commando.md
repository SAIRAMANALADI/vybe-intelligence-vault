## Summary
commando is an open-source configuration framework designed to set up and manage AI digital employees in under 25 minutes. It focuses on generating, versioning, and sharing configurations for AI agents while remaining agnostic to the underlying AI tools or runtime layers.

## Key Features
- Onboarding workflow for AI digital employees in 25 minutes
- Supports any AI tool (Claude Code, Cursor, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, etc.)
- Generates versionable and inspectable configurations (charter, skills, schedule)
- Integrates with OS schedulers (launchd/systemd) and messaging platforms (Feishu)
- Minimal runtime with a focus on configuration as the core differentiator

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It simplifies the setup and management of AI digital employees by abstracting configuration complexity, enabling faster deployment and scalability for RAG and AI stack builders.
