## Summary
Persnally is a local-first personal context engine that learns about users from their AI interactions, code, and other data sources to provide personalized context to AI tools. It ensures user data remains private and under their control while enabling AI systems to better understand and assist them.

## Key Features
- Local-first architecture ensuring all data stays on the user's machine
- Event-sourced system with append-only logs for full provenance and auditability
- Integration with popular AI clients (Claude, Cursor) via MCP for seamless context sharing
- Provenance-complete profile with evidence-linked claims for transparency
- Deterministic, offline-capable context serving for instant AI assistance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Persnally enables AI tools to provide deeply personalized and context-aware assistance by maintaining a user's private, local context history, reducing the need for repetitive inputs and improving relevance.
