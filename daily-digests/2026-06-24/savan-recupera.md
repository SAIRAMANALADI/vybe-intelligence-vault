## Summary
A WhatsApp-based out-of-court debt recovery platform for a footwear retailer's portfolio, enabling voluntary settlements with discounts, Pix payments with automatic splits, and operations via a no-code web dashboard. The system uses AI-driven negotiation and strict legal compliance for time-barred debts in Brazil.

## Key Features
- AI-powered negotiation bot for debt settlements with configurable discounts
- Automated Pix payment processing with 90/10 creditor/operator split
- No-code operations dashboard for campaign management and monitoring
- Strict legal compliance for time-barred debts (LGPD, Brazil)
- Orchestration via n8n workflows with Supabase Edge Functions for heavy lifting
