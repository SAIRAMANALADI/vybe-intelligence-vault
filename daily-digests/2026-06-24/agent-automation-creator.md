## Summary
AAC is a process-first framework for designing AI-augmented workflows by mapping processes before assigning runtimes, ensuring reliability, observability, and cost discipline. It includes a 34-page specification, a diagnostic tool (AgentTwin), and visual guides to enforce structured AI workflow design.

## Key Features
- Process-first framework for AI workflow design, reversing the traditional AI-first approach
- Three-layer architecture: Process Map, Runtime Assignment, and Closed-Loop AI Framework
- AgentTwin diagnostic tool for evaluating AI agents against the AAC rubric
- 57-item evaluation rubric for production-ready workflows
- Pressure-tested against real-world workflows in healthcare operations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AAC provides a structured, auditable approach to designing AI workflows that ensures reliability, governance, and cost discipline, critical for RAG builders integrating AI into production systems.
