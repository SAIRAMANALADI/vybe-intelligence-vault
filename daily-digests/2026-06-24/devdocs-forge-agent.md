## Summary
devdocs-forge-agent automates the conversion of video tutorials and recordings into structured technical documentation, including blog posts, manuals, and diagrams. It processes transcripts and leverages AI to generate Markdown files, FAQs, and visual flows for platforms like Docusaurus or GitBook.

## Key Features
- Automated summarization of video transcripts into structured documentation
- Support for multiple output formats (Docusaurus, GitBook, Markdown)
- Built-in Markdown editor for manual adjustments
- FAQ and diagram generation from video content
- Local-first processing with privacy-focused data handling

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates the creation of technical documentation from video content, reducing manual effort for developers and writers while ensuring consistency and scalability.
