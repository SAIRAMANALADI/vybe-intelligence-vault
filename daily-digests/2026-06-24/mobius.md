## Summary
Möbius is a self-hosted AI agent that builds custom applications, edits its own interface, and autonomously improves itself overnight. It operates entirely on your server, ensuring data privacy while enabling offline-first functionality and seamless app integration.

## Key Features
- Self-improving AI agent with nightly reflection and memory optimization
- Builds, edits, and deploys custom mini-apps directly from chat prompts
- Full write access to its own platform with git-based versioning and recovery
- Offline-first PWA with local caching and sync capabilities
- Integrated app store with curated starter apps and community-driven extensions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Möbius enables AI engineers to rapidly prototype and deploy custom AI-driven applications while maintaining full control over data and infrastructure, making it ideal for building personalized AI operating systems.
