## Summary
Karna is a self-hosted AI chief of staff that integrates into 13+ messaging channels (Slack, Discord, Telegram, etc.), voice notes, and web/mobile/CLI interfaces to capture context, remember ongoing work, and turn conversations into actionable tasks. It combines multi-agent orchestration, semantic memory, and 97+ tools for automation and productivity.

## Key Features
- 13+ messaging channel integrations (Slack, Discord, Telegram, etc.)
- Three-tier memory system (working, short-term, long-term) for context retention
- 97+ built-in tools for automation (shell, files, web, calendar, email, etc.)
- Multi-agent orchestration with delegation and handoff capabilities
- Self-hosted deployment with Docker/Kubernetes support and privacy controls
