## Summary
Aitne is a local-first personal AI agent that proactively monitors your digital life (calendar, mail, GitHub, notes) and drafts a daily plan, sends reminders, and journals your activities—all while keeping your data on your laptop. It integrates with AI backends like Claude Code, Codex, or Gemini CLI to perform tasks based on natural-language commands.

## Key Features
- Proactive daily planning and briefing via natural-language summaries
- Local Markdown-based memory system for persistent context
- Multi-backend AI integration (Claude, Codex, Gemini, OpenCode)
- Real-time monitoring of calendar, mail, GitHub, and notes
- Customizable notifications and reminders via DMs or dashboard
