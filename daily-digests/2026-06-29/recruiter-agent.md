## Summary
An open-source AI recruiter that evaluates resumes by extracting and analyzing evidence from linked profiles (GitHub, blogs, YouTube, etc.) and scoring them against a customizable rubric for specific job roles. It automates the recruitment process by leveraging LLMs for consistent and fair assessments.

## Key Features
- Extracts and analyzes links from resumes (GitHub, blogs, YouTube, etc.) to build a comprehensive candidate profile
- Scores resumes against customizable role-specific rubrics using LLMs via LiteLLM
- Supports multiple LLM providers (Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, Ollama, etc.) for flexibility
- Provides detailed feedback on strengths and areas for improvement in resumes
- Open-source and extensible with community-contributed role rubrics

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It automates and standardizes resume evaluation for recruiters, reducing bias and improving consistency in hiring decisions.
