## Summary
Waizen is an AI agent framework designed to automate small and medium business operations, including customer management, order tracking, invoicing, accounting, and WhatsApp customer service through a unified web panel and real-time actions.

## Key Features
- End-to-end business operation automation (quotes, invoices, scheduling, accounting)
- Unified web panel for real-time monitoring and control
- WhatsApp AI agent for customer service and order processing
- PDF generation (client-side and server-side via Playwright)
- Proven in production for a real glass & aluminum business
