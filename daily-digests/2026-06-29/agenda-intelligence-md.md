## Summary
Agenda Intelligence MD is an evidence-readiness and trust-routing runtime for high-stakes AI-assisted decisions, transforming incomplete source packs, risk files, and project packets into structured human-review packets with evidence gaps, weak claims, owner actions, and decision-readiness routing. It operates as a deterministic CLI and MCP server with A2A/HTTP adapters, focusing on escalation and human oversight rather than autonomous decision-making.

## Key Features
- Structures incomplete source packs into human-review packets with evidence gaps and decision-readiness scoring
- Supports deterministic CLI, MCP server, HTTP API, and A2A adapters for multi-surface delivery
- Includes five deployed vertical profiles for specialized risk triage (e.g., sanctions exposure, maritime risk)
- Provides schema validation, evidence audit, and geography-routed reasoning for targeted analysis
- Offers confidential project-room workflows for strategic-infrastructure and AI-compute project files

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical layer for ensuring evidence integrity and trust in high-stakes AI-assisted decision workflows by routing gaps and weak claims to human review, reducing blind spots in RAG pipelines.
