## Summary
obsidian-llm-wiki transforms Obsidian markdown notes into a structured knowledge base for AI agents, enabling cited, accurate responses without hallucinations. It bridges personal notes with AI models like Claude, Codex, and Gemini, acting as a headless research expert.

## Key Features
- Converts Obsidian markdown notes into a structured knowledge graph for AI access
- Provides a six-persona team (Analyst, Writer, Archivist, Coder, Strategist, Teacher) for specialized responses
- Ensures cited answers with direct references to original markdown files
- Supports multi-model integration (Claude, Gemini, local tools) with a single-click switch
- Privacy-focused with local processing and no external data exposure
