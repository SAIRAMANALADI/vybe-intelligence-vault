## Summary
A local-first AI assistant for telecom engineering that enables Q&A and document summarization over 3GPP/telecom manuals using LangGraph, ChromaDB, and FastAPI. Designed for telecom professionals with a multi-agent workflow including retrieval, summarization, validation, and human approval.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent graph-based workflow with router, retrieval, and summarization agents
- Confidence-based validation with optional human approval for critical answers
- Local ChromaDB vector store with persistent storage and dense embeddings
- Context-aware Q&A and document summarization tailored for telecom engineering
- Production-ready deployment guide for AWS EC2, S3, and hosted vector DBs
