## Summary
AgentDesk is a multi-agent ticket automation system built on LangGraph that converts natural language tickets into structured tasks, orchestrating five specialized agents (intent understanding, classification, RAG-enhanced processing, review, and coordination) for end-to-end workflow automation with human-in-the-loop oversight.

## Key Features
- LangGraph-based state machine orchestration for ticket lifecycle management
- Five specialized agents (intent, classification, RAG processing, review, coordination) with clear role boundaries
- Human-in-the-loop audit workbench with AI-assisted decision-making and four resolution options
- Distributed tracing and decision chain logging for explainability and debugging
- Model routing, caching, and automatic degradation strategies for resilience

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AgentDesk provides a production-ready framework for building scalable multi-agent systems with RAG integration, human oversight, and observability, critical for enterprise-grade AI automation.
