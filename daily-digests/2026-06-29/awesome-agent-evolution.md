## Summary
A curated survey and evidence map focused on self-evolving AI agents, providing a framework to distinguish genuine self-improvement systems from superficial demos. It evaluates projects based on observable feedback loops, verifiable mechanisms, and evidence chains rather than popularity or marketing claims.

## Key Features
- Provides a strict framework to evaluate AI agent self-evolution mechanisms (Observe -> Interpret -> Modify -> Verify -> Retain)
- Includes the Evolve-AGI Index for assessing evidence maturity across benchmark performance, feedback loops, and governance
- Curates and cross-verifies raw evidence (GitHub, papers, blogs) into processed analysis and model cards
- Offers a taxonomy of five evolution loops (norm-to-execution, search, evaluator, reflection/memory, swarm/archive)
- Delivers actionable insights for researchers, engineers, and product teams to distinguish real capability accumulation from hype

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a rigorous, evidence-based framework to evaluate whether AI agents truly self-improve or merely simulate intelligence, critical for building reliable RAG and AI systems.
