## Summary
Amarnai is a Gmail-first AI email triage assistant that sorts incoming emails into a custom taxonomy, drafts responses for approval, and integrates with LLMs and embeddings for intelligent processing. It supports both local and cloud-based AI providers and is designed for self-hosting or cloud deployment.

## Key Features
- AI-driven email sorting into custom taxonomies
- Draft generation with human-in-the-loop approval
- Support for local (Ollama) and cloud-based LLMs/embeddings
- Real-time Gmail push notifications via Pub/Sub
- Self-hostable with Docker Compose for easy deployment

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Amarnai streamlines email management by automating triage and draft generation, reducing cognitive load for users while maintaining privacy and control through self-hosting options.
