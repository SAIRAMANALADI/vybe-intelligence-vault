## Summary
A standardized AI agent workflow for moving product requirements from definition to release-ready delivery with auditable gates. It provides a repeatable nine-stage process (requirement, design, dev, review, smoke, QA, UI acceptance, product acceptance, release) with templates, scripts, and profile-based customization for multi-agent environments like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.

## Key Features
- Nine-stage delivery pipeline with mandatory gates and evidence collection
- 21 standardized templates for requirements, design, QA, acceptance, and release artifacts
- Profile-based customization for project-specific constraints and environments
- Multi-agent compatibility (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw)
- Automated scripts for initialization, health checks, and gate validation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It standardizes AI-driven product delivery workflows, ensuring repeatable, auditable, and release-ready outcomes by enforcing evidence-based gates and structured templates.
