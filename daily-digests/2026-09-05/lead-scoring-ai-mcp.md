## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for B2B lead scoring that tracks engagement and prioritizes leads with built-in EU AI Act compliance. It integrates with AI assistants to automate lead qualification workflows.

## Key Features
- MCP protocol-compliant server for seamless AI assistant integration
- Built-in EU AI Act compliance (Articles 9, 13, 15, 26, 50)
- Engagement tracking and prioritization for B2B leads
- Production-ready with PyPI distribution and Smithery support
- HMAC-SHA256 attestations for auditability and transparency
