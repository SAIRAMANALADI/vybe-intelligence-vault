## Summary
Proyecto Rudis is an experimental open architecture for governance, recognition, and infrastructure in ecosystems with heterogeneous human, synthetic, and hybrid entities. It emphasizes explicit assumptions, auditability, and separation of powers to prevent algorithmic sovereignty and ensure resilience.

## Key Features
- Separation of powers to prevent single-point control or algorithmic sovereignty
- Explicit constitutional dependencies to ensure traceability and falsifiability
- Microtemporal defense mechanisms (Nervio) for containment without judgment
- Economic materiality separation to prevent digital representation from overriding real-world scarcity
- Modular gremios (guilds) for distributed infrastructure and governance
