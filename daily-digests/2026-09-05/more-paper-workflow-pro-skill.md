## Summary
A structured, evidence-centered workflow for academic paper writing in Chinese/English, covering topic definition, literature retrieval, PDF download routing, Zotero management, writing, citation auditing, and polishing. It emphasizes real literature over model memory, ensuring traceable and verifiable citations.

## Key Features
- 8-step transparent workflow for academic paper writing with clear input/output boundaries and failure recovery points
- Direct PDF reading for citation accuracy, minimizing reliance on RAG hallucinations
- Zotero integration for managing PDFs, BibTeX, and literature evidence with traceability
- Multi-source literature retrieval with scoring, saturation analysis, and quality control checks
- Modular direct-entry support for skipping steps if prior artifacts (e.g., DOIs, drafts) are available
