## Summary
Nomi is an open-source, local-first desktop workbench for AI video generation and editing. It enables users to combine multiple AI models, APIs, or local ComfyUI workflows to create, storyboard, generate, and edit video content entirely on their own machine without requiring an account or telemetry.

## Key Features
- Supports multiple AI video generation models and APIs, including local ComfyUI, with seamless integration and cost optimization strategies.
- Provides a visual timeline and storyboard editor for AI video creation, allowing users to plan, generate, and edit video content interactively.
- Enables MCP-driven automation, allowing AI agents like Codex or Claude Code to orchestrate video generation workflows directly.
- Local-first architecture ensures all footage, workflows, and provider setups remain on the user's machine, prioritizing privacy and control.
- Offers a modular provider system, allowing users to add custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints, relay services, or local models with minimal configuration.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Nomi provides a cost-effective, local-first platform for AI video generation that can be integrated with AI agents via MCP, making it essential for builders seeking to optimize and automate video workflows.
