## Summary
AI Product Photo Sorter automates the organization of raw product photo shoots into SKU-aware catalog workflows, combining desktop GUI and CLI tools with cloud/local vision models, human review, and Shopify/PIM exports while preserving original files.

## Key Features
- Multi-provider vision integration (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama) with fallback and key rotation
- Non-destructive product grouping with human review and SKU confirmation workflow
- Watched folder daemon for persistent, crash-safe automation with deterministic event handling
- Local-first operation with optional local embeddings, OCR, and barcode evidence
- Cross-platform desktop GUI and CLI with benchmarking, audit trails, and Shopify draft preparation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the critical but error-prone process of organizing product photo shoots into SKU-aware catalogs, reducing manual effort while ensuring accuracy and auditability for e-commerce workflows.
