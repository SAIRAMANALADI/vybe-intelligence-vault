## Summary
Cedulon is an audit layer for agent-to-agent spend, providing signed trade manifests, fail-closed policy enforcement, and SCITT-anchorable spend receipts. It operates above payment rails like x402 and AP2 without handling actual transactions, ensuring integrity through cryptographic verification and policy checks.

## Key Features
- Fail-closed policy enforcement for agent-to-agent spend
- Signed trade manifests and spend receipts (COSE_Sign1) for integrity
- Epoch checkpoints and transparency log for auditability
- MCP server integration for seamless tooling integration
- Mock rail for testing without real transactions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Cedulon ensures financial integrity in AI agent interactions by enforcing signed policies and receipts, preventing unauthorized spend and providing verifiable audit trails critical for RAG and AI stack builders.
