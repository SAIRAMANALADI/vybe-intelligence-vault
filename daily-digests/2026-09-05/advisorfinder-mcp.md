## Summary
An MCP server providing AI assistants with access to SEC-registered financial advisor data, enabling searches by name, firm, or location, and retrieval of profiles, disclosure status, and firm fees. It acts as a bridge to public SEC/FINRA databases for financial advisor research.

## Key Features
- Search SEC-registered advisors by name, firm, city, or state
- Retrieve full advisor profiles, employment history, and disclosure status
- Access firm profiles, fee schedules, and advisor roster counts
- Provide database-wide statistics and data freshness metrics
- Deliberately avoids numeric risk scores, surfacing raw facts for verification

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI assistants to safely and accurately retrieve verified financial advisor data from public SEC/FINRA databases, reducing hallucinations in financial advisory contexts.
