## Summary
Corply is an autonomous incorporation agent that enables startup founders to form a Delaware C-Corp entirely from within Claude Code. It guides users through a conversational incorporation process, generates legal documents, facilitates cofounder e-signatures, and ensures human-reviewed Delaware filings with a flat fee.

## Key Features
- Guided conversational incorporation process with founder equity and vesting details
- Automated generation of legal documents (Certificate of Incorporation, Bylaws, etc.)
- Conversational e-signature workflow for cofounders with audit trails
- Human-reviewed Delaware filing process for compliance
- Integration with Claude Code via MCP for seamless workflow

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the complex and costly process of forming a Delaware C-Corp directly from an AI coding assistant, reducing friction for founders and technical teams.
