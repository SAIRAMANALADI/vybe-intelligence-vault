## Summary
Osprey is a self-hosted, open-source project management agent that aggregates and prioritizes critical tasks across multiple sources (e.g., emails, calendars, Procore) to surface the most urgent items requiring immediate action, with built-in AI integration for natural language filtering and explainable scoring.

## Key Features
- Aggregates and prioritizes tasks from multiple sources (Outlook, Gmail, Procore, calendars, email forwarding) into a single prioritized hotlist.
- Explainable scoring engine with contract notice deadlines weighted highest to prevent missed claims.
- Pluggable AI layer (Claude, Ollama, or offline deterministic rules) for natural language filtering and signal extraction.
- Self-hosted, private-by-design architecture with encrypted token storage and least-privilege OAuth access.
- Supports live hotlist via WebSocket, Excel/PDF exports, and Tauri-based desktop/mobile clients.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Osprey streamlines critical task management for AI/RAG builders by automating the aggregation and prioritization of high-stakes project signals, reducing manual oversight while ensuring no critical deadlines are missed.
