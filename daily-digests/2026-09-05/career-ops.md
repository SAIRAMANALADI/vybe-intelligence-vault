## Summary
career-ops is an AI-powered job search system that transforms any AI coding CLI into a command center for evaluating job listings, generating tailored resumes and cover letters, and tracking applications. It uses multi-agent workflows to assess job postings, research companies, and optimize application materials for ATS compatibility.

## Key Features
- AI-driven job listing evaluation with structured A-H reports and holistic scoring
- Automated generation of ATS-optimized PDF resumes and cover letters tailored per job description
- Multi-portal scanner for Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, and company career pages
- Batch processing for parallel evaluation of 10+ job listings with sub-agents
- Terminal UI dashboard for browsing, filtering, and managing job applications

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It empowers AI engineers and job seekers to automate and optimize the tedious parts of job hunting, reducing time-to-application while improving quality through AI-driven insights and personalization.
