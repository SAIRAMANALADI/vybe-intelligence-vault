## Summary
Faktura is an autonomous invoice-financing desk built on the Casper blockchain, enabling real-world asset (RWA) credit underwriting via AI agents with on-chain risk policies and native-CSPR liquidity pools. It operates as a pay-per-call risk oracle (x402) and provides auditable, machine-to-machine credit decisions.

## Key Features
- Autonomous AI-driven underwriting with deterministic pre-checks and LLM risk scoring
- On-chain risk policy enforcement with hard caps (e.g., single-invoice, debtor exposure) to prevent fund misuse
- Native-CSPR liquidity pool for invoice factoring with auditable yield and loss tracking
- x402 pay-per-call risk oracle for machine-to-machine credit risk reporting with native settlement
- Comprehensive attestation log (SHA-256 anchored) for every AI decision, enabling full audibility

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates how AI agents can safely orchestrate real-world financial processes on-chain with auditable, policy-bound decision-making, bridging the gap between AI autonomy and blockchain trust.
