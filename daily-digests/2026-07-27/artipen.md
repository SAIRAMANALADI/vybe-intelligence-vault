## Summary
ArtiPen is a multi-platform content formatting engine that transforms markdown or plain text articles into optimized formats for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, and Juejin, including cover images, illustrations, and infographics. It also integrates AI content authenticity detection and topic intelligence for content creation workflows.

## Key Features
- One-click multi-platform formatting for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, and Juejin with platform-specific optimizations
- AI-powered cover image, illustration, and infographic generation with customizable styles
- Integrated AI flavor detection engine with 33 pattern checks and 5-dimensional scoring
- Topic intelligence and hotspot briefing for content planning and research
- Structured output validation and product convergence gates for quality assurance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ArtiPen streamlines multi-platform content publishing and ensures AI-generated content meets authenticity standards, reducing manual effort and improving output consistency for RAG and AI stack builders.
