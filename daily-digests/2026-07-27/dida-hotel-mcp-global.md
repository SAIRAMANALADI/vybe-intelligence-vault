## Summary
Dida Hotel MCP is an official Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that enables AI agents to search, compare, and book over 2 million hotels globally in real-time. It integrates with DIDA, the world's 3rd largest B2B travel platform, providing live rates, bookable inventory, and direct hotel contracts for seamless AI-driven travel planning and booking.

## Key Features
- Real-time hotel search and booking with live rates and availability
- Integration with 2M+ hotels across 500+ suppliers globally
- Direct hotel contracts with 110,000+ properties for competitive pricing
- Agent-ready with support for 40+ leading AI coding agents (Claude, Codex, Cursor, etc.)
- Comprehensive toolset for search, price comparison, and booking lifecycle

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to perform real-time, transactional hotel searches and bookings, bridging the gap between AI recommendations and actual travel reservations with minimal integration effort.
