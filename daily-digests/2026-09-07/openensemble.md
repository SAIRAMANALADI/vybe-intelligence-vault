## Summary
OpenEnsemble is a self-hosted, multi-user AI assistant platform that enables users to deploy and manage teams of specialist agents with isolated data per user. It supports voice interactions via open-hardware devices and provides customizable dashboards for wall tablets or browsers.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent chat with specialist roles (e.g., coding, email, research) and dynamic role assignment
- Self-hosted voice assistant with open-hardware ESP32-S3 devices for hands-free interaction
- Customizable dashboards with up to 32 panels per profile for wall tablets or browsers
- Built-in local reasoning and embeddings models (SmolLM2, nomic-embed-text-v1) for privacy
- Multi-user isolation with per-user agents, skills, files, and settings under dedicated profiles

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
OpenEnsemble provides a critical orchestration layer for RAG builders by enabling multi-agent workflows, local reasoning, and secure multi-user environments while supporting diverse LLM providers and voice integrations.
