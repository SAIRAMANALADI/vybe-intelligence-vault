## Summary
Field-agent automates field marketing workflows for one-person teams by generating targeted event briefings, guest lists, and follow-up plans using Exa search and Claude for qualification and drafting. It transforms raw account data into actionable, personalized event strategies with minimal human input.

## Key Features
- Generates ranked city recommendations for target segments with buyer density evidence
- Maps target accounts to qualified attendees with role-specific rationale grounded in public sources
- Produces personalized event briefings including seating choreography and conversation moves
- Drafts invites and follow-up emails tailored to individual roles and recent work
- Flags research gaps and human verification requirements for each account

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the creation of highly targeted, personalized event strategies for AI/tech sellers by automating research and briefing generation, reducing manual effort while improving relevance and conversion potential.
