## Summary
A thin orchestration skill for consumer shopping price comparison that triages buy intent across 13 shopping domains, ranks by landed cost (including tax, shipping, and coupons), and delegates live-price data collection to existing research tools. It enforces strict quality guardrails like timestamped snapshots, coupon-cart verification, and retailer trust tiers.

## Key Features
- Triage buy intent to 13 specialized shopping domains with automated tool detection and installation guidance
- Ranks results by landed cost (including tax, shipping, and coupons) rather than sticker price
- Enforces strict quality guardrails: timestamped snapshots, stock state tracking, and retailer trust tiers
- Delegates live-price data collection to existing research tools (e.g., Playwright, Keepa, Camelcamelcamel) to avoid reinventing the wheel
- Provides automated verification of coupon claims via cart testing and disconfirmation of fraud risks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines consumer price comparison workflows by integrating disparate shopping tools into a unified, quality-controlled pipeline for landed cost ranking and fraud-aware recommendations.
