## Summary
A non-commercial skill that transforms daily life entries, photos, knowledge notes, meeting summaries, and long-form content into a continuously growing comic-style journal. It organizes content into monthly volumes, quarterly parts, and annual compilations with customizable art styles and optional IP characters.

## Key Features
- Converts text, images, and structured data into comic-style layouts with multiple art presets
- Organizes content into monthly volumes, quarterly parts, and annual compilations for continuous growth
- Supports customizable IP characters and reference images for personalized storytelling
- Provides deterministic rendering with validation to ensure consistency across platforms
- Offers lightweight and full-fidelity export options for sharing and archiving

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI builders to transform raw user-generated content into visually engaging, structured narratives, enhancing personal knowledge management and creative expression in RAG applications.
