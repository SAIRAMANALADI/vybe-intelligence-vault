## Summary
Aevio is an autonomous AI company for crypto that operates as a research, arbitrage, and trading desk. It reads blockchain data, decomposes opportunities into real vs. subsidized returns, prices positions at your size, ranks them under custom mandates, and reports findings to a terminal without holding keys or executing transactions.

## Key Features
- Reads blockchain data (Solana) via Helius API to resolve wallet holdings and opportunities
- Decomposes yields into real revenue vs. subsidies with explicit provenance for every number
- Prices positions at your actual size, accounting for slippage, breakeven days, and capacity constraints
- Ranks opportunities under custom mandates (e.g., conservative, balanced, degen) with risk-adjusted excess returns
- Operates as a read-only terminal with no key custody or transaction execution

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Aevio provides a deterministic, auditable framework for decomposing and ranking crypto opportunities, enabling RAG builders to integrate transparent, risk-adjusted yield analysis into their pipelines.
