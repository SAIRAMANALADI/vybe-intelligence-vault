## Summary
g8e is a sovereign execution platform that enables frontier AI reasoning at the edge while preserving data custody. It treats cloud providers as stateless co-processors, ensuring raw data, state, and keys remain on the host, with all operations governed by cryptographic proofs and local-first audit architecture.

## Key Features
- Cloud as stateless co-processor with zero data custody, ensuring raw data remains on the host
- Local-First Audit Architecture (LFAA) for cryptographically verifiable state and context
- Outbound-only mTLS connections with no inbound ports or firewall rules required
- Zero standing privileges with just-in-time permission minting and immediate dissolution
- Multi-layer admission pipeline (L1-L5) for enforcing governance, security, and compliance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
g8e enables AI systems to perform high-stakes reasoning and execution while maintaining strict data sovereignty and compliance, eliminating the trade-off between frontier AI capabilities and regulatory requirements.
