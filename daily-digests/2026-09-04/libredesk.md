## Summary
LibreDesk is an open-source, self-hosted omnichannel customer support platform that consolidates live chat, email, and other communication channels into a single inbox. It includes an AI assistant, agent copilot, and automation tools for efficient customer support management.

## Key Features
- Omnichannel inbox combining live chat, email, and other channels
- AI-powered assistant grounded in knowledge base for automated responses
- Agent copilot for drafting replies, summarizing conversations, and knowledge base lookups
- Advanced automation rules for tagging, assigning, and routing conversations
- Granular permissions and role-based access control for teams
