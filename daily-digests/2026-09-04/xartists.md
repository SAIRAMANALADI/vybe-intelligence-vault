## Summary
xArtists is a full-stack dApp integrating AI-generated art, phygital NFTs, and RWA (Real World Assets) on the MultiversX blockchain. It combines generative AI agents, staking mechanisms, DAO governance, and a marketplace for trading NFTs and RWAs.

## Key Features
- AI-generated art with phygital NFTs (Warps/LIA agents)
- Staking for NFTs and tokens ($TRO) with liquidity pools
- DAO governance with 60% quorum for decision-making
- Marketplace for RWA escrow trading and terminal
- PWA with mobile navigation and E2E Playwright testing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a comprehensive framework for integrating AI agents, NFTs, and RWAs on a blockchain, enabling developers to build scalable, autonomous systems for generative art and decentralized trading.
