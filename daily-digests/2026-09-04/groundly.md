## Summary
Groundly enables the creation of local-first, portable knowledge bases for AI agents by indexing course materials into retrievable, verifiable, and shareable formats. It supports grounded Q&A, verified quizzes, flashcards, and mastery tracking while ensuring citations and preventing hallucinations.

## Key Features
- Hybrid retrieval with dense, sparse, and BM25 indexing for accurate document chunking and retrieval
- Verified generation of quizzes, coding challenges, and flashcards with execution checks to prevent hallucinations
- Page-level citations for answers, ensuring transparency and traceability to source materials
- Portable knowledge base export/import as a single shareable file for seamless sharing among peers
- Mastery tracking and study memory persistence across different agents and hosts

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Groundly ensures AI agents can answer questions with verifiable, citation-backed accuracy while eliminating hallucinations, making it essential for building reliable RAG systems in educational and professional contexts.
