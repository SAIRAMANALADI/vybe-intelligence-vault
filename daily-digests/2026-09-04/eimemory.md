## Summary
eimemory is a local-first runtime for long-running AI agents that provides durable memory, autonomous thinking, and self-evolution capabilities. It enables agents to retain decisions, correct mistakes, and safely improve over time through gated learning and governance mechanisms.

## Key Features
- Durable memory storage with JSONL and SQLite for long-term retention of decisions and outcomes
- Hybrid retrieval combining lexical, semantic, and graph-aware methods with provenance and confidence scoring
- Gated self-evolution pipeline with isolated evaluation, safety checks, and rollback mechanisms
- Autonomous thinking via scheduled review cycles that generate hypotheses and learning goals
- Strict governance model enforcing conservative autonomy with fail-closed policies for critical operations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a critical runtime for building long-running AI agents that can remember, learn, and improve over time while maintaining strict safety and governance controls.
