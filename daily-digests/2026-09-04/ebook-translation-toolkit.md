## Summary
A structured-first PDF ebook translation pipeline that converts English PDFs into Chinese HTML, Markdown, Obsidian notes, and reflowable EPUB 3 outputs while preserving structural integrity, terminology consistency, and reproducibility through deterministic builds and project-level QA gates.

## Key Features
- Deterministic builds with UUID and timestamp persistence for reproducibility
- Cross-page paragraph repair to maintain logical text flow from PDFs
- Project-level terminology lock and bilingual proper-noun rules for consistency
- Multi-format output generation (HTML, Markdown, Obsidian, EPUB 3) from a single structured JSON source
- Comprehensive QA gates for paragraph reflow, image anchoring, footnotes, and terminology validation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures high-fidelity, consistent, and reproducible translations of long-form academic or technical PDFs into multiple structured formats while preserving document integrity and terminology accuracy.
