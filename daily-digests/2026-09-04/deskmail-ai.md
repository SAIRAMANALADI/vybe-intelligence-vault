## Summary
DeskMail AI is a local, single-user desktop email client for Windows that integrates a safe, built-in Claude connector for AI-assisted email management. It fetches and caches emails locally, ensuring privacy while enabling AI to read, draft, and organize messages without sending or permanently deleting anything.

## Key Features
- Full IMAP/SMTP email support with two-way sync (reads, flags, moves, deletes)
- Local Claude connector for AI-assisted email management (read, draft, organize, but never send or delete permanently)
- Privacy-first design: emails cached locally, passwords encrypted, no data leaves your machine
- Rich email features: folders, labels, snooze, rules, smart views, calendar, contacts, and search
- Easy customization via AI (Claude) without coding knowledge
