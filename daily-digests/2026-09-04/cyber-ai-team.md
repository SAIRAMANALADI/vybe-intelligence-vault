## Summary
Cyber AI Team is a self-hosted AI operating system designed for solo founders and small startups, enabling the creation of an autonomous digital company with governed AI workers. It integrates ERPNext for business records, Qdrant for memory, and a governed multi-agent system for orchestration, long-term memory, and human-in-the-loop oversight.

## Key Features
- Governed multi-agent system with Chief Operating Agent and Observer Agent for safety and oversight
- Four-layer memory fabric combining Qdrant, PostgreSQL, and ERPNext for canonical business records
- Human-in-the-loop governance with approval gates, audit trails, and replay protection
- Self-hosted deployment with Docker Compose, CI/CD, and production-ready observability
- FOSS-first policy ensuring transparency, self-hostability, and minimal SaaS dependencies

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a production-ready, self-hosted framework for building autonomous AI-driven companies with governed multi-agent orchestration and long-term memory integration.
