## Summary
OpenChatCut is a local-first, agent-native video editor that enables AI agents (like Codex or Claude Code) to read, edit, and export real video projects with a professional multitrack timeline. It bridges conversational AI with precise manual editing, allowing projects to remain fully editable and undoable.

## Key Features
- Agent-native editing with MCP support for external AI tools like Codex and Claude Code
- Real multitrack timeline with transitions, effects, keyframes, and undo/redo capabilities
- Transcript-driven editing with word-level precision and linked captions
- Local-first architecture with BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) for privacy and control
- Production-ready exports including MP4, audio, captions, and FCPXML

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to participate directly in professional video editing workflows while preserving full project editability and manual control.
