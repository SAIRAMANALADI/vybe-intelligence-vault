## Summary
EvoLDO-Bench is an auditable benchmark suite designed to evaluate AI models or agents on their ability to reason about Large-Scale Design Optimization (LDO) tasks, including circuit design, simulation, and design closure. It provides controlled, replayable tests to assess reasoning, evidence handling, and tool usage in analog design workflows.

## Key Features
- 40-family / 120-instance controlled public pilot for LDO tasks
- Enforced `AnalogProbeContract` for simulator interactions to ensure disciplined tool usage
- Deterministic grading with family-macro scores, capability vectors, and efficiency metrics
- Support for controlled treatments (direct reasoning, agentic skills, simulation-assisted modes)
- Sealed exam operations for immutable tasks, oracles, and policy enforcement

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a rigorous, auditable framework to evaluate AI agents' reasoning and tool-use capabilities in analog circuit design, critical for building reliable RAG systems in engineering domains.
