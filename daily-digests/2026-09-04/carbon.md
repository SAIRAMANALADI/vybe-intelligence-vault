## Summary
Carbon is an open-source ERP, MES, and QMS platform designed for manufacturing workflows, offering modular apps for ERP, MES, QMS, and custom field management with full-stack type safety and real-time capabilities.

## Key Features
- Modular ERP, MES, and QMS applications with unified authentication and permissions
- Full-stack type safety from database to UI with TypeScript
- Real-time database subscriptions and row-level security (RLS)
- Attribute-based and role-based access control (ABAC/RBAC)
- Dependency graph for operations and nested Bill of Materials (BoM) support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Carbon provides a customizable, API-first manufacturing platform that addresses vendor lock-in and modern tooling gaps, making it essential for RAG builders needing domain-specific workflows and integrations.
