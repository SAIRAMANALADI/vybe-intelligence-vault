## Summary
Diariz is a self-hosted, multi-user platform for recording, transcribing, and analyzing meetings with speaker diarization, live transcription, and AI-powered summarization. It supports audio/video capture, speaker identification, and cross-recording voice recognition while prioritizing user privacy and data ownership.

## Key Features
- Real-time and post-meeting transcription with word-level timestamps and speaker diarization using WhisperX and pyannote
- Cross-recording speaker identification with optional voiceprint enrollment and platform-wide recognition
- Live transcription and assistant chat during ongoing meetings with provisional text and status tracking
- Privacy-focused design with user-controlled speaker data, GDPR compliance, and optional voiceprint opt-out
- Modular minutes generation using customizable templates and formulas with WYSIWYG editing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Diariz provides a privacy-preserving, self-hosted alternative for meeting transcription and analysis that integrates seamlessly with RAG pipelines while ensuring speaker attribution and contextual accuracy.
