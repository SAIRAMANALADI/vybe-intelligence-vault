## Summary
A self-hosted Telegram bot that integrates with Obsidian to serve as a personal AI agent for task management, knowledge retrieval, and personal finance. It captures inputs (text, voice, images) into a local Obsidian vault and uses an LLM for tool-based reasoning, ensuring data ownership and fail-closed module control.

## Key Features
- Telegram-to-Obsidian capture (text, voice, images, PDFs) with structured markdown output
- Modular fail-closed capabilities (planning, knowledge, finance) that can be toggled on/off
- Local RAG over Obsidian vault with retrieval evaluation (Recall@1 ≈ 0.76)
- Natural language expense tracking and ledger management with dashboards in Obsidian
- Guided onboarding via AI coding assistants (Cursor, Claude) or CLI scripts

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a vault-native, self-hosted alternative to cloud-based AI assistants, ensuring data ownership and modular control over capabilities for RAG and personal AI workflows.
