## Summary
Sky-Claw is an AI-assisted local control plane for managing Skyrim Special Edition and Anniversary Edition mods through Mod Organizer 2 (MO2), integrating natural language workflows with human-in-the-loop safety gates and toolchain integrations like LOOT, xEdit, and Nexus Mods.

## Key Features
- Natural language mod management with AI assistance for Skyrim SE/AE via MO2
- Multi-LLM support (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Ollama) with unified interface
- Human-in-the-loop (HITL) safety gates for critical operations like downloads and conflicts
- Integration with LOOT, xEdit, FOMOD, and Nexus Mods for automated mod handling
- Modern GUI, CLI, and Telegram bot interfaces with TOML-based configuration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Sky-Claw provides a critical bridge between AI automation and human oversight in mod management, ensuring safe, traceable, and efficient workflows for complex game modding ecosystems.
