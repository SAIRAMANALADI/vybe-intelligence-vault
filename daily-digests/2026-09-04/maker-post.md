## Summary
An AI-powered organizational assistant for solo makers that auto-classifies photos into projects, surfaces post ideas, and stores experiment results as a reference library. Designed to minimize manual effort by leveraging AI for organization while keeping the user in control of content creation.

## Key Features
- Auto-classification of photos into projects using vision models and LLMs
- Post idea surfacing when content thresholds are met
- Reference library for storing experiment results and notes
- Zero manual effort beyond taking photos (auto-sync from iOS)
- User-controlled content creation with AI-assisted organization

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines content organization for solo makers, reducing cognitive load and enabling better knowledge retention for future projects.
