## Summary
A modular AI orchestration platform enabling multi-agent workflows, real-world asset (RWA) tokenization, and institutional-grade AI agent management with a decoupled architecture for secure execution and settlement.

## Key Features
- Decoupled architecture separating AI orchestration (open-source) from secure execution (AetherNet QUAS)
- Pre-configured support for 30+ AI models, embeddings, RAG, and dataset management
- 44-field Real-World Asset (RWA) schema for structured asset tokenization and management
- Integrations with Salesforce, NetSuite, Shopify, Wix, and other enterprise platforms
- Stripe-powered metered subscriptions and perpetual AI ROI analytics
