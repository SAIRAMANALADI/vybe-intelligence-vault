## Summary
RippleProof is a bounded PostgreSQL column-rename agent that transforms DataHub-proven evidence into validated dbt repairs across repositories. It executes repairs in isolation, refuses ambiguous or stale evidence, and halts at human-reviewed pull requests, ensuring traceable and manifest-bound outcomes.

## Key Features
- Generates and validates dbt repairs across multiple repositories in isolated environments
- Refuses to act on ambiguous or stale lineage evidence, ensuring fail-closed behavior
- Produces human-reviewable pull requests with manifest-bound decisions and evidence
- Supports offline audits to verify campaign integrity without external dependencies
- Includes a corpus of 14 test cases covering repair and refusal scenarios for reproducibility

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
RippleProof ensures safe, evidence-backed automation for PostgreSQL column renames in dbt pipelines, reducing manual review burden while maintaining strict integrity through manifest-bound outcomes.
