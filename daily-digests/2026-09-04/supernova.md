## Summary
Supernova is an autonomous AI marketing agent that transforms a product URL into a complete marketing campaign, including market research, script generation, image creation, voiceovers, and video assembly. It unifies a fragmented workflow into a single platform powered by Genblaze and Backblaze B2.

## Key Features
- AI-powered market intelligence and competitor analysis from a product URL
- Automated script generation with multi-model fallback (Qwen, DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini)
- Professional image and video asset creation with Remotion for timeline-based assembly
- Integrated Backblaze B2 storage for persistent asset management and versioning
- Unified platform replacing 12+ traditional marketing tools with a single workflow

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Supernova streamlines the creation of AI-generated marketing campaigns, reducing time and cost while ensuring consistency and scalability for RAG and AI stack builders.
