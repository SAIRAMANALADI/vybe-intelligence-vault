## Summary
MAS-Sentry-Toolkit is an offensive-security scanner designed to audit multi-agent systems (MAS) for vulnerabilities across MCP, A2A, and MQTT protocols. It validates protocol clients against reference SDKs to ensure real-world accuracy and aligns with the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (2026).

## Key Features
- Validates protocol clients against reference SDKs to ensure real-world accuracy
- Supports MCP, A2A, and MQTT protocol scanning with active and passive probes
- Generates detailed reports in JSON, HTML, Markdown, SARIF, and JUnit formats
- Includes behavioral fingerprinting (ABFP) for detecting rogue agents and topology drift
- Aligns with OWASP Agentic Top 10 (2026) for comprehensive risk assessment

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides critical security validation for multi-agent systems, ensuring protocol implementations are robust against real-world threats and aligned with emerging agentic security standards.
