## Summary
A Microsoft Teams voice and video agent (CVI) plugin for OpenClaw that enables real-time two-way video conversations with animated, lip-synced avatars. It integrates Teams calls with OpenClaw's agent capabilities for perception, dialogue, and rendering.

## Key Features
- Real-time speech-to-speech or streaming STT-to-agent-to-TTS dialogue modes
- Vision capabilities for camera and screen-share frame processing with budget caps
- Animated avatar rendering with lip-sync and expression cues via StandIn bridge
- Meeting etiquette features like wake phrases, attribution, and outbound callbacks
- Secure transport with HMAC handshake, allowlists, and recording-status gates

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges Microsoft Teams calls with AI agents, enabling real-time interactive video conversations and avatar rendering for RAG and AI stack builders.
