## Summary
A diagnostic CLI and extensible framework for evaluating whether high-risk business judgments can be safely delegated to AI agents. It provides a structured 5-step readiness assessment, including task screening, process evaluation, judgment scoring, task contract validation, and audit log verification, with optional company-specific rule overlays.

## Key Features
- Structured 5-step delegation readiness framework with machine-readable definitions (YAML/JSON Schema)
- Deterministic exit codes for CI/CD pipeline integration and automated decision-making
- Extensible overlay system for company-specific rules while maintaining framework integrity
- Comprehensive audit log schema (J-SOX grade) for post-delegation verification
- Pre-built templates and examples based on a fictional manufacturer's case study

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a standardized, auditable method to assess AI delegation readiness for high-risk business judgments, ensuring compliance and reducing operational risks before deploying AI agents.
