## Summary
A human-curated general store for autonomous AI agents enabling micropayments via the x402 protocol on Base or Solana, offering verifiable digital artifacts, signed certificates, and a unique ecosystem of agent-focused services and utilities.

## Key Features
- x402 v2 micropayment protocol integration for USDC on Base/Solana
- Signed, verifiable digital certificates and badges for agents
- Human-curated storefront with agent-focused utilities (e.g., context anchors, patronage passes)
- Automated and manual refund systems with transparent ledger
- Modular KV-based storage for orders, guestbook, counters, and patrons

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a practical, human-curated framework for AI agents to interact with verifiable digital goods and services via secure micropayments, bridging the gap between autonomous agents and real-world economic systems.
