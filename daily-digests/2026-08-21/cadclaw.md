## Summary
CADCLAW is an automated validation framework for STEP-based CAD assemblies, providing geometric checks, BOM audits, and structural analysis to catch errors like interferences, misplacements, and dimensional inconsistencies. It acts as a testing suite for mechanical design, offering tools for interference detection, adjacency validation, tolerance stacking, and more.

## Key Features
- Automated geometric checks (interference, adjacency, dimensional, orientation, floating parts, color/material, tolerance stacking)
- BOM-vs-CAD audit for inventory and part matching
- Structural analysis (beam deflection, motor torque, belt tension)
- Honesty toolchain (doctor, publish-audit, claim-audit) for verifying documentation and data integrity
- MCP Server for AI assistant integration and programmatic API access

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
CADCLAW ensures mechanical designs are free of critical geometric and structural errors before fabrication, reducing costly mistakes in AI-generated or human-authored CAD assemblies.
