## Summary
PrepAI is a privacy-first, on-device health intelligence application that combines AI with a clinical knowledge graph to provide personalized health insights. It analyzes health data like sleep, nutrition, and lab results locally, offering evidence-based recommendations without cloud processing or data sharing.

## Key Features
- On-device AI processing with Llama 3.2 and LFM2-VL for vision-based tasks
- Curated clinical knowledge graph with 197 entities and 550+ relations for evidence-based insights
- Cross-domain pattern detection across sleep, nutrition, activity, and lab results
- Privacy-first architecture with no cloud processing or data sharing
- Comprehensive health tracking including symptoms, medications, and lab results

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PrepAI demonstrates how on-device AI and local knowledge graphs can deliver personalized, privacy-preserving health insights without relying on cloud infrastructure.
