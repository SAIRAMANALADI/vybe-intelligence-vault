## Summary
Palimpsest is a tamper-evident, append-only ledger system designed to create verifiable records of AI model evaluations and censorship events. It ensures transparency by allowing anyone to verify the integrity of published data without trusting the operator, using cryptographic hashing, Merkle trees, and public anchoring mechanisms.

## Key Features
- Sealed append-only ledger with cryptographic hashing to detect any post-publication edits
- Pre-registration of AI evaluation probe sets to prevent cherry-picking or p-hacking
- Merkle root inclusion proofs for efficient verification of individual entries
- Public git history and third-party anchoring (Internet Archive, Bitcoin) for long-term integrity
- Independent witness system to detect retroactive rewrites or split views

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Palimpsest provides a critical infrastructure layer for ensuring the integrity of AI evaluations and censorship data, enabling trustless verification that is essential for auditing and regulatory compliance in AI systems.
