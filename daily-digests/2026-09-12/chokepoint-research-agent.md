## Summary
A multi-agent research workstation for supply-chain analysis and investment thesis generation using Chokepoint Theory. The system orchestrates specialized agents to reverse-engineer industry bottlenecks, generate structured reports, and perform red-team analysis on investment theses.

## Key Features
- Specialized agent architecture (Lead Analyst + 5 domain specialists) for deep research synthesis
- Chokepoint Theory methodology for identifying critical bottlenecks in supply chains
- Built-in report generation, memo management, and evidence ledger for traceability
- Support for SEC EDGAR filings, PDF/DOCX processing, and bilingual research outputs
- Offline planning, quality scoring, and red-team stress-testing of investment theses

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, multi-agent approach to supply-chain analysis and thesis validation, enabling RAG builders to generate high-quality, evidence-backed research artifacts for AI-driven decision systems.
