## Summary
Trigger.dev is an open-source platform for building, deploying, and managing AI agents and workflows in TypeScript. It provides durable, long-running tasks with retries, queues, observability, and elastic scaling without infrastructure management.

## Key Features
- Long-running tasks without timeouts, unlike traditional serverless platforms
- Durable execution with retries, queues, and idempotency for robust AI workflows
- Human-in-the-loop capabilities for approvals and feedback during task execution
- Comprehensive observability with tracing, logging, and real-time monitoring
- Multi-environment support (DEV, PREVIEW, STAGING, PROD) for seamless deployment

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI engineers to build and deploy scalable, long-running AI agents and workflows with minimal infrastructure overhead, ensuring reliability and observability.
