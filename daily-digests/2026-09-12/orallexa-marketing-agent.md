## Summary
Orallexa Marketing Agent is an open-source Python SDK and CLI tool designed to automate the generation and distribution of platform-specific marketing content for AI and open-source projects. It helps solo founders and developers create tailored posts for multiple platforms (X, Reddit, LinkedIn, etc.) with minimal manual effort.

## Key Features
- Hybrid LLM content generation with fallback to deterministic templates for cost efficiency and reliability
- Multi-platform support (X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Dev.to, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, 知乎, 小红书) with platform-specific tuning
- Self-evolving marketing strategies using bandit algorithms, reflexion memory, and ICPL preference stores
- Proactive trend-to-draft generation with engagement tracking and cooldown mechanisms to avoid spam
- Human-in-the-loop (HITL) approval workflows with Obsidian-friendly markdown and cost budgeting

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the distribution of AI and open-source projects by automating content creation and multi-platform posting, reducing the manual effort required for marketing while improving reach and engagement.
