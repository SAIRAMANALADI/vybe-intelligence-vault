## Summary
ArcReel is an open-source, self-hosted AI video production workbench that transforms novels, scripts, or product materials into consistent, controllable, and cost-trackable short videos. It provides a unified pipeline for content analysis, asset management, storyboarding, media generation, and exportable drafts compatible with Chinese video editing tools like CapCut.

## Key Features
- Unified production pipeline for novels, scripts, and product materials with role, scene, and prop asset management
- Agent-driven workflows with human-in-the-loop review, versioning, and rollback capabilities
- Multi-vendor support for text, image, video, and TTS generation with unified cost tracking
- Exportable drafts compatible with Chinese video editors (e.g., CapCut) for further editing
- Real-time task progress monitoring, interruption recovery, and detailed cost breakdowns

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ArcReel streamlines AI-driven video production by unifying asset management, generation, and cost tracking into a single self-hosted platform, making it essential for teams building scalable RAG or AI video workflows.
