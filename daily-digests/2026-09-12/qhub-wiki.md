## Summary
QHub-Wiki is a self-hosted AI-powered knowledge base for small and medium businesses, combining WikiJS and Qdrant vector search with a custom Chrome extension for AI-driven queries. It enables teams to centralize operational knowledge and reduce SaaS costs by owning their data.

## Key Features
- Integrates WikiJS with Qdrant for vector-based AI search
- Custom Chrome extension for in-browser AI queries
- Single-server deployment via Ansible for simplicity
- Supports multiple LLM APIs (paid) with GROQ fallback
- Markdown-first content structure for LLM compatibility

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
QHub-Wiki provides a cost-effective, self-hosted alternative to SaaS knowledge bases, enabling AI-driven retrieval of internal documentation while ensuring data ownership and reducing dependency on third-party tools.
