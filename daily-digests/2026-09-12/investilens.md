## Summary
InvestiLens is an autonomous multi-source investment intelligence system that generates institutional-grade equity research dossiers by cross-verifying data across 12 tier-1 financial repositories. It leverages Nous Research's Hermes Agent and Webcmd browser automation for real-time data extraction, scoring, and interactive analysis with strict human-in-the-loop governance.

## Key Features
- Autonomous 3-tier live hybrid extraction engine for real-time financial data retrieval
- Strict 2-stage Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) governance for enterprise-grade decision support
- Multi-source cross-verification across 12 tier-1 financial and regulatory repositories
- Interactive DCF valuation sandbox and peer comparison matrix for financial analysis
- Embedded RAG sidekick for conversational interrogation of research dossiers with citations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
