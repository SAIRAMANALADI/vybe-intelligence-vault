## Summary
odai is a governance-powered framework for AI agents that embeds governance into task execution, ensuring alignment with objectives, facts, assumptions, authorization, risks, and acceptance criteria while dynamically adapting to task complexity and domain needs.

## Key Features
- Embeds governance into execution with alignment to objectives, facts, and risks
- Dynamically adapts depth of handling based on task ambiguity, complexity, and domain needs
- Supports managed capability routing for project-specific role assignments
- Verifies deliverables against user-defined acceptance criteria and constraints
- Persists durable information with provenance, scope, and invalidation conditions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
