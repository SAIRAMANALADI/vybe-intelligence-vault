## Summary
DenariusAI is a personal and family finance management platform built on double-entry accounting principles. It integrates budgeting, bank reconciliation, savings tracking, investment portfolio management, and AI-powered financial insights into a single secure workspace.

## Key Features
- Double-entry accounting with accounts, categories, and transactions for balanced financial records
- AI-assisted bank reconciliation, transaction classification, and financial reporting
- Portfolio tracking for stocks, savings certificates, and insurance policies with market data integration
- Monthly budgeting and analytics with period-based comparisons and trend identification
- MCP-compatible read-only financial tools for external AI integrations
