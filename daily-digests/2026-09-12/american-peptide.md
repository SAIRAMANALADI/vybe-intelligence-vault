## Summary
AmericanPeptide.com is a research-focused platform offering a curated catalog of 56 peptides with mechanisms, dosing references, and synthesis notes, powered by a Claude-based AI agent that grounds responses in PubChem, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, and UniProt. It includes offline bench calculators, a COA decoder, and a remote MCP server for integration with AI tools.

## Key Features
- Curated peptide catalog with 56 compounds, mechanisms, and research summaries
- Claude-powered AI agent that grounds responses in primary scientific sources (PubChem, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, UniProt)
- Offline bench calculators (reconstitution, blend, COA decoder) as a PWA
- Remote MCP server for integrating the catalog and tools into AI clients like ChatGPT or Cursor
- CC BY 4.0 licensed content for open reuse with attribution

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a trustworthy, AI-grounded research layer for peptide data with open licensing, enabling RAG builders to integrate verified scientific sources and offline tools into their workflows.
