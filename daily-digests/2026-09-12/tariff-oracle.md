## Summary
TariffBrain is an AI-native trade compliance microservice that automates HTS classification, duty calculation, and regulatory screening across 217 jurisdictions for autonomous agents and enterprise supply chains. It replaces static lookup tables with real-time, explainable AI reasoning for cross-border trade compliance.

## Key Features
- Autonomous HTS classification across 217 jurisdictions using structured AI reasoning
- Real-time duty calculation with support for ad valorem, specific, and compound rates
- PGA screening against 50+ regulatory agencies (FDA, FCC, REACH, etc.)
- Explainable AI outputs with full citations and confidence scoring
- Profile-based configuration for per-client, per-product, or per-region customization

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
TariffBrain automates critical trade compliance tasks for AI agents and supply chains, reducing duty leakage and eliminating manual classification errors in cross-border logistics.
