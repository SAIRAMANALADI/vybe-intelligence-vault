## Summary
A full-stack Legal AI Assistant designed to simplify complex Indian legal documents, statutes, and court procedures using a hybrid RAG architecture. It leverages FastAPI, Google Gemini 3.5 Flash, and vector embeddings to deliver accessible legal insights with real-time streaming and source attribution.

## Key Features
- Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with vector embeddings and cross-encoder reranking for high-precision legal context retrieval
- Domain guardrails and noise filtering to reject off-topic queries and prevent hallucinations
- Real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming for low-latency responses
- Multi-turn chat history with SQLAlchemy database persistence for conversation context
- Dynamic source attribution citing specific legal documents used in response generation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This repository provides a production-ready hybrid RAG pipeline tailored for legal document understanding, making it essential for AI engineers building domain-specific retrieval systems.
