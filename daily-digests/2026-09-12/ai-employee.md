## Summary
AI-Employee is a self-hosted multi-agent AI platform designed for teams requiring compliance, governance, and data isolation. It enables the deployment of isolated Docker-based agents with semantic memory, knowledge bases, and governance rules, supporting collaboration, automation, and real-time voice interactions.

## Key Features
- Isolated Docker-based agent containers with per-agent semantic memory and knowledge bases
- Visual workflow builder (n8n-style) with drag-and-drop automation and live execution monitoring
- Multi-user support with role-based data isolation and audit logging for compliance
- Real-time voice assistant with STT/TTS and live steering for mid-task corrections
- LLM-agnostic architecture supporting Claude, OpenAI Codex, Gemini, Bedrock, Azure, and local models

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AI-Employee provides a compliant, self-hosted multi-agent orchestration platform with built-in governance and isolation, critical for regulated industries and teams prioritizing data sovereignty in RAG and AI workflows.
