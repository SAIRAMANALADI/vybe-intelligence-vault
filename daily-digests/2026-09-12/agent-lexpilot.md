## Summary
AGENT-lexpilot is a privacy-first legal compliance agent built on the Hypha framework, offering two core capabilities: a free legal self-check tool for individuals and a controlled natural language data analysis workbench for legal professionals. It ensures verifiable legal references, PII sanitization, and strict output validation.

## Key Features
- Privacy-first design with deterministic PII sanitization and local encryption
- Verifiable legal references with SHA-256 auditing and official source validation
- Controlled AI output with structured validation and fallback to deterministic pipelines
- Read-only data analysis with schema drift detection and user confirmation workflows
- Hypha-based FSM workflows for legal domain-specific agent logic
