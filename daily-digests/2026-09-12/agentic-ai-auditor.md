## Summary
Agentic AI Compliance Extractor is a RAG-based tool that extracts and verifies compliance policies from regulatory documents using vector search and LLM inference. It enables semantic querying of complex PDFs with fast, context-aware responses.

## Key Features
- Intelligent document parsing for complex PDFs
- Hybrid vector database management (persistent and temporary)
- Semantic search with high-speed LLM inference
- Interactive Streamlit-based web interface
- Supports custom and pre-loaded regulatory documents

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines compliance policy extraction from regulatory documents, making it essential for AI systems requiring adherence to frameworks like NIST AI RMF.
