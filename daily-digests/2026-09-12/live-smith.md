## Summary
Live Smith is an AI-powered assistant that integrates with Ableton Live to enable conversational editing, arrangement, and analysis of musical projects. It allows users to interact with tracks, clips, devices, and mixer settings through natural language while maintaining granular control over edit permissions and approval workflows.

## Key Features
- Natural language interaction with Ableton Live projects (tracks, clips, devices, mixer settings)
- Granular edit scope and approval controls (Manual, Low Risk, Accept Everything)
- Multi-model support with context window customization and hosted web search
- Session-based conversation history with attachments (images, documents, audio)
- Built-in musical skills and direct API/model provider integrations (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Live Smith bridges the gap between AI-driven music production and professional DAW workflows, enabling RAG builders to integrate real-time, context-aware editing tools for creative applications.
