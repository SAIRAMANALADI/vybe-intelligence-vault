## Summary
FUSION is a framework designed to unify scientific intelligence in nuclear physics by providing an AI agent with 26 expert skills that automate the installation, execution, and verification of open-source nuclear physics codes. It includes a 61,000+ page offline knowledge base of nuclear physics literature and supports multiple AI agents like opencode, Claude Code, and Codex.

## Key Features
- 26 expert skills for installing, running, and verifying nuclear physics codes (e.g., FRESCO, TALYS, SMASH)
- 61,167 pages of offline nuclear physics literature for context-aware AI responses
- Cross-platform support (macOS, Linux) with pre-built binaries and verified installation paths
- Tiered verification system (Tier 1: reference output reproduction, Tier 2: physics invariants or analytic solutions)
- Adversarial review process for each skill to ensure robustness and correctness
