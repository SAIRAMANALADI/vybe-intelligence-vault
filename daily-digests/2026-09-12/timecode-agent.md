## Summary
Timecode-agent is a Windows desktop application that converts video files into organized, timestamped evidence ledgers for coders and editors. It automates the extraction of key moments from videos, enabling faster review and integration into coding workflows without manual effort.

## Key Features
- Automated creation of timestamped evidence ledgers from video files
- Supports common video formats (MKV, MP4, MOV) with local processing
- Exports evidence in industry-standard formats (FCPXML, EDL, SRT) for editors and coders
- Lazy verification system to review segments without rewatching full videos
- Privacy-focused with all processing done locally to protect intellectual property

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines video evidence extraction for coders, reducing manual review time and integrating seamlessly into local workflows for AI and RAG pipeline builders.
