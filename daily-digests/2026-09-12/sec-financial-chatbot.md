## Summary
Filing Room is an open-source SEC research workspace that enables users to explore and analyze public company 10-K and 10-Q filings through a conversational interface. It combines deterministic financial calculations, filing-scoped retrieval, and source-level citations in a React-based editorial interface.

## Key Features
- Deterministic financial calculations from SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q)
- Filing-scoped retrieval with exact source citations and excerpts
- Streamed answers and real-time progress via Server-Sent Events (SSE)
- Structured chunking of narrative disclosures and standalone tables
- Deterministic XBRL fact selection with amendment handling and growth metrics

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a specialized, citation-backed RAG system for querying complex SEC financial documents, enabling accurate and traceable AI-driven financial research.
