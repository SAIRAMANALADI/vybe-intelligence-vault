## Summary
Achiote Food Memory Researcher is a structured research agent designed to reconstruct half-remembered family dishes, drinks, or food rituals from fragmented sensory clues, sound-alikes, or cultural references. It operates as a companion tool for heritage taste recovery, using evidence-based research methods to produce a 'Memory Receipt' with a testable first taste before full recipes.

## Key Features
- Structured research protocol for food memory reconstruction with evidence separation (user-said, researched, inferred, unknown)
- Generates a 'Memory Receipt' with a testable first taste before full recipes
- Supports CLI, MCP, and skill-based agent entrypoints for integration with AI workspaces
- Uses a source quality ladder and evidence-ledger for disciplined research
- Handles sparse or messy input with targeted questions and hypothesis testing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a rigorous, evidence-based framework for reconstructing lost or half-remembered food memories, which is critical for preserving cultural heritage and personal identity tied to cuisine.
