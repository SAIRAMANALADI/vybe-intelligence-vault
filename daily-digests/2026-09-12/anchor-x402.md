## Summary
Anchor-x402 provides sixteen on-chain paid services for AI agents, including commodity primitives (hash anchoring, wallet screening, transaction decoding), trust infrastructure (signed attestations, sanctions checks), and LLM utilities (text analysis, RNG). It operates as a serverless AWS Lambda with dual-chain (Base/Solana) and JPYC/Polygon payment rails, offering verifiable, pay-per-call cryptographic receipts.

## Key Features
- Dual-chain (Base/Solana) and JPYC/Polygon payment rails with USDC/JPYC support
- Sixteen pay-per-call services including hash anchoring, wallet screening, and LLM utilities
- Signed cryptographic receipts for trust infrastructure (attestations, sanctions checks)
- Serverless AWS Lambda deployment with OpenAPI/Swagger documentation
- Auto-indexed across agent-discovery surfaces (CDP Bazaar, agentic.market, etc.)
