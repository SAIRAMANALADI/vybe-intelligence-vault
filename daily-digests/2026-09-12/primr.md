## Summary
Primr is a CLI and agent framework designed to generate evidence-grounded, long-form strategic reports for companies by analyzing public web data, DNS reconnaissance, and other open sources. It produces structured strategic overviews and AI strategy documents with uncertainty labels and citations, supporting both zero-cost local execution and provider-backed paid research paths.

## Key Features
- Generates structured strategic reports with confidence labels (Confirmed/Reported/Estimated/Hypothesis)
- Supports both zero-cost local execution (Primr Zero) and provider-backed paid research paths
- Automated evidence collection via web scraping, DNS recon, and public data sources
- Produces reusable Markdown and DOCX artifacts for human and agent consumption
- Includes cost controls, dry-run estimates, and explicit approval workflows for billable runs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Primr streamlines the creation of high-quality, evidence-backed strategic reports for companies, reducing manual research effort and enabling AI agents to generate reliable, structured insights for RAG pipelines.
