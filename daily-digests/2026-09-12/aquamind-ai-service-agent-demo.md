## Summary
AquaMind AI Service Agent is a demo industrial equipment after-sales AI diagnostic assistant that transforms unstructured customer fault descriptions into structured, traceable service workflows using LLM, RAG, and agent-based diagnosis. It bridges the gap between expert knowledge and non-expert users by guiding step-by-step troubleshooting with grounded answers and seamless human handoff.

## Key Features
- Intent recognition for fault descriptions with structured extraction of device, error codes, and urgency
- Grounded RAG with source attribution for verifiable answers and reduced hallucination risk
- State-machine-driven diagnosis workflow with step-by-step user guidance and branching logic
- Seamless human-in-the-loop escalation with preserved context and automated ticket generation
- Multi-role web dashboard for customers, engineers, and admins with role-based access control

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates a production-ready blueprint for integrating LLM, RAG, and agent workflows to standardize and automate complex diagnostic processes in industrial after-sales, reducing expert dependency and improving service efficiency.
