## Summary
OMEM is a memory layer for AI agents that tracks beliefs, contradictions, and provenance with an explicit audit trail. It enables agents to reason about what they know, when they learned it, and why, while handling conflicts transparently and refusing to overwrite evidence.

## Key Features
- Explicit belief state management with states like BELIEVED_TRUE, CONTRADICTED, and UNKNOWN
- Provenance tracking for every memory, enabling 'why' queries with full evidence chains
- Contradiction handling that surfaces conflicts without overwriting evidence
- Self-healing infrastructure with policy-based repairs and human approval gates
- Semantic recall with offline-compatible embeddings and optional provider integration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
OMEM provides the critical memory layer for RAG systems that require verifiable, conflict-resilient, and auditable knowledge tracking, ensuring agents can justify their actions with evidence and human oversight.
