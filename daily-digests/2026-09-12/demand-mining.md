## Summary
A daily demand mining tool that extracts, pools, and ranks user demands from product communities (e.g., Discord) using deterministic gates and LLM-powered scoring. It generates prioritized iteration queues for shipped products while ensuring privacy through PII redaction and fail-closed rules.

## Key Features
- Privacy-first demand extraction with PII redaction and fail-closed gates
- Multi-axis demand ranking using RICE, Opportunity, WSJF, and Kano models
- Daily EOD brainstorm generation with prioritized iteration queues
- Configurable per-product tunables and secrets management
- Deterministic offline preview mode for testing without network or writes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, privacy-compliant way to mine and prioritize user demands from raw community signals, enabling data-driven product iteration without exposing sensitive data.
