## Summary
9router_proxy is a local Windows-based proxy tool designed to intercept, inspect, modify, and route AI service requests before they reach external providers. It enables users to manage and sanitize data sent to AI tools, ensuring privacy and customization.

## Key Features
- Intercepts and logs AI service requests locally
- Allows real-time modification of request payloads and headers
- Supports reverse and forward proxy modes for flexible routing
- Runs entirely on-device with no external data transmission
- Provides a user-friendly dashboard for managing and editing requests

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It empowers AI engineers to control and customize AI request data before it reaches external providers, enhancing privacy and enabling precise modifications for better AI interactions.
