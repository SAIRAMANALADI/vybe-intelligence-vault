## Summary
Pickle is a free, open-source MCP server that audits ClickUp, Slack, and Microsoft Teams for missed tasks, dropped promises, and decisions buried in DMs. It runs locally on your machine with your own tokens, ensuring no telemetry or third-party data exposure.

## Key Features
- Cross-tool auditing across ClickUp, Slack, and Teams using a single local server
- Read-only by default with optional write capabilities for reminders or tasks
- No telemetry, no account required, and no third-party data exposure
- 25-pattern detection engine for stale tasks, dropped promises, and buried decisions
- MCP-compatible for seamless integration with AI clients like Claude, Cursor, and Zed

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Pickle enables RAG builders to audit and monitor cross-platform workflows locally, ensuring no critical tasks or decisions slip through while maintaining strict data privacy.
