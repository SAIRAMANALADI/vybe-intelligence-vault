## Summary
Seiche is a free, open-source terminal for monitoring funding-stress signals in US money markets, Treasury markets, and global funding basins. It aggregates public APIs to provide forward-looking analytics, alerts, and accountability layers for detecting and forecasting financial stress events.

## Key Features
- 22 specialized engines analyzing funding stress signals across US and global markets
- Forward-looking analytics with walk-forward validation and reliability tables
- Hash-chained, tamper-evident track record for accountability and provenance
- Integration with 12+ free public APIs (FRED, NY Fed, Treasury, CFTC, etc.)
- Advanced statistical and machine learning models (e.g., Bathymetry, Undertow, Swell Forecast)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Seiche provides a unique, opinionated fusion layer for detecting and forecasting funding stress events using public data, offering transparency and accountability critical for RAG builders in financial AI systems.
