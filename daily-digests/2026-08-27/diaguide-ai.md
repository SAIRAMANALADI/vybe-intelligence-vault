## Summary
DiaGuide AI is an evidence-based diabetes guidance assistant that leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to answer questions using only clinical guidelines from ADA, NICE, and CDC. It ensures answers are grounded in retrieved text with transparent citations and structured drug references.

## Key Features
- Grounded RAG pipeline with strict adherence to retrieved clinical guidelines
- Scope detection to prevent out-of-domain hallucinations
- Conversation-aware follow-up question rewriting for multi-turn interactions
- Automatic medication extraction and drug classification from answers
- Transparent source citations with grouped deduplication and persistent chat history

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DiaGuide AI demonstrates a production-ready RAG pipeline for healthcare applications, emphasizing evidence grounding, transparency, and scope control—critical for building trustworthy AI systems in regulated domains.
