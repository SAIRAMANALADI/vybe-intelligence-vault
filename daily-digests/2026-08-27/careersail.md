## Summary
CareerSail is an AI Agent-driven job search dashboard that automates job discovery, application tracking, and resume customization for campus recruitment. It leverages browser automation and external tools to fetch real-time job postings from company career pages and syncs company databases via Feishu.

## Key Features
- AI-driven job discovery with real-time browser automation to fetch postings directly from company career pages
- Seamless integration with Feishu for syncing 450+ autumn recruitment companies via lark-cli
- Automated resume customization using AI tools like personal-career-os for tailored applications
- Progress tracking dashboard with status updates (Pending, Submitted, Offer, Rejected) and timeline visualization
- Cross-agent compatibility supporting Trae, Claude Code, Codex, and others with standardized MCP configurations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the entire campus recruitment process by automating job discovery, application tracking, and resume customization, reducing manual effort for AI engineers and job seekers.
