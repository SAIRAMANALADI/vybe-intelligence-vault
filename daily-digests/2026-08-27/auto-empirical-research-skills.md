## Summary
Auto-Empirical Research Skills (AERS) is a curated collection of 1,151 AI agent skills designed to automate the end-to-end workflow of empirical research, from ideation to publication. It integrates 74 skill collections, including 7 Stanford REAP × CoPaper.AI proprietary tools, to support data acquisition, causal inference, statistical modeling, robustness checks, and academic writing.

## Key Features
- 74 curated skill collections covering the full empirical research pipeline (ideation, literature review, data acquisition, causal inference, modeling, robustness checks, and publication)
- 7 proprietary Stanford REAP × CoPaper.AI tools (e.g., StatsPAI, Paper-WorkFlow) for end-to-end automation and reproducibility
- 1,151 vendor-integrated skills with machine-readable catalog and CI-enforced freshness
- 17 numeric benchmarks and 37 behavioral evaluation scenarios for rigor validation
- Multi-language support (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean) and security audits (OpenSSF Scorecard, security scan reports)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
