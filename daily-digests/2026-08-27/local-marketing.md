## Summary
A local-first, agent-agnostic marketing automation tool that enables solo founders to automate customer acquisition workflows entirely on their machine. It interviews users about their product, researches prospects, drafts outreach sequences, and sends emails via SMTP—all with human approval at every step.

## Key Features
- Interactive init interview to scaffold local marketing data directory
- Agent-assisted research and scoring of manually curated prospect lists
- Drafts multi-email sequences stored in `pending_review/` for human approval
- File-based approval workflow (move to `approved/` to send)
- Circuit-breaker safety checks for bounce/complaint rates before sending

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a privacy-preserving, locally controlled alternative to SaaS-based outreach tools, ensuring full data ownership while leveraging AI agents for automation.
