## Summary
Doodleworks MCP is an MCP App that transforms ideas, blog posts, or articles into hand-drawn 'Tinku' illustrations explaining key concepts. It integrates with AI hosts to render interactive viewers for browsing, regenerating, and downloading illustrations.

## Key Features
- Converts text-based ideas into hand-drawn contraption-style illustrations using AI (OpenAI or Gemini API)
- Provides an interactive viewer within supported AI hosts for browsing, regenerating, and downloading illustrations
- Supports one idea per illustration with clean, minimalist, and explanatory visuals
- Integrates seamlessly with MCP-compatible AI hosts like Cursor, VS Code, and Claude Desktop
- Allows customization and regeneration of individual illustrations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Doodleworks MCP enhances AI-driven content creation by transforming complex ideas into intuitive, hand-drawn visual explanations, improving clarity and engagement for RAG and AI stack builders.
