## Summary
TradeMind is an open-source AI-driven cross-border e-commerce operations platform designed for sellers and development teams. It streamlines the workflow from product collection to AI content optimization, publishing, and order/inventory synchronization across multiple platforms.

## Key Features
- AI-powered product content generation (titles, descriptions, images) with provider abstraction
- Multi-platform product publishing and order/inventory synchronization (Douyin Shop, 1688, Taobao, etc.)
- Modular provider architecture for extensibility (AI, storage, image, platform, collector)
- Self-hosted deployment with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL/Redis backend
- Comprehensive documentation, roadmap, and demo scripts for quick onboarding

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
TradeMind provides a critical orchestration layer for RAG builders by automating and optimizing cross-border e-commerce workflows, reducing manual effort in product content generation and synchronization.
