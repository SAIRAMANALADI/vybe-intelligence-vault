## Summary
A lightweight AI agent that analyzes Polymarket prediction markets to identify mispriced opportunities by comparing AI-estimated fair values against market odds. It flags actionable edges where the AI disagrees with the market by more than 5%.

## Key Features
- Scans 18+ prediction markets across 6 categories (politics, crypto, sports, tech, finance, culture)
- AI fair value analysis using historical data and current events
- Edge detection flags opportunities where AI disagrees with market odds by >5%
- Real-time AI reasoning with visual typing effects
- Interactive sentiment gauge and probability bars for market mood
