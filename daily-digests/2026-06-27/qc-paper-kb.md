## Summary
QC-Paper-KB is a Windows-based knowledge base application designed to organize and provide searchable access to over 20,000 quantum computing research papers from 25 major venues. It offers semantic search, trend analysis, venue recommendations, and LLM integration to streamline quantum computing literature review and discovery.

## Key Features
- Curated database of 20,000+ quantum computing papers from 25 venues
- Semantic search and topic-based browsing capabilities
- Trend analysis and venue recommendation tools
- LLM integration for research assistance and paper discovery
- Local Windows application with offline access and sync options
