## Summary
An AI-agent workspace designed to assist social workers and nonprofit teams in navigating public benefits for residents. It screens needs, retrieves program guidance, generates document checklists, and ensures auditable handoffs while avoiding final eligibility decisions.

## Key Features
- Agent loop with safety classification, tool execution, and trace persistence
- Program retrieval for food, healthcare, housing, and childcare support
- Crisis escalation for unsafe situations and fraud/bypass guardrails
- Document checklist generation and missing-information detection
- React-based social-worker console with trace inspection

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines public benefits navigation for social workers, reducing fragmentation and improving resident access to critical services through AI-driven guidance and auditable workflows.
