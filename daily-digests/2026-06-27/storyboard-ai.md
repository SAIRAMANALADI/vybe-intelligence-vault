## Summary
storyboard-ai is a Windows desktop application designed to help users quickly create and organize visual storyboards for video planning, pitch decks, or project preproduction. It simplifies scene and shot planning with a drag-and-drop interface and note-taking features.

## Key Features
- Drag-and-drop frame reordering for shots and scenes
- Add scene notes directly next to frames for clarity
- Save and export storyboards for sharing or review
- Lightweight and fast setup with no complex dependencies
- Supports short films, school projects, and pitch decks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
