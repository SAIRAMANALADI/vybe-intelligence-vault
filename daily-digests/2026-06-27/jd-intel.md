## Summary
jd-intel is a toolkit that enables AI assistants to fetch and normalize job descriptions directly from Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) across seven major platforms, eliminating manual copy-pasting and ensuring structured, up-to-date data for AI-driven job searches and resume tailoring.

## Key Features
- Direct JD fetching from 7 major ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, etc.) without scraping or browser automation
- Normalized job data schema across all platforms, including salary, location, department, and clean markdown descriptions
- MCP server integration for seamless AI assistant tool calling (Claude, Cursor, VS Code, etc.)
- CLI and library support for programmatic access to job postings and company registries
- Company registry with 300+ verified companies and support for adding new entries via PRs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It automates the tedious process of manually feeding job descriptions into AI assistants, enabling seamless, real-time job search and resume tailoring for AI-driven workflows.
