## Summary
A single-file Python framework that transforms LLMs into autonomous research scientists capable of designing, executing, and refining experiments without human intervention. It automates hypothesis generation, experiment orchestration, and statistical analysis to accelerate scientific discovery.

## Key Features
- Self-directed hypothesis generation and testing with iterative refinement loops
- Multi-model support (OpenAI, Claude, local HuggingFace models) with automatic fallback
- Built-in statistical analysis (p-value, effect size, confidence intervals) for rigorous validation
- Customizable research profiles to tailor AI behavior (risk tolerance, creativity balance)
- Real-time experiment simulation and result logging with exportable research reports

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to autonomously design and validate experiments, reducing manual effort in RAG pipelines by automating hypothesis-driven research and iterative refinement.
