## Summary
Lever-GaoKao is an AI Agent designed to assist with college admissions in China by helping students and families make informed decisions about university and major choices. It provides structured guidance, risk assessments, and actionable recommendations based on user inputs like scores, preferences, and constraints.

## Key Features
- Structured intake of student and family constraints (scores, preferences, budget, etc.)
- Generates candidate university and major lists with risk assessments and verification steps
- Provides clear reasoning for each recommendation to avoid emotional or biased decisions
- Includes mechanical validation tools for candidate tables to ensure data integrity
- Non-commercial, open-source license with strict usage restrictions to prevent exploitation
