## Summary
buyer-eval-skill is a Windows-based tool designed to help B2B teams systematically evaluate software vendors by guiding structured conversations, scoring vendor responses against evidence, and maintaining shared notes for buying teams.

## Key Features
- Domain-expert questions to test vendor understanding of business needs
- Vendor AI agent conversation review for detecting vague claims or gaps
- Evidence-based scoring system to quantify feature match, clarity, and risk
- Shared buyer notes for collaborative vendor comparison
- Structured workflow from vague claims to clear, defensible scores

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a repeatable framework for B2B teams to objectively evaluate vendor claims and AI agents, reducing guesswork in procurement decisions.
