## Summary
A Codex Skill designed to generate structured, exam-ready PDF review packs for open-book exams by integrating course materials, past papers, textbooks, and formatting requirements into a printable and searchable document.

## Key Features
- Automatically integrates course materials, past papers, and textbooks into a structured PDF
- Generates chapter-wise quick-reference sections, high-frequency question templates, and calculation steps
- Supports OCR for scanned PDFs and handles Chinese character paths
- Offers multiple modes (pack, page-index, past-paper, calculation, polish) for tailored outputs
- Provides verified textbook page indexes and compact yet readable formatting
