## Summary
A cross-CLI skill for Obsidian that transforms a static vault into a self-maintaining, AI-first second brain. It enables 45+ commands across Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, and OpenCode to automatically update notes, reconcile contradictions, synthesize insights, and integrate real-time research from the web and local sources.

## Key Features
- 45+ cross-CLI commands for real-time vault updates and research synthesis
- Automatic contradiction reconciliation and stale fact replacement in existing notes
- Scheduled agents (nightly, weekly) for vault maintenance and pattern discovery
- Dual-track research: open-web (Perplexity/Grok) and vault-grounded (Gemini File Search)
- AI-first note format with structured frontmatter and retrieval-optimized content

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables RAG builders to maintain a continuously updated, contradiction-free knowledge base that compounds insights over time without manual intervention.
