## Summary
ALwrity is an AI-powered digital marketing platform that acts as a contextual content operating system, enabling users to create, manage, and publish branded content across multiple channels like blogs, social media, YouTube, and podcasts. It leverages brand-specific data, competitor analysis, and multi-agent workflows to generate on-brand, factual, and SEO-optimized content.

## Key Features
- Contextual content OS with brand memory and competitor analysis
- Multi-channel content generation (blogs, social media, YouTube, podcasts)
- Agent-driven workflows for research, planning, writing, and optimization
- Production-ready features like JWT/OAuth2 auth, usage tracking, and cost awareness
- Grounded content generation with citations and SEO integration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ALwrity streamlines AI-driven content creation by centralizing brand context and multi-agent workflows, reducing tool fragmentation and ensuring consistent, on-brand outputs across diverse marketing channels.
