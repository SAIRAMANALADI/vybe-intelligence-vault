## Summary
A polyglot data-quality and entity-resolution toolkit designed for AI-native workflows. It provides a suite of tools (GoldenCheck, GoldenFlow, GoldenMatch, etc.) for profiling, standardizing, deduplicating, and analyzing data, with zero-config defaults and high scalability from CSV to 100M+ rows.

## Key Features
- Zero-config entity resolution with Fellegi-Sunter probabilistic matching, beating hand-tuned Splink in benchmarks
- Polyglot support across Python, TypeScript, Rust, and SQL (PostgreSQL/DuckDB) with parity guarantees
- Identity graph for durable entity tracking across runs and merges/splits
- Privacy-preserving record linkage (PPRL) for cross-organization matching
- AI-native integration with MCP, REST APIs, and A2A agents, plus auto-configuration and telemetry

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
GoldenMatch provides a robust, scalable, and AI-native solution for entity resolution and data deduplication, essential for building high-quality RAG pipelines with clean, deduplicated data sources.
