## Summary
Morning Intel automates the daily market research workflow by aggregating fresh articles from RSS feeds across finance, geopolitics, tech, and AI, filtering and deduplicating them, then using Google Gemini to generate structured insights. It saves these insights to Notion and delivers a formatted HTML email digest every morning.

## Key Features
- End-to-end automation of daily market research workflow
- Filters and deduplicates articles from multiple RSS feeds
- Uses Google Gemini to generate structured, conversational insights
- Saves insights to Notion for long-term knowledge management
- Delivers formatted HTML email digests via Gmail
