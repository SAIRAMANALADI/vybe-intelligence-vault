## Summary
HumanStudy-Bench provides a standardized framework for replaying human-subject experiments with AI agents, enabling evaluation of agent behavior, effects, and scientific findings through runnable social-science studies. It includes an execution engine, a growing collection of studies, and tools for both automated and manual study contributions.

## Key Features
- Standardized execution engine for replaying human-subject experiments with AI agents
- Collection of 12+ runnable social-science studies covering cognition, strategic interaction, and social psychology
- Dual contribution paths: automated Build Study via HumanStudy-Hub and direct pull requests
- Playground for replaying studies with custom models and comparing results against original human data
- Validation pipeline for ensuring study integrity and reproducibility

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables rigorous evaluation of AI agents by simulating human-subject experiments, bridging the gap between AI behavior and real-world social science findings.
