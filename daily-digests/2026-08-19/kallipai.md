## Summary
Kallip AI is an early-stage agent harness designed as a multi-agent system where agents autonomously manage subagents, context, and coordination across projects without relying on a top-down orchestration layer. It emphasizes explicit context management and asynchronous approval workflows for multi-agent scenarios.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent coordination without top-down orchestration
- Explicit agent-managed context with tools like `context_pin`, `context_evict`, and `context_status`
- Asynchronous approval system for risky actions with programmatic or human oversight
- Emergent skill management through file-based context pinning
- Headless CLI and TUI for human interaction and automation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Kallip AI introduces a novel approach to multi-agent systems with explicit context management and asynchronous approvals, enabling scalable and autonomous agent coordination critical for advanced RAG and AI workflows.
