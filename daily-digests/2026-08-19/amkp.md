## Summary
AMKP (Agentic Multimodal Knowledge Plane) is an API/SDK/MCP-first platform that provides Evidence, Policy, and Tenancy management for multi-product RAG systems. It enables platform teams to ingest multimodal documents once, enforce hard tenant isolation, and perform guarded agentic retrieval with budgets and traces, eliminating the need to rebuild RAG stacks per product.

## Key Features
- Hard tenant isolation for multi-product RAG systems
- Structure-aware document ingest with Parse Ladder and TableEvidence
- Guarded agentic retrieval with budgets, traces, and metrics
- Built-in evaluation and POC pack for self-serve testing
- TypeScript SDK and MCP-first architecture for seamless integration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AMKP provides a unified, scalable foundation for multi-product RAG systems, eliminating redundant RAG stack development and ensuring secure, governed retrieval across tenants.
