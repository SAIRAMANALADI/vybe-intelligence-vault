## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server providing real-time market intelligence, macroeconomic analysis, institutional positioning, and real estate calculators for AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor. Powered by the BullrunData API, it offers tools for recession probability modeling, sector rotation analysis, cascade scenario simulations, and economic data retrieval.

## Key Features
- Real-time recession probability modeling with confirmation layers
- Sector rotation and institutional positioning analysis
- Macro cascade scenario engine for risk assessment
- Comprehensive economic indicator time-series data
- Real estate investment calculators (cap rate, DSCR, BRRRR analysis)
