## Summary
EduFlow is an AI-driven self-learning platform for students, centered around personalized learning paths, adaptive assessments, and multi-agent AI support. Unlike traditional LMS tools, it prioritizes student autonomy while leveraging AI agents for tutoring, content recommendation, and progress tracking.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent AI support (Tutor, Buddy, Examiner, Planner) for personalized learning
- Adaptive learning paths with spaced repetition and progress tracking
- Interactive content marketplace and resource recommendation system
- Gamification elements (achievements, leaderboards, challenges)
- Comprehensive documentation and deployment guides for scalability
