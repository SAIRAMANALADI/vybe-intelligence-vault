## Summary
AgentDoc is an autonomous AI document workspace that transforms natural language prompts into publication-grade business reports, strategic plans, and technical architectures. It combines multi-stage LLM reasoning with real-time interactive editing and professional document exports (PDF/DOCX/Markdown) for consultants and analysts.

## Key Features
- Autonomous planning and reasoning with LLM-powered agents for structured document generation
- Human-in-the-loop review mode for inline editing and task reordering before execution
- Real-time streaming execution with Server-Sent Events (SSE) for live progress tracking
- Professional-grade document exports in PDF, DOCX, and Markdown with custom styling
- Notion-inspired workspace with client-side IndexedDB storage for document history and insights

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AgentDoc streamlines the transformation of raw LLM outputs into polished, executive-ready documents, bridging the gap between AI generation and professional deliverables for RAG stack builders.
