## Summary
An n8n-based AI agent that transforms simple chatbots into autonomous systems capable of capturing leads, logging them to Google Sheets, booking meetings on Google Calendar, and notifying teams via Gmail. Designed to automate front-office tasks for Zambian SMEs.

## Key Features
- Autonomous lead capture and logging to Google Sheets with structured data extraction
- Automated meeting booking in Google Calendar with visitor attendance
- Real-time team notifications via Gmail for high-priority leads
- Conversation memory with a rolling 20-message window for context retention
- Guardrails to prevent hallucinations and escalate uncertain queries to humans

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates how low-code orchestration tools like n8n can bridge the gap between AI chatbots and fully autonomous agents, enabling SMEs to automate critical front-office workflows without expensive infrastructure.
