## Summary
Agent Oasis is a native macOS application that serves as a local-first, encrypted ledger for tracking AI agents, products, APIs, experiments, costs, and business value. It enforces strict provenance rules to distinguish between measured cash flows and estimated modeled values, ensuring transparency and integrity in financial and operational tracking.

## Key Features
- Strict separation of measured cash flows and estimated modeled values with provenance tracking
- AES-256-GCM encrypted workspaces with Keychain integration and Touch ID authentication
- Attention Inbox for actionable gaps (blocked agents, stale sources, zero-evidence modeled value)
- Command palette (⌘K) and keyboard shortcuts for efficient navigation and operations
- Append-only audit trail with tamper detection and encrypted backups

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Agent Oasis provides a rigorous, local-first framework for tracking AI agent value and costs with uncompromising provenance, ensuring RAG builders can maintain transparent and auditable financial and operational records.
