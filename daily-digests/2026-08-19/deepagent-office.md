## Summary
DeepAgent Office is an open-source conversational tool for creating, revising, and reviewing professional Office files (PowerPoint, Word, Excel) using natural language. It enables users to generate polished documents from structured data, templates, or instructions while maintaining consistency and quality.

## Key Features
- Natural language-driven document creation and editing for PowerPoint, Word, and Excel
- Template and visual identity enforcement for consistent output
- Multi-file deliverable coordination and content consistency checks
- Pre-delivery quality review and refinement via conversation
- Open-source personal edition with enterprise-grade features in paid version
