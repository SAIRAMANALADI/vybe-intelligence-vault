## Summary
A Gemini CLI extension providing real-time crypto market insights, including whale trades, Smart Money Radar, liquidations, and market snapshots across 15 exchanges and multiple blockchains. It offers live data on whale activity, DEX swaps, and market sentiment without requiring API keys for basic features.

## Key Features
- Live whale trades across 15 CEXs, Hyperliquid, and Ethereum/Base/Arbitrum DEXs
- Smart Money Radar for identifying unusual whale activity
- Market snapshots with 24h stats, funding, open interest, and liquidations
- On-chain DEX whale swap tracking with wallet identification
- Free tier with no API key required for basic features
