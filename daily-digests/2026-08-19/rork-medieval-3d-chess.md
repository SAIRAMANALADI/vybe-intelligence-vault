## Summary
A cinematic 3D chess game in the browser featuring three historical armies (medieval European, Mesoamerican, and Napoleonic) with rigged 3D characters, dynamic animations, and a computer opponent. Built as a static site with no backend, it emphasizes immersive visuals, sound, and gameplay mechanics.

## Key Features
- Rigged 3D character animations with skeletal rigging for realistic movement and combat
- Three distinct historical armies with unique visuals, sounds, and mechanics
- AI opponent with adjustable difficulty running in a Web Worker for smooth performance
- Cinematic camera effects, dynamic lighting, and immersive audio feedback
- Static site deployment with no backend required, supporting offline play
