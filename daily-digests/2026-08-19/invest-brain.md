## Summary
InvestBrain is an MCP-compatible tool designed to enforce investment discipline by acting as a 'mirror' and 'discipline anchor' for experienced investors. It combines RAG-based investment knowledge retrieval, behavior pattern mining, and reminder systems to help users align their actions with their investment principles.

## Key Features
- MCP-compatible agent with 40+ tools for investment workflows (thought recording, RAG queries, reminders, pattern detection)
- Behavior pattern mining to identify and correct recurring investment mistakes (e.g., panic selling, chasing gains)
- RAG-based retrieval of insights from 16 investment masters (e.g., Buffett, Munger, Howard Marks) and 5 core concepts (e.g., moat, margin of safety)
- Local memory system with SQLite for storing user decisions, thoughts, and reminders without cloud dependency
- Multi-channel reminders (Feishu, DingTalk, Bark) triggered by price, time, or custom conditions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
InvestBrain provides a structured framework for AI-driven investment discipline, bridging the gap between knowledge and action through RAG-powered insights and behavior pattern analysis.
