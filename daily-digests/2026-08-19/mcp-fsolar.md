## Summary
mcp-fsolar is a multi-mode toolkit that connects to Felicity Solar battery systems, exposing real-time battery data (SOC, SOH, cell voltages, temperatures, alerts) via MCP, REST API, and a JavaScript/TypeScript library. It enables natural-language queries, automated monitoring, and event-driven integrations for battery management.

## Key Features
- Multi-mode access: MCP server, REST API, JS/TS library, and event-driven webhooks
- Real-time battery metrics: SOC, SOH, cell voltages, temperatures, and BMS counters
- Advanced analytics: balance trends, health metrics, autonomy estimates, and cost savings
- Flexible deployment: standalone server, library integration, Docker, or Home Assistant add-on
- Event-driven architecture: SSE streams and webhooks for real-time alerts and snapshots

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides critical real-time battery data and analytics for AI-driven energy management systems, enabling proactive maintenance and optimization in RAG stacks.
