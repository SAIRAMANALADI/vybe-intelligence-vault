## Summary
Novagait Back Office is a production-grade AI agent demonstration for accounts-payable invoice intake with 3-way match, featuring synthetic data for a fictional physical-therapy clinic. It includes a published evaluation report, audit trails, human approval gates, and cost tracking across model variants.

## Key Features
- Published evaluation report with golden dataset and failure taxonomy
- Full per-run audit trail tracking model calls, tool usage, and costs
- Human approval gates implemented programmatically
- Cost-per-run measurement across multiple AI models
- Shadow/assisted/autonomous rollout modes for controlled deployment
