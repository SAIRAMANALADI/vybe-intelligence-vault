## Summary
An AI-powered travel planning assistant that generates executable travel plans using Dify Agent, LLMs, and RAG-based knowledge retrieval. It parses user requirements, validates constraints, and produces detailed itineraries including routes, daily schedules, budget analysis, and recommendations.

## Key Features
- Extracts and validates travel requirements (destination, dates, budget, preferences)
- Performs budget feasibility analysis with adjustment suggestions
- Generates multi-city travel plans with daily itineraries
- Integrates RAG for destination-specific knowledge retrieval
- Validates constraints (dates, budget, feasibility) before final output

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
