## Summary
Lex is a point-in-time retrieval system for regulatory text, enabling precise queries about past versions of laws and regulations. It maintains immutable historical records with validity intervals, provenance hashes, and machine-readable refusals for unavailable data.

## Key Features
- Point-in-time retrieval of regulatory text with exact validity intervals and timestamps
- Immutable historical records with hashed provenance for verifiable data integrity
- Machine-readable refusals for unavailable or ambiguous queries
- MCP-compatible toolset for AI agents to query legal documents deterministically
- Bitemporal indexing and signed index stamps for auditability and trust

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Lex provides a critical foundation for RAG systems in legal and regulatory domains by ensuring accurate, verifiable, and timestamped retrieval of past versions of laws, eliminating hallucinations and enabling trustworthy AI responses.
