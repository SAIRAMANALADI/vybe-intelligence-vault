## Summary
Etles is an open-source autonomous AI agent platform designed to run an entire business end-to-end, including executive strategy, department management, sales, marketing, engineering, finance, and operations. It features a C-suite of agents, cross-departmental orchestration, and deep integrations with over 1000 platforms for real-time automation.

## Key Features
- C-suite agent hierarchy with autonomous execution directives
- 85+ specialized agents across 15 departments with department leads
- Agent-to-Agent (A2A) collaboration with parallel and sequential workflows
- Deep integrations with 1000+ platforms via Composio
- Human-in-the-loop approvals for irreversible actions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Etles provides a production-grade framework for building and deploying autonomous AI agents capable of end-to-end business operations, making it essential for developers seeking scalable agentic systems.
