A Claude Code skill pack for AI-driven investment research on Japanese stocks, leveraging EDINET DB MCP (primary data from Japanese financial filings) to generate comprehensive research reports. It includes seven specialized skills for financial analysis, peer comparison, and governance deep dives.

- Seven specialized skills for Japanese stock research (e.g., investment-research, earnings-review, shareholder-analysis)
- Primary data sourcing from EDINET DB MCP (structured Japanese financial filings) for accuracy and traceability
- Common research discipline rules defined in `docs/research-discipline.md` to prevent fabricated data
- Complementary web search integration for real-time market context and narrative validation
- Designed for both large-cap and mid/small-cap stocks with structured financial data
