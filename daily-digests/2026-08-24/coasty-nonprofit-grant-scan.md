## Summary
An AI agent that automates scanning federal grant portals to identify open opportunities in specified fields, prioritize deadlines, and report results. It uses computer-use automation to drive a real browser, eliminating the need for selectors or scraping rules, and includes an offline mock for zero-cost testing.

## Key Features
- Zero dependencies and offline mock for $0 testing
- Drives real browsers using vision models with no selectors or scraping rules
- Structured safety mechanisms to prevent accidental spend (fail-closed defaults, dual consent)
- Self-documenting with provenance and per-frame hashes for transparency
- Modular design with one prompt and automation.json per task
