## Summary
DelegAI introduces a trustless machine-to-machine delegation economy enabling AI agents to autonomously spend funds with cryptographically enforced spending hierarchies. It leverages ERC-7710 caveats, MetaMask Smart Accounts, and x402 micropayments to allow secure, gasless delegations with <10s settlement.

## Key Features
- Cryptographically enforced spending hierarchies with ERC-7710 caveats for secure agent delegation
- 18 verified SDK integration points across MetaMask Smart Accounts, x402, 1Shot, and Venice AI
- Gasless transactions via 1Shot relay and MetaMask Smart Accounts
- Real-time LLM reasoning with Venice AI for budget allocation and decision-making
- On-chain settlement in <10 seconds with cryptographic proof on Ethereum Sepolia

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DelegAI enables AI agents to securely and autonomously spend funds without requiring full wallet access, solving a critical trust and security gap in AI-driven financial operations.
