## Summary
OntoRamp Knowledge Cartographer is an MCP server that transforms scattered organizational documents into a governed knowledge graph, enabling AI agents to retrieve and understand structured knowledge with health scores and coverage maps.

## Key Features
- Ingests documents into a governed knowledge graph with provenance tracking
- Provides knowledge health scores and coverage maps for organizational knowledge assessment
- Identifies knowledge gaps with domain-specific analysis
- Offers free read-tier tools for initial knowledge state evaluation
- Integrates with AI agents via MCP for structured knowledge retrieval

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to understand and retrieve structured, governed knowledge from organizational documents, bridging the gap between raw data and actionable insights.
