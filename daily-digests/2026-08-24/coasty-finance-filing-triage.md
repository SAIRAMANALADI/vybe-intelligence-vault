## Summary
An AI agent that automates SEC EDGAR filing triage by driving a real browser to search for and extract filing information (e.g., form type, date, accession number) from annual reports. It operates without dependencies, runs offline for free, and can execute live with minimal cost using the Coasty API.

## Key Features
- Zero dependencies and runs offline for free with bundled mocks
- Drives real browsers to interact with dynamic web interfaces like EDGAR
- Structured safety mechanisms to prevent accidental spend or data leaks
- Records and renders its own actions as a demo video for transparency
- Uses plain English prompts instead of brittle selectors or DOM parsing

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
