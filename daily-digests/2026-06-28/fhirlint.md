## Summary
FHIRLINT is a fast, JSON-native linter for validating FHIR R4/R5 resources and bundles against profiles like US Core, providing precise, line-level error reporting for healthcare and life-sciences data.

## Key Features
- Validates FHIR R4/R5 resources and bundles against profiles (e.g., US Core)
- Provides precise, line-level error reporting for debugging
- Supports multiple output formats (JSON, SARIF, table)
- Self-hostable, scriptable, and CI-ready with exit codes
- MCP-native for integration with AI agents and workflows

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
FHIRLINT ensures data quality and compliance for healthcare AI applications by validating FHIR resources against standards, reducing errors in RAG pipelines that rely on clinical or life-sciences data.
