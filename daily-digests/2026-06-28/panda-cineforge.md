## Summary
PandaCineForge is a self-contained skill-forging engine designed to generate, recall, and orchestrate structured cinematic skills for AI agents in film and video production. It bridges the gap between human-readable prompts and AI-executable workflows by producing a unified `SkillAsset` contract that AI agents can directly execute without parsing.

## Key Features
- Dual-mode skill forging (Cold/Hot) with real-time feedback flywheel for continuous improvement
- Layered cascade recall (R0-R5) combining structured routing, semantic search, context-aware retrieval, and safety fallbacks
- Unified `SkillAsset` protocol enabling direct AI agent execution without human intervention or Markdown parsing
- Multi-system orchestration via `module_target` dispatching to specialized production agents
- Graceful degradation architecture ensuring resilience even with missing dependencies

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
PandaCineForge eliminates the adapter layer between human prompts and AI execution by providing a structured, AI-to-AI contract for cinematic skills, enabling seamless integration into RAG and AI agent workflows for film and video production.
