## Summary
An unofficial Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that integrates Rohlik.cz grocery services with AI assistants, enabling natural language interactions for product searches, cart management, recipe planning, and order tracking via a Streamable HTTP transport.

## Key Features
- 28+ tools for product search, cart management, and order tracking
- Seamless integration with MCP-compatible AI assistants (Claude, IDEs, etc.)
- Supports natural language queries for grocery shopping and recipe planning
- Dockerized deployment with secure credential handling
- Read-only operations for product info and account overview, with cart modification capabilities

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges AI assistants with real-world grocery services, enabling hands-free shopping and automated cart management for RAG/AI stack builders.
