## Summary
GASPROFILER is a per-opcode and per-function gas profiler for Solidity smart contracts that identifies unbounded loops, DoS-prone patterns, and regressions against a baseline. It enables self-hosted, CI-ready gas analysis with support for multiple output formats and polyglot ports.

## Key Features
- Flags unbounded loops and gas regressions in Solidity contracts
- Supports per-function and per-opcode gas profiling
- CI-ready with exit codes and SARIF output for automation
- Self-hostable and MCP-native for AI agent integration
- Polyglot ports available in Python, JavaScript, Go, and Rust

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables proactive detection of gas-related vulnerabilities and performance regressions in smart contracts, critical for secure and efficient blockchain deployments.
