## Summary
DECYRA is a production-ready Android app leveraging AI to provide personalized academic and career guidance for Computer Science students, including Erasmus programs, Master's studies, and early career planning. It combines an AI mentor system, real-time communication, and community-driven features in a modern mobile interface.

## Key Features
- AI-powered personalized academic and career recommendations using RAG with OpenAI models
- Real-time P2P chat and video conferencing via WebRTC and ZEGOCLOUD
- Face ID authentication and AR guidance for secure user onboarding
- Community forum for sharing experiences and insights among students
- Speech-to-text and text-to-speech support for accessible interaction

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DECYRA demonstrates a production-ready RAG-based AI agent framework integrated with real-time communication and modern mobile UI, offering a scalable template for academic and career guidance applications.
