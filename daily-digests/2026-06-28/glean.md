## Summary
Glean is a self-hosted RSS reader and personal knowledge management tool designed for information-heavy users. It aggregates, organizes, and enhances RSS feeds with features like smart reading, bookmarking, folders, tags, and upcoming AI-powered recommendations.

## Key Features
- RSS/Atom feed aggregation with OPML import/export
- Smart reading interface with content filtering and organization (folders, tags, bookmarks)
- Background sync for automatic feed updates every 15 minutes
- Upcoming AI features including smart recommendations, auto-tagging, and keyword extraction
- MCP server support for AI assistant integration (e.g., Claude Desktop)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Glean provides a robust, self-hosted platform for aggregating and organizing RSS feeds with built-in AI readiness, making it essential for users who need scalable, customizable knowledge management and retrieval workflows.
