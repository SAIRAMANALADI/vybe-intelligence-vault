## Summary
A professional engineering framework for building AI-powered trading assistants and Expert Advisors (EAs) for MetaTrader 5 (MT5), integrating MQL5, Python, and large language models to automate trading strategies, risk management, and AI-assisted analysis.

## Key Features
- Modular EA architecture for strategy, risk, and signal management
- LLM-assisted trading analysis with structured context management
- Python-MT5 bridge for data retrieval, order execution, and monitoring
- Comprehensive risk control (max drawdown, daily loss limits, position sizing)
- AI-driven trade review, logging, and performance analysis tools
