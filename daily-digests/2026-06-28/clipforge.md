## Summary
ClipForge is an open-source AI-powered tool that automates the creation of short-form e-commerce videos from a single product image. It generates sales scripts, preserves product authenticity, adds subtitles, and synthesizes videos optimized for platforms like TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and TikTok Shop.

## Key Features
- AI-driven sales script generation with platform-specific SEO optimization
- Product authenticity preservation (no deformation of original product images)
- Multi-platform video export (TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, TikTok Shop)
- Zero-cost batch video production using free assets and TTS
- MCP Server integration for AI agent-driven video generation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
ClipForge streamlines the creation of high-conversion e-commerce videos, reducing production time from days to minutes while preserving product integrity and platform algorithm compliance.
