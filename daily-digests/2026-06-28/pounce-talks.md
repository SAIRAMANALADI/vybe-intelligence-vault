## Summary
Pounce is an AI-powered outbound calling system that automates the entire sales funnel by making real phone calls to prospects, conducting live two-way voice conversations, qualifying leads, and booking demos in under 5 minutes without human intervention.

## Key Features
- Fully automated outbound calling with real-time two-way voice conversations
- Live lead qualification using a scored rubric during the call
- Personalized pitch delivery based on real-time prospect responses
- Automated demo booking via Cal.com with instant calendar invites
- Full transcript and outcome logging in a dashboard for review

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Pounce demonstrates how AI agents can handle complex, real-time interactions like phone calls, bridging the gap between automation and human-like conversation for sales and outreach workflows.
