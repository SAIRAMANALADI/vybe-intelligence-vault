## Summary
RAGKIT is a lightweight, self-hostable local RAG pipeline designed for ingesting, indexing, and serving document data with minimal dependencies. It supports extractive Q&A, search, and CI integration, making it ideal for local AI workflows and agent-driven systems.

## Key Features
- Local RAG pipeline with ingest, index, and serve capabilities
- Supports multiple output formats (JSON, table, SARIF) for interoperability
- MCP-native for seamless integration with AI agents and tools
- Polyglot ports available (Python, JavaScript, Go, Rust)
- CI-ready with exit codes and automated scanning

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
RAGKIT simplifies local RAG deployments with a dependency-light, scriptable pipeline that integrates effortlessly into AI stacks and CI workflows.
