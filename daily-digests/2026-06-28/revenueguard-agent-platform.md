## Summary
A full-stack RevOps AI agent platform designed to detect revenue leakage, contract entitlement drift, usage anomalies, and renewal risks before they impact financial close or customer escalations. It provides deterministic agent orchestration with guardrails, audit traces, and operational scorecards while avoiding irreversible actions by default.

## Key Features
- Deterministic observe-decide-act-validate-handoff agent loop for RevOps workflows
- Seeded evidence retrieval across contracts, usage, billing, support, and renewal notes
- Policy guardrails blocking fabricated evidence, approval bypass, and external write instructions
- Human-in-the-loop gates for writeback, discounting, credit memos, and high-value accounts
- React operations console with risk posture, leakage summary, action plans, citations, and trace inspection

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a production-grade blueprint for building safe, auditable, and deterministic AI agents in revenue-critical workflows, ensuring operational reliability and traceability for RAG builders.
