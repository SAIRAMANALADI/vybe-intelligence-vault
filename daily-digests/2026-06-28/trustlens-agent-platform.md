## Summary
A full-stack AI governance agent platform designed for vendor intake, evidence-grounded risk scoring, control mapping, and auditable approval workflows. It enables transparent, traceable AI agent processes with human review gates and operational metrics.

## Key Features
- Deterministic agent loop with observe-decide-act-validate workflow
- Policy guardrails preventing approval-bypass and secret-exfiltration
- Evidence retrieval via local control library for governance obligations
- Explainable risk scoring with tiered decision routing and drivers
- React-based dashboard for intake, decision handoff, and traceability

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, auditable framework for AI governance that automates vendor risk assessments while ensuring transparency and compliance.
