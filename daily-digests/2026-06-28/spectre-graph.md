## Summary
SpectreGraph is an autonomous OSINT intelligence engine that builds dynamic knowledge graphs for AI assistants, resolving entity ambiguities and providing contextual intelligence through semantic relationship mapping and multi-agent collaboration.

## Key Features
- Autonomous entity disambiguation resolving ambiguities like 'Apple' (fruit/company/person)
- Dynamic graph pruning to maintain freshness and accuracy of intelligence
- Multi-agent collaboration integrating OpenAI and Claude for consensus-based reasoning
- Semantic relationship mapping for contextual understanding (e.g., 'financed by', 'opposes')
- Privacy-first design with local processing and no third-party telemetry

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SpectreGraph transforms raw OSINT data into a verifiable, self-healing knowledge graph, eliminating hallucinations and enabling AI assistants to provide contextually accurate, citation-backed responses.
