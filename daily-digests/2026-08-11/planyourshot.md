## Summary
planyourshot is an AI-powered cinematography planning engine that automates the conversion of film scripts into structured, production-ready shot sheets. It leverages AI to analyze scripts and generate detailed shot breakdowns for filmmakers.

## Key Features
- AI-driven script parsing to extract scene and shot requirements
- Automated generation of structured shot sheets from unstructured scripts
- Integration with cinematography best practices for shot recommendations
- Support for multi-camera and complex scene setups
- Exportable shot sheets in industry-standard formats

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
