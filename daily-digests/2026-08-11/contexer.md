## Summary
Contexer is a tool that captures, stores, and replays engineering decisions (architecture, constraints, conventions, and patterns) made during AI coding sessions, ensuring consistency across multiple AI assistants like Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex without requiring manual re-explanation.

## Key Features
- Captures and stores engineering decisions (architecture, constraints, conventions, patterns) during AI coding sessions
- Replays relevant decisions automatically in future AI sessions to avoid re-explanation
- Works across multiple AI assistants (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI) for consistent knowledge sharing
- Uses deterministic hooks and MCP tools for seamless integration with AI workflows
- Supports versioning, governance, and human approval of decisions before storage

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
