## Summary
A local-first personal finance assistant that aggregates A-shares, funds, bank wealth management, cash accounts, digital assets, and quantitative bots into a unified dashboard. It provides objective market insights, AI-powered Q&A, and decision-support tools without offering buy/sell advice.

## Key Features
- Unified portfolio dashboard for stocks, funds, cash, digital assets, and bots with real-time market data
- AI-powered market Q&A agent with 32+ data tools for objective insights (no buy/sell advice)
- Plate radar for A-share vs. sector performance comparison with alpha analysis
- Morning briefing with LLM-generated summaries of news, announcements, and technical positions
- ETF theme transparency tool to validate fund holdings against declared themes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a privacy-focused, all-in-one local solution for tracking and analyzing personal investments with AI-driven market insights, reducing reliance on fragmented third-party tools.
