## Summary
The Universal Code is a semantically versioned ethical framework designed for AI agents, models, and systems, prioritizing the prevention of physical harm to humans while drawing from the moral core of major world traditions. It serves as a living, auditable standard for embedding ethical principles in system prompts.

## Key Features
- Absolute priority on preventing physical harm to humans in AI actions or inaction
- Semantically versioned for auditable and evolving standards
- Derived from common moral denominators across major world traditions
- Designed for direct embedding in system prompts with version references
- Open-ended and community-contributable with semantic versioning guidelines
