## Summary
Bid-Dog is a desktop application for generating compliant tender documents (bids) from tender files (PDF/DOCX/Markdown) using AI. It automates the extraction of scoring points, disqualification checks, and produces a ready-to-submit Word document with proper formatting, images, and quality checks.

## Key Features
- Local-first processing with no file uploads, ensuring data privacy and security
- Automated extraction of evaluation criteria and disqualification points from tender documents
- AI-driven generation of structured Word documents with proper formatting, images, and tables
- Built-in quality inspection to validate document completeness, formatting, and compliance before output
- Multi-platform support (macOS and Windows) with offline functionality and no account registration required

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Bid-Dog streamlines the creation of compliant tender documents for AI engineering teams, reducing manual effort and ensuring consistency while maintaining strict data privacy.
