## Summary
Juno Open Health Tools provides privacy-conscious, open-source resources for organizing and preparing health-related symptom notes for medical appointments. It includes tools like a symptom diary schema, Notion templates, a Chrome extension, and MCP apps for AI assistants to streamline patient-doctor communication.

## Key Features
- Open JSON Schema and CSV format for portable symptom diaries
- Local-first Chrome/Edge extension for secure note-taking and appointment planning
- Notion and Canva templates for appointment preparation
- MCP app for integration with ChatGPT and Codex
- Explicit privacy boundaries and synthetic data for safety

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
