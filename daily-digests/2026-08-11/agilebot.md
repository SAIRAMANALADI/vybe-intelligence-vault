## Summary
AgileBot is an agentic AI scrum master built on the Claude Agent SDK that automates sprint administration tasks such as ticket grooming, blocker detection, async standups, and ceremonies within Microsoft Teams. It integrates with JIRA and uses a multi-agent architecture to streamline scrum processes and improve team velocity.

## Key Features
- Blocker detection via Teams chat and JIRA ticket analysis with human-in-the-loop approval
- Automated ticket grooming and JIRA health monitoring (ungroomed stories, unpointed tickets)
- Async standup collection and summarization with blocker flagging
- Sprint ceremonies (planning, retrospectives, reviews) with analytics and traceable metrics
- Multi-agent architecture (supervisor + specialists) for separation of concerns and maintainability

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AgileBot demonstrates how agentic AI can automate repetitive scrum tasks while maintaining human oversight, reducing administrative overhead and improving team productivity.
