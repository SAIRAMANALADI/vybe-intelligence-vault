## Summary
Job Hunter is an AI-native career intelligence system that transforms raw PDF CVs into structured profiles, ranked job opportunities, personalized application materials, and skill-building roadmaps using LLM extraction, vector search, and agentic workflows.

## Key Features
- AI-first product flow converting CVs into structured profiles and ranked job matches
- LangGraph-powered agentic workflows for roadmap planning and milestone generation
- Supabase pgvector for semantic job matching and retrieval-augmented reasoning
- Provider isolation with replaceable interfaces for LLM and embedding services
- Human-in-the-loop validation for extracted CV data and generated outputs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
