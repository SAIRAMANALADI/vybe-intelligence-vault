## Summary
A production-ready AI Meeting Assistant built using AI-assisted development and vibe coding techniques. It processes audio meetings, transcribes with Whisper, generates summaries, extracts action items and decisions, and enables RAG-powered meeting chat with full-text search and analytics.

## Key Features
- Whisper-based audio transcription with OpenAI
- GPT-powered meeting summaries, action items, and decision extraction
- RAG-enabled meeting chat with pgvector embeddings
- Full-text search and cross-meeting discovery
- Enterprise features including multi-tenancy, RBAC, and GDPR compliance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a complete reference architecture for building AI-powered meeting assistants with RAG capabilities, demonstrating best practices in AI integration, scalability, and enterprise-grade features.
