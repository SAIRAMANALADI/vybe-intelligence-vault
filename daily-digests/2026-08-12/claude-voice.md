## Summary
claude-voice enables hands-free, real-time voice interaction with the Claude AI assistant directly from the terminal. It converts speech to text, sends queries to Claude, and streams responses back as audio for a conversational experience.

## Key Features
- Real-time streaming of voice input and audio output
- Barge-in support to interrupt the AI during speech
- Local processing for reduced latency
- Simplified terminal-based interface
- High-quality voice output for natural interaction

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
