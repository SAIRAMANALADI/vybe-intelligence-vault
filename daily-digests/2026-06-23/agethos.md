## Summary
Agethos is a cognitive architecture library for AI agents that provides persistent identity, long-term memory, dynamic emotional states, self-reflection, and social learning capabilities. It enables agents to maintain personality continuity, emotional consistency, and adaptive behavior grounded in psychological models.

## Key Features
- Psychological grounding with OCEAN (Big Five) personality traits and PAD emotional state modeling
- Dynamic memory system with arbitrated writes, associative evolution, and vitality-based decay
- Self-reflection and persona evolution through playbooks and lessons
- Theory of Mind and relationship dynamics for social cognition
- Cross-platform export and LLM-agnostic integration (100+ providers)
