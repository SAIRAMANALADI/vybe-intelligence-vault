## Summary
MateClaw is a Spring Boot-based multi-agent orchestration framework designed as a 'second brain' for IT departments, offering enterprise-grade AI agent management with multi-vendor failover, knowledge digestion, and RBAC security. It provides a unified platform for deploying, monitoring, and scaling AI agents across web, desktop, and IM channels.

## Key Features
- Multi-vendor failover with health tracking and cooldown for uninterrupted AI operations
- LLM Wiki for structured knowledge digestion with citations and auto-linking
- Digital employees with ReAct and Plan-and-Execute reasoning, plus multi-user RBAC
- Workflow orchestration with approval gates, triggers, and audit trails
- Multi-surface deployment (web, desktop, widget, IM channels) with one JAR deployment

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
MateClaw provides a production-ready, enterprise-grade framework for deploying and scaling AI agents with built-in failover, security, and knowledge management, reducing vendor lock-in and operational overhead for RAG/AI stack builders.
