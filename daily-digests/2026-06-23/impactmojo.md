## Summary
ImpactMojo is a free, open-source learning platform offering rigorous educational content on Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL), development economics, gender studies, and public policy tailored for NGOs, practitioners, and students in South Asia. It combines interactive courses, labs, tools, and premium services to bridge gaps in development education.

## Key Features
- 15 flagship and 45 foundational courses covering MEL, gender studies, and public policy
- 13 interactive labs for hands-on skill development (e.g., Theory of Change, MEL Design)
- 134-game library with simulations and quizzes for engaging learning
- Premium tools like RQ Builder Pro, TOC Workbench Pro, and VaniScribe AI for advanced users
- MCP server for AI assistant integration to search all platform content
