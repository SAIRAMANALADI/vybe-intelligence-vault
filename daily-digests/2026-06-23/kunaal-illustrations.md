## Summary
A specialized AI agent skill that transforms cognitive anchors in English articles into hand-drawn, in-text illustrations. It guides agents to generate clean, bizarre, and memorable visual metaphors using predefined character IPs and style rules.

## Key Features
- Predefined character IPs (Boy & Girl) for consistent visual language
- Structured workflow for generating 4-8 illustrations per article
- Style guide enforcing pure white background, sparse annotations, and wide composition
- QA checklist for validating generated images
- Dual-licensed (MIT for code, CC BY 4.0 for assets)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI-driven content creators to automatically generate unique, hand-drawn illustrations that enhance readability and engagement in articles without requiring manual design work.
