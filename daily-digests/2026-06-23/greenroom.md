## Summary
greenroom is an open-source AI-powered interview preparation tool that automates the entire process of researching companies, crafting tailored responses, and simulating mock interviews. It transforms a user's resume and job description into a structured workspace with reusable interview cards, scripts, and industry-specific playbooks.

## Key Features
- End-to-end interview prep pipeline: from resume and JD analysis to mock interviews and debriefs
- 155 role-specific playbooks across 15 industries with insider insights and question chains
- Generates verbatim spoken scripts tailored to user's voice and style, not just bullet points
- Local-first Markdown workspace with zero data uploads, ensuring privacy and control
- Live copilot and mock interview simulator with real-time feedback and grading

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the tedious and time-consuming process of interview preparation by automating research, scripting, and mock interviews, enabling candidates to focus on refining their delivery and confidence.
