## Summary
HyperTrade is a crypto trading agent designed for market research and execution, integrating agent-based workflows, RAG citations, and paper trading capabilities. It supports live and simulated trading, strategy experimentation, and risk-gated execution with OKX integration.

## Key Features
- Agent graph execution with provider routing and tool calling
- RAG citations for market research and strategy validation
- Risk-gated live execution with paper trading support
- Strategy library with lifecycle management and backtesting artifacts
- BitPro MCP adapter for external data access and integration
