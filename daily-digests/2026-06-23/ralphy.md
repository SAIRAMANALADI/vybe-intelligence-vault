## Summary
Ralphy is an open-source runtime for agent-driven AI video generation, enabling coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, etc.) to autonomously produce video content from text prompts. It acts as a bridge between agents and video generation tools, handling reproducibility, cost tracking, and quality control.

## Key Features
- Agent-driven video generation with reproducible model calls and project state tracking
- Built-in cost preview and rollup for transparency before spending
- Modular provider support (OpenRouter for models, ElevenLabs for TTS/music)
- HyperFrames composer for deterministic HTML+GSAP video rendering
- Append-only generation logs, postmortems, and template library for versioning

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Ralphy enables AI agents to autonomously generate high-quality video content while maintaining reproducibility, cost transparency, and quality control, making it essential for scalable AI-driven media production.
