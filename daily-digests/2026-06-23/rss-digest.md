## Summary
rss-digest is an agentic tool that automates the process of scanning, triaging, reading, and synthesizing RSS feeds into a focused, high-signal digest. It leverages the `feed` CLI to surface relevant posts and skip noise, making it ideal for daily news catch-ups or themed reading briefs.

## Key Features
- Automated scanning of unread RSS entries with metadata extraction
- AI-driven triage to select 5–10 high-signal posts based on user prompts
- Full-content reading and synthesis into concise 2–3 sentence summaries
- Thematic grouping of results for better readability
- Auto-bootstrap with OPML starter set if no feeds are configured

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the process of curating and summarizing RSS content, reducing manual effort for AI engineers who need to stay updated with minimal noise.
