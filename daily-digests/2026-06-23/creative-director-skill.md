- Supports 20+ creativity methods (SIT, TRIZ, SCAMPER, Bisociation)
- Scores ideas using Cannes Lions-calibrated metrics
- Integrates with major AI models (Claude, GPT, Gemini)

- No coding required; designed for non-technical users
