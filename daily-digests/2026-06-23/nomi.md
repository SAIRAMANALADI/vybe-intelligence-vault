## Summary
Nomi is a local-first, open-source AI director's workspace for creating videos from text prompts. It enables users to compose 3D scenes, lock character identities, direct camera movements, and generate images/videos with AI, all within a unified timeline-based interface.

## Key Features
- AI-assisted script breakdown into storyboards with editable prompts and camera directions
- 3D director's desk for precise scene composition, character posing, and camera framing
- Seamless integration with external AI models (Sora, Veo, Claude, etc.) via API keys
- Timeline-based editing with automatic asset flow from generation to export
- Project-level asset consistency (characters, styles, prompts) to reduce drift across segments

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Nomi bridges the gap between abstract AI prompts and precise creative control, enabling RAG builders to generate and edit video content locally with minimal friction.
