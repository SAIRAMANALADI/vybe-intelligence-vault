## Summary
myKG is an automated knowledge graph extractor that converts mixed-format documents (PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML, etc.) into a confidence-scored knowledge graph grounded in an induced RDFS/OWL ontology. It supports human-in-the-loop schema design, incremental updates, and multiple output formats for RAG and graph analysis.

## Key Features
- Automated extraction of entities and relationships from mixed-format documents with confidence scoring
- Self-evolving RDFS/OWL ontology with human review and validation capabilities
- Support for incremental updates and cross-session merging of knowledge graphs
- Multiple output formats including JSONL, Turtle RDF, NetworkX, Obsidian vault, and Neo4j LOAD CSV
- Provider-agnostic LLM integration with Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama, and OpenRouter

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
myKG enables RAG builders to transform unstructured documents into structured, queryable knowledge graphs with verifiable ontologies, enhancing context grounding and retrieval accuracy.
