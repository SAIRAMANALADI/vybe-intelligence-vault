## Summary
AI Contract Agent enforces hard contracts in AI-assisted software development by ensuring the AI persists artifacts, follows test-driven development, and adheres to user-defined pipelines. It acts as an accountability engine between developers and LLMs, halting execution on contract violations.

## Key Features
- Hard contract enforcement requiring AI to persist artifacts to disk before proceeding
- Multi-phase PDLC pipeline with configurable phases (up to 31) and enforcement rules
- Provider-agnostic integration with OpenAI, Claude, and local models via unified adapters
- Failing test validation to ensure genuine TDD before implementation
- Audit trail logging all contract violations, bypasses, and AI responses for compliance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI-generated code and artifacts are verifiable and persistent, reducing the risk of untested or undocumented outputs in production workflows.
