## Summary
SAC Capital is a multi-stage LLM-driven equity research and paper-trading agent that generates deterministic, verifiable AI trading decisions anchored on the Mantle blockchain as bytes32 commitments. It combines equity analysis, risk management, and frontend verification for transparent AI-driven investment strategies.

## Key Features
- Multi-stage LLM equity research pipeline with Haiku pre-filter, Sonnet analyst, and auditor stages
- Deterministic bytes32 commitments for AI decisions anchored on Mantle blockchain for verifiability
- Risk kernel with fractional Kelly sizing, sector caps, and daily loss limits
- Frontend verification panel for client-side hash recomputation and on-chain record matching
- Politician disclosure screen (optional) for ethical compliance using public STOCK Act filings
