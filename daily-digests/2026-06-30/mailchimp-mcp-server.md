## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server providing 117 tools to interact with the Mailchimp Marketing API, enabling full lifecycle management of campaigns, audiences, reports, segments, automations, and e-commerce data with built-in safety modes like read-only and dry-run.

## Key Features
- 117 tools covering campaigns, audiences, reports, segments, automations, and e-commerce operations
- Read-only and dry-run safety modes for secure testing and exploration
- Multi-account support with configurable read-only and dry-run flags per account
- Full lifecycle management of campaigns, members, templates, and webhooks
- A/B campaign support, geographic reporting, and CRM-style member notes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a standardized, MCP-compatible interface to automate and integrate Mailchimp's extensive marketing API, reducing complexity for RAG builders who need to manage email campaigns and audience data programmatically.
