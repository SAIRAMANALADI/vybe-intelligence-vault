## Summary
A specialized AI-driven system for generating high-quality short drama videos from a single sentence input, featuring a 22-master closed-loop pipeline that reduces waste, optimizes tokens, and ensures consistency across episodes.

## Key Features
- 22 specialized AI 'masters' for end-to-end short drama creation, from scriptwriting to final output
- BX Protocol reduces waste rate from 60% to 15% and saves ~75% tokens compared to traditional workflows
- Precision-engineered prompts (≤400 words) generate 1080P quality from 720P input
- Self-evolving system with daily midnight audits, auto-fixes, and platform optimizations
- Character consistency enforced via LoRA, seed locking, and multi-view reference images
