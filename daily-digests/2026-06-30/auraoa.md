## Summary
AuraOA is a minimalist, AI-driven audit framework designed to bring transparency and intelligence to enterprise workflows by automating OA (Office Automation) process reviews. It integrates with enterprise OA systems, applies custom audit rules, and leverages LLMs for compliance checks and archival reviews.

## Key Features
- Two-phase AI audit (reasoning → structured extraction) with configurable strictness levels
- Multi-tenant architecture with tenant-isolated data and AI model assignments
- Extensible OA adapter system supporting WeCom E9 and other enterprise systems
- Automated batch audits, scheduled reports, and archival review capabilities
- Support for both local (Ollama, vLLM) and cloud-based (DeepSeek, OpenAI) LLM integrations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
AuraOA streamlines enterprise workflow audits by automating compliance checks and leveraging LLMs, reducing manual review efforts while ensuring consistency and transparency in OA processes.
