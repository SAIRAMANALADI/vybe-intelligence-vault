## Summary
ElizaOS plugin enabling gasless treasury management for AI agents on Base mainnet, allowing agents to park idle USDC into the MEV-protected GBLIN index, monitor treasury health, and JIT-swap back to USDC for x402 invoice payments.

## Key Features
- Gasless treasury operations for AI agents via Coinbase CDP facilitator
- JIT-swap capability to convert GBLIN holdings back to USDC for x402 invoice payments
- Live treasury health monitoring with NAV, basket weights, and Crash Shield status
- Integration with Base mainnet and x402 protocol for paid API endpoints
- Security-focused design with dedicated hot wallet recommendations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to autonomously manage idle capital efficiently with minimal gas costs and MEV protection, directly supporting RAG systems that require liquidity for real-time operations.
