## Summary
Lawbster provides verified, citable German and EU legal context via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor. It delivers real-time access to official legal sources with structured tools for legal research and compliance checks.

## Key Features
- Verified legal context from official sources with daily updates
- Nine MCP tools for legal discovery, lookup, and contextual analysis
- Supports six MCP Resources (e.g., `legal://rechtsrahmen`, `legal://eu_celex_registry`)
- Hybrid search with AI reranking and pagination for efficient research
- OAuth 2.1 and Bearer token authentication for seamless integration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Lawbster eliminates legal hallucinations in AI responses by providing real-time, citable legal context for RAG systems, ensuring compliance and accuracy in legal queries.
