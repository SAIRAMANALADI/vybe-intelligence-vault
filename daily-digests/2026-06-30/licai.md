## Summary
A privacy-focused local personal finance assistant that aggregates A-shares, funds, bank wealth management, cash accounts, digital assets, and quantitative robots into a unified dashboard. It provides market AI Q&A, stock details, sector comparisons, and objective risk alerts without cloud dependencies or trading advice.

## Key Features
- Unified portfolio dashboard for stocks, funds, cash, and digital assets with real-time pricing
- Market AI Q&A agent with 28 data tools for objective market analysis and industry chain insights
- Early morning briefing with LLM-generated summaries of portfolio risks and news
- Sector radar for comparing individual holdings against industry benchmarks
- Unwind position calculator for pyramiding cost-averaging strategies
