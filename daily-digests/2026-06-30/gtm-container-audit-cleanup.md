## Summary
A reusable AI skill designed to audit and clean up Google Tag Manager (GTM) containers by evaluating their hygiene, business logic, and technical safety. It helps analysts, marketers, and AI agents identify redundant, misconfigured, or risky GTM configurations to ensure measurement goals are met efficiently and securely.

## Key Features
- Mechanical hygiene checks for duplicates, unused objects, and naming inconsistencies
- Business logic validation to ensure tags align with measurement goals
- Custom-code and technical safety reviews for maintainability and security
- Deterministic helper scripts for reproducible audits and cleanup plans
- Human-readable cleanup plans with actionable QA instructions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
