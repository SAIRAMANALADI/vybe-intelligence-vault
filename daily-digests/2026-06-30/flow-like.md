## Summary
Flow-Like is a Rust-powered, visual workflow automation platform that runs entirely on local hardware or self-hosted infrastructure. It enables drag-and-drop workflow creation with full data lineage, type safety, and audit trails, designed for offline use, edge deployment, and AI-native integrations.

## Key Features
- Rust-based engine with near-instant execution (~0.6ms per workflow) enabling deployment on edge devices like phones and Raspberry Pis
- Full visual workflow builder with type-aware drag-and-drop nodes and real-time validation
- Complete data lineage and audit trails with process, data, and execution perspectives for transparency
- AI-native integration with built-in LLM orchestration, guardrails, and full execution tracing for every AI call
- White-label and embeddable SDKs (Node.js, Python) for integrating workflows into custom applications or SaaS platforms

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Flow-Like provides a high-performance, local-first alternative to cloud-dependent workflow engines, critical for building trustworthy, auditable AI systems that require full data control and offline capabilities.
