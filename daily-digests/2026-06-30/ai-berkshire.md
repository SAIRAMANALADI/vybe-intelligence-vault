## Summary
AI Berkshire is a structured investment research framework that systematizes value investing methodologies from Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger, Duan Yongping, and Li Lu using AI agents. It transforms raw AI analysis into actionable investment decisions with multi-agent parallel research, rigorous financial validation, and bias-checking mechanisms.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent parallel research simulating a full investment team with conflicting viewpoints for robust decision-making
- Structured bias-checking mechanisms (A/B/C information grading, reverse thinking, quick veto lists) to prevent AI hallucinations in financial analysis
- Rigorous financial validation tools (precise decimal arithmetic, cross-source data verification, Benford's Law checks) ensuring data accuracy
- Four legendary investor perspectives (Buffett, Munger, Duan, Li) integrated to challenge assumptions and reduce blind spots
- Actionable output format with clear investment conclusions, price targets, and risk assessments rather than balanced but indecisive analysis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
