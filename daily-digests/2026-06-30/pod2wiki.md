## Summary
pod2wiki automates the conversion of podcast audio and long-form articles into structured, wiki-style summaries optimized for local knowledge management systems like karpathy-claude-wiki. It leverages AI transcription and summarization to save users time by distilling hours of content into key insights.

## Key Features
- Converts podcast audio and articles into structured summaries using Whisper and DeepSeek
- Supports RSS feeds for automated batch processing of multiple podcasts
- Generates output compatible with local knowledge management systems (e.g., karpathy-claude-wiki)
- Local-first processing with no cloud storage of personal data
- Configurable summarization modes (short, detailed, bullet points)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the creation of high-quality, structured knowledge bases from audio content, reducing manual effort for RAG pipeline builders.
