## Summary
CareerByte AI is an open-source AI-powered career management platform that streamlines job discovery, resume optimization, application tracking, and interview preparation. It integrates AI-driven tools like resume parsing, ATS scoring, and job matching to enhance job seekers' efficiency and success rates.

## Key Features
- AI-driven resume parsing and ATS optimization with scoring and keyword analysis
- Multi-source job discovery engine with automated parsing and duplicate detection
- Personalized application generation (resumes, cover letters, recruiter messages)
- Comprehensive application tracking with status management and analytics dashboard
- Interview preparation hub with AI mock interviews and company-specific question banks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
CareerByte AI provides a unified platform for job seekers to automate and optimize critical career tasks, reducing manual effort and improving application success rates through AI-driven insights.
