## Summary
KubeOpenCode is a Kubernetes-native platform that extends OpenCode AI agents for team and enterprise use, adding governance, shared configurations, scalability, and enterprise infrastructure integration. It enables multi-agent orchestration, task management, and real-time agent interaction within Kubernetes environments.

## Key Features
- Kubernetes-native deployment and scaling of AI agents
- Multi-agent governance and shared configurations for teams
- Task and CronTask management with retention policies
- OpenTelemetry-based observability and metrics collection
- CLI and UI for agent interaction and management
