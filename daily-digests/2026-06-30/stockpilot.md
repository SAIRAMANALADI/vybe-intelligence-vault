## Summary
StockPilot is an AI-powered investment research workbench designed for long-term investors to track a focused set of stocks. It enables structured, persistent AI analysis with daily reports, position-aware specialist insights, and a searchable archive of research, all stored locally.

## Key Features
- Structured daily report generation with persistent storage in SQLite and Markdown/HTML files
- Position-aware specialist agent analysis for held stocks
- Watchlist management with bulk and per-stock analysis capabilities
- Scheduled notifications via email or WeCom webhook for daily summaries
- Local-first architecture with SQLite-based settings and configuration

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
StockPilot provides a structured, persistent AI research workflow for investors, enabling long-term thesis tracking and repeatable analysis that is critical for building conviction-driven investment strategies.
