## Summary
A multistep AI agent designed to assist users in finding the right car to rent or buy through interactive interviews, marketplace research, and AI-ranked recommendations. The project is currently in pre-implementation with detailed planning and specifications in place.

## Key Features
- Interactive conversational interview to capture user preferences
- Marketplace research across 100+ car listings and 10+ categories
- AI-ranked car recommendations with detailed explanations
- Multi-channel communication (email, SMS, voice, Slack, WhatsApp)
- Voice support via Deepgram for speech-to-text and text-to-speech

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, AI-driven pipeline for personalized car recommendations, integrating multimodal interactions and marketplace research to enhance user decision-making.
