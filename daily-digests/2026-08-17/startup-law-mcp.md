## Summary
This repository provides an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that enables LLMs to accurately retrieve and cite Korean startup-related laws and regulations, including 50 legal documents with 8,191 articles, as well as K-Startup support programs. It ensures factual accuracy by verifying citations and avoids hallucinations common in generic legal searches.

## Key Features
- Precise retrieval of 50 curated startup-related laws and 8,191 legal articles from Korea's National Law Information Center
- Real-time verification of legal citations to prevent LLM hallucinations (e.g., non-existent laws or articles)
- Integration with K-Startup support programs, including current recruitment status and deadlines
- Automated synchronization of legal data via GitHub Actions and Fly.io for up-to-date compliance
- Modular toolset for law search, citation verification, reference tracing, and startup stage-specific guidance
