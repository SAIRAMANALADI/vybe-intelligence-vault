## Summary
SWARM is a research framework for measuring emergent failures in multi-agent AI systems, focusing on systemic risks like deception, adverse selection, and governance failures that arise from agent interactions rather than individual agent behavior. It provides tools to quantify and analyze these risks through metrics like illusion delta and quality gaps.

## Key Features
- Interaction-level safety metrics (e.g., illusion delta, quality gaps)
- Reproducible multi-agent safety benchmarks and scenarios
- Governance experiments (audits, staking, sanctions, circuit breakers)
- Automated red-teaming and adversarial attack scenarios
- Integration with external platforms (e.g., LangChain, AutoGPT, Mesa ABM)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SWARM provides critical tools to identify and mitigate systemic risks in multi-agent AI systems, which are often invisible in single-agent evaluations but critical for safe deployment.
