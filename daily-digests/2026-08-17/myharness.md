## Summary
MYH (Make Your Harness) is an identity sovereignty governance framework for AI Agents, providing immutable identity anchoring, hierarchical permissions, and hash-chain auditing to ensure traceable and accountable agent behavior.

## Key Features
- Anchored identity via CTID-compliant network IDs with immutable genesis blocks
- Hierarchical permission system with guest/user/founder roles and decay-based access control
- SHA-256 hash-chain auditing for tamper-proof behavior logging and traceability
- Six-layer validation gates (format, semantic, information flow, authorization, circuit breaker, trajectory audit)
- Self-healing capabilities with L1-L4 recovery tiers and automated skill installation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
MYH provides the critical missing layer of identity governance and auditability for AI agents, enabling traceable, accountable, and compliant agent behavior in RAG and AI systems.
