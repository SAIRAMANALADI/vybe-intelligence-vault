## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server designed for EU AI Act compliance, providing governance and risk management features for AI systems. It enables automated compliance checks, transparency reporting, and bias detection to ensure adherence to regulatory standards.

- Automated risk management and transparency reporting
- Bias detection and testing capabilities
