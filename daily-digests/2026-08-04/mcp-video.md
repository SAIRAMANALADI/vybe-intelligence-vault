## Summary
mcp-video is an MCP server providing 8 specialized tools for cinema-grade video production, including recording, editing, color grading, audio manipulation, text overlays, TTS, and smart screenshots. It integrates ffmpeg and Playwright to enable high-fidelity, frame-by-frame video capture and processing for AI agents.

## Key Features
- 8 specialized MCP tools for end-to-end video production (recording, editing, effects, captions, TTS, screenshots)
- 60fps frame-by-frame capture with zero frame drops using Playwright and ffmpeg
- Smart element-aware screenshots with auto-detection of 15+ UI components
- Narrated video generation with synchronized AI voiceovers (ElevenLabs/OpenAI TTS)
- Dual transport modes (stdio and HTTP) for flexible deployment in AI agent workflows

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to autonomously produce high-quality, cinematic videos for demos, social media, or documentation without manual intervention.
