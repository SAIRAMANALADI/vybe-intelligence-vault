## Summary
Nirium Protocol provides an Infrastructure-as-Software (IaaS) platform for building autonomous treasury and settlement operations on the Stellar blockchain. It offers a TypeScript/Python SDK, a 55-endpoint API, and Soroban smart contracts for institutional fintechs to deploy agentic execution nodes for 24/7 cross-border settlements and liquidity management.

## Key Features
- 55-endpoint API with multi-tier authentication and RBAC for institutional use
- Autonomous execution nodes for 24/7 cross-border settlements and liquidity management
- Soroban smart contracts for treasury, flash loans, and strategy execution
- x402 and Machine Payment Protocol (MPP) for per-request monetization of AI agent access
- Immutable compliance archive with HMAC-SHA256 signed records for regulatory reporting

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Nirium enables fintechs to build autonomous, compliant, and scalable treasury operations on Stellar, reducing manual intervention and regulatory overhead while leveraging blockchain-native payment and escrow mechanisms.
