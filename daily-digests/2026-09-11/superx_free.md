## Summary
SuperX is an open-source, self-hosted growth workstation for X (Twitter) that learns user writing patterns, drafts posts in their voice, and schedules publishing. It serves as a free alternative to hosted tools like Hypefury or Typefully, running entirely on the user's machine with their own API keys.

## Key Features
- Drafts posts in your writing voice by analyzing your existing content and high-performing posts from a shared corpus.
- Schedules and publishes posts automatically with approval workflows and retries.
- Includes an inbox for mentions and topic feeds, with AI-assisted drafting for replies.
- Provides analytics, contacts management, and inspiration tools for content creation.
- Offers an API, MCP server, and CLI for programmatic control and integration with AI tools.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a privacy-focused, cost-effective alternative to hosted X automation tools, enabling users to control their data while leveraging AI for content creation and scheduling.
