## Summary
mu-skill-creator is a quality assurance framework for AI agent skills, featuring a 53-item, 10-layer audit model and an 8-stage gated workflow to ensure robustness, security, and performance. It includes automated scanning, anti-pattern detection, and context-aware loading to optimize AI skill development and deployment.

## Key Features
- 53-item, 10-layer audit model with 27 automated checks and 26 human-judgment items
- 8-stage gated workflow for skill creation and refinement
- 34 anti-pattern catalog (AP-1 to AP-34) with real-world incident tracing and fixes
- Automated audit script (`skill-audit.sh`) for batch scanning and quality checks
- Security-first design with pre-publish credential and PII scanning

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, automated approach to ensure AI agent skills are high-quality, secure, and free from common anti-patterns, reducing deployment risks and improving reliability.
