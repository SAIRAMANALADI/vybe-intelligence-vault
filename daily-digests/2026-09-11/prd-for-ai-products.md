## Summary
A structured prompt package for generating Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) tailored specifically for AI products. It uses a systematic 15-decision-dimension framework to guide AI platforms in producing comprehensive, actionable PRDs with AI-specific chapters and quality checks.

## Key Features
- 15 decision dimensions for AI product PRDs, including 6 AI-exclusive dimensions (e.g., AI capability boundaries, data flywheel, AI UX paradigms).
- Conditional deep-dive dimensions triggered by product type (e.g., evaluation strategies, agent behavior definitions, tool interfaces).
- 15-chapter PRD structure with 4 AI-specific chapters (AI UX, AI tech stack, data flywheel, AI safety/ethics).
- Quality self-assessment (★★★★★) with 4-dimensional checks (decision completeness, AI depth, executability, risk control).
- Dual delivery modes: full skill package (for AI IDEs) and zero-dependency single-file prompt (for any AI platform).

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It standardizes the creation of AI-specific PRDs, ensuring structured, actionable, and AI-aware documentation that bridges the gap between vague ideas and executable AI product plans.
