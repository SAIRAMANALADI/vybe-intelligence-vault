## Summary
A language-agnostic quality engineering suite for AI agents that enforces a 'understand, implement, deliver with evidence' workflow. Provides a protocol, CLI, state machine, and CI gates to standardize agent-agnostic task execution, approvals, and delivery verification across .NET, Node, Python, and other stacks.

## Key Features
- Agent-agnostic protocol for task execution and approvals, ensuring consistency across Claude Code, Pi, Codex, and others
- Unified CLI (`aq.ps1`) and state machine for managing work items, approvals, and delivery verification
- Multi-language gate adapters for .NET, Node.js, Python, and custom toolchains with deterministic checks
- Hard, non-bypassable CI gates and branch protection rules to enforce quality standards
- Automated evidence collection, hashing, and verification for all stages of the delivery pipeline

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a standardized, agent-agnostic framework for enforcing quality gates and evidence-based delivery in AI-driven development workflows, ensuring consistency and reliability across diverse AI agents and toolchains.
