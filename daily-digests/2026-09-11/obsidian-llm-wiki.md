## Summary
obsidian-llm-wiki transforms Obsidian markdown notes into a structured knowledge base accessible by AI agents, enabling cited, accurate responses without hallucinations. It bridges personal notes with AI models like Claude, Gemini, or local tools, acting as a headless research expert.

## Key Features
- Converts Obsidian markdown notes into a structured knowledge graph for AI access
- Provides a six-persona team of AI agents (Analyst, Writer, Archivist, Coder, Strategist, Teacher)
- Ensures cited responses with direct links to original notes to prevent hallucinations
- Supports multi-model integration (Claude, Gemini, local tools) with a single-click switch
- Privacy-focused with local processing and no cloud storage of personal notes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables RAG builders to integrate personal knowledge bases with AI agents while ensuring accuracy through direct citations and structured note relationships.
