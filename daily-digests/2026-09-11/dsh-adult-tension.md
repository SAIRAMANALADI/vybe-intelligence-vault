## Summary
A DeepSeek-compatible AI skill plugin for adult-themed interactive narrative generation, enabling NPCs with memory, autonomous decisions, and continuous world progression. Features a large built-in content library for randomized or controlled story openings, YAML-based save systems, and strict boundary enforcement for 18+ content.

## Key Features
- NPCs remember interactions, maintain stances, and make autonomous decisions based on relationships and pressure
- Built-in content library with thousands of randomized or pre-locked story elements (eras, locations, NPCs, tensions)
- Continuous world progression with off-screen event tracking and YAML-based save/load systems
- Strict boundary enforcement for 18+ content, including pause/safety word triggers and consent checks
- Modular architecture with CLI tools for content validation, state management, and script-based world generation
