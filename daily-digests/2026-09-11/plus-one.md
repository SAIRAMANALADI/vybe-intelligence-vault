## Summary
Plus One is a self-hosted household finance agent for couples, enabling multi-turn financial interactions via Telegram with deterministic accounting and PostgreSQL-backed auditability. It coordinates financial data analysis, proposals, and verified mutations while ensuring idempotency and data integrity.

## Key Features
- Multi-turn financial interactions with Telegram for expense/income capture and reporting
- Deterministic accounting with append-only ledger and PostgreSQL constraints for auditability
- Maker-checker verification for mutations with policy validation and idempotency
- Durable working memory for household goals, preferences, and conventions with safe views
- Modular architecture with orchestrator, query, accounting, ingestion, and reporting components
