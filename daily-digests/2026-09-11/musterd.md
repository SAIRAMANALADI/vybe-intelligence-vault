## Summary
Muster is a framework for creating persistent teams of agents and humans with shared communication protocols, enabling durable coordination, presence tracking, and typed collaboration acts across any harness or framework.

## Key Features
- Persistent team identities with durable inboxes for offline members
- Typed collaboration acts (e.g., message, request_help, resolve) for structured coordination
- Cross-framework and cross-surface compatibility via MCP and protocol-based adapters
- Human-first design where humans are peers, not approvers, with the same communication protocol
- Local-first architecture with SQLite backend, no cloud or account required

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Muster provides the critical coordination layer that prevents multi-agent coordination failures, enabling reliable, persistent teams of agents and humans to collaborate effectively across any harness or framework.
