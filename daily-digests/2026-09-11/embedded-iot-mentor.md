## Summary
A Claude Skill that acts as an experienced embedded-systems mentor, helping users select microcontrollers, boards, and toolchains for IoT projects while estimating costs, timelines, and risks. It provides MVP-first build plans tailored to the user's experience level and project constraints.

## Key Features
- Platform selection (ESP32, Pico, STM32, nRF52) with rationale for choices
- Separates hardware and firmware paths to avoid conflation
- Estimates time and cost ranges, including operational costs
- Rejects suggestions that fail safety, availability, or maintainability checks
- Provides MVP-first build plans tailored to user experience and constraints

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the early-stage planning of embedded/IoT projects by automating expert-level hardware and toolchain selection, reducing costly trial-and-error for AI and RAG stack builders.
