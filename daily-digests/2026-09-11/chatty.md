## Summary
Chatty is a free, open-source, browser-based AI agent platform that enables users to create, train, and manage custom AI agents with personalities, knowledge, and tools. It supports multi-provider AI models, scheduled background tasks, integrations with business tools, and local-first deployment.

## Key Features
- Browser-based UI for agent management and interaction
- Multi-provider AI support (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Ollama, Together AI)
- Scheduled background tasks with guaranteed delivery (heartbeat, reminders, cron)
- Integration with business tools (Gmail, Google Calendar, QuickBooks, Telegram, etc.)
- Knowledge import/export and training mode for agent customization

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Chatty provides a flexible, local-first framework for building and orchestrating custom AI agents with robust scheduling and integration capabilities, reducing vendor lock-in and enabling personalized automation for businesses and individuals.
