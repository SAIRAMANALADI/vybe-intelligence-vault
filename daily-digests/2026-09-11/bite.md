## Summary
Bite is an iOS health logging and AI coaching application that integrates health data, nutrition tracking, and encrypted lab reports with an AI-driven coach. It provides tool-driven interactions, long-term memory for user context, and source-aware health data synchronization while prioritizing privacy and verifiable data handling.

## Key Features
- Tool-driven AI coach with 27+ typed tools for health data interaction, nutrition logging, and workout planning
- Long-term memory system using embeddings to deduplicate and contextualize user data for future conversations
- Source-aware health data synchronization with Apple Health and Google Health/Fitbit, ensuring data provenance and auditability
- Encrypted lab report processing with structured biomarker extraction and clinical phenotypic age calculation
- Verifiable privacy lifecycle with local and cloud exports, checksum verification, and authenticated account deletion

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Bite demonstrates a robust, privacy-first approach to integrating AI agents with sensitive health data, offering a blueprint for secure, auditable, and user-controlled AI-driven health applications.
