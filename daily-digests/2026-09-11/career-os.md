## Summary
Career OS is a local AI-powered career decision-making platform that helps users explore job directions, analyze job descriptions, customize resumes, and manage job applications through an interactive dashboard and decision-agent workflows.

## Key Features
- Interactive career profile builder with AI-assisted data extraction and confirmation
- Decision-agent workflows for job direction exploration, company analysis, and resume customization
- Visual dashboard (workbench) with decision timelines, information graphs, and application tracking
- Markdown-based data storage for job descriptions, decisions, and profiles
- Privacy-first architecture with strict separation of system assets and personal data

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, AI-driven approach to career decision-making and job application management, reducing ambiguity in career transitions and improving resume targeting.
