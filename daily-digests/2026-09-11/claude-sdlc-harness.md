## Summary
A self-evolving SDLC enforcement system for AI coding agents like Claude Code, automating planning, testing, and escalation workflows. It enforces engineering discipline through hooks, scoring, and cross-model validation while adapting over time based on performance metrics.

## Key Features
- Automated SDLC enforcement with hooks for planning, testing, and review
- Statistical validation and E2E scoring in CI to measure compliance and quality
- Cross-model review (e.g., Codex) to catch issues missed by primary AI agents
- Self-evolving workflows that adapt based on performance metrics and drift detection
