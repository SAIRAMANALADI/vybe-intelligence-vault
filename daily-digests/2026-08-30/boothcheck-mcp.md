## Summary
boothcheck-mcp is an MCP server that decomposes US stock prices into implied operating-income growth rates, duration, and margins by analyzing SEC EDGAR filings. It provides structured financial insights without offering investment advice, price targets, or ratings.

## Key Features
- Inverts stock prices into implied growth, duration, and margin assumptions using reverse-DCF methodology
- Provides structured financial insights from SEC filings without offering investment advice or ratings
- Offers both keyless and authenticated endpoints for varying access levels
- Supports 2,000+ US tickers with tools ranging from free to research and analyst plans
- Integrates seamlessly with MCP clients like Claude, ChatGPT, and VS Code

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI assistants to provide transparent, data-driven insights into stock price assumptions without generating investment advice.
