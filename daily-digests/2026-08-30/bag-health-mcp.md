## Summary
MCP server providing access to Swiss Federal Office of Public Health (BAG) infectious disease surveillance data and health indicators. Enables querying epidemiological trends, wastewater signals, and multi-source health metrics for 51 pathogens across Switzerland and Liechtenstein.

## Key Features
- Access to 51 disease topics and 1,386 data series from BAG's Infectious Disease Dashboard (IDD)
- Multi-source health indicators from Obsan, Versorgungsatlas, and Sucht Schweiz
- Real-time wastewater viral load monitoring as early outbreak indicator
- Comprehensive toolset for canton-level and national health data analysis
- Secure deployment options with HTTP transport, authentication, and CORS support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides critical, real-time Swiss public health data for AI systems to generate accurate, evidence-based insights on infectious disease trends and public health interventions.
