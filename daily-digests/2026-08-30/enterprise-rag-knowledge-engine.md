## Summary
A production-grade, containerized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) backend API designed as a centralized 'AI Brain' for enterprise use. It autonomously ingests, vectorizes, and retrieves proprietary business frameworks, internal documentation, and client histories to augment AI reasoning capabilities like Anthropic's Claude.

## Key Features
- Headless API-first design with FastAPI for seamless integration into any frontend or MCP client
- Autonomous ingestion and vectorization of proprietary business documents and client histories
- Strict grounding of AI responses in localized ChromaDB vector store to eliminate hallucinations
- Containerized deployment with Docker for scalability and portability across environments
- Built-in support for RAG evaluation frameworks to measure retrieval precision and performance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This repository provides a production-ready, enterprise-grade RAG orchestrator that ensures hallucination-free AI responses while enabling scalable, automated knowledge retrieval for AI agents and systems.
