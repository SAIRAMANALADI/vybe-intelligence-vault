## Summary
mcp-jobs is an AI-powered job-search assistant that integrates with Claude Desktop to automate job discovery, CV/cover letter tailoring, application tracking, recruiter email scanning, and interview preparation. It operates locally with no API costs beyond your existing subscriptions.

## Key Features
- Automated job discovery via Apify and web search
- AI-driven CV and cover letter customization per job application
- Local SQLite database for tracking applications and pipeline status
- Gmail integration for recruiter email scanning and draft replies
- Interview preparation briefs generated from calendar data

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the job search process by automating tedious tasks like CV tailoring and recruiter outreach, allowing AI engineers to focus on higher-value activities while maintaining full control over submissions.
