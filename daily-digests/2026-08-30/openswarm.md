## Summary
OpenSwarm is an autonomous AI agent orchestrator that coordinates multiple AI agents to solve software engineering tasks, such as fixing bugs or reviewing code. It integrates with Linear, Discord, Slack, and other tools, and supports various AI models including OpenAI, OpenRouter, local models, and Claude Code.

## Key Features
- Autonomous AI agent orchestration with Worker/Reviewer pipelines
- Integration with Linear, Discord, Slack, Telegram, and webhooks for notifications
- Long-term memory via LanceDB for per-repository knowledge retention
- Cost-aware routing across AI models (L0–L6 benchmark ladder)
- TUI chat interface and CLI commands for task execution and management

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
OpenSwarm streamlines the deployment and management of autonomous AI agents for software engineering tasks, reducing manual intervention and enabling scalable, cost-effective automation in RAG and AI stacks.
