## Summary
T-RADAR is an interactive trademark clearance system that combines multimodal retrieval (text and image) with agentic simulation to assess trademark conflict risks. It enables users to simulate trademark examination processes, refine inputs, and compare outcomes interactively.

## Key Features
- Hybrid multimodal retrieval combining BM25 and embedding-based ANN search for logo and mark name queries
- Agentic simulation of trademark examination protocols (Examiner–Applicant exchanges) with Conflict Risk and Registrability scoring
- Interactive refinement loop for comparing outcomes after adjusting inputs like mark name or goods/services scope
- Grounded judgments using real trademark office actions and decisions of refusal (KIPRIS data)
- Modular architecture supporting local and cloud deployment with optional GPU offloading for retrieval

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
T-RADAR demonstrates how multimodal retrieval and agentic simulation can be integrated to build interactive RAG systems for domain-specific legal and compliance workflows.
