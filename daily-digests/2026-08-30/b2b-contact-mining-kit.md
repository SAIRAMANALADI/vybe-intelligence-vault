## Summary
A free, open-source toolkit for mining B2B contacts by scraping Google, Telegram, and Discord without relying on purchased databases. It provides scripts, prompts, and methodologies for AI agents to automate contact discovery and validation.

## Key Features
- Open-source scripts for Google, Telegram, and Discord scraping to find B2B contacts
- Email validation via SMTP to ensure contact accuracy
- AI-agent-friendly prompts and structured methodology for automated contact mining
- Integration with TAPAC MCP server for zero-effort contact discovery
- Methodology documentation to avoid decayed database pitfalls

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a cost-effective, fresh alternative to decayed contact databases for RAG builders needing accurate B2B leads.
