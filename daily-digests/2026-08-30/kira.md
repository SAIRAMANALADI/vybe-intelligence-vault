## Summary
Kira is an MCP server that helps AI agents avoid repeating past failures by recording, storing, and surfacing 'scars'—detailed records of mistakes and their solutions. It enables agents to learn from community-shared failures and personal experiences, reducing redundant errors and improving task execution efficiency.

## Key Features
- Records and stores agent failures as 'scars' for future avoidance
- Surfaces personal and community scars at session/task start to prevent repeated mistakes
- Supports keyword-based lookup and goal-driven planning with failure heat-maps
- Community-driven corpus of skills and scars that compounds knowledge across users
- Privacy-first design with local-only personal data and opt-in community telemetry

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Kira ensures AI agents learn from past failures, reducing redundant errors and improving reliability in production environments by leveraging a shared corpus of real-world mistakes and solutions.
