## Summary
Ratatoskr is a self-hosted Telegram bot that aggregates, archives, and synthesizes content from web articles, YouTube videos, Twitter/X posts, and Telegram forwards into structured, searchable summaries. It supports multi-source aggregation, semantic search, and integrates with various LLM providers for summarization.

## Key Features
- Multi-source content aggregation (web, YouTube, Twitter/X, Telegram forwards)
- Structured JSON summaries with 35+ fields for consistency and validation
- Pluggable LLM providers (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, local models)
- Semantic search via Qdrant vector store with configurable embeddings
- Self-hosted, single-tenant architecture with owner-only access

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Ratatoskr streamlines the ingestion, synthesis, and retrieval of diverse digital content into structured summaries, making it essential for AI engineers building RAG systems that require high-quality, multi-source data pipelines.
