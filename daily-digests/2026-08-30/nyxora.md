## Summary
Nyxora is a secure, non-custodial runtime infrastructure for autonomous onchain AI agents, enabling Web3 workflows with strict client-side key isolation and zero-trust execution. It integrates with multiple AI providers and DeFi protocols while enforcing cryptographic approvals and human-in-the-loop controls.

## Key Features
- Zero-trust, defense-in-depth security architecture with cryptographic approvals and human-in-the-loop controls
- Native support for Model Context Protocol (MCP) to transform external AI agents into secure Web3 actors
- Advanced DeFi automation including cross-chain swaps, liquidity provisioning, and MEV protection
- Strict client-side key isolation and BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) for DeFi and market data providers
- Dynamic policy engine with immutable guardrails and adaptive slippage protection
