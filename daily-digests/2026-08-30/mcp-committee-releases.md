## Summary
A toolkit for accessing and searching documents published by U.S. congressional committees directly from their websites, including press releases, oversight letters, and investigation files. It provides real-time access to committee-specific data without relying on GovInfo's delayed official records.

## Key Features
- Search committee documents by headline with filters for committee, document type, and date
- Fetch full document details including text, publication date, and attached PDFs
- List available document types per committee
- No authentication required for access
- Host-allowlisted for security to committee domains

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Provides real-time access to congressional committee documents that are otherwise delayed or unavailable in official government repositories, enabling AI systems to incorporate the most current legislative and oversight materials.
