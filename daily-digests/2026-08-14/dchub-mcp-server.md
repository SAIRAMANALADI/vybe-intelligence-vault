## Summary
DC Hub MCP Server provides real-time intelligence on data-center infrastructure, power grids, interconnection queues, and energy markets for AI agents. It aggregates live telemetry across major US ISOs and global regions, enabling AI assistants to query structured data for site selection, energy analysis, and market research.

## Key Features
- 82 MCP tools for querying live infrastructure data (facilities, grids, fiber, gas pipelines)
- Real-time grid telemetry across 7 US ISOs and 49 global regions (refreshed every 5 minutes)
- Interconnection queue snapshots with per-ISO BUILD/CAUTION/AVOID verdicts
- 13 guided prompts for AI assistants (e.g., site analysis, market selection, fiber planning)
- Free tier with 10 calls/day and paid plans for higher usage tiers

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
