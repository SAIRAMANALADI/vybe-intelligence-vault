## Summary
JobHunter AI is a CLI tool that automates job searching on LinkedIn by scraping posts, identifying relevant opportunities, generating tailored resumes with AI, and sending personalized application emails to recruiters across multiple industries.

## Key Features
- Automated LinkedIn job scraping with Playwright and persistent sessions
- Multi-agent AI system (Gemini) for filtering, resume generation, email drafting, and query optimization
- Personalized resume and email generation tailored to each job posting
- Gmail SMTP integration for sending applications with PDF attachments
- Interactive CLI with dry-run mode, history tracking, and company blacklisting

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the job application process by automating repetitive tasks like resume customization and outreach, leveraging AI to improve application relevance and efficiency for job seekers.
