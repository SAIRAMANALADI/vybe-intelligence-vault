## Summary
Bilby is an Android-native client for bilibili that prioritizes user control over content recommendations. It eliminates algorithmic feeds, allowing users to curate their feed from followed creators and manual searches, with optional AI-assisted search capabilities.

## Key Features
- No algorithmic recommendations—users manually curate their feed from followed creators and searches
- AI-powered assistant for contextual video search with reasoning explanations
- Customizable playback experience with support for subtitles, danmaku, and background audio
- Open-source under GPL-3.0 with verifiable builds and SLSA attestations
- User privacy-focused with no data retention or hidden tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Bilby demonstrates how to build a user-controlled, privacy-preserving content platform that avoids algorithmic manipulation, offering a blueprint for ethical AI-driven applications.
