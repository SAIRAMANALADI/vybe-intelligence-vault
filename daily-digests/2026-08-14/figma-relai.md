## Summary
Figma-Relai bridges MCP-compatible AI clients (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) with Figma, enabling AI-driven design operations like editing, auditing, and building design systems directly within Figma files. It leverages a Figma plugin for writes, ensuring compatibility with all Figma plans.

## Key Features
- Connects any MCP client (Claude, Cursor, Codex) to Figma for AI-driven design operations
- Enables bulk edits, audits, and design system management with real-time feedback in a Figma plugin
- Supports file-carried rules, precedents, and conventions for consistent AI behavior across sessions
- Includes advanced features like ghost census, token binding, and AI no-go zones for designer control
- Provides a local relay with WebSocket communication, ensuring secure and persistent AI-Figma connections

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It empowers AI-driven design workflows by integrating AI clients with Figma, enabling automated, auditable, and designer-controlled design operations without requiring paid Figma plans.
