## Summary
Humanix Académie is an open-source French cybersecurity awareness platform designed for SMEs, offering gamified training modules, compliance tools, and phishing simulations. It integrates natively with CISO Assistant for governance and threat intelligence, emphasizing sovereign hosting and AGPLv3 licensing.

## Key Features
- Multi-tenant web platform with 58 seasons and 344+ MDX training modules covering phishing, vishing, smishing, MFA, NIS2, RGPD, and AI literacy
- Gamified learning with XP, badges, leaderboards, and real-time analytics (forecast, regression, risk scoring)
- Native integration with CISO Assistant for governance and threat intelligence, including OSCAL and CEF exports
- Sovereign hosting stack (France/EU) with AGPLv3 licensing, zero Cloud Act dependencies, and CNIL-friendly cookie consent
- Quick setup wizards, white-labeling, phishing simulations, and MCP server for AI assistants (Claude, Mistral, GPT)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a sovereign, open-source alternative for cybersecurity awareness training critical for SMEs, reducing reliance on US-based proprietary tools while ensuring compliance with EU regulations like NIS2 and RGPD.
