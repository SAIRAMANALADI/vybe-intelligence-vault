## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that enables AI assistants to build, price, and customize partnership packages with the Engineering Leaders Community (ELC) for 3,100+ engineering leaders across Central Europe. It automates the creation of 12-month partnership journeys with real-time pricing and a 16% discount for AI-channel inquiries.

## Key Features
- Five MCP tools for end-to-end partnership building (qualification, matching, customization, journey design, and offer request)
- Automated 16% discount for AI-channel inquiries applied at submission
- Real-time pricing from a public offer catalog with deterministic 12-month journey planning
- Streamable HTTP endpoint with no authentication or signup required
- Seamless integration with AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the process of building and pricing engineering community partnerships directly from AI assistants, reducing friction and enabling scalable outreach with automated discounts.
