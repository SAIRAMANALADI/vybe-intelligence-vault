## Summary
Monarch Art Vault is a desktop application that transforms unorganized photo folders into print-ready art catalogs by deduplicating, sorting, and formatting images for professional use. It prepares metadata and structured outputs for AI tools to generate descriptions and product listings.

## Key Features
- Automated deduplication of identical photos to clean up collections
- Metadata extraction and intelligent cataloging by date, color, or quality
- Print-ready image formatting for standard sizes and resolutions
- AI-ready output formats for seamless integration with LLMs like Claude or GPT
- Privacy-focused local processing with no cloud dependency
