## Summary
Auteur is an end-to-end AI-driven short video production pipeline that automates the entire workflow from topic selection to final video rendering. It simulates a 16-role virtual film crew, where each role operates as an independent AI agent, collaborating via a shared database to produce Chinese short videos with scripts, subtitles, storyboards, images, voiceovers, music, and editing.

## Key Features
- 16 independent AI roles (e.g., scriptwriter, cinematographer, composer) collaborating via a shared database for modular workflows
- Precise-by-cue alignment of subtitles and visuals using SRT anchors for frame-perfect video editing
- Agent-based conversational control for natural language-driven video production and edits
- Preset-driven customization for different video formats and styles without code changes
- Data-driven feedback loop with browser extension analytics to refine future content

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Auteur provides a modular, self-hosted AI orchestration framework for RAG builders to automate complex, multi-step content generation pipelines with built-in collaboration, validation, and data-driven optimization.
