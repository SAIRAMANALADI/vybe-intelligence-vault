## Summary
A commercial Bluesky automation agent that drafts daily posts in the user's voice using AI (Claude/Sonnet 5), gates them behind a human-review approval queue, and publishes via the open AT Protocol. It also discovers and follows relevant accounts while ensuring compliance with Bluesky's automation policies.

## Key Features
- AI-powered post drafting in user's voice using Claude/Sonnet 5
- Human-review approval queue before publishing to ensure quality control
- Automated discovery and following of relevant accounts (e.g., Pine Script/MT5 traders)
- Compliance with Bluesky's open AT Protocol for sanctioned automation
- Configurable modes (review or auto) for posting and following

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a compliant and automated way to generate and publish AI-driven content on Bluesky while ensuring human oversight, reducing manual effort for creators and traders.
