## Summary
DeepPPT is an AI-driven system that transforms a topic or source document into a natively editable PPTX presentation. It automates deep research, narrative planning, visual identity extraction, asset acquisition, SVG authoring, validation, and export while preserving machine-readable contracts for quality and reproducibility.

## Key Features
- Automated deep research with 7-step workflow (outline → search → analysis → narrative → visual strategy)
- Native-editable PPTX export with structured templates and quality gates
- Dashboard for artifact visualization, quality reporting, and workflow tracking
- Deterministic chart recall with semantic fallback constraints and key validation
- Consulting-style evidence-backed narratives with traceable evidence rows and SCR alternatives

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
DeepPPT streamlines the creation of high-quality, research-backed presentations with machine-enforced contracts, reducing manual effort and ensuring reproducibility for AI engineering workflows.
