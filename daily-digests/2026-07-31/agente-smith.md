## Summary
Agente Smith is an AI-powered legal assistant specialized in Argentine law, designed to route queries across legal domains, compute deadlines, diagnose drafts, and perform real-time legal research using SAIJ's 899K+ documents. It operates as a Claude Code agent with a focus on accessibility for litigants.

## Key Features
- Domain-aware routing for 11+ legal areas (laboral, civil, penal, etc.)
- Real-time legal research via SAIJ (899K+ documents) with no external dependencies
- Multi-jurisdiction support (CABA/Nacional, Jujuy, Salta) with extensible province templates
- Private case workspace with gitignore fail-safe to prevent data leaks
- Deadline computation, draft diagnostics, and automated legal reasoning

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
