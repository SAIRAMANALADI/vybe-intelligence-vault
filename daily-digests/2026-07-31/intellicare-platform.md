## Summary
IntelliCare is a comprehensive AI-powered patient management system designed for medical clinics, integrating patient records, appointments, billing, and messaging with a Claude Opus 4.8 clinical agent. It enables clinicians to interact with the system in plain language, automating complex tasks like drug safety checks, document generation, and insurance claims.

## Key Features
- AI-powered clinical agent with 3,600+ functions for real-time patient management and decision support
- Real-time voice scribe and hands-free voice interaction via ElevenLabs integration
- Multi-tenant architecture with database-per-organization isolation and role-based access control
- Automated document analysis pipeline converting unstructured medical documents into structured data
- Drug safety and clinical decision support using OpenFDA, RxNorm, and DailyMed integrations

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
IntelliCare demonstrates how AI agents can orchestrate complex, domain-specific workflows in healthcare, offering a blueprint for integrating large-scale clinical data with autonomous decision-making in regulated environments.
