## Summary
OpsWarden is a real-time incident management platform designed to reduce Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR) by coordinating incidents and releases with a hexagonal architecture. It features automated workflows, team collaboration tools, and a modular design for extensibility.

## Key Features
- Incident lifecycle management with real-time collaboration (timeline editing, emoji reactions, presence tracking)
- Automated Action→REAction rule engine (e.g., GitHub CI failure triggers incident creation)
- Release management with step validation and automatic blocking by linked incidents
- Modular hexagonal monolith with Rust/Axum backend and Next.js/Tauri frontends
- RBAC (Observer/Responder/Manager) with team-based permissions and moderation tools

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
OpsWarden provides a robust, modular foundation for building AI-driven incident response systems with its hexagonal architecture and real-time collaboration features.
