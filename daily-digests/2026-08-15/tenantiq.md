## Summary
TenantIQ is an AI agent designed to automate the leasing process for office rentals, guiding renters from natural language search to a booked viewing. It combines LLM-driven conversation with deterministic business logic for matching, pre-qualification, application intake, and scheduling.

## Key Features
- End-to-end leasing automation from natural language search to booking
- Deterministic pre-qualification and eligibility checks separate from LLM reasoning
- Guardrails against unreliable model output (e.g., date validation, action overrides)
- Risk-scoring model (logistic regression) for applicant evaluation
- Dual frontend for renters and landlords with synthetic data seeding

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates a robust pattern for integrating LLMs with deterministic business logic in high-stakes applications like tenant screening, ensuring reliability while leveraging AI for conversational interfaces.
