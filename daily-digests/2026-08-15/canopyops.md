CanopyOps is a desktop application designed to help cannabis cultivators manage crop operations, track plant health, perform harvest calculations, and maintain detailed records for data-driven decision-making throughout the growth cycle.

- Plant health diagnostics with AI-driven recommendations based on logged observations
