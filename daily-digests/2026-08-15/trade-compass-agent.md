## Summary
A local-first AI research and trading workbench tailored for China's A-share market, integrating market data, technical and fundamental analysis, specialized agents, simulated portfolios, automated workflows, and long-term memory into a unified Web and CLI application.

## Key Features
- Specialized AI agents for intraday technical analysis, stock research, macro sentiment, and risk management
- Integrated market data tools with support for multiple data sources (AkShare, Baostock, Tushare)
- Simulated portfolio tracking with performance evaluation and signal backtesting
- Automated research workflows for pre-market briefings, stock screening, and end-of-day reviews
- Local-first memory system for session history, research notes, and decision rationales
