## Summary
An autonomous HR agent that automates recruiting and leave management workflows using LLMs and policy engines, with human oversight capabilities. It screens resumes, books interviews, processes leave requests, and logs all decisions for review.

## Key Features
- Automated resume screening with LLM scoring against job descriptions
- Google Meet interview scheduling with real-time calendar integration
- Leave request processing with policy-based decision-making (balance, notice periods, blackout dates)
- Human override console for reviewing and overturning automated decisions
- Prompt-injection defense with multi-layered scanning and hard-coded rules

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It demonstrates a practical, production-ready autonomous agent for HR workflows, combining LLMs with deterministic policy engines and human oversight, offering a blueprint for scalable AI-driven automation in regulated domains.
