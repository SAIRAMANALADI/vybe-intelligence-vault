## Summary
OntoRamp Decision Intelligence is an MCP server that enables AI agents to log, evaluate, and ground consequential decisions against an organization's documented authority graph, creating traceable audit trails for governance and compliance.

## Key Features
- Log consequential AI-assisted decisions with authority context for traceability
- Evaluate decisions for authority gaps, precedent conflicts, and governance alignment
- Look up prior decisions relevant to proposed actions via `find_decision_precedent`
- Multi-tier pricing model (Free, Developer, Org) with scalable features
- Pre-seeds organization knowledge graphs to accelerate Agentic Readiness Assessments

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides the critical governance layer for AI agents by ensuring decisions are logged, evaluated, and grounded against organizational authority, enabling traceability and compliance in agentic workflows.
