## Summary
PUA is an AI Coding Agent skill plugin that uses corporate 'PUA' (motivational pressure) tactics to drive AI agents to exhaust all solutions before giving up. It supports 10+ agents like Claude Code, Trae, and Cursor, with 15 corporate-style methodologies and pressure escalation mechanisms.

## Key Features
- 15 corporate-style methodologies (e.g., Alibaba, ByteDance, Huawei) with tailored pressure tactics
- Pressure escalation system (L0-L4) to force AI agents to persist or escalate solutions
- Multi-agent support across 10+ platforms (Claude Code, Trae, Cursor, etc.)
- Built-in evidence chain and KPI reporting for delivery validation
- Offline mode and automatic iteration loops for sustained pressure
