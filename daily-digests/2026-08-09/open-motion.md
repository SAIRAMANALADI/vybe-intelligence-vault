## Summary
OpenMotion is an AI-native motion design platform that enables conversational animation creation, editing, and export. It allows users to design animations through natural language, fine-tune keyframes, and package motions as reusable skills for AI agents or web/video output.

## Key Features
- Conversational motion design with an AI agent for rapid prototyping and refinement
- Real-time animation editing with keyframe and timeline control
- Export animations to HTML, video, or code (CSS/JSON/React) with one click
- Packaging animations as reusable skills for AI agents or team workflows
- Physical easing and spatial motion with 2D/3D transform support

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
OpenMotion bridges the gap between AI-driven design and executable motion artifacts, enabling seamless integration of animations into AI workflows and production-ready outputs.
