## Summary
An AI-powered interview preparation tool that automates company research, generates customized interview materials, simulates real interviews with STAR framework scoring, and provides performance feedback. It leverages multi-dimensional research engines and cross-references interview experiences to tailor preparation for specific companies and roles.

## Key Features
- Multi-dimensional research engine with 15+ targeted searches per company/job
- AI-driven mock interviews with company-specific interviewer simulations
- STAR framework-based scoring with calibrated benchmarks (1-5 scale)
- Cross-referenced interview experience analysis for topic probability prediction
- Post-interview debriefing and data backflow for continuous improvement

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
