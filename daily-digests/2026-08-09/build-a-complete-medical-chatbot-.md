## Summary
A medical chatbot leveraging Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide context-aware, multilingual responses using OpenAI GPT-4o mini and Pinecone vector database. The system integrates structured symptom extraction, medical risk assessment, and source-grounded answers from curated medical content.

## Key Features
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for medical responses
- Structured symptom extraction and medical risk assessment
- Multilingual support (English, Swedish, Spanish, Arabic)
- Source-grounded answers with citations from MedlinePlus
- Automated CI/CD pipeline with Docker and AWS

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
This repository provides a production-ready RAG-based medical chatbot with multilingual support, structured symptom extraction, and source-grounded responses, making it a valuable reference for AI engineers building domain-specific conversational agents.
