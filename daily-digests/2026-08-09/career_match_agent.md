## Summary
Career Match Agent is an AI-powered tool that tailors job applications by analyzing CVs against job offers, providing fit scores, ATS coverage, and actionable improvements. It generates ATS-optimized CVs and cover letters in French or English, with autonomous agentic loops for iterative optimization and integrity verification.

## Key Features
- Fit score (0-100) combining LLM assessment and deterministic ATS keyword coverage
- ATS-optimized CV and cover letter generation with bilingual support (FR/EN)
- Autonomous agentic loops for iterative optimization and integrity verification
- ATS parse audit to verify PDF extractability and keyword retention
- Internship sourcing and application pipeline with relevance ranking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines and enhances job application processes by leveraging AI to tailor CVs and cover letters for ATS compatibility and recruiter appeal, reducing manual effort and improving success rates.
