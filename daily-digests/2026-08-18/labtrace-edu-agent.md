## Summary
A specialized agent for grading and providing feedback on university lab reports in DOCX format. It parses native Word content, applies rubric-based scoring with evidence localization, and writes back annotated results to editable DOCX files, ensuring teacher oversight and compliance with privacy standards.

## Key Features
- Native DOCX parsing with paragraph, table, and image localization
- Rubric-driven evidence-based scoring with confidence thresholds
- Teacher-in-the-loop review with native Word annotation and backwriting
- Privacy-compliant report processing (PII redaction, image opt-in)
- Class-level analytics for weak dimensions and feedback synthesis

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines evidence-based grading workflows for educators while ensuring compliance and teacher oversight, making it essential for AI-assisted educational tools.
