## Summary
An AI-powered Chinese podcast project that automatically fetches trending Hacker News articles daily, generates Chinese summaries using AI, and converts them into podcast content with TTS. The project provides multi-platform access via RSS, web, and major podcast apps.

## Key Features
- Automated daily scraping of Hacker News trending articles
- AI-powered Chinese summarization and script generation
- TTS-based audio conversion for podcast delivery
- Multi-platform accessibility via RSS, web, and podcast apps
- Cloudflare-based deployment with R2 storage and KV for data management
