## Summary
A long-running research service that transforms source-backed findings into bounded, reviewable decision artifacts using LangChain, DeepAgents, and LangGraph. It provides durable workflows, evidence tracking, and controlled review processes for AI-driven decision-making.

## Key Features
- Long-running research execution with bounded `run_id` scoping
- Durable evidence ledger and review workflows with feature flags
- Idempotency-key support for lost-response reconciliation
- React-based Agent Research Operations Console for lifecycle management
- Controlled verification and failure cause tracking with deterministic proofs

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a robust framework for orchestrating AI-driven research workflows with durable state management, evidence tracking, and controlled review processes, essential for building reliable RAG systems.
