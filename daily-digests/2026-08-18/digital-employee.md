## Summary
A self-hosted runtime for deploying role-based digital employees that leverage approved knowledge sources and tools. The initial profile, `answer-agent`, provides read-only team support with citations, refusal of unsupported claims, and human escalation for uncertain queries.

## Key Features
- Modular runtime for deploying role-based digital employees with configurable profiles
- Read-only `answer-agent` profile with citation-backed responses and escalation policies
- Support for multiple knowledge sources (filesystem, Git, DWS) with strict access controls
- Human escalation and verified FAQ feedback mechanisms for safety and accountability
- Multiple entry points (CLI, HTTP, DingTalk) with stateless design and security defaults

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a secure, self-hosted framework for deploying role-based AI agents with strict knowledge boundaries and human oversight, critical for enterprise RAG systems requiring citation integrity and safety.
