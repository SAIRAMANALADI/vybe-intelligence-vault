## Summary
RecoverIQ is an autonomous AI agent designed for revenue recovery in India's UPI Autopay and recurring commerce ecosystem. It detects at-risk revenue, diagnoses failures using NPCI response codes, applies Bayesian Thompson Sampling for optimal intervention selection, and ensures 100% RBI/TRAI compliance while maintaining an immutable audit ledger.

## Key Features
- Autonomous revenue recovery using Bayesian Thompson Sampling for optimal intervention selection
- Proactive mandate expiry interception (T-72h pre-failure prevention) to prevent churn
- 10 deterministic RBI/TRAI guardrails ensuring 100% compliance and DND adherence
- Contextual customer trust scoring and spend pattern anomaly detection for personalized recovery
- Immutable audit ledger for verified recovery tracking and ROI analysis
