## Summary
A monetized MCP server providing an AI training dataset marketplace with 2M+ museum artworks enriched with 111-field Golden Codex metadata. Supports autonomous x402 USDC micropayments for AI agents and Stripe for human buyers, with compliance manifests and on-demand enrichment services.

## Key Features
- 2M+ CC0 museum artworks from 7 institutions with 111-field Golden Codex enrichment
- Dual payment systems: x402 USDC for AI agents and Stripe for humans
- 13 MCP tools for search, metadata retrieval, enrichment, and compliance
- Autonomous micropayment orchestration via MonetizedMCP broker
- Auto-generated AB 2013 and EU AI Act compliance manifests
