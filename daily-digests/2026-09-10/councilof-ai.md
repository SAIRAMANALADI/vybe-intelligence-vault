## Summary
Council of AI is an independent body for measuring and attesting AI system behavior using frozen instruments and Ed25519-signed credentials. It provides deterministic grading, transparency, and re-attestation to ensure ongoing accountability without certification or commercial bias.

## Key Features
- Independent AI behavior measurement using frozen instruments
- Ed25519-signed, offline-verifiable measurement credentials
- Deterministic grading with no LLM-as-judge bias
- Public, loginless verification of results via DID
- Re-attestation cadence to maintain current evidence

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a trustworthy, transparent framework for measuring and verifying AI system behavior, critical for building reliable and accountable RAG systems.
