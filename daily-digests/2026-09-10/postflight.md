## Summary
Postflight is an OpenClaw skill that automates drafting and scheduling posts for an X (Twitter) account while enforcing human approval before publishing. It drafts posts three times a day based on open-source work, sends them to Telegram for review, and only publishes when the user replies 'ship'.

## Key Features
- Drafts posts 3 times daily from open-source work without autonomous publishing
- Enforces human approval via Telegram (reply 'ship' to publish)
- Supports customizable content pillars and voice examples for tailored posts
- Includes scouting mode to identify reply opportunities with drafts
- State management outside the skill folder for seamless upgrades

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a disciplined, human-approved approach to maintaining social media cadence for open-source contributors, reducing the risk of account suspension while automating content creation.
