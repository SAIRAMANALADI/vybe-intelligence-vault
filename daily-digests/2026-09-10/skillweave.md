## Summary
SkillWeave is a multi-agent AI skill orchestration framework that automates AI-assisted product development across 7 lifecycle phases and 13 modular skills, enabling seamless collaboration between AI coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, and Gemini CLI.

## Key Features
- 13 modular skills covering discovery, blueprinting, design, build, release, launch, and post-release phases
- 7-phase lifecycle automation with testing pyramid (5 levels) and gate decisions
- LLM Council for multi-model deliberation with web search and consensus scoring
- Host-neutral skill design with MCP and Markdown-based skill distribution
- Risk-aware execution modes (conservative, medium, unicorn) with configurable overrides

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
SkillWeave streamlines AI-driven product development by orchestrating multi-agent workflows, reducing manual coordination overhead and enabling scalable, automated execution from idea to post-release.
