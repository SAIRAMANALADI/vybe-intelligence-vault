## Summary
JarvisNano transforms the Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75C into a voice-first desk assistant with native Gemini Live integration, reactive AMOLED display, and physical privacy controls. It operates entirely on-device with no cloud relay beyond Gemini, supporting voice commands, live tool calls, motion awareness, and adaptive power management.

## Key Features
- Native Gemini Live integration for real-time voice conversations with wake-word detection
- Reactive round AMOLED display with gesture-based navigation and live data feeds
- On-device tool execution (weather, news, Wikipedia, etc.) via JarvisMCP with read-only allowlist
- Adaptive power management with motion-aware deep sleep and wake-on-motion
- Physical privacy controls including glass touch mute and face-down privacy mode

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
JarvisNano demonstrates a fully on-device voice-first AI assistant architecture with tight hardware-software integration, offering a blueprint for building privacy-preserving, low-latency edge AI systems that combine voice interaction with reactive displays and adaptive power management.
