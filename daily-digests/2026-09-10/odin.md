## Summary
O.D.I.N. is a zero-cost decision-support system that employs four independent reasoning passes—cold optimization, adversarial strategy, behavioral-bias audit, and first-principles arbitration—to synthesize high-stakes decisions with mathematical, strategic, and behavioral rigor using Google's Gemini Flash model and Streamlit.

## Key Features
- Four independent reasoning personas for rigorous decision synthesis
- Zero-cost deployment and hosting using free-tier models
- Privacy trade-off with public repo and potential model logging
- Pre-commit hooks to prevent secret leaks
- Disposable architecture with fixed reasoning design
