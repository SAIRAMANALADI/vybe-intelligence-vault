## Summary
Celorus plugin provides decision-ready financial intelligence for companies by generating cited financial reports, cap tables, and answers to specific questions directly from official company records. It ensures data provenance and transparency by never inventing or estimating missing figures.

## Key Features
- Generates financial-health reports (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow) with cited sources
- Provides cap table analysis with funding rounds, share classes, and dilution
- Answers targeted company-specific questions with attached sources
- Supports cross-company synthesis for broader queries
- Delivers branded, scannable financial summaries with provenance tracking

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Celorus ensures RAG builders can ground financial and company data in verifiable, official records, eliminating hallucinations and improving trust in AI-generated insights.
