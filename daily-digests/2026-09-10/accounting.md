## Summary
A production-grade, multi-country accounting engine built for Node.js and TypeScript, supporting US GAAP, Mexican NIF, and IFRS standards. Features double-entry bookkeeping, AP/AR automation, bank reconciliation, fixed asset management, inventory costing, and triple-entry accounting via blockchain attestation.

## Key Features
- Supports multiple accounting standards (US GAAP, Mexican NIF, IFRS) with full double-entry bookkeeping
- Triple-entry accounting via blockchain attestation (zkVerify + Bitcoin anchoring)
- Automated AP/AR, bank reconciliation, fixed asset management, and inventory costing
- Multi-tenant architecture with schema-per-tenant isolation in PostgreSQL
- Compliance features for Mexico (CFDI/SAT) and US GAAP
