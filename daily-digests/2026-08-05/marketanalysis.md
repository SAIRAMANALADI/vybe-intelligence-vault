## Summary
A structured market analysis template for AI chat agents that enforces disciplined, consistent research output using Astronacci/Elliott Wave, Fibonacci retracements, and risk management rules. Designed for Hermes, Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI agents to generate market insights across assets like XAUUSD, BTC, forex, and indices.

## Key Features
- Enforces a strict, repeatable market analysis format for AI agents to ensure consistency and discipline in outputs.
- Integrates Astronacci/Elliott Wave, Fibonacci retracements, SMA20, RSI, and Bollinger Bands for technical analysis.
- Includes fixed risk parameters (30 pip entry, 100 pip SL, TP1-4 ladder) to standardize trade setups.
- Requires minimum 3-4 embedded news links per analysis to ensure data reliability and reduce bias.
- Designed for cross-asset compatibility (XAUUSD, BTC, forex, indices) with adaptable templates.
