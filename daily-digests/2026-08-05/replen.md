## Summary
Replen is a local intelligence layer for AI coding agents that maps projects to their technical capabilities, builds a knowledge graph, and autonomously surfaces high-signal OSS suggestions tailored to your codebase. It operates entirely on-device, ensuring privacy while providing context-aware recommendations.

## Key Features
- Autonomous capability-based matching of OSS projects to your codebase using per-capability embeddings and semantic analysis
- Three-part system: Brainstem (matching core), Watchtower (maintained source network for alerts), and Atlas (knowledge graph of your dev world)
- Self-tuning learning loop that adapts recommendations based on your adoption, skip, and review actions
- Privacy-first design: all processing occurs locally; your code never leaves your machine
- Seamless integration with AI tools like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor via MCP server and skills

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Replen streamlines AI-driven development by providing hyper-relevant, context-aware OSS suggestions that align with your actual codebase, reducing manual research and improving productivity.
