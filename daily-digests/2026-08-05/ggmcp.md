## Summary
GitGuardian MCP Server integrates GitGuardian's secret detection and incident management capabilities into AI agents, enabling proactive scanning for leaked credentials, incident triage, honeytoken generation, and automated remediation directly within IDEs or chat clients.

## Key Features
- Proactive secret scanning for over 600 credential types in codebases
- Incident management with filtering, assignment, resolution, and tagging capabilities
- Honeytoken generation and monitoring for detecting unauthorized access
- Automated code-fix remediation via pull requests for detected secrets
- Multi-deployment options including hosted, self-hosted, and local stdio modes

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to proactively secure codebases by detecting and remediating secrets before leaks occur, integrating seamlessly into existing development workflows.
