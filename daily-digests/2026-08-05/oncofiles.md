## Summary
Oncofiles is an MCP server designed for cancer patients and caregivers to organize scattered medical records from Google Drive, Gmail, and Calendar into a structured, AI-accessible format. It enables natural language queries about health data through AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT.

## Key Features
- 76 MCP tools across 14 modules for comprehensive medical data management
- Multi-patient support with full data isolation and per-patient OAuth
- Bidirectional Google Drive sync with automatic OCR and document versioning
- AI-powered metadata extraction, summaries, and structured data extraction
- Integration with Claude, ChatGPT, and other MCP clients for natural language queries

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Oncofiles streamlines medical record organization for AI-driven healthcare applications, enabling seamless integration of scattered patient data into RAG systems for natural language querying and analysis.
