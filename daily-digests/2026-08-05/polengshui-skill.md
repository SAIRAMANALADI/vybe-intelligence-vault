## Summary
A skill framework that transforms AI critique into an evidence-based, verifiable, and actionable adversarial review process. It helps users challenge assumptions, validate claims, and identify risks in decisions, plans, or ideas by enforcing structured evidence audits and premortem analyses.

## Key Features
- Structured adversarial review process for AI-generated or human ideas
- Evidence auditing to separate facts, inferences, and assumptions
- Premortem analysis to identify failure conditions and termination criteria
- Low-cost validation pathways for high-risk assumptions
- Modular and extensible framework for custom scenarios and domains

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a systematic way to challenge AI outputs and human decisions with verifiable evidence, reducing blind spots and improving decision quality in RAG pipelines.
