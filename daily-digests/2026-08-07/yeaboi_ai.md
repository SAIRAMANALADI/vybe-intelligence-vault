## Summary
yeaboi.ai is a terminal-based AI-powered Scrum Master that automates project planning, standups, retrospectives, performance tracking, and reporting for engineering teams. It decomposes projects into epics, stories, tasks, and sprint plans while integrating with tools like Jira, GitHub, and Notion.

## Key Features
- Full-screen terminal UI with animated splash and dark/light themes for seamless team interaction
- Smart project intake that extracts requirements and generates sprint plans from natural language descriptions
- Seven operational modes: Planning, Standup, Retro, Planning Poker, Performance, Reporting, and Team Analysis
- Integration with 37+ tools including Jira, GitHub, Azure DevOps, Confluence, and Notion for real-time sync
- Five export formats (Markdown, HTML, JSON, Jira sync, Azure DevOps sync) and session persistence with SQLite

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines Agile workflows by automating repetitive project management tasks, reducing coordination overhead and improving team productivity through AI-driven planning and reporting.
