## Summary
A skill for AI agents that transforms them into a long-form bedtime storyteller, remembering story elements between sessions and compiling the narrative into a printable book with illustrations, PDF, EPUB, and HTML formats.

## Key Features
- Persistent story world and characters across sessions
- Customizable story genres and age filters
- Child-specific hero portraits with local image processing
- Structured book assembly with PDF, EPUB, and HTML outputs
- Time-controlled chapter lengths and narrative closure

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI agents to create cohesive, long-form narratives with persistent memory and deliver them as structured, printable books, enhancing interactive storytelling for children.
