## Summary
HUQAN is a local-first judgment and verification layer designed to add trust boundaries around AI claims, memory writes, and risky actions by connecting decisions to evidence, scope, policy, and auditable Trust Receipts.

## Key Features
- Graph-backed claim verification and contradiction detection
- Explicit relation markers (CAUSES, PREVENTS, ENABLES, DEPENDS_ON)
- Memory admission and workspace isolation with provenance tracking
- Risk classification and safety gates for guarded actions
- Portable Trust Receipts and audit records for compliance

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
HUQAN provides a critical trust and verification layer for AI systems, ensuring decisions are evidence-based, auditable, and policy-compliant, which is essential for building reliable and transparent RAG applications.
