## Summary
An MCP server that enables AI to learn and interact with music through piano, guitar, and vocal synthesis. It provides 120 songs across 12 genres, six sound engines, a browser-based cockpit, and a practice journal for iterative learning.

## Key Features
- 41 MCP tools for AI-driven music interaction and learning
- Six sound engines (piano, guitar, vocal) with physical modeling and additive synthesis
- Browser cockpit with interactive piano roll, vocal synthesizer, and tuning lab
- Practice journal for iterative AI learning and reflection
- Public dataset (jam-actions-v0) for training LLMs in grounded tool-use over symbolic music

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges the gap between AI's text-based capabilities and real-world sensory experiences like music, enabling more grounded and interactive AI learning systems.
