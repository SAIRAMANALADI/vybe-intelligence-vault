## Summary
An MCP server that enables AI to learn and interact with music through piano, guitar, and vocal synthesis. It provides 120 songs across 12 genres, six sound engines, interactive tools, and a practice journal for iterative learning.

## Key Features
- 41 MCP tools for AI interaction with music (playback, annotation, transposition, etc.)
- Six sound engines (piano, guitar, vocal tract, additive synth, layered combinator)
- Interactive browser cockpit with piano roll, vocal synthesizer, and tuning lab
- Practice journal for iterative AI learning and reflection
- Public dataset (jam-actions-v0) for training LLMs in grounded tool-use over symbolic music

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It bridges the gap between AI text generation and real-world sensory interaction by enabling models to learn and play music through structured tools and feedback loops.
