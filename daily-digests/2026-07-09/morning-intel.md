## Summary
Morning Intel automates the daily market research workflow by aggregating fresh articles from RSS feeds, filtering and deduplicating them, and sending them to Google Gemini for structured analysis. It then pushes insights to Notion and delivers a formatted HTML email digest every morning.

## Key Features
- Automated daily news aggregation from multiple RSS feeds
- 24-hour freshness filtering and deduplication of articles
- Structured analysis of news using Google Gemini
- Seamless integration with Notion for knowledge management
- Scheduled execution via GitHub Actions for hands-off operation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It streamlines the process of turning raw news into actionable insights, reducing manual effort for AI engineers and researchers who need to stay informed.
