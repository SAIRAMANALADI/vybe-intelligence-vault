## Summary
A personal knowledge management system designed for AI agents to accumulate, refine, and synthesize knowledge over time. It emphasizes compile-time knowledge integration over runtime retrieval, ensuring compounding improvements through iterative updates and cross-referencing.

## Key Features
- Iterative knowledge accumulation with periodic linting for stale or orphaned content
- Writeback mechanism to update and refine knowledge after queries or discoveries
- Cross-referencing and contradiction detection to maintain consistency
- Inspired by compile-time knowledge integration principles for long-term retention
- Designed for AI agents to carry forward knowledge between sessions without re-derivation

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured approach for AI agents to build and refine persistent knowledge bases, reducing redundant retrieval and improving decision-making over time.
