## Summary
An MCP server that provides AI assistants with precise mathematical logic for calculating peptide reconstitution matrices, syringe mappings, and standard laboratory protocols to avoid dosage hallucinations in LLMs.

- U-100 syringe unit conversion for precise dosing
- Standard laboratory protocols for compounds like Semaglutide and BPC-157
- Plunger parallax guidelines to avoid volumetric errors
- Integration with AI assistants (Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT)
