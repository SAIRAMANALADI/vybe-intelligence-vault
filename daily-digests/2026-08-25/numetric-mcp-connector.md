## Summary
A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that provides read-only access to NuMetric.work's accounting, ERP, and business data, exposing 39 tools for querying financial statements, invoices, KPIs, documents, and more directly from live business records.

## Key Features
- Exposes 39 read-only tools for querying live business data (financial statements, invoices, KPIs, documents, etc.)
- OAuth 2.1 with PKCE authentication for secure access without exposing credentials to AI clients
- Supports multi-business selection and permission-based access control
- Provides verified and guarded financial figures with confidence indicators
- Integrates seamlessly with MCP-compatible AI clients (Claude, Cursor, VS Code, etc.)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It enables AI assistants to retrieve real-time, accurate financial and business data from NuMetric.work, ensuring responses are grounded in live records rather than guesses.
