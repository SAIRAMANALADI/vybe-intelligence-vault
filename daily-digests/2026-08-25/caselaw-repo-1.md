## Summary
Swiss Case Law Open Dataset provides a comprehensive, machine-readable archive of Swiss legal documents including 1.05M+ court decisions, 5,525 federal laws, 15,600 cantonal laws, and a 9.65M-edge citation graph. It offers 44 MCP tools for AI agents and legal research, with daily updates and CC0-licensed data.

## Key Features
- 1.05M+ court decisions (1875–present) with full text and metadata
- 5,525 federal and 15,600 cantonal laws with article-level indexing
- 9.65M-edge citation graph linking decisions and statutes
- 44 MCP tools for AI agents and legal research workflows
- Daily updates with cryptographic provenance (Merkle root + OpenTimestamps)

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a free, open, and comprehensive legal dataset with tools for AI agents to perform accurate Swiss legal research, reducing hallucinations and enabling equitable access to legal knowledge.
