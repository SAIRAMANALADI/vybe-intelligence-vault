## Summary
A Claude Code plugin that automates the used-car buying process by scraping 9 sites in parallel, drafting counter-offers, and generating buyer-grade dossiers. It enforces strict negotiation rules (e.g., OTD-only pricing, 3-anchor counters) to save buyers $5-9k on average.

## Key Features
- Parallel scraping of 9 car-buying sites (e.g., CarMax, Carvana) with VIN deduplication
- 15-minute Gmail polling to triage dealer replies and draft counter-offers using 3-anchor logic
- 50-state + DC tax/fee database for accurate OTD calculations and state-specific rules
- Generates 8-page buyer-grade dossiers (HTML → PDF) with market comps, CPO eligibility, and red-flag analysis
- Supports 6 buyer paths (cash, financing, trade-in, EV, pickup, private-party) with tailored workflows

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
