## Summary
A TypeScript-based local toolkit designed to record, validate, and review AI-assisted workflows by treating tasks as bounded records. It enables regression testing, quality gating, and human review of AI-generated outputs without executing the AI agent itself.

## Key Features
- Validates JSON schemas for AI task records, ensuring structural integrity of inputs and outputs.
- Executes public-safety scans to detect configured risk terms in documentation and prompts.
- Evaluates golden cases (positive/negative) to assess risk scores and review requirements.
- Performs regression checks against baselines to detect degradation in evaluation metrics.
- Generates comprehensive Markdown reports and JSON artifacts for human review and audit trails.

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It provides a structured, repeatable way to validate and review AI-assisted workflows, ensuring consistency and traceability without relying on external model APIs or production data.
