## Summary
Lucy is a personal AI assistant that integrates with iMessage and Slack, managing tasks like Gmail, Google Calendar, reminders, restaurant reservations, and long-term memory. It operates entirely on user-owned accounts and infrastructure, ensuring data privacy and self-hosting control.

## Key Features
- Multi-channel communication via iMessage and Slack with human-in-the-loop approvals
- Natural language reminders with escalating follow-ups and conversational rescheduling
- Integration with Gmail, Google Calendar, and Tasks for task and event management
- Long-term memory and diary functionality for contextual recall
- Restaurant reservation sniping with automated booking and availability checks

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
Lucy provides a fully customizable, self-hosted personal AI assistant framework that prioritizes privacy and integrates seamlessly with existing tools, making it ideal for users who want full control over their AI interactions.
