## Summary
Agent-FleetOps provides operational tooling and discipline for managing a multi-agent AI engineering fleet, emphasizing verifiable checks, mutation-proven guards, and revertible workflows. It includes a terminal monitor, generalized agent-discipline procedures, and export pipeline gates designed to ensure operational integrity and safety.

## Key Features
- Mutation-proven guards that can fail, ensuring verifiable integrity
- Textual-based fleet-tui for real-time monitoring of multi-agent operations
- Generalized agent-discipline procedures (24 skills) for evaluation, routing, and workflows
- Export pipeline with provenance wall-checkers and secret scanners for safe publishing
- Revertible and diffable workflows with human gate approval for rule evolution
