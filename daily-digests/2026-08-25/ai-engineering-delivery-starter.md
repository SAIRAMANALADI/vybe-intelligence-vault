## Summary
A lightweight delivery starter kit that helps independent developers and small teams transform vague AI product ideas into executable, verifiable engineering plans within a week. It provides diagnostic checklists, sprint templates, and AI collaboration protocols to streamline AI product development.

## Key Features
- AI project diagnostic checklist to identify goals, users, constraints, and risks before development
- One-week sprint template breaking down vague ideas into daily verifiable deliverables
- AI Agent collaboration protocols ensuring structured, reviewable, and mergeable outputs
- Predefined task contracts with acceptance criteria and stop conditions for AI or human collaborators
- Free-to-use templates with optional paid 90-minute engineering diagnosis and roadmapping sessions

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It accelerates the transition from conceptual AI product ideas to structured, executable plans, reducing ambiguity and improving collaboration efficiency for small teams and solo developers.
