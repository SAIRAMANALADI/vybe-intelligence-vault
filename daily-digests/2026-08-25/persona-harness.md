## Summary
Persona Harness is a CLI tool designed to enforce gate-first workflows for AI coding agents building Java/Spring backends. It records and verifies evidence, reports, and verification results to ensure agents demonstrate their work before claiming completion.

## Key Features
- Enforces completion gates requiring real test evidence before marking tasks as 'Done!'
- Records and verifies local evidence of agent actions, reports, and verification results
- Integrates with OpenCode CLI for AI agent workflows in Java/Spring projects
- Provides TDD rail for opt-in test-driven development workflows
- Supports external authority enrollment for trusted finish verification

## Why It Matters for RAG Builders
It ensures AI agents provide verifiable proof of their work, reducing the risk of premature or unverified task completion in Java/Spring backend development.
